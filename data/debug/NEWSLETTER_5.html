Bulk Deals

Sahajanand Medical Technologies

Invesco Mutual Fund purchased 8 lakh shares of Sahajanand Medical Technologies at Rs 374.91 apiece and another 28.07 lakh shares at Rs 371.44 apiece. The transactions represented a 3.5 percent stake in the company's paid-up equity capital, valued at approximately Rs 134.3 crore.

Tonbo Imaging India

HDFC Mutual Fund purchased 5 lakh shares of Tonbo Imaging at Rs 1,154.64 apiece and another 5 lakh shares at Rs 1,200 apiece. The transactions represented a 1.74 percent stake in the company's paid-up equity capital, valued at Rs 117.73 crore.

Bajaj Life Insurance also purchased 9 lakh shares, equivalent to a 1.57 percent stake, for Rs 102.14 crore at Rs 1,134.96 per share. Meanwhile, Buoyant Capital acquired 5.54 lakh shares, representing a 0.96 percent stake, for Rs 62.34 crore at Rs 1,124.43 apiece.

On the other hand, Yali Capital, through Yali Deeptech Fund I, exited Tonbo Imaging by selling its entire holding of 24 lakh shares. The sale represented a 4.18 percent stake in the company's paid-up equity capital and was valued at Rs 256.61 crore, at a price of Rs 1,069.22 per share.

Purple Agrotech Industries

Ajay Bagga acquired 50,000 equity shares, equivalent to 0.57 percent of paid-up equity, in Purple Agrotech Industries, an agri-commodities trader, for Rs 3.42 lakh at Rs 6.84 per share.

Hathi Darshak Sanmukhrai picked up 73,500 shares at same price, valued at Rs 5.02 lakh.
