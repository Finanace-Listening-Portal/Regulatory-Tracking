Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach review: Its dual personality is its biggest strength

Most homes end up with a cluttered cleaning corner: a vacuum for the floors, a mop for the kitchen, a hand brush for the sofa and a long stick for the cobwebs.

The Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach, priced at Rs 44,999 in India, wants to replace all of them with one modular machine. Ideally, it is an upgraded version of the H12 Dual, and if there's one thing that it brings over the previous model, it's that it can ‘flex’. Not talking about the one that millennials call showing off. It can literally flex, and that's its biggest USP for obvious reasons --  because Dreame chose to add FlexReach in the name.

Otherwise, it has MSPs — Multiple Selling Points — at least as per Dreame's official website. For instance, the mop drying is heated, and there is a dedicated quick-drying option. Then there is the TangleCut brush, auto-clean feature, LED-enabled scraping brush, replaceable battery and more. It is basically a long list of features that Dreame hopes will make the H12 Dual FlexReach more than just another wet-and-dry vacuum cleaner.

And that is where things get interesting. The machine is not only meant to clean the floor. It can vacuum and mop at the same time, lie flat to reach under furniture and then transform into a dry vacuum for sofas, mattresses, carpets and even those cobwebs that somehow always appear in the highest corner of the ceiling.

So, yes, Dreame is trying to solve the cluttered cleaning corner with one machine. But does having so many tricks actually make cleaning easier, or does it just mean there is one more complicated gadget sitting in that corner? I used it across my roughly 1,870 sq ft home to find out.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Setup and assembly
The versatility begins with a large box packed with parts, and this is where patience becomes important. Putting the entire thing together is more like assembling the LEGO pieces. It takes time -- no denying it. But it is a one-time thing, and once you are done with it, the entire setup becomes seamless plug-and-play.

The process looks something like this -- you unbox, take everything out, and put the handle in the main body at the top -- it is clearly indicated. You take the base dock and plug it in with the included adapter. You also see another handle; that's the second part -- the vacuum and handheld part of it. That's it; you are ready to use it. The mop and vacuum are already attached. You can add other brushes with a simple press of a button-- easy to take out and plug in

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Design and build

The core of the setup is the wet-and-dry floor cleaner, with the dry vacuum module docking beside its charging base. The main unit's battery and motor are meant to be fitted onto the dry cleaner when needed. While you can do it easily, it also feels reasonably balanced, and the weight is balanced enough to allow users to lift it and clean windows, spider webs, etc.

The build is reassuring in other places. The components and attachments use good-grade plastic, the black finish keeps the look restrained, and the locking mechanism for attachments is simple and secure.

Together, these details point to a machine built for durability rather than showiness.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Floor cleaning
On floors, the H12 vacuums and mops in one pass. Dreame claims 23,000Pa of suction, and the 180-degree lie-flat design with two rugged rollers and one ball at the body back lets the body slip under beds and sofas.

This pairing of strong suction and a flexible body is uncommon among lower-priced vacuum cleaners, and it is the strongest argument in the machine's favour.

The body drops to roughly 14cm when reclined, and the brush head needs about 9.6cm of clearance. This enabled me to clean tough-to-reach areas as well, which otherwise would have been impossible to reach and clean.

For what it is worth, the cleaning is spotless. Look at the image below. I spilt coffee, and it remains on the floor, and I added water to make it a mud-like scene.  The first image itself shows how precise the mopping and suction feature is. Once I was done, there's nice glaze to the floor and once the floor was dry -- it was literally spotless and the added cleaner helps add that gliding softness to the floor too.



Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach

But there's one thing to note is that to use the flex feature -- one needs to bend down and then push the cleaner for cleaning. Now, it is certainly difficult for people of older age, but imagine trying to clean a space that is 15cm or less clearance on your own. For me, that's far more difficult to clean than bending down and using the H12 Dual FlexReach to clean.

Despite weighing 5.4kg, the main unit was easy to manoeuvre with relatively little effort, and the roller glided over uneven sections of the floor. Several cleaning modes are on offer, though I stuck mostly to Smart and Turbo. Smart mode suits everyday use because it adjusts its behaviour to how dirty the floor is.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Water tanks
The fresh-water tank holds 780ml and the dirty-water tank holds 700ml, and I wished both were larger.

Cleaning around 1870 sq ft, along with sofa and ceiling-corner dry-vacuum tasks, meant emptying the dirty-water tank three to four times and refilling the fresh-water tank twice, which is not a deal breaker. Just something to keep in mind. But 1870 sq ft is a big area to clean, and the amount of water used is comparatively less than what my maid consumes to clean the floor manually.


The good thing is that the machine can stand on its own without wall support, and it also turns off automatically when standing. This enables easy access to water tanks.
Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Dry vacuum and attachments
Fitting the motor and battery module onto the dry vacuum lets it handle dry waste on floors and carpets.
The attachments are where the kit shines. One brush tackles pet and human hair, dander, dust and other fine particles on upholstery and mattresses, and I also used it to lift hair off clothes. A TangleCut Resilient Scraper cuts through hair and pushes it towards the suction path, while a light on top of the brush illuminates what is being cleaned under beds, sofas and in crevices.




A separate brush-and-vacuum attachment removes cobwebs from ceiling corners and can be extended for extra reach. Lighter manual brushes exist for these jobs, but having everything in one kit has its own appeal.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Maintenance
Upkeep is the least glamorous part of owning any vacuum cleaner, and the H12 is no exception. The tanks need emptying, and the brushes, filters and rollers need regular cleaning. To ease some of that burden, Dreame offers a self-cleaning cycle that uses water at up to 90°C, followed by heated-air drying. The idea is to reduce germ build-up and odours over time. Over weeks of use, it helps, and the automatic mop cleaning and drying keeps it ready for the next day.

And, if you are worried about the spare accessories, well, you can find them online. However, Dreame does include additional extras in the box. For example, the additional brush, filter and even the cleaning liquid are in the box.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Battery and charging

Dreame claims up to 60 minutes of runtime in dry vacuuming and up to 50 minutes in wet floor cleaning, depending on the mode.

I would treat these figures as a ceiling rather than a promise, since runtime shifts with the mode chosen.



For most moderate-sized homes, that should be enough, though larger homes may need charging breaks. A full charge takes about two hours. The battery is removable, so a spare pack can extend cleaning time, but that adds to the cost.

For me, I managed to mop the entire house in a single charge without worries.

Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach: Verdict

At Rs 44,999, the Dreame H12 Dual FlexReach makes a case for itself through versatility rather than simplicity. It can vacuum and mop floors, reach under low furniture and switch to dry cleaning for sofas, mattresses and ceiling corners.

The 180-degree design and useful attachments add genuine convenience, while the removable battery and self-cleaning system make everyday use easier. The weight, assembly process and need to switch attachments are compromises, but for buyers seeking one machine for multiple cleaning jobs, the H12 Dual FlexReach fits the bill pretty well.
