Articles 2 min read India Inc registered ECBs worth USD 8.13 Bn in Aug'26, fin services account for nearly USD 4 Bn: RBI Indian companies registered external commercial borrowings (ECBs) worth USD 8.13 billion in August 2026, with financial services accounting for nearly USD 4 billion of the total. Flipkart, Adani Airport Holdings, Power Finance Corporation, REC and Reliance Industries were among the major borrowers during the month. The data is based on applications for ECBs and foreign currency convertible bonds (FCCBs) that were allotted a loan registration number during the month.Financial services account for nearly USD 4 billionFinancial services companies accounted for nearly USD 4 billion of the ECB registrations during August, with a number of lenders raising funds mainly for on-lending or sub-lending. Advt Export-Import Bank of India registered three ECBs aggregating USD 1.31 billion, with the funds earmarked for on-lending or sub-lending. The lender registered ECBs of USD 150 million, USD 240 million and USD 760 million during the month.Power Finance Corporation registered an ECB of USD 775 million for general corporate purposes, while REC registered USD 500 million for general corporate purposes.Tata Capital registered USD 325 million for on-lending or sub-lending, while Piramal Finance registered nearly USD 500 million for the same purpose.Other financial-sector borrowers included India Infrastructure Finance Company, which registered two ECBs of USD 100 million each, MAS Financial Services with USD 250 million and Wizzmoni Financial Services with USD 207.5 million.Among non-financial companies, Flipkart Internet registered the largest ECB of the month at USD 1.14 billion. The three-year borrowing was for on-lending or sub-lending and was raised from a foreign equity holder.Adani Airport Holdings registered an ECB of USD 1 billion for modernisation and expansion of existing units, with a five-year maturity and a financial institution in the International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) as the lender.Reliance Industries registered an ECB of about USD 382.3 million for refinancing of existing ECBs, while Indianoil-Adani Gas registered about USD 157.1 million for refinancing of rupee loans. Advt Tejas Networks registered USD 50 million for refinancing of rupee loans, while Tata Electronics registered USD 190 million for refinancing of an existing ECB.The August data also includes InterGlobe Aviation, which registered a USD 180 million ECB under the special permission route for import of capital goods, with a leasing company as the lender.
