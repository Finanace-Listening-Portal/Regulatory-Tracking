The approval comes nearly three years after regulatory restrictions on Paytm Payments Bank disrupted its wallet services and forced the company to reassess its payments business. The latest development could pave the way for Paytm to re-enter the digital wallet space, subject to fulfilment of the conditions stipulated by the central bank.The approval allows the wholly owned subsidiary of Paytm parent One 97 Communications to set up a system for issuing and operating prepaid payment instruments, including digital wallets, subject to final authorisation, the company said in a stock exchange filing on Friday.PPSL must complete a systems audit, including a cybersecurity audit, and submit the report to the RBI (Reserve Bank of India) within six months, failing which the in-principle approval would lapse.The approval allows PPSL to issue and operate prepaid payment instruments, including digital wallets, subject to final authorisation, the company said in a stock exchange filing.Earlier in January 2024, the RBI barred PPBL from accepting fresh deposits and credits, including wallet top-ups, citing persistent compliance concerns. The restrictions, which took effect that March, disrupted several services and forced Paytm to shift its payments operations to other banking partners.RBI has subsequently cancelled PPBL's banking licence in April 2026, citing concerns over its operations and management.
