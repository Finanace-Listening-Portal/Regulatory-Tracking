Articles 1 min read Outward remittances from India rise ~1% month on month in July India's outward remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) increased around 1 per cent month on month to USD 2.6 billion in July 2026. Education's share of total outward remittances rose to 6 per cent from 4 per cent in June, while travel remained the largest component at 57 per cent. Remittances towards medical treatment, however, moderated during the month. Education had accounted for 4 per cent of total outward remittances during the April-June period.Travel continued to account for the largest share of India's outward remittances, with its contribution increasing to 57 per cent in July from 54 per cent in June. Travel had accounted for 53 per cent of remittances during the April-June period. Advt Family maintenance accounted for 11 per cent of total outward remittances in July, while gifts contributed 9 per cent. The share of family maintenance was unchanged from June, while gifts increased marginally from 8 per cent in the previous month.Remittances towards medical treatment moderated in July, with their share declining to 13 per cent from 18 per cent in June. The category had accounted for 15 per cent of outward remittances during the April-June period.On an annual basis, India's outward remittances have remained broadly stable after moderating from FY24 levels. Total remittances stood at USD 32 billion in FY24 and USD 29 billion in both FY25 and FY26.The composition of remittances has also evolved over the period. Travel accounted for 54 per cent of total remittances in FY24, rising to 57 per cent in FY25 and remaining at 57 per cent in FY26.
