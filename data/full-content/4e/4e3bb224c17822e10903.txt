The physical card is therefore not required for the supported transactions, although the underlying card account and Visa network remain the payment infrastructure.PhonePe had launched Visa device tokenisation in February 2025, allowing users to save cards for online transactions. The latest partnership extends the use of tokenised cards to contactless payments at physical stores and Visa-supported QR payments overseas.How Tap to Pay works Advt Under Tap to Pay, Android users can tokenise an eligible Visa card on the PhonePe app and use their smartphone to make contactless payments at compatible POS terminals.The phone uses NFC to communicate with the terminal, while the payment is processed against the tokenised Visa card. PhonePe said the service will roll out in phases from September 9 and is designed to work even in low-connectivity environments.This means the consumer does not need to carry the physical Visa card for such transactions. The card itself, however, is not being replaced as the underlying payment instrument; its credentials are represented by a token.How overseas QR payments will workThe second component, Cross Border Scan to Pay, is aimed at Indian travellers.PhonePe users will be able to scan Visa-supported QR codes at merchants across 14 international markets, including Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Japan, China and South Korea. The feature will be rolled out to users soon.Importantly, this is a Visa card transaction initiated through a QR code, not a UPI transaction. The user's tokenised Visa card is the underlying payment instrument, while Visa provides the international payment network.For consumers, the process is intended to provide a familiar scan-and-pay experience while travelling abroad, without requiring them to physically carry the card.PhonePe's move comes as India's payment ecosystem becomes increasingly international. UPI is currently live in 11 countries, while the government is pushing for greater integration with overseas payment systems. Advt How Smart Accept worksThe third product, Smart Accept, targets small merchants.It will allow eligible merchants to accept card payments directly on their smartphones, removing the need for a separate card POS machine.A customer can tap a physical contactless card or a tokenised mobile wallet against the merchant's compatible smartphone. The merchant's phone effectively becomes the card-acceptance device.PhonePe said Smart Accept will be integrated into its existing QR dashboard, allowing merchants to manage card payments alongside their other payment activity, with unified reconciliation and simplified dispute resolution.This could lower the cost of accepting card payments for smaller merchants who may not otherwise find a dedicated POS terminal economical. Advt What changes for PhonePe and Visa?For consumers, the partnership makes the physical Visa card less necessary at the point of payment, while retaining the card account and Visa network underneath the transaction. For merchants, it potentially turns a smartphone into a card-acceptance terminal.For PhonePe, the partnership expands its payments business beyond its UPI-led model into card-based payments and international transactions. For Visa, it provides access to PhonePe's large consumer and merchant ecosystem. PhonePe said it had more than 71.5 crore registered users and over 5 crore merchants as of July 2026.The broader significance is that the distinction between a card payment and an app-based payment is becoming less visible to consumers. The card remains the underlying payment instrument, but the smartphone increasingly becomes the interface through which the transaction is initiated.
