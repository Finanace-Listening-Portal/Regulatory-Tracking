} } Listen to This ArticleThe government and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) should consider curbs on non-essential remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) and closer scrutiny of overseas direct investment (ODI) outflows to ease pressure on the rupee, State Bank of India (SBI) said in a report on Thursday. Defending the rupee would, therefore, require addressing vulnerabilities in both the current and capital accounts, it said. The report also called for government measures on taxation and external commercial borrowings (ECBs) to deepen domestic borrowing avenues and support capital flows. The report further said that the rupee could retain an appreciating bias following recent measures by the RBI to stabilise market conditions, although global uncertainties remained significant. Non-essential foreign exchange outflows warranted immediate attention, with the report pointing to a sharp rise in overseas travel expenditure compared with spending on studies abroad. General-purpose travel expenditure stood at $665.8 million in July, against $148.5 million for studies abroad. The ratio had exceeded five times in June. Between March and July, expenditure on studies abroad stood at $2.47 billion, compared with $6.36 billion on travel. Outflows under the gifts category amounted to around $1.2 billion during the period, against $2.6 billion in the whole of 2025-26, the report said.
