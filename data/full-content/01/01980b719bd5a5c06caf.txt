Key developments included changes at Fino Payments Bank, Canara HSBC Life Insurance and Avendus Finance, covering senior roles across business and financial operations.Avendus appoints Shuja Siddiqui as CEO of its NBFC armAvendus has appointed Shuja Siddiqui as the Chief Executive Officer of Avendus Finance Pvt Ltd, its non-banking financial services unit. Nilesh Dhedhi, who previously held the position of MD and CEO, will now head the group's private credit business. Siddiqui brings over two decades of experience across wealth management, credit and capital markets, and previously served as Managing Director and Senior Advisor at Julius Baer Capital. Advt Fino Payments Bank gets RBI approval to appoint Pratap Singh as MD & CEOFino Payments Bank has received Reserve Bank of India's approval to appoint Pratap Singh as its Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer for a three-year term. His tenure will begin on November 27, 2026, or the date he takes charge, whichever is earlier. Singh brings over 29 years of banking experience across India and the UK, having worked with ICICI Bank India, ICICI Bank UK and SBI UK.Canara HSBC Life Insurance approves Indu Swaroop's appointment as CFOCanara HSBC Life Insurance has approved the appointment of Indu Swaroop as its Chief Financial Officer. The leadership change comes at a time when the insurance sector continues to operate within an evolving regulatory environment. The available information identifies the appointment but does not provide further details about Swaroop's professional background or the effective date.
