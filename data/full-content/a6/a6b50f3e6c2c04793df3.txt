Summary: The Memorandum of Association (MOA) is a foundational constitutional document of a company that establishes its identity, objects, registered-office jurisdiction, liability structure, capital provisions and subscription particulars. Often described as the charter of a company, the MOA defines the essential framework within which the company is incorporated and conducts its activities. This article examines the importance of the MOA under the Companies Act, 2013, particularly Section 4, and explains the name, registered-office, object, liability, capital and subscriber clauses. It also discusses major principles of corporate jurisprudence, including separate legal personality, lifting of the corporate veil, ultra vires, indoor management and constructive notice. These doctrines help explain the relationship between a company, its members, management and persons dealing with it. The article further examines the process of altering the MOA through corporate approvals, general meetings, resolutions and filings with the Registrar of Companies. Particular attention is given to changes in company name, registered office, objects and share capital, including forms such as MGT-14, INC-24, INC-22 and related filings. As different alterations involve distinct statutory requirements, approvals and procedures, companies must examine the applicable provisions before undertaking amendments. A properly drafted and maintained MOA supports legal compliance, corporate transparency and informed decision-making by investors and other stakeholders. Procedure for Alteration of Memorandum of Association As we previously described about the alteration of MOA so it can be performed with these valid steps: Step 01: Proposal and Drafting Step 01: in the first step proposal and drafting of the new MOA for the company for changing and enhancing the business operations with new expansions, ideas, propose the drafted MOA before the board of the company. Step 02: Board Approval and EGM Step 02: after reviewing of the new document of MOA with proposed models, if the majority board member assented for it then convey the EGM (EXTRAORDINARY GENERAL METTING). Step 03: Notice of EGM Step 03: before the EGM pass the notice to other key management person before 21 days. Such as auditor, company secretary, chartered accountant, legal practitioner. Step 04: General Meeting and Approval Step 04: in the EGM, the board member will review the proposal and amendment made in the drafted MOA properly and after successfully passing from the review process then all the member as well as the practitioner made their hand seal and sign on it. Step 05: Filing with Registrar of Companies Step 05: in the final step, pass the proposal before the ROC (MGT 14). Different Types of Alteration of MOA Alteration of Name Clause Alteration of name clause: For the alteration of the name clause of the company the company must file the special resolution document of the company along with the (INC 24) before ROC. After review process it generally takes 2-3 days and after that the business gets a new name and it’s the duty to reconcile with the revised name in the other elementary documents by changing the new name from old one. Section 13(2) talks about it. Alteration of the Address Alteration of the Address: This is the process where a company changes its legal address where it was continuing its operation there are certain requirements for the different procedure. For changing the location of the company, the company must have to file the (MGT 14) and for changing the document of address from another location of the same city the company must have to file the (MGT 14) along with the (INC22). For changing the location from one jurisdiction of ROC to another the company must have to file (MGT 14) along with the (INC22) form. In the next steps the company file (INC 23), (INC 28), (INC 22) subsequently. Depends upon Registrar of director order. For changing the business address from one state to another state the company must have to file (MGT 14) along with (GNL2), subsequently it must have to file (INC26) for the advertisement and (INC22). Alteration of Objects Alteration of objects: This process can be done by the company by making the proposed (MGT 14) documentation before the ROC Alteration of Shareholdings Alteration of shareholdings: This is the process where the company changes the 1.
