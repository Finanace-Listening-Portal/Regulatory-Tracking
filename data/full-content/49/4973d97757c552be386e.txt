Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Sep 21, 2026 at 08:22 PM IST var news_msid = 134392310; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Reserve Bank of India’s latest data released on Monday has put total forex inflows under its special USD-INR forex swap facility at USD 143.596 billion, with the final and reconciled FCNR(B) mobilisation at USD 132.980 billion.The latest figures are higher than the provisional numbers released by the central bank on September 2. At that time, RBI had reported total inflows of USD 136.377 billion, including USD 127.226 billion through FCNR(B) deposits, USD 5.260 billion through OFCBs and USD 3.891 billion through ECBs.RBI had then said the figures were provisional and pending final reporting, accounting and reconciliation. Advt The latest data shows FCNR(B) inflows have been revised upwards by USD 5.754 billion to USD 132.980 billion. OFCB inflows have increased by USD 60 million to USD 5.320 billion, while ECB inflows have risen by USD 1.405 billion to USD 5.296 billion.Overall, the reported inflows are now USD 7.219 billion higher than the provisional August 31 figure.Data reported by RBI in July had shown total inflows of about USD 20.718 billion as of July 17, comprising USD 17.406 billion through FCNR(B), USD 1.970 billion through OFCBs and USD 1.342 billion through ECBs. RBI data reported in August showed total inflows at around USD 56.85 billion, with FCNR(B) deposits accounting for about USD 52.3 billion.The government had also told Parliament that inflows under the facility had reached about USD 60.55 billion as of July 30, compared with USD 32.56 billion on June 5.The facility was introduced by RBI in June to facilitate foreign-currency inflows through FCNR(B) deposits, ECBs and OFCBs. RBI subsequently extended the FCNR(B) mobilisation window to August 31, while keeping the ECB and OFCB windows open until December 31.
