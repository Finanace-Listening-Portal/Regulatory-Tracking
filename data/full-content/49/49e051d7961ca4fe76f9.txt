} } Listen to This ArticleTruck rentals on major trunk routes held firm above year-ago levels due to steady freight activity, according to the Shriram Mobility Bulletin released on Tuesday, reflecting resilient economic momentum. Bengaluru–Mumbai–Bengaluru led with a month-on-month rise of 1.3 per cent. Delhi–Mumbai–Delhi followed, with a rise of 1.1 per cent and Delhi–Hyderabad–Delhi at 1 per cent. Rentals on Delhi–Bengaluru–Delhi and Bengaluru–Kolkata–Bengaluru nudged up, while the remaining routes remained unchanged. Rentals grew across all key routes tracked year-on-year (Y-o-Y). Delhi–Kolkata–Delhi grew 13 per cent, followed by Bengaluru–Mumbai–Bengaluru at 11 per cent and Kolkata–Guwahati–Kolkata at 10 per cent. Rentals on the remaining routes were higher by 4 to 9 per cent. Vehicle sales grew sequentially in September, led by core sector segments. Construction equipment sales rose by 31 per cent, followed by earth-moving equipment at 17 per cent and goods carriers at 13 per cent, while motorcycles and scooters and motor cars each grew by 4 per cent. Agricultural tractor sales declined by 15 per cent and commercial tractors by 22 per cent. Sales rose across almost all categories Y-o-Y. Maxi cabs led with a 75 per cent increase, followed by three-wheeler goods vehicles at 63 per cent. Goods carrier sales increased by 40 per cent, reflecting robust demand for heavy-duty trucks. Motorcycles/scooters grew by 33 per cent, motor cars by 31 per cent, construction equipment by 25 per cent and earth-moving equipment by 19 per cent.
