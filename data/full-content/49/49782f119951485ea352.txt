Issued under paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, and in continuation of Public Notice No. 19/2026-27 dated 9 July 2026, the notice specifies the application period from 6 November 2026 to 21 November 2026. The quota covers specified completely built unit (CBU) vehicles that have not been registered anywhere before importation. For passenger vehicles, the TRQ quantity is 6,000 units for engine capacities not exceeding 1,500 cc, 6,000 units for the specified intermediate engine-capacity category and 12,500 units for higher-capacity petrol and diesel vehicles. A further 2,750 units are allocated for motor vehicles transporting goods, excluding electric and hydrogen-fuel vehicles, imported as CBUs. The total notified quota is 27,250 vehicles. Import procedures will be governed by Annexure VII of Appendix 2A under the Foreign Trade Policy framework, as notified through Public Notice No. 19/2026-27. The notice opens the application window for eligible importers seeking TRQ allocation for CY 2027. Government of India Ministry of Commerce & Industry Department of Commerce Directorate General of Foreign Trade Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi Public Notice No. 39/2026-27-DGFT | Dated: 06th November, 2026 Subject: Inviting TRQ Applications under India-United Kingdom Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) for Calendar Year (CY) 2027 – reg.
