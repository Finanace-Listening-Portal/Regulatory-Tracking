Sequentially, it declined from $5.77 billion in July, according to the data from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Outbound FDI, expressed as a financial commitment, has three components: Equity, loans, and guarantees. Outbound equity FDI commitment surged to $1.14 billion in August from $1.06 billion a year ago. It was lower than $2.06 billion in July 2026. At the same time, debt (loans) dropped to $464 million in July from $845 million in the same month of 2025. It was also lower than $878.9 million recorded in March 2026. Guarantees for overseas units rose to $2.23 billion in August from $1.2 billion a year ago but were lower than $2.8 billion in July 2026.
