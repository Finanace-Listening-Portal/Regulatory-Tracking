Articles 1 min read PNB inks pact with Deutsche Bank AG to explore collaboration in acquisition financing PTI Published On Nov 25, 2026 at 05:53 PM IST var news_msid = 136161457; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook New Delhi, Punjab National Bank (PNB) on Wednesday said it has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Deutsche Bank AG to establish a framework for cooperation in facilitating financing for share acquisitions by clients in India.Under the proposed arrangement, the two banks will explore opportunities to collaborate on acquisition financing transactions through various modes, including underwriting, arrangement, participation, syndication, referral, and facilitation, based on clients' specific requirements.The collaboration aims to leverage the respective capabilities of both institutions to facilitate financing solutions for clients undertaking share acquisition transactions. Advt Speaking on the new partnership, PNB MD & CEO Ashok Chandra said the collaboration with Deutsche Bank marks an important step towards exploring new opportunities in acquisition financing."By leveraging our respective strengths and capabilities, we aim to facilitate tailored financing solutions for clients while strengthening our presence in the corporate banking ecosystem," Chandra said.Kaushik Shaparia, CEO, Deutsche Bank Group, India & Emerging Asia, said, "We see considerable opportunity to support Indian companies as they grow in scale, pursue their strategic ambitions and deepen their connections to global markets.""Our collaboration with PNB brings together complementary capabilities and creates a framework to explore financing solutions for clients pursuing growth opportunities, while preserving the independent credit, risk and governance standards of both institutions," Shaparia added.
