Articles 2 min read Singapore's Temasek to invest Rs 2,500 crore in Godrej Capital at Rs 28,000 crore valuation Temasek Holdings will invest Rs 2,500 crore in Godrej Capital, valuing the financial services arm of Godrej Industries Group at up to Rs 28,000 crore, as the group looks to scale its lending business to Rs 1 lakh crore in assets under management by 2031. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 12, 2026 at 03:14 PM IST var news_msid = 135763895; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Godrej Industries Group on Thursday announced that Singapore-based Temasek Holdings will invest Rs 2,500 crore in Godrej Capital, its financial services arm, at a valuation of up to Rs 28,000 crore.The investment will be made through Temasek's affiliate Jongsong Investments Pte Ltd. Following the transaction, the Godrej Industries Group will retain approximately 80% ownership in the company. The transaction is subject to requisite regulatory approvals.Founded in 2020, Godrej Capital has built a lending business spanning home loans, loans against property, working capital loans, construction finance, gold loans, supply chain finance and personal loans. Advt The company had assets under management (AUM) of Rs 35,000 crore as of October 31, 2026, and has a presence in more than 100 cities across India.The investment comes as Godrej Industries Group looks to make financial services a key long-term growth area. The group has set an AUM target of Rs 1 lakh crore for Godrej Capital by 2031."We are delighted to welcome Temasek as a long-term investor in Godrej Capital. This investment is a testament to the exceptional business Manish and his team have built in a remarkably short period, and reflects confidence in the significant opportunity ahead," Pirojsha Godrej, Chairperson, Godrej Industries Group, said.He said the group remains committed to building Godrej Capital into a Rs 1 lakh crore AUM franchise by 2031 through disciplined execution and strong governance.Manish Shah, MD & CEO of Godrej Capital, said the investment would help the company accelerate growth, expand its reach across India, deepen its offerings for businesses and individuals, and strengthen its technology-led capabilities.Earlier this week, US-based alternative asset manager Blackstone agreed to invest Rs 5,000 crore in IIFL Finance for an approximately 16 per cent stake, as part of a larger Rs 6,414 crore primary capital raise by the NBFC. Advt The fundraise also includes Rs 1,264 crore from IIFL Finance's promoters Nirmal Jain and R Venkataraman, and Rs 150 crore from Anchorage Capital. The investment is expected to strengthen IIFL Finance's capital adequacy ratio from around 17 per cent to approximately 30 per cent on a pro forma basis.
