National Stock Exchange of India Circular Department: Listing Compliance Circular Ref. No: NSE/CML/2022/09 Date: February 17, 2022 To, All Listed Entities, Dear Sir/Madam, Subject: Release of New Digital Portal for filing of Information with the Exchange – for Equity Announcements & Disclosures under SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 This is in continuation to Exchange Circular no NSE/CML/2022/03 dated January 06, 2022 informing about the launch of Digital Portal for filings done with the Exchange.
