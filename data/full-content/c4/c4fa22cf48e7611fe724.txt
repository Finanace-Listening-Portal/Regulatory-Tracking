Articles 2 min read Non-life insurers' premiums rise 10.5% to Rs 32,761 crore in October; health insurers grow 21.3% Standalone health insurers continue to outperform general insurers, while the industry's cumulative gross direct premiums rise 9.13% to Rs 2.12 lakh crore during April-October FY27, according to General Insurance Council data. Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Nov 18, 2026 at 10:03 AM IST var news_msid = 135949036; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Gross direct premiums underwritten by non-life insurers rose 10.52% year-on-year to Rs 32,760.81 crore in October 2026, compared with Rs 29,641.25 crore in the corresponding month last year, according to provisional data released by the General Insurance Council (GIC).Premiums of general insurers grew 10.15% year-on-year to Rs 28,062.14 crore during the month, while standalone health insurers reported a stronger growth of 21.29% to Rs 4,548.55 crore. Specialised insurers, however, recorded a 63.84% decline in premiums to Rs 150.12 crore.Among general insurers, HDFC Ergo General Insurance reported a 34.87% increase in premiums to Rs 1,661.84 crore in October. SBI General Insurance recorded 31.98% growth to Rs 1,550.04 crore, while Bajaj General Insurance reported a 12.14% increase to Rs 2,142.02 crore. Tata AIG General Insurance's premiums rose 15.20% to Rs 2,264.64 crore. Advt Among public sector general insurers, National Insurance Company's premiums increased 16.11% to Rs 3,287.11 crore, while New India Assurance reported 2.94% growth to Rs 3,879.72 crore. United India Insurance recorded an 11.55% increase to Rs 1,774.78 crore, while Oriental Insurance reported a 2.64% decline to Rs 1,479.02 crore.Health insurers outperform general insurersStandalone health insurers continued to outperform the broader non-life insurance industry, with premiums growing 21.29% year-on-year to Rs 4,548.55 crore in October.Among the standalone health insurers, Star Health and Allied Insurance reported a 17.65% increase in premiums to Rs 1,721.49 crore, while Niva Bupa Health Insurance recorded 20.05% growth to Rs 825.66 crore. Care Health Insurance's premiums increased 34.56% to Rs 1,128.77 crore, while Aditya Birla Health Insurance reported 10.05% growth to Rs 590.92 crore.Industry grows 9.13% in April-OctoberDuring April-October FY27, gross direct premiums underwritten by the non-life insurance industry rose 9.13% year-on-year to Rs 2.12 lakh crore, compared with Rs 1.95 lakh crore in the corresponding period last year.General insurers reported 9.23% growth in cumulative premiums to Rs 1.79 lakh crore, while standalone health insurers recorded a stronger 29.18% increase to Rs 30,134.86 crore. Specialised insurers, meanwhile, saw premiums decline 53.10% to Rs 3,655.06 crore.Among standalone health insurers, Care Health Insurance recorded 40.99% growth in cumulative premiums to Rs 7,321.49 crore, followed by ManipalCigna Health Insurance at 33.34% to Rs 1,570.11 crore and Aditya Birla Health Insurance at 31.84% to Rs 4,293.58 crore.
