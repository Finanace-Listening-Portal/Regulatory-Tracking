Summary: Renewal of registration of charitable and religious trusts or institutions under Sections 12AB and 80G of the Income-tax Act, 1961 involves submission of Form 10AB and supporting documents relating to the trust’s constitution, existing registration, charitable activities, financial records and other statutory compliances. The documentation commonly sought includes the trust deed, Charity Commissioner registration certificate, earlier registration orders, evidence of activities undertaken during the preceding three years, photographs, audit reports, Forms 10B or 10BB, bank statements, cash books, ledgers, details of donations received and given, and applicable FCRA, NGO Darpan and institutional approvals. Trusts may also face practical difficulties where trust deeds are prepared in regional languages but English translations are requested by the Income Tax Department. The article highlights the extensive documentation involved in renewal proceedings and questions the necessity of repeatedly furnishing information already available with the department, particularly for institutions holding existing registrations. Introduction We know that from assessment year 2021-22 that financial year 2020-21 and onwards, when a trust or institution is registered under Section 12A or Section 12AA has been given a time limit of 5 years or 3 years, after that they have to make an application for renewal of registration. The same formality is applicable for registration under Section 80G of Income-tax Act, 1961. Copy of Trust Deed with English translation. Here the difficulty is that normally Trust Deed is prepared in local language of the state. For example in Gujarat when a Trust is to be registered with Charity Commissioner’s office they demand that Trust Deed must be in Gujarati, while the same trust is to be registered with Income Tax, they demand that Trust Deed must be in English Language. In short the Trust Deed should be drafted in both languages. Copy of certificate, registered with Charity Commissioner. If an application u/s 12A or 80(G) was rejected before this application, copy of rejection order is to be attached. Copy of Existing 12A or 80(G) certificate. Trust Activities and Premises During previous three years, the trust have made any activity, proof of that activity with photograph. It should be for the objects of the trust. Along with proof, it should be explained with note, regarding activity. Details of Premises and Institutions Where this type of activities are done and it should be in the trust premises. Financial Books and Statements Over and above following proofs are also attached. Last three years audit reports. Form 10B or 10BB for last three years. Copy of Bank Statements for all the three years as well as current year. Copy of Bank Books and Cash Books. Details or the ledgers copy of the activities are made during last three years, with the amount of expenditure incurred for the objects of the trust. Charitable and religious expenditure ledgers. Details of Donations Received and Given During last three years top 5 donations received by the trust in each year, along with name, address and PAN of the donors. Copy of receipts issued to the donors. During last three years top 5 donations given to another trusts in each year, along with name, address, purpose and PAN of the donee, along with receipts issued by the donee. During last three years any donation received by the trust in cash, details of that donations with name, amount, address and purpose of the donor is to be furnished. Other Government Registrations If the donation is received by FCRA, the copy of FCRA certificate, validity period is to be taken from the donor. When any donation is received from a trust, Darpan Registration certificate, NITI Aayog registration. If the donation is received from School, Hospital, their local authorities licenses. If donation is received from an Educational Institution RTE Compliance statement is to be obtained. Certification of Documents by Trustees The details mentioned above are to be signed by the trustee as true copy. Why Are Extensive Documents Required Again for Renewal? From the above particulars, we do not understand why the department is asking for the details which are already with them. If any trust requires first-time registration, then it is understandable, but for renewal why are they insisting on such details?
