The committee, in its first two meetings, discussed key areas to be examined under its Terms of Reference and identified stakeholder groups for consultation, including banks, non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), development finance institutions (DFIs), regulators, industry associations and fintech organisations. To facilitate a structured consultative process, the committee has circulated questionnaires to various stakeholder groups, inviting their views and suggestions on key areas of banking sector reforms.
