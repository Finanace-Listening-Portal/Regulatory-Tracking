The company reported a significant increase in booking value for H1FY27, reaching Rs. 10,199.57 crores, a notable rise from Rs. 2,937.73 crores in the same period last year. The consolidated revenue for H1FY27 stood at Rs. 3,098.06 crores, up from Rs. 2,918.82 crores recorded in H1FY26. The second quarter of FY27 also saw an increase in revenue, amounting to Rs. 1,736.37 crores compared to Rs. 1,361.69 crores in the preceding quarter. The company’s earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA) for H1FY27 reached Rs. 1,879.53 crores, with Q2FY27 contributing Rs. 1,084.59 crores to this figure. Profit After Tax (PAT) for H1FY27 was reported at Rs. 1,303.72 crores, marking an increase from Rs. 1,180.46 crores in H1FY26. The PAT for Q2FY27 was Rs. 759.01 crores, up from Rs. 544.71 crores in Q1FY27. Vikas Oberoi, Chairman and Managing Director of Oberoi Realty, attributed the company’s performance to sustained demand for real estate in India, driven by a preference for quality and brand credibility. He highlighted the company’s strategic focus on executing existing developments while exploring growth opportunities aligned with its long-term vision. Oberoi Realty’s recent entry into the National Capital Region (NCR) with the Three Sixty North project was noted as a significant milestone. The company continues to see strong demand in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), with expectations of positive trends continuing into the festive season. The company has also been recognised with several awards, including accolades for its hospitality and retail ventures, which underscore its reputation in the industry. Analysts suggest that Oberoi Realty’s diversified portfolio and strategic market positioning could continue to drive its growth trajectory in the coming quarters.
