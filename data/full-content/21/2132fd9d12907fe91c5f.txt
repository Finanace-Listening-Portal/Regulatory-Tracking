While the company expects gross yields on its gold loan products to remain in the 17.5-18.5% range, elevated competition could limit its ability to raise lending rates to offset higher funding costs.The expected increase in borrowing costs reflects anticipated movements in market interest rates and is broadly in line with the guidance given in the previous quarter. Management has reiterated its strategy of offering attractive lending rates to retain existing borrowers and acquire new customers amid intense competition in the gold loan market, an Axis Securities report said. Advt The pressure on margins comes even as the gold loan business remains the principal growth driver. Gold loan AUM rose 92% year on year and 6% sequentially in the September quarter, taking its share of consolidated AUM to 83%, against 69% a year earlier. Management reiterated its FY27 gold loan AUM growth guidance of 25-30%, with an expectation of achieving around 30%.Higher loan yieldsHigher gold loan yields and a marginal decline in the cost of funds helped improve margins during the quarter. Calculated net interest margins, measured as a percentage of AUM, rose 63 basis points sequentially to 11% from 10.3%. Gold loan yields increased 20 basis points sequentially, while the cost of funds edged lower.However, the brokerage expects higher borrowing costs to weigh on margins over the medium term. It builds in a 15-20 basis point compression in net interest margins over FY28-29, although stable gold yields and a higher share of gold loans are expected to cushion some of the pressure. It has raised its net interest income estimates by 3-4% over FY27-29 on expectations of stronger asset growth.Operating efficiency is expected to provide some support to profitability. Digital gold loans account for around 91% of the gold loan book, allowing the existing branch network to support incremental business without a proportionate increase in employee costs. Manappuram plans to add 500 branches in FY27, having opened 100 in the first half, while work on another 250 branches is under way. Advt The brokerage expects the company to deliver a return on assets of 3.1-3.2% and return on equity of 14-16% over FY27-29. It has valued the stock at 1.7 times its estimated adjusted book value for September 2028 and reduced its target price to Rs 400 from Rs 415 a share. It maintained its Buy recommendation.
