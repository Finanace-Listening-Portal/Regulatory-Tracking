Net interest margin stood at 3.16% on total assets and 3.32% on interest-earning assets.The lender’s asset quality remained stable in the September quarter as gross non-performing assets (GNPA) at Rs 35,810 crore, marginally lower than Rs 35,846 crore in June. Net non-performing assets (NNPA) declined to Rs 11,851 crore from Rs 12,357 crore in the previous quarter, the bank said. Advt The gross NPA ratio eased to 1.11% from 1.17% in the June quarter, while the net NPA ratio fell to 0.37% from 0.41%.
