RBI/2026-27/306. Effective 1 April 2027, the amendments replace paragraphs 26 and 27 of the RBI’s 2025 NBFC securitisation directions and insert paragraph 27A. Under revised paragraph 26, securitisation notes must be issued, held and subsequently transferred in dematerialised form. The minimum ticket size is ₹1 crore at issuance and on subsequent transfers. Ticket size means the amount invested by a single investor. The originator must ensure that its agreement with the special purpose entity (SPE) requires the SPE to make the arrangements necessary to enforce the minimum ticket size with the registrar, depository, stock exchange or other entities, as applicable. The proviso permits securitisation notes with amortisation structures to trade at their amortised value if the ticket size falls below ₹1 crore. Revised paragraph 27 requires public offers of securitisation notes to comply with Regulation 21 of the SEBI (Issue and Listing of Securitised Debt Instruments and Security Receipts) Regulations, 2008, as amended from time to time. New paragraph 27A requires transactions in notes issued before 1 April 2027 to comply with paragraphs 26 and 27 as well. *** Reserve Bank of India RBI/2026-27/306 DOR.STR.REC.258/21-04-177/2026-27 |Dated: November 23, 2026 Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026 Please refer to Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025. 2. The Amendment Directions modify the Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025 as under: (1) Para 26 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “26. The issuance, holding and subsequent transfer of securitisation notes under these Directions shall only be in a dematerialised form. The minimum ticket size shall be ₹1 Crore at the time of issuance as well as at any subsequent transfers. The originator shall ensure that the agreement with the SPE requires the latter to put in place necessary arrangements with entities such as the registrar, depository, stock exchange etc., as may be applicable, to ensure compliance with the minimum ticket-size requirement. Provided that securitisation notes with amortisation structures shall be permitted to be traded at amortised value, if the ticket size falls below ₹1 crore. Explanation: Ticket size, for the purpose of these Directions, refers to the size of investment by a single investor.” (2) Para 27 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “27.
