Tata Trusts (led by Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and Sir Ratan Tata Trust) are the largest shareholder of Tata Sons with a 66 per cent stake. On meeting the minimum public shareholding (MPS) threshold, experts believe an offer for sale (OFS) route involving existing shareholders — rather than a fresh dilution — is the likely path.
