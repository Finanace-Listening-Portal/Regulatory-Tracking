ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Updated On Oct 1, 2026 at 06:36 PM IST var news_msid = 134620138; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Largest private sector lender HDFC Bank on Thursday said that the Reserve Bank of India has approved the appointment of Anup Bagchi as Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer of HDFC Bank for a period of three years, effective October 27, 2026.Bagchi will take over from Sashidhar Jagdishan, who will complete his tenure as HDFC Bank’s MD & CEO at the close of business hours on October 26, 2026. Bagchi’s three-year term will run until October 26, 2029.Anup Bagchi has over three decades of experience across banking, capital markets, wealth management and insurance. He has been part of the ICICI Group since 1992 and has held leadership roles across treasury, retail banking, wholesale banking, investment banking and digital financial services. Advt He served as Executive Director of ICICI Bank from 2017 to 2023, where he oversaw retail, business and rural banking and later wholesale banking. He was also CEO & MD of ICICI Securities and has served as Chairman of ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company.Since June 2023, Bagchi has been the MD & CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance. Under his leadership, the insurer crossed Rs 10,000 crore in annualised premium equivalent for the first time in FY2025, with APE rising 15% year-on-year to Rs 10,407 crore and profit after tax increasing nearly 40% to Rs 1,189 crore.Bagchi holds a management degree from the Indian Institute of Management, Bangalore and an engineering degree from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur.The appointment comes after a series of leadership changes at HDFC Bank this year. Part-time chairman Atanu Chakraborty resigned from the bank on March 18, 2026, before former Finance Secretary and former Chief Election Commissioner Rajiv Kumar was appointed as the lender’s new part-time chairman in June.Jagdishan, whose current term ends on October 26, had initially indicated his willingness to seek another term. However, in August, HDFC Bank said that despite the board’s persuasion, Jagdishan reiterated his decision not to seek reappointment as MD & CEO. The board subsequently decided to fast-track the process for selecting his successor.
