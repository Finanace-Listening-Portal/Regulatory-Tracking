Complimentary insurance covers to end from December 15ICICI Bank will discontinue several complimentary insurance benefits linked to selected credit cards from December 15, 2026. The changes cover three categories of insurance.Air accident and related insurance cover: This benefit will end on the Emeralde Private Metal, Times Black, Emeralde, Emeralde Private, Diamant, Sapphiro and Rubyx credit cards. Cancellation for Any Reason (CFAR) cover: This protection will be discontinued on the Emeralde Private Metal, Times Black, Emeralde and Emeralde Private cards. Personal and air accident insurance: Holders of the Parakram and Parakram Select credit cards will lose this complimentary cover. These benefits are particularly relevant for customers who rely on their credit cards for additional travel and accident protection. Cardholders should review their existing insurance arrangements and check whether they need separate policies. They should also examine the coverage, exclusions and claim conditions of any alternative insurance before purchasing it. INOX movie benefit to be discontinuedThe bank will also withdraw its complimentary INOX benefit from December 15. The affected cards include Rubyx, Coral, Expressions, Chennai Super Kings, MakeMyTrip Platinum, MakeMyTrip Signature, MakeMyTrip, HPCL Super Saver and Emirates Rubyx credit cards. Customers who regularly use this benefit will need to consider other entertainment offers or pay for their movie tickets. International transaction charges to rise for business cardsFrom November 25, 2026, ICICI Bank will increase the Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) fee from 1 per cent to 3.50 per cent on selected business, corporate and purchase credit cards. DCC applies when customers make an overseas transaction in Indian rupees at an international location. It can also apply to payments in rupees made to merchants located in India but registered in a foreign country. The affected cards include Business Black Advantage, Business Blue Advantage, Travel Agent Card, Business Platinum and select corporate and purchase cards. The bank will also increase the replacement fee on eligible business, corporate and purchase cards from Rs 200 to Rs 300, excluding GST, from the same date. What should cardholders do?Customers should identify the exact variant of their ICICI Bank credit card and check the bank's revised terms. Those relying on complimentary travel insurance should assess whether their existing policies provide adequate protection. Business cardholders making international payments should also compare the applicable forex mark-up and DCC charges before choosing the payment currency. The changes do not apply uniformly across all ICICI Bank credit cards. Therefore, checking the individual card's revised fee structure and benefits is important before assuming that an existing facility will continue.
