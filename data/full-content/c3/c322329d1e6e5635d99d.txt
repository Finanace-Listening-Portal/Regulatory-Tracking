Articles 3 min read Union Bank sees over Rs 110 crore fee income impact from proposed IRDAI’s distribution norms: CEO Union Bank of India expects the proposed IRDAI distribution norms to have a Rs 100–110 crore impact on its fee income at current business levels, CEO Asheesh Pandey said, and that insurance and other commission income accounts for around 7.8-8.1 per cent of fee income. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Oct 19, 2026 at 05:58 PM IST var news_msid = 135048824; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Union Bank of India expects the proposed distribution norms by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) to have an impact of around Rs 100-110 crore on its fee income at current business levels, managing director and CEO Asheesh Pandey said on Monday.The bank has already worked out the potential impact even though the proposed norms are still at the draft stage, Pandey said, and that insurance and other commission income accounts for around 7.8–8.1 per cent of the bank’s total fee income.“If you see, around 7.8, 8, 8.1 per cent is my insurance and other commission income to the total of the fee income,” Pandey said during the bank’s Q2 FY27 media interaction. Advt “So certainly we have worked out and it comes around Rs 100–110 crore. Even if we take at the current level of business and the commission and everything, even then,” he said.The CEO, however, described the impact as relatively small for the bank and said Union Bank is increasingly focusing on building sustainable sources of core fee income.The bank’s FY26 annual report shows that income from bancassurance stood at Rs 475.45 crore, comprising Rs 396.72 crore from life insurance policies, Rs 38.09 crore from non-life insurance and Rs 40.64 crore from health insurance.Union Bank’s FY26 annual report separately reported Rs 2,726.43 crore as commission, exchange and brokerage income on a consolidated basis.Pandey said the proposed framework should not be viewed only through the lens of the commission income that banks earn from third-party products, as the distribution process also involves customer consent and preventing bundling of products.“It is not only about, you know, if we topple this sort of regulation from IRDAI to the bank, how you have to sell the third-party product. The entire circle is there, so that you ensure customer centricity, take care of the customer’s interest and consent, and ensure that you don’t bundle it,” he said.He said the proposed norms could also create an opportunity for banks to expand their third-party distribution business rather than focus solely on higher commissions. Advt “So I think you have a good opportunity to expand and get it more. Rather than, you know, only on the income commission, higher commission,” Pandey said.Pandey also said the bank is looking to build “perennial, sustainable streams” of fee-based income rather than depend on such commission income.How did Q2 FY27 fare for Union Bank of India?Union Bank of India reported a 30.31 per cent year-on-year increase in net profit to Rs 5,537 crore in Q2 FY27, while net interest income rose 20.09 per cent to Rs 10,583 crore. Operating profit increased 18.99 per cent year-on-year to Rs 8,108 crore. Advt Global advances grew 18.53 per cent year-on-year to Rs 11.56 lakh crore, while global deposits increased 6.87 per cent to Rs 13.19 lakh crore. Domestic advances grew 17.35 per cent to Rs 11.06 lakh crore, with MSME advances rising 22.39 per cent and retail advances 11.24 per cent.Asset quality also improved, with gross NPA falling 87 basis points year-on-year to 2.42 per cent and net NPA declining 13 basis points to 0.42 per cent. Net interest margin stood at 2.86 per cent, up 19 basis points year-on-year, while core RoA stood at 1.03 per cent.
