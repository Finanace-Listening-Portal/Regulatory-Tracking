Advt Expressed in dollar terms, foreign currency assets include effects of appreciation or depreciation of non-US units, such as the euro, pound, and yen, held in foreign exchange reserves.The value of gold reserves dropped sharply by USD 6.183 billion to USD 98.613 billion during the week, the RBI said.The special drawing rights were down USD 92 million to USD 18.517 billion, the central bank said.India's reserve position with the IMF was also down by USD 32 million to USD 4.758 billion, the RBI added.
