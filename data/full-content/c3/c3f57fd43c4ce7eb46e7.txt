No. 36 read with section 11 of the Income Tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025) in the case of Telangana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board- Notification No. 150 /2026-IT Summary: The Central Government, through CBDT Notification No. 150/2026 dated 20 November 2026 and published as S.O. 6324(E), has notified the Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (PAN AAEAT9368D) for a conditional income-tax exemption under serial number 36 of the table in Schedule III, read with section 11 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Board was constituted by the State Government of Telangana. The exemption covers three categories of income arising to the Board: cess received, registration and renewal fees received or collected from building and other construction workers, and interest received on bank deposits. It applies for tax years 2026-27 and 2027-28. To qualify, the Board must refrain from commercial activity, file its return of income in accordance with section 263(9)(c)(xiii), and ensure that both its activities and the nature of the specified income remain unchanged throughout the two tax years. The notification treats these requirements as conditions of the exemption. Paragraph 3 provides that failure to comply with the conditions in paragraph 2 will result in withdrawal of the exemption under Schedule III, read with section 11, and initiation of proceedings under the Income-tax Act, 2025. The exemption applies to the three specified receipts of the Board for the two tax years stated in the notification. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) (CENTRAL BOARD OF DIRECT TAXES) Notification No. 150/2026-Income Tax | Dated: 20th November, 2026 S.O. 6324(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Schedule III [Table: Sl. No. 36] read with section 11 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby notifies for the purposes of the said clause, ‘Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board’ (PAN AAEAT9368D), a Board constituted by the State Government of Telangana, in respect of the following specified income arising to the said Board, namely:- (a) cess received; (b) registration & Renewal fee received/collected from the Building and other Construction Workers; and (c) interest received on bank deposits. 2. This notification shall be effective subject to the conditions that ‘Telangana Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board’ (PAN AAEAT9368D):- (a) shall not engage in any commercial activity; (b) shall file return of income in accordance with the provision of 263(9)(c)(xiii) of the said Act, 2025; and (c) activities and the nature of the specified income shall remain unchanged throughout the tax years. 3. Failure to comply with the conditions specified in paragraph 2 shall result in withdrawal of exemption under Schedule III [Table: Sl. No. 36] read with section 11 and initiation of proceedings under the said Act. 4. This notification shall be applicable for the tax years 2026-27 and 2027-28. [Notification No. 150 /2026 / F. No. 300196/58/2024-ITA-I] HARDEV SINGH, Under Secy.
