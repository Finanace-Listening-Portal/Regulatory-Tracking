Articles 2 min read Canara Robeco AMC enters Asia and India Book of Records for largest mutual fund investor awareness campaign Canara Robeco Asset Management Company has entered the Asia Book of Records and India Book of Records for its year-long 'Nivesh Bus Yatra' campaign. Traveling over 28,000 km across 12 states, the mobile classroom initiative reached over 26,000 investors to promote awareness on SIPs, STPs, and SWPs.
