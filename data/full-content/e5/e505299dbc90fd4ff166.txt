Rebuilding the CASA franchise and improving the earnings profile of the enlarged bank will be among the key challenges for Bagchi as he begins a three-year term on October 27.Bagchi will succeed Sashidhar Jagdishan, whose tenure ends on October 26. The external appointment gives HDFC Bank an opportunity to reassess its strategy and challenge existing practices at a time when the bank is navigating deposit mobilisation, loan growth and margin recovery, according to Nomura. Advt The first challenge will be rebuilding the low-cost deposit franchise. Total deposits grew 14.7% year-on-year to Rs 31.71 lakh crore at the end of June 2026, but CASA deposits grew only 9.4% to Rs 7.01 lakh crore, while time deposits increased 17.4% to Rs 21.46 lakh crore. The CASA ratio consequently stood at 32.3%. The ratio has fallen from 34.1% in FY26 and 34.8% in FY25, and was 44.4% in FY23.Bagchi will also have to arrest the pressure on net interest margins without sacrificing loan growth. HDFC Bank’s NIM fell to 3.26% in Q1FY27 from 3.38% in the March quarter, its lowest level on record. The bank's FY26 NIM was 3.34%, down from 4.1% in FY23, highlighting the extent of the margin compression since the HDFC Ltd merger.The challenge is particularly significant because the bank is still growing its loan book at a healthy pace. Gross advances rose 15.4% year-on-year to Rs30.61 lakh crore as of June 30, while advances under management increased 12.4%. Retail loans grew 7.2%, compared with 18.7% growth in the small and mid-market enterprise segment. Bagchi will therefore have to balance the need for faster growth with the need to improve the profitability of that growth.Focus on returnsImproving returns will be another priority. Net profit rose 5% year-on-year to Rs19,059.72 crore in Q1FY27, while net interest income increased 6.7% to Rs33,535 crore. The relatively modest profit growth, despite 15.4% growth in advances, underlines the pressure on margins and the need to extract more earnings from the enlarged balance sheet. Advt The HDFC Ltd merger will remain an important part of that equation. Completed in 2023, the merger substantially increased the size and changed the composition of HDFC Bank’s balance sheet. The bank has since been working to bring down its elevated loan-to-deposit ratio while rebuilding its deposit base. Its credit-deposit ratio was 95.8% at the end of June, according to post-results analysis.Bagchi will also inherit the task of rebuilding investor confidence amid a leadership transition and recent governance concerns. Chairman Atanu Chakraborty resigned in March citing differences over values and ethics, following which the bank commissioned an external review. The review found no evidence substantiating the governance concerns raised by Chakraborty. Separately, the board penalised Jagdishan and two other senior executives over a matter related to large-deposit pricing. Advt The new CEO will have to combine a strategic reset with execution on the bank’s core operating priorities. Deposit mobilisation, CASA growth, margin recovery, sustained loan growth and improved returns will be key measures of Bagchi’s first phase at HDFC Bank.
