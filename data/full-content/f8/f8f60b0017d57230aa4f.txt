} } Listen to This ArticlePrivate sector lender YES Bank on Saturday reported a 65 per cent year-on-year rise in standalone net profit to ₹1,080 crore for the second quarter of FY27, compared with ₹654 crore in the year-ago period, on the back of a sharp rise in net interest income and lower provisions. Sequentially, net profit rose 0.8 per cent from ₹1,071 crore in Q1FY27. For the first half of FY27, net profit rose 47.8 per cent to ₹2,151 crore from ₹1,456 crore. However, the results do not reflect the potential financial impact of the Supreme Court's ruling on the bank's write-down of Additional Tier-1 (AT1) bonds worth ₹8,415 crore. The Supreme Court on October 15 upheld the Bombay High Court judgment and dismissed the appeals against it in a case relating to the write-down of AT1 bonds worth ₹8,415 crore in March 2020. The bank said it is evaluating the consequential implications and the legal remedies available and has not recognised any financial impact in the reported results. The joint statutory auditors have issued a qualified review conclusion on the results on this account. A pro forma table in the results shows that, had the impact been recognised, the bank would have reported a net loss of ₹5,217 crore for the quarter and ₹4,146 crore for the half-year. Net worth would have been ₹46,970 crore and borrowings ₹86,149 crore, with return on assets at minus 4.3 per cent for the quarter. The capital adequacy ratio would remain at 14.5 per cent. Other income rose 14.4 per cent year-on-year to ₹1,882 crore from ₹1,644 crore and 4.7 per cent sequentially from ₹1,798 crore. The treasury segment's profit before tax rose 88.3 per cent to ₹715 crore from ₹380 crore a year ago. The retail banking segment reported a loss of ₹15 crore against a loss of ₹358 crore in the year-ago quarter. Operating profit before provisions and contingencies rose 29.4 per cent year-on-year to ₹1,678 crore from ₹1,297 crore in Q2FY26.
