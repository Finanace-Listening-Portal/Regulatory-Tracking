The index touched an intraday high of 61,394.30 before giving up some gains to close at 60,792.40, up 1.76%, or 1,051.15 points. Its record high stands at 61,764.85, recorded in February.The index has gained around 12% so far in November and risen about 13.4% from its October 28 low of 53,626 at Wednesday's close. It had recovered nearly 14.5% from that low at its intraday peak. The rally has been supported by renewed foreign portfolio investor (FPI) buying, with financial stocks among the beneficiaries. FPIs net bought shares worth Rs 56,771 crore over the eight trading sessions covered by the latest exchange data. Foreign investors have bought nearly $6 billion in Indian equities so far in November, their largest monthly inflow since September 2024. Advt "Bank Nifty closed forming a bearish candlestick on the daily chart and is currently trading in the overbought zone on both daily and hourly timeframes, indicating the possibility of an intermediate retracement from current levels. Following the strong run-up rally, the index may witness mild profit booking towards the 60,000–59,800 levels. Key support for the index is placed at 60,000 and 58,800, while 61,000 remains an immediate resistance. However, the broader trend remains positive; hence, investors should maintain a buy-on-dips strategy in Bank Nifty," said Vatsal Bhuva, Technical Analyst at LKP Securities.Easing crude oil prices have also supported sentiment by reducing concerns over imported inflation and pressure on the rupee. Brent crude traded near $93 a barrel on Wednesday, after falling to a two-month low of $89.16 in the previous session. Public sector banks led the sectoral advance, with the Nifty PSU Bank index gaining 3.5%, compared with a 2.12% rise in the Nifty Private Bank index. Buying was broad-based across the 14 constituents of the Bank Nifty.Next resistanceThe next resistance is around 61,300-61,400. A sustained move above this range could open the way towards the previous peak of 61,765 and potentially a fresh record. However, the index's inability to hold above its intraday high of 61,394, followed by late-session profit-taking, suggests that selling pressure is emerging at elevated levels. Advt Technical indicators remain positive, with the index trading above its 50-day and 200-day exponential moving averages. Shorter-term moving averages are also above longer-term averages, while the 50-day average is approaching the 200-day average, raising the possibility of a bullish crossover. The moving average convergence divergence indicator continues to strengthen, with its positive histogram expanding.However, the relative strength index has risen to 78.61, close to the overbought threshold of 80. This raises the possibility of intermittent profit-taking or consolidation, particularly after the sharp recovery from October lows. A failure to cross 61,400 decisively could keep the index in a consolidation phase before another attempt at the record high. Advt The immediate support zone is around 60,350-60,400, with 60,000 emerging as another important support. Options positioning also points to 60,500 as a key near-term level, where open interest is concentrated on both the call and put sides. The 61,000 and 62,000 strikes have significant call open interest and could act as resistance, while the 60,000 strike has substantial put open interest and could provide support. A sustained break below 60,000 could expose the index to the next support around 58,800.The broader outlook remains constructive as long as the index holds above its near-term support levels. The sustainability of foreign fund inflows, movements in crude oil prices and continued participation by public and private sector banks will determine whether the index can move decisively beyond its February peak. A breakout above 61,400 would strengthen the case for a fresh record, while failure to do so could result in consolidation after the sharp November rally.
