Advt "On the downside, 25,400 has emerged as immediate support, followed by the 25,300 level," market experts noted.The rally was broad-based, with automobile stocks emerging as the biggest gainers.Maruti Suzuki India, Eicher Motors and Mahindra & Mahindra led the advances on the Nifty index.Among sectoral indices, the Nifty Auto was the standout performer, rising more than 4 per cent during the session.Banking stocks also remained in focus, with the Nifty Bank touching a fresh all-time high of 63,053.25 before ending the day with solid gains.Consumer-focused sectors participated actively in the rally, with the Nifty FMCG and Nifty Consumer Durables indices outperforming the broader market.Public sector banks also attracted buying interest, helping the Nifty PSU Bank index post notable gains.Market experts said that the strong gains in autos, banks and consumer-oriented stocks lifted overall market sentiment."Indian equities extended their recovery despite weak Asian cues and concerns over a hawkish US Fed, supported by sustained FII inflows and easing commodity prices, from copper to crude," analysts noted."The trend reflected a shift in investor preference towards domestic demand-driven sectors, while IT remained under pressure amid expectations of higher US interest rates," market experts added.
