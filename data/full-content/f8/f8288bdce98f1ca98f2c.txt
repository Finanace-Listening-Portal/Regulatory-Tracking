Requirements covering round-the-clock security monitoring, real-time fraud detection, disaster recovery infrastructure, annual security audits and vendor oversight could weigh more heavily on smaller operators with limited financial and technology resources.The classification brings payment aggregators such as Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, Paytm Payments Services, PhonePe, Pine Labs and BillDesk under the same regulatory category, despite significant differences in their scale of operations. Smaller and specialised operators such as Easebuzz, Decentro, PayGlocal, Paysharp, SabPaisa and Aurus Paytech also fall within the large category. Large and medium operators must comply with the directions from November 5, 2026, while small prepaid payment instrument issuers and instant money transfer operators have until April 1, 2028. Advt The differences in financial scale are substantial. One 97 Communications, Paytm's parent, reported consolidated revenue of Rs 8,437 crore in FY26, while Pine Labs reported revenue of Rs 2,711 crore. Cashfree reported operating revenue of Rs 967.2 crore in FY26. Razorpay reported consolidated revenue of Rs 3,783 crore in FY25. These figures cover businesses with different revenue compositions and are not directly comparable, but illustrate the variation in financial resources among payment companies.The impactThe requirements could increase recurring technology and personnel expenditure. Operators must maintain security operations centres for continuous network monitoring, deploy automated systems to detect suspicious transactions and maintain round-the-clock response facilities to handle unauthorised transactions and law enforcement requests. Cyberattacks, critical system outages, internal fraud and settlement delays must be reported to the RBI within six hours of detection.Companies must also conduct application security testing at least annually, obtain independent cybersecurity assurance for vendors involved in critical processes and arrange annual cybersecurity audits of cloud service providers. They must maintain disaster recovery facilities in a different seismic zone from their primary data centres and conduct recovery drills at least twice a year. Smaller aggregators could find it harder to spread these fixed costs across their transaction volumes and merchant base.The compliance burden also extends to third-party service providers, payment gateways and technology vendors connected to payment operators' digital infrastructure. Operators must ensure that vendors involved in critical processes meet prescribed security requirements and provide independent assurance of their cybersecurity capabilities. This could increase scrutiny of vendor contracts and favour technology providers with established security infrastructure and audit capabilities. Advt The market is concentrated in terms of consumer-facing digital payment volumes. PhonePe accounted for 45.34 per cent of UPI payer-app transactions in October, followed by Google Pay at 32.09 per cent and Paytm at 7.96 per cent. Together, the three platforms accounted for more than 85 per cent of UPI transactions initiated through payer applications. However, these shares represent UPI app usage and not the market shares of payment aggregators.The directions also introduce additional safeguards for customers. Payment operators must implement device binding of mobile applications with the device and SIM, while online transactions will be subject to a minimum 12-hour cooling period following a change in the registered mobile number or email ID linked to the payment instrument. Customers must also be provided with facilities to identify and report fraudulent transactions through the operators' websites or mobile applications. Advt The November 5 directions consolidate the cybersecurity framework issued in July 2024 with earlier requirements governing third-party ATM switch service providers and payment system audits. The framework retains a phased compliance approach for smaller prepaid payment instrument issuers and instant money transfer operators, giving them until April 2028 to meet the requirements.
