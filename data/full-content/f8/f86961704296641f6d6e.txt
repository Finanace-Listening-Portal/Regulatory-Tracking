Bajaj Finance, through Bajaj Finserv Intelligence, has invested approximately $2 million in Soket AI. Pennant Technologies, an investee company of Bajaj Finance, has invested an additional $2 million in the same funding round. Soket AI is developing indigenous large language models (LLMs) focused on mathematics, coding and reasoning. Backed by the government's IndiaAI Mission, the company has launched EKA-1, a 26-billion-parameter foundation model under its flagship Project EKA initiative.
