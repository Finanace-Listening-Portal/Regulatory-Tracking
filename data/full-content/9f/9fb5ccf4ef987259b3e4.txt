( Representative Image: NASA/unsplash)Anjaly Raj New Delhi 3 min read Last Updated : Nov 17 2026 | 12:33 PM IST @media (min-width:1100px) and (max-width:1280px) { .storymeta .meta-info { flex: 0 0 45%; } } Listen to This ArticleAround 40 Chinese spaceplanes could begin descending towards almost any location on Earth within minutes, a new study exploring the potential of a fleet of reusable orbital spacecraft has stated. The study found an orbital constellation that could allow spacecraft to begin descending towards any location on Earth after a maximum wait of 2.5 minutes. For most of the world's populated regions, the wait could be as short as about a minute, the South China Morning Post reported. The findings are based entirely on computer simulations. The one-to-two-minute figures refer to the maximum wait for a suitable opportunity to begin the return journey, not the time required for a spacecraft to reach its destination. The research was conducted by Du Chongrui and his team at the School of Astronautics at Northwestern Polytechnical University in Xian, China. The study was published on October 30 in the peer-reviewed Chinese journal Acta Aeronautica et Astronautica Sinica, the South China Morning Post report stated. The proposed constellation could have implications for rapid global transportation as well as potential military applications, including space-based strikes. Spacecraft already positioned in orbit could begin their descent towards distant targets shortly after receiving an order, potentially leaving little time for a target to detect or respond to an incoming vehicle, the study found.
