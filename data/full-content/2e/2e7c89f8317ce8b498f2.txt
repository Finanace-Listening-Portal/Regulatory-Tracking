Our partnership with BlueOrchard strengthens our ability to deepen our reach in the MSME segment and support a more inclusive credit landscape". Advt BlueOrchard Finance Chief Credit Officer Normunds Mizis said, "Our investment in Capri Global reflects our approach of partnering with financial institutions to support the flow of capital to this segment, while contributing to broader economic resilience and sustainable growth".
