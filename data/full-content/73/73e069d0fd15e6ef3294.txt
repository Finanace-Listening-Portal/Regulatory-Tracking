The Rules establish a regulatory framework for the installation, operation, inspection, maintenance and licensing of lifts, escalators and moving walks throughout Maharashtra. They prescribe procedures for obtaining installation permissions, submitting architectural and structural certificates, furnishing technical drawings, securing operating licences and obtaining licence renewals. The Electrical Inspector is responsible for examining applications and conducting inspections, while the concerned Superintending Engineer exercises specified licensing powers. An operating licence is valid for twenty years, with renewal applications required at least thirty days before expiry. The Rules also prescribe procedures for issuing duplicate licences and transferring licences following changes in ownership, making compliance particularly important for residential buildings, cooperative housing societies, commercial establishments and property developers. The Rules introduce important technical and operational safety requirements by requiring compliance with the applicable Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Codes of Practice, National Building Code, Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) and electrical safety regulations. Lift installations must have Closed Circuit Television (CCTV) systems, while existing lifts without CCTV, Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD) and Overload Protection Devices (OLPD) must be equipped with these facilities within one year from publication of the Rules in the Official Gazette. Owners must appoint approved contractors for maintenance, ensure examination and servicing at least once every month, maintain prescribed logbooks, undertake periodic testing and safety audits, and display operating licences and safety instructions. The Rules also provide for annual inspections, rectification of defects, restrictions on unauthorised interference with safety mechanisms and requirements for reporting accidents, including submission of a detailed accident report within twenty-four hours. Children below twelve years of age are not permitted to use lifts, escalators or moving walks unless accompanied by an adult. The Rules further strengthen accountability by prescribing eligibility conditions for approved electrical contractors engaged in the erection and maintenance of lifts, escalators and moving walks. Contractors must maintain qualified engineering and technical personnel, suitable workshops, prescribed tools and testing equipment, safety devices, transportation facilities and arrangements for emergency assistance. Additional service stations are required in specified circumstances involving the number or location of installations, while arrangements must be maintained for receiving and responding to emergency calls on a twenty-four-hour basis. The regulatory framework also addresses unsafe installations, discontinuance of operation, sealing and decommissioning of lifts, escalators and moving walks. Owners and facility managers should therefore review existing licences, maintenance contracts, inspection records, safety equipment and emergency-response procedures to identify compliance gaps. The Rules are especially significant for housing societies, building management agencies, commercial complexes, hospitals, shopping centres and lift maintenance companies, as they establish more detailed responsibilities for ensuring the safe and reliable operation of vertical transportation systems across Maharashtra. MAHARASHTRA LIFTS, ESCALATORS AND MOVING WALKS RULES, 2026. No. CEI-2025/C.R.55/Energy-1.- In exercise of the powers conferred by section 37 of the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalator and Moving Walks Act, 2017 (Mah. XV of 2018), the Government of Maharashtra hereby makes the following rules to carry out the purposes of the said Act, namely:- 1. Short title.- These rules may be called the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Rules, 2026. 2. Definitions.- (1) In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires,- (a) “Act” means the Maharashtra Lifts, Escalators and Moving Walks Act, 2017 (Mah. XV of 2018); (b) “Annexure” means the annexure appended to these rules; (c) “FORM” means a FORM appended to these rules; (d) “section” means a section of the Act. (2) Words and expressions used in these rules but not defined shall have the same meanings as are respectively assigned to them in the Act or Code of Practice of Bureau of Indian Standards, as the case may be. 3. Qualification for appointment as Chief Electrical Inspector, Superintending Engineer, Electrical Inspector and Assistant Electrical Inspector.- (1) Chief Electrical Inspector.- No person shall be appointed to be Chief Electrical Inspector, unless he – (i) possesses a Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from recognized University or Institution; and (ii) has been regularly engaged for a period of at least twenty years in the practice of Electrical Engineering. (2) Superintending Engineer.- No person shall be appointed to be the Superintending Engineer, unless he – (i) possesses a Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from recognized University or Institution; and (ii) has been regularly engaged for a period of at least fifteen years in the practice of Electrical Engineering . (3)Electrical Inspector.- No person shall be appointed to be the Electrical Inspector, unless he – (i) possesses a Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from recognized University or Institution; and (ii) has been regularly engaged for a period of at least ten years in the practice of Electrical Engineering. (4) Assistant Electrical Inspector.- No person shall be appointed as the Assistant Electrical Inspector an officer to assist unless he- (i) possesses a Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from a recognized University or Institution; and (ii) has been regularly engaged for a period of at least three years in the practice of Electrical Engineering. 4. Qualification for appointment as technical and other persons to assist Electrical Inspector and Assistant Electrical Inspector.- (1)Assistant Engineer- No person shall be appointed as an officer to assist an Electrical Inspector and Assistant Electrical Inspector as Assistant Engineer, unless he possesses a Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from a recognized University or Institution. (2) Sectional Engineer or Junior Engineer- No person shall be appointed as an officer to assist an Electrical Inspector and Assistant Electrical Inspector as Sectional Engineer or Junior Engineer, unless he possesses a Diploma or Degree in Electrical Engineering or its equivalent from a recognized Institution. 5. Permission to erect lift, escalator or moving walk.- (1) Every owner of a place intending– (i) to install a lift or escalator or moving walk in such place, or (ii) to make additions or alterations to a lift or escalator or moving walk installed at such place, shall make an application for a lift in FORM A and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM B to the Electrical Inspector, before any work in connection with the installation of the lift, escalator or moving walk addition or alteration thereto, as the case may be, is started. (2) Every such application shall be accompanied with sets of erection plans and the following documents, namely:- (a) architect certificate in FORM C; (b) structural engineer certificate in FORM D; (c) declaration by approved contractor for erection of lift, escalator or moving walk in FORM E, as the case may be; (d) lift, escalator or moving walk installation drawing duly signed by owner and approved contractor; (e) Power of attorney. (3) Every such application shall also be accompanied by such fee as may be specified under section 4 by the Government by notification in the Official Gazette. Fees shall be paid into the Government Treasury or the Reserve Bank of India through Government Receipts Accounting System or e-payment gateway. (4) On receipt of an application under sub-rule (1), the Electrical Inspector shall, after making such inquiries and requiring the applicant to furnish such information as he may deem necessary, forward the application along with his remarks and duly filled scrutiny FORM (for a lift in FORM F and for an escalators or moving walk in FORM G), to the concerned Superintending Engineer who may, thereupon, either grant or refuse the permission applied. The permission for lift may be granted in Form H and permission for Escalator and moving walk may be granted in Form I. The permission granted shall be valid for period of one year. The period of validity may be extended subject to production of structural stability certificate. 6. Grant of licence to electrical contractor for erection and maintenance of lifts, escalator or moving walk,- (1) Any person or body of persons or body corporate having valid electrical contractor licence issued by Licencing Board constituted under provisions of Central Electricity Authority (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply) Regulations, 2023 framed under the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003), who intends to obtain licence for erection and maintenance of lift or escalator or moving walk, shall make application to the Electrical Inspector in FORM J, accompanied with application fee, as notified under section 5 by the Government in the Official Gazette and following documents, namely:- (a) self-attested photo copy of valid electrical contractor’s licence; (b) documents of the workshop, office premises, electric bill and telephone bill; (c) FORM K specifying a list of employees along with their photo copies of qualifications and experience certificates verified by the contactor; (d) declaration of contractor declaring that his employees are not working in any other organization; (e) list of spare parts of lifts, escalators or moving walk available with them for erection and maintenance therefore; (f) list of tools and plants available with them for erection and maintenance of lifts, escalators or moving walk; (g) xerox copies of documents of vehicle in possession for transportation; (h) a xerox copy of shop and establishment licence; (i) documents showing the facility for providing training to the technical staff. (2) The electrical contractor for obtaining the licence for erection and maintenance of lift, escalator or moving walk,- (i) shall have independent office premises with an independent telephone connection within the State of Maharashtra: Provided that, where the number of lifts, escalators or moving walk under maintenance exceeds fifty in a city or town or such lifts are located more than 100 kilometres from the contractor’s main office, an additional service station shall be established by the contractor in that city or town, having the necessary facilities to provide timely maintenance and emergency services; (ii) shall have to produce a certificate from any nationalized Bank or Reserve Bank of India approved Bank or by a Tahsildar to the effect that he is solvent to the extent of rupees five lakhs; (iii) shall have at least in his regular employment following minimum staff,- (a) at least one Electrical Supervisor possessing Electrical Supervisor’s licence from the Maharashtra State Licencing Board and having an experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company, as the case may be; (b) at least one Engineer possessing,- (I) a Degree in Mechanical Engineering or its equivalent qualification from the recognized university or Institution and having experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; or (II) a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering or its equivalent qualification from the recognized Institution and having experience of at least eight years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (c) at least one Engineer possessing,- (I) a Degree in Electrical or Electronics Engineering or its equivalent qualification from the recognized University or Institution and having experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (II) a Diploma in Electrical or Electronics Engineering or its equivalent qualification from the recognized Institution and having experience of at least eight years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (d) at least one person possessing a Certificate in the trade of Electrical or Electronics from the Industrial Training Institute or any other recognized Institution approved by Government and having experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (e) for erection of lift or escalator and moving walk one erection team for twenty five new lifts, escalators and moving walks to be erected during one year consisting of,- (I) one Senior Erector having an experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (II) one Junior Erector having an experience of at least five years in the field of lifts, escalators or moving walk with a licence holder firm or company; (III) one helper, (f) for maintenance of lift or escalator or moving walk, one maintenance pair for fifty lift or escalator and moving walk under maintenance during one year period consisting of one Mechanic having experience of at least five years in the field with a licence holder firm or company and one helper: (g) to provide a 24×7 call attendance service at the service station to receive and promptly respond to complaint and emergency calls from lift owners: Provided that, when a person applying for licence under this rule is himself qualified Mechanical or Electrical or Electronics Engineer with requisite experience, the employment of another Mechanical or Electrical or Electronics Engineer shall not be necessary; (iv) shall have a workshop or warehouse in his own possession or on hired basis in prescribed agreement having minimum area of 100 square meter with independent facility and telephone and separate power connection of at least 15 H. P. Such workshop shall be equipped with following minimum machineries and testing instruments either stationary or portable, namely:- (a) drill machine; (b) welding machine; (c) hacksaw machine; (d) chain pulley block: Provided that, when the approved contractor is having his own manufacturing unit, he shall not be required to have a separate workshop but he shall be required to have the minimum machineries and instruments as mentioned above; (v) shall have necessary safety gadgets like safety belts and head gears for entire staff engaged in the work of maintenance and erection; (vi) shall have suitable independent vehicle in his possession for maintenance. (3) On receipt of an application for licence to electrical contractor for erection and maintenance of lift or escalator and moving walk, the Electrical Inspector shall, after making an inspection of office and workshop premises and such other inquiry and as he considers necessary and after obtaining such further information as may be required to be furnished by the applicant, forward the application, with his remarks to the Superintending Engineer. The Superintending Engineer may thereupon either grant or refuse the licence. Where the licence is granted, it shall be issued in FORM L. (4) The licence granted under this rule shall be renewed on an application made by the licence holder to the Superintending Engineer through the concerned Electrical Inspector. The application shall be submitted in Form M, alongwith, such fee as notified under sub-section (5) of section 5 by the Government in the Official Gazette and documents specified in sub- rule (1) above, at least thirty days before the date of expiry of the licence or the renewed licence, as the case may be. The applicant shall also submit the certificate from minimum ten owners of the lift or escalator or moving walk who’s lift or escalator or moving walk is maintained by the licence holder stating that licence holder has maintain the lift or escalator or moving walk, as the case may be, in good condition. (5) On receipt of an application made under sub-rule (4), the Electrical Inspector shall after making inspection of office and workshop premises of the applicant and such other inquiries as he considers necessary and after obtaining such further information as may be required to be furnished by the applicant, forward the application, with his remarks to the Superintending Engineer. The Superintending Engineer may, after considering the application and the remarks of the Electrical Inspector either grant or refuse the renewal of the licence. Where the renewal is granted, the Superintending Engineer shall renew the licence in Form L. (6) If, the approved contractor fails to apply with requisite documents for renewal of licence in the said manner within aforesaid period of the date of expiry, the licence shall become void and approved contractor shall be required to obtain a fresh licence under sub-rule (1). 7. Initial inspection before grant of licence for working of lift, escalator or moving walk and licence for working of lift, escalator or moving walk.- (1) Every owner of a place who is permitted to erect or install a lift or escalator or moving walk in such place shall, immediately but not later than one month after the lift or escalator or moving walk the respective unit is erected or installed, deliver or send or cause to be delivered or cause to be sent to the Electrical Inspector, notice in writing of such erection or installation in the FORM N, and shall make an application to him for licence for working or using the lift or escalator or moving walk. (2) Every such application shall be accompanied with such fee as notified under sub-section (2) of section 7 by the Government in the Official Gazette and the set of following documents, namely:- (a) Declaration of approved contractor after completion of erection of lift or escalator or moving walk in FORM O; (b) Test report, (for a lift in FORM P and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM Q); (c) Certificate of approved contractor under sub-section (6) of section 7; (d) fees paid receipt; (e) Inspection report (for a lift in FORM R and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM S). (3) On receipt of application under sub- rule (1),- (i) The Electrical Inspector shall send the notice for inspection in FORM T to the applicant and carry out the inspection. (ii) If the Electrical Inspector finds that the lift, escalator or moving walk, as the case may be, conforms to the standards prescribed in the relevant Code of Practice of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), he shall forward the application, along with his remarks and the inspection report in the FORM (FORM U for a lift and FORM V for an escalator or moving walk) to the concerned Superintending Engineer. (iii) If the Electrical Inspector finds any defect in the lift, escalator or moving walk, or finds that it does not conform to the standards prescribed in the Code of Practice of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), he shall communicate the defects to the applicant in FORM W. Upon rectification of the defects and verification of compliance, he shall forward the application, along with his remarks and the inspection report, to the concerned Superintending Engineer. (4) After receipt of application Superintending Engineer may either grant the licence for a lift in FORM X and for an escalator and moving walk in FORM Y, subject to provision of rule 9 or refuse the licence. (5) The licence granted for working of lifts, escalator or moving walk shall be valid for a period of twenty years from the date on which it is granted. (6) At least thirty days, before the expiry of the period of licence, the licencee may make an application for its renewal, in FORM Z for a Lift and in FORM ZA for an escalator or moving walk, along with fee as notified under sub-section (4) of section 8 by the Government in the Official Gazette and following documents, namely:- (a) Architecture Certificate in FORM C; (b) Structural Engineer Certificate in FORM D; (c) fees paid receipt; (d) test report, for a lift in FORM P and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM Q; (e) certificate of approved contractor under sub-section (6) of section 7; (f) inspection fees paid receipt for last year; (g) inspection report for a lift in FORM R and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM S; (h) Original licence. (7) On receipt of an application for renewal under sub-rule (4), the Electrical Inspector shall issue a notice for inspection in Form T and carry out the inspection. Where any defects are noticed, the Electrical Inspector shall communicate such defects to the applicant in Form W or issue a notice requiring the replacement or modification of the lift, escalator, or moving walk in accordance with the relevant Code of Practice of the Bureau of Indian Standards for the up-gradation of existing installations. Upon compliance with the defects or the requirements specified in such notice, or where no defects are found during the inspection, the Electrical Inspector shall, after making such inquiries as he considers necessary, forward the application, with his remarks and the Inspection FORM (FORM U for lifts or FORM V for escalators or moving walks), to the concerned Superintending Engineer. The Superintending Engineer may, subject to the provisions of these rules, either grant or refuse the renewal of the licence. Where the renewal is granted, the renewed licence shall be issued in Form ZB. 8. Issuance of duplicate licence or Transfer of ownership:- (1) When licence issued under the provisions of rule 5 is lost or destroyed or the ownership of the premises where lift, escalator or moving walk is in working has been transferred, the licence holder may apply to the Electrical Inspector for obtaining the duplicate licence or Transfer of licence, as the case may be. (2) Every such application shall be made, for duplicate licence in FORM ZC and for transfer of ownership in FORM ZD, accompanied with fee as notified by the Government in the Official Gazette and following documents, namely:- (a) Architecture Certificate in FORM C; (b) Structural Engineer Certificate (If old record is not available) in FORM D; (c) Technical drawing duly signed by owner and approved contractor ( If old record is not available); (d) Police N. C. (If old licence is missing); (e) Test report, for a lift in FORM P and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM Q; (f) Certificate of approved contractor under sub-section (6) of section 7; (g) inspection fees paid receipt for last year; (h) Inspection report for a lift in FORM R and for an escalator or moving walk in FORM S; (i) copy of original Licence; (j) copy of Annual Maintenance Contract; (k) affidavit of licencee as per FORM ZE; (l) documents showing Change of ownership ( In case of Change of Ownership application). (3) On receipt of an application under sub-rule (2), the Electrical Inspector shall, after making such inquiries as he considers necessary, forward the application, together with his remarks, to the concerned Superintending Engineer. The concerned Superintending Engineer may, subject to the provisions of these rules, either grant or refuse the application. Where the application is granted, the licence shall be issued in FORM X for a lift and in FORM Y for an escalator or moving walk. (5) A duplicate licence or Transfer of ownership of licence issued under this rule shall clearly mention the date of original issuance and stamped as “Duplicate Licence” or “Change In Name” as the case may be. 9. The specifications for lift, escalator, moving walk under section 12.- (1) No licence for working of the lift, escalator or moving walk shall be granted unless it complies with the applicable relevant National Building Code or Code of Practice of Bureau of Indian Standards, and the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR), particularly for the lift, escalator or moving walk as mentioned in Annexure. (2) The lift, escalator or moving walk and the respective installation shall comply with the Electricity Act, 2003 (36 of 2003) and the Central Electricity Authority (Measures Relating To Safety And Electricity Supply) Regulations, 2023, made there under. (3) It any terms or provisions regarding lift, escalator or moving walk are not covered under the provision of Code of Practice of Indian Standard, the National Building Code of India and the Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations (UDCPR), but provided in the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) or European Standardization (EN) then the said terms and provision may be adopted after approval of Government of Maharashtra and thereafter followed while granting the licence. (3) The lift installation shall have close circuit television (CCTV) system installed in lift car: Provided that, before the date of commencement of these rules, in the existing lifts where Close Circuit Television (CCTV), Automatic Rescue Device (ARD), Overload Protection Device (OLPD) are not provided, in such existing lifts the Close Circuit Television (CCTV), Automatic Rescue Device (ARD), Overload Protection Device (OLPD) shall be provided within a period of one year from the date of commencement of these rules i.e. from the date of publication of these rules in the Official Gazette. 10. Terms on which lift or escalator or moving walk shall be worked. – Every lift or escalator or moving walk equipment and the installation shall be worked subject to the following terms, namely:- (1) The licencee shall maintain every lift, escalator or moving walk and its installation in accordance with the requirements specified in rule 9. (2) The licencee or the licencee through its authorized contractor shall carry out periodic audits and testing of lift, escalator or moving walk, as the case may be, in accordance with the schedule specified in FORM R for a lift and in FORM S for an escalator and moving walk, and shall certify, not less than once in a year or at such earlier intervals as required by the Electrical Inspector. The report shall be submitted to the Electrical Inspector. (3) The licencee shall not carry out any additions or alterations to a lift or escalator or moving walk or to the respective installation at the place which cause change in the basic characteristics and or may have bearing on safe working of the equipment or safety of the user, maintenance persons or persons in the vicinity of the installation and shall not erect or install lift or escalator or moving walk at any place without obtaining permission in that behalf from the Superintending Engineer as required by the provisions of section 10 as well as rule 9. (4) The licencee shall entrust the maintenance of the equipment and the respective installation to an approved contractor. On such appointment, the approved contractor shall be responsible for carrying out the duties and obligations imposed on the licencee under this rule, to the extent assigned to him, and the licencee shall not obstruct or prevent the contractor from discharging such duties and obligations. (5) The licencee shall enter every repair and alteration to the lift, escalator or moving walk in a log book in Form (for a lifts in FORM ZF and for an escalators and moving walks in FORM ZG) which shall be maintained in each lift or escalator or moving walk installation. (6) The licencee shall take remedial measures in every fault in the equipment or the installation, reported by the approved contractor, immediately. (7) The licencee shall ensure that the maintenance and examination of lifts, escalators and moving walks are carried out by the approved contractor at least once every month. The results of such maintenance and examination shall be recorded by the contractor in the log book of lifts in FORM ZF and for escalators and moving walks in FORM ZG. (8) The licencee shall display licence for working of lift, escalator or moving walk at ground level or entry level or as specified by the Electrical Inspector. (9) The licencee shall display safety instructions boards and symbols as provided in FORM ZH and ZI for a lift and in FORM ZJ for an escalator or moving walk, as specified by the Electrical Inspector. (10) No person shall wilfully interfere with any mechanism of the lift or escalator or moving walk installation. (11) No lift or escalator or moving walk which has been kept disconnected for a period of six months or more, from the system for alterations or repair, shall be put into use until such apparatus, cable or supply line are subjected to the site tests as per relevant standards by the approved contractor. (12) No person below the age of eighteen years shall be engaged as a lift operator. The lift operator shall be a person who is mentally and physically fit and trained in the correct operation of the lift. (13) Children under twelve years of age shall not be allowed to use the lift or escalator or moving walk unless accompanied by an adult. (14) Every licence issue under this rule shall be treated as revoked if it contrivance to any of the provisions mentioned in this rule. (15) If any part, enclosure, gate or fastening of a lift or escalator or moving walk is damaged or broken, the licencee shall immediately repair and put it in good working order. He shall keep the safety gears in good order and all parts of the lift or escalator or moving walk and safety gear clean and free from rubbish, dust or dirt. The licencee shall not weld any broken or damaged parts which are subject to tension, torsion or bending or parts on which the passenger moving parts are supported. 11. Intimation and report of accident and the time within which intimation and report of accident to be given under section 15.- (1) Every accident shall be reported in FORM ZK as per the procedure laid down in section 15 with a detailed report of the accident in FORM ZL within a period of twenty four hours to the Electrical Inspector and the local police station. (2) The Electrical Inspector shall inquire and report as per sub-section (4) of Section 15 of the Act. 12. Periodical Inspections of Lift or Escalator or Moving Walk.- (1) Where a lift, escalator or moving walk is in operation, every such lift or escalator or moving walk shall be periodically inspected and tested by the Electrical Inspector as per provisions of section 17 of the Act. (2) Before carrying out such inspection, a notice shall be issued to the owner of the premises in FORM ZM and if any repairs or alterations are required then an order to directing to discontinue the use of the lift, escalator or moving walk as the case may be, shall be issued in FORM ZN. 13. Decommissioning and Sealing of Lifts, Escalators or Moving walks.- (1) Intimation of non-use.- The owner or licencee shall inform the Superintending Engineer of any lift, escalator or moving walk that is intended to be decommissioned, permanently removed from service or discontinued from service for a period exceeding six months. (2) Sealing of Lifts, Escalators or Moving Walks in case of Unsafe condition.- The Superintending Engineer shall issue the directions in FORM ZO for carrying out the repairs and alterations to be made in the lift, escalator or moving walk, as per report submitted to him under section 21. If directions given by the Superintending Engineer are not followed or complied then Superintending Engineer may, seal such lift, escalator or moving walk in accordance with the provisions of the Act. (3) Unused Lifts, Escalators or Moving walks.- Where a lift, escalator, or moving walk installed at any place ceases to be used as such, the owner shall either remove it or maintain it in a safe mechanical condition after completely disconnecting it from the electric supply. All gates and doors openings and access points shall be securely locked or barricaded so as to prevent the entry of unauthorized persons to the lift-well, escalators or moving walks. Unused escalators or moving walks shall not be permitted to be used as stairs or walkways.
