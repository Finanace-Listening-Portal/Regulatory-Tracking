Exercising powers under Section 14(2) of the Customs Act, 1962, the Board has substituted Table-1, Table-2 and Table-3 of Notification No. 36/2001-Customs (N.T.) dated 3 August 2001. The tariff value of gold eligible for benefits under Serial No. 194 of Notification No. 45/2025-Customs has been fixed at US$ 1,149 per 10 grams. The tariff value applicable to specified gold bars, gold coins and gold findings is also stated as US$ 1,149 per 10 grams. Silver continues to attract a tariff value of US$ 1,496 per kilogram. The tariff values of crude palm oil, RBD palm oil, other palm oil, crude palmolein, RBD palmolein, other palmolein and crude soybean oil remain unchanged at US$ 1,141, US$ 1,193, US$ 1,167, US$ 1,201, US$ 1,204, US$ 1,203 and US$ 1,282 per metric tonne, respectively. Brass scrap and areca nuts retain their tariff values of US$ 8,154 and US$ 10,433 per metric tonne, respectively. The principal notification was last amended through Notification No. 94/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 19 November 2026. The notification prescribes tariff values for customs valuation purposes and does not itself amend the applicable customs duty rates.
