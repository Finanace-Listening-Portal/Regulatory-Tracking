ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Updated On Nov 13, 2026 at 07:41 PM IST var news_msid = 135805543; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook State Bank of India (SBI), the country's largest lender, has advertised six senior specialist cadre officer positions in artificial intelligence (AI), including a General Manager and Chief AI Officer, as banks step up investments in building dedicated AI capabilities. The lender has set an annual cost-to-company (CTC) ceiling of Rs 1.6 crore for the Chief AI Officer, Rs 1.2 crore for Deputy General Manager-level positions and Rs 90 lakh for AI architect roles.The six positions, advertised on a fixed-term basis, include one General Manager and Chief AI Officer, three Deputy General Managers and two AI architects. Advt The three DGM positions are for Risk and Operational Analytics; AI Governance and Compliance; and Data Engineering, Model Application Development and AI Ops. The remaining two positions are AI Solution Architect and Enterprise AI Architect.The remuneration offered by SBI comprises 90 per cent fixed pay and 10 per cent variable pay. The last date to apply for these positions has been extended to November 16, 2026.Eligibility requirements vary by position, with relevant qualifications and five to 10 years of experience stipulated across the roles.Earlier in June 2026, Axis Bank appointed Namrata Dubashi as its Artificial Intelligence Officer, making it among the early Indian lenders to create a dedicated leadership role for AI as competition around the technology intensifies in the banking sector.Meanwhile, Indian Bank has also advertised eight contractual vacancies for its AI Cell, covering AI product management, engineering, architecture and responsible AI.The positions include two Assistant Vice President roles for AI Product Manager (Business and Use-Case Leadership), one Vice President and one Deputy Vice President role for AI Engineer/AI Architect, three Assistant Vice President roles for AI Engineer/AI Architect, and one Assistant Vice President role for Responsible AI Officer.
