} } Listen to This ArticleBajaj Finance, the country’s largest private sector non-bank lender, on Monday reported a 29 per cent year-on-year (YoY) rise in consolidated net profit to ₹6,367 crore for the July-September quarter of FY27 (Q2FY27), driven by strong growth in net interest income (NII) and assets under management (AUM). NII grew 24 per cent YoY to ₹13,335 crore, compared with ₹10,785 crore a year ago. Net total income increased 23 per cent to ₹15,974 crore, while AUM rose 26 per cent to ₹5.85 trillion as of September 30, 2026. The company added ₹37,680 crore to its AUM during the quarter and booked 13.45 million new loans, up 11 per cent from a year ago. However, it said the YoY growth in loan volumes was not comparable because the festive season had started earlier last year. Bajaj Finance added 4.42 million customers during the quarter, taking its total customer franchise to 128.85 million. It expects to book 60-62 million new loans and add 18-20 million customers in FY27. Loan losses and provisions rose 4 per cent YoY to ₹2,166 crore in Q2FY27, from ₹2,080 crore a year ago. The amount included a prudent management and macroeconomic provision of ₹337 crore.
