} } Listen to This ArticleIn a landmark policy decision to strengthen Tamil Nadu’s aviation ecosystem, the state government has announced a significant reduction in value-added tax (VAT) on aviation turbine fuel (ATF) for scheduled domestic airlines. VAT at Chennai Airport has been reduced from 29 per cent to 16 per cent, and at other airports to 1 per cent for scheduled domestic airlines. Other airports include Coimbatore, Madurai and Tiruchirapalli. The move is aimed at reducing airline operating costs, improving the competitiveness of airports across the state and creating greater opportunities for enhanced domestic air connectivity. The revised tax structure marks an important step towards building a more competitive and geographically distributed aviation network, with Chennai continuing to serve as a major aviation gateway and regional airports emerging as potential centres of expanded domestic flight operations. For scheduled airlines operating aircraft with a maximum take-off mass (MTOM) below 40,000 kg at airports other than Chennai, VAT has also been reduced from 5 per cent to 1 per cent. Aviation turbine fuel constitutes a substantial share of airline operating expenses.
