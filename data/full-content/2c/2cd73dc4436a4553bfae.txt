#AD For startups, MSMEs and growing enterprises in India, a brand name, logo or a punchline is often the most valuable asset of the company. Without formal registration under the Trade Marks Act, 1999, businesses continue to be exposed to the threats of copycats, brand dilution and costly legal battles. Establishing exclusive IP positions provides market credibility, allowing for franchising and licensing royalties and enhancing the long-term value of the business. Applications can be filed on the IP India portal in Form TM-A. But to navigate the 45 NICE classes of trademarks, do conflict searches, write correct trade descriptions and handle objections raised by the registry during examination requires procedural correctness. We put five online trademark registration services through their paces against nine practical benchmarks to help founders, finance heads and legal readers choose the right partner. Brand Name / Logo Copy: Digital image of logo, if any, and particulars of goods/services covered. Signed Authorization Letter (Form TM-48): 1 page power of attorney to enable online submission.
