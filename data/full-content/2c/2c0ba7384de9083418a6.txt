The notification has been issued in exercise of powers under Sub-Section 5 of Section 10 read with Section 37 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. The amendment inserts new entries in the existing table after Serial No. 78. The notified officers have been assigned to the Eastern, Northern, Southern and Western Regions, with their areas of jurisdiction covering the respective States/Union Territories under those regions. The entries include Ms Palleboina Mounika and several other officers for the Eastern Region; officers including Ms Parvatam Ravali, Ms Boddu Gayathri, Sh. Rahul Jain and others for the Northern Region; a substantial number of officers for the Southern Region; and officers including Shridhar Sameta, Sh. Pratik Shelke, Sh. Shivkumar Palimkar and others for the Western Region. The notification further records that the principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part III, Section 4 dated 2 September 2021 and was last amended through notification F. No. RCD-15001/4/2021-Regulatory-FSSAI-part(1) dated 6 April 2026. TaxGuru has published the earlier 2021 principal notification and the 6 April 2026 amendment concerning Central Food Safety Officers.
