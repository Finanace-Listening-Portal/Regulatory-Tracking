The FAQs explain that restrictions on trading while possessing UPSI extend to securities beyond equity shares and apply to persons possessing UPSI irrespective of their designation. They address SDD maintenance by listed companies, intermediaries and fiduciaries, including internal information sharing, cloud-based systems and record retention. Clarifications concerning trading plans cover the 120-day cooling-off period, price limits, permissible deviations, multiple plans and pre-clearance requirements. The FAQs also discuss disclosure obligations arising from securities transactions exceeding ₹10 lakh, corporate actions, transactions involving immediate relatives and ESOP-related activities. They distinguish between ESOP exercise, cashless exercise involving market sales, off-market transfers and transactions undertaken during trading-window closure. Further, the FAQs explain the applicability of six-month contra-trade restrictions, including their treatment in relation to pledges, securities lending, gifts, debt securities and corporate actions. Additional guidance covers the identification of designated persons, disclosure of relatives, responsibilities of compliance officers, promoter groups, foreign employees and the approval authority for compliance officers’ trades. The compilation provides a consolidated reference for listed companies, directors, compliance officers, designated persons, intermediaries and fiduciaries seeking to understand their obligations under the insider trading framework.
