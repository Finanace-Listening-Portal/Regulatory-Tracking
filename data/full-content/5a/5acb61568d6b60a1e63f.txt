IDFC First Bank and Bank of Baroda led the percentage gains, while index heavyweights HDFC Bank and State Bank of India (SBI) contributed the most to the upward momentum. The index opened marginally higher at 62,081.30 compared to its previous close of 62,038.65. Following a brief dip to an intraday low of 61,951.10, sustained buying interest lifted the index by 1,024.35 points, or 1.65 per cent, to close at a new record high and 52-week peak of 63,063.00.All 14 constituents of the banking gauge ended in positive territory, with total traded volume reaching 3,812.68 lakh shares and turnover standing at Rs 12,274.61 crore. The rally in financials also helped lift the broader benchmark Nifty 50 by 286.35 points, or 1.14 per cent, to close at 25,514.30. Advt Thursday's surge built directly on the momentum established earlier in the week on Tuesday, November 17, when Nifty Bank crossed the 62,000 milestone for the first time in its history to close at 62,273.25. Following a brief consolidation on Wednesday, November 18, bulls returned in force on Thursday to propel the index past 63,000.Heavyweights power the index upwardIndex heavyweights did the heavy lifting to propel Nifty Bank past 63,000, led by HDFC Bank and State Bank of India, which emerged as the top two index movers in terms of points contributed.Largest private sector lender HDFC Bank rose 1.69 per cent to close at Rs 821.80, acting as the single largest contributor to the index's point gain. State-owned banking giant SBI followed closely, advancing 1.85 per cent (Rs 21.00) to settle at Rs 1,156.00, recording the highest turnover among banking stocks at Rs 3,044.87 crore.Other major private lenders also posted solid advances. Kotak Mahindra Bank climbed 1.65 per cent to close at Rs 503.00, while Axis Bank gained 1.53 per cent to end at Rs 1,380.80. ICICI Bank edged up 0.47 per cent to close at Rs 1,608.90.Mid-sized and PSU banks lead percentage gainsWhile heavyweights drove the index points, mid-sized private lenders and public sector banks topped the percentage gainers' chart.IDFC First Bank was the best-performing stock on the index, surging 4.37 per cent (Rs 3.60) to close at Rs 86.05. Public sector lenders extended their strong November momentum, with Bank of Baroda jumping 4.15 per cent to Rs 285.90 and Punjab National Bank (PNB) rising 3.53 per cent to Rs 154.85. Advt Canara Bank added 1.97 per cent to close at Rs 136.03, while IndusInd Bank and Federal Bank gained 1.29 per cent (Rs 923.65) and 1.15 per cent (Rs 364.50), respectively. Union Bank of India rose 1.02 per cent to Rs 210.00, Yes Bank gained 0.90 per cent to Rs 17.84, and AU Small Finance Bank finished 0.71 per cent higher at Rs 983.90.
