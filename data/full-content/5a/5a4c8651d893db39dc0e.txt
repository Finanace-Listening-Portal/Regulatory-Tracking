It exercises powers under Sections 7, 8 and 21 of the Union Territory Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, read with Sections 15, 16 and 148 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. The amendments take effect on 1 November 2026. The notification revises input tax credit conditions for hotel accommodation, restaurant services, railway catering and outdoor catering. It permits specified same-line-of-business input credits while restricting other credits. For hotel accommodation and outdoor catering, where an eligible input service attracts Union territory tax above 2.5%, the available credit is restricted to the amount calculated at 2.5%. Illustrations explain the restriction through hotel bookings and wedding catering arrangements. New entries address passenger transportation by electrically operated vehicles and renting of electric vehicles for passenger or goods transportation where battery-charging costs form part of the consideration. These entries provide a 2.5% Union territory tax option subject to specified ITC restrictions and an alternative 9% rate. Under the 2.5% option, eligible same-line-business input service credit is capped at the equivalent of 2.5% where the supplier charges a higher rate. [Please refer to Explanation No. (iv)] Provided further that where the supplier of input service in the same line of business charges Union territory tax at a rate higher than 2.5%, credit of input tax charged on the input service in the same line of business in excess of the tax paid or payable at the rate of 2.5%, shall not be taken.
