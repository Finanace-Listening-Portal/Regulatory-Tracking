Gross domestic revenue increased 15.8% to Rs 1,59,044 crore, while gross import revenue rose 42.2% to Rs 72,246 crore. Total refunds amounted to Rs 27,934 crore, up 6.5% from Rs 26,225 crore a year earlier. After refunds, net GST revenue stood at Rs 2,03,356 crore, compared with Rs 1,61,900 crore in October 2025, an increase of 25.6%. Net domestic revenue was Rs 1,45,037 crore, and net customs GST revenue was Rs 58,319 crore. For the April–October comparison shown in the release, gross GST revenue reached Rs 14,77,569 crore, up 13.2%, and net GST revenue reached Rs 12,69,486 crore, up 12.6%. The accompanying tables provide state- and Union Territory-wise SGST figures before and after IGST settlement, state-wise domestic revenue growth and jurisdiction-wise collection details. These tables distinguish the different measures of collections and should not be treated as interchangeable. The release expressly cautions that the figures are provisional and may change on finalisation.
