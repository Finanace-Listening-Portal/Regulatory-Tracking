RBI/2026-27/300 dated October 26, 2026, has assigned and reviewed Lead Bank responsibilities following the re-organisation of districts in the National Capital Territory of Delhi. The Delhi Government’s Gazette Notification No. F.223/ SDM-II (HQ)/ Land/2025/285 dated December 25, 2025, created three new districts—Old Delhi, Central North and Outer North—and Shahdara district ceased to exist. For the newly created districts, Canara Bank has been assigned Old Delhi with District Working Code 03E, State Bank of India has been assigned Central North with code 03F, and Punjab National Bank has been assigned Outer North with code 03G. Each new code begins with numeral zero, followed by numeral three and the respective alphabet. RBI has also revised assignments for two existing districts: Central, with District Working Code 358, moves from Canara Bank to State Bank of India, while Northwest, with code 362, moves from Punjab National Bank to Union Bank of India. Since Shahdara district has ceased to exist, its Lead Bank allocation to Bank of Baroda, carrying District Working Code 369 under circular RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No.12/02.08.001/2013-14 dated July 11, 2013, stands revoked. Existing Lead Bank responsibilities in the remaining districts of Delhi continue unchanged until further communication.
