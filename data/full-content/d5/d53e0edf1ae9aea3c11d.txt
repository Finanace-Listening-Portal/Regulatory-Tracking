November 2026 has turned out to be a fabulous month for Indian equities, with nearly 200 out of the Nifty 500 stocks gaining more than 10 per cent each as of Thursday, shows ACE Equity data. Nischal Jain, Quant Researcher at PhonePe Invest says that market sentiment shifted rapidly on the back of converging domestic and global catalysts including record foreign portfolio investors buying in November. "This liquidity wave was reinforced by global rotation away from volatile, crowded artificial intelligence and US technology trades toward markets with clearer domestic demand, alongside resilient Q2 Nifty 50 earnings growth, dip in Brent crude and a steadier rupee," said Jain. Among individual stocks, The Jammu & Kashmir Bank, Asahi India Glass and Motilal Oswal Financial Services were the top Nifty 500 gainers, up over 30 per cent each.
