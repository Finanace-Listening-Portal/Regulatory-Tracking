While general depositors can enjoy rates of up to 7.25%, senior citizens benefit even more, with rates peaking at 7.50% for the same duration. Notably, City Union Bank and Karnataka Bank offer competitive options. It's advisable for depositors to check the latest rates before making any decisions on fixed deposits.
