Circular Department: Download Ref No: NSE/CML/2023/16 Date: February 16, 2023 To, All Listed Entities All Issuers who propose to list specified securities, Subject: Introduction of Issue Summary Document (ISD) and dissemination of issue advertisements. SEBI vide Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/29 dated February 15, 2023 has introduced filing of Issue Summary Document (ISD) in Extensible Business Reporting Language (XBRL) format and dissemination of issue advertisements on website of the Exchange. A copy of the aforesaid circular is available on the NSE website (https://www.nseindia.com/companies- listing/circular-for-listed-companies-equity-market) for your reference. You are requested to please take note of the same and ensure compliance. Yours faithfully, For National Stock Exchange of India Limited Prakash Kelkar Manager Encl: SEBI Circular
