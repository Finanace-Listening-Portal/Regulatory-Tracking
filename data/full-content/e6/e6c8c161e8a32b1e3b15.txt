Check - TOP GAINERS NSE | TOP LOSERS NSE 'Q2 strengthens fundamental case' Sahil Kapoor, executive director, market strategist and head of products, DSP Mutual Fund, said that private banks are probably one of the clearest cases where business performance and stock performance have diverged. The latest quarter strengthened the fundamental case. "Major private banks generated around 19.8 per cent Y-o-Y profit growth in Q2. Credit grew around 19.6 per cent and deposits around 21 per cent. Asset quality remains extremely strong. Axis grew advances by 23 per cent, ICICI by around 30 per cent, HDFC Bank by around 16 per cent, and Kotak Mahindra Bank by around 25 per cent," he said. What has held the sector back is margins and relative expectations. NIMs fell in Q2, partly because banks raised large FCNR(B) deposits that had not yet been fully deployed. "As that liquidity gets deployed into loans or replaces expensive deposits, part of this temporary drag can reverse," he said. SBI Securities favours private banks Sunny Agrawal, head - fundamental research at SBI Securities, said that strong systemic credit growth of 19.4 per cent Y-o-Y as per latest fortnightly RBI update, across segments, positive margins outlook underpinned by repo rate hikes, sufficient liquidity aided by FCNR (B) deposit accretion, lower slippages resulting in benign asset quality are some of the reasons for the recent banking sector rally. Further, the valuations had turned attractive over the past quarters amid a downbeat market environment, especially the large cap private sector banks which have been driving majority of the gains in the recent Bank Nifty rally. "Going ahead, we are constructive on large private banks (ICICI, AXIS, HDFC), SMIDs (DCB, KVB, CUBK).
