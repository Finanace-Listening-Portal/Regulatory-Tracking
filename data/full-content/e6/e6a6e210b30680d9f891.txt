No. IRDAI/PP&GR/ORD/MISC/154/11/2026 dated 23 November 2026, has reconstituted the five-member Advisory Committee responsible for reviewing the performance of the Insurance Ombudsman system and examining measures to improve its institutional efficiency. The Committee will be chaired by Shri Sriram Taranikanti, IAS (Retd.), with Shri Parmod Kumar Arora, former Whole Time Member of IRDAI; Shri Atul Sahai, former Insurance Ombudsman and former Chairman and Managing Director of New India Assurance Company Limited; Ms. Pushpa Girimaji, consumer rights advocate; and Ms. Mandakini Balodhi, Director (Insurance), Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, serving as members. Ms. Balodhi represents the Central Government. The Head of the Policyholders’ Protection and Grievance Redressal Department of IRDAI will serve as the Committee’s convener. Its terms of reference encompass reviewing the performance of the Insurance Ombudsman and considering matters aimed at enhancing the efficiency of the institution. The Committee will have a tenure of two years, ordinarily meet at least twice annually and submit an annual report to IRDAI for review and any further action considered necessary. Three members will constitute the quorum. The Chairperson and members will be eligible for travel, boarding and lodging expenses, together with sitting fees at par with other committees constituted by IRDAI. The reconstitution establishes an institutional mechanism for periodic oversight and evaluation of the Insurance Ombudsman system, which operates within the framework of the Insurance Ombudsman Rules, 2017, including their provisions concerning advisory review. The order has been issued with the approval of the competent authority. INSURANCE REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY OF INDIA Ref.
