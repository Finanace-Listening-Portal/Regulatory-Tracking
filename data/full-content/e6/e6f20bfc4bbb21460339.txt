And when will the revised Basel III framework take effect? RBI said intermediate transition scalars have been in effect since April 1, 2024.How has the treatment of the trading book changed?RBI has removed the separate instructions on the definition of the trading book from the final Directions. Advt The central bank said its Investment Directions already provide a clearly identifiable trading book under the Held for Trading (HFT) accounting sub-classification. The final Directions therefore make a reference to the Investment Directions instead of separately defining the trading book.What has changed for forex risk?RBI has incorporated revised instructions relating to the Net Open Position and forex risk capital charge.The changes are based on revised instructions contained in the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Tenth Amendment Directions, 2026.What is the change in interest-rate risk capital requirements?The specific-risk tables for interest-rate risk have been revised.RBI said the revised tables align with the guidelines of the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision and provide a more concise treatment.How will debt mutual funds and ETFs in the trading book be treated?RBI has revised the capital treatment for debt mutual funds and exchange-traded funds held in the trading book.The revised treatment is intended to ensure that capital computation is based on the underlying risk drivers, while also providing sufficient guardrails.What has changed for positions hedged through credit derivatives? Advt RBI has revised the instructions for the specific-risk capital requirement for positions hedged by credit derivatives.The revised treatment includes positions hedged through total return swaps, where such swaps are permitted under the Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Credit Derivatives) Directions, 2026.When will the new Directions come into effect?The final Directions will take effect from April 1, 2027.RBI said the implementation date provides sufficient lead time for banks. It also noted that intermediate transition scalars have already been in effect since April 1, 2024 to facilitate a smooth transition.
