} } Listen to This ArticlePunjab National Bank (PNB) share price movement Share price of Punjab National Bank (PNB) extended its north-bound journey, soaring 5 per cent to ₹151.60 on the BSE in Tuesday’s intraday deals amid heavy volume. In the process, the stock also hit an over eight-year high. The stock price of the public sector bank quoted higher for the sixth straight trading day, surging 21 per cent during the period. In the past month, it zoomed 31 per cent. PNB share price quoted at its highest level since February 2018. The stock hit a record high of ₹279 on November 9, 2010. At 12:44 PM, PNB traded 4 per cent higher at ₹149.75, against a 0.81 per cent rise in the BSE Sensex. The average trading volume at the counter jumped 1.5 times with a combined 46.59 million shares changing hands on the NSE and BSE. Why has PNB outperformed the market? PNB's global net interest margin (NIM) expanded 8bp quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) to 2.58 per cent in the July to September 2026 quarter (Q2FY27), while domestic NIM expanded 10bp to 2.74 per cent, reflecting benefits from balance sheet optimization and improved lending yields. The management expects a further 7–8bp/9–10bp improvement in global/domestic NIM, respectively, supported by ongoing rate hikes, corporate portfolio repricing, a higher proportion of relatively high-yielding retail, agriculture, and MSME (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (RAM) advances, and optimization of funding costs. The bank aims to improve blended corporate yields by 40–45bp from the earlier level of 7.28 per cent, targeting yields above 7.5 per cent, with 20–25bp improvement anticipated in Q3FY27.
