Issued under sub-section (1) of section 43, in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court of Rajasthan, the amendment substitutes serial number 22 and its corresponding entries in the table of principal Notification S.O. 372(E), dated 5 February 2016. Under the substituted entries, the Court of Special Judge (CBI) No. 3, Jaipur, is assigned the districts falling within the jurisdiction of the Rajasthan High Court Bench, Jaipur. The Court of Special Judge (CBI), Jodhpur, is assigned the districts falling within the jurisdiction of the Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur. The intervening entries specify that the Courts of Special Judge (CBI) No. 2, Jaipur; No. 1, Jaipur; and No. 2, Jaipur, respectively, will hear only cases transferred by the Rajasthan High Court. Court No. 2, Jaipur, appears twice in the supplied notification table, and both entries are retained below. The notification thus distinguishes the territorial jurisdiction assigned to the specified Jaipur and Jodhpur courts from the limited role of the other listed Jaipur courts in hearing transferred cases. Its closing note records that the principal notification was last amended through S.O. 5082(E), dated 16 September 2026. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 23rd October, 2026 S.O. 5757(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 43 of the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 (15 of 2003) and in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court of Rajasthan, the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section(ii) vide number S.O.372(E), dated the 5th February, 2016, namely :- In the said notification, in the TABLE, for serial number 22 and the entries relating thereto, the following Sl. No. and entries shall be substituted, namely :- (1) (2) (3) (4) “22 Rajasthan Court of Special Judge, (CBI) No.3, Jaipur The districts falling in jurisdiction of Rajasthan High Court Bench, Jaipur. Court of Special Judge, (CBI) No.2, Jaipur Will hear transferred cases only as transferred by the Rajasthan High Court. Court of Special Judge, (CBI) No.1, Jaipur Will hear transferred cases only as transferred by the Rajasthan High Court. Court of Special Judge, (CBI) No.2, Jaipur Will hear transferred cases only as transferred by the Rajasthan High Court. Court of Special Judge, (CBI), Jodhpur The districts falling in jurisdiction of Rajasthan High Court, Jodhpur.” [F. No. C-18015/6/2024-Ad.ED] RAJEEV LOCHAN, Under Secy. Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 372(E), dated the 5th February, 2016 and lastly amended vide number S.O 5082(E), dated the 16th September 2026.
