Allegedly, 187 transactions involved 36 separate accounts. RTGS services have since been restored without further complications. The bank said a technical issue was observed in the interface environment of its localised Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) server, while its core banking system (CBS) remains safe and secure.Central Bank of India also confirmed that RTGS services have been fully restored across all channels.The bank’s clarification comes amid media reports that the Central Bank of India had reported 187 unauthorised transactions worth Rs 13.97 crore from its settlement account with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). These transactions reportedly took place from October 15 to 17. The bank also confirmed that RTGS services had resumed fully across all channels.Are Central Bank of India customers affected?Central Bank of India in its message to its customer stated, “We wish to clarify that there was a technical issue observed in the interface environment of the localised RTGS server maintained at the bank’s end and no customer account is impacted. Also our CBS system remains entirely secure and safe.”Rs 13.97 crore in unauthorised transactions reportedAccording to media reports, Central Bank of India reported unauthorised transactions worth Rs 13.97 crore from its settlement account maintained with the RBI. Advt The complaint reportedly led to the registration of a first information report (FIR) at Mumbai's Nodal Cyber Police Station. The complaint was reportedly filed by the bank's general manager (treasury).As per the reports, 187 unauthorised transfers took place over three days. The complaint details six transactions on October 15, followed by 88 on October 16 and 93 on October 17.The money was reportedly transferred to 36 bank accounts.
