Summary: The Tax Compliance Tracker for October 2026 sets out important Income Tax and GST compliance requirements falling due during the month. The Income Tax section covers TDS and TCS payments, quarterly statements, prescribed forms and reports under the Income-tax Act, 1961 and Income-tax Act, 2025, along with return filing and various certificates, audit reports and declarations due on October 7, October 15, October 30 and October 31, 2026. The GST section covers GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-5, GSTR-5A, GSTR-6, GSTR-7, GSTR-8, GSTR-11, IFF under the QRMP scheme, PMT-06 and CMP-08, together with the applicable October 2026 due dates. Taxpayers, deductors, collectors, non-resident taxpayers, input service distributors, e-commerce operators, UIN holders and composition taxpayers should review the applicable requirements and complete the relevant filings or payments within the prescribed timelines.
