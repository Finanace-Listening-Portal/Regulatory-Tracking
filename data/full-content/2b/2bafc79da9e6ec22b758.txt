Summary: The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), through Notification No. 95/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 20 November 2026, published as S.O. 6328(E), has revised the routes specified for the Dharchula land customs station on the India–Nepal frontier. The notification was issued under clauses (b) and (c) of section 7(1) of the Customs Act, 1962, which empower the Board to appoint land customs stations for clearance of goods and specify routes for the movement of goods. It substitutes the entry in column (4) against item (6), Dharchula, under serial number 6 of the table to Notification No. 63/1994-Customs (N.T.) dated 21 November 1994. The substituted entry lists two routes. The first is the road connecting Dharchula in Pithoragarh district, India, with Darchula in Nepal. The second is the Chharchhum motorable bridge connecting Malla Chharchhum Village in Dharchula, India, with Ashigadama in Darchula Headquarters, Nepal. The bridge is thus listed alongside the existing road under the same Dharchula land customs station entry. The Gazette note records that the principal notification was originally published as S.O. 830(E) on 21 November 1994 and was last amended by Notification No. 43/2026-Customs (N.T.), published as S.O. 2256(E) on 6 May 2026. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) Notification No. 95/2026 – Customs (N.T.) | Dated: 20th November, 2026 S.O. 6328(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (b) and (c) of sub-section (1) of section 7 of the Customs Act, 1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), No. 63/1994-Customs (N.T.) dated the 21st November, 1994, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 830 (E), dated the 21st November, 1994, namely: – In the said notification, in the TABLE, against serial number 6 relating to land frontier of Nepal, against item (6) in column (3) relating to Dharchula, for the corresponding entry in column (4), the following entry shall be substituted, namely: – (3) (4) (6)Dharchula “(a) Road connecting Dharchula in district Pithoragarh in India and Darchula in Nepal. (b) Chharchhum motorable Bridge for connecting Malla Chharchhum Village, Dharchula, India with Ashigadama in Darchula Headquarters, Nepal.” [F. No. 552/01/2023-LC] INDRAJIT PANDA, Under Secy. Note: The principal notification No. 63/1994-Customs (N.T.), dated 21st November, 1994 was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, vide number S.O. 830(E), dated the 21st November, 1994 and was last amended, vide notification No. 43/2026-Customs (N.T.) published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub­section (ii), vide number S.O. 2256(E), dated the 06th May, 2026.
