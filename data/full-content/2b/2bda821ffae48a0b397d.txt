Articles 2 min read Bajaj Finance to launch share sale worth up to Rs 11,700 cr this week: Report Bajaj Finance Ltd. is gearing up for a major share sale aimed at raising an impressive 117 billion rupees. The initiative seeks to draw in institutional investors, with the specifics of the offering currently being finalized. Following the completion of investor roadshows, a qualified institutions placement may take place soon. Advt Deliberations are ongoing and details including the size and timing of the offering could still change, the people said. A representative for JPMorgan declined to commetn. Representatives for Bajaj Finance and the other banks didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.The company’s board on Oct. 1 approved plans to raise as much as 175 billion rupees from a 117 billion-rupee QIP and 58 billion rupees from warrants. Bajaj Finance on Nov. 10 raised about 58 billion rupees by allotting 57.43 million warrants to its promoter and holding company, Bajaj Finserv Ltd., at 1,010 rupees apiece, according to an exchange filing.India has been one of the world’s busiest markets for equity fundraising this year, with companies raising about $50.25 billion from equity offerings so far, compared with $58.78 billion for all of last year. Major transactions this year include Jio Platforms’ $3.1 billion IPO, National Stock Exchange of India Ltd.’s $2.4 billion listing and a $3.3 billion share sale by Life Insurance Corp. of India.Bajaj Finance shares have gained nearly 13% over the past month, broadly in line with a 12.5% advance in the benchmark Nifty 50 Index.IFR on Nov. 10 reported that Bajaj Finance plans to launch share sale as early as this week.
