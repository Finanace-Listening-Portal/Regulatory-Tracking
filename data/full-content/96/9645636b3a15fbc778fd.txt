Summary: LLP Form 8, also known as the Statement of Account and Solvency, is an important annual compliance requirement for Limited Liability Partnerships under Section 34 of the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, read with Rule 24 of the LLP Rules, 2009. The form records the financial position of an LLP through information relating to assets, liabilities, income, expenditure and the prescribed declaration of solvency. Every LLP is generally required to comply with the applicable annual filing requirements, irrespective of whether it has earned profits, incurred losses or undertaken limited business activities during the financial year. For LLPs following the financial year from 1 April to 31 March, Form 8 must ordinarily be filed within 30 days from the end of six months of the financial year. Accordingly, the normal filing due date for FY 2025–26 is 30 October 2026, subject to any applicable extension or change notified by the authorities. Before filing, designated partners should verify the LLP’s books of account and ensure that the financial statements, partners’ contributions, solvency declaration, outstanding payments to Micro and Small Enterprises and contingent liabilities are correctly disclosed wherever applicable. Form 8 is submitted electronically through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal. The filing requires appropriate digital signatures and professional certification wherever prescribed. Any applicable audit requirements must also be examined before submission. Failure to file within the prescribed time may result in additional filing fees and statutory penalties, besides creating difficulties in completing other regulatory formalities. Proper maintenance of accounts, advance preparation of disclosures and timely electronic filing help LLPs maintain accurate statutory records and avoid unnecessary compliance costs. LLPs are also required to maintain proper financial records and complete their annual compliance filings within the prescribed time. One such important compliance is Form 8, also known as the Statement of Account and Solvency. It provides information about the financial position of an LLP and includes a declaration regarding its solvency. Even if an LLP has limited business activity, its designated partners should ensure that the applicable annual filing requirements are met. What Is LLP Form 8? Form 8 is an annual filing required under Section 34 of the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, read with Rule 24 of the LLP Rules, 2009. Through this form, an LLP reports its financial information, including details of its assets, liabilities, income and expenditure, along with the prescribed statement of solvency. In simple terms, Form 8 helps the Registrar understand the financial position of an LLP and whether it has made the required declaration regarding its ability to meet its liabilities. Who Is Required to File Form 8? Every LLP registered under the LLP Act is generally required to file Form 8 annually, irrespective of whether it has earned a profit or incurred a loss. An LLP should not assume that it is exempt from filing merely because it has not started business, has limited transactions or has remained inactive during the financial year. Its filing obligations should be checked and completed as applicable. What Is the Due Date for Filing Form 8? Form 8 must generally be filed within 30 days from the end of six months of the financial year. For an LLP following the financial year from 1 April to 31 March, the due date is 30th October every year. For example, Form 8 for the financial year 2025–26 is ordinarily due by 30th October 2026, subject to any applicable extension or change notified by the authorities. What Information Is Required in Form 8? Before filing Form 8, the LLP should prepare and verify its financial records. The form generally covers: Details of the LLP, including its LLP Identification Number (LLPIN). Statement of assets and liabilities. Statement of income and expenditure. Details of partners’ contributions, as applicable. Declaration of solvency. Disclosure of outstanding payments to Micro and Small Enterprises under the MSME law. Details of contingent liabilities, where applicable. The information reported in Form 8 should be consistent with the LLP’s books of account and finalised financial records. Any applicable audit and certification requirements should also be checked before submission. How Is Form 8 Filed? Form 8 is filed electronically through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal. The LLP should first ensure that its financial information is ready and that the details entered in the form are accurate. The form must be digitally signed by the appropriate authorised persons and certified by a practising professional wherever required under the applicable rules and MCA filing instructions. Before submitting the form, designated partners should carefully review the figures, declarations and supporting information to minimise the possibility of errors or compliance issues. What Happens If Form 8 Is Filed Late? Missing the due date can result in additional filing fees and statutory penalties, as applicable. Delayed filings may also create difficulties when the LLP needs to complete other regulatory formalities or provide evidence of its compliance status. It is therefore advisable not to wait until the last day. Preparing the accounts and checking the relevant disclosures in advance can help avoid unnecessary delays and costs. Conclusion Form 8 is an important annual compliance requirement for LLPs. It is not simply a formality; it records the LLP’s financial position and its statement of solvency. Designated partners should ensure that the LLP’s accounts are properly maintained, the applicable disclosures are complete and the form is filed within the prescribed timeline. Timely compliance helps an LLP maintain accurate regulatory records and avoid avoidable penalties. ****** Disclaimer: The content of this article is intended to provide a general guide to the subject matter, Specialist advice should be sought about your specific circumstances.
