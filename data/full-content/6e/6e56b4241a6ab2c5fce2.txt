Articles 1 min read Aditya Birla Capital Q2 profit rises 38% to Rs 1,288-cr Aditya Birla Capital reported an impressive 38% profit increase for the September quarter of FY27, with profit after tax soaring to Rs 1,288 crore, up from Rs 933.52 crore year-over-year. Operational revenues also climbed significantly, reaching Rs 14,003 crore. Lending assets under management witnessed a robust 32% growth to Rs 2.34 lakh crore, while shares gained 1.75% on the Bombay Stock Exchange.
