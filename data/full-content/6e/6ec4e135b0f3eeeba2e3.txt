Traders said the cutoff yields likely came in above the Reserve Bank of India's expectations.Here are some details:Last week, the RBI had sold 91-day notes at a 5.73% yield and 182-day and 364-day papers at 6.29% and 6.49%, respectivelyA Reuters poll on Wednesday had forecast cutoff yields for the three notes at 5.85%, 6.42% and 6.60% Advt "Demand may have been poorer than anticipated and with the government not in serious need of funds, cancellation was a better signal," a trader with a private bank said"Markets are expecting at least another 75 basis points of rate hikes and people are now on the negative side, which is leading to more cautious bidding for bills," said Avnish Jain, CIO for fixed income at Canara Robeco Mutual Fund.In the five weeks to October 14, the cutoff yields on the three maturities jumped by 64 bps, 83 bps and 69 bps, respectivelyThe spread between the 364-day notes and RBI policy rate had jumped to 99 bps last week, a four-year highThe RBI cancelled the 182-day and 364-day auctions in June, while it rejected bids for all three tenors at the end of March($1 = 94.6350 Indian rupees)(Reporting by Dharamraj Dhutia; Editing by Sonia Cheema) Reuters Published On Oct 21, 2026 at 04:11 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
