The shift is changing the role of software developers, with some moving from writing code themselves to supervising AI agents, while banks look to achieve higher productivity and accelerate technology development.Wells Fargo has indicated that it is already making decisions to use more autonomous coding agents instead of hiring additional employees or engaging contractors. Productivity gains from agentic AI could increase further as developers adopt these tools, raising the risk of further deflation in the base business of IT services companies, according to a report by Kotak Institutional Equities. Advt The trend has implications for Indian IT services companies. Aggregate BFSI/BFS revenues of Indian IT services firms grew 6.1 per cent year on year in the September 2026 quarter, even as technology spending growth was strong across five of the six largest US banks. The report warns that rising productivity could put further pressure on the base business of IT services companies.Most financial services firms have reported productivity improvements of 20-40 per cent in software development. The report says the impact of these gains on IT services companies has not changed significantly over the past six months, despite improvements in AI model capabilities. However, productivity benefits could increase in the near term as developers adopt agentic software development tools.DNB has noted that developers are increasingly supervising agents that write code rather than writing the code themselves. Citizens Financial has said its focus is shifting towards shaping solutions and validating output. PNC indicated that using AI agents could provide productivity improvements of five to 10 times.SaaS impactThe shift could also change banks' technology sourcing decisions. PNC suggested that the high productivity possible from agentic AI could benefit banks choosing to build software in-house, pointing to the possibility of a shift from software-as-a-service to custom applications. Advt Visa said its stablecoin platform was designed, built and shipped in seven weeks with 12 people, using its agentic tool chain. The company also said 60 per cent of its global product development and engineering teams had adopted agentic AI tools, with a target of reaching 100 per cent adoption in 2026.Financial services firms expect AI efficiencies to reduce incremental headcount requirements for business growth and backfilling attrition. In technology functions, the focus is on limiting new hiring and using excess capacity to accelerate product development and innovation, rather than reducing existing headcount. Some firms pursuing large cost-saving programmes have also indicated workforce reductions in technology functions. Advt Most financial services firms are currently directing productivity gains from AI adoption in software development back into technology to support faster product development, innovation and modernisation. A continuation of this reinvestment as productivity benefits increase would be important for IT services companies.The report expects Persistent to lead growth among mid-tier Indian IT services firms in BFS, followed by Coforge, while noting that insourcing and the potential deflationary impact of AI adoption in mortgage business process outsourcing could pose challenges.
