Articles 1 min read IndusInd Bank ends ex-CEO Kathpalia's employment, seeks to recover pay IndusInd Bank has ended the employment of Sumant Kathpalia following a disciplinary investigation into misconduct. After misreporting accounting details led to significant financial losses, disciplinary proceedings were initiated against him. The bank's board decided to terminate his employment and pursue recovery of his compensation since March 2020. Concurrently, the bank experienced a return to double-digit growth in loans and deposits. IndusInd's shares have shown recovery, rising by 16% this year.
