The theme of the conference was ‘Role of Quality of Expenditure, Effective Cash and Prudent Debt Management in Enhancing States’ Fiscal Sustainability’. Addressing the participants, the Governor emphasised the importance of maintaining expenditure quality to ensure the long-term fiscal health of the states, the RBI said in a press release. Malhotra emphasised the need for states to adopt prudent borrowing strategies and efficient cash management practices.
