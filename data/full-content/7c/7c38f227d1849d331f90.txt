Commodity trading refers to trading undertaken by a unit of an International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) under a commodity trading contract, involving shipment of goods from one country to another or within a foreign country, with or without touching Indian ports. The notification defines commodities as goods capable of delivery and trading but excludes actionable claims, money, currency, securities, species or specimens covered under the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES), and items specified under Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies (SCOMET). The notified activity remains subject to applicable Indian laws, and the notification takes effect from its publication in the Official Gazette. Through Notification S.O. 6196(E), dated 13 November 2026, the Central Government has separately notified ‘commodity trading contract’ as a financial product under Section 3(1)(d)(vi) of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019. A commodity trading contract means a contract under which an IFSC unit trades a commodity with another party. The notification adopts the same definition of commodity and exclusions specified in S.O. 6195(E). Together, the two notifications formally recognise commodity trading activities and their underlying contracts within the statutory framework governing financial services and financial products in IFSCs. This provides a defined legal basis for the specified cross-border commodity trading activities undertaken by IFSC units, subject to applicable regulatory and legal requirements. Both notifications are effective from their respective dates of publication in the Official Gazette. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Economic Affairs) NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 13th November, 2026 S.O. 6195(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-clause (xiv) of clause (e) of sub-section (1) of section 3 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 (50 of 2019), the Central Government hereby notifies ‘commodity trading’ as financial service. Explanation. – For the purpose of this notification, – (a) “commodity” shall mean goods of every description, which are capable of being delivered and traded, but excludes:- (i) actionable claims, money, currency, securities; (ii) any species or specimens listed under the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora; and (iii) any items specified under Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies by the Central Government; (b) “commodity trading” shall mean trading of a commodity under a commodity trading contract by a unit of an International Financial Services Centre, involving its shipment from one country to another, or within a foreign country, with or without touching Indian ports. 2. The activity of commodity trading shall be subject to the provisions of the applicable laws in force in India. 3. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. [F. No. 3/15/2022-EM] ALOK TIWARI, Jt.
