Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); In re Candid Guard Services Pvt. Ltd. (NCLT Cuttack) Summary: The NCLT Cuttack Bench, in the matter of Candid Guard Services Pvt. Ltd., ordered liquidation of the corporate debtor under section 33 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. The resolution professional submitted that the company had been admitted into CIRP on 9 September 2025. The first publication of Form G on 9 November 2025 did not produce a positive result. Its republication on 27 March 2026 attracted four expressions of interest. Praveen Singh submitted a resolution plan, which the CoC considered at its eighth meeting on 3 June 2026 and requested him to revise. No revised proposal was received, and the submitted plan was put to vote along with an agenda for liquidation if the plan was not approved. According to the applicant’s counsel, the GST Department, holding 99.66% of the CoC voting share, rejected the plan and approved liquidation through its email dated 21 June 2026. The ninth CoC meeting followed on 22 June 2026 to take the requisite decisions and approvals. The Tribunal appointed Sanjit Kumar Das as liquidator and directed him to take custody of the company’s assets, preserve and protect its properties, issue a public notice and discharge the statutory duties of liquidation. The powers of the board and key managerial personnel were to vest in the liquidator, with the company’s personnel required to assist him. The order also prescribed restrictions on instituting proceedings, filing the order with the Registrar of Companies, determination of the liquidator’s fee and submission of a preliminary report. The resolution professional was discharged and directed to hand over the company’s documents, records and access and control of assets to the new liquidator within two days of receiving the order. The Tribunal separately disposed of the application seeking recall of the ₹50,000 cost order as infructuous because the cost had been deposited. It allowed the application seeking recall of the ex parte order dated 7 August 2026, vacated that order and permitted a reply and rejoinder in IA (IB) No. 129/CB/2026. It also issued directions concerning replies in the other pending applications, including the application filed by the Regional P.F. Commissioner, Raipur, and listed those matters for further consideration on 30 October 2026. IA (IB) (Liq) No. 1/CB/2026 This is an application filed under Section 33(1) of IBC for Liquidation by Prabhat Jain RP of Candid Guard Services Pvt. Ltd. Ld. Counsel Mr. Shashwat Kumar Rout appeared through VC for the Applicant. It is submitted by the Applicant that the CD was admitted in the CIRP vide order dated 09.09.2025 and the present applicant was appointed as IRP and constituted the CoC and carried out all actions in pursuance of ensuring successful resolution of the CD as required under the Code. The applicant issued Form G for the first time on 09.11.2025 which did not positive result. Subsequently, Form G was republished on 27.03.2026 inviting the expression of interest from PRAs and in response to which the applicant received four expression of interest. One of the PRAs namely Mr. Praveen Singh submitted a resolution plan which was considered by the Coc in its 8th meeting held on 03.06.2026 wherein the Resolution Applicant was requested to revise the plan proposal. However, no revised proposal was received from the Resolution Applicant and hence the plan submitted by the SRA was put to vote. The applicant along with Resolution to approve the Plan submitted by Mr. Praveen Singh also put forth the agenda for initiating liquidation of the CD in case the plan is not approved. The Ld. Counsel for the Applicants submits that the GST Department which holds 99.66% voting share in the CoC vide its e-mail dated 21.06.2026 rejected the plan and approved the initiation of liquidation of the CD. The Applicant subsequently held the 9th CoC meeting on 22.06.2026 to take decisions and approvals from the CoC as required under Regulation 39B of CIRP Regulation. 1. In light of the observations made above CANDID GUARD SERVICES Pv Ltd is Ordered to be Liquidated under section 33 of IBC,2016 and the following orders are passed: a. Mr Sanjit Kumar Das having Registration No. IBBI/IPA-002/IP-N-01174/2021-2022/13939, residing at Cuttack Odisha and having Contact No.- 8260389511, E-mail id – [email protected] is hereby appointed as the Liquidator. b. The Liquidator is directed to forthwith take into her custody all the assets, Properties, equipment, machineries, and actionable claims of the corporate debtor and take necessary steps to ensure preservation, protection security and maintenance of those properties as provided under section 35(1)(b) & (d) of IBC 2016. c. The Liquidator is directed to adhere to Section 33(1) (ii) & (iii) and discharge his powers and duties as specified under Section 35 to 41 of IBC, 2016 and meticulously adhere to the Rules and Regulations issued by IBBI in this regard from time to time. d. The Liquidator is directed to issue public Notice as contemplated under section 33(1) of the Code in one morning, English daily and in one morning regional language newspapers. e. All the powers of the Board of Directors of the Corporate Debtor and of its key managerial personnel, shall cease to exist in accordance with section 34(2) of the Code. These powers shall henceforth vest in the Liquidator. The personnel of the Corporate Debtor shall extend all assistance and cooperation to the Liquidator as may be required by him in the Liquidation process of the Corporate Debtor. f. On initiation of the Liquidation process but subject to section 52 of the Code, no suit or other legal proceeding shall be instituted by or against the Corporate Debtor, save and except the liberty to the liquidator to institute a suit or other legal proceeding on behalf of the Corporate Debtor with prior approval of this Adjudicating Authority, as provided in section 33(5) of the Code read with its proviso. g. In accordance with section 33(7) of the Code, this liquidation order shall be deemed to be a notice of discharge to the officers, employees and workmen of the Corporate Debtor, except to the extent of the business of the Corporate Debtor continued during the liquidation process by the liquidator. h. In terms of Section 33(1) (b) (iii), the Liquidator shall file a copy of this Order with the Registrar of Companies within whose jurisdiction the Corporate Debtor is registered. i. The fee of Liquidator to be determined as provided under Regulation 4 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation process) Regulation 2016. j. As per Regulation 13 of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulation,2016, the liquidator shall submit a preliminary report to the Adjudicating Authority within 75 days from the liquidation commencement date providing various details/information as mentioned in the said regulation. k. The Registry is directed to send e-mail copies of the order forthwith to all the parties and their Ld. Counsel and to ROC, Odisha for information and for taking necessary steps. 2. The Applicant is discharged from his duties as RP and is directed that all documents, books of accounts, official records, access and control of assets of the corporate debtor which he holds in his posseion in the capacity of RP shall hand it over to the newly appointed Liquidator within 2 days of receipt of this order. 3. The Registry is hereby directed to send e-mail copies of the order forthwith to all the parties and their counsel and the newly appointed Liquidator for information and for taking necessary steps. 4. Let the certified copy of the order be issued upon compliance with requisite formalities. 5. Accordingly, IA (IB) (Liq) No. 1/CB/2026 is Allowed and Disposed of.
