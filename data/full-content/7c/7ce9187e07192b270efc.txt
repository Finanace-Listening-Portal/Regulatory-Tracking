During the day, it jumped 3.98 per cent to Rs 1,117.80.At the NSE, the stock closed 3.35 per cent higher at Rs 1,115.The stock was the biggest gainer among the Sensex firms.A Special Committee of the board at its meeting held on Wednesday considered and approved authorising the opening of the issue to eligible qualified institutional buyers in accordance with the terms of the issue, Bajaj Finance said in a regulatory filing. Advt The panel also approved the floor price at Rs 992.25 per equity share, based on the pricing formula as prescribed under the Sebi ICDR Regulations, the non-banking financial company (NBFC) said.Pursuant to the shareholders' approval, accorded through a special resolution dated November 2, 2026, the company has been authorised to offer a discount of not more than 5 per cent on the floor price calculated for the issue, it said.On Tuesday, Bajaj Finance allotted 5.74 crore warrants to promoter Bajaj Finserv on a preferential basis for Rs 5,799 crore.
