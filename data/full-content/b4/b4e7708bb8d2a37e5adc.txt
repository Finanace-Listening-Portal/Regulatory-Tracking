NOTICE NoticeNo. 20260722-7 NoticeDate 22Jul2026 Category CircularsListedCompanies Segment Equity Department ListingCompliance-Monitoring Subject SEBI(ListingObligationsandDisclosureRequirements)(Second Amendment)Regulations,2026 Attachments SEBI_LODR_SecondAmendment_Regulations_2026 To All Listed Companies Dear Sir \ Madam The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued notification No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/312 dated July 10, 2026, regarding further amendment to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure requirements) Regulations, 2015. These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. The aforesaid notification is attached for reference. All Listed companies are requested to take note and comply accordingly. Jayshree Soni Deputy Vice President-Listing Compliance Shyam Bhagirath Manager-Listing Compliance
