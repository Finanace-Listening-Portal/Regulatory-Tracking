Articles 3 min read Mid, smallcap mutual funds see inflows jump in September, large-caps return after 2 months. Is investor confidence holding up despite market decline? Despite market corrections in September, small and midcap mutual funds recorded strong monthly inflows of Rs 9,038 crore and Rs 7,834 crore respectively. Largecap funds also returned to positive territory with Rs 859 crore in inflows, signaling strong investor confidence and strategic value buying across all capitalisations. Midcap funds received the second highest inflows of Rs 7,834 crore in September.Large cap funds, after witnessing outflows for two consecutive months, received an inflow of Rs 859 crore in September. Advt Also Read | AMFI Data: MF SIP inflows hit record high of Rs 32,490 crore in SeptemberHimanshu Srivastava, Principal, Manager Research, Morningstar Investment Research India, said together small and mid caps accounted for approximately 44% of total net equity inflows during September. The sustained interest in these categories, despite the correction in broader markets, suggests that investors continue to remain confident about the long-term growth potential of emerging businesses.Srivastava further said that the continued concentration of flows into mid and smallcap categories warrants attention, particularly given valuation concerns in parts of the broader market.How large, mid, and small caps performed in September?The Nifty 50 TRI declined 5.93% in September, Nifty Midcap 150 TRI slipped 5.93% and the Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI was down 3.03%.Largecap mutual funds on average lost 5.05% in September, with Franklin India Large Cap Fund losing the most at around 6.79% in the said time period. Taurus Large Cap Fund lost the lowest at around 1.81%. ICICI Pru Large Cap Fund, the largest large cap fund based on assets managed, lost 5.30%.Mid cap funds on average lost 5.20%. Mahindra Manulife Mid Cap Fund lost the most at around 7.24%, whereas Taurus Mid Cap Fund lost the lowest at around 2.12%. HDFC Mid Cap Fund, the largest midcap fund based on assets managed, lost 6.73%. Advt Small cap funds on average lost 2.04% in September. Edelweiss Small Cap Fund lost 5.13%, while Quant Small Cap Fund lost the lowest at around 0.80%. Four small cap funds gave positive returns, with Samco Small Cap Fund delivering the highest return of 6.11% in the said time period.Nirav Karkera, Head of Research and Fund Manager, W by Groww, said that strong flows into small and midcaps show investors are growing more comfortable with volatility and more confident in the riskier end of the market. Interest in largecaps is also returning. They offer a wider margin of safety today, and sectors like financial services, represented well in the pack, are garnering strong investor interest. Advt Another expert, Nitin Agrawal, CEO, Mutual Funds, InCred Money, said that the inflows in mid and small caps show the maturity of value buying across investor segments.Also Read | Equity mutual fund inflows jump 31% MoM to Rs 38,306 crore in September; smallcap funds log record collectionAgrawal further said that largecap turned positive at Rs 859 crore for the first time in three months, leading to investors finding value entry points in quality largecap stocks.
