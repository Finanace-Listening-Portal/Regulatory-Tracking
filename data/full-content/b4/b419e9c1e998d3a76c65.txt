Summary: SEBI’s public-issue filing list dated 9 November 2026 records an Updated Draft Red Herring Prospectus-I for SNVA Traveltech Limited, accompanied by a draft abridged prospectus dated 5 November 2026. The proposal is a 100% book-built offer for sale of up to 127,525,000 equity shares of face value Re 1, split equally between promoters Alok Kumar Singh and Vivek Kumar Singh. It contains no fresh issue, so the company will not receive offer proceeds other than the listing benefits described in the draft. The offer’s rupee size, price band, lot size, anchor date, opening date and closing date remain blank at UDRHP-I stage; the filing is not an open offer and does not permit an application today. SNVA operates a technology-driven, asset-light online travel agency model, including its Travomint platform and service-support channels, and reported consolidated revenue from operations of Rs 1,891.09 million and profit after tax of Rs 708.10 million for FY 2026 in the draft abridged prospectus. Those numbers must be read with concentration, travel-partner, subsidiary, geographic and technology risks. This article explains only verified draft-stage facts and does not predict valuation, subscription, listing performance or investment returns. Promoter Shareholding Context The draft abridged prospectus shows pre-offer holdings of 255,050,000 shares for Alok Kumar Singh, representing 50.00%, and 254,794,950 shares for Vivek Kumar Singh, representing 49.95%. Post-offer percentages are blank because the price band, actual subscription and allotment are not yet final. The OFS cap is a maximum, not confirmation that every offered share will be sold. SNVA Business Model and Travomint Revenue Engine SNVA describes itself as a technology-driven, asset-light online travel agency platform. Its service portfolio covers domestic and international flight bookings, hotel reservations, holiday packages, car rentals, airport transfers, travel insurance and ancillary airline-related services. The draft identifies Travomint as the digital platform used by customers who manage bookings independently, while service-support staff assist customers who prefer personalised support. With effect from 1 April 2025, management reports one segment: Travel Related Service Operations. As at 31 August 2026, the draft says the company served end customers across more than 50 countries, with subsidiaries in the UAE, United States, United Kingdom and Singapore and payment options in more than 150 currencies. These statements describe operational reach; they do not establish equal revenue contribution, regulatory permissions or profitability in every market. Partner and Ancillary Concentration The top five travel partners contributed 98.13% of FY 2026 revenue from operations and 99.10% for the three months ended 30 June 2026. The draft withholds partner names because consents were not received and confidentiality was to be preserved. Ancillary services are also meaningful: FY 2026 ancillary revenue was Rs 501.79 million, or 26.53% of revenue from operations. Class upgrades were the largest listed ancillary component for that year at Rs 239.64 million. High partner concentration can support scale while relationships remain stable, but it exposes the business to commercial-term changes, inventory withdrawal, API disruption and partner loss. Ancillary revenue can improve monetisation, yet it is sensitive to airline rules, customer behaviour, cancellation patterns and service availability. These are operating dependencies, not merely disclosure formalities. Restated Financials and Operating Indicators The draft abridged prospectus provides restated consolidated information for FY 2025 and FY 2026 and a consolidated three-month period ended 30 June 2026. FY 2024 is presented on a standalone basis, so a direct trend reading must account for the change in reporting perimeter. Revenue from operations rose from Rs 1,195.91 million in FY 2025 to Rs 1,891.09 million in FY 2026. Profit after tax increased from Rs 407.70 million to Rs 708.10 million, while EBITDA rose from Rs 527.43 million to Rs 898.85 million. For the three months ended 30 June 2026, the draft reports revenue from operations of Rs 512.68 million, EBITDA of Rs 268.55 million and profit after tax of Rs 234.37 million. A short interim period should not be annualised mechanically because travel demand, cancellations, partner settlements and ancillary activity can be seasonal. The document reports no total borrowings for that interim date and FY 2026, compared with Rs 44.02 million at 31 March 2025. Risk Disclosures That Shape the Draft Stage Review The abridged prospectus identifies air ticketing dependence, ancillary-service exposure and reliance on certain travel partners among the leading internal risks. Industry disruption, demand volatility, inventory access and changes in airline pricing can affect revenue and margins. The company’s high concentration in the top five partners means a single relationship or commercial change could have a disproportionate effect. SNVA also discloses a significant contribution through its Dubai subsidiary and material exposure to North America. Geopolitical developments, payment-settlement issues, regulatory changes and regional travel demand can therefore affect consolidated results. Certain subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries have incurred losses in the past, and continued underperformance or going-concern uncertainty at those entities could affect cash flows and financial condition. Travomint and related technology infrastructure are operationally central. Outages, cyber or systems failures, and interruption of global distribution systems, APIs, reservation systems or payment gateways can impair bookings and customer experience. Pending trademark applications and domain dependencies create intellectual-property and brand risks. The issuer’s entire operating revenue depends on the global travel and tourism industry, which remains sensitive to economic, geopolitical, climatic and public-health events. Litigation and Auditor Disclosures The draft abridged prospectus states that there were no audit qualifications or emphasis-of-matter items requiring adjustments for the periods presented. Its litigation summary reports no criminal, tax or regulatory proceeding involving the company and no such proceedings involving subsidiaries under the table, but it reports one other material proceeding against subsidiaries with an unascertainable amount. This compact summary should be read with the full UDRHP-I litigation chapter rather than interpreted as a blanket absence of disputes. Application Tax and GST Points for a Later Offer No application can be made from the UDRHP-I. If the issuer later files an RHP and announces a price band and dates, the applicant should use the official exchange, issuer, BRLM or registrar channels identified in those final materials. The verified draft names Equirus Capital Limited and Anand Rathi Advisors Limited as book running lead managers and KFIN Technologies Limited as registrar. A later application should be reconciled to the final UPI mandate, PAN, demat account and category rules rather than to social-media messages. Subscription money for equity securities is not consideration for a supply of goods or services under GST because securities are excluded from both definitions. Brokerage and other separately supplied post-listing services can carry GST. Income-tax is generally not charged merely because shares are allotted, but a later sale, transfer or other disposal can produce capital gains or another tax consequence. Current Income Tax Department guidance distinguishes short-term and long-term listed-equity gains and applies special rates and STT conditions. Cost, holding period, residential status, category and transaction route should be verified for the investor’s facts when a disposal occurs. The draft states that the proposed shares are intended for listing on NSE and BSE and records in-principle approvals dated 19 June 2026. In-principle approval is part of the listing process; it is not SEBI approval of the investment, a guarantee of the filing’s accuracy, confirmation that the offer will proceed, or assurance of a liquid or profitable market after listing.
