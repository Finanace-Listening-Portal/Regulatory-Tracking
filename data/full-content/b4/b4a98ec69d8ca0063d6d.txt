Articles 1 min read Govt appoints Lavanya Mundayur as CMD of New India Assurance Lavanya R Mundayur has been appointed as the Chairman and Managing Director of New India Assurance Company Ltd. This appointment will be effective until May 31, 2029, according to the Finance Ministry's Department of Financial Services. Mundayur, currently serving as CMD of Agriculture Insurance Company of India Ltd, will have a pay scale of Rs 2,05,400 to Rs 2,24,400.
