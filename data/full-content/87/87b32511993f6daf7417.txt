Large private banks could see NIM compression of 8-20 basis points, Motilal Oswal Financial Services said in a report. Margins are expected to improve gradually as banks deploy surplus liquidity for lending and retire high-cost liabilities. Advt System-wide credit growth stood at 18.8% year-on-year as of September 15, driven by sustained retail demand, higher utilisation by MSME borrowers and healthy corporate borrowings amid elevated bond yields. Higher-than-expected FCNR(B) inflows of $133 billion have also supported the growth momentum. Motilal Oswal expects credit growth to moderate to 15.5% in FY27.Deposit growth accelerated to 17% year-on-year from 11-12%, supported by strong FCNR(B) inflows, which accounted for about 4.5% of system deposits. The stronger deposit mobilisation has brought the credit-deposit ratio down to 80.8% from a peak of 83.4%. However, banks continue to face challenges in mobilising low-cost deposits, with term deposit rates expected to remain broadly sticky with an upside bias.PSBs seen strongPublic sector banks (PSBs), meanwhile, are expected to show greater margin resilience because of the lower proportion of FCNR(B) deposits mobilised relative to overall deposits and continued efforts to retire high-cost liabilities. Their NIMs are expected to remain broadly range-bound.For the second quarter of FY27, Motilal Oswal estimates net interest income for its banking coverage universe to rise 11.9% year-on-year and 2.2% sequentially, while pre-provision operating profit is expected to increase 4.1% sequentially. Private banks are expected to report 24% year-on-year and 1.7% sequential growth in profit after tax, while PSU banks are expected to post 27% and 19% growth, respectively. Advt The brokerage expects banks under its coverage to deliver a 15% earnings CAGR over FY26-28, driven by a 20% CAGR for private banks. PSU banks are expected to report an 11% earnings CAGR over the same period. The brokerage's top picks are ICICI Bank, State Bank of India, Kotak Mahindra Bank and AU Small Finance Bank.The asset quality outlook remains benign, with most banks indicating that stress across secured and unsecured segments remains controlled. Slippages and credit costs are therefore expected to remain broadly steady. However, the brokerage remains watchful of the impact of an uncertain macroeconomic environment on domestic business growth and credit quality, as well as the impact of a below-normal monsoon. Advt Among large private banks, ICICI Bank's net interest income is estimated to grow 16.8% year-on-year in the second quarter, followed by Kotak Mahindra Bank at 14%, HDFC Bank at 8.9% and Axis Bank at 8.5%.Among small finance banks, AU Small Finance Bank is expected to post 53.6% year-on-year growth in profit after tax to Rs8.6 billion, supported by 29.3% growth in net interest income and a 4-basis-point sequential expansion in NIM to 5.94%. Equitas Small Finance Bank is estimated to report a 3.8% sequential increase in profit after tax to Rs1.91 billion.In the payments and fintech segment, Paytm's revenue from operations is expected to rise 27% year-on-year and 7% sequentially to Rs26.3 billion, while its gross merchandise value (GMV) is estimated to grow 32% year-on-year and 6% sequentially to Rs7.5 trillion. Pine Labs is expected to report 22% year-on-year and 7% sequential growth in revenue to Rs7.9 billion, while GMV is estimated to rise 13% year-on-year and 14% sequentially to Rs4.8 trillion.
