Articles 1 min read Bajaj Finance to raise Rs 17,500 cr via QIP, pref issue Bajaj Finance plans to raise up to Rs 17,500 crore through a Rs 11,700-crore QIP and Rs 5,800 crore warrant issue to promoter Bajaj Finserv. The fundraising requires shareholder approval; institutional investors will receive QIP shares while promoter warrants could help maintain Bajaj Finserv’s stake and limit dilution. Separately, Rs 5800 crore will be raised through the issue of warrants via a preferrential allotment to promoter Bajaj Finserv, the company said in a notice to the stock exchanges."The company will be seeking approval of the shareholders for the proposed QIP and by convening an Extra Ordinary General Meeting," the company said.In total, the company will raise up to Rs 17,500 crore.In the QIP, institutional investors will be allotted shares, while warrants will be issued to promoters likely to ensure that their stake is not diluted.
