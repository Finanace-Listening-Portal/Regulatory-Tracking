Customers can choose between the two payment networks based on the offers and benefits available on each, giving them greater flexibility to select what works best for their needs, while managing both cards through a single credit relationship.With the launch of the YES BANK novio Credit Card, novio is now expanding its offering to include unsecured credit, giving eligible consumers more ways to access credit as their financial needs evolve. Rewards can be redeemed for discounts on flight bookings and e-vouchers from top brandsAditya Gupta, Founder, novio, said, "Millions of Indians already have a credit card, but their first card was often designed around eligibility rather than how their needs evolve. We believe every credit card user deserves more - exciting rewards, discounts, lifestyle benefits and the convenience to pay the way they want, benefits that have traditionally been more accessible to higher-income consumers. With our entry into unsecured credit, we are bringing these experiences to a much broader set of consumers and raising the bar for what everyday credit can offer."Anil Singh, Country Head - Credit Cards and Merchant Acquiring, YES BANK, said, "YES BANK has consistently advanced innovation in India's digital payments ecosystem. The YES BANK novio Credit Card operates under a single shared credit limit between physical and virtual credit card, providing consumers real flexibility in how they access credit and earn appropriate reward points across relevant large consumer brand platforms. Tailored for the affluent customer, the card features seamless conversion of purchases into EMIs, allowing us to deliver this innovation with a fully digital, customer-first experience that makes premium credit benefits highly accessible."
