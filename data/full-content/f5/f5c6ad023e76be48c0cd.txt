Vivek Gupta - Chief Operations & Customer Experience Officer - Bajaj Life InsuranceBajaj Life Insurance has appointed Vivek Gupta as Chief Operations and Customer Experience Officer. In his expanded role, Gupta will oversee end-to-end operations, customer experience, and the company's AI and innovation strategy, including policy servicing, claims management, risk governance, and digital self-service platforms.Gupta brings over two decades of insurance industry experience to the role, having previously led business transformation and tech-driven customer engagement initiatives, including virtual reality and conversational AI integration at the insurer. Advt Commenting on the appointment, Tarun Chugh, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer at Bajaj Life Insurance, said, "Vivek has been an integral part of Bajaj Life's journey, contributing significantly to our strategic thinking and transformation agenda. As he takes on this expanded mandate, we look forward to his leadership in bringing together our operations, customer experience and innovation priorities."Gupta added, "Every interaction is an opportunity to strengthen trust, simplify the experience and give customers greater confidence in the future they are planning for. I look forward to working with our teams to build on the strong foundation we have created and further embed a culture of customer-centricity and excellence."
