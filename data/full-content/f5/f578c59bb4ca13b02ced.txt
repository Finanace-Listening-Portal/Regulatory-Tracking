Articles 1 min read Revolut raises valuation to $115 billion in latest fundraising round The fundraising, led by five global investors, comes amid Revolut’s expansion across international banking markets, with new bank launches in four countries and banking licences secured in three others in 2026. Rowe Price Associates, Inc., and Baillie Gifford. The fundraising process attracted participation from both long-standing partners and new global investors.Since the start of 2026, Revolut has launched banks in the UK, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand, while securing banking licences in France, Colombia and Argentina. It has also received conditional national bank charter approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) in the US. Advt The expansion builds on Revolut's established European banking operations across 30 European Economic Area (EEA) markets.In 2025, the company's revenue grew 46% to $6 billion (£4.5 billion), while pre-tax profit climbed 57% to $2.3 billion (£1.7 billion). Its pre-tax margins expanded to 38%.“Every bank we launch means more people get a better way to manage their money, wherever they live. Our team makes that happen, and employee ownership has been central to our culture from day one. That’s whywe’ve once again given them the chance to share in the value they’ve created, as we work towards our next milestone of serving 100 million customers,” said Nik Storonsky, CEO & Founder of Revolut.
