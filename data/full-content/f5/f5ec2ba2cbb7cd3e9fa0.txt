Articles 1 min read HDFC Capital, Shalimar create Rs 750 crore platform for UP real estate HDFC Capital Advisors has joined forces with Shalimar Corp to establish a significant Rs 750 crore real estate development platform. This collaboration aims to enhance residential and mixed-use projects in prominent Uttar Pradesh cities like Lucknow and Varanasi. The venture envisions a development potential surpassing 5 million sq ft, inclusive of around 3,000 residential units. Advt Shalimar has launched projects worth Rs 6,000 crore in the last three years. In FY27, it plans to launch projects worth Rs 4,000 crore.The company recently entered Varanasi, which it has identified as a key growth market, citing its increasing religious significance, spiritual tourism and large-scale infrastructure investments."Over the years, Shalimar has built a legacy of trust and excellence through a diversified portfolio of residential townships, IT parks and mixed-use developments, and with this platform, we plan to take the development ahead," said Khalid Masood, MD, Shalimar Corp.Shalimar Corp has delivered more than 70 projects and developed more than 25 million sq ft of premium real estate.
