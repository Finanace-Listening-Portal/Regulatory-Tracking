The latest inflation number reflects the “continued broadening of inflationary pressures and the transmission of global shocks, amid seasonal increases in clothing prices and higher car prices,” said Tanay Dalal, Axis Bank’s Senior Vice President of Business Economic Research. According to Dalal, inflation is likely to stay above 6% until June 2027. The RBI is legally mandated to keep the headline inflation rate at 4% in a band of 2-6%. However, not only is this the fifth month in a row that inflation has come in above 4%, it has also breached the 6% upper bound of the flexible inflation target for the first time since October 2024.
