Advt In line with the government's directions to fill backlog vacancies in a time-bound manner, the bank has undertaken a special recruitment drive to fill backlog vacancies in reserved categories, he said."This initiative reflects our commitment to strengthening representation, promoting diversity and ensuring that deserving candidates get an opportunity to be part of the SBI family and contribute to the nation's progress," Setty added.The induction of the new junior associates in customer support and sales vertical will further augment the Bank's customer-facing capabilities, support efficient branch operations while ensuring accessible, responsive and seamless banking services across its extensive network, SBI said in a statement.The selection process comprised a preliminary examination held on September 16, followed by the main examination on October 29.SBI is one of the country's largest employers with a total workforce of 2.47 lakh employees. PTIDuring FY27, SBI plans to onboard approximately 11,000-12,000 personnel across clerical and officer cadres, factoring in retirements and emerging business requirements.
