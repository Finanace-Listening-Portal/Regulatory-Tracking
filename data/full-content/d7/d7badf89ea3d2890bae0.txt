Articles 2 min read AU Small Finance Bank says investigating potential data leak involving Salesforce hosted platform AU Small Finance Bank said that the lender is investigating a potential data leak involving a Salesforce-managed SaaS platform that holds limited data relating to its vehicle loan customers, and that its own systems remain unaffected and banking services continue normally. Advt The development comes after AU Small Finance Bank was listed on a leak site associated with the Scarlettgroup cybercrime group on October 29. The listing identifies AU’s website as a victim in the financial services sector. However, the specific data allegedly accessed or the method of compromise has not been independently established. In October, State Bank of India said it had identified 3,897 customer records containing personally identifiable information among around 3.75 lakh records found on the dark web. The information reportedly included names, mobile numbers, email and residential addresses, dates of birth, branch and CIF identifiers, and metadata linked to loan and lead records. SBI said its core banking operations and business continuity were not materially impacted and that an investigation was underway.In July, Bank of Baroda disclosed a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorised access through an employee’s email account. The bank said its core banking systems were not affected and remained secure. Media reports subsequently said data and internal documents had appeared on the dark web, although the scale and source of the alleged leak were under investigation.Globally, crypto exchange Binance is also investigating a security incident that it estimates may have affected assets worth around USD 6.3 billion.
