} } Listen to This ArticleShares of gold-loan financiers Muthoot Finance and Manappuram Finance fell nearly 4 per cent each on Wednesday as a correction in gold prices weighed on investor sentiment towards lenders that rely on the metal as their primary collateral. On Wednesday, Manappuram Finance shares fell 4.26 per cent to ₹283 on the NSE, while Muthoot Finance declined nearly 4 per cent to ₹2,640. The decline in gold prices has raised concerns over the growth and asset quality of gold-loan-focused non-banking financial companies (NBFCs). The sector is also facing pressure from revised regulatory norms governing loan-to-value (LTV) calculations and renewals. “The correction in gold prices has been one of the key reasons behind the weakness in these stocks. Gold prices have come under pressure due to profit booking after a strong rally, a stronger US dollar and changing expectations around US interest rate cuts,” said Gaurav Arora, head of research at SAHI. A sustained decline in gold prices could affect collateral values and LTV ratios, while weighing on investor sentiment towards gold-loan companies. Broader concerns over competition and asset quality have also contributed to the weakness, Arora said. International gold prices have fallen to a 14-month low of $3,749 per ounce.
