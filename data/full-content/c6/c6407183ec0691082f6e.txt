Articles 1 min read Central Bank of India Q2 profit rises 21% as loans grow 30% Central Bank of India reported a 21% rise in September 2026 quarterly net profit to Rs 1,469 crore, supported by 26% net interest income growth and 30% loan expansion. Asset quality improved, with net NPA declining to 0.31%, while operating efficiency strengthened. Cost to Income ratio reduced to 58.95% in September 2026 from 62.42% a year ago. Advt Total advances increased 30% to Rs 3.81 lakh crore in September 2026 from Rs 2.93 lakh crore led by a 21% growth in retail, agriculture & MSME (RAM) business. Retail loans grew by 24.49% to Rs 1.12 lakh crore.Asset quality improved with net NPA at 0.31% down 17 bps, from 0.48%, year on year.
