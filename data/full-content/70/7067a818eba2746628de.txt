Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Nov 3, 2026 at 12:53 PM IST var news_msid = 135487325; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook PhonePe presents affordable insurance against firecracker accidents this festive seasonWith firecracker-related accidents posing risks of burns, hospitalisation and property damage during Diwali, niche insurance products offering short-duration protection are finding a place in India's digital insurance distribution market.PhonePe has reintroduced its firecracker insurance product for the third consecutive year, offering coverage of up to ₹25,000 at premiums starting at ₹11. The product, underwritten by Aditya Birla Health Insurance, is available in two variants of 11 and 30 days.Insurtech platform CoverSure is also offering firecracker insurance, with premiums starting at ₹10, providing accidental death coverage of ₹50,000 and protection of up to ₹10,000 against fire-related burns, according to recent reports. Advt The products are examples of seasonal micro-insurance, which offers limited financial protection against specific risks for a short period at relatively low premiums. Unlike conventional health or personal accident policies, these covers are designed around a particular event or exposure.What do these firecracker insurance products cover?PhonePe's firecracker insurance offers three benefits: hospitalisation, day-care treatment and accidental death.The policy provides medical coverage of up to ₹25,000 for hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours and eligible day-care procedures requiring less than 24 hours of admission. The medical benefits cover the primary policyholder, spouse and up to two dependent children.It also provides a fixed accidental death benefit of ₹25,000 for the primary policyholder.The platform is offering a 30-day plan at ₹21, which went live on October 15 and remains valid until November 14. An 11-day variant priced at ₹11 is scheduled to become available on November 5, with coverage until November 15.CoverSure's offering provides accidental death coverage of ₹50,000 and fire-related burn protection of up to ₹10,000. The platform had offered a similar product for ₹5 during Diwali 2025, with a 10-day coverage period.While the two products offer different benefit structures, their low premiums and digital distribution make them examples of how insurers and intermediaries are attempting to package specific risks into affordable micro-insurance products. Advt Do consumers need a separate firecracker insurance policy?Firecracker insurance is not the only way consumers can protect themselves against Diwali-related accidents. Existing health, personal accident and home insurance policies may already cover different aspects of such incidents, subject to policy terms and exclusions.A standard health insurance policy generally covers eligible hospitalisation expenses arising from accidental burns and other injuries. However, outpatient consultations and minor treatments may not be covered unless the policy includes OPD benefits.Personal accident insurance can provide lump-sum benefits in the event of accidental death or specified permanent disabilities. Home insurance, meanwhile, can cover fire-related damage to an insured building and its contents, depending on the policy. Advt Consumers should also distinguish between medical coverage and property protection. A ₹25,000 medical cover, for instance, does not necessarily protect a household against fire damage or third-party property claims.The emergence of such products highlights an opportunity for insurers to distribute low-ticket covers through digital platforms, although the actual value of these policies will depend on their coverage limits, exclusions and ease of claims settlement.
