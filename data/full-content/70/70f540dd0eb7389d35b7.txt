Bond issuance by Bajaj Finance, L&T Finance and HDB Financial Services has also lagged the year-ago period in the first half of FY27, according to a Nomura report.The current AAA-rated three, five and 10-year bond yields were at 8.2-8.4% as of September 29, higher than the yields on bonds maturing over the next few quarters for the covered NBFCs. Bond market activity is expected to pick up in the second half of FY27, but at a higher cost. Advt Global volatilityVolatile global events during the financial year have kept bond yields volatile and prompted several NBFCs to stay away from the bond market. The lower bond issuance run-rate at Bajaj Finance, L&T Finance and HDB Financial Services could therefore add to funding cost pressure when these companies return to the market.The pressure on funding costs has already emerged. In the first quarter of FY27, the cost of funds for the covered NBFCs increased by 6-42 basis points sequentially. NBFCs were expected to benefit from relatively low-cost FCNRB flows raised by banks, but banks are now expected to first use these flows to lower their own cost of funds and then lend to low-risk segments such as mortgages and large corporates.As a result, only select NBFCs are expected to benefit from FCNRB flows in the second quarter of FY27. If the rate-hike cycle begins in October 2026, no benefit from these flows is expected to be passed on to the covered NBFCs.The economists' house view is for two rate hikes totalling 50 basis points during October-December 2026. The scenario analysis assumes the entire 50-60 basis point increase takes effect in October 2026, lifting NBFCs' cost of funds from the third quarter of FY27, while NBFCs pass the increase on to end-customers only from FY28.Under this scenario, FY27F EPS for the covered NBFCs could take a 1-8% hit. As lending rate hikes begin from FY28, the EPS impact is expected to decline through FY28-29. Despite the near-term pressure, the structural EPS compounding rate over FY27-29 remains healthy at around 16-28%. Advt The impact of higher rates is expected to remain manageable because many diversified NBFCs have a healthy mix of floating-rate assets. NBFCs with fixed-rate loans typically have shorter tenures, which should also support profitability over FY27-29 in a rising-rate environment.However, regulatory developments are emerging as a bigger concern. These include insurance distribution reforms affecting credit-life insurance policies offered by the covered NBFCs and regulatory hesitation over flexi loans offered by NBFCs.The brokerage continues to prefer Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital and L&T Finance among diversified lenders, and Shriram Finance among vehicle financiers. Housing financiers are also seen as comfortably placed in a rising-rate environment, with Aadhar Housing Finance carrying a Buy rating. Advt While higher funding costs could put pressure on NBFC earnings in FY27F-28F, the report said the sector's structural strengths remain intact in a rate-hike scenario. The more concerning issue, in its assessment, is the regulatory landscape, particularly changes affecting insurance distribution and flexi-loan products.
