The bank's USD 1.5 billion deposit raise from the diaspora under the FCNR(B) scheme helped in both reducing the reliance on bulk deposits, which widened the net interest margin and in funding assets, he said, adding that the bank booked retail assets of another Rs 9,438 crore using the leverage facility made available. Federal Bank is circumspect when it comes to loan growth in the agri portfolio, he said, adding that it has not noticed any adverse impact of the El Nino factor on its book. It is also cautious about the power and infrastructure sectors due to their long-term funding demand. Gold loans, which surged 37 per cent in Q2, can rise further as well, a senior official said, pointing out that it can take the overall contribution from the sector to 20 per cent of the overall book from the current 14.5 per cent.
