Life insurance accounted for Rs 272.49 crore of the FY26 income, while general insurance contributed Rs 24 crore.IRDAI’s proposed framework seeks to overhaul insurance distribution economics, including bringing back product-level commission caps, tightening expense-of-management limits and restructuring the existing distributor framework. The consultation paper also proposes tighter conditions around insurance bundled with loans and greater disclosure of commissions. Advt The proposed commission caps are significantly lower than some prevailing payouts. For instance, the draft proposes a cap of 2 per cent on credit-life insurance, compared with substantially higher existing commission levels, while also proposing caps across life, health and general insurance products.For banks and other distributors, the changes could therefore reduce the economics of selling insurance through the bancassurance channel. The proposed framework is intended to reduce distribution costs, improve transparency and curb mis-selling, but could also compress distributor revenues.Applying management’s estimated 50-60 per cent impact to RBL Bank’s FY26 income base would imply a potential annual hit of around Rs 148-178 crore to bancassurance income.“The way the draft is currently worded, I think it can have a substantial impact on the bank assurance income. Minimum 50-60 per cent, if it goes through without any changes,” RBL Bank management said during its Q2 FY27 analyst call.RBL Bank’s core fee income increased 12 per cent year-on-year to Rs 1,039.6 crore in Q2 FY27, while net advances grew 42 per cent to Rs 1.42 lakh crore and deposits rose 34 per cent to Rs 1.56 lakh crore.The bank’s fee income ratio declined to 56.5 per cent in Q2 FY27 from 70.7 per cent a year earlier, according to the investor presentation.Within retail banking, core retail fee income rose **9 per cent year-on-year to Rs 826.7 crore** in Q2 FY27. The mix included processing fees, payments, general banking, foreign exchange, distribution, and trade and other fees. Advt The annual report data also shows that bancassurance has been a growing contributor to the bank’s fee franchise. Income from the segment increased from Rs 226.23 crore in FY25 to Rs 296.49 crore in FY26, with life insurance accounting for the bulk of the increase.Lender also said Q2 included a small one-off fee from an FCNR-related fixed-to-floating currency swap, while fee income from better-rated corporate customers may also be lower relative to some of the bank’s traditional segments.RBL Bank reported Rs 1,077.4 crore of other income in Q2 FY27, up 16 per cent year-on-year and 12 per cent sequentially, while net interest income rose 37 per cent to Rs 2,130.9 crore. Net profit increased 170 per cent year-on-year to Rs 481.8 crore. Advt The 50-60 per cent estimate is management’s assessment based on the draft as currently worded, and the eventual impact will depend on the provisions in the regulations finally notified by IRDAI and how the framework is implemented.
