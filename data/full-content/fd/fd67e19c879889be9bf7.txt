Articles 1 min read AU Small Finance Bank Q2 profit jumps 53 pc to Rs 858 cr PTI Published On Oct 24, 2026 at 05:08 PM IST var news_msid = 135169691; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook New Delhi, AU Small Finance Bank on Saturday reported a 53 per cent surge in net profit at Rs 858 crore for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026 on the back of improvement in interest income.The lender had earned a net profit of Rs 561 crore in the year-ago period.Total income rose to Rs 6,382 crore in the quarter under review from Rs 5,224 crore in the same period a year ago, AU Small Finance Bank said in a regulatory filing.Interest income increased to Rs 5,612 crore as against Rs 4,511 crore in the corresponding second quarter of the previous year.Net interest income of the bank improved to Rs 2,820 crore in the July-September quarter, from Rs 2,144 crore in the September quarter of 2025, registering a growth of 32 per cent. Advt Net interest margin increased to 5.8 per cent from 5.5 per cent at the end of September 2025.Asset quality of the bank also improved with gross non-performing assets (NPAs) rising to 1.99 per cent of gross advances at the end of the September quarter 2026, as against 2.41 per cent a year ago.Net NPAs or bad loan too moderated to 0.81 per cent, as against 0.88 per cent in the year-ago period.However, the Capital Adequacy Ratio of the bank decreased to 18.54 per cent, from 18.78 per cent at the end of September 2025."This quarter's performance is a reflection of the institution we are steadily building, one that combines growth with profitability, and ambition with prudence," AU Small Finance Bank MD & CEO Sanjay Agarwal said.The balanced growth of the franchise, improving profitability and stable asset quality reinforce confidence that the foundations for sustained, long-term performance are firmly in place, he added.
