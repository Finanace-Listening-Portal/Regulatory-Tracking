How to Obtain Better Results From These Prompts The Five Elements of an Effective Audit Prompt Role: Specify the relevant expertise, such as statutory central auditor, internal auditor or forensic auditor. Context: Provide the entity type, financial year, audit scope and applicable reporting framework. Task: State precisely what the AI should analyze, calculate, draft or review. Output: Request a checklist, working paper, exception report, financial impact calculation or formal audit observation. Verification: Require the AI to distinguish verified findings from assumptions, identify missing evidence and avoid unsupported conclusions.
