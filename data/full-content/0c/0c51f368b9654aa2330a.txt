This strategic move, in collaboration with Uğur Motorlu Araçlar, marks a significant expansion of TVS Motor’s international portfolio, aligning with the increasing demand for efficient urban mobility solutions in the region. The launch event, held on 11th November 2026, was attended by over 50 guests, including automobile enthusiasts and representatives from both TVS Motor and UMA. The TVS Callisto 110 is designed to cater to the needs of urban commuters, offering a blend of performance, comfort, and modern design. The scooter is equipped with a 113.3 cc fuel-injected engine and a CVTi automatic transmission, featuring an Integrated Starter Generator for silent starts and Stop-Go technology that enhances fuel efficiency by up to 10%. The TVS Callisto 110 boasts several first-in-segment features aimed at improving rider and passenger comfort. These include the longest seat in its segment, increased legroom, and 12-inch alloy wheels with three-step adjustable rear shock absorbers. Additionally, the scooter offers practical features such as a 33-litre under-seat storage, front-facing fuel filling, and a glove box with mobile charging capabilities. Safety and technology are also priorities, with the inclusion of a metal body, Follow Me Headlamp, hazard lamps, and a fully digital coloured instrument cluster with Bluetooth connectivity and navigation. Peyman Kargar, President of International Business at TVS Motor Company, expressed enthusiasm about the launch, stating, “Türkiye is an important market in our international growth journey, and we are pleased to introduce the TVS Callisto. The product has been developed around the needs of everyday urban riders, bringing together refined performance, comfort, and relevant modern design.” The TVS Callisto 110 is available in three colour options—Blue, White, and Red—at a starting price of ₺132,600. As TVS Motor continues to expand its global footprint, the company remains committed to enhancing customer engagement and establishing a sustainable foundation for long-term growth in the region. Analysts view this launch as a strategic step in reinforcing TVS Motor’s presence in international markets, particularly in regions with a growing demand for efficient and practical urban transportation solutions.
