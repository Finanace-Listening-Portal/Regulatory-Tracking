ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Sep 22, 2026 at 05:49 PM IST var news_msid = 134412196; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook SIGN gets RBI nod to operate as Online Payment AggregatorSamvriddhi Inclusive Growth Network Pvt.Ltd. (SIGN), a subsidiary of MOS Utility Limited announced on Tuesday that the financial services firm has received in-principle Authorisation from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to operate as an Online Payment Aggregator, expanding its online digital payments footprint.The approval from RBI will let SIGN build and offer online payment aggregation services for merchants, with focus on small businesses operating in rural, semi-urban and underserved markets. The planned offering will include payment gateway services for UPI, QR-based payments, credit and debit cards and net banking, along with digital collections, payment links and other online payment management capabilities. According to the company, the PA-Online business will operate as a dedicated digital segment, separate from the company’s existing CSP-led assisted banking and financial inclusion activities. Advt Commenting on the development, Atish Shelar, Group CEO, Samvriddhi Inclusive Growth Network Pvt. Ltd (SIGN), said, “The Payment Aggregator authorization strengthens our ability to enter the online digital payments market and build technology-led payment solutions for merchants. This is a new growth opportunity for Samvriddhi as we expand from last-mile banking into online payment aggregation.”Chirag Shah, Chairman and Founder, MOS Utility Limited, said, “The PA-Online authorisation gives us the foundation to build digital payment acceptance and online collection capabilities and, over time, evaluate additional digital payment and financial services, subject to the relevant regulatory framework. Our focus at this stage is on building a strong online payment aggregation platform for merchants.”Headquartered in Kolkata, SIGN currently works with leading banks such as the State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, Odisha Gramin Bank, West Bengal Gramin Bank, and UCO Bank.
