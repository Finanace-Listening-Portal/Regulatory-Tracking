Attachments SEBI_LODR_THIRD_AMENDMENT To, All Listed Entities Dear Sir/Madam, The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued notification No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/329 dated October 12, 2026, regarding further amendment to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure requirements) Regulations, 2015. These regulations may be called the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. The aforesaid notification is attached for reference. All Listed companies are requested to take note and comply accordingly. Amish Shah Assistant Vice President Listing Compliance Shyam Bhagirath Manager Listing Compliance
