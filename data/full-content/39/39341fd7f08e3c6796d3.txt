SEBI observed that GMP figures are widely published across websites, digital platforms, newspapers and certain SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants, portraying them as indicators of likely listing prices or anticipated returns. SEBI clarified that such figures are unofficial, unverified and unregulated. Transactions in the grey market take place outside the recognised stock exchange framework, without regulatory oversight, settlement guarantees, investor protection or formal price discovery mechanisms. The publication of such figures may adversely affect investor interests and undermine transparency and accountability in the securities market. Accordingly, SEBI has advised discontinuation of the publication, display, circulation and dissemination of GMP figures across the securities market. Securities and Exchange Board of India Press Release No. 75/2026 | Dated: November 23, 2026 Advisory Regarding Unregulated Pre-listing Premium often called as Grey Market Premium (GMP) for Upcoming Initial Public Offers (IPOs) It has come to the notice of SEBI that Unregulated Pre-listing Premium often called as Grey Market Premium figures for upcoming IPOs are being widely published across various websites, digital platforms, newspapers and some SEBI registered intermediaries and market participants, where they are portrayed as indicators of the likely listing price or anticipated returns upon listing. Investors are hereby cautioned that such pre-listing premium figures are unofficial, unverified, and unregulated at which shares are supposedly traded before they are formally listed on a recognized stock exchange. These transactions are executed outside the exchange framework, are not subject to regulatory oversight, carry no settlement guarantee or investor protection, and follow no formal price-discovery process. The publication of such pre-listing premium figures may be detrimental to the interests of investors and contrary to the core principles of transparency and accountability in the securities market. In order to safeguard the interest of the investors and maintain transparency and integrity in the securities market, the publication, display, circulation or dissemination of such Unregulated Pre-listing Premium often called as Grey Market Premium through any mode or platform may be discontinued across the securities market. All SEBI-registered intermediaries and market participants are advised to refrain from publishing, displaying, or broadcasting GMP figures for IPOs on their websites, platforms, or any other communication channels-including email, SMS, and social media. While investing in the securities market, investors are advised to: Not treat GMP as an indicator of listing price or expected returns; Refrain from engaging in any GMP-linked transactions, as they fall entirely outside the regulatory framework; and Transact in IPOs exclusively through SEBI-registered intermediaries and authorized platforms. Non-Availability of Dispute Redressal Recourses Investors should note that in the event of any dispute arising from or relating to GMP transactions, none of the following regulatory recourses will be available: Investor protection benefits under SEBI or Stock Exchange jurisdiction; and Investor grievance redressal mechanisms administered by Stock Exchanges, or the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal administered by Exchanges/Depositories (in).
