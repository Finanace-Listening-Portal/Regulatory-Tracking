The amendment takes immediate effect and modifies Notification No. 07/2026-27 dated 10 April 2026. It has been issued under Section 3 read with Section 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992, and paragraphs 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023. Under the amended conditions, exports of Basmati and Non-Basmati rice to European Union member states and specified European countries, namely the United Kingdom, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland, are permitted subject to the issuance of a Certificate of Inspection by the Export Inspection Council (EIC) or Export Inspection Agency (EIA). This requirement continues to apply to the specified destinations. However, the notification provides that the Certificate of Inspection shall not be mandatory for exports to the remaining European countries until 1 April 2027. The amendment applies to the notified ITC(HS) codes 1006 2000, 1006 3011, 1006 3019, 1006 3091, 1006 3099 and 1006 4000 for Non-Basmati rice, and 1006 3012 and 1006 3092 for Basmati rice. The amendment continues the destination-specific approach to rice export inspection requirements while extending the exemption period for other European countries. Exporters shipping to EU member states and the five specified European countries must continue obtaining the prescribed inspection certificate. Exporters shipping to other European countries may avail themselves of the exemption during the notified period. The notification is effective immediately and does not specify any separate transitional procedure. Exports to other European countries are exempt from this requirement till April 01, 2027.
