The RBI states that it has made the change following a review and in exercise of its powers under section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, together with all other enabling provisions and laws. It records its satisfaction that issuing the amendment is necessary and expedient in the public interest. The operative change is the deletion of the identified section and paragraph; the notification does not reproduce the deleted provision, prescribe replacement limits, or set out a separate transitional arrangement. Its stated subject is non-option derivative contracts under the miscellaneous directions applicable to Small Finance Banks. The instrument specifies its formal title, immediate commencement and the precise provision being removed. It is signed by Sunil T S Nair, Chief General Manager. Accordingly, the amendment takes effect from its issuance on October 19, 2026.
