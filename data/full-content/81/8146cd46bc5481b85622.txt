Credit growth has remained resilient, hovering between 19.08 per cent and 19.54 per cent at successive month-ends since July. Deposit growth, meanwhile, has been more volatile, easing from 17.83 per cent at the end of September to 16.86 per cent in October. The divergence between the two has widened in recent months. The gap between credit and deposit growth stood at 2.68 percentage points at the end of October, compared with 1.55 percentage points at the end of July. Outstanding bank credit stood at ₹231.82 trillion at the end of the October 31 fortnight, registering an increase of ₹37.89 trillion over the year. Aggregate deposits rose to ₹282.51 trillion, up ₹40.75 trillion over the same period.
