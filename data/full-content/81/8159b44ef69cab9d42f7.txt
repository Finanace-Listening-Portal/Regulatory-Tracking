Articles 1 min read SBI to up lending under 'MSME Dream' to Rs 50 cr by March 2027 The State Bank of India is gearing up to boost its MSME Dream lending initiative to a remarkable Rs 50 crore. This move comes on the heels of introducing loans of up to Rs 5 crore and Rs 10 crore at select branches. By the end of November, all branches will offer loans up to Rs 10 crore. With Rs 2. Advt The bank will increase the per loan borrowing under the product to Rs 50 crore across all branches by March next year, the official said.The country's largest lender has sanctioned Rs 2.25 lakh crore under the 'Dream' product till now, the official said, stressing that the asset quality is also much better on such loans.The gross Non-Performing Assets (NPA) ratio for loans under the product is under 0.50 per cent as against the level of over 1.50 per cent for the loans to the micro, small and medium enterprises, the official said.A borrower fills in a set of details on a digital platform to check his or her credit worthiness, and a loan is sanctioned within 45 minutes by the engine, the official said.Earlier, it used to take around a month to disburse a loan which has been now compressed to around a week using the digital inputs, he said.
