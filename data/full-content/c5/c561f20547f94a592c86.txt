Articles 2 min read Relief for Chanda Kochhar as HC quashes ICICI Bank's nod to prosecute ex-CEO in 'loan fraud' case The Bombay High Court quashed the ICICI Bank's sanction for prosecuting Chanda Kochhar in an alleged fraud case. The court found that the authority granting the sanction did not apply proper judgment to the circumstances. Chanda Kochhar was arrested in December 2022 and received interim bail in January 2023. The case involved a loan of Rs 3,250 crore given to Videocon Group during her tenure. Advt The sanction order was unsustainable in law and hence rendered null and void, the HC said.The court passed the order on a petition filed by Kochhar claiming that ICICI Bank had not followed proper procedure while granting sanction to prosecute her.The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) had arrested Chanda Kochhar and her husband Deepak Kochhar in December 2022 in the alleged loan fraud case. The couple was granted interim bail in January 2023.In June 2023, the CBI had informed a special court here that the bank had granted sanction to prosecute Chanda Kochhar as there appeared to be a quid pro quo (exchange of favours between two parties) in the loan given to Videocon.Also read: Payment firms seek early MDR rollout for select UPI transactions while they stay 18 months without incentivesSuch a sanction is mandatory under the Prevention of Corruption Act, as a bank employee is deemed to be a public servant.The case concerns a Rs 3,250 crore loan granted by ICICI Bank to the Videocon Group in 2012, when Chanda Kochhar was heading the bank. The CBI has alleged cheating and irregularities in the sanctioning of the loan.According to the probe agency, Kochhar had cleared the loan to the Videocon Group because her husband and family stood to benefit from such transactions. In return, her husband's company, Nu Renewable, allegedly received investment from Videocon, the CBI said. Advt The loan subsequently turned into a non-performing asset (NPA) and was classified as a case of bank fraud.
