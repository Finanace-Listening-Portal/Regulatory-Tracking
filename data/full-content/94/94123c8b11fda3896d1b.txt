The relaxation operates for 90 days from 1 October 2026, the date of Instruction No. 18/2026-Customs, and temporarily permits eligible consignments to be released against submission of an end-use bond instead of insisting on an import permit at clearance. The measure addresses difficulties faced by traders, intermediaries and importers handling liquid bulk chemicals for multiple industrial users, particularly where consignments contracted or shipped earlier remain detained at ports. The underlying import permit requirement arises from the Insecticides Third (Amendment) Rules, 2026, notified through G.S.R. 597(E) dated 8 July 2026, under the Insecticides Act, 1968. To obtain clearance under the temporary arrangement, importers must execute an end-use bond and furnish an undertaking confirming that the goods are intended exclusively for non-insecticidal purposes and will not be used, sold or supplied for insecticidal use. They must also disclose the names and addresses of actual users. The facility extends to existing consignments held solely because the import permit has not been produced. However, it does not apply where another violation is detected or specific intelligence indicates possible diversion to insecticidal use. CBIC has directed Customs formations to align their procedures, while the National Customs Targeting Centre (NCTC) will make appropriate risk-management-system modifications. The interim arrangement will be reviewed in consultation with CIB&RC, the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT). No. 450/51/2018-Cus.III Government of India Ministry of Finance Department of Revenue Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs To, All Principal Chief Commissioners/Chief Commissioners of Customs/Customs (Preventive)/Customs & Central Tax; All Principal Commissioners/Commissioners of Customs/Customs (Preventive); All Principal Director Generals/Director Generals under CBIC. Madam/Sir, Subject: Requirement of import permit for non-insecticidal use of insecticides — interim facilitation for a period of 90 days — reg. 1. Reference is invited to Instruction No. 18/2026-Customs dated 01.10.2026, whereby the requirement of an import permit for import of substances included in the Schedule to the Insecticides Act, 1968 for non-insecticidal uses, including acrylonitrile, was brought to the notice of field formations. The said requirement arises from the Insecticides Third (Amendment) Rules, 2026, notified by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare vide G.S.R. 597(E) dated 08.07.2026, and is in force from 08.07.2026. 2. Representations have been received from the trade stating that importers who are not themselves manufacturers, including traders who import liquid bulk chemicals on behalf of several actual users, are unable to apply for the import permit and that consignments contracted and shipped earlier are being held at ports. Further, representations from trade have also stated that in view of the time required to obtain import permit suitable relief may be provided for a transitionary period. The issue was examined in consultation with CIB&RC and the Directorate General of Foreign Trade. 3. In view of above and ongoing litigation on the issue, as a facilitative measure, the Board has decided that, for a period of 90 days from the date of issuance of Instruction No. 18/2026-Customs dated 01.10.2026, consignments of substances included in the Schedule to the Insecticides Act, 1968 declared for non-insecticidal use shall be released based on the submission of end use bond. 4. The importer shall also furnish an undertaking that the goods are imported for non-insecticidal use and goods shall not be used, sold or supplied for insecticidal use, along with the name and address of the actual user(s). This facility shall also apply to consignments declared for non-insecticidal use presently held solely for want of the import permit. It shall not apply where any other violation is noticed or where there is specific intelligence of diversion to insecticidal use. NCTC shall also undertake suitable modifications in the risk management system in this regard. 5. This Instruction shall be an interim facilitation measure. It shall be reviewed in consultation with the CIB&RC, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and the Directorate General of Foreign Trade. 6. Instruction No. 18/2026-Customs dated 01.10.2026 stands modified to the above extent. 7. Board desires that for the sake of uniformity the Custom Houses shall immediately align their local procedures in line with the above clarification. Suitable public notice may be issued to guide the trade and industries. Difficulties, if any, in implementation of these instructions may be brought to the notice of the Board.
