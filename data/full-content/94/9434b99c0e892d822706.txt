(IEC: 0595052606) – Consolidated Adjudication of Multiple Show Cause Notices arising from SVB Investigation Report No. CUS/SVB-DEL/72/2017-18 dated 01.12.2017. Summary: The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, through Notification No. 87/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 21 October 2026, has appointed the Principal Commissioner/Commissioner of Customs (Nhava Seva-I), JNCH, Mumbai Zone-II, as the common adjudicating authority for seven specified show cause notices concerning Amway India Enterprises Pvt. Ltd., formerly Amway India Enterprises, bearing IEC 0595052606. The appointment has been made under section 4(1), read with section 3 and sections 5(1) and 5(1A) of the Customs Act, 1962. The notices were issued by Customs authorities at the Air Cargo Complex in New Delhi, Jawaharlal Nehru Custom House at Nhava Sheva, the Air Cargo Complex at Sahar in Mumbai, Chennai-II (Import), and Chennai-VII Air Cargo Commissionerate. They comprise SCN No. 52/2019-20 dated 29 August 2019; SCN No. 81/AC/OPM/2024-25 dated 21 August 2024; SCN No. 629/2020-21 dated 4 December 2020; SCN No. 515/2025-26/DC/Gr.I&IA/NS-I/CAC/JNCH dated 23 July 2025; notices dated 14 February 2023 and 4 August 2021; and SCN No. 177/2023 dated 16 October 2023. For adjudicating these notices, the appointed authority will exercise the powers and discharge the duties conferred or imposed on the respective officers identified in the notification’s table. The notification concerns the assignment of adjudicating authority for the listed proceedings; it does not determine the allegations or liabilities arising from those notices.
