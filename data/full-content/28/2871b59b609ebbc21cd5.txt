The revision follows the recommendations of the Committee on Framing Guidelines for Insolvency Proceedings in the Real Estate Sector (April 2026), which observed that a simplified, allottee-specific claim form would improve homebuyer participation, facilitate claim verification and help collect project-level information for preparing the Information Memorandum and formulating resolution plans. The revised Form CA has two parts. Part I applies to financial creditors in a class other than real estate allottees, while Part II specifically caters to real estate allottees. The latter requires details concerning the allottee’s identity, real estate project, unit or apartment, purchase value, payments made, principal and interest claimed, and possession status. It also enables allottees to indicate their preferred relief, including possession of the unit, execution and registration of a conveyance deed, refund or another specified relief. Such preferences are intended only to assist the resolution professional in collecting project-wise information and prospective resolution applicants in formulating resolution plans; they do not confer an enforceable right to the indicated relief. The revised form must be used for claims filed on or after 30 October 2026. Claims already submitted using the earlier Form CA before that date do not require resubmission. The circular has been issued under section 196 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, and partially modifies IBBI Circular No. IBBI/CIRP/94/2026 dated 2 June 2026. INSOLVENCY AND BANKRUPTCY BOARD OF INDIA 7th Floor, Mayur Bhawan, Connaught Place, New Delhi – 110 001 Circular No. IBBI/CIRP/109/2026 | Dated: 30th October, 2026 To All Registered Insolvency Professionals All Recognised Insolvency Professional Entities All Registered Insolvency Professional Agencies (By mail to registered email addresses and on the website of the IBBI) Subject: Introduction of revised Form CA for filing of claims by real estate allottees. The Committee on Framing Guidelines for Insolvency Proceedings in the Real Estate Sector (April 2026) observed that a simplified and allottee-specific claim form would improve participation by homebuyers, facilitate claim verification, and assist in collection of project-level information necessary for preparation of the Information Memorandum and formulation of resolution plans. 2. Accordingly, in partial modification of IBBI’s Circular No. IBBI/CIRP/94/2026 dated 2nd June 2026, the Form CA (Submission of Claim by Financial Creditors in a Class) issued pursuant to regulation 8A of the IBBI (Insolvency Resolution Process for Corporate Persons) Regulations, 2016 is hereby revised. The revised Form CA is enclosed as Annexure to this Circular. 3. The revised Form CA is divided into two parts: Part I is to be filled by Financial Creditors in a Class other than real estate allottees, and Part II is to be filled by real estate allottees. 4.The financial creditors in a class, including real estate allottees, shall file their claims in the revised Form CA on or after the date of this Circular. The claims already filed in the earlier Form CA before the date of this Circular need not be re-submitted. 5. This Circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under section 196 of the Code.
