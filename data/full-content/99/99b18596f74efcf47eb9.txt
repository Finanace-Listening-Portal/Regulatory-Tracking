DICGC Protection Is a Bank-Level Limit Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation protection generally covers eligible deposits at insured banks up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank in the same right and capacity, combining principal and interest. Multiple branches of the same bank are not separate insurance limits. Savings balances, current-account balances and fixed deposits at that bank can therefore form part of the same aggregation. Choose Cumulative or Periodic Interest Based on Cash Flow A cumulative deposit generally suits an investor who does not need regular interest payments and wants the proceeds at maturity, while periodic-payout options may suit monthly or quarterly spending needs.
