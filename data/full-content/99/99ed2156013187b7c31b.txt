The appointment has been made under Section 4(1), read with Section 3 and Sections 5(1) and 5(1A) of the Customs Act, 1962. The notification identifies the concerned importers or exporters, show cause notice numbers, notice dates, existing order-in-original deadlines and extended dates wherever specified. The notification covers proceedings involving several importers and exporters, including Sara Healthcare Private Limited, NS International, Sumitomo Chemical India Limited, Titan Biotech Limited, Indofil Industries Limited, Indian Oil Corporation Limited, JSW Steel Limited and other entities listed in the Schedule. The accompanying table specifies the relevant adjudication particulars for each proceeding. Certain cases contain express extended adjudication deadlines extending into 2027, while other entries show the last date for passing an order-in-original without a separate extended date in the final column. The notification does not disclose the substantive allegations or proposed duty demands underlying these show cause notices. The appointment is relevant to the administration of customs adjudication proceedings and the allocation of statutory adjudicating authority for the identified cases. The Commissioner of Customs (Adjudication), Mumbai, is authorised to exercise the specified jurisdiction in place of the Commissioner of Customs, NS-I Commissionerate, Mumbai Customs Zone-II, for the listed notices. Importers, exporters and authorised representatives should verify their respective show cause notice numbers and the applicable dates in the notification. The notification expressly provides that it comes into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette.
