Articles 1 min read RBI extends overseas KYC certification facility to FPIs The Reserve Bank of India has extended the facility of accepting original certified copies of KYC documents to Foreign Portfolio Investors, allowing documents to be certified by specified overseas banks, notaries, judges, magistrates and Indian missions. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Sep 18, 2026 at 07:16 PM IST var news_msid = 134337247; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on Friday extended an existing KYC facility available to Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs), allowing banks to accept original certified copies of KYC documents certified by specified authorities abroad.Under the existing KYC framework, banks are required to compare a copy of the customer's Aadhaar proof or officially valid document with the original and record the comparison on the copy. RBI has now provided an alternative route for NRIs, PIOs and FPIs, under which banks can obtain an original certified copy of the document instead. Advt The certified copy can be issued by authorised officials of overseas branches of Scheduled Commercial Banks registered in India, branches of overseas banks with which Indian banks have relationships, a Notary Public abroad, a Court Magistrate, a Judge, or an Indian Embassy or Consulate General in the country where the non-resident customer resides.The move extends the certification facility that was earlier available to NRIs and PIOs to FPIs. RBI said the existing instructions were reviewed and the facility was extended to FPIs.The amendment has been made across RBI's KYC directions for commercial banks, small finance banks, local area banks, regional rural banks, rural co-operative banks and urban co-operative banks.The respective amendments replace the existing provision defining a "certified copy" to include the alternative certification route for FPIs along with NRIs and PIOs.The directions come into force with immediate effect from September 18, 2026.
