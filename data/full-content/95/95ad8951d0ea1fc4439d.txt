} } Listen to This ArticleBanks parked ₹79,655 crore at the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI’s) 29-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction on Thursday, against the notified amount of ₹2 trillion. The central bank accepted the bid amount at a cut-off rate of 5.49 per cent. Market participants expect the RBI to announce open market operation (OMO) sales after Friday’s government bond auction, as other liquidity absorption tools appear limited. Dealers said banks showed limited interest in VRRR auctions because the money is parked only for the specified tenor and returns to the banking system afterwards. They said banks would rather deploy funds in OMO sales, where they buy securities from the central bank and liquidity is absorbed for longer, than in VRRR auctions. “Already the call rates have come up a bit. But that is because of the repo rate hike. RBI has said it will deploy instruments to absorb liquidity. Everyone is just waiting for the OMO sale. So, no one is very much interested in the VRRR, knowing full well that that money is going to come back after a certain period of time,” said a dealer at a primary dealership. The weighted average call rate was at 5.30 per cent on Thursday, against the previous close of 5.36 per cent.
