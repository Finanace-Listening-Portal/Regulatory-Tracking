The company reported a consolidated revenue of Rs. 1,907 crore, marking a 7% increase compared to the same period in the previous fiscal year. This growth was accompanied by a significant rise in net income, which reached Rs. 702 crore, representing a 29% year-on-year increase. The company’s operating income for the quarter stood at Rs. 911 crore, a 25% increase from the previous year. The products business, a major contributor to the company’s revenue, posted Rs. 1,716 crore, reflecting a 6% growth. Meanwhile, the services business demonstrated a robust 15% increase, generating Rs. 191 crore in revenue. The remaining performance obligation, a key indicator of future revenue, rose by 14% to Rs. 7,211 crore. For the half-year period ending 30th September 2026, Oracle Financial Services Software reported a consolidated revenue of Rs. 5,032 crore, a substantial 38% increase from the previous year. The operating income for this period surged by 77% to Rs. 2,772 crore, while net income soared by 78% to Rs. 2,118 crore. The products and services segments both showed strong performance, with revenues increasing by 41% and 11%, respectively. Avadhut Ketkar, the company’s Managing Director and CEO, attributed the strong financial performance to the company’s deep domain expertise and comprehensive portfolio of solutions. He highlighted the company’s advancements in AI-powered capabilities as a key factor in addressing the evolving needs of financial institutions globally. Manish Bhandari, the CFO, noted the company’s commitment to operational efficiency, which resulted in a healthy net margin of 37% for the quarter, an increase of six percentage points year-on-year. Analysts view Oracle Financial Services Software’s results as a testament to its strategic positioning in the financial technology sector. The company’s ability to maintain strong operating and net margins, alongside its focus on innovation and client engagement, positions it well for continued growth in the competitive financial services market.
