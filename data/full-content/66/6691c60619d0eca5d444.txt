National Stock Exchange of India Circular Department: Listing Circular Ref. No.: NSE/CML/2026/12 Date: May 04, 2026 To, The Company Secretary, All Listed Entities Subject: Filing of disclosures in XBRL format under Regulation 7(2) and Regulation 7(3) of SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 Dear Sir/Madam, This is to inform you that disclosures under Regulation 7(2) and Regulation 7(3) of SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 were previously being filed through the following path: NEAPS  Compliance  Disclosures  Insider Trading The above-mentioned filing path has now been discontinued, and a new XBRL based utility has been introduced for submission of such disclosures. Accordingly, all listed companies, REITs and InvITs are required to submit the aforesaid disclosure only in XBRL format through the revised process as detailed below: To download XBRL utility: NEAPS  Compliance  Download XBRL Utilities For Submission of disclosures: NEAPS  Compliance  Common XBRL Upload  Prohibition of Insider Trading Please note that previously filed disclosures shall continue to be available at the following link: https://www.nseindia.com/companies-listing/corporate-filings-insider-trading-archive-data Further, with reference to Exchange Circular No. NSE/CML/2026/11 dated May 04, 2026, the said utility will also be available for Single Filing system through API-based integration. You are requested to take note of the above and ensure compliance. Yours faithfully, For National Stock Exchange of India Limited Harshad Dharod Chief Manager-Listing Compliance
