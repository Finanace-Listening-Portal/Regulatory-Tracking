CompaniesCBI Files Case Against Subhash Chandra, Others Over Alleged Rs 1,322 Cr LIC Housing Finance FraudReported by: Bhavatosh SinghUpdated Sep 5, 2026, 14:06 ISTCBI books Subhash Chandra and three others over alleged cheating involving more than Rs 1,322 crore in LIC Housing Finance loans.Subhash Chandra (File Photo) follow usfollow usThe Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has registered a criminal case against Essel Group Chairman Subhash Chandra and three others over alleged financial fraud involving more than Rs 1,322 crore owed to LIC Housing Finance Ltd (LICHFL). The case follows a complaint filed by LICHFL, which has accused Chandra, Pankaj Suroliya, Amish Pandya and Rajeev Dholakia of allegedly cheating the housing finance company and conspiring to secure credit facilities on the basis of disputed financial disclosures.According to the complaint, Chandra submitted a net worth certificate in 2018 declaring his net worth at Rs 59,113 crore. LICHFL has alleged that the certificate was used to obtain loan facilities for M/s Vasant Sagar Properties Pvt Ltd and other companies linked to him.Relying on the financial certificate, LICHFL subsequently extended credit facilities to three companies associated with Chandra, the complaint stated.The housing finance company has now raised objections over Chandra's statements made during subsequent insolvency proceedings, particularly his denial that he had ever possessed a net worth of Rs 50,000 crore, as reflected in the certificates submitted to LICHFL.LICHFL has alleged cheating, criminal breach of trust and conspiracy in its complaint to the investigating agency.The complaint given to the CBI Head Office reads, "A loan facility of Rs. 500 Crores to Vasant Sagar Properties Pvt. Ltd. and Pan India Infra projects Pvt. Ltd. (Co-borrower) as a Home Entity Loan (Takeover & Top-up for Business Expansion) under Loan Account No. 610700003341 secured by a Letter of Continuing Guarantee dated 28th March 18 executed by Subhash Chandra (Vasant Sagar Loan Facility) and a loan facility of Rs. 480 Crores to Digital Subscriber Management and Consultancy Services Pvt. Ltd. and Spirit Infra power and Multi Ventures Pvt. Ltd. (Co-borrower) as a Rental Discounting facility under the Scheme of Rental Securitization under Loan Account No. 610700003500 secured by a Letter of Continuing Guarantee dated 10th August 18 executed by Subhash Chandra (Digital Loan Facility).""The Vasant Sagar Loan Facility was granted inter alia on the basis of the Net Worth Certificate submitted by Subhash Chandra, issued by 28th March 2018 issued by DIM & Co, certifying that his networth was US$ 6,197.62 Million (approximately Rs. 59,113.21 Crores), as on 31st March 2017 Similarly. the Digital Loan Facility was granted inter alia on the basis of the Net Worth Certificate submitted by Subhash Chandra, issued by MPJ & Co.. Chartered Accountants on 06.07.2018, certifying that his networth was Rs. 40,562 Crores," it added."Thereafter, defaults have occurred in the Vasant Sagar Loan Facility and Digital Loan Facility. In the course of the subsequent proceedings under the Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code, 2016 for the personal insolvency resolution of Subhash Chandra, he categorically denied having the Net Worth as stated in the Net Worth Certificates, which he had submitted to LICHFL to cause approval and disbursal of the loan detailed above," the complaint states."In the course of the proceedings. he has stated that his net worth in 2024 is Rs. 31.79 crore and stated that, even in 2017-18. he did not have the net worth of more than Rs. 40.000 Crores," it noted.Subhash Chandra’s Statement In Insolvency ProceedingsMeanwhile, on 27th August, Zee Group founder Subhash Chandra, in a statement, claimed he has not borrowed any money from any lender and is only a personal guarantor. Total claim against him as a Personal Guarantor, in the personal insolvency proceedings, is only Rs 3,992 crore by the objectors (creditors who had rejected the plan) of the plan and not Rs 22,000 crore (which is the total claim), out of which Rs 21,696 crore was admitted by the Resolution Professional. “Out of which (Rs 3,992 crore), also a claim of Rs 620 crore has been settled and further Rs 1,063 crore offered to be paid by the borrower entities,” Chandra said.
