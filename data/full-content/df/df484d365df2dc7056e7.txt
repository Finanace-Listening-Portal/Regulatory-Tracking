The deal is the largest foreign investment in India's financial sector so far and a major milestone in the country's banking privatisation drive.In a clarification on the legislative developments regarding reduction in government holding in IDBI Bank, the ministry said the Delhi High Court on December 17, 2018, held that the IDBI (Repeal) Act, 2003, contained no prohibition against reducing the government's stake in IDBI Bank Ltd. It may be recalled that during the Union Budget, 2003, discussions and the parliamentary debate on the IDBI (Repeal) Act, 2003, the government had moved an amendment seeking to restrict the dilution of the government's stake. But, the amendment was rejected and did not form part of the enacted legislation.Following that, the Delhi High Court in the All India IDBI Officers' Association v. Union of India & Others, case decided on December 17, 2018, that "there is no ambiguity in the language of the IDBI Repeal Act, 2003 and there is no scope to read any provision into the said enactment which proscribes the government from reducing or divesting its stake in IDBI Ltd." The judgment also addressed the doctrine of promissory estoppel, which was relied upon in challenging the government's decision to dilute its shareholding.
