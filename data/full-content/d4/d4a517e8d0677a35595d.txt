Articles 1 min read Edelweiss Financial Services Q2 profit rises 18 pc to Rs 207 cr Edelweiss Financial Services has reported an impressive 18% rise in consolidated net profit for the second quarter, totaling Rs 207 crore, compared to Rs 175 crore from the previous year. Total income surged to Rs 2,602 crore, a marked increase from Rs 1,900 crore last year. Notably, interest income also grew, reaching Rs 720 crore, while their subsidiary, Edelweiss Alternatives, successfully raised Rs 1,200 crore through its IPO last month.
