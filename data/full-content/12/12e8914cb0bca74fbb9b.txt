Articles 4 min read Gold loans grow 60.6% to ₹23.3 lakh crore, retail credit reaches ₹185.2 lakh crore: Report According to a recent report by CRIF Credit Information Services Private Limited, India's retail credit portfolio expanded 18.8% year-on-year to ₹185.2 lakh crore as of September 2026, led by gold loans, secured business loans and consumer durable loans. Housing loans remained the largest asset class, accounting for 25.1% of the portfolio, while higher ticket sizes and deeper credit penetration beyond the top 100 cities shaped lending trends. The report highlighted that housing loans continued to remain the largest asset class at ₹46.4 lakh crore, accounting for 25.1% of the overall portfolio. Advt The expansion was supported by higher growth in gold loans, secured business loans and consumer durable loans. Their increasing contribution also reflected changes in the composition of retail credit, alongside a shift towards higher ticket sizes and deeper penetration across semi-urban and rural markets.Gold Loans SurgeGold loans recorded 60.6% year-on-year growth to reach ₹23.3 lakh crore as of September 2026, increasing their share of the retail credit portfolio by 3.3 percentage points to 12.6%. The segment also registered 7.5% quarter-on-quarter growth, according to the report.Gold loan originations value increased 27.2% year-on-year to ₹8.15 lakh crore in Q2 FY27, while originations volume declined 4.1%. The average ticket size increased 32.7% year-on-year to ₹2.21 lakh, reflecting a shift towards higher-value borrowing.The report further highlighted that loans of ₹5 lakh and above accounted for 40.3% of gold loan originations value in Q2 FY27, up from 28.8% in the corresponding quarter of the previous financial year. Southern states continued to account for more than 70% of the gold loan portfolio.Business Lending ExpandsSecured business loans grew 45.2% year-on-year to ₹19.6 lakh crore, increasing their share of the retail credit portfolio by 1.9 percentage points to 10.6%. The report highlighted that sole-proprietor loans, including loans against property, business loans and commercial vehicle loans, accounted for approximately 30% of total retail credit in Q2 FY27. Advt Property loans also recorded growth of 22.4% year-on-year to ₹16.9 lakh crore, with a 7.8% quarter-on-quarter increase. Unsecured business loans, meanwhile, remained unchanged year-on-year at ₹10.5 lakh crore, although the portfolio grew 7.3% sequentially.Consumer Credit GrowsConsumer durable loans expanded 44.5% year-on-year to ₹1.3 lakh crore as of September 2026. Originations value increased 45.8% year-on-year, while originations volume grew 40.9% in Q2 FY27.The report noted the segment's importance in bringing new borrowers into the formal credit system, with consumer durable loans accounting for 30.7% of overall new-to-credit accounts in Q2 FY27, compared with 32.6% in Q2 FY26. Advt Personal loans grew 13.8% year-on-year to ₹17.5 lakh crore, while auto loans increased 18.2% to ₹10.2 lakh crore. Housing loans recorded 10.4% growth to ₹46.4 lakh crore. In contrast, credit card outstanding declined 3.8% year-on-year to ₹3.4 lakh crore. Two-wheeler loan portfolios grew 4.3% year-on-year to ₹1.8 lakh crore, although they contracted 5.2% quarter-on-quarter.Ticket Sizes IncreaseThe report findings pointed to a continued shift towards higher-value lending across several retail credit categories. Home loans above ₹75 lakh accounted for 42.1% of originations value in Q2 FY27, increasing from 40.9% in Q1 FY27. Advt In auto lending, loans above ₹10 lakh represented 52.3% of originations value, up 2.3 percentage points quarter-on-quarter. Gold loans of ₹5 lakh and above accounted for 40.3% of originations value, indicating an increasing contribution from higher-ticket borrowing.Geographic Penetration DeepensCredit penetration continued to expand beyond India's top 100 cities, particularly across consumer durable and two-wheeler lending. The report highlighted that 45.9% of consumer durable loan originations value came from beyond the top 100 cities in Q2 FY27, compared with 43.7% in Q2 FY26.For two-wheeler loans, the corresponding share stood at 49.9%, unchanged from the previous year. Gold loans also recorded an increase in their contribution from these markets, with their share of originations value rising to 34.4% from 33.4%.The report further identified Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan as important growth markets across home, personal, auto and consumer durable lending.Lender Shares ShiftPublic sector banks increased their share of home loan originations value to 44.5% in Q2 FY27 from 40% in Q1 FY27, supported by higher-ticket lending. NBFCs continued to dominate originations volume in personal loans and consumer durable loans, accounting for 92.1% and 90.3%, respectively.In consumer durable lending, NBFCs also accounted for 85.1% of originations value. In gold lending, their share of originations value increased to 31.4% in Q2 FY27, up around 4 percentage points quarter-on-quarter.The report also noted that PSU banks increased their share of personal loan originations value to 25.8% from 23.3% in the preceding quarter, supported by a higher contribution from loans of ₹10 lakh and above.Asset Quality MonitoredDespite overall portfolio expansion, the report flagged emerging asset-quality concerns across select lending categories. Personal loan PAR 91–180 increased to 1.1% in September 2026 from 0.8% in June 2026, with the increase driven by NBFCs and ticket sizes between ₹2 lakh and ₹10 lakh.There was an increase in PAR 91–180 for consumer durable loans among NBFCs and loans below ₹50,000. In two-wheeler lending, early-bucket delinquency remained concentrated in NBFC portfolios and loans below ₹1 lakh.Overall, the report highlighted the continued expansion of India's retail credit market alongside changes in product composition, ticket sizes and geographic penetration, while identifying asset-quality trends across select portfolios as an important area for monitoring.
