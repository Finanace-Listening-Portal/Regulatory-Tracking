The amendment addresses difficulties faced by seafarers in furnishing overseas address proof because of the nature of their employment. It modifies paragraph 8.3(a) of the KYC/AML/CFT Master Circular dated 25 September 2025, as previously amended by PFRDA Circular No. PFRDA/2025/22/REG-POP/04 dated 2 December 2025. Under the revised provision, regulated entities must accept either a valid employment contract with the employer or shipping company for a seafarer proceeding on a fresh contract, or a Continuous Discharge Certificate (CDC) containing a disembarkation stamp or record not older than six months, as proof of address. For seafarers currently onboard a ship, the current address may be furnished as either the overseas address of the employer or the subscriber’s Indian address. The relaxation is specifically applicable to NRI subscribers having a seafarer work profile and does not generally dispense with identity verification or other applicable customer due diligence requirements. All remaining provisions of the December 2025 circular and the September 2025 Master Circular continue unchanged. Issued under Section 14(1) of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority Act, 2013, the amendment takes effect immediately from 13 November 2026. The measure is intended to simplify NPS onboarding for eligible maritime professionals while maintaining the prescribed KYC and anti-money laundering safeguards. PENSION FUND REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY Circular No. PFRDA/2026/55/REG-POP/10 | Dated: 13th November 2026 To, All Points of Presence (PoPs), All Central Recordkeeping Agencies (CRAs), NPS Trust and other stakeholders Subject: Amendment to Circular dated 02.12.2025 regarding KYC requirements for Non-Resident Indian (NRI) and Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) subscribers to NPS Reference is invited to PFRDA Circular No. PFRDA/2025/22/REG-POP/04 dated 02.12.2025 on “Amendment to Master Circular – Guidelines on Know Your Customer / Anti Money Laundering/Combating the Financing of Terrorism (KYC/AML/CFT) issued on 25.09.2025 with an emphasis on KYC requirements for Non-Resident Indian (NRI) and Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) onboarding to NPS”. 2. The aforesaid circular prescribes the documents admissible as Proof of Identity (PoI) and Proof of Address (PoA), along with the applicable Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) requirements for onboarding of NRI/OCI subscribers to NPS. However, the NRIs, who are in ‘Seafarer” profile, are finding it difficult to provide overseas address proof at the time of onboarding into NPS. In order to facilitate appropriate verification of the employment and non-resident status of NRI subscribers having a seafarer work profile, it has been decided to prescribe the documentary requirements for such subscribers. 3. Accordingly, the following provision shall be inserted after the existing provisions under “Note” as point no. iv to Para 8.3 (a), as amended vide circular dated 02.12.2025: (iv) For the purpose of opening NPS Tier I accounts, all Regulated Entities (REs) shall accept any one of the following documents as Proof of Address (PoA) in respect of an NRI subscriber having a seafarer work profile. Further, in respect of such subscribers who are currently onboard a ship, the current address may be furnished as either the overseas address of the employer or the Indian address of the subscriber: 1. A valid employment contract with the employer/shipping company, in the case of a seafarer proceeding on a fresh contract; 2. A Continuous Discharge Certificate (CDC), provided that the disembarkation stamp/record therein is not more than six months old;” 4. All other provisions of the circular dated 02.12.2025 and the Master Circular on KYC/AML/CFT dated 25.09.2025 shall remain unchanged. 5. This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14(1) of the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority Act, 2013, and shall come into effect from the date of its issuance.
