This accolade is a significant milestone for KCL, recognising its commitment to responsible copper production under a globally acknowledged framework. The certification was announced on 11 November 2026, highlighting KCL’s adherence to rigorous environmental, social, and governance (ESG) standards. The Veramark Assurance framework is an independent assurance system that evaluates and promotes responsible practices across the copper, nickel, and zinc value chains. Facilities awarded this certification undergo thorough third-party audits to ensure compliance with high ESG performance standards. Kutch Copper’s achievement places it among an elite group of global producers, aligning with international standards and reinforcing its commitment to sustainable production. Dr. Vinay Prakash, CEO of Adani Natural Resources and Managing Director of KCL, emphasised the importance of this milestone. He stated, “Achieving the Copper Mark, a first for any Indian producer, underscores our unwavering commitment to sustainable and responsible production. This milestone positions Kutch Copper among an elite group of leading global producers adhering to internationally recognised standards.” The certification not only enhances KCL’s reputation but also advances its goals to align with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, particularly SDG 12 on responsible consumption and production. The demand for copper is escalating due to rapid advancements in renewable energy, electric mobility, and digital infrastructure. In this context, the Copper Mark certification strengthens KCL’s position as a trusted supplier of responsibly produced copper. This recognition is expected to bolster the company’s objective to become a preferred partner for global businesses focused on building sustainable and resilient supply chains. As the market for responsibly sourced materials grows, KCL’s certification is likely to attract increased interest from customers, investors, and supply chain partners prioritising sustainable production practices.
