SBI Life has gained share in non-par savings at the expense of unit-linked insurance plans (ULIPs) and group savings, while HDFC Life has increased its contribution from annuity and non-par business. Stronger protection business has also supported margins across several insurers.Value of new business (VNB) margins expanded by 90-450 basis points year on year for most life insurers in the September quarter. The improvement was driven by stronger protection business, favourable interest-rate movements that benefited non-par products, higher annuity contributions relative to ULIPs and operating leverage, according to Kotak Institutional Equities. Advt SBI Life reported a 418-basis-point increase in non-par savings, which gained more than 200 basis points of market share from ULIPs and group savings. The insurer is also shifting towards pure-term products from return-of-premium plans, with pure-term products carrying significantly higher margins. Its VNB grew 12% and its margin stood at 29.2%. However, annualised premium equivalent (APE) growth remained muted at 7%, with single-digit growth in both the agency channel and its parent bank.HDFC Life's product mix shifted from participating products towards annuity and non-par business, which carry higher margins. The insurer reported 15% growth in VNB, while APE increased 13%. Savings APE grew 12% and protection APE rose 21%. Its bancassurance channel grew 12%, but agency growth slowed to 6% from 20% in the first quarter. Individual protection business growth also moderated to 15% from 41% a year earlier.Shifting matrixICICI Prudential Life reported 20% growth in VNB, with its margin expanding 100 basis points year on year to 26%. APE grew 16%, while protection APE increased 34%, riding the GST exemption-driven rally. Partnerships, including brokers, were the fastest-growing channel, rising 38% and accounting for 15% of APE. Agency business also returned to growth, increasing 4%.Axis Max Life reported 8% APE growth and 15% VNB growth. Its VNB margin stood at 27%, with group credit protection supporting performance even as ULIP business lost momentum. LIC, meanwhile, reported just 4% APE growth but recorded 17% VNB growth as its margin expanded 250 basis points to 21.8%. The insurer continued to increase the share of non-par savings and ramp up non-par protection business. Advt The second half of the financial year could be shaped by higher interest rates, a high base of term business and business in the sunset period before new distribution reforms take effect. The potential impact of these reforms on credit protection business remains a concern, particularly for HDFC Life and ICICI Prudential Life. SBI Life is considered better placed, with minimal disruption expected.
