Advt Power and low-carbon fuels attracted about USD 19 billion in equity funding between 2021 and October 2026, while transport received USD 9.5 billion. Manufacturing and waste, and agriculture, attracted only around USD 1 billion each, highlighting the concentration of capital in commercially established segments.India's climate equity funding reached USD 6.2 billion across 243 rounds in the first 10 months of 2026, indicating a shift towards fewer but larger transactions."India's climate transition is no longer a question of intent. Capital is arriving, but unevenly," said Anirban Mukherjee, managing director and senior partner at BCG.While renewable power and electric mobility have attracted investment as their economics have improved, the next challenge is to build investor confidence in energy storage, grids, green fuels, industrial decarbonisation and climate adaptation, he said.The report identifies energy storage, grid infrastructure, fast-charging networks, fleet electrification and domestic battery manufacturing as emerging investment opportunities. Green hydrogen and biofuels could also attract capital as industrial demand and policy support develop.Climate adaptation is another emerging market, particularly in water management, cooling, resilient infrastructure, insurance and climate-risk analytics. The report cites potential economic benefits of USD 2-19 for every dollar invested in adaptation. Advt However, financing constraints remain a major obstacle. Climate projects can require 12-15 years to mature, compared with typical venture fund lifetimes of seven to 10 years. Early-stage projects also struggle to secure funding because they lack established customers, testing infrastructure and predictable revenues.The report calls for a national climate-finance taxonomy, shared testing facilities, reliable climate-risk data and greater mobilisation of patient and catalytic capital through blended-finance structures. Stronger payment security, green public procurement and streamlined project approvals could also improve investment viability. Advt Nakul Zaveri of LeapFrog Investments and co-chair of IVCA's Climate & Sustainability Council said the priority was to broaden investable opportunities and develop financing structures suited to businesses at different stages of maturity.The report argues that India's next climate investment cycle will depend not simply on raising more capital but on directing it towards underfunded sectors and technologies that can deliver commercially viable solutions at scale.With much of the country's future infrastructure yet to be built, investment decisions over the coming decades could shape how effectively India combines economic growth, energy security and its 2070 net-zero ambition. PTI Advt New Delhi, Nov 24 (IANS) Apex business chamber CII said on Tuesday that the government’s sustained efforts to strengthen India’s investment climate through policy reforms, infrastructure development and regulatory improvements have created a strong foundation for private investment, with encouraging evidence of growing investment intentions, corporate capital formation and project execution.
