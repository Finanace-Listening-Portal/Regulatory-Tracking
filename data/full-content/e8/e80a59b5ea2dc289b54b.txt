Articles 2 min read India bonds decline ahead of fresh debt sale as elevated oil prices fan inflation worries Indian government bonds decreased in early trading due to rising oil prices and cautious investor sentiment. The benchmark 6.94% 2036 bond yield increased significantly amid higher expectations for a rate hike. New Delhi is set to raise 330 billion rupees through a bond sale, contributing to market tension. Concerns over food-price pressures and inflation continue to influence the Reserve Bank of India's upcoming decision. The yield is already up by 8 basis points for the week.New Delhi is due to raise 330 billion rupees ($3.57 billion) through a bond sale later in the day."Oil (price) remains the key risk for the bond market and if it stays high, it will add to more selling pressure on bonds in the run-up to Reserve Bank of India's decision," a trader with a state-run bank said. Advt The benchmark Brent crude contract held above $96 a barrel in Asian trading, as fresh escalation in the Middle East conflict renewed fears that supply routes could be disrupted.For India, the rise in crude prices is a significant macroeconomic concern, as the country is one of the world's largest oil importers. More expensive crude can put pressure on its current-account deficit, the rupee and add to already heightened inflation pressures.Investor sentiment was also dampened after the RBI said food-price pressures remained broad-based and pointed to a "higher generalisation of price pressures" across the economy.The comments come after India's retail inflation rose above the RBI's upper tolerance threshold of 6% in October, increasing pressure on policymakers to maintain a hawkish bias at their December 4 meeting.A majority of economists polled by Reuters expect a 25-basis-point rate increase. Some market participants see scope for a larger move, as indicated by overnight index swaps and treasury bill yields.RatesOIS rates climbed 4-6 basis points across major points of the curve, tracking bond yields.The one-year rate was at 6.57%, while the two-year rate was at 6.51% and the liquid five-year rate was at 6.50%.
