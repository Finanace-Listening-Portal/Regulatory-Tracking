We take any report of potential violations seriously,” an HDFC Bank spokesperson said in a statement. Advt “In line with our policies, an internal review into this matter is currently underway to gather and evaluate all relevant facts. To protect the integrity of the process and the privacy of all parties involved, we do not comment on active internal reviews. Appropriate action will be taken based on the findings, once the review is complete,” the spokesperson added.An internal investigation had found evidence that two senior HDFC Bank executives disclosed confidential business strategies and possible market-sensitive information to outsiders.The bank’s review is still underway and it is examining whether the information was misused, according to the report.The development comes ahead of Anup Bagchi taking charge as HDFC Bank’s managing director and chief executive officer on October 27. Bagchi, who is currently a senior executive at ICICI Group, is set to succeed Sashidhar Jagdishan, who is stepping down after six years at the helm.
