The notification amends Notification No. 36/2001-Customs (N.T.) by substituting Tables 1, 2 and 3 containing tariff values for specified imported goods. The revised values cover crude palm oil, RBD palm oil, other palm oil, crude palmolein, RBD palmolein, other palmolein, crude soybean oil and brass scrap in Table 1; specified gold and silver goods in Table 2; and areca nuts in Table 3. The notification also refers to Notification No. 45/2025-Customs dated 24 October 2025 for specified gold and silver entries. The revised tariff values take effect from 1 October 2026. The principal Notification No. 36/2001-Customs (N.T.) has been amended periodically, including most recently through Notification No. 75/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 15 September 2026.
