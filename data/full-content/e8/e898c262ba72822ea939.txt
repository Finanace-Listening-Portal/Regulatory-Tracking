The picks reflect opportunities across small finance banks and large private sector lenders, with improving asset quality, loan book growth and business fundamentals emerging as key investment considerations.Here are the top Muhurat picks for the banking sector:HDFC BankJainam has included HDFC Bank among its five stock picks for Samvat 2083. The selection is based on a combination of fundamental and technical analysis, with the research house evaluating business performance, financials, valuations, growth prospects and key risks. Advt Jainam's report does not disclose a target price or implied upside for the stock.Kotak Mahindra BankKotak Mahindra Bank is another banking pick identified by Jainam for Samvat 2083. The private sector lender also features in Motilal Oswal Wealth Management's list of Diwali recommendations.Motilal Oswal has assigned a target price of ₹1,050 against a reference market price of ₹972, implying an upside of around 8%. The brokerage's recommendation places the bank among its selected investment ideas for the new Samvat.Ujjivan Small Finance BankKotak Neo has picked Ujjivan Small Finance Bank as one of its top investment ideas for Samvat 2083, assigning a target price of ₹85 against a market price of ₹53. This implies a potential upside of around 60%.The brokerage's positive outlook is supported by 32% growth in the lender's loan book, improving asset quality and credit costs falling below 1%. Kotak Neo has recommended the stock with a Buy rating and a 12-month investment horizon.ICICI BankICICI Bank features in the Diwali recommendations of multiple brokerages, including JM Financial, Angel One and Bajaj Broking Research. Angel One has assigned a target price of ₹1,800 against a reference market price of ₹1,363, implying an upside potential of 32%. The brokerage values the bank at 18.8 times its estimated FY29 earnings per share of ₹95.70, factoring in expectations of mid-teens earnings growth. Advt Angel One noted that the bank raised around $18 billion through FCNR(B) deposits under the RBI's swap facility in Q2 FY27. This pushed deposit growth to 27.1%, while loan growth stood at 30%. The brokerage expects the impact of the funding mix on margins to ease as the funds are deployed and higher-cost deposits are retired. JM Financial has set a target price of ₹1,710, implying an upside of 24%, while Bajaj Broking Research has a target of ₹1,570. State Bank of IndiaState Bank of India is among Monarch Networth Capital's 14 Diwali stock recommendations. The brokerage has assigned a target price of ₹1,265 to the lender, indicating an upside potential of 31%. Advt SBI is one of the banking stocks in Monarch's portfolio, which also includes companies across defence, healthcare, technology, capital goods and consumer sectors.DCB BankDCB Bank features in Axis Securities' nine Diwali stock recommendations for Samvat 2083. The brokerage has indicated an upside potential of around 20% for the private sector lender.Axis Securities' recommendation reflects its positive view on the private banking theme and the opportunities arising from the credit growth cycle.Karur Vysya BankKarur Vysya Bank is among Shriram Research's 11 Diwali stock picks. The brokerage has set a target price of ₹418 against a reference market price of ₹345, implying an upside potential of 21%. Advt The bank is the only BFSI stock in Shriram Research's list, which also includes companies across consumer, industrial and other sectors.
