Canara Bank, Union Bank of India and HDFC Bank each held a 6 per cent share, while Axis Bank accounted for 5 per cent of the market.Axis Bank recorded the highest year-on-year growth of 13 per cent in active debit cards in October 2026, followed by State Bank of India, HDFC Bank and Punjab National Bank, each reporting a 5 per cent increase, according to RBI data collated by 1Lattice.Bank of India and Punjab National Bank each held a 4 per cent share, while ICICI Bank accounted for 3 per cent. The Others category, comprising banks with less than 1 per cent of new cards added, accounted for 21 per cent of the active debit card market. Advt On a year-on-year basis, Bank of Baroda recorded a 12 per cent contraction in active debit cards, the steepest decline among the individual banks tracked. The Others category also contracted 6 per cent. ICICI Bank registered a 1 per cent increase, while Bank of India, Canara Bank and Union Bank each reported 2 per cent growth.HDFC Bank, SBI Card lead credit card market; Bank of Baroda sees robust growthHDFC Bank continued to hold the largest share of the active credit card market at 22 per cent, followed by SBI Card at 19 per cent and ICICI Bank at 16 per cent, according to the data.Axis Bank held a 13 per cent share, while Kotak Mahindra Bank and RBL Bank each accounted for 4 per cent. Bank of Baroda held a 3 per cent share, followed by IndusInd Bank at 2 per cent and American Express at 1 per cent. The Others category accounted for the remaining 16 per cent.In terms of year-on-year growth, Bank of Baroda recorded a 21 per cent increase in active credit cards, the highest among the individual issuers tracked. ICICI Bank, Axis Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank each recorded 9 per cent growth, while HDFC Bank and SBI Card reported an 8 per cent increase.RBL Bank registered a 6 per cent increase in active credit cards. However, IndusInd Bank and American Express recorded contractions of 6 per cent and 5 per cent, respectively. The Others category registered a 24 per cent year-on-year increase.
