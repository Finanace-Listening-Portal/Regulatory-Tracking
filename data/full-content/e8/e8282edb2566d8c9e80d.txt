Articles 1 min read Aadhar Housing Finance’s net profit rises 16% to ₹308.29 crore in Q2 FY27 Aadhar Housing Finance has achieved an impressive 15.7% increase in net consolidated profit after tax for Q2 FY27. During this quarter, the firm also reported a total income surge of 16.5%, totaling ₹1,047.76 crore. As of September 30, 2026, their assets under management rose by 18% to reach ₹32,643 crore. Loan disbursements climbed 22% to ₹2,577 crore, while they maintained a net profit margin of 29. It had posted a net profit of ₹266.35 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous fiscal.The company’s net consolidated total income increased 16.5 per cent to ₹1,047.76 crore in Q2 FY27 from ₹899.19 crore in Q2 FY26.Assets under management (AUM) increased 18 per cent year-on-year to ₹32,643 crore as of September 30, 2026, from ₹27,554 crore a year earlier. Disbursements during Q2 FY27 stood at ₹2,577 crore, registering a 22 per cent year-on-year growth. Advt The company had more than 3.48 lakh loan accounts as of September 30, 2026.Gross non-performing assets stood at 1.35 per cent, while net NPA was 0.89 per cent.For H1 FY27, the company’s net consolidated total income increased 17 per cent to ₹2,048.84 crore from ₹1,750.47 crore in H1 FY26. Net consolidated profit after tax for the first half increased 17.3 per cent to ₹590.60 crore from ₹503.65 crore in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal.As of September 30, 2026, its net worth stood at ₹8,195.02 crore. Its debt-equity ratio was 2.35, while total debt-to-total assets ratio stood at 70.26 per cent.The company reported a net profit margin of 29.42 per cent, capital adequacy ratio of 42.11 per cent and liquidity coverage ratio of 239.76 per cent.
