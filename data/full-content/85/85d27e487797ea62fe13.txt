Typical risks include an invoice appearing in GSTR-2B but not in books, an invoice in books missing from GSTR-2B, duplicated purchase entries, unmatched credit notes and incorrect IGST or CGST/SGST classification. A correct invoice match does not establish eligibility where Section 17(5) blocks credit or other Section 16 conditions are unsatisfied. Check applicable statutory deadlines and reversal or reclaim provisions for the period.
