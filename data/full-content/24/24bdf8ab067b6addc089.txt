MarketsAxis Bank Q2 Results 2026: Net Profit Surges 38% To Rs 7,003 Crore YoYEdited by: Priya RaghuvanshiUpdated: Oct 17, 2026, 15:55 ISTAxis Bank Q2 for the financial year 2027 net profit rose 38 per cent to Rs 7,003 crore, supported by higher NII, stronger lending growth and improved asset quality.Axis Bank Q2 FY26 Results (File Photo)follow usfollow usAxis Bank reported a strong rise in earnings for the September quarter of FY27, with net profit increasing 38 per cent year-on-year to Rs 7,003 crore. The private sector lender had posted a net profit of Rs 5,089 crore in the same quarter a year ago. Higher net interest income and an improvement in asset quality supported the bank’s performance. Net interest income (NII), which represents the difference between interest earned and interest paid, grew 12 per cent year-on-year to Rs 15,371 crore. The bank’s net interest margin (NIM) stood at 3.36 per cent, rising to 3.47 per cent after excluding the impact of foreign currency non-resident (FCNR-B) deposits.Operating profit rose 6 per cent to Rs 11,083 crore, while core operating profit increased 11 per cent to Rs 10,998 crore. Other income slipped 1 per cent to Rs 6,531 crore, although fee income grew 6 per cent to Rs 6,381 crore.Managing director and CEO Amitabh Chaudhry said the bank continued to prioritise digital payments, secure banking, AI-driven innovation and strategic partnerships to improve its customer offerings.Axis Bank Q2FY27: Asset Quality Shows ImprovementAxis Bank recorded a decline in both gross and net non-performing asset ratios during the September quarter. The gross NPA (GNPA) ratio fell by 30 basis points year-on-year to 1.16 per cent, while the net NPA (NNPA) ratio declined by 9 basis points to 0.35 per cent.Fresh additions to bad loans also moderated. Gross slippages stood at Rs 4,762 crore in Q2FY27, down from Rs 5,566 crore in the preceding quarter and Rs 5,696 crore in the corresponding period last year. The net slippage ratio improved by 30 basis points year-on-year to 0.75 per cent.The lender’s annualised net credit cost declined to 0.45 per cent, down 28 basis points from a year earlier and 18 basis points sequentially. Its provision coverage ratio remained unchanged at 70 per cent compared with both the previous quarter and the year-ago period.Provisions and contingencies at Rs 1,694 croreThe bank set aside Rs 1,694 crore for provisions and contingencies during the quarter. This included Rs 1,564 crore in specific loan-loss provisions.As of September 30, 2026, Axis Bank had built up Rs 15,860 crore in provisions against standard assets and additional provisions outside those maintained for non-performing assets. These were over and above the NPA provisions used to calculate the provision coverage ratio.Advances rise 23%, deposits increase 21%Axis Bank’s total advances expanded 23 per cent year-on-year and 9 per cent sequentially to Rs 13.74 lakh crore as of September 30, 2026.Retail lending grew 16 per cent to Rs 7.40 lakh crore, accounting for 54 per cent of net advances. Loans to small and medium enterprises (SMEs) increased 25 per cent to Rs 1.65 lakh crore, while corporate lending recorded growth of 34 per cent.Deposits rose 21 per cent year-on-year and 6 per cent quarter-on-quarter to Rs 14.52 lakh crore. Term deposits grew 27 per cent, while current account deposits advanced 7 per cent and savings account deposits increased 12 per cent. The current account and savings account (CASA) ratio stood at 36 per cent.The bank’s cost of funds declined by 8 basis points compared with the year-ago period.Axis Bank reported a capital adequacy ratio of 16.27 per cent as of September 30, 2026. Its common equity tier-1 (CET-1) ratio was 14.61 per cent, having improved by 23 basis points during the first half of FY27.The lender also held Rs 8,006 crore in additional provisions beyond those reflected in its reported capital adequacy ratio. These provisions provided a capital cushion equivalent to around 49 basis points.Axis Bank H1FY27 Earnings: Profit Climbs 30%For the first six months of FY27, Axis Bank’s net profit increased 30 per cent year-on-year to Rs 14,117 crore from Rs 10,896 crore in the corresponding period of FY26.Net interest income rose 10 per cent to Rs 30,018 crore, while fee income advanced 6 per cent to Rs 12,536 crore.
