The $8.6 billion fall in forex reserves in the week takes the cumulative decline to $59.7 in five weeks. Reserves had risen to a record high of $785.71 billion on September 4 after the RBI opened a concessional swap window in June to attract foreign inflows. Meanwhile, the rupee continued to strengthen on Friday, settling at 94.96 per dollar — the highest level since September 8 — compared to the previous close of 95.06. It gained 1.9 per cent in the week, marking its strongest weekly performance in seven months, after approaching a record low of 96.96 per dollar earlier this month. The drawdown in forex reserves comes amid attempts to shore up the rupee, which has faced sustained pressure from elevated crude oil prices, foreign portfolio investment outflows and demand for dollars.
