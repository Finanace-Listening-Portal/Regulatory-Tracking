Articles 2 min read Indian bonds surge after oil slide eases aggressive rate hike concerns On Tuesday, Indian government bonds rallied significantly, buoyed by falling oil prices that shifted market sentiment. The 6.94% 2036 bond's yield dipped to 7.2528%, a drop from 7.3169% the previous day. Analysts posit that lower oil prices could relieve inflationary pressure and bolster the rupee, alongside speculation of an upcoming RBI rate hike based on fresh inflation reports.
