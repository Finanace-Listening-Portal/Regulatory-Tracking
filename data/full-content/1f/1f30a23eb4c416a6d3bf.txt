Advt On asset quality terms, the bank witnessed improvement with gross non-performing assets (NPAs) declining to 3.11 per cent of gross advances at the end of the September quarter 2026 against 5.02 per cent a year ago.Net NPAs, or bad loans, moderated to 0.92 per cent from 1.3 per cent in the year-ago period.Provision coverage ratio including write-offs as of September 30, 2026, stood at 86.7 per cent.The capital adequacy ratio also rose to 18.32 per cent from 18.23 per cent at the end of September 2025.
