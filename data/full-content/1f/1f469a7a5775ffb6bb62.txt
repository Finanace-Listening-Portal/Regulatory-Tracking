Details of the changes it intends to make are yet to be disclosed, leaving investors awaiting greater clarity on the potential impact of the reforms on its business model.The proposed distribution reforms remain a key concern for the company despite a healthy quarterly performance, with net profit and EBITDA beating estimates. Management has indicated that it is working towards evolving the business while maintaining its customer proposition, but has not yet outlined the measures it intends to take, according to a report by Nomura. Advt PB Fintech reported a net profit of Rs 192 crore in the September quarter, 13% higher than the brokerage's estimate. EBITDA also exceeded estimates by 12%, while the EBITDA margin stood at 9.92%, against the expected 9%. The margin expanded 25 basis points year on year as expenses outside contribution declined by 140 basis points as a proportion of revenue. Contribution margins remained flat.The company's insurance platforms continued to report strong growth. Premiums across Policybazaar and other insurance platforms increased 38% year on year to Rs 9,385 crore, supported by 46% growth in core insurance premiums. Core insurance premiums without savings have grown faster than total core insurance premiums for seven consecutive quarters.The insurance platform's take rate remained unchanged sequentially at 18.5%. Management expects some moderation in protection insurance premiums, comprising term and health insurance, in the second half of FY27 because of a higher comparison base following the GST cut. However, it remains optimistic about the two-year compound annual growth trend.The businessPremium growth in the first half of FY27 stood at 31%, ahead of the brokerage's full-year growth estimate of 25% for the financial year.Paisabazaar's overall disbursals declined 45% year on year in the September quarter and 42% in the first half of FY27. However, core disbursals grew 22% and 27%, respectively, during the two periods. The company is recalibrating its strategy through PB Connect. Nomura said core disbursals should be tracked to assess the underlying trend during this transition. Advt The credit segment's take rate expanded 88 basis points year on year, although it moderated 30 basis points sequentially as core disbursals remained flat compared with the preceding quarter.Nomura retained its Neutral rating on PB Fintech and its target price of Rs 1,100, pending further clarity on the regulatory changes. It said the company's quarterly performance was healthy, but the potential changes to its distribution model remained the larger concern for investors.
