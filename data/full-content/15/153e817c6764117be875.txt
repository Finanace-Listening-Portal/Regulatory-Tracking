Focus on customer acquisition and data-driven strategies in India. Strengthening performance marketing for the fintech sector. WPP Media has partnered with global fintech company Branch International to drive performance marketing for its India business, with a focus on customer acquisition through digital marketing.The mandate will focus on using data, audience insights and campaign optimisation to reach relevant consumers and improve acquisition efficiency as Branch expands its business in India.Branch International provides mobile-first financial services and credit solutions across emerging markets. The partnership will draw on WPP Media’s experience in BFSI, app marketing and performance-led customer acquisition to develop digital strategies based on consumer behaviour and intent. Advt Sricheta Das, head of marketing, India, Branch International, said, “Building customer acquisition in digital financial services requires a clear understanding of consumer behaviour, intent and the points that influence consideration. Our focus is to create a more connected approach to reaching and engaging customers while continuing to improve efficiency as we scale. This partnership will help us strengthen that approach through focused digital marketing and performance-led execution.”Shekhar Banerjee, president, client solutions, WPP Media South Asia, said, “With Branch International, our ambition is to bring together the best of WPP’s capabilities across data, performance marketing, technology and AI, combined with our deep BFSI and app-marketing experience, to build a powerful growth engine for the brand. From identifying high-intent audiences to improving conversion journeys and making every media investment work harder, our focus is clear to be the most effective growth partner for Branch as they scale across India’s rapidly evolving digital financial ecosystem"The partnership adds to WPP Media’s focus on the BFSI and app performance marketing sectors, where it works with digital-first financial businesses on customer acquisition through data-led media, audience intelligence and performance solutions.
