Articles 3 min read IL&FS crisis was India's Lehman moment, swift action helped contain contagion: Uday Kotak Uday Kotak has described the 2018 IL&FS crisis as India's Lehman Brothers moment, warning that the collapse could have triggered widespread contagion across the financial sector. He credited swift government intervention and coordination with regulators and stakeholders for containing the crisis. IL&FS had 340 companies and defaulted on over Rs 91,000 crore despite holding a triple-A rating. And I got a call from the principal secretary to the Prime Minister, basically asking me to come back because what looked like at that stage was India's Lehman Brothers moment," he said.He said IL&FS, which was established in 1996, had 340 companies operating across different segments and was facing major defaults despite carrying a triple-A rating.Also Read: RBI cancels registrations of 49 NBFCs"It was truly an India's Lehman moment," Kotak said, recalling his role in working with regulators and various stakeholders, particularly the government, including the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, the Finance Ministry and the Prime Minister's Office, which was directly monitoring the situation.Kotak said the government and other stakeholders worked together to prevent the crisis from spreading across the financial system."And with all the support, a company which could have created as much impact and contagion... was fortunately, with significant partnership with the state and all the players, we were able to control that," he said. Advt The IL&FS crisis of 2018 was a major financial collapse in India triggered when Infrastructure Leasing & Financial Services (IL&FS), a massive infrastructure lender, defaulted on over Rs 91,000 crore in debt.The crisis happened because the company borrowed short-term money to fund slow-moving, long-term infrastructure projects like roads and power plants.To prevent a wider market crash, the Indian government stepped in, dismissed the company's board, and appointed a new leadership team to sell off assets and recover funds for lenders.Uday Kotak also highlighted the progress made in resolving cases under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), saying the recovery outcome in the IL&FS case could be significantly higher than the usual success ratio under the framework. Advt He attributed the outcome to swift action by the government, which helped prevent a financial crisis from escalating.Drawing a comparison with the global financial crisis, Kotak said the potential impact of the IL&FS collapse could have been significant, recalling how the failure of Lehman Brothers in 2008 affected the US and global economies.He also highlighted the importance of entrepreneurship, institutional reforms and coordination between the government, society and businesses in the process of nation-building.
