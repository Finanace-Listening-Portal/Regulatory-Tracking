} } Listen to This ArticleAfter spending more than three decades across ICICI group’s banking, securities, and insurance businesses, Anup Bagchi is preparing for a new chapter at HDFC Bank, where he will take over as managing director (MD) and chief executive officer (CEO) later this month. Bagchi’s departure from ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, which he has led since June 2023, marks the end of a 34-year association with the group. On Tuesday, he attended the insurer’s 2026-27 (FY27) second-quarter (July-September/Q2) earnings call to introduce his successor, Siddhartha Mishra, before moving on to his next assignment. Bagchi, who resigned as MD and CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance on October 1, said in his opening remarks that the board had appointed Mishra as MD and CEO for a five-year term beginning October 14, subject to the requisite approvals. Mishra took over as chief operating officer on October 6. “As my tenure as MD and CEO comes to an end (on Tuesday), I want to sincerely thank each one of you for the trust and support you have extended to me, not just at ICICI Bank, but also over more than three decades with ICICI group,” Bagchi said. He also wished Mishra success, expressing confidence that his successor would lead the company to continued growth. Bagchi has headed ICICI Prudential Life Insurance since June 2023. He succeeded N S Kannan, another ICICI group veteran who led the insurer from June 2018 to June 2023. Bagchi will now move to the corner office at HDFC Bank. Although he officially takes over from the current MD and CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan on October 27, he has been an additional director on the bank’s board since October 2. Just as Bagchi introduced Mishra on the insurer’s earnings call, Jagdishan is also expected to introduce Bagchi to the investor community during HDFC Bank’s Q2FY27 earnings call on Saturday. An alumnus of the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore and the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, Bagchi has more than three decades of experience across banking, capital markets, wealth management, and insurance. He has held several senior leadership positions, including MD and CEO of ICICI Securities and executive director of ICICI Bank. He will be the first outsider to occupy the corner office at HDFC Bank. Bagchi served as executive director of ICICI Bank from 2017 to 2023, overseeing retail, business and rural banking before moving to wholesale banking. During his tenure, the bank’s retail mortgage portfolio crossed ~2 trillion, making it the first private-sector lender in India to achieve the milestone. He was earlier MD and CEO of ICICI Securities and served as chairman of ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company until May 2023. He was also a director of ICICI Pension Fund Management and ICICI Home Finance. Bagchi’s appointment as MD and CEO of HDFC Bank has brought clarity to the lender’s leadership succession. The focus will now shift to execution, with deposit mobilisation, loan growth, and margin recovery emerging as pritority areas, according to analysts. Brokerages have said the appointment provides an opportunity for a strategic reset.
