} } Listen to This ArticleMicrofinance lenders expect disbursement momentum to sustain in the second half of FY27, supported by seasonal demand and improving activity in markets affected by weather-related disruptions earlier in the year, they said in their post-earnings analyst calls. While lenders reported healthy business growth in the first half, they expect the traditionally stronger second half to support further expansion, even as they maintain tighter underwriting and closer monitoring of local risks. CreditAccess Grameen’s assets under management (AUM) grew 19.5 per cent year-on-year (Y-o-Y) to ₹30,946 crore in Q2FY27, while disbursements rose 19.4 per cent to ₹6,355 crore. However, its group loan portfolio declined 2 per cent from the start of the financial year to ₹23,746 crore, partly due to write-offs and customer graduation. “With write-offs normalising and the seasonally stronger second half ahead and increase in customer acquisition during H2, we expect the Group Loan book growth to return to guided levels,” said Ganesh Narayanan, managing director and chief executive officer, CreditAccess Grameen. The company retained its FY27 AUM growth guidance of 20-25 per cent. Satin Creditcare reported consolidated disbursements of ₹3,697 crore in Q2, up 41 per cent Y-o-Y, while AUM rose 31 per cent to ₹16,589 crore.
