Articles 3 min read Bajaj Finance shares jump 3% after UBS upgrades to Neutral, hikes target price while Jefferies screams Buy Bajaj Finance shares jumped 3% on Wednesday following bullish brokerage calls. Global brokerages Jefferies and UBS issued positive outlooks, with Jefferies maintaining a Buy rating with a target of Rs 1,280, while UBS upgraded the stock to Neutral with a target price of Rs 1,100. The stock is currently leading gains on benchmark indices Sensex and Nifty, which are trading with minor gains. Bajaj Finserv, meanwhile, is also up over 2%.Jefferies on Bajaj Finance share priceJefferies hosted Bajaj Finance MD Rajeev Jain and other executives for a roadshow, following which it reaffirmed its Buy call and said the stock remains among its top picks. The international brokerage has fixed a target price of Rs 1,280 apiece for the shares of the company, implying around 27% upside potential from the previous closing price of Rs 1,009 apiece. Advt The international brokerage noted that Bajaj Finance management is confident about short-term and long-term growth and profitability. In the short term, strong demand, asset quality, and efficiencies will make up for slightly lower net interest margins (NIM), and management will use tailwinds to build resilience, it added.Over the next 10 years, Bajaj Finance management plans to grow AUM at a 23% CAGR and profits faster, Jefferies said, adding that AI gets high attention from management and the board. It noted that succession will be an internal selection, with the announcement likely in January or February of 2028.UBS on Bajaj Finance share priceUBS upgraded its rating for shares of Bajaj Finance to Neutral from Sell, and increased its target price to Rs 1,100 apiece from Rs 910 apiece. The latest target price implies more than 9% upside potential from the stock's previous closing price.We believe India is entering a strong unsecured credit growth cycle, led by personal loans. We think this is being supported by healthy asset quality across banks and NBFCs, flat unsecured household leverage over the past three years, abundant system liquidity and a more risk-on stance from lenders, UBS said in its latest note. Advt The international brokerage believes Bajaj Finance has cleared its asset quality issues across unsecured products, while an increased provision coverage ratio acts as a cushion against macro headwinds. This could provide a cyclical push toward higher-yielding loan growth in the near term, driving cyclical earnings acceleration. We think the company's EPS downgrades have largely passed and foresee strong EPS growth of 30%+ in FY27, although it may slow to the high teens in FY28. Bajaj Finance is trading at nearly 23x one-year-forward PE, capturing most of its upside potential and leaving us at Neutral, it added.
