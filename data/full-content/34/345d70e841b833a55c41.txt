Advt "We neither cannot avoid Russian oil nor at the same time afford the pressure of US Tariffs," he said, highlighting, with the probability of Banks' strike from 28th to 30th September followed by 2nd October bank holiday, there would be only one working day in the coming week, i.e., 1st October.As per Dey, RBI's intervention in the currency market would continue to avoid any undue speculative volatility. "There is a high chance of Rupee correcting back towards 95.20/30 levels shortly," he noted.Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, noted, "The rupee has found some relief as crude prices have eased, trading below the Rs 95.80 level against the dollar in early trade."The currency nevertheless remains sensitive to oil prices, global yields and foreign portfolio flows, as per Ponmudi.At the same time, USD/INR is testing the 95.70-95.80 support zone, trading marginally lower on the day by 0.12%."Immediate resistance is placed at 96.00-96.10, with a sustained break above this zone opening the way toward the next resistance at 96.40-96.50. On the downside, a decisive break below 95.70 could extend the pair's weakness toward the next support at 95.40-95.50," Ponmudi noted.As per Ponmudi, the RSI (Relative Strength Index) stands at 61, remaining elevated but easing slightly from recent highs, indicating that bullish momentum is moderating. Advt Also, with crude oil prices easing on signs of a recovery in Saudi exports, one of the key pressure points on the rupee has softened somewhat today."From the rupee's perspective, the near-term bias remains cautious above 95.70, while a sustained move above 96.00 would signal renewed weakness in the currency. A break below 95.50, meanwhile, could provide further relief to the rupee," he noted. (ANI) ANI Published On Sep 21, 2026 at 11:56 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
