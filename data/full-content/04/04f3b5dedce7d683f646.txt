Addressing the Global FinTech Fest 2026, Malhotra said the central bank will continue to support responsible innovation while ensuring that trust, safety and consumer welfare remain at the core of the financial system. It has taken banking from the branch to the hand of every citizen,” Malhotra said. Advt He said the impact of India’s digital financial infrastructure is now visible across geographies and demographics, with shopkeepers in small towns, farmers in remote villages and young professionals in metro cities increasingly using common digital rails to transact, save and borrow.UPI, Aadhaar-enabled payment systems and the Jan Dhan ecosystem have turned financial inclusion from a policy aspiration into an everyday reality for hundreds of millions of Indians, he said.A key impact of fintech, Malhotra said, has been in credit delivery to micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs), where innovations such as cash-flow based lending, Account Aggregators and the Unified Lending Interface are helping address India’s long-standing credit gap.Fintechs are enabling formal and collateral-light credit for millions of small businesses that remained “invisible” to traditional underwriting models for decades, he said.“This is credit reaching the last mile of entrepreneurs, powering jobs and livelihoods across the country,” Malhotra added.The RBI Governor also underlined that the central bank sees fintech as a partner in building India’s future financial system, while making it clear that innovation must be accompanied by safeguards around trust and safety.Malhotra pointed to the RBI’s regulatory sandbox, the Reserve Bank Innovation Hub and its continued engagement with startups and fintech companies as examples of the regulator’s approach towards encouraging innovation through continuous dialogue with the ecosystem. Advt The central bank has encouraged self-regulation within the sector, supported the development of digital public infrastructure and remained open to new ideas and players, he said.“We have promoted responsive, responsible innovation while safeguarding trust that is the very foundation of finance,” Malhotra said.Going ahead, the RBI will continue to follow this approach with the objective of building a financial system that is safer, more trustworthy, inclusive, fair and efficient, while supporting economic growth and public welfare, he added.
