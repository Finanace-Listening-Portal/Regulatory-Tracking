Net interest income (NII), or the difference between interest earned and interest expended, rose 26.4 per cent Y-o-Y to Rs 3,153.4 crore. The bank's other income rose 37.7 per cent to Rs 1,490.4 crore, with fee income increasing 31.9 per cent to Rs 1,167.7 crore. Its net interest margin (NIM) expanded by 26 basis points (bps) to 3.32 per cent during the quarter. “Last two quarters we have given this guidance that we expect five to six basis points improvement in NIM. While it will not be linear, we had guided and we continue to stick to our guidance on that,” said K V S Manian, MD and CEO, Federal Bank. Asset quality improved further, with the gross non-performing asset (GNPA) ratio declining to 1.32 per cent as of September 30, 2026, compared with 1.52 per cent as of June 30, 2026. The net NPA ratio fell to 0.17 per cent from 0.18 per cent. Both ratios were at their lowest levels in a decade, the bank said. Fresh slippages declined 27.5 per cent Y-o-Y to Rs 420 crore, while the slippage ratio fell to 0.59 per cent from 0.94 per cent a year ago.
