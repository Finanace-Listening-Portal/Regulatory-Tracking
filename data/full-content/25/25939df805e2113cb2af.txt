(File Photo)follow usfollow usGlobal crude oil prices could undergo a major correction by 2027, with Brent crude potentially falling to around $70 per barrel as geopolitical tensions ease and Middle East supply increases. According to a State Bank of India (SBI) report, reduced tensions between Washington and Tehran, stronger crude exports, and more favourable global economic conditions could help bring down international oil prices.The bank's projections suggest that the anticipated improvement in the geopolitical and supply environment may provide relief to oil markets, which have faced considerable uncertainty in recent months.In its latest assessment, SBI expects Brent crude prices to soften over the coming 10 days, with the average price likely to hover around $87 per barrel, based on its LTSM forecast.Looking further ahead, the report points to the possibility of prices returning to levels last witnessed in February 2026, potentially reaching approximately $70 per barrel during 2027.Explaining the factors behind its outlook, the report said, “Possibility of peace deal, more predictable US after mid-term elections, increasing crude exports from Middle East despite shortage in diesel will help prices stay on the lower side and could touch February 2026 levels of around USD 70 in 2027”.SBI Expects RBI To Opt For A Smaller Rate HikeThe changing global economic environment could also influence the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) forthcoming monetary policy decision, according to SBI.India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation climbed to 6.09 per cent in October 2026, crossing the 6 per cent threshold for the first time under the new series. The latest reading also pushed inflation beyond the RBI's upper tolerance limit.SBI anticipates that inflation will remain above 6 per cent in the following month before beginning to ease. Nevertheless, the bank believes that improved external conditions, a relatively stable rupee and stronger foreign investment inflows have reduced the likelihood of an aggressive monetary policy response.Assessing the possible rate decision, SBI stated, "We believe that the balance of probabilities has decisively shifted towards a 25-bps rate hike in December, rather than a larger 50-bps adjustment," the report stated.Four Key Developments Behind SBI's Policy ForecastThe bank's expectation of a relatively moderate interest rate increase is based on four major developments:Greater stability in the rupee against the US dollar.An improving international economic and financial environment.The possibility of reduced tensions between the US and Iran.Expectations of softer yields on US Treasury securities.SBI also observed that the RBI's measures to contain fluctuations in the domestic currency appear to have delivered positive results. The rupee briefly moved below the Rs 94-per-dollar level before subsequently strengthening further.Meanwhile, foreign investor inflows have gathered momentum, reaching approximately $7.65 billion since November 6, according to the report. This improvement in capital movement could provide additional support to India's external financial position.Terminal Repo Rate May Settle Around 6%Looking beyond the December policy announcement, SBI expects the terminal repo rate to stabilise near 6 per cent, although it sees room for a lower outcome of 5.75 per cent.The bank does not consider another rate increase in February advisable, particularly because the monetary policy decision would come close to the presentation of the Union Budget.SBI has also offered a relatively measured assessment of concerns surrounding the potential effects of El Niño on food prices.While weather-related disruptions could affect agricultural production and contribute to inflationary pressures, the bank believes the overall impact may be less severe than anticipated.Get Latest News live on Times Now along with Breaking News and Top Headlines from Business, Companies and around the world.Latest News worldSingapore Accuses Trump Administration Of Providing 'Little' Help In Probe Into Alice Huang, AI Chip DealseducationAfter 55 Years, Telangana to Replace Separate Intermediate System; Classes 11, 12 to Join SchoolsviralThree Alleged Affairs, One Resignation: Internet Goes Brutal With Jensen Huang MemesindiaModi Won't Remain PM After Debate With Me: Rahul Gandhi's Fresh DaredelhiMicrosoft Outage Hits IndiGo, Akasa Air Email Services, Airlines Issue Passenger AdvisorylifestyleRadhika Merchant's Manish Malhotra Lehenga Marries Bandhani and Brocade in a Blush Blue Dreamentertainment newsBandit Queen Sequel In Works After Three Decades; Satluj's Honey Trehan To Direct - Deets InsidePriya Raghuvanshi authorShe is working as a Chief Copy Editor at Times Now’s Business Desk, where she covers key developments in the stock market, Indian corporates across se...
