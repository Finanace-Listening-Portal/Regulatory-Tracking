} } Listen to This ArticleAditya Birla Capital on Friday posted a 38 per cent year-on-year (YoY) rise in consolidated net profit to ₹1,288 crore in the July-September quarter of the financial year 2026-27 (Q2FY27), driven by strong growth in its lending businesses, with both its non-banking financial company (NBFC) and housing finance arms reporting higher profits. The financial services company’s consolidated segment revenue rose 31 per cent YoY to ₹16,545 crore during the quarter. The company’s lending business, excluding housing finance, reported a 41 per cent rise in profit to ₹1,008 crore, while Aditya Birla Housing Finance reported a 73 per cent increase to ₹335 crore. The NBFC’s assets under management (AUM) grew 26.5 per cent YoY to ₹1.77 trillion at the end of September. Its net interest income, including fee income, increased 24.7 per cent to ₹2,488 crore. Growth was broad-based across the lending portfolio. Business loans increased 30 per cent YoY to ₹1.01 trillion, while personal and consumer loans rose 37 per cent to ₹24,955 crore. Corporate and mid-market loans grew 15 per cent to ₹50,483 crore. Total disbursements by the NBFC rose 12 per cent YoY to ₹24,708 crore in Q2FY27.
