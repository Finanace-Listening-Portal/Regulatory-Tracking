National Stock Exchange of India Circular Department: Download Ref No: NSE/CML/2022/45 Date: September 22, 2022 To All Social Enterprises All Listed Companies Subject: Framework on Social Stock Exchange SEBI vide notification dated July 25, 2022 amended Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 (“ICDR Regulations”), Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (“LODR Regulations”) and Securities and Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012 (“AIF Regulations”) to provide a broad framework for Social Stock Exchange. In terms of the amendment, SEBI has now issued a detailed framework on Social Stock Exchange (“SSE”) vide Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-1/P/CIR/2022/120 dated September 19, 2022. A copy of aforesaid SEBI circular is enclosed. This is for your information please. Yours faithfully, For National Stock Exchange of India Limited Priya Iyer Senior Manager Encl: SEBI Circular
