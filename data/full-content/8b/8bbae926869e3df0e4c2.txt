Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Goa, November 27, 2026: Eicher Motors Limited has announced the launch of the Royal Enfield Himalayan 750, a new addition to its adventure motorcycle lineup. The Himalayan 750, unveiled at Motoverse 2026 in Goa, is designed to build on the legacy of its predecessors by offering a powerful yet uncomplicated riding experience. This model features a 743cc air/oil-cooled parallel-twin engine that delivers 63 Nm of torque at 5,000 RPM, ensuring robust performance across diverse terrains. The Himalayan 750 is engineered to cater to a wide range of riding styles, equipped with five curated ride modes and advanced features such as a six-axis Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), cornering ABS, and lean-sensitive traction control. These features, combined with a 19-inch front and 17-inch rear tubeless tyre setup, aim to provide riders with enhanced control and stability on and off the road. The motorcycle also boasts a 195kg payload capacity, making it suitable for long-distance touring. Bookings for the Himalayan 750 will commence on December 1, 2026, across India via the Royal Enfield website and authorised dealerships. Attendees at the Motoverse 2026 event in Goa will have exclusive early access to book the motorcycle from November 28-29, 2026. The Himalayan 750 is available in multiple variants and colour options, with prices starting at ₹4,89,490 (ex-showroom Chennai) for the cast wheel option in Aztec Green and Tectonic Black. The flagship variant, featuring bespoke luggage in collaboration with GIVI, is priced at ₹6,29,000 (ex-showroom Chennai). B. Govindarajan, Managing Director of Eicher Motors Ltd. and CEO of Royal Enfield, highlighted the brand’s commitment to adventure touring, stating, “The Himalayan 750 is a powerful new expression of the Himalayan spirit. We are taking the Himalayan legacy even further by bringing together a decade of learning. Our first 750cc motorcycle is built for riders looking to cross borders, take on diverse terrains, and experience the freedom of going anywhere.” The introduction of the Himalayan 750 comes at a time when the demand for adventure motorcycles continues to grow globally. Analysts note that Royal Enfield’s focus on combining classic design with modern technology positions the brand well within the competitive middle-weight adventure touring segment. As the motorcycle market evolves, the Himalayan 750 is expected to attract a diverse range of riders seeking both performance and the spirit of exploration.
