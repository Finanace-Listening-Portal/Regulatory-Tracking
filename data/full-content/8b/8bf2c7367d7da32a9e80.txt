Articles 1 min read BlackRock raises its stake in Axis Bank to 5% BlackRock has successfully increased its stake in Axis Bank to 5 per cent by acquiring more shares from the open market. Specifically, the investment giant procured 18,560 crore additional shares, nudging its ownership from 4.99 per cent. This elevates BlackRock's total holding to 155,726,461 shares. Notably, during the second quarter ending September 2026, Axis Bank showcased impressive growth, with a substantial surge in both net and operating profits.
