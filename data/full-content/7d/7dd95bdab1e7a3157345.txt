The key moves include the appointment of former Financial Services Secretary Nagaraju Maddirala as IL&FS Chairman and Managing Director, Federal Bank’s elevation of Virat Sunil Diwanji as Executive Director, and Barclays India’s appointment of Sonali Sharma as Managing Director and Head of Legal.Nagaraju Maddirala appointed IL&FS Chairman and Managing DirectorThe Ministry of Corporate Affairs designated retired IAS officer Nagaraju Maddirala as Chairman and Managing Director of Infrastructure Leasing & Financial Services (IL&FS) for two years from the date of joining. His appointment follows the end of Nand Kishore’s tenure on September 30, 2026. Maddirala was appointed to the IL&FS board by the Mumbai bench of the National Company Law Tribunal on October 1, 2026. Advt Federal Bank elevates Virat Sunil Diwanji as Executive DirectorFederal Bank has elevated Virat Sunil Diwanji, its former National Head of Consumer Banking, as Executive Director for a three-year term from the date he assumes charge. He took charge as Additional Executive Director on October 12, 2026, following approval from the Reserve Bank of India. Diwanji joined the bank on April 10, 2025, after spending more than 30 years with the Kotak Mahindra Group, where he served as Group President and Head of Consumer Bank.Barclays India appoints Sonali Sharma as Managing Director and Head of LegalBarclays India has appointed Sonali Sharma as Managing Director and Head of Legal, including oversight of its Global Capabilities Centre. She will also lead regulatory and government relations for the bank in India. Sharma previously served as Head of Legal and General Counsel at Bank of America. Her professional experience includes work in capital markets and risk management.Ageas Federal Life Insurance appoints Puneet Suri as National Head and CAOAgeas Federal Life Insurance has appointed Puneet Suri as National Head and Chief Administrative Officer. Suri most recently headed the insurer’s agency channel and brings more than 25 years of experience across life insurance and consumer goods. In his new role, he will work on scaling distribution, aligning administrative operations and driving profitability frameworks across the company’s pan-India operations.Equirus Capital appoints Ashish Rao as Chief Risk OfficerFinancial services firm Equirus Capital has appointed Ashish Rao as Chief Risk Officer. Rao brings more than three decades of global experience across risk management, governance, financing, wealth management and strategic leadership in financial services. Based in Mumbai, he has worked with senior leadership teams on risk and governance frameworks and regulatory requirements. His appointment is aimed at strengthening the firm’s enterprise risk management and governance functions. Advt Nexedge Capital appoints Apurva Sahijwani as co-founderNexedge Capital has appointed Apurva Sahijwani, former Managing Director and CEO of Avendus Wealth Management, as co-founder. Sahijwani brings more than 28 years of experience in private banking and wealth management. He led Avendus Wealth Management from May 2024 to September 2026 and previously served as Head of Private Banking at Axis Bank, where he was involved in launching and scaling Burgundy Private.The appointments reflect continued leadership movement across the banking and financial services sector, spanning senior management, governance, legal, risk and distribution functions. From board-level changes to key executive appointments, the week's developments cover a broad cross-section of the BFSI industry.
