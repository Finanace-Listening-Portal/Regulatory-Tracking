The memorandum modifies the earlier Office Memorandum F.No. GoM-RealEstateSector/GSTC/2021 dated 24 June 2024 and specifies a seven-member GoM headed by Dr. Pramod Sawant, Chief Minister of Goa, as Convenor. Shri Devendra Gangadharrao Fadnavis, Chief Minister and Finance Minister of Maharashtra, has been nominated as a Member. The other members are Shri Bijendra Prasad Yadav, Deputy Chief Minister of Bihar; Shri Suresh Kumar Khanna, Minister for Finance and Parliamentary Affairs, Uttar Pradesh; Shri Harpal Singh Cheema, Minister for Finance, Planning, Programme Implementation, Excise and Taxation, Punjab; Shri Kanubhai Desai, Minister for Finance, Energy and Petrochemicals, Gujarat; and Shri V.D. Satheesan, whose designation is recorded in the memorandum as Chief Minister and Minister in charge of Finance, Keralam. GOODS AND SERVICES TAX COUNCIL F.No. 09/GoMonRealEstateSector/GSTC/2018 | Dated: 13-11-2026 OFFICE MEMORANDUM Subject: Partial Modification to the Group of Ministers (GoM) for boosting Real Estate Sector under GST regime -reg. In partial modification to the Office Memorandum No. F.No.GoM-RealEstateSector/GSTC/2021 dated 24.06.2024 with respect to the Group of Ministers for boosting Real Estate Sector under GST regime, the composition of GoM would be as follows: S.
