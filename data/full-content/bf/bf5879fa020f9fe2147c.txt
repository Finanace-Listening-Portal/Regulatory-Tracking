RBI/2026-27/310 dated 27 November 2026, reduced the minimum daily cash reserve ratio (CRR) maintenance requirement for all scheduled banks under Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. The daily minimum falls from 99% to 90% of the required CRR from the reporting fortnight beginning 1 December 2026. The RBI based its decision on an assessment of current and evolving macroeconomic conditions and liquidity. The circular refers to its earlier direction dated 9 October 2026, which had raised the daily minimum from 90% to 99% from the fortnight beginning 16 October 2026. The October circular also states that the average CRR maintained over the reporting fortnight must meet the RBI’s prescribed CRR. The November circular changes the daily minimum as a percentage of that prescribed requirement. The October circular had explained that the daily maintenance requirement applies on every day of the reporting fortnight. Under the updated requirement, each scheduled bank will apply the 90% floor to its required CRR on those days from the effective fortnight. The circular bears reference DoR.RET.REC.262/12.01.001/2026-27 and was signed by J P Sharma, Chief General Manager-in-Charge. It was addressed to all scheduled banks and issued under the same statutory provision as the October circular. Reserve Bank of India RBI/2026-27/310 DoR.RET.REC.262/12.01.001/2026-27 | Dated: November 27, 2026 All Scheduled Banks, Madam / Sir, Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – Change in Daily Minimum Cash Reserve Maintenance Requirement Please refer to our circular DoR.RET.REC.247/12.01.001/2026-27 dated October 09, 2026 on the captioned subject. 2. On the basis of an assessment of the current and evolving macroeconomic situation and liquidity conditions, it has been decided to reduce the minimum daily maintenance of the cash reserve ratio (CRR) from 99 per cent of the requirement to 90 per cent effective from the fortnight beginning December 1, 2026.
