Gross direct tax collections rose by 14.02%, boosting receipts to Rs 17.50 lakh crore. Corporate tax collections grew by about 17.84%, amounting to Rs 6.32 lakh crore. Non-corporate tax collections saw a rise of 5.30%, increasing to Rs 7.56 lakh crore. Direct tax refunds increased significantly, reaching Rs 3.
