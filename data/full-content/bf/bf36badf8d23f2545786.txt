However, the country’s overall BoP position improved significantly, swinging to a surplus of $66.8 billion in H1FY27 from a deficit of $6.4 billion a year earlier, supported by a sharp increase in capital inflows, particularly through banking capital. The merchandise trade deficit widened to $176.1 billion in H1FY27 from $158.1 billion a year ago. Merchandise exports increased to $265.3 billion from $221.5 billion, but imports rose at a faster pace to $441.4 billion from $379.6 billion. The net income deficit also widened to $32.4 billion from $25.8 billion during the period. This was partly offset by an increase in net services exports to $105 billion from $98.8 billion, and net transfers, which include remittances, to $77.7 billion from $67.2 billion. Meanwhile, the capital account surplus surged to $92.6 billion in H1FY27 from $11.6 billion a year ago. Banking capital inflows stood at $125.2 billion, compared with just $0.3 billion in the year-ago period.
