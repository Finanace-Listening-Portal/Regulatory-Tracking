The stock price of the private sector lender quoted close to its 52-week high of ₹89.58 touched on September 23, 2026. In comparison, the BSE Sensex was down 0.2 per cent at 84,158.93 at 09:40 AM. A combined 7.3 million shares changed hands on the NSE and BSE. Jefferies maintains ‘Buy’ rating on IDFC First Bank with TP ₹100 Jefferies maintains a ‘Buy’ rating on IDFC First Bank with a target price ₹100 as the brokerage firm believes that with Common Equity Tier 1 (CET-1) at 13 per cent & higher growth, the bank may consider raising capital. Expansion in return on asset (ROA) is key to rerating, the brokerage firm said, and sees 20 per cent compound annual growth rate (CAGR) in loans over FY27-29 and ROA expanding to 1.2 per cent by FY29. Growth in deposits should be slightly higher, helping lower loan-to-deposit ratio (LDR). The bank is investing in the current account franchise through lending relationships, tech platforms & services. This can help bring down funding costs and improve corporate lending ROA, analysts at Jefferies said in a report. The Bank’s net interest margins (NIMs) may be stable in near-term and can expand from Q4 aided by rate hikes. Over the medium term, NIMs may moderate as the share of lower-risk loans rises, but a positive jaws effect should open, with operating leverage aiding ROA expansion, the brokerage firm said. IDFC First Bank’s robust deposit growth is expected to sustain, resulting in a higher share of lower-cost deposits in overall funding. Stronger growth in rural, retail & small and medium enterprises (SME) segments will support yields & drive margin expansion. Scale benefits will accrue over the next few years & drive a lower cost-to-income ratio as past investments start to bear fruit, e.g., credit cards. Bank has successful track record of managing retail asset quality across cycles to control credit costs, Jefferies said.
