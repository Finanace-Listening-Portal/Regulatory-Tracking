The performance points to strong investor enthusiasm for smaller companies in the primary market over the past six months, even as the underlying pattern of fundraising has remained largely unchanged.Primary market activity has remained robust in 2026, although total issuance, the composition of offerings and the objectives of fundraising have remained broadly similar to 2024 and 2025, according to Kotak Institutional Equities. Advt Offers for sale accounted for 60 per cent of IPO issuances in 2026. Investment in new plant and machinery accounted for less than 25 per cent of IPO proceeds, while balance sheet enhancement accounted for almost half of the funds raised. These patterns indicate that the nature of IPO fundraising has not changed materially despite the strong investor appetite for smaller issues.The enthusiasm for smaller IPOs has coincided with the outperformance of small and mid cap stocks in the secondary market over the past six months. However, this outperformance has been concentrated in select themes, including capital goods, contract development and manufacturing organisations, defence and electronics manufacturing services. The government's push for domestic manufacturing should support revenue growth in these sectors over the next few years.Retail flowsRetail investor flows have increasingly favoured small and mid cap funds amid their recent outperformance. Dedicated small and mid cap funds accounted for 32 per cent of overall active equity fund flows in the first eight months of 2026, compared with 15 per cent in 2024 and 25 per cent in 2025.However, strong inflows have not consistently translated into sustained performance when investment narratives have not been backed by execution. An analysis of the top-performing 10 per cent of small and mid cap stocks in the Nifty 500 index found that their outperformance was driven primarily by earnings upgrades, coupled with meaningful valuation rerating. Continued outperformance of this group will depend on companies delivering earnings in line with sharply higher expectations. Advt The report also noted that investors remain sceptical about the earnings prospects of larger companies amid uncertainty over macroeconomic conditions, policy and regulatory risks. It cautioned that the narratives supporting enthusiasm for smaller companies must translate into business performance for the gains to be sustained.
