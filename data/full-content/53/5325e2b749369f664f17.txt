Investors across the world, including in India, are increasingly looking to diversify their investment instruments and assets," he told PTI.The guarantees provided by the International Finance Corporation (IFC) and the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA) are well received by the investor community, he added."Institutional investors value these guarantees because they are familiar with the World Bank Group's underwriting standards and governance practices. As a result, these assets are increasingly sought after by institutional investors," he said.The World Bank Group acts as a bridge between government development priorities and private investment by strengthening markets, structuring viable projects and reducing risks through financing, guarantees and partnerships, he said.Under the USD 436-million Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) programme, the World Bank supported reforms related to the Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS), a digital platform that enables businesses to convert unpaid invoices into cash.The initiative has provided more than 2.5 lakh MSMEs with faster access to capital, while 30 non-banking financial companies have joined the platform, expanding the pool of lenders.
