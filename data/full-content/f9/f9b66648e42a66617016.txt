This approval marks the introduction of the first generic equivalent of Flovent® HFA in 110 mcg and 220 mcg strengths under an Abbreviated New Drug Application (ANDA). The product is indicated for the long-term maintenance and prophylactic treatment of asthma in patients aged four years and older. In this strategic partnership, Cipla is tasked with the manufacturing and supply of the product, while Teva holds the ANDA and will be responsible for its commercialisation in the United States. The approval of Fluticasone HFA, an inhaled corticosteroid, represents a significant milestone in expanding access to affordable asthma treatments in the U.S. market. Achin Gupta, Managing Director & Global Chief Executive Officer of Cipla, emphasised the company’s longstanding commitment to respiratory care, stating, “This product reinforces our commitment to expanding access to trusted treatment options for patients living with asthma.” Marc Falkin, Chief Executive Officer of Cipla North America, highlighted the importance of this development in supporting Cipla’s growth trajectory in the U.S., noting the company’s dedication to ensuring a consistent supply to meet market demands. The U.S. market for fluticasone HFA 110 and 220 mcg is valued at approximately $350 million, according to IQVIA. This approval positions Cipla and Teva to capture a significant share of this market, further strengthening their presence in the respiratory segment. Cipla, established in 1935, is a global pharmaceutical leader known for its innovation in complex generics and a robust portfolio across multiple therapeutic segments. With 48 manufacturing sites worldwide, Cipla’s commitment to accessibility and affordability continues to drive its global impact. Analysts view this FDA approval as a positive step for Cipla, reinforcing its market position and potential for growth in the competitive U.S. pharmaceutical landscape.
