Articles 1 min read Will IDBI Bank public shareholders get any benefit from Fairfax stake sale? The government and LIC will together sell a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank for over Rs 52,878 crore at Rs 81 per share. LIC will divest 30.24% and the government 30.48%. The transaction will generate about Rs 26,337 crore for LIC, while the government will receive proceeds from its own stake sale. The deal will monetise the holdings of the Union government and Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), but will not directly bring sale proceeds to other shareholders.The government and LIC will together sell a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank for over Rs 52,878 crore at Rs 81 per share. LIC will divest 30.24% and the government 30.48%. The transaction will generate about Rs 26,337 crore for LIC, while the government will receive proceeds from its own stake sale. Advt Valuation is keyFor shareholders who continue to hold IDBI Bank shares, the potential benefit will depend on how the transaction affects the bank’s market valuation and future business performance, rather than on receiving a share of the sale proceeds. The agreed price of Rs 81 per share provides a transaction reference point for valuing the lender, but does not guarantee that the market price will move to that level or remain there.LIC will retain around 19% of IDBI Bank after the sale, equivalent to roughly 204.3 crore shares valued at nearly Rs 18,566 crore at the transaction price. The change in ownership could alter the bank’s strategic direction under Fairfax, but the sale itself does not create an automatic payout for minority shareholders.The transaction is therefore primarily a change in ownership, with the government and LIC realising value from their existing investments. Any gains for other IDBI Bank shareholders would depend on the market’s assessment of the bank’s future prospects and the price at which they hold or sell their shares.
