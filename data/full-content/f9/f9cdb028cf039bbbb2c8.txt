Articles 5 min read UPI MDR could add up to Rs 2,000 crore revenue for PhonePe, Rs 550 crore for Razorpay: Analysts PhonePe could add ₹1,600-2,000 crore and Razorpay ₹300-550 crore in annual revenue by FY28, as the new 0.4% MDR creates a potential ₹18,000-22,000 crore eligible UPI fee pool, analysts estimate. Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Sep 24, 2026 at 08:27 AM IST var news_msid = 134449949; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook PhonePe could add as much as ₹2,000 crore in annual revenue from the new UPI Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) framework by FY28, while Razorpay could gain up to ₹550 crore, according to estimates by analysts based on their respective exposure to eligible UPI transactions.The two fintechs, both preparing for potential public listings, stand to benefit differently: PhonePe's upside comes primarily from its dominant UPI app-provider position, while Razorpay's gain depends on its eligible merchant TPV and the share of acquiring economics it retains.“The overall UPI MDR pool could be in the ₹20,000-25,000 crore range annually at scale. PhonePe is clearly the biggest beneficiary given its ~48% share of UPI value, but it will only capture a portion of the overall pool. For PhonePe, I would see several hundred crore of incremental annual revenue as a reasonable medium-term opportunity, with the potential to move towards ₹1,000 crore or more as the eligible pool scales,” said Aakash Agrawal, Associate Director – Digital and New Age Business, Anand Rathi Investment Banking. Advt PhonePe: ₹1,600-2,000 crore revenue opportunityThe estimates become significantly larger when the new MDR is applied to PhonePe's eligible transaction base rather than its overall UPI market share.Naresh Biyani, Founder and CEO of Capwise Financial Services, estimates the eligible UPI value for PhonePe at around ₹20 lakh crore in FY27, rising to ₹25 lakh crore in FY28. On this base, every 1 basis point of effective monetisation would generate approximately ₹200 crore and ₹250 crore, respectively.At an effective take-rate of 5 bps, PhonePe could generate around ₹1,000-1,250 crore annually; at 8 bps, the figure rises to ₹1,600-2,000 crore; and at 10 bps, including some acquiring-side economics, it could reach ₹2,000-2,500 crore.“PhonePe's eligible value is about ₹20 lakh crore in FY27, rising to about ₹25 lakh crore in FY28. Each basis point is therefore worth about ₹200 crore in FY27 and ₹250 crore in FY28. At 5 bps that is ₹1,000-1,250 crore; at 8 bps ₹1,600-2,000 crore; at 10 bps, including some acquiring, ₹2,000-2,500 crore,” Biyani said.The distinction is important because the headline MDR is 40 bps, but PhonePe does not retain the entire amount. Under the framework used by Biyani, the app-provider slice is around 8 bps, with 12 bps going to the acquiring bank, 16 bps to the issuing bank and 4 bps to the payer-side PSP. Advt PhonePe's roughly 48% UPI value share therefore cannot simply be applied to the entire industry MDR pool.Biyani estimates that eligible P2M transactions could generate a gross annual MDR pool of around ₹18,000-22,000 crore by FY27-28, based on eligible value of ₹45 lakh crore in FY27 and ₹55 lakh crore in FY28.MDR could add 15-18% to PhonePe's FY28 revenueThe incremental revenue could become particularly significant against PhonePe's existing revenue base.PhonePe reported consolidated revenue of around ₹7,920 crore in FY26. Biyani estimates its revenue could reach around ₹11,000 crore by FY28 before the MDR impact. On that base, an additional ₹1,600-2,000 crore would represent roughly 15-18% incremental revenue. Advt The operating leverage could be even higher because the payment infrastructure required to process the transactions is already largely in place.Biyani estimates 65-75% of the incremental MDR revenue could flow through to EBITDA, translating into roughly ₹1,100-1,500 crore of incremental EBITDA by FY28.“The more important benefit is operating leverage. A large portion of incremental MDR revenue should have strong contribution margins because the underlying payment infrastructure is already in place. I would therefore expect a substantial portion to flow through to EBITDA as the business scales, although not all of it given bank sharing, technology and other costs,” Agrawal said. Advt Razorpay: ₹300 crore base case, up to ₹550 croreRazorpay's opportunity is smaller in absolute terms but could still be meaningful relative to its existing profitability.The fintech's FY25 revenue stood at around ₹3,783 crore, while gross profit was approximately ₹1,277 crore. Its total payment volume, however, includes cards, net banking, UPI and other modes, making the MDR-eligible base harder to establish.Biyani estimates Razorpay's annual TPV at around ₹16 lakh crore, with UPI accounting for roughly half. After excluding transactions below the threshold and exempt categories, he estimates ₹3.5-5.5 lakh crore could be eligible for MDR.His scenario analysis puts incremental annual revenue at:₹140 crore in the conservative case;₹315 crore in the base case; and₹550 crore in the upside case.“Razorpay sits on the acquiring side and shares the 12 bps acquirer slice with its sponsor bank. Large enterprises will also negotiate below 40 bps. Conservative: ₹3.5 lakh crore eligible at 4 bps retained, about ₹140 crore. Base: ₹4.5 lakh crore at 7 bps, about ₹315 crore. Optimistic: ₹5.5 lakh crore at 10 bps, about ₹550 crore,” Biyani said.Agrawal's estimate is broadly similar, putting Razorpay's incremental revenue at ₹100-150 crore in a conservative case, ₹300-350 crore in the base case and ₹500-600 crore in an upside scenario.The base-case ₹315 crore revenue addition would be equivalent to around 8% of Razorpay's FY25 revenue, but its impact is larger at the gross-profit level, where it would amount to roughly 25% of FY25 gross profit.Different exposure, same new monetisation leverThe estimates show why the MDR framework cannot be applied uniformly across fintech companies.For PhonePe, the key variable is its effective take-rate on eligible UPI value, given its dominant app-provider position. For Razorpay, the critical variables are its UPI share of TPV, eligible transaction value and the portion of acquiring economics retained after sharing with its sponsor bank.The overall industry pool is estimated at around ₹20,000-25,000 crore annually at scale, although the exact size will depend on transaction mix, exemptions and the proportion of P2M value above ₹2,000.The new framework therefore creates a potentially meaningful recurring revenue pool for the two IPO-bound fintechs, but the headline 40-bps MDR is not the number that will appear on either company's income statement.For PhonePe, analysts' estimates point to up to ₹2,000 crore of annual incremental revenue, while Razorpay's upside could reach ₹550 crore, making effective monetisation and eligible UPI volume key metrics for investors assessing both listings.
