Lender also announced that DMD Kaizad Bharuch has seeked early retirement, ending his tenure by December end.Axis Bank declined around 5.6 per cent for the week. The stock fell around 4.2 per cent on Monday, October 19, contributing to its broader weekly decline.PSU banks outperformed private lenders at the start of the week. The Nifty PSU Bank index gained around 2.5 per cent on Monday, supported by improved margins and asset-quality trends at select lenders.
