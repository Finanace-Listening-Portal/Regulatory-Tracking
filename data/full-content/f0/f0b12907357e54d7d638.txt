Articles 2 min read India insurance regulator to oversee smooth transition to new commission rules, chairman says India's insurance regulator, the IRDAI, plans to implement reforms for insurance sales to improve the market. Proposed changes include capping commissions and tightening expense limits to reduce costs and mis-selling. Ajay Seth stated the intention is to avoid disruption within the industry during this transition period. Insurers and distributors have expressed concerns regarding the potential impacts on business models and job security. A draft regulation will follow the consultation, giving stakeholders another chance to comment.Brokers have sought to push the October 25 consultation deadline to late December, Reuters reported earlier this month. Seth did not commit to an extension and urged stakeholders to send their comments on time.India is the world's 10th-largest insurance market, with $124.4 billion in premiums in fiscal 2025 across roughly 60 insurers, including Prudential and Sun Life Financial.The proposed rules are part of the IRDAI's push to reform the sector ​after New Delhi this year opened it to 100% foreign ownership, ​and target costs it says have ballooned since commission limits ⁠were scrapped in 2023.
