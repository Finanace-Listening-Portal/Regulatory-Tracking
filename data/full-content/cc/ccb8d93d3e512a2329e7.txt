Articles 3 min read JPMorgan working with NPCI on cross-border payments, can support in close to 70 countries: Christine Tan JPMorgan is working with NPCI on cross-border payments, can support close to 70 countries, said Christine Tan. “We’re trying to connect the dots on the cross-border and remove that friction,” she said, as real-time payment rails expand across Asia. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Updated On Sep 10, 2026 at 02:14 PM IST Read by 100 Professionals var news_msid = 133973005; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook New York headquartered JPMorgan is working with the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) on cross-border payments and can support NPCI in the jurisdictions where it operates, which are close to 70 countries, said Christine Tan, Head of Financial Institutions Group Sales, Asia Pacific, Payments, J.P. Morgan.“We're working with NPCI also specifically on the cross-border payment side of things, that we can support them in the jurisdictions that we operate, which is close to 70 countries,” Tan said.Tan said there is an emergence of real-time payments across Asia, with Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia among the markets developing such payment systems. She said UPI has been a role model in terms of building payment infrastructure that is accessible to people and corporates. Advt “UPI has been a role model in terms of looking at the infrastructure and ensuring that the rails are actually accessible to all people and all corporates,” she said.According to Tan, the next area of opportunity is connecting these real-time payment systems across borders and removing friction from cross-border payments.“From a comparison standpoint, so many of the locations in APAC are real-time. We're trying to connect the dots on the cross-border and remove that friction,” she said.Tan said JPMorgan continues to see payments as an area of growth, particularly with the growth of e-commerce and Asia's role in manufacturing and distribution within the region.“The growth in terms of e-commerce and Asia being very prevalent in that space continues to actually inspire us to develop and find solutions in terms of existing rails and then also innovating into the new rails to take this forward,” she said.She also highlighted the role of blockchain in the evolution of payments. Tan said JPMorgan is seeing growing adoption of blockchain for money movement, including the use of atomic settlement to remove friction and release capital for companies and organisations.“Money movement is one example where we move money for banks, we move money for fintechs. And we are seeing a growth of adoption on blockchain, leveraging atomic settlement, removing friction, and finally, also releasing capital for the companies and organizations,” she said. Advt Tan said JPMorgan is also working on fraud protection using blockchain account validation. She compared this with the validation of where money is being sent and received in the UPI ecosystem.“Now, on the cross-border, there is still friction,” she said, adding that JPMorgan has a solution for banks to process and validate transactions before payments go out.On infrastructure, Tan said the scale of the payments ecosystem means that existing machinery and infrastructure need to be enhanced. She said JPMorgan is looking at enhancing existing corridors and scale while ensuring resilience.“The second unlock in terms of theme is obviously banks. We've been here in India for 80 years. And certainly, the scale in terms of the machinery and infrastructure needs to be enhanced,” Tan said. Advt “So, we are looking at up-tiering in terms of enhancing existing corridors and scale in terms of making sure that they are resilient. Cyber security becomes a prevalent theme,” she added.Tan also identified AI as another major theme for the bank, saying JPMorgan is investing in and focusing on the technology.“And then finally, on AI, that's another big theme. And we're spending a lot of investments and focus on AI,” she said.On the relationship between banks and fintechs, Tan said JPMorgan works with fintechs in multiple capacities—as collaborators, clients and competitors.“We collaborate with fintechs. They are our clients and we co-compete with them as well. Ultimately, it's the collaboration and co-existence that's very important,” she said. Advt Tan said innovation in Asia has been particularly progressive, citing the region's real-time payment systems and digital payment ecosystems.She also pointed to different approaches across Asian markets, including China's digital banking and AI developments and its wallet ecosystem supporting e-commerce and trade flows.“There's slightly different in terms of prioritization on how you build rails for accessibility, and then you have rails for e-commerce. But you will see convergence in terms of adoption,” Tan said. By Shrishti Sharma , ETBFSI Published On Sep 9, 2026 at 08:00 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
