Articles 1 min read Expect more coordination among regulators: RBI DG At a recent event, RBI deputy governor SC Murmu addressed the challenges of overlapping regulatory jurisdictions in India's financial sector. He emphasized the need for better coordination on related-party transactions and the mis-selling of financial products. Highlighting the critical nature of cybersecurity, Murmu urged regulators to adopt common standards for resilience and to articulate expectations clearly, rather than simply adding to existing regulations as the sector evolves. "As regulators, our mandates intersect at many points," he said. Advt The distribution of insurance and mutual fund products by banks is another area of overlap, with mis-selling falling within the jurisdiction of multiple regulators. "Banks distribute insurance and mutual funds, so mis-selling concerns all three regulators," he said.Cybersecurity presents a similar challenge because an incident at one institution can affect entities overseen by different regulators. Murmu said regulators needed to establish common standards for cyber resilience. "A single incident can hit a bank, an insurer, and a market infrastructure institution at once," he said.He said coordination among regulators would be necessary to address these overlapping responsibilities. "You can expect closer coordination among us," he said. Murmu also said regulation needed to focus on clearly communicating expectations rather than attempting to keep pace with every new development through additional rules.
