Advt IndusInd, IDFC First carry highest exposureAccording to Jefferies, bancassurance income in FY26 was equivalent to 18% of FY27 normalised profit at IndusInd Bank and 17% at IDFC First Bank.The exposure was lower for AU Small Finance Bank at 11%, Axis Bank at 9%, HDFC Bank at 7% and Kotak Mahindra Bank at 5%.Among large private and PSU lenders, SBI’s bancassurance income was equivalent to 2% of FY27 profit, while the ratio stood at 2% for PNB and Bank of Baroda. ICICI Bank had the lowest exposure at 1%, according to Jefferies.The brokerage’s analysis is based on FY26 bancassurance income as a proportion of FY27 estimated normalised profit, and therefore indicates the relative earnings sensitivity rather than a direct estimate of the eventual earnings hit.Credit-protection business key pressure pointJefferies said the proposed commission caps would be “tad negative” for banks’ bancassurance fees, particularly for credit-protection premiums.The reason is the structure of these products: credit-protection insurance is largely sold as a single-premium policy, and such products currently carry relatively high commission rates. Any reduction in the permissible commission would therefore directly affect the fee income banks earn from distributing these policies.The proposed regime is scheduled to apply prospectively from FY28, giving banks and insurers time to adjust their distribution economics. Advt The regulator has sought public feedback on the draft norms over the next month. Jefferies expects the impact to be more visible in lenders where bancassurance contributes a larger share of profits.What the broader proposal means for banksThe IRDAI proposal is aimed at changing the way insurance commissions are capped across products and distribution channels. The broader framework includes product-, channel- and architecture-specific commission caps, with lower caps proposed for open architecture and mandatory or easier-to-sell products.For banks, the distinction between distribution models could matter because the proposed commission structure varies depending on whether the distributor operates as an insurance distribution entity, agent or through a distribution-cum-lending structure. Advt The impact will therefore not be uniform across lenders and will depend on their product mix, insurance partnerships and the share of bancassurance fees coming from products where commissions are being reset.Jefferies' assessment is that IndusInd Bank and IDFC First Bank have the highest relative exposure, while ICICI Bank and PSU banks have comparatively lower earnings sensitivity to the proposed changes.
