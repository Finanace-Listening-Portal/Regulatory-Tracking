Articles 1 min read RBI extends supersession of Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank board by one year The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the supersession of Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank's board for another year, nearly three years after it first took control of the Mumbai-based lender in November 2023 over governance concerns. The extension comes even as the former directors' challenge to the supersession has been dismissed by the Supreme Court, keeping the bank under an RBI-appointed Administrator. The supersession was subsequently extended for another year in November 2024 and November 2025.Following its latest review, RBI said it was satisfied that it was necessary to extend the period of supersession beyond the close of business on November 23, 2026.
