Despite that, the company's counter came under pressure on Tuesday. At 09:17 AM, Bajaj Finance’s share price was trading 2.4 per cent lower at ₹935 per share on NSE. In comparison, Nifty 50 was up 0.71 per cent at 23,051.10. In intraday trade, the stock fell 2.8 per cent to touch the day's low at ₹930 per share. Bajaj Finance Q2 results highlights: Bajaj Finance reported a 29 per cent year-on-year (YoY) rise in consolidated net profit to ₹6,367 crore for the July-September quarter of FY27 (Q2FY27), driven by strong growth in net interest income (NII) and assets under management (AUM).NII grew 24 per cent YoY to ₹13,335 crore, compared with ₹10,785 crore a year ago. Net total income increased 23 per cent to ₹15,974 crore, while AUM rose 26 per cent to ₹5.85 trillion as of September 30, 2026.The company added ₹37,680 crore to its AUM during the quarter and booked 13.45 million new loans, up 11 per cent from a year ago. However, it said the YoY growth in loan volumes was not comparable because the festive season had started earlier last year.
