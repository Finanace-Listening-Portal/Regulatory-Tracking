Articles 2 min read Uday Kotak at ₹1.19 lakh crore, Zerodha's Kamath brothers at ₹98,900 crore in M3M Hurun India Rich List Uday Kotak leads the financial services wealth table at ₹1.19 lakh crore, while Nithin Kamath and Nikhil Kamath together account for ₹98,900 crore in M3M Hurun India Rich List 2026. Zerodha’s Nikhil Kamath also jumped 52 places to enter the top 100. Nikhil Kamath, co-founder of Zerodha, has ₹39,900 crore, while Sanjay Dangi and family of Authum Investment & Infrastructure are at ₹36,300 crore. Advt The five together account for ₹4.10 lakh crore in wealth.Nikhil Kamath climbs 52 places into top 100Nikhil Kamath is among the biggest movers in the latest rich list, climbing 52 places to rank 67 with wealth of ₹39,900 crore.His rank now puts him alongside established business leaders across banking, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing and technology. He shares the 67th position with PV Ramprasad Reddy of Aurobindo Pharma.Nithin Kamath and family, meanwhile, have ₹59,000 crore, taking the combined wealth of the two Zerodha founders to ₹98,900 crore.The Kamath brothers’ combined wealth is therefore equivalent to more than 83% of Uday Kotak’s ₹1.19 lakh crore wealth.Kotak, Kamaths account for a significant chunk of financial wealthThe concentration at the top of the Financial Services cohort is pronounced. Uday Kotak’s ₹1.19 lakh crore wealth is followed closely by Khosla’s ₹1.16 lakh crore, leaving a combined ₹2.35 lakh crore with just two names.The Kamath family adds another ₹98,900 crore, while Dangi’s ₹36,300 crore takes the wealth represented by the five names to ₹4.10 lakh crore.Beyond the top five, the sector’s wealth creation is spread across banking, broking, investment and other financial businesses, reflecting the widening pool of wealth creators beyond traditional financial institutions. Advt The broader Hurun list has 1,810 individuals with combined wealth of ₹187.5 lakh crore. The top 100 now requires a minimum wealth of ₹33,000 crore, meaning both Nikhil Kamath at ₹39,900 crore and Sanjay Dangi at ₹36,300 crore clear the latest top-100 threshold.The report also shows that 23 individuals with wealth above ₹1 lakh crore collectively hold ₹54.21 lakh crore, or 28.9% of the total wealth represented by the list.
