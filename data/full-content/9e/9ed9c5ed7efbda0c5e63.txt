The maximum amount that can be received is Rs 10,000 per transaction, subject to a monthly limit of Rs 25,000 per beneficiary.For cash transfers through business correspondents, ATMs and other permitted channels, the limit is Rs 5,000 per transaction, subject to a monthly ceiling of Rs 25,000 per remitter. Advt The rules largely consolidate existing instructions rather than introduce substantial changes to transaction limits. The framework will help migrant workers, daily wage earners and people who need to send money to family members in other parts of the country but may not have access to regular banking facilities.Customers using these channels must register with a verified mobile number and submit a self-certified officially valid document in accordance with the RBI's Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements.Every transaction must also undergo an additional factor of authentication. Under this facility, a person can receive money sent from another person's bank account and collect it in cash through an ATM, business correspondent or another authorised payment channel.The bank sending the money must record and maintain the beneficiary's name and address. This facility can help people who receive financial support from family members working in other cities but do not have bank accounts of their own.The central bank has directed banks and non-bank entities to establish monitoring systems to prevent fraud and misuse of cash-based transfers, including monitoring transaction frequency, sending alerts during crediting amounts and investigating unusual increases in the number of credits into a particular account or group of accounts.-IANSaar/ag IANS Published On Nov 13, 2026 at 03:01 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
