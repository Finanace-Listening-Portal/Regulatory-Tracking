However, Micro exposure businesses, continued to account for 84.4% of all active loan accounts in the market, dominating the field, according to the report. Advt The expansion in MSMEx credit has been driven predominantly by banks, while NBFC credit growth has remained comparatively moderate.Private Banks lead cluster financingPrivate Sector banks emerged as the dominant lender type across most major MSME clusters, including Surat, Ahmedabad, Tiruppur, and Rajkot, accounting for 41.9% of the outstanding portfolio as of June 2026. Their portfolio rose 13.2% year-on-year.According to the Report, in Rajkot’s Engineering & Machinery cluster, private banks' credit share rose sharply from 45.5% to 57.6% over the last three years, while in Surat's wholesale trade hub, they held a commanding 75.9% share.Public sector banks accounted for another 31.6% of the portfolio and recorded 13.5% growth during the period. The report also states that PSBs led on active loan volumes with 41.6% share.NBFCs gain in Chemical industriesWhile PSBs and PVBs dominate the lending in most clusters, NBFCs have quietly strengthened their position in certain sectors like the Chemicals, where private banks are keeping low pace in key districts like Ahmedabad, Mumbai and Hyderabad. Report mentions that even though the Private banks are leading the lending in many top districts, they are steadily losing ground to NBFCs and other lenders, potentially indicating a moderation in this credit activity. Advt NBFCs, meanwhile, held a 15.6% share of the portfolio. Their MSMEx credit grew 4.2% year-on-year, considerably slower than the growth recorded by both private and PSU banks.Term loans continue to dominate while Working Capital demand acceleratesThe Report mentioned that on the product side, term loans continue as the largest component of MSMEx credit, accounting for 51.3% of portfolio outstanding, growing 13.3% year-on-year, as of June 2026.On the other hand, Working capital-Overdraft facilities grew the fastest at 14.6% year-on-year, pointing towards the growing demand for both long term financing as well as working capital requirements among MSMEx borrowers. Advt The report emphasized that the sharpest build-up of lender exposure came in Wholesale Trade and Engineering & Machinery, where high-volume districts more than doubled and nearly doubled respectively, over three years, at 71.7% and 63.5% of their sector portfolios.Manufacturing emerged as the fastest-growing major activity, with credit rising 17.4% year-on-year, while trading grew 12.7%.The report also noted expansion in high-credit clusters across Engineering & Machinery, Wholesale and Retail Trade, with several of these clusters recording stable or improving portfolio-quality indicators.
