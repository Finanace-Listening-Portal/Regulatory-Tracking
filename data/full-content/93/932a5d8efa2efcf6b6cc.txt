Summary: The Department of Commerce, SEZ Division, through letter K-43022/239/2025-SEZ dated 25 November 2026, has informed Development Commissioners about the integration of SEZ Online with the Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN). The integration enables electronic transmission of approved invoices relating to supplies from the Domestic Tariff Area (DTA) to Special Economic Zone (SEZ) entities. SEZ Online, managed by NDML, has been connected with GSTN through the MPLS protocol, with APIs facilitating transmission of invoice data under the DTA Procurement (Goods), DTA Procurement with Export Benefit (Goods) and DTA Services Procurement Form (DSPF) modules. Development Commissioners have been requested to discontinue manual endorsement wherever it is still followed and instruct SEZ Units, Developers and Co-developers to ensure timely filing of invoices through the applicable modules. The administrative direction changes the mode of endorsement without dispensing with the statutory requirement of establishing receipt of goods or services for authorised operations under the GST refund framework. Electronic endorsement is expected to reduce documentation-related disputes, facilitate verification and improve refund processing. However, DTA suppliers will increasingly depend on SEZ customers for timely invoice submission and approval. The letter does not clarify the treatment of previously endorsed invoices, pending refund applications or the manner in which electronic endorsements will appear on the GST portal. Suppliers should reconcile invoice particulars across GST returns and SEZ Online, retain approval references and monitor jurisdictional instructions and subsequent GSTN or CBIC advisories. NDML, (Kind Attn: Shri Vijay Gupta) Earlier Position Under Section 16(1)(b), IGST Act, 2017, a supply of goods or services to an SEZ developer or SEZ unit is a zero-rated supply. The supplier may supply under LUT without payment of tax and claim refund of unutilised input tax credit, or pay IGST and claim refund of that tax. The second proviso to Rule 89(1), CGST Rules, 2017 allows a supplier of goods to an SEZ to file the refund application only after the goods are admitted in full in the Zone for authorised operations, as endorsed by the specified officer of the Zone. A supplier of services must file the application with evidence of receipt of services for authorised operations, as endorsed by the specified officer. Rule 89(2), CGST Rules, 2017 accordingly requires this endorsement evidence to accompany the refund application. In practice, several zones continued to give this endorsement as a physical stamp and signature on the invoice. Practical Headaches Faced Chasing the stamp: Suppliers had to follow up with the SEZ unit and the SEZ office, often for months, to obtain endorsed copies of invoices. Disputes on genuineness: Refund officers frequently questioned endorsements on scanned copies, leading to deficiency memos and show cause notices. Non-uniform practice: Some zones endorsed electronically, others manually. Suppliers dealing with several zones faced different procedures. Delay in refund: The refund clock could not start until endorsed documents were in hand, blocking working capital. The Change The letter records three things. First, SEZ Online, managed by NDML, has been successfully connected with GSTN through the MPLS protocol. Second, SEZ Online APIs are ready to transmit invoices filed and approved under three modules: a. DTA Procurement (Goods); b. DTA Procurement with Export Benefit (Goods); and c. DTA Services Procurement Form (DSPF). Third, Development Commissioners are requested to issue instructions conveying that the existing manual endorsement process, if still followed by SEZ officials, is discontinued with immediate effect. They are also to direct SEZ Units, Developers and Co-developers to ensure timely filing of invoices through the DSPF module, wherever applicable. The letter is copied to the Joint Secretary (GST Policy), CBIC, the Director General, SEZEPC and NDML. The copy to CBIC indicates that a corresponding instruction on the GST side may follow. Comparison at a Glance Aspect Earlier Now Mode of endorsement Physical stamp and signature of SEZ officer, in zones still following it Electronic, on approval in SEZ Online Flow of data Paper invoice furnished by supplier with refund claim Approved invoice data sent from SEZ Online to GSTN through APIs over MPLS Modules Not applicable DTA Procurement (Goods), DTA Procurement with Export Benefit (Goods), DSPF Verification by refund officer Manual, on the face of the document Against data received from SEZ Online Responsibility Supplier chases the SEZ office for a stamp SEZ Unit, Developer or Co-developer files the invoice on time Applicability and Points to Note Nature of the Instrument This is an administrative letter to Development Commissioners. It does not amend the SEZ Act, the SEZ Rules or Rule 89, CGST Rules. The legal requirement of endorsement continues; only its mode changes. Effective Date Discontinuance of manual endorsement is with immediate effect, subject to instructions issued by each Development Commissioner. Who Is Covered DTA suppliers of goods and services to SEZ Units, Developers and Co-developers, and the SEZ entities themselves. Dependence on the Buyer The endorsement now arises only when the SEZ entity files and gets the invoice approved on SEZ Online. A supplier cannot obtain it independently. Portal-Side Questions The letter does not specify how the transmitted data will appear on the GST common portal or link to the refund application. A GSTN or CBIC advisory is awaited. Pending and Past Invoices The letter is silent on invoices already endorsed manually and on refund claims already filed with manual endorsements. Drafting Quirk Paragraph 3 of the letter, dated 25-11-2026, calls for an Action Taken Report by 18-11-2026, a date earlier than the letter itself. Practical Checklist Review all open supplies to SEZ customers and confirm the invoice has been filed in the correct SEZ Online module. Ask SEZ customers to share the SEZ Online approval reference for each invoice. Match GSTIN, invoice number, date and value across the e-invoice, GSTR-1 and the SEZ Online filing before filing the refund. Add a clause in purchase orders or contracts with SEZ customers requiring timely filing on SEZ Online. SEZ entities should file DTA procurement invoices and DSPF promptly, as delay now directly holds up the supplier refund. Track the instruction of the jurisdictional Development Commissioner and any GSTN or CBIC advisory on refund filing. Concluding Remarks The integration moves the SEZ endorsement from paper to data. It should reduce disputes on the genuineness of endorsements and speed up verification by refund officers. The burden, however, now sits with the SEZ customer. DTA suppliers should build the SEZ Online filing into their order-to-refund process and watch for the GST-side advisory before changing refund practice.
