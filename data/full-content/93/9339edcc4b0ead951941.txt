Articles 5 min read UPI MDR could add $2-3 billion to PhonePe valuation, up to $1 billion to Razorpay: Analysts PhonePe could get $2.2-3 billion of incremental valuation support and Razorpay $400 million-$1 billion, as MDR converts UPI scale into recurring revenue and EBITDA, analysts estimate. Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Oct 1, 2026 at 01:01 PM IST var news_msid = 134611915; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook UPI MDR charge (Representational image)The new UPI MDR framework could give PhonePe $2.2-3 billion of incremental valuation support and Razorpay as much as $1 billion, according to analyst calculations based on the potential EBITDA contribution from the new fee regime. The potential uplift comes as both fintechs prepare for public listings, with PhonePe's latest reported IPO valuation target at $9-10.5 billion and Razorpay's proposed valuation estimated at around $5-6 billion, making the new UPI revenue pool a potentially important variable in their listing valuations.“On the maths, ₹1,100-1,500 crore of incremental EBITDA is about $120-165 million. At 25x forward EBITDA, that is worth $3.1-4.1 billion. After a 30% haircut for regulatory, competitive and reinvestment risk, it adds about $2.2-3.0 billion. That moves a $13-15 billion range to roughly $15-18 billion,” said Naresh Biyani, Founder and CEO of Capwise Financial Services. Advt PhonePe: ₹1,100-1,500 crore EBITDA could mean $2.2-3 billionThe valuation calculation is built on the potential earnings rather than the headline 0.4% MDR.Biyani estimates that PhonePe could generate ₹1,600-2,000 crore of annual MDR revenue by FY28, with 65-75% flowing through to EBITDA. This translates into roughly ₹1,100-1,500 crore of incremental EBITDA, or around $120-165 million at an assumed ₹90 to the dollar.At a 25x forward EBITDA multiple, the incremental earnings would theoretically support around $3.1-4.1 billion of additional enterprise value.Applying a 30% discount for regulatory, competitive and reinvestment risks reduces the estimated valuation support to approximately $2.2-3 billion.Aakash Agrawal, Associate Director – Digital and New Age Business at Anand Rathi Investment Banking, takes a lower valuation view.“I don't think MDR by itself fundamentally changes the valuation framework, but it materially strengthens the earnings story. If PhonePe can build ₹750-1,000 crore or more of incremental, recurring MDR revenue, I think it can provide roughly $0.5-1 billion of additional valuation support, depending on the multiple investors assign to that revenue and its eventual EBITDA conversion,” Agrawal said.The divergence illustrates the sensitivity of the valuation calculation to both the eventual MDR revenue and the multiple assigned to it. Advt $9-10.5 billion IPO target makes starting point criticalThe valuation discussion also needs to distinguish between different reported starting points for PhonePe. According to media reports in March, PhonePe was targeting a $9-10.5 billion valuation for its proposed IPO, with the company looking to raise around $900 million-$1.05 billion. The IPO was subsequently paused amid market volatility, although later reporting indicated the company continued to target that valuation range when it resumes the offering.The $13-15 billion starting range used in Biyani's calculation is therefore different from the latest media reported IPO target. That distinction materially changes the implied post-MDR valuation. Using Biyani's $2.2-3 billion incremental valuation support, a $13-15 billion starting valuation would translate into approximately $15-18 billion. Advt Against the latest reported $9-10.5 billion IPO target, the same incremental value would instead imply a substantially different valuation range. This makes the key investment question less about a single "new valuation" and more about how public-market investors value the recurring MDR earnings stream.Razorpay could get up to $1 billion upliftRazorpay's potential valuation gain is smaller in absolute terms but significant relative to its expected IPO valuation. Biyani estimates a base-case ₹315 crore incremental MDR revenue, with around 70% flowing through to EBITDA. That produces approximately ₹220 crore of incremental EBITDA, or around $25 million. Advt At a 25-30x EBITDA multiple, the incremental enterprise value works out to roughly $625-750 million before applying a risk discount. Biyani's broader calculation puts the base-case valuation support at around $400-700 million, while the upside case could reach $1 billion.“Razorpay's current IPO discussions have been around $5-6 billion, so MDR improves the fundamental case for a higher valuation, but I would not assume it automatically takes the company back to its earlier $7.5 billion private valuation. MDR does not change Razorpay's equity story, but it weakens the ‘UPI earns zero’ bear case and supports gateway margins,” Biyani said.Razorpay's FY25 revenue was around ₹3,783 crore, with gross profit of approximately ₹1,277 crore. Its confidential IPO filing has been reported to include a fresh issue of around ₹2,700 crore, with the overall IPO potentially worth around ₹5,000-6,000 crore.10-15% uplift possible in Razorpay base caseAgainst a reported $5-6 billion expected IPO valuation, Biyani's estimated $400-700 million base-case valuation support translates into a potential uplift of roughly 7-14%. At the upper end, a $1 billion valuation addition would represent approximately 17-20% of a $5-6 billion starting valuation.Agrawal similarly estimates $300 million-$1 billion of incremental valuation support for Razorpay, depending on the extent to which the MDR revenue flows through to EBITDA.The wide range reflects a key limitation in Razorpay's calculation: the company does not publicly disclose the precise share of its total TPV represented by eligible UPI transactions.Biyani assumes UPI accounts for around half of Razorpay's approximately ₹16 lakh crore annual TPV, with around 20-35% of total TPV ultimately qualifying for MDR.MDR changes the valuation equation, but take-rate remains keyFor both companies, the new MDR framework introduces a valuation variable that did not previously exist: a recurring, merchant-funded revenue stream from UPI.For PhonePe, analysts estimate the incremental EBITDA could reach ₹1,100-1,500 crore, producing potential valuation support of $2.2-3 billion under one methodology.For Razorpay, the estimated incremental EBITDA is around ₹220 crore in the base case, with potential valuation support of $400 million-$1 billion.But neither number follows directly from the 0.4% headline MDR. The fee is distributed across the payments ecosystem, while exemptions and negotiated rates reduce the addressable pool.The valuation outcome will ultimately depend on three numbers: eligible UPI value, effective take-rate and EBITDA flow-through.For PhonePe, the 8-bps app-provider economics assumed by analysts are particularly important. For Razorpay, the crucial variables are its eligible UPI TPV and the portion of the acquiring-side economics it retains.The MDR framework therefore gives both IPO candidates a new monetisation lever, but the eventual public-market valuation will depend on how much of that theoretical pool converts into recurring earnings.
