CompaniesHDFC Bank's New CEO: Anup Bagchi Takes Charge As MD And CEOAuthored by: Sakshi BajajUpdated: Oct 27, 2026, 13:40 ISTAnup Bagchi has taken charge as HDFC Bank's Managing Director and CEO, marking a leadership transition at one of India’s largest private sector lenders.HDFC Bank's New CEO: Anup Bagchi Takes Charge As MD And CEO (File Image)Photo : ANIfollow usfollow usCountry's largest private sector lender HDFC Bank on Tuesday said that Anup Bagchi has taken charge as its Managing Director and CEO. His predecessor, Sashidhar Jagdishan, vacated office on Monday after the completion of his term, the bank informed exchanges.Bagchi, an ICICI Group veteran, is the first outsider to head HDFC Bank in its over-three-decade history. His previous assignment was as the MD and CEO of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance. He joins HDFC Bank at a time when the lender is battling a slew of challenges, including ones on core income growth and margins, which have made investors wary.In brief comments on an earnings call which he attended, Bagchi said a part of the work on the bank's 2023 merger with housing finance major parent HDFC is yet to be executed and also announced that he will be meeting all the stakeholders understand the current situation at the bank. Bagchi had also appreciated the strong and committed leadership at HDFC Bank, amid speculation over whether he continues with the same team or gets new people.Ahead of his joining, Kaizad Bharucha, a HDFC Bank veteran who is serving as the Deputy Managing Director, announced his intent to retire by December this year. Jagdishan had announced his unavailability for an extension amid allegations of shortcomings on the governance front. The bank's own probe had also found shortcomings in the practice of booking high quantum deposits by paying off an extra amount over and above the card rate to the depositor under a different head. Jagdishan was also slapped with a fine for the practice.The surprise resignation of the bank's Non-Executive Chairman Atanu Chakraborty in March this year, flagging values and ethics, blew the lid off certain practices at the largest private lender.
