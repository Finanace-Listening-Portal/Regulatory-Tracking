Articles 1 min read HSBC has used over half of $1-billion startup fund within a year: Global innovation banking head David Sabow HSBC has deployed more than half of its $1 billion loan commitment to Indian startups within a year of launching its innovation banking platform. The British lender has reported strong demand across consumer technology, e-commerce and enterprise software, and is open to increasing its funding allocation. HSBC Global Head of Innovation Banking David Sabow said the bank expects to add at least 500 clients in India over the next year. This deployment is spread mostly on the portfolio companies across consumer tech, business to business (B2B), business to consumer (B2C) commerce, and a little bit on the enterprise software companies also.
