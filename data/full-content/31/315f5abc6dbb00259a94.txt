Articles 3 min read Bank of Baroda Q2 Results: Standalone profit jumps 36% YoY to Rs 6,532 crore; NII up 9 Bank of Baroda reported a 35.8% year-on-year rise in standalone net profit to Rs 6,532 crore in Q2FY27. Net interest income grew 9% to Rs 13,026 crore, while asset quality improved, with the gross NPA ratio declining to 1.78%. Global advances and deposits also recorded double-digit growth. For H1FY27, operating profit stood at Rs 19,557 crore, registering a growth of 23.7% YoY. Advt Global net interest margin (NIM) stood at 2.74% during the quarter. Return on Assets (ROA) improved by 19 basis points (bps) YoY to 1.26%, while Return on Equity (ROE) increased by 363 bps YoY to 19.28%.Bank of Baroda Q2: Asset QualityOn the asset quality front, Bank of Baroda reported an improvement in its gross non-performing assets (GNPA) ratio, which declined to 1.78% in Q2FY27 from 2.16% in Q2FY26.The net NPA (NNPA) ratio improved by 20 bps YoY to 0.37%. The slippage ratio declined by 22 bps YoY to 0.69% during the quarter, while the credit cost stood at 0.55%.The bank's provision coverage ratio (PCR), including technical write-offs (TWO), improved by 144 bps YoY to 94.65%. Excluding TWO, the PCR improved by 535 bps to 79.48%.Bank of Baroda Q2: Business PerformanceOn the business front, the bank's global advances increased 18.3% YoY to Rs 15,12,448 crore as of September 2026. Domestic advances grew 13.5% YoY to Rs 11,87,944 crore, while international advances registered a growth of 39.7% to Rs 3,24,504 crore.Global deposits increased 16.4% YoY to Rs 17,45,435 crore, while domestic deposits grew 16.6% to Rs 14,83,521 crore.Domestic CASA deposits registered a growth of 10.4% YoY to Rs 5,39,515 crore as of September 30, 2026. International deposits increased 14.9% YoY to Rs 2,61,915 crore.Also read: Sun Pharma Q2 Results: Profit falls 36% YoY to Rs 1,989 crore on exceptional charges Advt Among key business segments, organic retail advances grew 18.6% YoY, driven by growth in auto loans (27.8%), mortgage loans (20.3%), home loans (14.6%) and education loans (9.9%).The agriculture loan portfolio increased 19% YoY to Rs 2,01,946 crore, while the organic MSME portfolio grew 21.3% to Rs 1,75,207 crore. Corporate advances registered a growth of 6.3% YoY to Rs 4,25,736 crore.Bank of Baroda Q2: Capital AdequacyThe bank's capital adequacy ratio (CRAR) stood at 16.61% as of September 2026. Tier-I capital stood at 14.70%, comprising CET-1 of 14.20% and additional Tier-I capital of 0.50%, while Tier-II capital stood at 1.91%. Advt The consolidated CRAR and CET-1 ratios stood at 17.01% and 14.65%, respectively.
