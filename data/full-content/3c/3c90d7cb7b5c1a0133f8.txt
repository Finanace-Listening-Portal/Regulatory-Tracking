(PTI Photo/Shashank Parade)Photo : PTIfollow usfollow usThe Reserve Bank of India is scheduled to hold a meeting with non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) on November 23, with microfinance sector developments, gold loan-related matters and cybersecurity issues expected to be among the topics of discussion. RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra and the Deputy Governors are likely to hold these discussions with executives from the non-banking financial services sector While the RBI is expected to hold an interaction with senior executives of NBFCs as part of their ongoing engagement with the banking and financial services sector, the outcome will be crucial to understand RBI's assessment and outlook on various issues. Experts believe this will also enable them to understand RBI's expectations from lenders on risk management, asset quality and consumer/customer protection. RBI's observations on the rising gold loan delinquencies, repayment pressures in microfinance and preparedness against cyber fraud will also be crucial to watch out for, analysts believe.
