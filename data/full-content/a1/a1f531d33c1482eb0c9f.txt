PTI Published On Nov 18, 2026 at 07:00 PM IST var news_msid = 135964771; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Domestic and global institutional investors like Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund and BlackRock increased their stake in Groww by acquiring shares worth Rs 1,905 crore, according to NSE block deal data.The investors collectively purchased 8.70 crore equity shares, representing a 1.38 per cent stake in Billionbrains Garage Ventures, the parent company of Groww, at an average price of Rs 219 apiece on Tuesday.Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund (MF) led the buying spree, acquiring 2.05 crore shares or a 0.33 per cent stake, for Rs 448.95 crore, making it the biggest buyer in the transaction.The share purchase increased Kotak MF's stake in Groww to 2.21 per cent from 1.89 per cent. Advt BlackRock, the world's largest asset management company, was the second-largest buyer of Bengaluru-based Groww, picking up more than 1.34 crore shares for Rs 294.55 crore.Other domestic institutional investors participating in the transaction included Tata Mutual Fund, National Pension System (NPS) Trust, and HDFC Life Insurance.The overseas buyers included Societe Generale, BNP Paribas, Fidelity Investments, Viridian Asset Management, Susquehanna International Group, Canada's RBC Global Asset Management, Swiss banking major UBS, Morgan Stanley, and Ghisallo Capital Management.London-based Investment management firm Redwheel, Australia's largest superannuation fund Rest Super, Teachers' Retirement System of the State of Illinois and Norway's Government Pension Fund Global were also among the buyers.On the selling side of the deal, US-based venture capital firm Ribbit Capital, through two of its affiliates, offloaded 8.70 crore equity shares at the same price, as per the data.After the latest transaction, Ribbit Capital's combined stake in Groww declined to 6.91 per cent from 8.29 per cent.The churn in Groww's shareholding comes as its early investors continue to unlock value following the company's stock market debut in November 2025.The latest transaction follows a series of stake sales by its early backers, who have been gradually trimming their holdings even as a wider pool of institutional investors builds exposure to the online brokerage platform. Advt In May this year, early investors Peak XV Partners, Ribbit Capital and Y Combinator collectively sold a 4.71 per cent stake in Groww for Rs 5,326 crore following the expiry of the six-month post-IPO lock-in period.The stake sale continued in the following months. Early-stage startup accelerator Y Combinator divested nearly a 1.2 per cent stake for Rs 1,435 crore in August, while Ribbit Capital sold a 1 per cent stake in a transaction valued at Rs 2,217 crore during the same month.In September, Peak XV Partners offloaded a 1.46 per cent stake in Groww for Rs 1,756 crore, reducing its holding in the firm to 14.22 per cent from 15.68 per cent. Advt Earlier this month, Peak XV Partners, Y Combinator and Goldman Sachs sold shares worth a combined Rs 2,457 crore, representing a 1.84 per cent stake in the company.According to market analysts, the successive stake sales have not meant a complete departure of the early backers, who continued to hold substantial stakes at the end of the September quarter.Peak XV Partners, through its affiliates, remained Groww's largest public shareholder with a 15.54 per cent stake in the country's largest broking firm by active investors.Y Combinator held a combined 8.61 per cent stake, while Tiger Global Management, another early investor, owned 3.27 per cent. Advt Other minority public shareholders included San Francisco-based ICONIQ Capital, which held a 1.27 per cent stake, and Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC, with a 1.07 per cent holding.Groww's founders and promoters also have a significant ownership position.Co-founder, promoter and Chief Executive Officer Lalit Keshre, along with the other promoters, collectively held 27.13 per cent in Groww at the end of the September quarter.The investor reshuffle has its roots in Groww's Rs 6,632-crore IPO, through which its parent company Billionbrains Garage Ventures went public with a fresh issue and an Offer for Sale (OFS) of over 55.72 crore shares.Peak XV Partners was the largest seller in the OFS, offloading shares worth Rs 1,582.81 crore, followed by Ribbit Capital at Rs 1,181.32 crore and Y Combinator at Rs 1,054.82 crore.Shares of Groww pared early gains and settled 1.66 per cent lower at Rs 223.75 apiece on the National Stock Exchange on Wednesday.
