Lender is also proposing V Srinivasa Rangan’s reappointment as ED and Jimmy Tata’s appointment as an ED. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Sep 12, 2026 at 06:12 PM IST var news_msid = 134132070; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Largest private sector lender HDFC Bank's board on Saturday approved the submission of two names in order of preference to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for appointment as the bank's next Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer, setting the succession process in motion ahead of current CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan's exit.The candidates' names have not been disclosed by the bank.Jagdishan had decided not to seek reappointment for a third term when his current tenure ends on October 26, 2026. He subsequently reiterated his decision, following which the bank said he would retire from the lender at the close of business on October 26. Advt The candidates' names have not been disclosed and the appointment will be for a period of three years and will be subject to RBI approval, HDFC Bank said in a regulatory filing on Saturday.The board also approved the reappointment of V Srinivasa Rangan as Whole-time Director, designated as Executive Director, for a period from November 23, 2026 to November 22, 2027.Lender has proposed the appointment of Jimmy Tata as Whole-time Director, designated as Executive Director, for three years from the date of RBI approval or such other date or period as specified by the central bank.The bank has also approved the creation of one additional Whole-time Director position, taking the number of Executive Directors on its board to four, in addition to the MD & CEO.The additional position is aimed at sharper synergy and oversight, including over the bank's subsidiaries, as well as creating a larger pipeline for succession planning, according to the filing.The position will be filled in consultation with the new MD & CEO after the incoming chief takes charge. HDFC Bank said the necessary shareholder approvals will be sought in due course.Rangan, 66, is currently an Executive Director at HDFC Bank and oversees human resources, corporate legal, group oversight and secretarial, investment banking, information security, ethics, and fraud and vigilance functions. Advt Prior to the amalgamation of HDFC Limited with HDFC Bank in July 2023, Rangan was Executive Director & CFO of HDFC Limited. He has expertise across finance, accountancy, audit, economics, corporate governance, legal and regulatory compliance, risk management and strategic planning.He is also a director on the boards of Credila Financial Services and HDFC Asset Management Company.Tata has over 35 years of experience in the banking and financial services sector and has been associated with HDFC Bank since 1994.He joined the bank as a Relationship Manager in corporate banking and subsequently headed the corporate banking department. He was appointed Chief Risk Officer in June 2013 and is currently the Chief Credit Officer of HDFC Bank. Advt The appointments and remuneration proposed for the executive directors will also be subject to the requisite regulatory approvals.
