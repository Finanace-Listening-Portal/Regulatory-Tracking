Articles 1 min read IPO-bound Paramotor announces partnerships with NSDL Payments Bank, Mastercard, TBX Paramotor Digital Technology Ltd has announced new partnerships to strengthen its digital payment offerings. The fintech firm is collaborating with NSDL Payments Bank and Mastercard for new gift and prepaid cards. An agreement with TBX aims to expand the distribution of Paramotor's platform ecosystem. The company is also providing technology services for Osmo Atorie's closed-loop card. These collaborations focus on building connected digital value infrastructure for consumers and businesses.
