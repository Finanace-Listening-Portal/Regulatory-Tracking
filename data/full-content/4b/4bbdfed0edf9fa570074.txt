Advt Vehicle loans grew 15.4 per cent year-on-year to Rs 6.37 lakh crore, while housing loans rose 12.1 per cent to Rs 8.62 lakh crore.Credit to industry grew at a slower pace of 8.4 per cent year-on-year to Rs 22.29 lakh crore in August 2026.Within industry, infrastructure credit grew 7.2 per cent year-on-year to Rs 20.28 lakh crore, while power-sector credit rose 8.3 per cent to Rs 14.49 lakh crore.Credit to the services sector rose 16.2 per cent year-on-year to Rs 7.79 lakh crore. Commercial real estate credit grew 21.8 per cent to Rs 1.04 lakh crore, while trade credit increased 15.6 per cent to Rs 1.17 lakh crore.Credit to agriculture and allied activities rose 17.4 per cent year-on-year to Rs 80,109 crore in August 2026.
