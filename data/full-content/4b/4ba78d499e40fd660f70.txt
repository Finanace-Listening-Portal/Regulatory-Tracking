The stake sale will not only push the government's divestment proceeds in the ongoing financial year (2026-27) to an eight-year high but also exceed the annual target of ₹80,000 crore. The total stake sale approved in IDBI Bank to Canada-based Fairfax Holdings is ₹52,878 crore, with Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) and the government selling a combined 60.72 per cent stake at ₹81 apiece. LIC is selling a 30.24 per cent stake, and the government will offload 30.48 per cent. According to data available on the Department of Investment and Public Asset Management (DIPAM), the total divestment receipts for the government is ₹55,757 crore for the ongoing fiscal. The government stake sale in IDBI Bank would amount to nearly ₹26,400 crore, taking the total proceeds for the year to ₹82,196 crore, the most in eight financial years.
