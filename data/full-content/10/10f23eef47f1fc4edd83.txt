Summary : Livepeer is an open network that coordinates independently operated GPU infrastructure for video transcoding and, under its current Livepeer 2.0 direction, real-time AI video workloads. The protocol uses Ethereum-based coordination and operates its production staking and payment mechanics on Arbitrum One. GPU providers participate as orchestrators: they run the software, advertise capacity and pricing, perform eligible work and may receive service fees and protocol rewards. LPT is the protocol coordination token. An orchestrator needs LPT stake to compete for work, while a holder who does not operate hardware may delegate LPT to an orchestrator. Delegation is not a deposit account, fixed-interest product or guarantee of work. Rewards, fees, active-set position and unbonding outcomes depend on protocol rules, operator performance, network demand and on-chain execution. Indian users must separately address custody, token migration and network selection, transaction valuation, the special virtual-digital-asset tax regime and applicable tax deduction at source. This guide explains the current network roles, staking cycle, operational risks and Indian record keeping without recommending LPT or predicting its price. The supply layer consists of independent GPU operators. The coordination layer discovers orchestrators, routes jobs, settles payments and applies quality-of-service controls. The demand layer consists of applications and services that use the network for streaming, inference, generation, analysis or other video work. The protocol is therefore not a video-hosting company and LPT is not a claim on a single corporate balance sheet. A developer normally reaches compute through a gateway or application, while an orchestrator supplies the underlying processing capacity. The 2.0 evolution extends the network toward GPU-powered AI video tasks such as inference, generation, analysis and transformation. The official site now presents both capabilities, but a user should not assume that every orchestrator supports every model or that every historical transcoder is configured for AI workloads. Hardware, CUDA, container, endpoint and model requirements can differ materially. Service Fees and Protocol Rewards Come From Different Sources An orchestrator can receive fees for completed work and protocol rewards tied to stake and participation. Current operator documentation states that Arbitrum One ETH is needed for activation, reward calls, ticket redemption and gas. Pricing may be advertised off-chain per pixel or workload and can be adjusted for redemption overhead. These mechanics mean revenue is variable. A large LPT stake does not create demand, a listed price does not assure selection, and displayed network yields are scenario outputs rather than promised returns. LPT Staking Delegation and Unbonding Mechanics LPT coordinates the active orchestrator set. Operators bond their own tokens and may attract delegated stake. Delegators signal which operators they consider reliable and share in the operator’s reward and fee arrangements after the operator’s stated reward cut and fee share. Selection requires more than looking at an annualised figure. Commission terms, service history, active-set status, stake concentration, operational continuity and the delegator’s ability to monitor transactions all matter. Wallet Network and Smart Contract Controls Livepeer’s production mechanics use Arbitrum One, an Ethereum layer-two network. Users therefore face ordinary self-custody risks plus bridge and layer-two dependencies. A wallet must hold the correct native gas asset, display the correct token contract and sign the intended method. Token approvals should be limited where possible. Hardware-wallet confirmation, test transfers, bookmarked official interfaces and independent address checks reduce avoidable mistakes but do not eliminate contract, sequencer or wallet-software failures. A token shown with the LPT symbol is not sufficient proof of authenticity. Wrapped, bridged or counterfeit tokens may share a ticker. An exchange withdrawal screen can also choose a network that differs from the wallet’s intended route. Users should record the withdrawal network, transaction hash, destination, token contract and fees. Recovery is not guaranteed when a centralised exchange or bridge sends to an unsupported route. Indian VDA Tax TDS and Records for LPT LPT falls within the broad Indian concept of a virtual digital asset when the statutory conditions are met. The special regime generally taxes income from a transfer at thirty per cent, plus applicable surcharge and cess, and restricts deduction to the permitted cost of acquisition. A loss from one VDA transfer is not generally available for set-off against other income or another VDA gain, and the special loss cannot ordinarily be carried forward. A token-for-token exchange, sale for rupees, payment for goods or transfer to another person can be a disposal even when cash is not received. The one per cent TDS framework applies to consideration for transfer of a VDA to a resident when statutory thresholds and mechanics are satisfied. The commonly applied annual thresholds are ₹50,000 for a specified person and ₹10,000 for others. Non-cash consideration requires special attention because tax may need to be ensured before release. Staking rewards and service fees require fact-specific characterisation: receipt can create income at one stage and a later transfer can create separate VDA income. Business users must also examine GST, books, invoicing and foreign-asset disclosure consequences. Evidence File for Each Wallet and Exchange Maintain dated screenshots or exports of deposits, withdrawals, bonds, reward claims, unbonding locks, swaps and sales. Record INR values using a consistent, supportable method at the relevant time. Separate transfers between wallets beneficially owned by the same person from disposals, but retain proof of ownership and both transaction hashes. Reconcile Form 26AS or AIS TDS entries with exchange statements rather than treating either source as complete. Livepeer Specific Risks Requiring Ongoing Review Work demand can change independently of token market activity, and orchestrators may receive little or no service work. AI workloads introduce model, licensing, content, privacy and output-quality risks beyond traditional transcoding. Delegation rewards depend on protocol issuance, stake participation and operator terms; displayed yields are not guaranteed. Arbitrum, Ethereum, bridges, smart contracts, gateways and wallets add separate technical and counterparty dependencies. Protocol upgrades can alter hardware requirements, reward mechanics, interfaces or the relative role of LPT. Frequently Asked Questions Is Livepeer only a video transcoding network? No. Transcoding remains part of the network, while Livepeer 2.0 also targets real-time AI video inference, generation and analysis through community-operated GPUs. Does holding LPT automatically earn income? No. A holder must use the relevant protocol route, such as delegation, and rewards remain variable. Holding alone does not guarantee work, fees or appreciation. What does an orchestrator need besides LPT? Official guidance lists compatible GPU hardware, Linux production infrastructure, stable public connectivity, software dependencies, Arbitrum ETH for gas and sufficient stake. Can delegated LPT be sold immediately? Not while bonded. The holder must initiate unbonding, observe the protocol waiting process and complete the required transaction before the token becomes freely transferable. Are Livepeer rewards tax free in India until sold? No blanket exemption should be assumed. Receipt and later transfer can have separate tax consequences depending on facts and accounting treatment. Does moving LPT between my own wallets create tax? A genuine transfer between wallets under the same beneficial ownership may not be a disposal, but ownership and transaction records should be retained. Is an explorer forecast a guaranteed staking return? No. Forecasts depend on changing inflation, participation, stake, operator terms and fees. They are not contractual promises. Can an Indian resident use any overseas exchange for LPT? Availability does not establish Indian compliance. The user must consider platform status, FEMA, tax, KYC, custody and reporting obligations. LPT supports orchestrator selection and delegation, while work fees, protocol rewards and token market value remain separate variables. Production participation relies on Arbitrum One, correct token and wallet configuration, and operational gas funding.
