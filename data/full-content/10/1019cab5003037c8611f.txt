No 28/2026-27 October 23, 2026 To, All Authorised Dealer Category-I banks and other Authorised Persons Madam / Sir, Delegation of Compounding of certain Contraventions under FEMA, 1999 to Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank Attention of Authorised Dealer (AD) Category - I banks and other Authorised Persons is invited to i. A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 17/2024-25 dated October 1, 2024 and ii. Master Directions on Compounding of Contraventions under FEMA, 1999, dated April 22, 2025 2. As laid down in the above directions, the applications for compounding of contraventions pertaining to transactions related to Overseas Investments and Borrowing and Lending, and Guarantees are currently being received at Central Office of the Reserve Bank. The compounding of contraventions related to certain categories of transactions related to foreign direct investment were being carried out by the Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank. 3. As a measure for promoting customer service, the powers to compound contraventions related to Overseas Investments and Borrowing and Lending, and Guarantees transactions have also been delegated to the select Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank as follows: A.
