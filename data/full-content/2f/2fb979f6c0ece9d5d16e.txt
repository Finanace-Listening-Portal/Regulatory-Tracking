Yes Bank crashes 20% after SC ruling on AT-1 bond write-offHeena Ojha3 min read Last Updated : Oct 15 2026 | 3:16 PM IST @media (min-width:1100px) and (max-width:1280px) { .storymeta .meta-info { flex: 0 0 45%; } } Listen to This ArticleYes Bank’s share price came under pressure on Thursday after the Supreme Court upheld the Bombay High Court's ruling quashing the lender's Additional Tier-1 (AT1) bonds worth ₹8,415 crore. On Thursday, as of 03:06 PM, Yes Bank’s share price was trading 14.15 per cent lower at ₹18.21 per share on NSE. In comparison, Nifty 50 was up 1.45 per cent at 22,951. In intraday trade, the stock crashed 20 per cent to touch the day's low at ₹16.96 per share. A Bench of Justices Dipankar Datta and AG Masih dismissed appeals filed by Yes Bank, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Centre challenging the high court's January 2023 judgment. “We have decided on merits, and our answers to both the questions are in the negative. Therefore, for the reasons assigned in our judgment, we have held that we see no reason to interfere with the impugned judgment. The appeals are dismissed,” the Bench said, reported LiveLaw. The dispute stems from the RBI-led reconstruction plan for Yes Bank in March 2020, under which AT1 bonds worth more than ₹8,300 crore were written down. The Yes Bank Reconstruction Scheme, 2020, was framed by the RBI under the Banking Regulation Act after Yes Bank faced a severe financial crisis in March that year. In simple terms, it was the rescue plan designed to prevent the bank from collapsing.
