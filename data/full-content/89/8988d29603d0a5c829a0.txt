The yield on India's 10-year bonds jumped 5 basis points to 7.27 per cent after the decision and was up 8 bps compared to Tuesday's close of 7.19 per cent, according to data on Bloomberg. Meanwhile, the Indian rupee also declined following the RBI's policy announcement. Currently, the domestic unit was down 11 paise to 96.54 a dollar against its last close of 96.43. According to analysts, the hawkish RBI commentary has dampened the currency market sentiment. Harshal Dasani, business head at INVasset PMS said that a fully priced hike normally produces a yield that falls on the announcement, because the uncertainty is removed.
