Advt * The bonds are rated BBB by S&P and BBB- by Fitch Ratings, and the proceeds will be used to meet funding requirements at the bank's India head office and foreign branches, and for general corporate purposes.* Including this deal, Indian banks have now raised $13 billion through dollar bonds since June, when the central bank announced the swap window.* State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank and the Union Bank of India are the other state-run lenders that have tapped the dollar debt market in the first nine months of 2026.(Reporting by Dharamraj Dhutia; Editing by Harikrishnan Nair) Reuters Published On Sep 18, 2026 at 11:56 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
