Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Naresh Kumar & Company Private Limited Vs Commissioner of Service Tax (CESTAT Kolkata) Summary: The Customs, Excise and Service Tax Appellate Tribunal (CESTAT), Kolkata, in Naresh Kumar & Company Private Limited Vs Commissioner of Service Tax, Kolkata, examined whether transportation of dolomite within mining premises, involving incidental loading and unloading, was classifiable as cargo handling service under the Finance Act, 1994. The dispute arose from a work order awarded by TISCO covering trucking of dolomite boulders from mines to a crusher plant, transportation to railway sidings, haulage from stockpiles and loading into railway wagons. The Department classified three activities as cargo handling services and confirmed a service tax demand of ₹15,01,089, along with applicable interest, a penalty of ₹5,000 under Section 77 and a penalty of ₹15,01,089 under Section 78. The assessee challenged the classification, arguing that the contract predominantly involved transportation within mining areas and that loading and unloading were merely incidental activities necessary for completing the transportation work. The Tribunal examined the nature of the composite contract and held that its essential character must determine the applicable service tax classification. It observed that the existence of separate rates for different activities under a single work order did not automatically establish that each activity constituted an independent taxable service. Applying Section 65A(2)(b) of the Finance Act, 1994, the Tribunal noted that four of the five activities were principally concerned with transportation of dolomite, while loading and unloading were incidental to that principal service. Approximately 83% of the total contractual consideration related to transportation activities. Accordingly, the Tribunal concluded that transportation, rather than cargo handling, constituted the predominant character of the composite service. It relied on the coordinate Bench’s decision in Tycoons Industries Pvt. Ltd. Vs Commissioner of Service Tax, Kolkata, which concerned similar transportation activities within TISCO’s mining premises. The Tribunal also distinguished Calcutta Industrial Supply Corporation Vs Commissioner of Service Tax, Kolkata, where loading and unloading constituted the predominant contractual activities.
