Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); In re P A S Exports (GST AAR Tamil Nadu) The Tamil Nadu Authority for Advance Ruling (AAR) held that textile carry bags manufactured by P A S Exports from a blend of polyester, cotton and viscose are classifiable under HSN 4202 2990 as shopping bags and attract GST at 18%. The ruling, dated 23 October 2026, examined whether bags described by the applicant as eco-friendly recycled textile carry bags should be classified under Chapter 63, covering certain textile articles, or Chapter 42, which specifically includes shopping bags. The Authority concluded that the intended use and characteristics of the product justified classification under Chapter 42. P A S Exports, based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, manufactured textile carry bags supplied to textile shops, supermarkets, corporate customers and other businesses. The bags were intended for customers to carry purchased goods. The applicant claimed that its products were manufactured using waste cloth materials comprising polyester, cotton and viscose and sought an advance ruling on their appropriate HSN classification and applicable GST rate under Section 97(2) of the CGST Act, 2017. The applicant submitted a test report issued by the Textile Testing and Development Centre, Kappalur, Madurai. The report established that the fabric contained 46% polyester, 39.5% cotton and 14.5% viscose. During the personal hearing held on 18 August 2026, the applicant’s authorised representative explained the manufacturing process and produced a sample bag. Although the product was described as recycled, the Authority recorded the applicant’s clarification that no actual recycling was involved and that the bags were manufactured using comparatively lower-grade cotton yarn. The jurisdictional State GST authority reported that the applicant was classifying its products under Chapter 63. The AAR therefore examined Heading 6305, which covers sacks and bags of a kind used for packing goods. Referring to the HSN explanatory notes, the Authority observed that the heading concerns bags ordinarily used for packing goods for transportation, storage or sale, including bulk commodities and similar merchandise. The applicant’s bags, however, were designed for use by retail customers carrying purchases from shops and supermarkets. The Authority distinguished ordinary packing sacks from reusable shopping bags. It observed that the bags manufactured by the applicant had handles and were supplied to retail establishments for onward distribution or sale to customers. Their essential function was to carry purchased articles rather than to pack loose or bulk goods. Consequently, the Authority rejected classification under Heading 6305. Applying Rule 1 of the General Rules for the Interpretation of the Harmonised System, the AAR examined Heading 4202, which expressly covers shopping bags and other specified carrying articles. Although Chapter 42 principally concerns leather articles, the relevant headings also cover specified products manufactured from other materials. The Authority held that the textile carry bags were appropriately covered under Heading 4202 because of their nature, design and intended use. The Authority further considered the material composition and exterior characteristics of the bags. It observed that the blended textile fabric combined properties of cotton, viscose and polyester. Since the bags were made from blended fabric and did not have an outer surface covering, the Authority classified them under the residual subheading 4202 2990 — Other, rather than the specific entries covering shopping bags made of cotton or jute. On the applicable GST rate, the AAR referred to Notification No. 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate), dated 17 September 2025, which superseded the earlier goods-rate notification. Serial No. 145 of Schedule II covers goods falling under Heading 4202, subject to specified exclusions for cotton and jute shopping bags. Since the applicant’s blended-fabric shopping bags fell within the relevant entry, the Authority held that they attracted 9% CGST and 9% SGST, aggregating to 18% GST. Accordingly, the AAR answered both questions in favour of classification under HSN 4202 2990 and application of 18% GST. The ruling demonstrates that GST classification of textile carry bags depends upon the product’s actual characteristics, construction and intended use rather than its commercial description as an eco-friendly or recycled product. It also distinguishes shopping bags from sacks and packing bags for the purposes of tariff classification. No judicial precedents were cited or discussed in the supplied advance ruling. The Authority based its findings on the HSN classification framework, explanatory notes, product characteristics and applicable GST rate notifications. Statement of relevant facts having a bearing on the questions raised. The Applicant states that they are engaged in the business of manufacturing recycled textile carry bags and supply to corporate and other local/other state customers for purpose of packing. The applicant stated that their products are made out of used/waste cloth bits of polyester, cotton & viscose and requested the correct HSN classification of their product and the rate of tax for the same. Classification for their product – Eco friendly recycled textile carry bags and HSN thereof; 2. Rate of tax for the Eco friendly recycled textile carry bags. 4. Applicant’s interpretation of law: The Applicant has stated that their product, eco friendly textile carry bags are made out of waste cloth bits of polyester, cotton & viscose; that they procure yarn from various vendors and then send it to the job workers to convert to fabric. The fabric is further stitched and converted to textile carry bags that are sold to textile chain of stores, for packing goods. They also stated that their product was eco friendly. 5. Discussions and Findings: 7.1 We have carefully examined the submissions made by the applicant in their advance ruling application and the submissions made during the personal hearing. We have also considered the issue involved, the relevant facts and the applicant’s submission / interpretation of law in respect of question on which the advance ruling is sought. 7.2 We find that the query is liable for admission as it gets covered under Section 97(2)(a) and Section 97(2)(b) of CGST/TNGST Act, 2017, under, (a) classification of any goods or services or both; (b) applicability of a notification issued under the provisions of the CGST/TNGST Act, respectively. 7.3 We find that the applicant, M/s PAS EXPORTS, are engaged in the business of manufacturing textile carry bags and they supply the same to corporate and other local/other state customers for the purpose of packing of goods and the customers are largely textile outlets and super markets. We see that their product- carry bags are made out of a blend of cotton/polyester/viscose fabric. They have addressed the Advance Ruling forum to give the correct HSN classification of their product and applicable rate of tax for the same. We find that they have submitted a test report issued by Textile Testing and Development Centre, Kappalur, Madurai, which is a division of Madurai Spinner Association Trust, a private organisation. It has been reported that the fabric sample is a blend of polyester (46%), cotton (39.5%) and viscose(14.5%). Even though the applicant has termed their product to be ‘recycled textile carry bag’, the applicant confirmed during the personal hearing that factually, there was no ‘recycling’ and as such, their product was made out of a slightly lower grade cotton yarn. We now proceed to analyse the facts on hand with the provisions of law and answer the query of the applicant. 7.4 Question (1) Classification of the product – Eco friendly Textile carry bags and HSN thereof: 63.05 – Sacks and bags, of a kind used for the packing of goods (+). 6305.10 – Of jute or of other textile bast fibres of heading 53.03 6305.20 – Of cotton \- Of man-made textile materials: 6305.32 – – Flexible intermediate bulk containers 6305.33 – – Other, of polyethylene or polypropylene strip or the like 6305.39 – – Other 6305.90 – Of other textile materials This heading covers textile sacks and bags of a kind normally used for the packing of goods for transport, storage or sale. These articles, which vary in size and shape, include in particular flexible intermediate bulk containers, coal, grain, flour, potato, coffee or similar sacks, mail bags, and small bags of the kind used for sending samples of merchandise by post. The heading also includes such articles as tea sachets. It is seen that the jurisdictional State authority, has reported that the applicant is, at present filing returns by classifying their product under Chapter 63. Chapter heading 6305 covers Sacks and bags, of a kind used for the packing of goods. On a plain reading of the above explanatory notes to Chapter 6305 , it is seen that the bags mentioned under this Chapter is evidently for packing of goods for transport, storage or sale and does not mean the carry bags, in question. The bags under this chapter are intended for transport of goods that are loose/bulk/ free flowing and requires to be packed in bags. However, in the instant case, as stated by the applicant, the impugned goods are carry bags, meant to carry goods/textiles etc. by customers, who purchase such from the textile stores or super markets that they visit and therefore we find that the CH 6305 is not the appropriate classification for the impugned goods. We shall now proceed to determine the correct HSN for the impugned product. We find from the facts presented to us that, the impugned product- ‘Textile carry bag’ with handles, are nothing but ‘shopping bags’ that are sold to the applicant’s clients, who are basically textile chain stores, super market outlets and other corporates, who in turn, sell or supply these bags to their customers to carry the goods so purchased from their textile showrooms/shops/super markets etc. As such, these shopping bags, are also reusable. It is further seen from the facts presented by the applicant that, the impugned product- textile carry bag is made from a blend of various fabrics, viz. cotton, viscose and polyester. This blend ensures soft, breathable structure, is eco-friendly and eases cleaning from the cotton content, gets the smooth, silk-like finish and lightweight flexibility from viscose and increases the strength, durability and water resistance nature of the fabric, due to the polyester content. As per Rule 1 of the General Rules for The interpretation of the Harmonized System, the titles of Sections, Chapters and sub-Chapters are provided for ease of reference only and for legal purposes, classification shall be determined according to the terms of the headings and any relative Section or Chapter Notes. Accordingly, we find that, though the title of Chapter 42 of HSN is ‘Articles of leather; saddlery and harness, travel goods, handbags and similar containers; articles of animal gut (other than silkworm gut)’, as per the general notes to this Chapter, though Chapter 42 principally covers articles of leather or composition leather, headings 4201 and 4202 also includes certain articles characteristically of the leather trade, but made from other materials. 42 GENERAL This Chapter principally covers articles of leather or composition leather; however, headings 42.01 and 42.02 also include certain articles characteristically of the leather trade but made from other materials. It further covers certain articles of gut, goldbeater’s skin, bladders or tendons. Accordingly, we find that the impugned goods viz. ‘Shopping bags/Carry bags’, can be appropriately fixed under the specific heading for Shopping bags under CSH 4202 (extracted below). 4202 — TRUNKS, SUIT-CASES, VANITY-CASES, EXECUTIVE-CASES, BRIEF-CASES, SCHOOL SATCHELS, SPECTACLE CASES, BINOCULAR CASES, CAMERA CASES, MUSICAL INSTRUMENT CASES, GUN CASES, HOLSTERS AND SIMILAR CONTAINERS; TRAVELLING-BAGS, INSULATED FOOD OR BEVERAGES BAGS, TOILET BAGS, RUCKSACKS, HANDBAGS, SHOPPING-BAGS, WALLETS, PURSES, MAP-CASES, CIGARETTE-CASES, TOBACCO-POUCHES, TOOL BAGS, SPORTS BAGS, BOTTLE CASES, JEWELLERY BOXES, POWDER-BOXES, CUTLERY CASES AND SIMILAR CONTAINERS, OF LEATHER OR OF COMPOSITION LEATHER, OF SHEETING OF PLASTICS, OF TEXTILE MATERIALS, OF VULCANISED FIBRE OR OF PAPERBOARD, OR WHOLLY OR MAINLY COVERED WITH SUCH MATERIALS OR WITH PAPER. Further examination of CH 4202, reveals that sub headings 4202 21, 4202 22 and 4202 29 cover bags, irrespective of the presence of shoulder strap/handle and includes shopping bags, hand bags, vanity bags. It is also seen that the bags are differentiated by the material they are made of and the outer surface covering. The impugned goods- ‘Shopping bags/Carry bags’ are made out of blended fabric and as such, do not have any outer surface covering and hence can be appropriately placed under “CSH 4202 2990 — Other” (extracted under) HSN Description Unit Hand-bags, whether or not with shoulder strap, including those without handle: 4202 21 With outer surface of leather or of composition leather: 4202 21 10 Hand-bags for ladies NOS 4202 21 20 Vanity-bags NOS 4202 21 90 Other NOS 4202 22 With outer surface of sheeting of plastics or of textile materials: 4202 22 10 Hand-bags and shopping bags, of artificial plastic material NOS 4202 22 20 Hand-bags and shopping bags, of cotton NOS 4202 22 30 Hand-bags and shopping bags, of jute NOS 4202 22 40 Vanity-bags NOS 4202 22 90 Other NOS 4202 29 Other: 4202 29 10 Hand bags of other materials excluding wicker-work or basket work NOS 4202 29 90 Other NOS Accordingly, we classify the impugned product — Textile carry bag’ under CSH ‘4202 2990- Other’ . 7.5 Question (2) Rate of tax for the product – Textile carry bags: Based on the recommendations of the GST Council, Notification 9/2025- CT (R) dated 17.09.2025, was issued in supersession of the Rate notification 1/2017- CT(R), which notifies the rate of central tax on goods. Similarly, Notification II(2)/CTR/1016(b-1)/2025 vide G.O. (Ms) No.132 dated 17.09.2025, was issued by the State Government of Tamil Nadu, notifying the state tax on goods, under seven schedules, specifying CGST/SGST rates of 2.5%, 9%, 20%, 1.5%, 0.125%, 0.75% and 14%, respectively. It is seen that S. No. 145 of Schedule II, covers within its scope, all goods under CSH 4202, except hand bags and shopping bags made of cotton or jute (extracted below). Sl. No. Chapter/Heading Description of goods 145 4202 Trunks, suit cases, vanity cases, executive cases, brief cases, school satchels, spectacle cases, binocular cases, camera cases, musical instrument cases, gun cases, holsters and similar containers; travelling bags, insulated food or beverages bags, toilet bags, rucksacks, handbags, shopping bags, wallets, purses, map-cases, cigarette cases, tobacco pouches, tool bags, sports bags, bottle cases, jewellery boxes, powder boxes, cutlery cases and similar containers, of leather, of sheeting of plastics, of textile materials, of vulcanised fibre or of paperboard, or wholly or mainly covered with such materials or with paper [other than handbags and shopping bags, of cotton or jute] The impugned product —‘Textile carry Bag’ classified under Chapter heading 4202 2990, is made from a fabric which is a blend of cotton, viscose and polyester, hence squarely covered by the entry at 145 of Schedule II of the above notification, attracting 9% CGST and 9% SGST. Accordingly we hold that the impugned goods, ‘Textile Carry bags’ classified under CSH 4202 2990, are subject to GST @ 18%, as per S. No. 145 of Schedule II of the notification 9/2025- CT (R) dated 17.09.2025. 8. In view of the above discussions, we rule as under: RULING (1) Classification of the product – Textile carry bags and HSN thereof : Ans. The applicant’s product — ‘Textile carry bags’ is classified under ‘4202 2990 – Other’. (2) Rate of tax for the product – Textile carry bags: Ans. The applicant’s product — Textile carry bags, classified under 4202 2990 attracts 18% GST, under S.No.145 of Schedule II of Notification 9/2025- CT (R) dated 17.09.2025.
