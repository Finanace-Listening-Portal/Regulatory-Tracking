The renewal follows the application made by NSE Clearing Limited under Regulation 12 of the 2018 Regulations. SEBI, having considered the application and being satisfied that the renewal would be in the interest of the trade, the securities market and the public interest, has granted renewal of recognition to the Clearing Corporation. The renewal is granted in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 read with sub-section (4) of section 8A of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956. The renewed recognition is for a period of three years, commencing on 3 October 2026 and ending on 2 October 2029. The recognition is subject to the conditions stated in the notification and any further conditions that may be prescribed or imposed. One specific condition requires the Clearing Corporation to comply with conditions specified by SEBI from time to time. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA NOTIFICATION Mumbai, the 14th September, 2026 No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/316.—Securities and Exchange Board of India, having considered the application for renewal of recognition made under Regulation 12 of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) (Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations) Regulations, 2018 by NSE Clearing Limited, Exchange Plaza, C-1, Block-G, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai – 400051 and being satisfied that it would be in the interest of the trade, in the interest of securities market and also in the public interest so to do, hereby grants, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 read with sub-section (4) of section 8A of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, renewal of recognition to the said Clearing Corporation for three years commencing on the 3rd day of October, 2026 and ending on the 2nd day of October, 2029, subject to the conditions stated herein below or as may be prescribed or imposed hereafter: The Clearing Corporation shall comply with conditions specified by SEBI from time to time. K.V.R.
