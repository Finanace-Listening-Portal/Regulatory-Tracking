Articles 1 min read CCI approves Kotak Mahindra Bank's acquisition of Deutsche Bank's India retail banking business Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited has received approval from the Competition Commission of India for its acquisition. This acquisition involves Deutsche Bank AG's retail, private banking, and wealth management businesses in India. The deal includes significant assets like loans and deposits, benefiting around 150,000 customers. Additionally, a referral arrangement will be established between both banks through their affiliates. Advt "This transaction aligns well with our focus on the affluent and SME segments," Ashok Vaswani, Managing Director and CEO, Kotak Mahindra Bank had said."It is a strong strategic fit and makes sound commercial sense. It also brings a high-quality customer franchise and experienced teams and adds incremental scale and adjacency opportunities. We look forward to warmly welcoming these customers and colleagues to the Kotak family, and our priority will be on disciplined integration and ensuring continuiy, while building further depth and capability in this business," he added as the lender announced the deal in June.Amid the transition, approximately 1,000 Deutsche Bank employees in India are expected to join Kotak, underscoring the importance both banks place on continuity for customers and long-term opportunities for employees.
