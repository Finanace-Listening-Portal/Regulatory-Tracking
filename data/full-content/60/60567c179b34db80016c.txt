Goods and Services Tax Government of India, States and Union Territories Advisory: Introduction of Metro Rail as an Additional Mode of Transport in the E-Way Bill System Nov 20th, 2026 In accordance with Notification No. 05/2026–Central Tax dated 26.10.2026, effective from 01.11.2026, Metro Rail has been included under Rule 138(2A) of the CGST Rules, 2017, for the transportation of goods. Accordingly, Metro Rail has been introduced as an additional mode of transport in the E-Way Bill system. Taxpayers and transporters can now select Metro Rail as the mode of transportation while generating E-Way Bills or updating Part-B details. 1. Availability of the Facility The new transport mode has been enabled for the following functionalities through Web, Excel and API interfaces: 1. E-Way Bill Generation 2. Part-B Update 3. Vehicle Update 4. Consolidated E-Way Bill Generation 5. Consolidated E-Way Bill Re-generation Current Deployment Status: Web Users: The facility is currently available in the Production environment. API Users: The functionality has been released in the Sandbox environment to facilitate integration and testing. API users are advised to undertake the necessary changes and complete testing before its rollout in Production. 2. Important Instructions a) Taxpayers and transporters transporting goods through Metro Rail are required to select Metro Rail as the mode of transportation. b) Entry of the Transport Document Number is mandatory when Metro Rail is selected. c) The correct Transport Document Number and other relevant transportation details must be furnished before submission. 3. Action Required Taxpayers and transporters are advised to select Metro Rail as the appropriate mode of transport while generating E-Way Bills or updating Part-B details for goods transported through Metro Rail. API users are advised to make the necessary changes in their systems and validate the functionality in the Sandbox environment. The availability of the facility in the API Production environment will be communicated separately. All stakeholders are requested to take note of the above changes.
