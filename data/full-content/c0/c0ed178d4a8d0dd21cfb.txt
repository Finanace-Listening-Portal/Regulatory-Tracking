} } Listen to This ArticleCanadian billionaire Prem Watsa’s Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd is set to acquire a controlling stake in IDBI Bank Ltd for about ₹53,000 crore ($5.5 billion), the largest foreign investment in an Indian bank, according to Bloomberg. The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved the proposed acquisition, which will give Fairfax a 60.72 per cent stake in IDBI Bank, with the government and Life Insurance Corp. together set to retain a 34 per cent stake. The deal marks the culmination of years of efforts by the central government to sell its controlling stake in the bank. Fairfax joins a wave of foreign firms betting on India, attracted by its expanding wealthy class and status as the fastest-growing major economy. Who is Prem Watsa? Hyderabad-born Prem Watsa left India for Canada in 1972. More than five decades later, the 76-year-old billionaire is at the helm of Fairfax Financial Holdings, a Toronto-based insurance and investment group with assets of about $100 billion. His latest ₹53,000-crore acquisition of a stake in IDBI Bank marks his biggest bet on India yet. An IIT Madras chemical engineer, Watsa paid for his MBA at Western Ontario by selling air conditioners and furnaces door to door.
