The company reported a 126% increase in Profit After Tax (PAT), reaching Rs. 53 crore, compared to the same quarter last year. This marks the fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit growth for the company. During the second quarter of the fiscal year 2027, ABLBL’s revenue rose by 11% year-on-year to Rs. 2,266 crore. The Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortisation (EBITDA) increased by 15%, amounting to Rs. 390 crore, with an EBITDA margin expansion of 60 basis points to 17.2%. The company’s Earnings Before Interest and Taxes (EBIT) saw a 29% rise, while Profit Before Tax (PBT) surged by 127% to Rs. 70 crore, reflecting the company’s robust operational performance. The company’s lifestyle brands, including Louis Philippe, Van Heusen, Allen Solly, and Peter England, contributed significantly to this growth, with revenue increasing by 9% to Rs. 1,906 crore. The emerging business segment, which includes brands like American Eagle, Reebok, and Van Heusen Innerwear, reported an impressive 27% revenue growth. This segment’s EBITDA margin expanded by 530 basis points to 6.8%, driven by strategic retail expansion and enhanced operational efficiencies. ABLBL continued to expand its retail footprint, adding over 100 new stores during the quarter, the highest in over two years. The total store count now stands at 3,434, covering approximately 5.1 million square feet. The company also reported a reduction in net debt to Rs. 966 crore, down from Rs. 993 crore in the same quarter last year, indicating improved financial health. Analysts view ABLBL’s consistent growth trajectory and margin expansion as a testament to its strong brand portfolio and strategic execution. The company’s focus on product innovation, retail expansion, and digital initiatives positions it well for sustained growth in the competitive Indian apparel market. As ABLBL continues to strengthen its market presence, investors and stakeholders remain optimistic about its future prospects.
