Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transactions.The Nifty Bank surged 5.90 per cent over the five trading sessions to close at 64,635.20 on Friday, November 20, after touching an intraday record high of 64,680.80. The banking index outperformed the benchmark Nifty 50, which gained 2.61 per cent during the same period. Advt The banking index scaled fresh intraday record highs on three trading sessions, November 17, 19 and 20, after also recording a fresh closing high on November 16. Nifty Bank hits fresh record high above 63,000; IDFC First Bank, Bank of Baroda, PNB lead gainsThe Nifty Bank index surged to an all-time high on Thursday, November 19, crossing the 63,000 mark for the first time. IDFC First Bank and Bank of Baroda led the percentage gains, while index heavyweights HDFC Bank and State Bank of India (SBI) contributed the most to the upward momentum.Third largest public sector lender Punjab National Bank (PNB) led banking stocks with an 8.77 per cent weekly gain, followed by Axis Bank at 8.64 per cent and State Bank of India (SBI) at 7.88 per cent.Public sector banks extend gainsPublic sector banks maintained their upward momentum during the week, with PNB emerging as the top performer among the lenders tracked.PNB gained 8.77 per cent over the week to close at Rs 155.33 on Friday. SBI followed with a 7.88 per cent gain to end at Rs 1,165.10, while Bank of Baroda rose 4.81 per cent to close at Rs 287.Private sector banks record broad-based gainsPrivate sector lenders also posted strong weekly gains, with all the major banking stocks tracked outperforming the benchmark Nifty 50.Axis Bank led private sector peers, surging 8.64 per cent during the week to close at Rs 1,446. The stock gained 4.72 per cent in Friday's session alone. Advt Kotak Mahindra Bank rose 5.58 per cent over the week to close at Rs 515.80, while HDFC Bank gained 5.22 per cent to end at Rs 846. ICICI Bank also finished in positive territory, rising 3.56 per cent to close at Rs 1,640.Insurance stocks largely end higherInsurance stocks largely ended the week in positive territory, led by state-run Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).LIC gained 2.85 per cent over the week to close at Rs 410.70 on Friday, followed by ICICI Lombard General Insurance, which rose 2.65 per cent to Rs 1,736. SBI Life Insurance gained 2.04 per cent during the week to end at Rs 1,826.50. Advt HDFC Life Insurance was the exception, declining 0.61 per cent over the week to close at Rs 588.65, despite gaining 2.01 per cent in Friday's session.NBFCs record mixed performanceNon-banking financial companies (NBFCs) recorded a mixed performance during the week, with modest gains in vehicle and diversified financiers contrasting with declines in other counters.Cholamandalam Investment and Finance led the NBFC stocks tracked, rising 1.33 per cent over the week to close at Rs 1,874.60. Bajaj Finance gained 0.45 per cent to end at Rs 1,122, while Shriram Finance edged up 0.21 per cent to close at Rs 972. Advt On the other hand, Tata Capital declined 2.32 per cent over the week to settle at Rs 345.25. Gold-loan financier Muthoot Finance was the worst performer in the segment, falling 10.19 per cent to close at Rs 2,550.50.Fintech stocks slide on UPI MDR uncertaintyFintech stocks witnessed a sharp sell-off during the week following media reports that the rollout of the proposed merchant discount rate (MDR) framework on select UPI transactions could face a further delay.The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) had initially scheduled the revised MDR framework to take effect from October 15, 2026. However, the implementation was deferred until after Diwali following opposition from merchants, with a fresh date yet to be announced.Under the proposed framework, a 0.4 per cent MDR would apply to eligible large-merchant person-to-merchant (P2M) UPI transactions above Rs 2,000, subject to a cap of Rs 300. Small-merchant payments and person-to-person transfers would remain outside the proposed charges.One 97 Communications, the parent company of Paytm, tumbled 10.30 per cent over the week to close at Rs 1,740, despite recovering 0.82 per cent in Friday's session.PB Fintech declined 7.44 per cent during the week to close at Rs 1,022.80, while One MobiKwik Systems fell 7.17 per cent to settle at Rs 211.82. OnEMI Technology Solutions also ended lower, declining 3.81 per cent to close at Rs 337.80.
