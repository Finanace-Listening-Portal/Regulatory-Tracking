Articles 2 min read IDBI Bank strike on November 2 and 3, 2026: Bank advises customers to use digital channels; removes fee on non-IDBI ATM withdrawals IDBI Bank strike today: IDBI Bank employees initiated a two-day strike on November 2 and 3, 2026, opposing the sale of the bank's majority ownership. The proposed transaction involves transferring IDBI Bank's stake to Canadian investor Fairfax Financial Holdings. IDBI Bank has encouraged customers to utilise digital services and complete essential transactions before the strike. The United Forum of IDBI Officers & Employees mentioned intimidation from bank management against striking employees. These bank employees are opposing the government’s decision to sell IDBI Bank’s own and the Life Insurance Corporation of India’s (LIC) 60% stake in the bank to Canadian investor Fairfax Financial Holdings.Even as the two-day strike started, IDBI has issued a customer advisory, suggesting them to use digital channels.In a social media post on X, IDBI has said that due to the proposed strike on November 2–3, 2026, digital channels (Go Mobile+, net banking, UPI), Automated Teller Machines (ATMs), and Customer Service Points (CSPs) will remain fully operational. Advt The bank also informed that non-IDBI ATM cash withdrawals will be free of charge.— IDBI_Bank (@IDBI_Bank)What did IDBI Bank say in its social media message?In the message available on its X account, IDBI said: “IDBI Bank has received a notice of strike by the association/union on November 2 and 3, 2026. The bank will endeavour to keep all banking transactions normal during the proposed days of the bank strike.” IDBI further said that as a measure of abundant caution, we appeal to our esteemed customers to complete their important banking transactions in advance of the strike dates.IDBI Bank has given six key suggestions to its customers for the two-day strike.·Complete important banking transactions in advance of the strike dates·Use ATM/Bunch Note Acceptor (BNAS) for their cash requirements·Utilise Customer Service Points (CSPs), wherever applicable, and·Use internet banking, Go Mobile+, UPI and other digital banking channels for their banking requirements, which will be seamlessly available·Use banking correspondent services/Sameep Outlets to meet customer banking needs·Customer can withdraw cash from other banks’ ATMs, in case of need, free of charge during the aforesaid two days Advt What IDBI employees said in their letter about strikeThe United Forum of IDBI Officers & Employees, in a letter dated October 31, 2026, written to S Kamble, Conciliation Officer under the ID Act 1947 and RLC (C), Mumbai, said that IDBI Bank’s regional heads (RHs), zonal heads (ZHs) and executive directors (EDs) are threatening employees with punitive action for participating in the proposed strike on November 2 and 3, 2026."Such intimidation and coercion are unacceptable. Members have recorded these calls as evidence. We request your immediate intervention and directions to the bank management to stop victimisation and respect employees’ legitimate trade union rights," says the letter, also available on the association’s official X account.
