The company has unveiled its first flagship store in São Paulo, marking a pivotal step in its strategy to strengthen its presence in one of the world’s leading motorcycle markets. This development is part of TVS Motor’s broader plan to enhance production capacity, expand its retail network, and reinforce after-sales support across Brazil. The new flagship store, located at Avenida dos Bandeirantes in Vila Olímpia, São Paulo, is a testament to TVS Motor’s commitment to the Brazilian market. The store is designed to bring the brand closer to consumers by offering a comprehensive brand experience that combines product displays, technology, and customer service in a single environment. The expansion plan includes increasing the annual production capacity from 110,000 units to 130,000 units by 2027, supported by a partnership with Mottu. This collaboration will enable TVS Motor to access over 500 points of sale and 168 service locations across Brazil, ensuring robust after-sales support. Peyman Kargar, President of International Business at TVS Motor Company, highlighted the strategic importance of Brazil in the company’s international growth ambitions. He emphasized that the partnership with Mottu and the opening of the flagship store are crucial milestones in building a sustainable business in Brazil. The company aims to deliver innovative, high-quality products that resonate with local consumers, supported by a strong service ecosystem. TVS Motor’s expansion in Brazil aligns with its international strategy to consolidate its presence in Latin America. With a diverse portfolio catering to different consumer profiles, the company is poised to offer Brazilian riders a range of mobility solutions that combine technology, design, and performance. This move not only strengthens TVS Motor’s commercial strategy but also reaffirms its long-term commitment to the Brazilian market. As the company continues to expand its footprint, analysts will be closely watching its impact on the competitive landscape of Brazil’s dynamic two-wheeler market.
