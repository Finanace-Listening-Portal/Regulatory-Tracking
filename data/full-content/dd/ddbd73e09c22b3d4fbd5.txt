While the bank has reiterated that the change will not affect its FY27 guidance or near-term strategic priorities, the eventual successor will be closely watched for continuity in executing the roadmap.Nautiyal, who joined Ujjivan SFB in July 2024, played a key role in accelerating the bank’s diversification, with the secured share of the loan book rising from around 31% in June 2024 to around 50% in June 2026. The bank’s FY30 roadmap also envisages CASA of 35%, a deposit base three times the Q1FY26 level, a branch network of 1,150 and a reduction in the cost-to-income ratio to 55% and credit-to-assets ratio to below 5%, according to Axis Securities. Advt The bank is targeting a further increase in secured loans to 65-70% of the portfolio by FY30 as it moves towards a more diversified asset mix. The strategy also includes building the loan book to Rs 1 lakh crore, strengthening the liability franchise and expanding its digital and physical distribution network.The leadership change comes after Nautiyal sought early retirement citing health concerns. He ceased to hold the positions of MD & CEO and key managerial personnel with effect from September 1 and will remain on leave during his three-month notice period ending November 30. The bank has maintained that his early retirement is unrelated to its strategy, governance or operations.The interim CEOCarol Furtado, the bank’s existing executive director, has been appointed interim MD & CEO for three months, with the Reserve Bank of India approving the appointment. Furtado has more than three decades of banking experience across business, strategy, operations, people and institutional functions. She had also taken charge as Officer on Special Duty and subsequently as interim CEO during an earlier leadership transition at Ujjivan in 2021.Ujjivan is expected to begin the succession process ahead of the original November timeline and submit a list of shortlisted candidates to the RBI over the next three to four months. Both internal and external candidates will be considered, with Furtado remaining a potential internal candidate for the permanent MD & CEO position. Advt Despite the leadership change, the bank has maintained its FY27 guidance and indicated that its broader diversification strategy remains on track. The MSME business is tracking in line with FY27 guidance, while asset-quality outcomes have remained better than or broadly in line with Q1 performance, with no material deviations currently visible.The bank has also appointed Vikas Agarwal as head of its MSME business as it seeks to strengthen its leadership bandwidth. Agarwal has more than 25 years of experience across MSME, business banking, retail banking, credit, product and distribution.Ujjivan has remained non-committal on the timeline for reapplying for a universal bank licence, choosing instead to focus on its broader diversification strategy. The immediate priority is therefore execution of the existing business plan, including the shift towards secured lending, strengthening the deposit franchise and expanding MSME operations. Advt The key uncertainty for investors will be whether the eventual successor maintains the strategic direction established under Nautiyal. While Furtado’s interim appointment provides continuity, the succession process will determine how closely the bank’s existing roadmap is maintained under its next regular MD & CEO.For now, the bank has reiterated that the leadership transition will not alter its FY27 operating trajectory, while its earnings estimates remain unchanged.
