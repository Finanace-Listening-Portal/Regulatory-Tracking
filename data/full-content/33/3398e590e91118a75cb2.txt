It stood at 476 billion rupees as of Friday.Banks are required to hold a CRR of 3% of the banks' overall deposit base, with the regulator mandating a minimum daily maintenance of this quantum. Advt Typically, this is at 90% under normal conditions and is raised when there is a large liquidity surplus to prevent exuberant lending or a pass-through to inflation.The Indian central bank raised rates for the first time in October and tightened liquidity through a series of tools, including bond sales and shorter-term variable-rate reverse repo auctions.Sharply tighter liquidity, partly due to weaker government spending, has now prompted the RBI to cut the reserve ratio maintenance back to 90%.Banks had suggested that the central bank reduce the daily reserve needs as the money was blocked and most lenders were forced to borrow at 5.75% - above the policy repo rate of 5.5%, a treasury head at a private bank said, declining to be identified as he is not authorised to speak to the media."Restoring the CRR limits helps banks manage short-term cash better in systemic deficit conditions.
