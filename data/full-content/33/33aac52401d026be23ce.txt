NOTICE NoticeNo. 20260504-17 NoticeDate 04May2026 Category CircularsListedCompanies Segment General Department ListingCompliance-Monitoring Subject XBRLbasedfilingforViolationsrelatedtoCodeofConduct(CoC) underSEBIPITRegulations,2015 Attachments NoAttachment To, Compliance Officers, All Listed Entities/Intermediaries/Fiduciaries Subject: XBRL based filing for Violations related to Code of Conduct (CoC) under SEBI PIT Regulations, 2015 SEBI, vide Circular No. SEBI/HO/ISD/ISD/CIR/P/2020/135 dated July 23, 2020, prescribed the format for reporting violations of the Code of Conduct under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations 2015 (SEBI PIT Regulations 2015). To enhance accuracy and efficiency in disclosures, the Exchange has introduced an XBRL utility for reporting such violations through the Listing Centre, with effect from May 05, 2026. Listed entities/intermediaries/fiduciaries shall submit the reporting through the following paths: • To download XBRL Utility: Listing Centre > Listing Compliance Module > XBRL > Download XBRL Utility • For submission: Listing Centre > Listing Compliance Module > XBRL > E-filing > Violation related to Code of Conduct (CoC) It may be noted that reporting shall be made only in XBRL mode, without submission of any PDF file. Further, any unlisted entity reporting in the capacity of Intermediary or Fiduciary shall continue to submit the disclosures through email at corp.relations@bseindia.com, as per Exchange Circular No. 20200724-10 dated 24 July 2020. Accordingly, the existing filing path i.e. Compliance Module > Non quarterly submissions > Reporting of Code of Conduct violations SEBI PIT Regulations 2015; as specified under Exchange Circular No. 20201223-8 dated December 23, 2020, shall be discontinued. The disclosures pertaining to violations of the Code of Conduct shall not be disseminated on the Exchange website. All listed entities are advised to mandatorily submit the reporting through the Listing Centre only. Submission through any other mode(s) shall not be taken on record and shall be considered as invalid submission. Listed entities/intermediaries/fiduciaries are advised to take note of the above and comply accordingly.
