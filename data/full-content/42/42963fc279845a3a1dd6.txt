The instruction refers to Customs Instruction No. 04/2026-Customs dated 20 April 2026 concerning authorised officers under Section 25 read with Section 47(5) of the Food Safety Standards Act, 2006 and Regulation 13(1) of the Food Safety Standards (Import) Regulations, 2017. It also refers to the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India notification dated 15 September 2026. Following a review of the Points of Entry notified for food imports, FSSAI has added ICD Dhanakya, Jaipur, Rajasthan, bearing port code INDNK6, to the existing 171 Points of Entry. The authorised officers at this Inland Container Depot and Special Economic Zone entry point are the Superintendent, Appraiser, Inspector or Examiner. Consequently, the total number of specified Points of Entry for food imports has increased to 172. An updated consolidated list specifying the port name, port code, State or Union Territory, authorised officer and port type has been provided in Annexure-A. Customs formations have been requested to sensitise officers under their jurisdiction regarding the change. Instruction No. 04/2026-Customs dated 20 April 2026 stands modified to this extent. Any difficulties arising in implementing the instruction may be brought to the notice of the Board.
