Final directions have come but there is a working group which is working with RBI to fine tune,” Setty said during the bank's Q2 FY27 press conference. Advt He said SBI has already moved to its ECL models and the models are in place. The estimated impact, after taking into account taxes, is Rs 19,500 crore.However, Setty said the final ECL framework is not entirely a closed matter from an implementation perspective, with the RBI working group continuing discussions around the nuances of the framework.Setty said that ECL is a complex mechanism and its implementation at such a large scale is a new exercise for the industry. While the final directions have already been issued, RBI remains open to discussions around the finer aspects of both the guidelines and their implementation.“The regulator definitely would like to have as much feedback as possible. And they are still open to discuss some of the nuances of both the guidelines as well as implementation,” Setty said.According to Setty, RBI is particularly looking at the technology preparedness of banks, how the models are working and the fine-tuning of these models as lenders prepare for the new provisioning framework.The central bank is also planning a CEO-level meeting with banks to review the industry's preparedness for ECL implementation, Setty said.“RBI is planning to have a CEO level meeting to review the preparedness of the industry in terms of implementation which shows that this is a lot of serious work is going on on the ECL side,” he said. Advt Four-year transitionOn the Rs 19,500 crore impact, Setty clarified that SBI has the option of either taking the impact upfront or using the transition mechanism.“As we speak we intend to take the transition timeline,” he said, referring to the four-year transition period.The approach means SBI does not currently plan to absorb the entire estimated ECL impact in one go, instead spreading the impact through the transition period.
