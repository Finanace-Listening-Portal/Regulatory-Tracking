Articles 2 min read Credit growth continues to outpace deposit mobilisation in August as loans rise 20%, deposits 18% Outstanding loans rose 20 per cent year-on-year (YoY) to Rs 223.9 lakh crore in August 2026, while deposits increased 18 per cent to Rs 278.7 lakh crore. Non-cash retail transactions also maintained strong momentum, rising 17 per cent annually and around 1.6 per cent sequentially. Advt Outstanding loans reached Rs 223.9 lakh crore in August from Rs 219.3 lakh crore in June and Rs 220.8 lakh crore in July. Deposits also strengthened to Rs 278.7 lakh crore, from Rs 265.4 lakh crore in June and Rs 269.4 lakh crore in July.Digital payments maintain double-digit growthNon-cash retail transactions by value increased 17 per cent YoY to around Rs 94.3 lakh crore in August 2026 and rose about 1.6 per cent month-on-month, maintaining the growth momentum seen in the previous months.The sequential increase was supported by higher-value transactions through the Unified Payments Interface (UPI), with transaction value rising to Rs 29.8 lakh crore in August from Rs 28.9 lakh crore in July.The National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) system remained the largest component by value, although transactions moderated marginally to Rs 44.1 lakh crore in August from Rs 44.5 lakh crore in July.Cheque Truncation System (CTS) transactions declined to Rs 5.8 lakh crore from Rs 6.1 lakh crore in July. Credit card transaction value remained broadly stable at around Rs 2 lakh crore, while debit card transactions stood at around Rs 0.4 lakh crore. The 'Others' category, comprising IMPS, mobile wallets and NACH, increased to Rs 12.6 lakh crore in August from Rs 11.3 lakh crore in July.
