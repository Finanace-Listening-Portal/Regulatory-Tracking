The bank received alerts on October 16 from its threat intelligence service provider, the Defence Cyber Agency and the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In), regarding the suspected exposure of customer data on the dark web. A preliminary analysis of around 375,000 records available on the dark web identified 3,897 customer records containing personal information.
