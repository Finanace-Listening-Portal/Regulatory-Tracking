Articles 1 min read Ashok Leyland ties up with Kerala Grameena Bank to offer loans for commercial vehicles Ashok Leyland has partnered with Kerala Grameena Bank to offer loans. This collaboration aims to enhance customer convenience and financial flexibility for buyers. The partnership will provide tailored repayment options to meet individual needs. Businesses and fleet operators can now invest with greater confidence. This initiative supports the growth of commercial vehicle businesses in Kerala. Advt Vimala Vijayabhaskar, Chairperson, Kerala Grameena Bank, said the partnership reflects the bank's dedication to serving the diverse financial needs of commercial vehicle customers."We are confident that this collaboration will enable us to extend our reach and provide tailored financing options to support the growth of businesses in the commercial vehicle segment in the state of Kerala," Vijayabhaskar said.
