ETBFSI Research ETBFSI Published On Oct 20, 2026 at 05:41 AM IST var news_msid = 135059050; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Axis Bank’s loan growth is projected to reach around 20% in FY27, while the forecast for FY28 remains unchanged at about 15%, as the underlying retail lending business has yet to show a meaningful pickup. Loans grew 9% sequentially and 23% year on year in Q2FY27, supported by foreign currency non resident (FCNR) loans and growth in small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and wholesale lending. Retail loan growth, excluding FCNR, remained muted at 3% sequentially despite retail disbursements sustaining 22% year on year growth.The bank’s net interest margin (NIM) are expected to improve over the next two quarters as the drag from FCNR lending eases and liquidity is deployed more efficiently, according to an ICICI Securities report. Overall NIM declined 10 basis points sequentially to 3.36% in Q2FY27, while NIM excluding FCNR remained stable at 3.47%. The brokerage raised its FY27 loan growth estimate to around 20% but retained its FY28 forecast at around 15%, pending clearer signs of a recovery in retail lending. Advt Deposits grew 6% sequentially and 21% year on year, aided by FCNR mobilisation of around Rs 1 trillion. The bank sanctioned Rs 120 billion under the Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS), equivalent to Rs 107 billion in loans, of which about 80% had been disbursed. SME and wholesale loans grew 8% to 9% sequentially, supporting overall credit expansion.Margin outlookThe margin outlook could benefit from the bank shedding about 9% of its bulk deposits, efficient liquidity deployment and rate tailwinds. The brokerage expects NIM to rise by 5 basis points and 15 basis points sequentially over the next two quarters. However, the cost of deposits increased 5 basis points sequentially to 4.80%, partly because of a lower share of current and savings account deposits.Asset quality indicators improved during the quarter. Slippages declined to 1.4% from 1.8% in the previous quarter and 2% a year earlier. Credit costs fell to 0.7% from 0.9% sequentially, while net credit costs eased to 0.45% from 0.6%. Net non performing assets moderated to 35 basis points and the provision coverage ratio remained stable at 70%. The bank also reported improving trends in retail products.Profitability remains constrained by costs and subdued fee income. Net interest income rose 5% sequentially, but core fee income grew only 6% year on year and treasury gains were muted. Operating expenses increased 11% sequentially, partly reflecting a one off impact of Rs 2.5 billion from labour code changes and FCNR expenses. Adjusted operating expenses rose 6%. Advt The brokerage estimates return on assets of around 1.5% and return on equity of 14% for FY27 and FY28. It maintained its BUY rating and target price of Rs 1,620, based on about 1.7 times core banking book value. A rise in stress remains a key risk to the outlook.
