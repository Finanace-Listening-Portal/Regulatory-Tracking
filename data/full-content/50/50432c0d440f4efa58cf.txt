SEBI observed that some reports and articles had misinterpreted the consultation paper as proposing a change in the age limit of Managing Directors (MDs) of MIIs. The regulator clarified that its consultation papers contain proposals only for provisions where changes are intended, and provisions intended to remain unchanged are not included as proposals. Accordingly, the consultation paper does not propose any change in the existing maximum age limit of 65 years applicable to MDs of MIIs. SEBI noted that its social media post concerning the consultation paper had also expressly stated that the age limit remains 65 years. The proposals concerning MDs relate only to their term and tenure within a single MII and across MIIs. The consultation paper further provides that a PID, MD or ED holding office when the amendments commence shall continue in office until the end of the ongoing term. SEBI emphasised that the proposals are subject to public consultation and that decisions will be taken only after that process. Public and stakeholder comments are invited through SEBI’s online public comments form until December 11, 2026. Media and other stakeholders have been requested to refer to the consultation paper itself when reporting on the proposals. Securities and Exchange Board of India PR No.77/2026 Clarification on certain media reports regarding Consultation Paper on Strengthening the Governance of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) SEBI has noticed that some media reports and articles are misinterpreting certain aspects of the Consultation Paper on “Strengthening the Governance of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)” issued on November 20, 2026. These reports mention that SEBI is proposing a change in age limit of Managing Directors (MDs) of the MIIs. In this regard, it is clarified SEBI’s consultation papers set out proposals only for those provisions in respect of which a change is intended. Provisions on which no change is intended are not included as a proposal in the consultation paper. It is further clarified that the instant consultation paper does not propose any change in the maximum age limit of 65 years applicable to the MDs of MIIs. The same was also clearly stated in SEBI’s post on the consultation paper on its social media handles, which mentioned that the age limit of MDs remains 65 years. The proposals relating to MDs in the consultation paper concern only to their term and tenure in a single MII and across MIIs. The consultation paper also provides that a PID, MD or ED holding office on the date of commencement of the amendments shall continue to hold office till the end of the ongoing term. The consultation paper has been issued for public comments, and any decision on the proposals will be taken only after the public consultation. Comments and suggestions from public and stakeholders are invited till December 11, 2026 through the online public comments form on the SEBI website: https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/publiccommentv2/PublicCommentAction.do?doPublicComments=yes Media and other stakeholders are requested to refer to the text of the consultation paper, available on the SEBI website while reporting on the proposals.
