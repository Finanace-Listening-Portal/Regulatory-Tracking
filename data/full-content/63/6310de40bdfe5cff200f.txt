Srivatsan is proposed to join the bank on January 4, 2027, the lender said in a regulatory filing. Srivatsan currently serves as Head – Compliance: Overseas Banking and Subsidiaries and Data Privacy Officer at ICICI Bank. He has around 34 years of professional experience, including nearly 25 years in the banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) sector, and close to 10 years of experience in compliance and regulatory inspection. In the interim, Venkateswaran L, currently Compliance Head – Credit, Asset Product & Regulatory Reporting at HDFC Bank, will hold the responsibility of CCO. The appointment follows the completion of the three-year tenure of Rakesh Kumar Rajput, who ceased to be HDFC Bank's chief compliance officer at the close of business hours on September 30. The appointment of Srivatsan was approved by the bank's board at its meeting on Wednesday, based on the recommendations of the Audit Committee and the Governance, Nomination and Remuneration Committee.
