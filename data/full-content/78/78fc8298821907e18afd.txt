Shashi, Kaizad are very warm. They are around and I’m going to take inputs from them,” he said.Asked about his immediate agenda and business targets once he takes charge, Bagchi said, “The immediate target will be, once I listen to everybody, then we'll drop the agenda.” Advt Bagchi, who will become HDFC Bank's first external CEO, also said he was “very, very lucky” to be taking over an institution that was “solid” and “very sound”, while pointing to the changes following the merger with HDFC Ltd.“Post merger, the balance sheet transformation that has changed, that is unwinding. Much of it is behind us. Some more work needs to be done, which collectively I'm sure we will do,” Bagchi said.He added that he was currently listening to feedback and understanding the mechanics of the bank's balance sheet and profit and loss, which, from outside, could only be understood to a certain extent.Leadership transitionBagchi's arrival also comes at a significant transition point for HDFC Bank's senior management.Outgoing MD & CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan will retire from the bank at the close of business on October 26 after deciding not to seek a third term. The bank had said Jagdishan reiterated his decision despite being persuaded to continue.Jagdishan, who succeeded Aditya Puri in October 2020, said during the Q2 interaction that his decision to leave was a personal one after more than three decades with the institution.“I think, sometimes after 31 years, one needs to also start to look at life outside of institutions that you have nurtured, you have grown with. It's a personal call that I have taken,” Jagdishan said. He added that he was leaving with “pride and satisfaction” and was confident that Bagchi would lead the bank admirably. Advt The transition has now been followed by the decision of Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha to step down from HDFC Bank with effect from December 31, 2026.Bharucha, who joined HDFC Bank in 1995 and became Deputy MD in 2023, had emerged as the leading internal contender for the CEO position alongside Bagchi, the external candidate. HDFC Bank had sent the names of Bagchi and Bharucha to the RBI during its succession process.At the CFO level, Srinivasa Vaidyanathan is also set to make way for Puneet Sharma. Sharma, who joined as CFO-designate on September 1, is scheduled to take over as CFO from December 1, 2026. Advt Meanwhile, V Srinivasa Rangan, the former HDFC Ltd CFO and currently an Executive Director at HDFC Bank, has been reappointed as Executive Director for another year, from November 23, 2026 to November 22, 2027.The bank has also proposed appointing Chief Credit Officer Jimmy Tata as a Whole-time Director, designated as Executive Director, for three years from the date of RBI approval or such other period as specified by the central bank.The board has additionally created one more Whole-time Director position, taking the proposed strength to four WTDs apart from the MD & CEO. HDFC Bank said the additional position is intended to provide sharper synergy and oversight, including over subsidiaries, while creating a larger pipeline for succession planning. The position will be filled in consultation with the new MD & CEO after Bagchi takes charge. Advt Taken together, the changes point to a broader reshaping of HDFC Bank's senior leadership rather than a CEO transition in isolation. Bagchi will take over from Jagdishan on October 27, while Bharucha will remain until December-end and Rangan has another year-long mandate. At the same time, Puneet Sharma is set to take over as CFO and Jimmy Tata is being elevated into the executive leadership structure.
