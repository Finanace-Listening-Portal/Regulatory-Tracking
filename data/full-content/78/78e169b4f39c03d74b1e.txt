Under its proposed leadership plan, IDBI Bank MD & CEO Rakesh Sharma is expected to continue, with former CSB Bank CEO C. V. R. Rajendran joining the leadership group and former ICICI Prudential Life MD & CEO and ICICI Bank CFO N. S. Kannan proposed as Chairman. Advt The proposed contribution of Digit and IIFL Capital will be made at their respective market prices, according to Fairfax.The move would give IDBI Bank access to Digit's general and life insurance businesses, while IIFL Capital would add investment banking, institutional equities, wealth and asset management and mutual fund distribution capabilities to the proposed platform.Fairfax said it does not expect any change in the governance, management or operating structure of Digit or IIFL Capital following the proposed contribution transactions. Digit will continue to be led by Kamesh Goyal, while IIFL Capital will continue to be managed by its existing leadership team.The proposed transactions will require approvals from the Reserve Bank of India and other regulators. The Digit contribution will also require approval from the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India, while both contribution transactions will be subject to Securities and Exchange Board of India approval.Fairfax said the proposed contribution transactions are separate from the purchase transaction and that the acquisition of IDBI Bank could occur without one or both of the proposed contributions.The company has not yet entered into binding definitive agreements for the transactions. Advt Following the proposed acquisition, Fairfax expects IDBI Bank's current MD and CEO Rakesh Sharma to continue in his role. It also plans to include former CSB Bank CEO C V R Rajendran in the leadership group and former ICICI Prudential Life MD & CEO and ICICI Bank CFO N S Kannan as Chairman, subject to RBI approval.Fairfax Chairman and CEO Prem Watsa said the combination of IDBI Bank, Digit and IIFL Capital would create a unified financial services company offering banking, insurance and investment banking and wealth management services.
