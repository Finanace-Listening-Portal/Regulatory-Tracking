Summary: Filing a Revised Form 36 before the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal (ITAT) enables taxpayers to update particulars furnished in their original appeal, including residential or business addresses, email IDs, mobile numbers, entity names and authorised representative details. It may also be necessary to rectify clerical errors or seek modification of the grounds of appeal. This guide explains the procedure for filing a Revised Form 36 through the ITAT e-Filing Portal, including accessing the appeal dashboard, selecting the relevant appeal, entering revised particulars, uploading supporting documents, completing electronic verification and downloading the acknowledgment. Taxpayers should keep their original appeal number, Bench details, supporting documents and applicable Digital Signature Certificate ready before initiating the process. Where additional grounds of appeal are proposed, permission from the Tribunal must be obtained through an appropriate application. Timely updating of contact information helps taxpayers receive hearing notices and reduces the risk of ex-parte proceedings. Taxpayers should also verify the applicable appeal form under the Income-tax Act, 1961 or the Income-tax Act, 2025, depending upon the relevant assessment or tax year. The guide further highlights practical precautions, documentation requirements and assistance available through the ITAT e-Filing Helpdesk and the concerned Bench registry. The ITAT is the final fact-finding authority under the appellate hierarchy and provides taxpayers an opportunity to challenge orders that they believe are incorrect either on facts or in law. In recent years, the functioning of the ITAT has been completely digitised. Appeals are now required to be filed electronically through the ITAT e-Filing Portal and authenticated using a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) as a mode of verification. Consequently, all particulars furnished in Form 36 become part of the electronic records maintained by the Tribunal and are relied upon for future communication and conduct of proceedings. However, subsequent to filing an appeal, various circumstances may arise requiring modification or updation of the information originally furnished in Form 36. For instance, there may be a change in the assessee’s residential or business address, email ID, mobile number, legal status, name of the entity, or authorised representative. In certain cases, the assessee may also notice an inadvertent error or omission in the original appeal form, or may wish to revise or supplement the grounds of appeal already filed. In such situations, it is important that the records of the Tribunal reflect the correct and updated particulars of the appellant. To facilitate this, the ITAT portal provides the facility for filing a Revised Form 36. Filing a revised form ensures that the Tribunal’s records remain accurate, communications are sent to the correct address, and any mistakes or omissions in the original filing are duly rectified, thereby enabling smooth and effective conduct of the appellate proceedings. Here is a step-by-step guide explaining how common taxpayers can file a revised Form 36 online through the ITAT e-Filing portal. Why Do You Need to File a Revised Form 36? Filing a revised Form 36 keeps your appeal active and legally valid. Common reasons for filing a revision include: Change in Contact Details: Updating your email address or mobile number so you do not miss automated SMS or email hearing alerts. Change in Address: Moving to a new home or office where physical notices should be delivered. Rule 9A of the Income Tax (Appellate Tribunal) Rules, 1963 specifically provides for filing a revised Form 36 where there is a change in the address of the parties. Entity or Name Changes: Updating records due to a business merger, demerger, conversion (such as a partnership becoming a private limited company), or change in legal representative. Rectifying Errors or Updating Grounds: Correcting clerical mistakes made in the original form or adding/modifying grounds of appeal with the permission of the Tribunal. (Note: Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, appeals relating to Tax Year 2026–27 onwards may require the newly notified appeal form—Form 115—instead of Form 36. Always verify the applicable assessment or tax year before filing.) What You Should Keep Ready Before Starting Before logging into the portal, gather these essentials to ensure a smooth filing process: 1. Original Appeal Details Your ITAT Appeal Number (ITA number), Bench location (e.g., Pune, Mumbai, Delhi), and the date of your original filing. 2. Updated Information The exact new details you wish to place on record. 3. Supporting Documents (in PDF Format) A short covering letter or application explaining why the revised form is being filed. Proof of the change (such as a new address proof, Certificate of Incorporation for a name change, or revised grounds of appeal). A copy of the originally filed Form 36 acknowledgment. 4. Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) Depending on your taxpayer category (such as companies or individuals liable for tax audit), a valid DSC may be mandatory to sign the submission. Step-by-Step Procedure to File Revised Form 36 Online Step 1: Log in to the ITAT e-Filing Portal Visit the official ITAT e-Filing portal. Click on the login option and enter your registered user ID (usually your PAN) and password, or verify using a One-Time Password (OTP). Step 2: Go to Your Appeal Dashboard Once logged in, open the Appeal Dashboard. This section displays all appeals linked to your PAN, along with their current status and appeal numbers. Step 3: Select the Relevant Appeal Locate the specific appeal you want to update using your ITA Number or Assessment Year. Open the appeal record to view the options available for filing additional documents or revised forms. Step 4: Fill Out the Revised Form 36 Select the option to submit a revised Form 36 or miscellaneous filing (depending on the nature of the update). Enter the updated details carefully: Clearly mention your existing Appeal Number so the system and registry link the revision to the right file. Fill in the updated address, email ID, phone number, or revised grounds of appeal. Double-check that all unchanged details—such as the Assessment Year, Assessing Officer’s ward/circle, and disputed tax amount—match your original appeal. Step 5: Upload Supporting PDF Documents Attach your covering application, proof of changes, and any revised statement of facts or grounds of appeal. Ensure that all files are clear, legible, and saved in standard PDF format within the portal’s file size limits. Step 6: Verify, Sign, and Submit Review the entire form on the preview screen to ensure there are no spelling mistakes or missing attachments. Complete the verification using your Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) or prescribed electronic verification method, and click Submit. Step 7: Download the Acknowledgment After submission, the portal generates an acknowledgment receipt. Download and save this PDF for your records. You should also check your dashboard periodically to confirm that the ITAT registry has updated your details on the official cause list and case status page. Important Tips for Taxpayers Do Not Delay Contact Updates: ITAT sends hearing notices primarily through email and SMS. If your email ID or phone number changes, update it immediately to avoid an ex-parte order (a decision passed in your absence). Additional Grounds Need Permission: If you are revising Form 36 to add completely new legal arguments (additional grounds of appeal) rather than just fixing an address or typo, you must also file a formal petition requesting the Tribunal’s permission to admit those new grounds. The filing and verification of additional grounds are governed by Rule 11 of the Income Tax (Appellate Tribunal) Rules, 1963. Verify the Applicable Appeal Form: Rule 193 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 prescribes Form 115 for appeals before the Appellate Tribunal under Section 362 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Seek Help When Stuck: If you face technical glitches on the portal, you can reach out to the ITAT e-Filing Helpdesk via email at [email protected] or check with the local ITAT Bench registry.
