In contrast, NIMs of the other PVBs rose 9 bps sequentially and 22 bps year on year to 3.06%. Advt PSBs saw lending yields rise 18 bps sequentially, compared with a 9-bp increase in funding costs. For PVBs, funding costs rose 14 bps, while lending yields increased 6 bps, putting greater pressure on their margins.The funding cost increase was sharper for large PVBs, whose cost of funds rose 15 bps sequentially, compared with an 8-bp increase for other PVBs. The report attributed the pressure partly to FCNR(B) deposits mobilised during the quarter.The difference was also reflected in net interest income. NII of select PSBs grew 18.4% year on year in the September quarter, compared with 14.4% growth for PVBs. Interest income from advances rose 15.3% for PSBs, against 14.1% for PVBs.PVBs also saw stronger growth in term deposits, which rose 27% year on year, contributing to a 13% increase in interest expenses. For PSBs, interest expenses rose 7.1%, with some lenders benefiting from the retirement of higher-cost bulk deposits.Q3 boost likelyThe margin trend could receive some support in the third quarter from the 25-bp repo rate increase to 5.50%, as repo-linked loans reprice faster than deposits. However, larger PVBs could continue to face funding-cost pressure from FCNR(B) deposits before the benefit of deploying those funds is reflected in earnings.The report indicates that banks with stronger deposit franchises and the ability to deploy surplus liquidity into granular, higher-yielding assets will be better placed to sustain margins and growth.
