Check state-wise list of closures for November 2026 Chhath Puja celebrations will continue until November 16, resulting in temporary bank closures in areas like Patna and Ranchi. Throughout November, multiple regional holidays will be enforced across India, so it's advisable for customers to check their local bank holiday schedules before visiting branches. However, digital banking will still be operational, ensuring clients can manage transactions online despite physical branch shutdowns during this festive season. While bank branches in some cities will remain closed on Monday on account of Chhath, the holiday is not applicable nationwide, with closures varying across states and cities.According to the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) November holiday calendar, banks in Patna and Ranchi will remain closed on Monday, November 16 for Chhath Puja. Branches in cities such as New Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata will remain open on the day.Chhath Puja is being observed over four days, from November 13 to 16 this year. The celebrations began with Nahay Khay, followed by Kharna on November 14. Devotees observed Sandhya Arghya on November 15, while the festival concludes on November 16 with Usha Arghya, when offerings are made to the rising Sun. Banks were also closed on November 14, the second Saturday, and will remain closed on November 28, the fourth Saturday.Customers should note that bank holidays apply to physical branches.
