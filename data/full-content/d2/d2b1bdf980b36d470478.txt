Consolidated profit after tax fell 51% year on year to around Rs 3.4 billion, missing estimates by 27%, even as profit before tax, excluding dividend income from the year ago quarter, grew 51% to Rs 4.9 billion.Net interest income rose 165% year on year to Rs 6.8 billion, beating estimates by around 18%, while other income declined 28% to Rs 4.4 billion. Other income included investment income of Rs 1.6 billion, compared with Rs 1.8 billion a year earlier, according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. Advt Employee expenses increased 55% year on year to Rs 1.5 billion. Consolidated pre-provision operating profit, excluding dividend income, grew 85% to Rs 5.7 billion. Credit costs rose to Rs 408 million from Rs 246 million in the preceding quarter, while the share of losses from joint ventures and associates increased to Rs 439 million from Rs 191 million a year earlier.The consolidation of Reliance Services and Holdings as a wholly owned step-down subsidiary from April 30, 2026, also changed the presentation of the company's results. The business was previously accounted for under the share of profit or loss from joint ventures and associates.Jio Credit, the lending arm, continued to expand, with assets under management rising 22% sequentially to around Rs 373 billion in September. Disbursements increased 98% year on year and 17% sequentially to Rs 131 billion. Net interest income rose 115% year on year to Rs 3 billion, while profit after tax increased 140% year on year and 25% sequentially to Rs 1.2 billion. The average cost of borrowings remained broadly stable at 7.08%, compared with 7.07% in the June quarter.The growth prospectsThe brokerage expects Jio Credit’s assets under management to grow at a compound annual growth rate of 89% over FY26-FY28, with profit after tax projected to grow at 190% over the same period. It expects return on assets and return on equity to reach 2.4% and 8.8%, respectively, in FY28. Advt Jio Financial’s asset management business also expanded, with assets under management rising to Rs 227 billion in September from Rs 184 billion in June. Its specialised investment fund platform reached Rs 4.8 billion in assets within three months of launch, placing it among the top 10 players in the category. Investors from cities beyond the top 30 locations contributed 40% of retail assets under management.In payments, transaction processing volumes at Jio Payment Solutions fell 24% sequentially to Rs 147 billion. However, net processing margin improved to 14 basis points from 12 basis points in the preceding quarter as the business focused on higher-quality merchants and product mix. Jio Payments Bank’s deposits rose to Rs 6.4 billion from Rs 6.2 billion, while its business correspondent network expanded to around 633,000 from 527,000. Its customer base stood at around 4 million. Advt The brokerage cut its FY27 earnings per share estimate by around 6.6% to account for higher provisions but raised its FY28 estimate to reflect improved net interest income. It retained its buy rating on the stock with a target price of Rs 300, based on a sum-of-the-parts valuation using September 2028 estimates. It expects consolidated profit after tax to grow at a compound annual rate of 54% over FY26-FY28.
