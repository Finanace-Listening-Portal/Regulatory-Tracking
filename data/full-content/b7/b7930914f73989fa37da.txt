Advt The bank said it has initiated a planned transition and integrated the Commercial Bank portfolio handled by Kothari into its Retail and Institutional business segments.Kothari, who has spent 31 years at Kotak, said the organisation had become a part of his identity over the years.“Kotak has been much more than a workplace to me; it has been a source of continuous learning and a place to fulfil collective aspirations and build enduring relationships,” he said.
