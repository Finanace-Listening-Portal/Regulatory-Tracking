Articles 3 min read Bank employees across PSBs, nine private lenders to join December 3 strike against IDBI sale Three bank unions have called a nationwide strike on December 3 against the proposed sale of IDBI Bank to Fairfax Financial Holdings, with around three lakh employees expected to participate across 10 public sector banks and nine private lenders. Participation could, however, be marginal at SBI and Indian Overseas Bank, where larger unions are staying out of the strike. Advt The other public sector banks where the participating unions expect stronger participation include Bank of Baroda, Bank of India, Bank of Maharashtra, Canara Bank, Central Bank of India, UCO Bank, Union Bank of India, Punjab National Bank and Punjab and Sind Bank.In the private sector, members of the three unions in nine banks are expected to participate. These include CSB Bank, City Union Bank, Dhanlaxmi Bank, Federal Bank, Jammu & Kashmir Bank, Karnataka Bank, Karur Vysya Bank, South Indian Bank and Tamilnad Mercantile Bank, as per UFIOE Convenor Devidas Tuljapurkar.He attributed the relatively lower participation expected at SBI and IOB to the larger membership base of unions that are not participating in the strike.Around three lakh bank employees are expected to participate in the strike, Devidas Tuljapurkar, Convenor of the United Forum of IDBI Officers and Employees (UFIOE), told ETBFSI.Tuljapurkar said that the National Confederation of Bank Employees (NCBE), the majority employee union at SBI and IOB, is not joining the strike, although it is expressing solidarity with the agitation.Similarly, the All India Confederation of Bank Officers, a majority officers' organisation in IOB and several other public sector banks, is also expressing solidarity but is not joining the strike, he said.The strike will be held alongside the strike called by the United Forum of IDBI Officers and Employees. IDBI Bank employees and officers had earlier observed a two-day strike on November 2 and 3 against the proposed sale. However, with the government continuing with the sale process, the forum called for another strike on December 3, the circular said. Advt The three bank unions said they had decided to extend solidarity through a nationwide strike, considering the gravity of the issue and the need to oppose the proposed sale.The unions have described the proposed sale as unwarranted and unacceptable, arguing that it is part of the government's broader agenda to privatise public sector banks. They have also raised questions over the sale process and valuation, saying that information emerging on these aspects needs to be investigated.The November 17 circular has outlined an agitational programme ahead of the December 3 strike. This includes physical and online meetings of union members between November 18 and 30, mass demonstrations on November 25, badge-wearing on November 27 and a social media campaign on November 28. Advt The unions have also called for members to submit memoranda to the Finance Minister on November 30, followed by demonstrations across centres on December 2. The programme will culminate in an all-India strike and strike rallies on December 3.
