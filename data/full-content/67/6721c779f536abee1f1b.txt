MINA is the native token used for fees, staking and delegation, while zkApps use off-chain computation and on-chain proof verification. This article explains what the frequently quoted 22 KB description does and does not mean, how block producers and SNARK workers interact, how wallet and delegation choices affect control, and how Indian virtual digital asset tax and withholding rules can apply. It does not predict price, adoption, returns or protocol success. Key Takeaways Show all 21 sections Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Mina Protocol and MINA Token: Verified Network Identity Mina is a layer-1 blockchain whose official documentation describes a production mainnet, a proof-of-stake consensus system and recursive zero-knowledge proofs. MINA is the network’s native token. It is used to pay transaction fees and can be staked directly by a block producer or delegated to a block producer. A MINA balance is not equity in Mina Foundation, o1Labs or any developer building a zkApp. It carries no contractual redemption right, dividend, deposit protection or guaranteed yield. The 22 KB statement describes the proof, not every underlying record Official project material describes Mina as maintaining a roughly 22 KB proof that attests to the current blockchain state. Recursive proofs allow a new proof to incorporate verification of the preceding state. This reduces what an ordinary verifier must download, but it does not mean every transaction, account record, archive or application data set physically occupies only 22 KB. Archive nodes and ecosystem services may retain additional historical data. Readers should therefore separate succinct verification from data availability, indexing and application-storage requirements. Block producers are selected under the protocol’s proof-of-stake rules. SNARK workers generate proofs for transactions and offer completed work through the SNARK marketplace. A block producer that includes new transactions must account for the required proof work. This division does not make the system trustless in the everyday sense of eliminating all operational dependencies: users still depend on wallet software, endpoints, explorers, exchanges and their own key handling. Ouroboros Samasika and epoch-based participation The official glossary identifies Ouroboros Samasika as Mina’s consensus mechanism and states that a slot lasts 90 seconds. Stake snapshots and protocol epochs determine which accounts are eligible for block production. Delegation is not the same as transferring token ownership to a validator at the protocol level, but a user who delegates through a custodial exchange may give the platform legal and practical control over the asset. The distinction should be documented because it affects access, reward reporting and recovery if the service fails. Proof generation has economic and technical dependencies A SNARK proves that a computation was performed correctly under the defined circuit; it does not prove that external data was truthful before being supplied. A zkApp that relies on an oracle, identity provider or web data attestation inherits risks in that input chain. Proof-system bugs, faulty verification keys, wallet signing errors, endpoint outages and application logic defects remain possible even when the underlying cryptography operates as designed. zkApps: Off-Chain Computation and On-Chain Verification Mina calls its zero-knowledge applications zkApps. Official documentation states that they are written in TypeScript through the o1js framework, execute largely off-chain and submit a proof for on-chain verification. Private inputs can remain undisclosed while the proof confirms that the programmed condition was satisfied. This architecture can reduce on-chain computation, but privacy is not automatic. Staking and Delegation Without a Return Promise Official Mina material describes MINA as inflationary and without a fixed supply cap. Block rewards create additional tokens. The published economics paper explains a target path that moved toward a default steady-state inflation rate, subject to governance. Tokenomics parameters can change, so a historic percentage should not be presented as a fixed future rate. Nominal token rewards may be offset by dilution, validator fees, missed production, tax and changes in market value. Delegation requires operator and custody checks Confirm whether delegation is performed from a self-custody wallet or through an exchange-controlled account. Identify the block producer, fee or commission policy, payout method and reward record available to the delegator. Keep the transaction hash, staking ledger or epoch reference and the INR value used for tax reporting. Do not treat a displayed annualised rate as a deposit interest rate or capital-protection promise. Check wallet compatibility before moving tokens between an exchange, a hardware wallet and a zkApp. Mina’s published material states that staking or delegation does not require protocol-level bonding or slashing in the manner used by some networks. That does not remove exchange lockups, withdrawal queues, operational losses, insolvency or contractual set-off rights that a third-party platform may impose. The platform’s terms and the protocol mechanics must be reviewed separately. Wallet Control, Account Creation Fee and Transaction Records The official getting-started page lists Auro, Clorio and Ledger-compatible options and warns users to protect the recovery phrase. It also states that a new Mina account incurs a one-time 1 MINA creation fee deducted from the first incoming transaction. A wallet brand listing is not an endorsement of a particular download, browser extension or custody provider. Users should obtain software from the authentic publisher and verify network and address details before transferring. Indian Tax and Compliance Treatment for MINA MINA ordinarily falls within India’s virtual digital asset definition when it represents cryptographically secured value transferred or stored electronically. The Income-tax Act, 2025 continues the special VDA regime: income from transfer is taxed at the prescribed special rate, only cost of acquisition is generally deductible for that computation, and losses cannot ordinarily be set off or carried forward under the special provision. The exact section numbering and return fields should be checked for the relevant tax year. Withholding and reward valuation need transaction-level evidence A transfer involving consideration can attract the VDA tax-deduction-at-source rule when its statutory conditions and thresholds are met. The person responsible for withholding, timing and valuation depend on the transaction structure. Staking rewards, SNARK-work receipts, airdrops or business receipts can also create taxable events on receipt or later transfer. The law does not provide a universal answer that every receipt is taxed only when sold. The taxpayer should determine character, accrual or receipt timing and fair value from the actual facts. Using an overseas exchange does not remove Indian reporting, withholding or foreign-asset analysis. FIU-IND registration of a VDA service provider concerns anti-money-laundering compliance; it is not approval of MINA, a wallet or an investment return. Users should preserve PAN/KYC records, bank flows, transaction exports and wallet evidence and reconcile them to the income-tax return. Mina-Specific Risks That a Reader Should Not Generalise Away Recursive-proof correctness depends on implementation, cryptographic assumptions and secure upgrades. A compact proof does not guarantee archive availability, explorer uptime or application data retention. zkApp privacy depends on circuit design, input handling and what the user elects to reveal. Inflation, governance and reward parameters can change and can dilute non-participating holders. Wallet, hardware, browser-extension and exchange failures can cause permanent loss or blocked access. Thin liquidity or network-specific withdrawal maintenance can prevent timely disposal or transfer. These are not predictions that a failure will occur. They identify the questions that must be answered before a reader relies on a protocol feature or a third-party service. Network design, token price, regulatory treatment and service-provider solvency are separate risk categories and should not be collapsed into one label. Frequently Asked Questions 1. Does Mina store its entire economic history in only 22 KB? No. The 22 KB description concerns the succinct proof used to verify the current chain state. Archive, index and application services can retain substantially more data. 2. What is MINA used for? MINA is the native network token used for transaction fees and proof-of-stake participation through block production or delegation. It is not a company share or guaranteed-return product. 3. Are zkApp inputs automatically private? Not automatically. A circuit can keep selected inputs private, but account state, metadata, outputs and external services may reveal information. Privacy depends on the application’s design and use. 4. Can a MINA holder delegate without giving away the private key? Protocol-level delegation from self-custody ordinarily does not require transferring the private key. Delegation through a custodial platform is different because the platform may control the wallet and withdrawals. 5. Is a staking percentage guaranteed? No. Rewards depend on protocol parameters, stake participation, operator performance, fees and distribution practices. Token value can also fall, and tax can apply. 6. Is the 1 MINA new-account charge a recurring fee? The official getting-started page describes it as a one-time account creation fee deducted from the first incoming transaction. Other transaction or service charges can still apply. 7. How is a MINA disposal taxed in India? A transfer can fall under the special VDA tax regime, including restricted deductions, loss-set-off limits and possible TDS. The actual computation depends on cost, consideration, status and transaction evidence. Key Takeaways Mina uses recursive zero-knowledge proofs to keep verification succinct; this is not the same as eliminating historical or application data. MINA supports fees and proof-of-stake participation but provides no ownership interest, redemption promise or guaranteed yield. zkApps execute largely off-chain and verify proofs on-chain; circuit, input, wallet and oracle risks remain. Delegation mechanics must be separated from custodial-platform terms, withdrawal controls and operator commissions.
