RBI/2026-27/305, DOR.STR.REC.257/21-04-177/2026-27 dated 23 November 2026, has issued the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026. The amendments modify the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025, in exercise of powers under Sections 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The revised requirements will take effect from 1 April 2027. The amendment substitutes paragraph 26 to require that the issuance, holding and subsequent transfer of securitisation notes take place exclusively in dematerialised form. A minimum ticket size of ₹1 crore will apply both at issuance and during subsequent transfers. The originator must ensure that its agreement with the Special Purpose Entity (SPE) requires arrangements with relevant entities, including registrars, depositories and stock exchanges, wherever applicable, to enforce this threshold. However, securitisation notes with amortisation structures may be traded at their amortised value even where the ticket size falls below ₹1 crore. Ticket size is expressly defined as the investment made by a single investor. *** Reserve Bank of India RBI/2026-27/305DOR.STR.REC.257/21-04-177/2026-27| Dated: November 23, 2026 Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026 Please refer to Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025. 2. The Amendment Directions shall come into effect from April 01, 2027. 4. Amendment Directions modify the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025 as under: (1) Para 26 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “26. “The issuance, holding and subsequent transfer of securitisation notes under these Directions shall only be in a dematerialised form. The minimum ticket size shall be ₹1 Crore at the time of issuance as well as at any subsequent transfers. The originator shall ensure that the agreement with the SPE requires the latter to put in place necessary arrangements with entities such as the registrar, depository, stock exchange etc., as may be applicable, to ensure compliance with the minimum ticket-size requirement. Provided that securitisation notes with amortisation structures shall be permitted to be traded at amortised value, if the ticket size falls below ₹1 crore. Explanation: Ticket size, for the purpose of these directions, refers to the size of investment by a single investor.” (2) Para 27 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “27. With respect to offer of securitisation notes to the public, Regulation 21 of SEBI (Issue and Listing of Securitised Debt Instruments and Security Receipts) Regulations, 2008, as amended from time to time, shall be complied with.” (3) Paragraph 27A shall be inserted as below: “27A.
