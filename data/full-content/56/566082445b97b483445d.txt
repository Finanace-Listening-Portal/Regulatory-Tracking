ICICI Bank’s overall retail and rural portfolio grew 14.8 per cent YoY and 3.2 per cent QoQ.Sandeep Batra, Executive Director at ICICI Bank, said, “Our objective on credit cards continues to be focusing on risk-calibrated profitable growth with every product segment. We have been focused on onboarding quality customers, driving profitable spends, and exiting riskier and unprofitable segments. That approach continues to be there,” Batra said. Advt “While there has been some moderation, the profitability across this remains quite healthy, and in fact has been improving due to lower credit cards,” he added.Batra attributed part of the YoY decline to higher festive spends in the corresponding quarter last year, when the festive season began earlier.“The year-on-year decline in credit cards portfolio is probably due to higher festive spends towards the end of Q2 last year, driven by early onset of festive season,” Batra said.He said the bank continues to see growth in the credit card business and will leverage its distribution through co-branded partnerships.“There have been a fair bit of new additions which are happening, and we hope that in the upcoming festive season Q3, we do expect to drive profitable growth across these segments,” Batra said.He said the bank was increasingly looking at the business through a customer lens rather than only a product lens, with credit cards forming part of the overall bouquet of services offered to customers. He also said spends could move up and down and that last year there had been a buildup in the book towards the end of September, whereas the corresponding buildup this year was expected in Q3.Bank continues to prune unprofitable segments Advt The bank has been actively reviewing the profitability of granular customer segments and exiting those that do not meet its profitability parameters.“It is not a particular segment. We are focused on a customer as opposed to a product. For good quality customers, we are very happy to give more cards and more limits and serve them to the best of our ability. That will continue,” Batra said.“What I did mention was if there were segments which were unprofitable, we have been exiting that. That is about it. It is not a credit issue,” he added.Asked which segments the bank was exiting, Batra said these were not broad product categories but granular micro-segments identified through the bank’s internal analysis. Advt “They are pretty granular in nature and whenever we find any particular micro segment which is unprofitable, we exit,” he said.What does the credit card portfolio look like?According to ICICI Bank’s investor presentation, credit cards accounted for 5.6 per cent of its retail and rural portfolio at September 30, 2026. The retail and rural portfolio stood at Rs 9.46 lakh crore, up 14.8 per cent YoY.The bank said growth in retail credit card spends is being driven by collaborations with leading brands, extending payment options through UPI on credit cards and increasing EMI campaigns to improve affordability. Advt Around 70 per cent of the credit card portfolio is to existing customers, while around 85 per cent is to salaried individuals, according to the investor presentation.How did Q2 FY27 fare for ICICI Bank?ICICI Bank reported a 20.1 per cent YoY rise in standalone profit after tax to Rs 14,843 crore in Q2 FY27. Net interest income rose 17.7 per cent YoY, while core operating profit increased 21.8 per cent to Rs 20,805 crore. The bank’s total loan portfolio grew 30 per cent YoY, or 24.1 per cent excluding loans provided by international branches against FCNR(B) deposits.The lender’s gross NPAs improved to Rs 23,335 crore at September 30, 2026, from Rs 23,850 crore a year earlier, while the net NPA stood at Rs 5,706 crore.
