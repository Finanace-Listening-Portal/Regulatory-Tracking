Banca annualised premium equivalent (APE) growth for covered life insurers is estimated at 8-12% year-on-year in FY27, compared with 12-17% growth in total APE.The key strength of the covered life insurers has been their banking parentage, which has translated into healthy distribution strength. However, each of them has faced challenges with its promoter banca channel over the past six years, with banca channel APE growth slowing to a 9-13% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) over FY24-26 from 9-18% over FY19-23, according to a report by Nomura. Advt The latest pressure has been particularly evident at HDFC Life, where elevated competition from non-bank promoted life insurers has negatively affected banca channel growth. ICICI Life was excluded from the comparison of banca APE growth because its channel declined.The slower growth in banca is expected to continue even as the overall APE growth of the covered life insurers remains higher, pointing to the increasing importance of alternate distribution channels for insurers.Non-bank insurers deepen bank partnershipsNon-bank promoted life insurers Tata AIA Life, Aditya Birla Sun Life and Bajaj Life reported individual new business premium CAGR of 11-22% over FY24-26. This was supported by robust growth in their banca channels, where individual new business premium grew at a 12-29% CAGR during the period.These insurers have also been rapidly scaling up their partnerships with banks. They have an estimated 8-17 bank partners, compared with 8-20 bank partners for the covered bank-promoted life insurers.More importantly, these insurers are scaling up their presence within their bank partners, based on management commentary, and are consequently posing as strong competitors to the in-house insurer.The development is increasing competition within bank distribution networks, which have traditionally been an important strength for bank-promoted life insurers.Alternate channels gain importanceWith competition at banks intensifying, most life insurers have indicated that they are focusing more on direct and individual agent channels. The key issue to monitor is how smooth and foreseeable the selling engines of these alternate channels can become. Advt There is healthy scope for improvement in individual agency, making the channel an important area of focus as insurers look to support growth beyond banca.At the same time, promoter banks continue to offer healthy distribution support to the covered life insurers, even if that support comes with heated competition.The ability to build consistent performance in alternate channels will therefore be critical. This becomes particularly important in FY27, when banca APE growth for the covered insurers is expected to remain below overall APE growth.The shift does not make banca irrelevant for bank-promoted insurers. Rather, it highlights the need for insurers to develop alternate channels alongside the distribution support provided by their promoter banks, particularly as non-bank promoted insurers deepen their presence across bank networks.
