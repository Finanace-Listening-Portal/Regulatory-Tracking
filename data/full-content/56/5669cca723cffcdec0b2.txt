Advt Going forward, the committee will hold consultation meetings with key stakeholders to gather more inputs for framing its recommendations on Banking for Viksit Bharat 2047.The consultations are aimed at helping the committee formulate recommendations to strengthen the Indian banking sector and make it competitive, resilient, inclusive and future-ready.Last month, the government set up the committee to assess the current banking practices as well as suggest reforms, including preparing a roadmap for AI and digital innovation.The panel is chaired by former Central Vigilance Commissioner Suresh N Patel. RBI Deputy Governor Shirish Chandra Murmu, former deputy governor N S Vishwanathan, and former Indian Bank MD Padmaja Chunduru are a part of the panel, which was initially announced in the Union Budget 2026-27.The committee has a tenure of six months. PTINew Delhi, Nov 18 (IANS) The Centre’s high-level committee on 'Banking for Viksit Bharat' has, in its first two sittings, discussed the key areas for examination under its terms of reference. It also identified stakeholder groups for consultation, including banks, non-banking financial companies, development financial institutions, regulators, industry associations, fintech organisations and others, according to a Finance Ministry statement issued on Wednesday.
