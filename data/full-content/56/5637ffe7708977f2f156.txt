(Image credit: Company website)Our Bureau Chennai 3 min read Last Updated : Oct 09 2026 | 10:04 PM IST @media (min-width:1100px) and (max-width:1280px) { .storymeta .meta-info { flex: 0 0 45%; } } Listen to This ArticleVayona Energy, a leading fully integrated onshore wind energy company across India and South Asia, dedicated its next-generation wind turbine, Vayona 5.0/193, built on an all-new 5 megawatt (MW) onshore wind turbine platform, to the nation at Windergy India 2026 in Chennai on Friday. Developed in close collaboration with Aerovide GmbH, Germany, the platform brings together German design and engineering expertise with Vayona’s deep understanding of India’s operating environment, manufacturing capabilities and lifecycle service network. Featuring a 193-metre rotor, the platform is being developed to maximise energy yield across India’s diverse wind conditions, including low and ultra-low wind conditions, while operating reliably in high-temperature environments. The platform reflects a clear product ambition: to deliver a world-class wind turbine engineered for Indian site conditions and manufactured in India through a local supply chain for major components. The platform marks Vayona’s expansion into the 5 MW segment and broadens its ability to address evolving project requirements. Designed in close collaboration with Aerovide, it combines a larger energy-capture area with engineering tailored to the country’s wind, climate and site characteristics. Prashant Jain, executive vice-chairman, Vayona Energy, said: “India’s next phase of wind growth will require technology that can unlock more sites, improve project economics and perform reliably across demanding operating conditions. The Vayona 5.0/193 represents our ambition to build a world-class wind turbine platform in India, backed by German engineering and strengthened by Vayona’s manufacturing, project execution and lifecycle service capabilities.
