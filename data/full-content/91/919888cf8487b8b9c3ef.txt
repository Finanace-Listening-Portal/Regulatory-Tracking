What analysts said on Tech Mahindra Q2 showSystematix Institutional Equities said that TechM delivered another strong performance, beating revenue and margin expectations on broad-based TCV bookings that mitigate vertical concentration risks. Operational rigor keeps the 15 per cent FY27E Ebit margin target is well within reach, with AI productivity offering a longer-term lever, said Systematix analysts. Further, it added that Project Helix, the SpaceXAI partnership, and TechM’s AI assets strengthen its AI-led services proposition, though the company indicated that it would disclose its AI-led revenue in the second half of the fiscal. Antique Stock Broking, too, said that Tech Mahindra delivered another strong quarter, with Q2 FY27 revenue growth of 2.4 per cent QoQ in CC terms versus its estimate of 1.5 per cent.
