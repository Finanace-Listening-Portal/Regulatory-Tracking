Summary: Directors holding a Director Identification Number (DIN) must ensure that their personal and identity particulars recorded with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) remain accurate. Two important forms used for this purpose are DIR-3 KYC Web and DIR-6, but they serve different functions. The revised DIR-3 KYC framework, effective from 31 March 2026 under the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025, replaces annual KYC with a requirement to complete periodic verification once every three consecutive financial years. The revised web form also facilitates updates to registered mobile numbers, email addresses and residential addresses. Such changes must be intimated within 30 days rather than deferred until the next periodic KYC filing. DIR-6, on the other hand, is used for changes or corrections in other DIN particulars, including the director’s name, father’s name, date of birth, nationality, gender and applicable identification details. Changes covered by DIR-6 must generally be reported within 30 days, with a separate obligation to intimate the companies concerned within 15 days. The distinction is important because filing the incorrect form can lead to resubmissions, delays and compliance difficulties. Directors should identify the nature of the change, select the appropriate MCA form and comply with the applicable documentary, verification and certification requirements. Every person who holds a Director Identification Number (DIN) is required to periodically confirm and update their KYC particulars with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Earlier, this was an annual compliance requirement. However, following the amendment effective from 31 March 2026, the annual requirement has been replaced with a simplified KYC filing once in every three consecutive financial years. The revised DIR-3 KYC Web form can be used for: Periodic KYC verification. Change in registered mobile number. Change in registered email ID. Change in residential address. Reactivation of a deactivated DIN. Example: Change in Mobile Number and Residential Address Suppose Mr. A is a director of a private limited company. He has shifted his residence from Kochi to Ernakulam and has also started using a new mobile number. He cannot simply ignore these changes until the next periodic KYC filing. He must intimate MCA through DIR-3 KYC Web within 30 days of the change. Verification and Supporting Documents For routine periodic KYC, where there is no change in particulars, the process is simplified and generally involves OTP-based verification without supporting document attachments. Where changes are being reported, the applicable digital signature, professional certification and supporting-document requirements must be followed. What Is DIR-6? DIR-6 is used to intimate MCA about changes in other personal particulars of a DIN holder that are not covered by the revised DIR-3 KYC Web process. Some common examples are: Change in the director’s name or surname. Change or correction in father’s name. Change or correction in date of birth. Change in nationality. Change in gender. Changes in other identity particulars covered by the form, such as PAN, passport or other identification details. Example: Change in Director’s Name Suppose Mr. B has changed his name from Biju Kumar to Biju K. Kumar following a legal name change. Since this is a change in the director’s name and not merely a change in contact information, the appropriate form is DIR-6. Directors should not postpone updating their particulars. The applicable deadlines depend on the nature of the compliance. DIR-3 KYC Web Filing Deadline Periodic KYC must be completed once every three consecutive financial years, by 30 June of the immediately following third financial year. Changes in mobile number, email ID or residential address must be intimated within 30 days of the change. DIR-6 Filing Deadline Changes in particulars covered by DIR-6 must be intimated to MCA within 30 days of the change. The director must also intimate the company or companies in which he or she is a director within 15 days of the change. Relevant Legal Provisions Governing DIR-3 KYC and DIR-6 Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014: Governs DIR-3 KYC compliance. Rule 12 of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014: Governs intimation of changes in DIN particulars through DIR-6. The changes to the DIR-3 KYC framework were introduced through the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Amendment Rules, 2025, notified vide G.S.R. 943(E) dated 31 December 2025, effective from 31 March 2026. Key Takeaways: Choosing Between DIR-3 KYC Web and DIR-6 A DIN is a unique identification number allotted to an individual and remains associated with that individual throughout their directorship journey. Keeping the information associated with that DIN accurate is an important compliance responsibility. Remember this simple distinction: DIR-3 KYC Web: For periodic KYC and changes in mobile number, email ID and residential address. DIR-6: For changes in other personal and identity particulars recorded against the DIN.
