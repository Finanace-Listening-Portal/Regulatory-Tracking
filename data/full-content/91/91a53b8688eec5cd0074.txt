NOTICE NoticeNo. 20260601-37 NoticeDate 01Jun2026 Category CircularsListedCompanies Segment SSE Department DCS-Listing Subject MCANotificationsrelatingtoCorporateSocialResponsibility(CSR) andSocialStockExchange Attachments PressRelease_PressInformationBureau;GSR416;GSR415 To, All Listed Entities All Not-for-Profit Organizations All Market Participants This is to inform all listed entities, not-for-profit organizations, market participants, and the public at large that the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (“MCA”) has issued the following notifications dated May 27, 2026: • G.S.R. 415(E) – Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Amendment Rules, 2026 • G.S.R. 416(E) – Amendment to Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013 (collectively referred to as the “Notifications”). Pursuant to the aforesaid MCA Notifications, subscription to Zero Coupon Zero Principal (“ZCZP”) instruments on Social Stock Exchange by companies has been added in the list of activities which may be included by the companies in their Corporate Social Responsibility (“CSR”) Policy (Schedule VII to the Companies Act, 2013). All listed entities and other stakeholders are advised to take note of the above amendments and align their CSR Policy to take benefit of the Social Stock Exchange Platform for discharging their CSR obligations. For details, stakeholders are advised to refer to the attached Notifications. The Press Release issued by the Press Information Bureau is also attached for ready reference. This Circular is disseminated for information purposes. In case of any inconsistency between this Circular and the aforesaid Notifications, the provisions of the Notifications shall prevail.
