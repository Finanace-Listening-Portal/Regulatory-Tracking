(Supreme Court of India) Summary: The Supreme Court of India, in Kapil Arora & Ors. Vs Union of India & Ors., Special Leave Petition (Civil) No. 34425/2026, along with connected Special Leave Petition (Civil) Nos. 35154–35158/2026, passed an order on 5 October 2026 dismissing the petitions challenging the judgments and orders of the Delhi High Court. The lead petition arose from the Delhi High Court’s judgment dated 5 August 2026 in W.P.(C) No. 2268/2026. The matters were heard together by a Supreme Court Bench comprising Justice Aravind Kumar and Justice Vipul M. Pancholi. The petitioners had approached the Supreme Court through special leave petitions seeking interference with the High Court’s decisions. The proceedings also included applications seeking exemption from filing certified copies of the impugned judgments, numbered IA Nos. 299095/2026 and 306132/2026. After hearing the matter, the Supreme Court recorded that it found no grounds to interfere with the judgments and orders of the High Court. Accordingly, all the special leave petitions were dismissed. The Court further directed that any pending applications would stand disposed of. The Supreme Court’s brief order does not identify the underlying statutory dispute, the grounds raised by the petitioners, or the reasoning adopted by the Delhi High Court. The dismissal therefore establishes that the Supreme Court declined to interfere in these proceedings; it does not, by itself, provide an independent determination of the underlying legal questions or an express endorsement of every reason given by the High Court.
