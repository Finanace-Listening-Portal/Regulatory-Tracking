Gross Stage 3 assets increased four basis points to 1.35%, while net Stage 3 assets rose two basis points to 0.9%. The rise in early-stage delinquencies comes as the lender continues to expand its home loan and loan-against-property portfolios.Credit costs, however, declined sequentially to around 30 basis points from 41 basis points in the previous quarter, but remained above the company's FY27 guidance of around 25 basis points. The credit cost was also six basis points higher than the estimate of JM Financial, according to the brokerage's report. Advt Stage 2 assets have increased around 55 basis points over the first half of FY27, while gross Stage 3 assets have risen around 26 basis points over the same period. However, gross Stage 3 assets remained 12 basis points lower year on year.Collection efficiency stood at 99%, while the share of accounts overdue by more than one day remained broadly stable at around 7%. Bounce rates were also stable. The provision coverage ratio stood at around 34.5%, while coverage against Stage 2 assets was approximately 13.5%.GNPAs stableGross non-performing assets in the home loan portfolio stood at around 1%, compared with around 2% in the non-home loan portfolio. The respective levels were broadly stable year on year, against 1.2% and 2.01% a year earlier.The lender has increased the share of non-home loans in disbursements to 26% in the September quarter from 24% in the previous quarter, following tighter underwriting in the March quarter. It aims to raise this share to around 28% by the March quarter of FY27 and is targeting a 70:30 mix between home and non-home loans in its assets under management by FY28.Aadhar Housing Finance expects to maintain assets under management and profit growth of around 20% in FY27. It has guided for gross Stage 3 assets to decline to 1.10-1.15% by the end of the financial year. The company has said it does not see geography-specific stress. Of the 88 high-priority districts identified for monitoring, 30 are considered vulnerable to El Nino conditions and account for around 5-6% of its AUM. Advt The rise in early-stage delinquencies and the planned expansion of non-home lending will remain important indicators to monitor as the lender seeks to sustain growth while bringing gross non-performing assets down towards its year-end guidance.
