Summary: Ethereum Classic is a separate proof of work smart contract network whose native asset is ETC. Its compatibility with the Ethereum Virtual Machine does not make ETC interchangeable with ETH, nor does a familiar address format remove network-selection risk. This guide explains the chain identity, mining and monetary design, operational risks and the Indian tax and compliance questions that arise when ETC is transferred. It is a factual reference, not a price forecast or a recommendation to acquire or trade ETC. Its native unit is ETC. Ethereum, whose native unit is ETH, followed a different chain history after the split and later moved to proof of stake. The two assets therefore represent different networks, consensus arrangements and economic systems. An exchange balance labelled ETC, an ETC address and an ETH balance should not be treated as interchangeable merely because both networks use EVM-style accounts and familiar hexadecimal addresses. The network supports smart contracts through Ethereum Virtual Machine compatibility. That makes some Ethereum tooling and contract concepts familiar, but compatibility is not equivalence. A contract deployed on one chain does not automatically exist on the other; token contract addresses, liquidity, oracles, bridges and application governance can differ. A user should identify the chain ID, asset symbol, destination support and contract address before approving a transfer or contract interaction. Nodes independently verify the blocks and follow the valid chain with the greatest accumulated proof of work. Confirmation depth is therefore a risk-control judgment, not an absolute guarantee of irreversible settlement. A competing branch with greater accumulated work can reorganise recent blocks. Exchanges and other receivers may apply different confirmation requirements after considering transaction value, current network conditions and their own risk policies. A proof of work chain can face majority-hash-power or related reorganisation risk when an attacker can marshal enough computational power relative to honest miners. Ethereum Classic has previously experienced deep reorganisations, which makes confirmation policy and deposit controls especially material. A reorganisation can reverse a recent receipt or enable double-spend attempts, although it does not by itself reveal a properly secured private key or allow arbitrary creation of valid signatures. Users should distinguish chain-level settlement risk from wallet compromise, exchange insolvency and smart-contract vulnerability. Nodes miners and holders perform different roles A full node applies the consensus rules and verifies blocks; a miner proposes blocks and supplies proof of work; a holder controls ETC only to the extent that the relevant private keys or enforceable custodial claim are controlled. Running a node can improve independent verification but does not eliminate device, key-management or network-availability risk. Mining adds hardware, electricity, pool-credit and business risks. Holding ETC through a centralised venue adds counterparty, withdrawal and account-control risks that do not arise in exactly the same way with self-custody. ETC Monetary Policy and Supply Claims Ethereum Classic protocol documentation identifies a fixed emission curve under ECIP-1017. The block reward is reduced by 20 percent every five million blocks, an event sometimes called a fifthening. Project documentation describes an approximate terminal supply of 210.7 million ETC. The word approximate matters because the precise aggregate depends on block-level rules, historical issuance and rounding. The supply schedule is a protocol characteristic, not a promise of purchasing power, liquidity, network security or market demand. A declining issuance rate does not create a guaranteed return. Market value can be affected by use of the chain, mining economics, security perceptions, exchange availability, regulation, competing networks and broad risk appetite. The scheduled reward reduction may also affect miner revenue. If fees and market value do not compensate miners, hash power could change. That relationship is dynamic and should not be converted into a deterministic price narrative. Imported keys may reveal control over corresponding addresses on more than one EVM-based chain. Moving funds with an old private key through an untrusted application can expose assets beyond the transaction the user intended. Indian VDA Tax TDS and Recordkeeping for ETC For Indian tax purposes, ETC ordinarily falls within the broad virtual digital asset framework. Income from transfer of a VDA is subject to the special 30 percent rate, with applicable surcharge and health and education cess. The acquisition cost is the principal deduction allowed in computing transfer income; other expenditure deductions and set-off or carry-forward of VDA transfer losses are restricted by the special regime. A crypto-to-crypto swap, spending ETC for goods or converting it to another asset can involve a transfer even when no rupees enter the bank account. The VDA withholding mechanism generally requires one percent tax deduction on consideration for transfer, subject to the statutory thresholds and responsibility rules. The operational result can vary between exchange transactions, peer-to-peer transfers and consideration wholly or partly in kind. A platform’s deduction does not settle the taxpayer’s final liability. The taxpayer must reconcile gross consideration, acquisition cost, TDS credit and each transfer in the return. Frequently Asked Questions Is ETC the same asset as ETH No. ETC and ETH are native assets of separate chains with different histories and consensus systems. A shared technical ancestry does not make balances interchangeable. Does Ethereum Classic still use proof of work Yes. Current project documentation describes Ethereum Classic as a proof of work smart contract blockchain using ETCHash. What is a fifthening It is the protocol-scheduled 20 percent reduction in the ETC block reward after each five-million-block interval. It changes new issuance, not previously issued balances. Is the ETC supply cap exactly 210 million Project material describes an approximate terminal supply around 210.7 million ETC. Rounding and the detailed issuance rules make precise wording important. How many confirmations make an ETC transfer final There is no universal number that eliminates all risk. Receivers set confirmation policies based on value, network conditions and their own reorganisation tolerance. Is an ETC to another token swap taxable in India A swap can be a transfer of ETC and may trigger VDA tax and TDS consequences even without a rupee withdrawal. Valuation and withholding facts should be documented. Can an ETC loss reduce salary or other crypto gains The special VDA rules restrict set-off and carry-forward of transfer losses. A taxpayer should obtain advice for the exact transaction and assessment year. Key Takeaways Ethereum Classic is a separate EVM-compatible proof of work network whose native asset is ETC.
