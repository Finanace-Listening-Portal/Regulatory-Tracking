Their combined gold loan portfolio nearly doubled year on year to about Rs 1 lakh crore, as lenders expanded secured lending amid stress in unsecured credit and microfinance. The rapid expansion is also bringing emerging signs of borrower stress into focus.Gold loans accounted for nearly 10% of the four lenders’ combined loan books, up from 9.3% in the June quarter and 6.23% a year earlier. Industry data showed that bank gold loans grew 83% year on year to Rs 5.6 lakh crore in August 2026, while NBFC gold loans increased 69% to Rs 3.6 lakh crore. Advt IIFL Finance had the largest gold loan portfolio among the four lenders at Rs 65,729 crore, equivalent to nearly 53% of its total assets under management (AUM) of Rs 1.24 lakh crore. Its gold loan book grew 90% year on year from Rs 34,577 crore in the September 2025 quarter, while the segment’s share of AUM rose from 38.4% a year earlier.Bajaj Finance’s gold loan AUM more than doubled year on year to Rs 24,195 crore from Rs 11,789 crore. It grew 14.4% sequentially in the September quarter, outpacing the company’s overall gross loan book growth of 6.4%. L&T Finance also reported a sharp increase, with growth aided by a low base and branch expansion. The company added more than 200 branches during the quarter and plans to add another 300 by year-end, taking its network to around 800.HDB Financial Services, a relatively new entrant, reported a gold loan book of Rs 2,007 crore, up from Rs 990 crore a year earlier. Gold loans accounted for 1.6% of its gross AUM of Rs 1.24 lakh crore, compared with 0.9% a year ago.Higher gold prices have increased the value of collateral available to households and small businesses, while tighter conditions in unsecured lending and microfinance have encouraged lenders to expand secured credit. Domestic gold prices fell 4.8% in September but remained broadly rangebound in October, with the World Gold Council reporting strengthening seasonal demand ahead of the festive and wedding period. Advt Stress signalsHowever, emerging signs of stress warrant attention. Piramal Finance has flagged that 15% to 23% of gold loan disbursements across the industry over the past year were reportedly extended to customers who were already more than 90 days overdue on another active loan. The share of customers holding an active gold loan with another lender has also risen to more than 35%, from 20% to 25% two years ago.Bajaj Finance has separately reported a 15 basis point sequential increase in assets classified as Stage 2 and Stage 3, with a large part of the increase coming from short-term gold loans. The lender has stopped offering these short-tenor products. While overall delinquencies in gold loans remain low, these developments point to the need for closer monitoring of borrower indebtedness and repayment capacity. Advt The expansion also exposes lenders to movements in gold prices. A sustained decline in collateral values could reduce the cushion available to lenders in the event of default. RBI directions governing loans against gold and silver collateral prescribe valuation and lending requirements to address risks associated with this form of secured credit.
