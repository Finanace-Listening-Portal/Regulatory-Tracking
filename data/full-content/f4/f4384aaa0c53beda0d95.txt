The approval from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) marks a significant milestone for the company, facilitating the introduction of 25 mg, 50 mg, and 100 mg tablets into the US market. These tablets are bioequivalent to the reference listed drug, Januvia® Tablets, developed by Merck Sharp and Dohme LLC. Sitagliptin Tablets are prescribed as an adjunct to diet and exercise to enhance glycemic control in adults diagnosed with type 2 diabetes mellitus. The reference drug, Januvia®, has reported substantial annual sales in the United States, estimated at approximately USD 2.33 billion as of September 2026, according to IQVIA data. This substantial market size underscores the potential impact of Lupin’s entry into the segment. Lupin Limited, headquartered in Mumbai, India, is renowned for its expansive footprint across multiple global markets, distributing products in over 100 countries. The company boasts a diverse portfolio that includes generics, complex generics, specialty medicines, and biosimilars. With a robust presence in the United States and India, Lupin maintains leadership in several therapeutic areas, including cardiovascular, respiratory, diabetes, gastrointestinal, and women’s health. The company’s strategic focus on sustainability and innovation is supported by 15 state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and six research centres. Lupin’s workforce of over 26,000 professionals is dedicated to expanding the healthcare ecosystem through initiatives in diagnostics, digital health, patient support, and disease management. Analysts view Lupin’s latest FDA approval as a testament to its commitment to enhancing its product offerings and strengthening its market position in the competitive pharmaceutical landscape.
