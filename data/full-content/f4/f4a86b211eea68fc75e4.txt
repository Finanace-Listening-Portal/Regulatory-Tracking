Articles 4 min read State of the economy: How India’s macro indicators performed in October 2026 The macroeconomic landscape saw inflationary pressures intensify, manufacturing activity moderate and the trade deficit widen, even as GST collections touched their second-highest monthly level on record this month. The RBI raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% and announced measures to support the rupee amid currency pressures. Here is a quick round-up of what key macroeconomic indicators showed in October 2026.
