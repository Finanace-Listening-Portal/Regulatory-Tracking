This development is aimed at enhancing the Indian Armed Forces’ capabilities and providing international customers with a robust, rapid-response mortar system. The R.A.M.S is designed to deliver swift and protected fire support in challenging terrains and adverse weather conditions, marking a crucial step in KSSL’s dedication to ensuring national security and supporting partner nations. The R.A.M.S system is engineered to allow crews to transition quickly from movement to firing, eliminating the need for dismounting and emplacement of the mortar. This capability is intended to reduce crew exposure, enhance mobility, and improve response times, providing a strategic advantage in high-altitude and extreme weather conditions. The system’s design focuses on future warfare requirements, including persistent surveillance and compressed targeting cycles, ensuring that commanders have a decisive edge in contested environments. Amit Kalyani, Vice Chairman and Joint Managing Director of Bharat Forge Ltd, emphasised the importance of the R.A.M.S programme during the festive season of Deepawali, highlighting its role in safeguarding Bharat and supporting nations facing hostile threats. He noted that the system is a testament to India’s commitment to Aatmanirbhar Bharat, showcasing indigenous design and manufacturing capabilities that bolster India’s standing as a trusted exporter of advanced defence platforms. As India celebrates the festival of lights, the advancement of the R.A.M.S programme underscores KSSL’s commitment to national security through technological innovation. The system is poised to enhance the operational readiness of the Indian Armed Forces while reinforcing India’s position as a key player in the global defence sector. Analysts view this development as a strategic move that aligns with India’s broader defence ambitions and strengthens its self-reliance in defence manufacturing.
