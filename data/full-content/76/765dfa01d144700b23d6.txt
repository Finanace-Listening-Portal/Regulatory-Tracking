Articles 1 min read CBI recovers Rs 1.86 crore in cash, gold, silver from arrested CGST superintendent's house The Central Bureau of Investigation recovered assets valued at approximately Rs 1.86 crore during recent searches. The officer was arrested for accepting a bribe of Rs 10 lakh on November 19. During searches, the CBI found cash, foreign currency, and valuable gold and silver items. The agency is investigating the officer's financial transactions and family investments further. Advt During the subsequent searches, the agency recovered Rs 24.29 lakh in Indian currency and foreign currency equivalent to around Rs 15.65 lakh. Gold jewellery, gold bullion and silver collectively valued at approximately Rs 1.46 crore were also seized, taking the total value of the recovery to around Rs 1.86 crore.The CBI also found documents relating to six bank accounts, investment portfolios, a life insurance policy and multiple immovable properties. Documents concerning a vehicle and financial transactions of the accused and his family members were also recovered, said CBI.The agency is now examining the documents to ascertain the officer's assets, investments and financial transactions, including those of his family members.
