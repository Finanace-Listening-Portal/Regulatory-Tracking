} } Listen to This Article Algo Check The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has warned brokers and empanelled algo providers against misusing static IP access by classifying ordinary retail investors as ‘tech-savvy’ clients. In a recent circular, the exchange said static IPs are meant only for investors who develop their own algorithms using programming knowledge. Brokers must obtain an undertaking from such clients confirming their eligibility and waiving recourse to the exchange’s investor grievance mechanism for losses arising from algo logic. Brokers may face a penalty of ₹5,000 per client, capped at ₹1 lakh, for failing to obtain the undertaking. Legal First The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) may see its first appeal against an enforcement order issued by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (Ifsca), marking a new addition to the tribunal’s jurisdiction, according to legal players familiar with a party preparing for the appeal. SAT typically hears appeals against orders passed by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi), the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) and the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Irdai). IFSCA has recently stepped up enforcement action against entities in GIFT City, including cases involving inadequate staffing and failure to meet minimum office requirements. Sensex Rejig Shriram Finance will replace Trent in the 30-share BSE Sensex as part of the latest index rejig announced by BSE Index Services. The changes will take effect from December 21. In the BSE Sensex 50, Divi’s Laboratories will replace Wipro. The BSE 100 will see five additions, including Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), Vedanta Aluminium, Laurus Labs, Bharat Heavy Electricals and GE Vernova T&D India.
