Articles 1 min read RBI net buyer of dollars for fourth straight month in September, data shows The Reserve Bank of India recorded net purchases of $6.94 billion in the foreign exchange market during September. It bought a total of $82.18 billion while selling $75.25 billion in currency transactions. Despite a decline in the rupee, capital inflows reached about $143 billion due to various measures. Indian equities have recently attracted investors, leading to a rise in the rupee's value. It had purchased a net amount of $43.26 billion in August.The sustained buying comes as measures aimed at capital inflows drew about $143 billion, mostly from a zero-cost hedging facility for banks' overseas FX deposits. The inflows came despite the rupee slipping 0.7% in September on elevated crude prices and surging global bond yields.
