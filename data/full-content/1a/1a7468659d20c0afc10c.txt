No. IRDAI/HLT/CIR/MISC/141/10/2026 dated 30 October 2026, has announced the migration of Health Regulatory Returns and Health Insurance Product-related functionalities to its upgraded Business Analytics Platform (BAP). The initiative aims to streamline regulatory reporting, improve integration and facilitate efficient management of health insurance product-related activities. The migration covers two major functional areas: the Health Regulatory Returns Module and the Health Product Module. Under the Health Regulatory Returns Module, all concerned insurers must submit Health Regulatory Returns for FY 2024–25 and FY 2025–26, along with RET 197 for Q1 and Q2 of FY 2026–27, except life insurers. Returns for subsequent reporting periods must also be submitted through the BAP portal in accordance with the prescribed frequency. Separately, with effect from 2 November 2026, all concerned insurers must exclusively use the Health Product Module of BAP for generating Unique Identification Numbers (UINs) for new Health Insurance Products, making modifications to existing products and submitting updates relating to product withdrawals. Consequently, the Health Department’s earlier email communication dated 10 June 2024 concerning UIN generation for Health Insurance Products will cease to apply. IRDAI has also clarified that operational guidelines and detailed instructions for both modules are available on the BAP portal for reference and compliance. The circular applies to all insurers except reinsurers, Foreign Reinsurer Branches (FRBs), Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AIC) and Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC). Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India Ref.No.: IRDAI/HLT/CIR/MISC/141/10/2026 | Date: 30th October, 2026 To, All Insurers excluding Reinsurers, FRBs, AIC and ECGC Subject: Migration of Health Regulatory Returns and Health Product UIN Generation to the Integrated Business Analytics Platform (BAP) 1. The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) has undertaken the upgradation of its Business Analytics Platform (BAP) to facilitate an integrated, streamlined and enhanced platform for regulatory reporting and Health product related functionalities. 2. As part of this initiative, functionalities related to the submission of Health Regulatory Returns, generation of Unique Identification Numbers (UINs) for new Health Insurance Products and modification to existing Health Insurance Products have been incorporated into the Health module of BAP. 3. Accordingly, the functionalities relating the submission of Health Regulatory Returns and Health Product UIN generation are now available through the BAP portal. Health Regulatory Returns module 4. All concerned insurers are required to submit the following Health Regulatory Returns through the Health module of BAP: a. Health Regulatory Returns for FY 2024– b. Health Regulatory Returns for FY 2025– c. Health Regulatory Return – RET 197 for Q1 and Q2 of FY 2026-27 (except life insurers) d. Health Regulatory Returns for subsequent reporting periods, in accordance with the prescribed frequency. Health Product module 5. All concerned insurers shall, with effect from 02nd November 2026, generate UINs for new Health Insurance Products and modifications to existing Health Insurance Products, and submit updates relating to the withdrawal of Health Insurance Products, exclusively through the Health Product Module of the BAP portal. 6. In view of the above, the Health Department’s email communication dated 10th June 2024 regarding the generation of UINs for Health Insurance Products shall cease to be applicable. 7. The operational guidelines and detailed instructions relating to the various functionalities of the Health Regulatory Returns Module and Health Product Module are available on the BAP portal for reference and compliance by all concerned users. This is issued with the approval of the Competent Authority.
