The memorandum modifies the composition specified in the earlier Office Memorandum dated 13 November 2025, while retaining the existing Terms of Reference. The reconstituted GoM comprises 12 members, with Dr. Pramod Sawant, Chief Minister of Goa, serving as Convenor. Other members represent Bihar, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, West Bengal and Uttar Pradesh. The revised composition includes senior state government representatives responsible for finance, taxation and related portfolios. The memorandum expressly provides that all other Terms of Reference governing the GoM on Analysis of Revenue from GST shall remain unchanged. Issued with the approval of the competent authority, the memorandum is signed by Rajeev Ranjan, Deputy Secretary, GST Council Secretariat. It modifies the ministerial composition of the GoM and does not itself announce any change in GST rates, exemptions or statutory compliance requirements.
