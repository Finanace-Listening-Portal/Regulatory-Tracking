Shetty has sought $5 billion each in direct and consequential damages against the defendants jointly and severally, and a further $5 billion from BoB for alleged reputational damage and loss of goodwill. He has also sought interest at 18 per cent a year. The suit names BoB and 10 of its officers, former NMC and UAE Exchange/Finablr executives Prasanth and Promoth Manghat, their wives and other associates, as well as two Indian entities — Avitis Super Speciality Hospitals and SMARD Building Solutions — which Shetty alleges hold assets acquired using diverted funds. An email seeking Bank of Baroda’s response to the lawsuit did not elicit a reply from the bank till press time. According to the lawsuit, Shetty relinquished control of the group companies in 2012 and stepped down as chief executive officer in March 2017. He alleges that he learnt only in early 2020 that he remained NMC’s chairman, following which he resigned.
