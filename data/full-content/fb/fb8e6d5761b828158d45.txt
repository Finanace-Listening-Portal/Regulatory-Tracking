Articles 1 min read IPO-bound Muthoot FinCorp Q2 profit doubles to Rs 779 cr; asset quality improves In the latest financial quarter, Muthoot FinCorp Ltd reported a remarkable profit after tax of Rs 779 crore, showing a remarkable doubling compared to the previous year. Their total income reached an impressive Rs 3,408 crore, marking a significant year-on-year growth of 71 percent. The company also saw enhanced asset quality, as Stage III loan assets decreased relative to gross loan assets. Advt Asset quality also improved, with Stage III loan assets as a proportion of gross loan assets declining to 1.07 per cent as of September 2026 from 1.41 per cent year-earlier. Net Stage III loan assets fell to 0.63 per cent from 0.76 per cent.For the six months ended September 2026, the company's PAT rose to Rs 1,485 crore from Rs 568 crore in H1 FY26. Total income increased 84 per cent to Rs 6,567 crore, compared to Rs 3,571 crore in the corresponding period last fiscal.In August, Muthoot Fincorp filed draft papers with the markets regulator SEBI to raise Rs 3,000 crore through an initial public offering (IPO) to strengthen its capital base.It is a diversified non-banking financial company, with gold loans as its flagship business. Its portfolio also includes business loans, loans against property, supply chain financing, digital loans, microfinance and housing loans, among others.
