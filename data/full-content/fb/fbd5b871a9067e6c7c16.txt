In an exchange filing on Saturday, the bank said it was evaluating the Supreme Court judgment that set aside the write-off of ₹8,415 crore worth of AT-1 bonds in March 2020 and assessing the options available to it. The bank said it had received queries from bondholders about coupon payments on its 9 per cent AT-1 bonds, issued under an information memorandum dated October 17, 2017. The coupon payment fell due on October 18, 2026. “Given that the Bank has not completed the evaluation and assessment, the Bank will communicate its decision, if any, on whether or not exercise of coupon discretion is required in respect of the AT-1 Bonds after it has completed the evaluation and assessment,” the bank said.
