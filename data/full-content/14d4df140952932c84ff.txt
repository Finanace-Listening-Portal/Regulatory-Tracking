Attention is invited to Circular Ref. IRDAI/INT/CIR/MISC/41/3/2026 dated 16th March, 2026 on "Transitional arrangements for payment of Annual Fee and issuance of Certificate of Registration (Intermediaries)" and Circular Ref. IRDAI/INT/CIR/MISC/54/4/2026 dated 07th April, 2026 relating to its applicability to Surveyors and Loss Assessors. 2. The aforesaid Circulars prescribed transitional arrangements for payment of annual fee and issuance of Certificate of Registration pending notification of the amended Regulations under the Insurance Act, 1938, as amended by the Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha (Amendment of Insurance Laws) Act, 2025. The transitional arrangements were made applicable up to 30th June, 2026. 3. Upon further examination of the matter, the Authority has decided that the transitional arrangements prescribed under the above Circulars shall continue to remain in force beyond 30th June, 2026 until the date of notification of the relevant amended Regulations governing payment of annual fee and related matters. 4. Accordingly: (i) the period specified in paragraph 4.3 of Circular Ref. IRDAI/INT/CIR/MISC/41/3/2026 dated 16th March, 2026, shall stand extended from 30th June, 2026 till 31 st August, 2026 or the date of notification of the relevant amended Regulations, whichever is earlier; and (ii) the period specified in paragraph 4.1 of Circular Ref. IRDAI/INT/CIR/MISC/54/4/2026 dated 07th April, 2026, shall also stand extended on the same terms. 5. All other provisions of the aforesaid Circulars shall remain unchanged and continue to be applicable. This Circular is issued with the approval of the Competent Authority. / Sd/- ( . / J.
