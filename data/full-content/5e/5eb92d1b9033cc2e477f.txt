Advt "This credit growth also provides an early indication of a revival in the private-corporate investment cycle, which is crucial for sustaining higher economic growth," the former RBI Governor added.The growth of private capital expenditure, which involves increased spending by corporates to establish new plants or expand existing facilities, is perceived as having the potential to boost overall economic growth.Private capex has been elusive for many years despite surveys pointing to higher capacity utilisation, forcing the government to do the heavy lifting on investments.He said the country's banking, financial services and insurance sector has to take the lead in financing large infrastructure and new-age manufacturing to support public and private asset creation."It must pivot from simple corporate lending to long-term capital formation. This would involve absorbing a significant portion of the financing needs of the national infrastructure pipeline through structured consortiums and corporate bond expansion," Das added.He also said smaller banks and financiers should look at funding the needs of individuals and small businesses in relatively smaller cities as they are closer to where the activity is, while large institutions should focus on large infrastructure projects or manufacturing facilities.He also underlined the need to keep in mind the country's strategic priorities by lending to new-age sectors like high-tech domestic manufacturing, including semiconductors, advanced electronics, and defence production. Advt As India marches towards the goal of Viksit Bharat by 2047, it is also necessary to create institutions with scale, resilience, and international presence to compete alongside the world's largest or leading banking entities, Das said.Amid concerns about a surge in household debt levels, he advocated closer monitoring of consumption-related borrowing by households.The high rate of urbanisation will require a larger number of civic bodies to access funding through the municipal bond market, Das said, making it clear that internal accruals and government transfers alone cannot help them meet financing needs. PTI Advt Shaktikanta Das says the BFSI sector must step up financing for infrastructure, high-tech manufacturing, MSMEs and agriculture to support India’s ambition of becoming a developed economy.
