The bank’s global business saw an impressive year-on-year increase of 15.88%, reaching ₹30,72,365 crore. This growth was driven by a 13.13% rise in global deposits, which totalled ₹16,97,305 crore, and a 19.46% surge in global advances, amounting to ₹13,75,060 crore. The bank’s net profit for the period rose by 8.40% year-on-year, reaching ₹5,175 crore. Operating profit also experienced a modest increase of 1.98%, standing at ₹8,758 crore. The earnings per share improved by 5%, reflecting the bank’s strong financial performance. Canara Bank’s asset quality showed significant improvement, with the gross non-performing assets (GNPA) ratio reducing to 1.33% from 2.35% in September 2025, while the net NPA ratio decreased to 0.30% from 0.54% over the same period. The bank’s retail credit portfolio expanded by 31.61% year-on-year, with notable growth in housing loans at 17.35% and vehicle loans at 25.50%. Credit to the RAM sector increased by 21.03%, reflecting the bank’s focus on retail, agriculture, and MSME segments. Furthermore, Canara Bank achieved its priority sector lending targets, with credit to small and marginal farmers, weaker sections, and micro enterprises exceeding the regulatory norms. Analysts view Canara Bank’s performance as a positive indicator of its strategic initiatives and operational efficiency. The improvement in asset quality and capital adequacy ratio, which stood at 17.19%, underscores the bank’s resilience in a competitive banking environment. The bank’s extensive network, comprising over 10,000 branches and ATMs, along with its overseas presence, positions it well to capitalise on future growth opportunities. As the banking sector continues to navigate economic challenges, Canara Bank’s robust financial health and strategic focus are expected to sustain its growth trajectory.
