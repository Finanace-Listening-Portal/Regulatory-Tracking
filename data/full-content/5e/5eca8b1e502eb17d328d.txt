Articles 1 min read Manipal Payment shares make muted market debut; down nearly 3 pc Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions shares opened lower on Thursday. The stock declined nearly three percent against its issue price. The company's initial share sale was subscribed 1.42 times last week. Manipal Payment plans to use IPO proceeds for equipment purchases. The company offers payment and identity solutions to various clients. The issue had a fresh issue of Rs 320 crore and an OFS of up to 1.43 crore shares worth Rs 485 crore. Advt Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Ltd had raised Rs 362.25 crore from anchor investors.Manipal Payment plans to use the proceeds from the fresh issue to purchase and install new and second-hand equipment across its various facilities in Manipal, Chennai, Navi Mumbai and Chhattisgarh RTO.The company provides payment, identification, secure solutions and smart tagging and Internet of Things (IoT) solutions to banks, fintechs, non-banking finance companies and governments across domestic and international markets.Its payment solutions include payment cards, cheque solutions, near-field communication (NFC) and quick-response (QR) codes, payment-enabled wearables and digital automation solutions.
