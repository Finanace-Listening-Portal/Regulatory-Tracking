Applicants can submit registration applications electronically through the GST portal under Services → Registration → New Registration. Step-by-step guidance is available through the GST Knowledge Portal, while contextual assistance, tool-tips and drop-down menus are intended to help taxpayers complete the registration process accurately. The proposed amendments to FORM GST REG-01 would introduce additional user-friendly features and clearer document requirements. The FAQs further explain that comprehensive guidelines have been issued to standardise the information and documents required for different fields of the registration application, including the constitution of business and Principal Place of Business (PPoB). These guidelines are intended to facilitate uniform and expeditious processing by Central and State tax officers. Additional clarifications regarding registration applications, supporting documents and procedural requirements are proposed through a circular. The GST registration form and portal interface would also be aligned with these clarifications, including auto-populated fields and guidance pop-ups. Regarding proof of Principal Place of Business and Additional Place of Business (APoB), the Government proposes a consolidated list of acceptable documents for different types of premises. The portal would display relevant documentary requirements according to whether the premises are owned, rented or fall under another category. The FAQs state that no additional documents should be uploaded or sought by officers beyond the prescribed requirements, subject to the proposed clarifications. These measures aim to simplify GST registration, reduce unnecessary documentation and improve predictability for applicants. Economy Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on GST registration application Posted On: 09 OCT 2026 4:57PM S. No. Question Answer 1. How can I submit an application for GST registration? You can submit the application for GST registration online on the GST portal (https://www.gst.gov.in/) through Services → Registration → New Registration. 2. Where can I find the step-by-step procedure for filing the GST registration application? A step-by-step manual/tutorial for filing the GST registration application is available on the GST portal. (https://www.gst.gov.in/) through Help and taxpayer facilities → GST Knowledge Portal → Register with GST. The application form on the portal also provides tool-tips and real-time contextual guidance to help applicants furnish the required information correctly. Further the amended GST registration FORM REG-01 will have drop down boxes and tool tips to guide you in filing the prescribed documents. 3. Whether any guidelines have been provided regarding the information/documents required to be furnished in respect of various fields in GST registration application? A comprehensive guideline has been issued to provide clear and consistent guidance on the information/documents required to be furnished in various fields of the GST registration application, such as constitution of business, Principal Place of Business, etc. They also provide guidance for the tax officers across the Centre and States for uniform and expeditious processing of registration applications. Furthermore, a detailed FAQ on various issues pertaining to GST registration application and its processing, shall be clarified through a circular The registration form will be aligned to the circular and the user interface will be enhanced for hand-holding of taxpayers through auto populated fields, drop-down menus and guidance pop-ups in respect of documents required to be submitted. 4. Is there a checklist of documents and information required for filing the GST registration application? Yes. The same shall be clarified through a circular, specifying the information and documents required for filing the registration application so that applicants can keep the required information and documents ready before submitting the application. 5. What documents are required as proof of Principal Place of Business (PPoB))? A consolidated list of documents required as proof of PPoB and APoB in different situations shall be provided in the circular and FORM GST REG-01, shall be suitably amended. Accordingly, when an applicant selects the type of premises on the GST portal, the portal will display only those documents that are relevant to that particular type of premises say owned or rented premises shall show different document requirements. No additional documents are required to be uploaded or sought by the officer in respect of the GST registration application. The same shall be clarified through a circular.
