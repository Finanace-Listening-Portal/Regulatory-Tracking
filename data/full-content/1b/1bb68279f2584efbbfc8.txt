The agreement was formalised on 27th November 2026, marking a significant collaboration between the two companies in the petrochemical sector. The MoU was signed by Sanjay Agarwal, Executive Director (Business Development, Start-up & R&D) of GAIL, and D.V. Parikh, Executive Director & Chief Financial Officer of GNFC, in the presence of R.K. Singhal, Director (Business Development) at GAIL. This partnership aims to leverage GAIL’s existing infrastructure to explore potential opportunities in the downstream petrochemical industry, thereby enhancing India’s petrochemical value chain. R.K. Singhal, Director (Business Development) at GAIL, expressed optimism about the collaboration, stating that the MoU represents a crucial step in strengthening the country’s petrochemical capabilities. He emphasised GAIL’s commitment to utilising its infrastructure to support the establishment of downstream petrochemical projects, which could contribute significantly to the sector’s growth. D.V. Parikh of GNFC echoed this sentiment, highlighting the potential of the partnership to open new avenues in the downstream petrochemical sector. The collaboration is expected to explore various opportunities, potentially leading to the development of projects that could enhance the production and supply of petrochemical products in India. Analysts view this MoU as a strategic move that could bolster the capabilities of both companies in the competitive petrochemical market. The collaboration aligns with the broader industry trend of forming strategic partnerships to drive growth and innovation in the sector. As the companies proceed with feasibility evaluations, the market will be keenly observing the outcomes and potential projects that may arise from this partnership.
