Summary: BookMyForex is a technology-enabled foreign-exchange marketplace operated by BookMyForex Private Limited, an Indian company that publishes RBI full-fledged money changer licence NDL-FFMC-0093-2023. That licence does not make the company an authorised dealer category-I bank or permit every cross-border transaction. Its outward-remittance workflow identifies RBL Bank Limited as the authorised dealer category-I institution that performs KYC and anti-money-laundering checks, executes the remittance, issues the SWIFT confirmation and provides the GST invoice; other disclosed authorised-dealer category-II partners support specified foreign-currency and card services. This profile separates the platform, the licensed money changer and the institution actually executing a transaction, and explains eligible purposes, documentation, quote-dependent costs, settlement, cancellation, grievance and Indian GST and TCS considerations without endorsing the service or any forex product. The same disclosure states that it holds RBI full-fledged money changer licence NDL-FFMC-0093-2023. A full-fledged money changer may conduct permitted money-changing activities within the RBI licence and directions, but the label is not interchangeable with an authorised dealer category-I bank. The exact activity, branch or digital channel, purpose and executing institution must therefore be checked for each order. An FFMC licence is narrower than an AD-I banking authorisation An FFMC may buy foreign currency and sell foreign exchange for permitted current-account purposes within the Money Changing Activities framework. It cannot be described as having an unrestricted mandate for capital-account transactions, speculative leveraged forex, overseas contracts for difference or remittances that RBI rules do not permit. A customer should read the order confirmation to identify whether BookMyForex is acting as the licensed money changer, a technology and referral interface, or a distributor for another authorised institution. Permitted Services, Customers and Purposes The platform advertises foreign-currency notes, prepaid forex cards and outward remittances. Availability depends on city, currency, stock, delivery capability, partner coverage and regulatory eligibility. A resident individual may use an eligible channel for a genuine permitted purpose such as education, medical treatment, travel, maintenance of relatives or another purpose accepted under FEMA and the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, subject to the current limits and documentary rules. A business, non-resident, foreign visitor or institutional customer may face a different permitted-purpose and documentation analysis. LRS is not a route for overseas margin or margin-call funding RBI’s framework does not permit a resident to use the Liberalised Remittance Scheme to remit margins or margin calls to an overseas counterparty. BookMyForex’s FFMC status, an authorised-dealer partner or a foreign broker’s overseas licence does not transform a prohibited or ineligible transaction into a permitted remittance. The platform should not be understood as offering permission to trade leveraged foreign-exchange or CFD products outside permitted Indian venues. Timestamped quote and written cancellation terms KYC, Purpose Documents and Source-of-Funds Review Documents vary by product and purpose, but identity and address evidence, PAN, contact details and payment from an acceptable bank account are baseline requirements. An outward remittance can additionally require a passport, visa, admission or fee letter, medical estimate, invoice, beneficiary bank details, relationship evidence, Form A2 or equivalent declaration, LRS declaration and source-of-funds evidence. Corporate or institutional orders can require incorporation records, board or authorised-signatory evidence, beneficial-owner information and purpose-specific contracts. A complete upload is not automatic approval The authorised institution must be satisfied about identity, beneficial ownership, sanctions and anti-money-laundering concerns, source of funds, purpose and FEMA eligibility. It may ask for clarification, reject a document, decline a transaction or return funds. Customers should not split transactions to evade thresholds, use another person’s LRS entitlement, disguise a beneficiary or route funds for an undisclosed third party. Those actions can create regulatory, banking and tax consequences beyond a delayed order. Rates, Fees, GST, TCS and Quote-Dependent Costs A displayed exchange rate is not the entire economic cost. The total can include the currency conversion spread, BookMyForex or partner service charge, convenience fee, delivery fee, correspondent or nostro-bank charge, card issuance or reload charge, GST and tax collected at source. Official terms state that some charges and cancellation deductions depend on the order type and the quoted amount. The current remittance disclosure also says nostro charges may be quote-dependent. A customer should obtain a written all-in breakdown before payment rather than infer cost from a headline rate. TCS depends on purpose, threshold, payer and current law Tax collection at source on an LRS remittance is collected under the applicable Income-tax provision by the authorised dealer at the legally prescribed rate after considering the purpose and threshold. BookMyForex’s current public fee material distinguishes education or medical purposes from other LRS remittances and notes the 1 April 2026 threshold framework. The customer’s aggregate remittances across authorised dealers matter, and declarations must be accurate. TCS is generally a tax credit reflected against the taxpayer’s PAN, not the same thing as the remittance service fee or GST. GST applies to taxable forex services under valuation rules GST can apply to money-changing or remittance services using statutory valuation rules and can also apply to separately charged service components. The GST invoice should identify the supplier and taxable components. Customers should not treat TCS, GST, conversion spread and bank charges as interchangeable labels; each has a different legal basis and accounting treatment. Rate-lock deposits and refunds follow written order terms BookMyForex’s official terms describe fixed-rate orders that may require a two per cent deposit and set cancellation consequences when the customer fails to complete payment or documentation. The terms also distinguish refunds after cancellation and provide indicative processing periods, but actual credit can depend on the payment method and bank. Before placing a fixed-rate order, the customer should verify whether the deposit is refundable, what event constitutes customer default, whether market loss can be deducted, and whether execution or delivery has already begun. A refund should ordinarily return through the documented banking trail after compliance checks. A customer should preserve the cancellation acknowledgement, refund computation and bank credit. If a beneficiary bank returns an outward remittance, deductions can arise from correspondent banks or exchange-rate changes; the original INR amount should not be assumed to be recoverable in full unless the written terms promise it. Grievance Routes and Order-Specific Escalation BookMyForex publishes customer-service and card-support contact routes. The first complaint should quote the order number, product, amount, dates, payment reference and the precise remedy sought, without sending full card credentials or one-time passwords. Where a partner bank, AD-II, card issuer or delivery partner performed the disputed act, the complaint should also go to that institution under its grievance mechanism. The invoice, application and cardholder terms help identify the responsible entity. Regulatory escalation depends on the entity and service An unresolved complaint against a regulated bank or other covered RBI-regulated entity may qualify for escalation through that entity’s nodal process and, where applicable, the RBI Integrated Ombudsman framework after the prescribed conditions are met. A commercial disagreement with the platform, an issuer dispute and a complaint about unauthorised money changing are not automatically the same regulatory case. The customer should use the route that corresponds to the licence and transaction document rather than rely only on the brand displayed on the website. BookMyForex Limitations That Should Remain Visible The FFMC licence does not authorise every remittance, capital transaction or overseas trading product. A live quote remains subject to payment, documentation, compliance and the stated validity window. Partner identity and role can vary by product, city and order; the executing authorised institution must be documented. Currency delivery, card availability and remittance settlement can be delayed by inventory, issuer, bank and holiday constraints. Rates and service charges can change; only the order-specific written quote and current terms govern. Neither platform availability nor RBI authorisation is an endorsement of a customer’s purpose, beneficiary or economic decision. Frequently Asked Questions on BookMyForex Frequently Asked Questions 1. Is BookMyForex itself an authorised dealer category-I bank? No. BookMyForex Private Limited publishes an RBI FFMC licence. Its disclosed RBL Bank arrangement identifies RBL Bank, an AD-I bank, as the institution that performs compliance checks and executes outward remittances. 2. Does an FFMC licence allow BookMyForex to offer leveraged overseas forex or CFDs to Indian residents? No. An FFMC licence concerns permitted money-changing activities. It does not authorise prohibited overseas leveraged forex, CFD access or LRS remittances for margin or margin calls. 3. Who issues the SWIFT confirmation for a remittance through the RBL arrangement? The official partner disclosure says RBL Bank executes the remittance and provides the SWIFT confirmation and GST invoice after its KYC and compliance process. 4. Are the rate and fees fixed when a customer views the website? Not necessarily. The binding economics depend on the order-specific quote, validity period, payment and documentation timing, service charges, taxes and any nostro or delivery charges. 5. What documents can an outward remittance require? Common items include PAN, passport and address evidence, beneficiary bank details, purpose documents, source-of-funds evidence and statutory declarations. The executing authorised dealer decides the final requirements. 6. Is TCS a BookMyForex service fee? No. TCS is tax collected under Income-tax law when the statutory conditions apply. It is distinct from GST, the exchange spread, platform charges and bank or correspondent fees. 7. Is a fixed-rate deposit always refundable? No universal assumption is safe. The customer must read the rate-lock and cancellation terms, including the two per cent deposit provisions, default events and possible deductions for the selected order. 8. Where should an unresolved complaint be filed? Start with BookMyForex and the regulated partner responsible for the disputed service. Use the bank, issuer or AD’s nodal and regulatory escalation route where applicable, supported by the order, invoice and payment records.
