His resignation comes amid investor concerns over a perceived conflict of interest linked to his daughter’s NBFC.Chennai-based Equitas Small Finance bank on Thursday announced the resignation of P. N. Vasudevan, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, as per the regulatory filing.In his resignation letter, Vasudevan said the decision was also intended to address investor concerns over a perceived conflict of interest arising from his daughter’s role as co-promoter of an NBFC that received a Reserve Bank of India licence in 2023. He stated that the relationship had been disclosed to the board and the RBI, and that there had been no transactions between the bank and the NBFC apart from a short-term deposit placed around two-and-a-half years ago and repaid at maturity. Advt Vasudevan said the perception of a conflict had persisted despite his explanations to investors. "Sometimes perception can be bigger than the reality," Vasudevan said in his resignation letter.The board has requested Vasudevan to continue as MD and CEO until his successor takes over. The bank’s Nomination and Remuneration Committee will identify and recommend suitable internal and external candidates for the board’s consideration. The appointment will be subject to RBI and other applicable regulatory approvals.Vasudevan has been associated with Equitas since its inception as a microfinance institution in 2007 and led its transition into a small finance bank in 2016 and is stepping down three months after his reappointment till July 2029.The bank’s stock opened today at 66.00 and closed at Rs 57.17, down 12.22 per cent from the previous close of Rs 65.13, as per the BSE. It also reported Q2 net profit of Rs 218 crore and gross NPA of 2.29 per cent.
