NOTICE NoticeNo. 20260409-3 NoticeDate 09Apr2026 Category CircularsListedCompanies Segment Equity Department ListingCompliance-Monitoring Subject MasterCircularforcompliancewiththeprovisionsoftheSEBI (LODR)Regulations,2015onnon-compliancewiththeMinimum PublicShareholding(MPS)requirements Attachments RelaxationfromtheapplicabilityofSEBIMasterCircularLODR To All Listed Companies This has reference to the SEBI circular No. HO/49/14/14(13)2026-CFD-POD2/ I/8772/2026 dated April 07, 2026, regarding relaxation from the applicability of SEBI Master Circular for compliance with the provisions of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 on non-compliance with the Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) requirements. On account of capital market volatility arising from ongoing geopolitical tensions in the Middle East, SEBI decided to grant one-time relaxation from the applicability of penal provisions under the Master Circular for listed entities whose due date for compliance with MPS requirements falls during the period from April 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The circular shall be applicable with immediate effect. The aforesaid circular is attached for reference. All listed company are requested to take note and comply accordingly.
