Articles 2 min read Stock markets decline in early trade amid rising oil prices, West Asia crisis Indian stock markets opened lower on Tuesday, with Sensex and Nifty declining. Rising crude oil prices and US-Iran hostilities impacted investor sentiment negatively. Major companies like Mahindra & Mahindra and Axis Bank saw significant drops in their share values. Asian markets showed mixed trading, while US markets remained closed for a holiday. Foreign investors purchased equities worth over Rs 280 crore on Monday.
