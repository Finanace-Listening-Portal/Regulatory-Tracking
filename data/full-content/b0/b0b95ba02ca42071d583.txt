The expansion, set to unfold over the next three to five years, will increase the company’s refined zinc capacity to 2 million tonnes per annum (MTPA) and its silver refinery capacity to 1,500 tonnes per annum (TPA). This strategic move positions Hindustan Zinc to independently meet 15% of the global zinc demand. The investment is a significant component of Vedanta Group’s broader commitment to Rajasthan, where it has already invested Rs. 1.5 lakh crore. This new initiative will further bolster the state’s mining and metals ecosystem, creating over 2 lakh jobs and supporting more than 500 micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs). The expansion is timely, addressing the ongoing global deficit in the zinc and silver markets, and underscores Hindustan Zinc’s role as India’s sole producer of zinc, lead, and silver. Hindustan Zinc’s growth trajectory has been remarkable since its acquisition by Vedanta Limited following the government’s disinvestment in 2002. The company’s zinc production has quadrupled, and its silver output has increased more than twentyfold. With the second-highest zinc reserves globally and a mine life exceeding 25 years, Hindustan Zinc is among the lowest-cost zinc producers worldwide. This expansion aligns with the rising demand for zinc, essential for industrial and infrastructure development, and silver, critical for electronics and renewable energy technologies. The company’s ambitious HZL 2.0 plan focuses on becoming a multi-metal enterprise, integrating sustainability, and investing in future-ready technologies. Hindustan Zinc is also establishing a Zinc Industrial Park in Rajasthan’s Bhilwara district, fostering innovation and providing a platform for industries across the zinc manufacturing ecosystem. The company’s commitment to sustainability is evident, with nearly 27% of its operations powered by renewable energy and a target of 70% by FY2028. As the metals and mining industry faces global challenges, Hindustan Zinc’s expansion and sustainable practices position it as a key player in supporting India’s industrial growth and energy transition.
