The company plans to develop a plotted residential project on this site, encompassing an estimated saleable area of approximately 0.7 million square feet. This strategic move is expected to bolster Godrej Properties’ presence in Indore, adding to its existing projects in the city, namely Godrej Greenview Estate and Verdania Estate, both of which have experienced robust customer demand. The Indore-Ujjain Road is recognised as one of the fastest-growing residential corridors in the city, offering convenient access to Indore International Airport, Ujjain city, and key commercial hubs. The area is further enhanced by well-established educational institutions and healthcare facilities. The ongoing expansion of the metro network, coupled with the growth of the corporate and IT sectors, has significantly strengthened Indore’s residential market. Within this dynamic environment, there is a notable increase in demand for plotted homes, as buyers increasingly seek the flexibility to design their own living spaces. Gaurav Pandey, Managing Director and CEO of Godrej Properties, expressed optimism about the venture, stating, “Indore is one of India’s most promising real estate markets, and we see significant long-term potential here. Located on Indore-Ujjain Road, the land gives us the opportunity to create a thoughtfully planned community and build on the momentum of our existing projects.” This new project marks Godrej Properties’ third development in the city, underscoring its commitment to delivering value to customers in high-growth markets. The proposed development will offer residential plots of varying sizes, complemented by planned infrastructure, recreational spaces, and premium amenities. As part of the Godrej Industries Group, Godrej Properties is renowned for its commitment to thoughtful design, sustainability, and quality. The company has been recognised globally for its excellence, ranking first in the real estate sector on the S&P Global Dow Jones Sustainability Indices and achieving a perfect score on the GRESB rankings. Analysts view this acquisition as a strategic expansion that aligns with the growing trend of plotted developments in emerging urban markets. As infrastructure and connectivity continue to improve in Indore, the demand for such residential projects is expected to rise, potentially offering significant returns for developers like Godrej Properties.
