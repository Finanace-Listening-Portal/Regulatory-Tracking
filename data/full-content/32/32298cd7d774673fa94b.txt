NFAC/2019-20/10445966. 2. The brief facts on record are that the assessee, an individual has filed his return of income for AY 2020-21, declaring a total income of Rs.3,36,240/-. Based on credible information that the assessee has purchased immovable property along with Rubi Rai and Chandra Mohan Ojha, and that payment of Rs 5 lakh was made by the assessee in cash, and that payment of Rs. 35,00,000/- was made by Chandra Mohan Ojha on behalf of the assessee, the Assessing officer had initiated proceedings under sec. 147 of the Act. In response to the notice issued under sec. 148, the assessee filed return of income declaring the same total income. However, the assessee did not give any satisfactory explanation for the source of Rs. 5,00,000/- paid by him in cash, and for the payment of Rs. 35,00,000/- paid on his behalf by Chandra Mohan Ojha before the Assessing officer. As a result, the Assessing officer made the impugned addition of Rs. 5lakh under sec. 69A of the Act, and Rs. 35 lakh as deemed gift , and disallowed the claim of deduction of Rs.1,50,000/- under sec. 80C for want of evidence, vide his order dated 11-2-2025. The said additions were also confirmed by the Ld CIT(A) in his order dated 1-1-26 (Appeal No. NFAC/2019-20/10445996). Aggrieved, this appeal has been preferred before us. 3. The Learned AR submitted that the Ld CIT(A) while confirming the impugned additions has also directed the Assessing officer to carry out certain verification. It was represented that the assessee may be afforded an opportunity to submit all evidence in his support before the Learned Assessing officer and requested that entire assessment be set aside for de novo consideration. 4. The Ld DR did not raise serious objections. 5. We have considered the rival submissions. It is seen that the assessee has given some information before the Assessing officer regarding the source for the impugned investments but failed to file supporting documents. The Assessing Officer also did not make any inquiry or verification on the submissions made. It is also noted that the Ld. CIT(A) has directed the Assessing Officer to verify the year of execution of the sale deed. In these factual scenario , we consider it fit to set aside the impugned assessment and direct the Assessing Officer to frame the assessment de novo after necessary inquiry and verification. The assessee shall produce the supporting documents and comply to the hearing notices of the Assessing Officer. 6. In the result, the appeal of the assessee is partly allowed for statistical purposes.
