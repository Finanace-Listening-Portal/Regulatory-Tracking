A joint working group of Sebi and RBI will also begin work on a common application form to streamline onboarding. The regulator is targeting a reduction in FPI onboarding time to as little as five business days, with certain FPIs from trusted jurisdictions already onboarded within five-day window. While Sebi has already digitised processes, accepted digitally signed powers of attorney and worked with the RBI to improve procedures, it sees further scope for simplification, the chairman said. Separately, know-your-customer (KYC) review cycles for applicable FPIs will be extended from December 1. Eligible non-resident Indians (NRIs), overseas citizens of India and foreign nationals can also complete KYC and re-KYC without visiting India. Separately, Sebi is reviewing the SME IPO framework and the Innovators Growth Platform, with the aim of addressing gaps and simplifying access to capital. The regulator aims to facilitate around 2,000 additional listings from the SME segment over the next few years.
