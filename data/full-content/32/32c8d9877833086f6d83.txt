Taking a business online in India has never been cheaper or easier. A consultant, a home bakery, a tuition centre or a small manufacturer can now have a website, a business email address and an online payment option running within a day. The challenge is not access but planning. Many first-time founders either overspend on tools they do not need or choose the cheapest option and pay for it later through renewals, downtime or compliance gaps. Here is a practical breakdown of what a basic online presence involves and what it costs. A sole proprietorship is the simplest route, while a partnership, LLP or private limited company offers more credibility with clients and investors. Companies and LLPs are registered through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, where you can also check whether your preferred business name is available. GST registration becomes mandatory once turnover crosses the applicable threshold, and in some cases from the first sale, for example for certain types of inter-state supply of goods. Many small businesses also register under Udyam to access MSME benefits. These steps affect your invoices, your payment gateway application and even the details you publish on your website, so it pays to sort them out before launch. Choosing and Registering a Domain Your domain name is your address online and, ideally, matches your business name. Short, easy-to-spell names work best, and it is worth avoiding hyphens and numbers that people will forget. Indian businesses usually choose between .in, .co.in and .com. A .in domain signals a local business, and the .in registry is managed by NIXI, the National Internet Exchange of India. A .com suits businesses that plan to sell abroad. Many founders register both and redirect one to the other, which also protects the brand. Domain prices vary by extension and registrar, and the first-year price is often lower than the renewal price. Always check the renewal rate before buying, because you will pay it every year after the first. Some hosting plans include a free domain for the first year when you choose a longer subscription, and registrars regularly run promotional codes for new registrations. Before you buy, it is worth comparing current Hostinger domain coupon codes for India, which Cybernews tracks along with their conditions. Most such codes apply only to new purchases and not to renewals, so read the terms carefully. Website and Hosting Costs A simple business website, with a home page, service pages, a contact form and a few blog articles, does not need expensive infrastructure. Shared hosting or managed WordPress hosting is usually enough at the start, with entry-level plans costing a few hundred rupees per month on long-term subscriptions. Website builders offer a drag-and-drop alternative for founders who do not want to deal with WordPress at all. The bigger cost is often design and content. A freelance developer might charge anything from a modest one-time fee for a template-based site to much more for custom work. If you build it yourself, budget time for writing clear service descriptions, taking decent photos and preparing pages such as a privacy policy and terms of service. An SSL certificate, which enables the padlock in the browser, is included free with most hosting plans today. Essential Digital Tools A professional email address on your own domain, such as [email protected], builds more trust than a free webmail address. Many hosting plans include basic mailboxes, while dedicated business email services cost more but add features such as shared calendars and larger storage. For payments, Indian businesses can choose between UPI collection, payment gateways and payment links. Gateways require KYC documents, a live website with clear refund and cancellation policies and, for registered entities, the relevant registration details. Add accounting software that generates GST-compliant invoices, a Google Business Profile so local customers can find you, and a password manager to keep the growing number of logins secure. Security and Data Protection A small website is still a target. Keep your content management system, themes and plugins updated, use strong unique passwords with two-factor authentication on your hosting and domain accounts, and schedule regular backups. Losing control of the domain account is one of the most damaging things that can happen to an online business, because it controls both the website and email. If you collect customer data through forms or orders, keep in mind India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Collect only what you need, explain how you use it and protect it properly. A Realistic First-Year Budget For a lean start, the essentials are a domain, hosting, a business email address and some time spent on content. Payment gateways usually charge per transaction rather than a monthly fee. Plan for renewal costs from the second year, keep invoices for all digital services for your accounts, and review your tools once a year. Starting small, with a clean domain and a fast, simple website, is usually the smartest way to build a business that can grow online.
