The amendment adds a proviso to clause 27.1.1 of Chapter IV. For specified products, market makers may fulfil their delivery obligations within an additional period of up to two working days after the applicable settlement cycle expires, subject to any conditions that may be specified. The Explanation to the proviso states that the recognised clearing corporation specifies the products in consultation with IFSCA. The circular took immediate effect. IFSCA made the amendment after receiving representations and consulting stakeholders. Stakeholders said additional time to meet delivery obligations could encourage market makers to participate more actively in the secondary market and submit more quotes, thereby improving liquidity in the order book. The circular is addressed to recognised stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories, as well as broker dealers and clearing members in the IFSC. It cites sections 12 and 13 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, read with regulation 72 of the Market Infrastructure Institutions Regulations and regulation 45 of the IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025, as its legal basis. International Financial Services Centres Authority Circular No. IFSCA-MIIT0IINX/1/2021-Capital Markets | Dated: November 25, 2026 To All the recognised Stock Exchanges in the International Financial Services Centres (IFSC) All the recognised Clearing Corporations in the IFSC All the recognised Depositories in the IFSC All Broker Dealers and Clearing Members in the IFSC Madam/Sir Sub: Amendment to the “Master Circular for recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing Corporations” Reference may be drawn to the Circular dated June 05, 2026, titled “Master Circular for recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing Corporations” (hereinafter referred to as the “Master Circular”) issued under International Financial Services Centres Authority (Market Infrastructure Institutions) Regulations, 2021 (the “MII Regulations”). 2. The Authority has received representations from stakeholders highlighting that providing additional time period to market makers to fulfil their delivery obligations would encourage greater participation by market makers in the secondary market and incentivise them to increase their quoting activities, thereby enhancing liquidity in the order book. 3. Based on the representations received and consultations held with stakeholders, the Authority has decided to amend the Master Circular to permit market makers for specified products to fulfil their delivery obligations within an additional period of up to two working days after expiry of the applicable settlement cycle, subject to such conditions as may be specified. 4. To operationalize the aforementioned, the Authority hereby amends the Master Circular, as under: a) under clause 27.1.1 of Chapter – IV of the Master Circular, the following proviso shall be added, namely: – “Provided that in case of specified products, market maker(s) may fulfil their delivery obligations within an additional period of up to two working days after the expiry of the applicable settlement cycle, subject to such conditions as may be specified. Explanation.- For the purpose of this proviso, the term ‘specified’ means specified by the recognised Clearing Corporation in consultation with the Authority.” 5. This Circular is being issued in exercise of powers conferred by Sections 12 and 13 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019, read with regulation 72 of the MII Regulations and regulation 45 of the IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025, and shall come into force with immediate effect. A copy of this Circular is available on the website of International Financial Services Centres Authority at www.ifsca.gov.in at “Legal Framework → Circulars”.
