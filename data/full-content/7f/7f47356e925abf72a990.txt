The Central Government exercised its powers under Schedule III, Table Serial No. 36, read with section 11 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. The notification, issued by the Central Board of Direct Taxes under the Ministry of Finance, applies to four tax years: 2026-27, 2027-28, 2028-29 and 2029-30. It covers government grants, levies received from airlines, amounts arising from forfeiture of bank guarantees when airlines fail to fulfil their obligations, and interest income earned on these three sources. The exemption is conditional. The Trust must refrain from engaging in any commercial activity and must file its return of income in accordance with section 263(9)(c)(xiii) of the Act. Its activities and the nature of the specified income must also remain unchanged throughout the covered tax years. These requirements are set out in paragraph 2 of the notification. Paragraph 3 provides that failure to comply with those conditions shall result in withdrawal of the exemption under Schedule III, Table Serial No. 36, read with section 11, and initiation of proceedings under the Income-tax Act, 2025. MINISTRY OF FINANCE Department of Revenue (CENTRAL BOARD OF DIRECT TAXES) Notification No. 152/2026 | Dated: 23rd November, 2026 S.O. 6349(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Schedule III [Table: Sl. No. 36] read with section 11 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025), (hereinafter referred to as the said Act), the Central Government hereby notifies for the purposes of the said clause, ‘Regional Air Connectivity Fund Trust’ (PAN : AADTR1130P), a Trust constituted by Central Government, in respect of the following specified income arising to the said trust namely:- (a) grant from Government; (b) receipt of Levy from Airlines; (c) forfeiture of Bank guarantee due to non-fulfillment of obligation by Airlines; and (d) interest income earned on (a) to (c) above. 2. This notification shall be effective subject to the conditions that ‘Regional Air Connectivity Fund Trust (PAN : AADTR1130P)’- (a) shall not engage in any commercial activity; (b) shall file return of income in accordance with the provision of section 263(9)(c)(xiii) of the said Act; and. (c) activities and the nature of the specified income shall remain unchanged throughout the tax years. 3. Failure to comply with the conditions specified in paragraph 2 shall result in withdrawal of exemption under Schedule III [Table: Sl. No. 36] read with section 11 and initiation of proceedings under the said Act. 4. This notification shall be applicable for the tax years 2026-27, 2027-28, 2028-29 and 2029-30. [No. 152 /2026 F. No. 300196/10/2025-ITA-I] HARDEV SINGH, Under Secy.
