During the day, it jumped 613.46 points, or 0.73 per cent, to 84,551.74.The 50-share NSE Nifty rose 65.25 points, or 0.25 per cent, to end at 25,884.50.From the Sensex pack, HCL Tech jumped 6.28 per cent followed by Tata Consultancy Services 4.97 per cent, Infosys 4.70 per cent, Tech Mahindra 3.33 per cent, HDFC Bank 2.70 per cent, and Tata Steel 2.67 per cent. Advt Asian Paints, Bajaj Finance, Bajaj Finserv and Trent were among the laggards.Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) bought equities worth Rs 4,479.08 crore on Wednesday, according to exchange data.Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, declined 0.79 per cent to USD 93.86 per barrel.The BSE Sensex has jumped 9,869.06 points, or 13.25 per cent in November so far, and the Nifty has rallied 2,831.4 points, or 12.28 per cent."Indian equities recovered from early losses in a volatile session, as sustained foreign inflows and selective buying outweighed firm crude and the proposed US tariffs. IT led the gains, tracking a rebound in Asian tech stocks after weeks of selling," Vinod Nair, Head of Research, Geojit Investments Ltd, said.In Asian markets, South Korea's Kospi, Japan's Nikkei 225, Shanghai's SSE Composite index and Hong Kong's Hang Seng index rebounded after Wednesday's sharp decline.Markets in Europe were trading significantly higher."Indian equities ended slightly higher on Thursday after a volatile session, with early gains tapering as uncertainty over the India-US trade agreement and firm US inflation data tempered risk appetite. The benchmarks oscillated between gains and losses through the session, reflecting a cautious approach amid renewed focus on global interest-rate expectations and the possibility of further Federal Reserve tightening in December," Ponmudi R, CEO of Enrich Money, an online trading and wealth tech firm, said. Advt Meanwhile, the US on Wednesday proposed 25 per cent tariffs on imports from India, with a provision to cap them at 17 per cent if the two countries agree to a bilateral trade deal, giving it a slight edge over Asian competitors.The US equity markets ended mostly lower on Wednesday. The Nasdaq Composite was down 0.62 per cent and S&P 500 declined 0.25 per cent, while Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed 0.33 per cent.On Wednesday, the Sensex jumped 1,502.29 points or 1.82 per cent, to settle at 83,938.28. The Nifty surged 469.60 points, or 1.85 per cent, to end at 25,819.25. PTI PTI Published On Nov 26, 2026 at 05:03 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
