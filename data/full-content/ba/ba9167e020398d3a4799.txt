Intraday, it rallied 602 points or 2.37 per cent, to 25,951.65. Advt From the Sensex pack, Hindustan Unilever soared 5.94 per cent followed by Trent 3.59 per cent, ICICI Bank 3.44 per cent, InterGlobe Aviation 3.24 per cent, HDFC Bank 3.18 per cent and Bajaj Finance 3.04 per cent.Tata Steel, Bharti Airtel and Titan ended lower.Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was down 0.19 per cent to USD 91.38 per barrel.Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) bought equities worth Rs 5,672.99 crore on Monday, according to exchange data.Stock markets were closed on Tuesday on account of Prakash Gurpurb Sri Guru Nanak Dev.In Asian markets, South Korea's Kospi tumbled 6.16 per cent, Japan's Nikkei 225 was down 2.78 per cent, Shanghai's SSE Composite index declined 2.03 per cent, and Hong Kong's Hang Seng index dipped 1.14 per cent."Bulls returned with renewed vigour after the market holiday, with domestic equities shrugging off weak Asian cues and subdued US futures ahead of the US PCE inflation data, even as hawkish Fed commentary reinforced expectations of a December rate hike. India continued to benefit as global investors rotated out of crowded AI-linked trades," Vinod Nair, Head of Research, Geojit Investments Ltd, said.Markets in Europe were trading marginally higher.The US equity markets ended mostly lower on Tuesday, with the Nasdaq Composite dropping 1.48 per cent and S&P 500 declining 0.50 per cent. The Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed 0.85 per cent. Advt On Monday, the Sensex tanked 1,483.72 points, or 1.77 per cent, to settle at 82,435.99. The Nifty tumbled 450.95 points, or 1.75 per cent, to end at 25,349.65.
