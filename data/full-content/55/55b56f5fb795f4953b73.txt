Articles 1 min read Global Investor Carlyle Asia Partners completes Rs 600 Cr transaction in Edelweiss’s Nido Home Finance; purchases 45% stake The global Investment firm’s Asia Partners arm has closed the transaction, first announced on February 10, with primary capital infusion set to nearly triple Nido’s net worth within 18 months. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Sep 25, 2026 at 08:02 PM IST var news_msid = 134487779; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai-based financial services company, Edelweiss announced on Friday that global investment firm, Carlyle Asia Partners have completed transaction of Rs 600 crore, acquiring 45% stake of Nido Home Finance Limited, the housing finance arm of Edelweiss.The transaction marks the completion of notification, announced on February 10, 2026 subject to the receipt of the requisite regulatory and statutory approvals.The transaction comprises of a primary capital infusion of Rs 1,450 crore and secondary purchase of a 45% stake from Edelweiss, valued at Rs 600 crore which has been completed, today.Separately, the primary infusion stands at approximately Rs 1,450 crore, of which around Rs 725 crore has already been received by Edelweiss, with the remaining balance to come over the next 18 months, which will lead to Nido’s net worth expand three times to Rs 2,300 crore. Advt With the completion of the transaction, Edelweiss will hold approximately 26% of Nido on a fully diluted basis and will receive a share of the upside subject to Carlyle realizing returns above a specified threshold.With a strong presence in India, with records across YES Bank, SBI Card and SBI Life, the transaction is lead by Sunil Kaul, Partner and Asia Financial Services Sector Lead, Carlyle, and Aditya Puri, Senior Advisor to Carlyle and the former Managing Director of HDFC Bank.
