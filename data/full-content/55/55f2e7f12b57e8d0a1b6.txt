The financial services group’s consolidated revenue rose 16.7 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹43,416 crore during the quarter from ₹37,215 crore in Q2FY26. Interest income rose nearly 21.56 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹23,824.03 crore from last year. Total expenses grew 14.5 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹34,811.80 crore in the quarter. For the first half of FY27 (H1FY27), the company’s consolidated revenue increased 17.8 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹85,453 crore, while consolidated net profit rose 21.6 per cent to ₹12,249 crore. Its assets under management (AUM) grew 26.5 per cent to ₹5.85 trillion as of September 30, 2026. Meanwhile, Bajaj Finance’s net total income was up by 23 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹15,974 crore from ₹12,982 crore in Q2FY26. It also booked 1.35 crore new loans during the quarter against 1.22 crore in the year-ago period, and added 4.42 million new customers to its franchise.
