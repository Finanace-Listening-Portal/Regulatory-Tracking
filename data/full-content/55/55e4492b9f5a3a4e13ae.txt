Articles 6 min read Fintech IPO pipeline crosses ₹30,000 crore as PhonePe, Razorpay, Navi line up for public markets Moneyview’s ₹1,092-crore issue kicks off the latest fintech listing cycle, with PhonePe, Razorpay, Navi, InCred, InsuranceDekho, Fibe and Upstox among companies preparing to tap the public markets, while Groww, Pine Labs, MobiKwik and PB Fintech offer a benchmark from the previous wave. The pipeline is led by some of the country’s largest privately held fintech names like PhonePe, Razorpay, Navi and InCred Holdings, alongside digital lenders and financial platforms including Fibe, InsuranceDekho and Upstox, highlighted data by Tracxn, which the company gathered using external research.The estimate excludes KreditBee and PayU India from the active pipeline because their plans are either unconfirmed or on hold. It also excludes any valuation for companies where no credible figure has been disclosed. Advt The immediate trigger is Moneyview, which fixed a ₹32–34 price band for a ₹1,091.68 crore IPO, comprising a ₹750 crore fresh issue and ₹341.68 crore offer for sale. At the upper end, the company seeked a valuation of about ₹5,985 crore, materially below its earlier private-market unicorn valuation. PhonePe, Razorpay account for the biggest chunk of the pipelinePhonePe is potentially the largest single fintech offering in the pipeline. The proposed issue is at around ₹11,000–12,000 crore, entirely through an offer for sale. Around 5.06 crore shares are expected to be sold, with Walmart looking to pare its stake while Tiger Global and Microsoft are reported to be exiting, Tracxn data revealed. The valuation, however, has shifted sharply during the IPO process. Earlier plans had indicated a valuation of around $15 billion, while media reported in March that the company was discussing a valuation of around $9–10.5 billion when the listing process was paused. PhonePe has since been reported to be restarting preparations.Razorpay is the other large payments fintech approaching the public markets. The company confidentially filed its DRHP in June, with market estimates putting the issue at ₹5,000–6,000 crore. The offering is expected to contain both a fresh issue and an OFS, although the split has not been disclosed. Media estimates have placed its potential valuation at around ₹50,000–60,000 crore, below the company’s earlier private-market peak valuation. Advt Together, PhonePe and Razorpay could therefore account for roughly ₹16,000–18,000 crore, or more than half of the currently quantified fintech IPO pipeline, Tracxn data pointed.Navi, InCred, InsuranceDekho add another ₹9,000–10,000 croreThe next layer of the pipeline includes Navi, InCred Holdings, InsuranceDekho and Upstox.Sachin Bansal’s Navi is reviving its earlier IPO plan, with a proposed issue of up to ₹3,000 crore. Current plans indicate a primary issue rather than an OFS, with the company targeting a valuation of as much as $2 billion. Navi is looking at a prospectus filing by December, although the final issue size, valuation and timing remain subject to change. Advt InCred Holdings has already filed an updated DRHP. Its proposed issue is estimated at ₹3,000-4,000 crore, comprising a ₹1,250 crore fresh issue and an OFS of up to 9.9 crore shares. The company is targeting a valuation of about ₹15,000 crore.InsuranceDekho, meanwhile, has appointed banks for a potential IPO targeting as much as $400 million, or roughly ₹3,400 crore at current exchange rates. The structure is expected to include a larger fresh issue along with an investor exit through an OFS, although the final split and valuation are not fixed.Upstox is at an earlier stage, with investment banks being approached and a potential issue of about $400 million, or roughly ₹3,400 crore, being discussed. Its fresh-issue/OFS split and valuation remain undisclosed. Advt Fibe and Moneyview show a different end of the IPO spectrumWhile PhonePe and Razorpay represent the large-scale payments platforms, the pipeline also has a strong digital-lending component.Fibe, operated by Social Worth Technologies, has received SEBI’s final observations for a proposed ₹750 crore fresh issue, along with an OFS of around 4.01 crore shares by existing investors. The company has not yet announced a price band or issue dates. The fresh capital is intended largely to strengthen the capital base of its lending subsidiary and support onward lending.That makes the Fibe IPO important for a different reason: unlike an entirely secondary-led transaction, a substantial part of the money raised will go into the operating business.Moneyview, meanwhile, is the first major test of how fintech valuations have reset since the funding boom. Its ₹5,985–6,000 crore IPO valuation is below its earlier private valuation of more than $1 billion, illustrating the gap that can emerge between late-stage private-market marks and public-market pricing.On the basis of disclosed or estimated issue sizes, the active pipeline works out to approximately ₹30,600–33,600 crore, including Fibe's ₹750 crore fresh issue but excluding the value of its OFS. The upper/lower range is primarily driven by the still-fluid estimates for PhonePe, Razorpay and InCred.Previous fintech IPO wave: nearly ₹40,000 crore from eight major listingsThe current pipeline follows a substantial first wave of fintech and new-age financial listings.Among the major completed IPOs in the Tracxn dataset are Paytm, PB Fintech, Fino Payments Bank, Angel One, Digit Insurance, MobiKwik, Groww and Pine Labs. Together, their IPOs raised approximately ₹39,500 crore, based on their reported issue sizes.The contrast between the two cycles is notable. Paytm's ₹18,300 crore IPO remains the largest fintech issue in the Tracxn 2020–26 dataset, while Groww's ₹6,632 crore issue and PB Fintech's ₹5,710 crore offering are also ranked among the largest. Paytm's IPO was priced at ₹2,150 and opened at ₹1,950, while PB Fintech listed at ₹1,150 against its ₹980 issue price.Groww's ₹6,632 crore issue consisted predominantly of an OFS, ₹5,572 crore versus ₹1,060 crore fresh capital, and listed at ₹112 on NSE and ₹114 on BSE against its ₹100 issue price. Pine Labs similarly had a roughly balanced structure, with ₹2,080 crore of fresh capital and ₹1,820 crore of OFS, and listed at ₹242 against an issue price of ₹221.MobiKwik's ₹572 crore issue was entirely fresh capital and saw one of the strongest recent fintech debuts, with the stock listing at ₹440 on NSE against an issue price of ₹279. Digit's ₹2,614.65 crore IPO had ₹1,125 crore of fresh issue and ₹1,489.65 crore of OFS; it listed at ₹286 on NSE against the ₹272 issue price.The earlier generation also shows how sharply debut outcomes can differ. Angel One, despite being a major digital brokerage listing, debuted at ₹275 against its ₹306 issue price. Fino Payments Bank had a ₹1,200.29 crore issue, split between ₹300 crore fresh capital and ₹900.29 crore OFS.The current pipeline therefore represents not just another fundraising cycle but a broader test of how public markets are pricing fintech businesses after the 2021–22 private funding boom. The pipeline now spans payments, digital lending, insurance distribution, broking and diversified financial services with issue structures ranging from largely secondary exits to fresh-capital-heavy offerings.For investors, the key data point will be the gap between the private valuation carried into an IPO process and the valuation ultimately accepted by public markets. Moneyview has already demonstrated that this gap can be substantial, while PhonePe, Razorpay, Navi and InCred will provide the next major benchmarks for how India's public markets value scaled fintech franchises.
