} } With the festive season approaching and rail reservations at their yearly peak, redRail, the IRCTC-authorised train ticket platform from redBus, has shared simple guidance to help travellers read their PNR details correctly. The focus is on the two updates shown on every ticket and what each one really means.During the festive months, many people reserve berths on busy routes and end up waitlisted. They look up the record several times a day. Yet many are not sure which of the two entries tells them whether they can actually travel.What Is a PNR?PNR stands for Passenger Name Record. It is a 10-digit number given with every rail reservation.PNR status holds key details such as the train number, travel date, class, boarding point and passenger names. It also lists the position of each person on the booking.Booking Status: The Starting PointThis number shows where a ticket stood at the time it was bought. If a passenger got waitlist number 32, it will read WL 32.This number never changes. It simply works as a record of the traveller's place on the day of purchase.Current Status: The Latest UpdateThis entry reflects the position today. It changes as others cancel their journeys and berths become free.For example, an entry that started at WL 32 may move to RAC 4 and finally to CNF, with a coach and berth number such as B3 41. In simple terms, the first tells you the starting point, while the second tells you where things stand now.Common Reservation CodesCNF means the seat or berth is confirmed. RAC allows passengers to board and share a side lower berth until a full one becomes free.WL means the passenger is on the waitlist. General Waitlist (GNWL) tickets usually have better chances of confirmation, while Remote Location (RLWL) and Pooled Quota (PQWL) lists tend to move more slowly.Why Chart Preparation MattersThe final position is fixed when the railway prepares the reservation chart a few hours before departure. After this step, nothing changes.If an online ticket is still fully waitlisted once the chart is ready, it is cancelled automatically, and the fare is returned as per railway rules. Such travellers should not board the train.More Ways to Get a Confirmed SeatUsers can track their reservation and follow live running updates on the redRail app and website. When a booking looks unlikely to clear, a few tools help.The redRail Confirm feature finds confirmed seats on the same train from a different starting station. For example, someone going from Pune to Bengaluru may get a seat booked from Mumbai CST while still boarding at Pune. The fare is charged from the booked station.When direct services are full, the Connecting Trains option lists two services with open seats, linked through a common junction. This gives travellers another way to reach their destination on time.Confirm or 3X RefundUsers can add the Confirm or 3X Refund option, formerly known as Seat Guarantee and now called Alternate Trip, while reserving on selected trains. If the passenger stays fully waitlisted after chart preparation, they receive a 3X refund benefit.This benefit can be used to book another train or a bus. Terms and conditions apply.Free Cancellation and Easy BookingFor those whose plans may change, a free cancellation service is available on selected trains and classes. It lets passengers cancel within the eligible time and get a full fare refund without railway cancellation charges.Users can log in using their existing IRCTC user ID and password. Festive offers, such as ₹100 off on rail tickets for logged-in users, and other coupon codes may be applied at checkout where available.Support When Passengers Need ItYou can raise questions about journey details, refunds, or reservation updates with the dedicated customer care team. Support is offered 24×7 in multiple languages, including English, Hindi, Kannada, Telugu and Tamil, with plans to add more languages in the future.Simple Tips Before TravellingPassengers should always go by the current status, not the original booking one. They should check the PNR again after the chart is prepared and carry a valid photo ID during the journey.About redBusredBus is one of India's leading online bus ticketing platforms. Through redRail, its IRCTC-authorised rail service, the company allows users to book train tickets, check PNR updates, track live running status and manage cancellations and refunds through its app and website.
