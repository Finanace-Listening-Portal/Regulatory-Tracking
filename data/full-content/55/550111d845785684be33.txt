Malhotra noted that workers in India’s MSME sector lagged behind their counterparts in other global economies in terms of productivity, which could be improved through the adoption of AI and newer technologies. “It is estimated that the productivity, that is, value added per worker of Indian MSMEs is 26% of that of large enterprises, which is much lower than that in advanced countries and even in Brazil and Mexico. If our MSMEs raised their productivity to the level of top-quartile firms in their sub-sectors in advanced economies, the gain would be equivalent to 11% of GDP. This is a prize too large to ignore,” said Malhotra.
