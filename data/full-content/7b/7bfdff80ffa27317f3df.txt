The company unveiled India’s first Super Hi-Speed BLDC+ Fan, the Silent Air Max with dual control, and the Agentic AI Fan, marking a new era in fan technology driven by performance, energy efficiency, and smart features. Traditionally, the Indian fan market has been dominated by induction fans, known for their high-speed performance but higher energy consumption. Havells’ new offerings aim to bridge the gap between speed and energy efficiency. The Super Hi-Speed BLDC+ Fan introduces a groundbreaking sixth speed level at 385 RPM and an Ultra Boost seventh speed at 400 RPM, offering high-speed performance without compromising on energy efficiency. The fan also features an innovative AC Mode for optimal air circulation, reduced noise, and energy savings of up to 65%. In addition to the Super Hi-Speed model, Havells introduced the Silent Air Max BLDC+ ceiling fan, designed for consumers seeking a quieter environment. Operating at a noise level of just 50 dB, this fan offers dual control through a regulator and remote, ensuring powerful air delivery and aesthetic appeal. It also includes features such as reverse rotation, sleep mode, and a 5-star energy rating. The company’s innovation extends to the Agentic AI Fan, which incorporates conversational AI technology, allowing interaction in over 10 Indian languages. This fan is designed to understand natural conversations, providing a more intuitive user experience. It also offers various modes, including boost, breeze, mop, and sleep, alongside significant energy savings. Kulbhushan Bhardwaj, Senior Vice President of Sales at Havells Fans, highlighted the company’s commitment to addressing consumer challenges through technology. He stated that these new products signify a shift towards more intelligent, efficient, and user-friendly solutions in the fan industry. The new fan range will be available across Havells’ retail and authorised channels, offering consumers a variety of models, colours, and sizes. Analysts suggest that Havells’ focus on innovation and energy efficiency could set a new standard in the Indian fan market, potentially influencing consumer preferences and industry trends.
