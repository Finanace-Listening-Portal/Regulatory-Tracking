The board approved the unaudited standalone and consolidated financial results for the second quarter and half-year ending 30th September 2026. These results were prepared in compliance with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. In a significant move, the board declared an interim dividend of 325%, translating to Rs. 6.50 per equity share with a face value of Rs. 2 each, fully paid up, for the financial year 2026-27. This dividend will be distributed to shareholders whose names appear on the company’s register as of 29th October 2026, with the payout scheduled before 20th November 2026. The decision underscores Shriram Finance’s commitment to delivering shareholder value amidst a challenging economic landscape. Additionally, the board approved a periodical resource mobilisation plan, which includes the issuance of redeemable non-convertible debentures and subordinated debentures on a private placement basis. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to raise funds for business operations from 1st November 2026 to 31st January 2027. The company has also sought member approval via a postal ballot for renewing the limit to issue debentures up to Rs. 35,000 crore. In a strategic leadership appointment, Mr. Panakala Raju M has been appointed as the Chief Software Officer, effective immediately. Mr. Raju brings nearly three decades of experience in the IT industry, with a strong background in technology architecture and enterprise solutions. His expertise is expected to bolster Shriram Finance’s technological capabilities, aligning with the company’s strategic vision. Market analysts view Shriram Finance’s decisions as proactive steps to strengthen its financial position and enhance operational efficiency. The interim dividend declaration reflects robust financial health, while the planned debt issuance is seen as a strategic move to leverage growth opportunities in both domestic and international markets.
