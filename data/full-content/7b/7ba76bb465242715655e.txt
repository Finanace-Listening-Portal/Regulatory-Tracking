The Board of Directors approved these results during a meeting on 4 November 2026. The financial statements have undergone a limited review by the company’s statutory auditors and will be published in accordance with SEBI regulations. Additionally, these results will be accessible on the company’s official website. In a significant development, the company announced changes in its senior management. Dr. Madhu Sasidhar, who served as the President & CEO of the Hospitals Division, will step down from his position after the successful completion of his tenure on 14 November 2026. Dr. Sasidhar has been instrumental in enhancing clinical excellence and advancing the company’s digital health capabilities during his tenure. To ensure continuity and strengthen leadership, the company has appointed Dr. Ritu Garg as the Chief Executive Officer for Multi Regions, covering the North, Central, Western, and Eastern regions. Dr. Garg brings over two decades of experience in healthcare operations, growth, and innovation. Additionally, Mr. Anand Wasker has been appointed as the Chief Business Officer for International operations, and Dr. N. Venkatesan will serve as the Chief Procurement Officer, each bringing extensive experience in their respective fields. Furthermore, Apollo Hospitals has completed a strategic divestment involving its subsidiary, Apollo Health and Lifestyle Limited (AHLL). The subsidiary has divested its entire shareholding in Apollo Specialty Hospitals Private Limited and Apollo Fertility Centre Private Limited to Kids Clinic India Limited, which operates under the brand “Cloudnine.” The transaction, valued at approximately ₹12,500 million, includes cash consideration and an equity stake in Kids Clinic India Limited, representing a 9.9% stake. This divestment aligns with Apollo Hospitals’ strategic focus on optimising its portfolio and enhancing shareholder value. The healthcare sector continues to navigate a dynamic landscape, with companies like Apollo Hospitals adapting to changing market conditions and focusing on strategic growth and leadership enhancement. Analysts will be observing how these developments impact the company’s performance and strategic direction in the coming quarters.
