Articles 1 min read Rupee plunges 24 paise to 92.62 against US dollar in early trade In early trading, the rupee slipped by 24 paise to reach 92.62 against the US dollar. This downturn was influenced by escalating crude oil prices and lackluster performance in local equity markets. On a positive note, foreign institutional investors acquired shares amounting to Rs 5,843.33 crore net on Thursday. However, the Sensex fell by 350.25 points, and the Nifty declined by 79. Advt The Indian currency became the best-performing Asian currency, recovering nearly 5 per cent from its lowest-ever close of 96.88 against the US dollar recorded on October 8.Meanwhile, the dollar index, which gauges the greenback's strength against a basket of six currencies, was at 103.65, lower by 0.07 per cent.Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, was trading 0.11 per cent lower at USD 96.46 per barrel in futures trade.Analysts said that oil prices breached the USD 95-a-barrel level after reports of vessels being targeted in the Strait of Hormuz, fuelling concerns over supply-chain disruptions.In the domestic equity market, Sensex fell 350.25 points or 0.42 per cent to 83,978.34, while the Nifty was down 79.05 points or 0.31 per cent to 25,807.25.Foreign institutional investors (FIIs) bought equities worth Rs 5,843.33 crore on a net basis on Thursday, according to exchange data.
