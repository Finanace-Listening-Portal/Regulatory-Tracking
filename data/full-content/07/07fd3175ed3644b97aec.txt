Articles 1 min read Bandhan Bank to issue 1 lakh credit cards by FY27 end: CEO Partha Pratim Sengupta Bandhan Bank is targeting around 1 lakh credit card issuances by the end of FY27, MD & CEO Partha Pratim Sengupta told ETBFSI and that the two month old card business has already issued 34,000 cards. The bank is positioning credit cards as a key part of its corporate salary account and customer acquisition strategy. Sengupta said the card would be a strategy towards acquiring corporate salary packages, while also supporting the bank's existing business growth and helping it acquire new customers. Advt The bank had earlier said it has a full bouquet of products that it can offer customers as part of its salary account proposition, with the credit card now forming part of that offering.Sengupta said the cards are intended for different segments of borrowers and customers, rather than being focused on a single category."These cards are intended for all segments of the borrower," he said, adding that the bank has launched four variants from the initial rollout.The variants are designed for different customer profiles, including frequent travellers and high-end HNI customers, among others.The bank's strategy is to use the cards both to deepen relationships with its existing customer base and to acquire new customers. Sengupta said the credit card would act as a support to the bank's existing business growth while also being used for new customer acquisition.
