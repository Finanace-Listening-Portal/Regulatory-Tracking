Articles 2 min read Union Bank of India Q2 net profit jumps 31 pc to Rs 5,781 cr PTI Published On Oct 19, 2026 at 06:13 PM IST var news_msid = 135049238; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai, State-owned lender Union Bank of India on Monday reported a 31 per cent rise in consolidated net profit to Rs 5,781 crore for the second quarter of FY27, helped by higher net interest income and improvement in asset quality.The bank earned a net profit of Rs 4425.95 crore in the year-ago period.On a standalone basis, net profit surged 30.32 per cent to Rs 5,537.20 crore in the July-September quarter from Rs 4,249.08 crore a year ago.Core net interest income increased by 20.09 per cent to Rs 10,583 crore in Q2 FY27 from Rs 8,812 crore in Q2 FY26. On a sequential basis, it grew by 5.43 per cent. Advt In the July-September quarter, domestic net interest margins (NIM) rose to 2.95 per cent against 2.87 per cent in the quarter-ago period, and 2.75 per cent a year ago.Similarly, global NIMs increased to 2.86 per cent as of September 30, 2026, from 2.80 per cent a year ago and 2.67 per cent as of September 30, 2025, according to the investor presentation.The non-interest income dropped 6.90 per cent to Rs 4,651 crore year-on-year in the reporting quarter from Rs 4,996 crore. This was mainly due to a sharp fall in the treasury income.In the July-September quarter, treasury income fell 34.60 per cent year-on-year to Rs 701 crore from Rs 1,073 crore, the investor presentation showed.Its global deposits increased by 6.87 per cent to Rs 13.19 lakh crore in the reporting quarter from Rs 12.35 lakh crore a year ago.Domestic deposits grew 6.88 per cent to Rs 13.19 lakh crore in the July-September quarter from Rs 12.34 lakh crore in the year-ago period.Share of the low-cost current and savings account CASA ratio improved to 34.93 per cent from 32.56 per cent a year earlier.Domestic advances grew 17.35 per cent year-on-year to Rs 11.06 lakh crore from Rs 9,42,273 crore.Retail, agriculture and MSME (RAM) advances increased 14.65 per cent year-on-year to Rs 6.36 lakh crore.Within RAM, MSME advances rose 22.39 per cent to Rs 1.80 lakh crore, retail advances grew 11.24 per cent to Rs 2.65 lakh crore, and agriculture advances increased 12.71 per cent to Rs 1.90 lakh crore. Advt In the reporting quarter, the bank witnessed an improvement in the asset quality, with gross non-performing asset (NPA) ratio declining to 2.42 per cent in September 2026 from 3.29 per cent in September 2025.The net NPA ratio eased to 0.42 per cent from 0.55 per cent. The provision coverage ratio rose to 95.20 per cent from 95.13 per cent. PTI PTI Published On Oct 19, 2026 at 06:13 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
