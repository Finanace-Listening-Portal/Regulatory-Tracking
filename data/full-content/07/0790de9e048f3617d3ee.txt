Advt Firms raised about 350 billion rupees in the first 10 days of November, after fundraising fell to a six-month low of 609 billion rupees in October, Prime Database data showed."The recent fall in yields has given borrowers an opportunity that was not available in September and October," said Nishchay Nath, founder and chief executive officer of BondScanner, an online bond trading platform."Several borrowers had postponed or withdrawn bond issues when yields were higher, and some of them are now likely to bring those plans forward," Nath said.LSEG AAA-rated benchmark yields have fallen 30 basis points for the three-year tenor and 35 bps for the five-year tenor so far this month, tracking a decline in government bond yields.Indian government bond yields tumbled as Brent crude prices slumped after US President Donald Trump's Republican Party lost its majority in the midterm elections.The benchmark 10-year yield fell 22 basis points last week, its steepest weekly decline in about seven years.But the window for cheaper borrowing could be brief, with the Reserve Bank of India and Federal Reserve expected to raise rates in December, market participants said.The RBI raised its policy rate to 5.50% in October and adopted a calibrated tightening stance, signalling further hikes.Indian companies are increasingly issuing three-and five-year bonds as the domestic debt market offers the cheapest borrowing costs, said Venkatakrishnan Srinivasan, founder and managing partner of debt advisory firm Rockfort Fincap.
