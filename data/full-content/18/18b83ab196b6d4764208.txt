The IBBI said the extension was also warranted in view of proposed changes and to ensure seamless compliance.The Board has directed that all applicable PGIRP forms must now be submitted by December 31. Penalties for delayed submission or modification of the forms will also be levied only after December 31. Advt The IBBI had mandated the filing of electronic forms PGIRP-1 to PGIRP-6 on its e-platform for monitoring insolvency resolution processes concerning personal guarantors to corporate debtors under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 and the regulations made under it.The Board had earlier extended the filing deadline to September 30 through a July 9 circular, after recognising the practical difficulties faced by stakeholders and the transitional time required.The latest circular further extends the deadline following feedback from insolvency professionals and insolvency professional agencies.The IBBI said stakeholders seeking clarification may refer to the frequently asked questions available on its website. Technical issues or difficulties with the electronic filing process can be reported to the support email specified by the Board.Fee for delayed liquidation formIn a separate circular, the IBBI said each liquidation form that is due on or before September 30, 2026 and is submitted after its due date will attract a fee of Rs 500 per month, along with applicable GST, for the period of delayed filing.The fee will apply to forms submitted after the due date through correction, updation or otherwise, the Board said. The circular is issued in continuation of its January 5, 2026 circular on the revised liquidation forms and in accordance with Regulation 47B of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016.
