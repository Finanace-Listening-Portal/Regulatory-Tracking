The policy, delivered against the backdrop of persistent global risks, marked the beginning of a rate-hike cycle that fund managers believe could cumulatively amount to 100 basis points (bps) or more. A rise in interest rates is negative for bonds, particularly longer-duration securities, as it leads to a decline in their prices, and results in mark-to-market losses for investors. According to most debt mutual fund (MF) managers, the current strategy is to focus on accruals or carry (interest income), while waiting for the right opportunity to add duration through higher-duration government bonds. “Our preference today is to earn carry at the shorter end, stay patient on duration, and use periods of yield repricing to gradually add duration when the risk-reward becomes more favourable,” Axis Mutual Fund said in a note on Wednesday.
