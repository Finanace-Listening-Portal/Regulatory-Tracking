The strike will be observed across the bank’s branches and offices nationwide. The two-day nationwide hunger strike will be held on November 2 and November 3 to protest the proposed sale of IDBI Bank to Canada-based Fairfax Financial Holdings Group. The forum has demanded that the government halt the proposed sale and acquire at least 6 per cent equity in IDBI Bank, either from Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) or through the market, to maintain its shareholding at a minimum of 51 per cent. It said this would honour assurances made in Parliament in December 2003 that the government would maintain majority ownership of the bank. The proposed transaction involves the dilution of the government’s and LIC’s shareholdings and the transfer of management control to the Fairfax group. The forum has also sought written statutory assurances that existing service conditions, welfare measures, pension benefits and reservation policies would be protected.
