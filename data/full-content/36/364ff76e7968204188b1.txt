Interest payout and maturity value are not the same thing Cumulative option Under a cumulative option, interest is added according to the bank’s compounding method and paid with principal at maturity. The effective maturity amount will not equal principal multiplied by the headline rate in every case because tenure, compounding dates, day count and tax deduction affect the cash flow. The bank says its calculation uses 365 days in a non-leap year and 366 days in a leap year. Periodic payout option A depositor choosing monthly or quarterly interest receives cash during the tenure instead of compounding the full amount to maturity. The bank notes that monthly interest for a period shorter than a quarter is paid at a discounted rate as directed. Therefore, multiplying the nominal annual rate by one-twelfth may not reproduce the actual monthly credit. Compare the official illustration or deposit advice for the chosen payout frequency. Booking-date evidence Preserve the booking confirmation showing principal, tenure, rate, maturity date, payout instruction, nomination, account ownership and lien status. A screenshot of a public rate page does not prove the contractual terms of an individual deposit. Where money is moved from a joint account or held for a minor, trust, firm or company, the title and capacity should be consistent across KYC, mandate and accounting records. Premature closure: actual stay controls the recalculation Kotak states that on premature closure it pays the lower of the contracted rate and the rate that prevailed on the original booking date for the period the deposit actually remained with the bank, after deducting the applicable penal charge. This means the original 365-days-to-under-15-months rate does not simply continue on a broken deposit. DICGC insurance: ₹5 lakh is an aggregate ceiling, not a per-FD promise The DICGC insured-bank list, updated on 29 September 2026, includes Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd. DICGC covers eligible deposits with an insured bank, including fixed deposits, up to ₹5 lakh for principal and accrued interest together in the same right and same capacity. Deposits held through different branches of the same bank in the same ownership capacity are aggregated. Opening several fixed deposits or using several branches does not multiply the ceiling. Different legal capacities may be treated separately under the scheme, but labels should not be manipulated to create artificial capacity. The DICGC guide explains joint, individual, trustee, guardian and other capacities through its own rules. Insurance is a statutory protection that operates on specified events; it is not a guarantee of uninterrupted access, market liquidity, every type of claim, or amounts above the ceiling. The depositor should verify the bank’s continuing insured status and the latest DICGC rules. Bank-deposit interest is generally taxable under income from other sources unless it is properly attributable to a business and accounted for under the applicable business-income rules. Tax follows accrual or receipt according to the taxpayer’s permissible method and facts; it is not deferred merely because the deposit is cumulative or has not matured. The Income Tax Department’s transition FAQ states that post-1-April-2026 withholding obligations use the new Act and that TDS categories are consolidated in section 393, while rates and monetary thresholds were retained. Kotak’s public disclosure still describes its operational withholding by reference to legacy section 194A; depositors preparing Tax Year 2026-27 records should use the current statutory mapping and portal forms. TDS deducted by the bank is a credit against final tax, not a separate or final tax on the interest. Eligible residents may submit the prescribed declaration for non-deduction only when all statutory conditions are satisfied; Form 15G or Form 15H is not a general exemption from tax. PAN, residential status, estimated total income and declaration validity should be checked. Reconcile interest certificates, account statements and the current annual-information statement or successor reporting form. If total estimated tax crosses the advance-tax threshold, deposit interest can affect instalments even when TDS has been deducted.
