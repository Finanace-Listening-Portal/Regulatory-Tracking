Articles 2 min read RBI examining NUCFDC's SRO application for urban cooperative banks The Reserve Bank of India is examining the application submitted by the National Urban Co-operative Finance and Development Corporation (NUCFDC) to become a Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) for the urban cooperative banking sector, with the umbrella body having responded to regulatory queries and held further discussions with the central bank, ETBFSI has learnt from the people aware of development. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Updated On Nov 26, 2026 at 08:06 AM IST Read by 100 Professionals var news_msid = 136176386; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Reserve Bank of India is examining NUCFDC's application for recognition as a Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) for the urban cooperative banking sector, with the application currently under discussion, according to people familiar with the development.The umbrella body for urban cooperative banks submitted its application to the central bank on April 17, 2026, after meeting the prescribed paid-up capital requirement of Rs 300 crore.RBI has raised certain queries on the application, which NUCFDC has responded to. Further discussions have also taken place between the regulator and the organisation on the matter. Advt The recognition process is ongoing, and no timeline has been indicated for the approval, the people said.RBI had granted NUCFDC approval to operate as an umbrella organisation for urban cooperative banks in August 2023, with its final certificate of registration issued on February 8, 2024.As part of the approval framework, the central bank had envisaged that the organisation would also undertake SRO functions, subject to achieving a paid-up capital of Rs 300 crore and complying with the applicable regulatory guidelines.The Ministry of Cooperation had earlier clarified that NUCFDC could approach RBI for SRO approval only after meeting these conditions. The scope of its SRO responsibilities would be prescribed by the central bank.NUCFDC is working on building a centralised digital infrastructure for cooperative banks, alongside providing liquidity support, refinance and capital assistance.It is also undertaking capacity-building initiatives for the sector. Its learning management system, developed under RBI's Mission Saksham, has onboarded around 70,000 bank employees.A Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) is an industry-led body recognised by a regulator to develop and promote professional, ethical and governance standards among entities in a particular sector. If approved, NUCFDC would become the first recognised SRO for the urban cooperative banking sector. Advt ETBFSI has reached out to the RBI for its comments, the story will be updated if the central bank responds.
