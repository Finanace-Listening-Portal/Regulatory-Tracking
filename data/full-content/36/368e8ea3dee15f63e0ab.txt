This strategic move was marked by the launch of two new models, the TVS Ronin and TVS NTORQ XP, at an event attended by notable figures including Osman Mahomed, Minister of Land Transport, Mauritius, and Pradeep Chaurasiya, Head of Africa Business at TVS Motor Company. For over a decade, TVS Motor Company has maintained a robust presence in the Mauritian market, contributing to the region’s mobility landscape. The new partnership with Le Warehouse aims to leverage TVS Motor’s manufacturing expertise and the local market knowledge of its partners to further strengthen the brand’s connection with Mauritian customers. This collaboration is expected to enhance TVS Motor’s market growth as it adapts to the evolving mobility needs of the island’s populace. Peyman Kargar, President of International Business at TVS Motor Company, highlighted the significance of the African market in the company’s international business strategy. He emphasised that the introduction of the TVS Ronin and TVS NTORQ XP models underscores TVS Motor’s commitment to offering a diverse range of mobility solutions to Mauritian customers. Pradeep Chaurasiya echoed these sentiments, expressing confidence in the partnership’s potential to deepen customer engagement and contribute to the changing mobility landscape in Mauritius. Amit Kantilal Shah, CEO of Le Warehouse, noted that the collaboration with TVS Motor Company aligns with their philosophy of innovation, performance, and value. He stated that the partnership represents a strategic diversification for Le Warehouse, enabling them to play a more significant role in the daily lives of Mauritian customers. By leveraging 361’s retail network and digital channels, the companies aim to expand the brand’s reach and improve product accessibility. The launch of these new models comes amidst ongoing discussions in Mauritius about road congestion and the evolving demands of modern life. Two-wheelers are increasingly viewed as practical and convenient alternatives for daily commuting. Analysts suggest that TVS Motor’s strengthened presence in Mauritius, supported by its established reputation and new local partnerships, positions the company well to address these mobility challenges and expand its footprint in the region.
