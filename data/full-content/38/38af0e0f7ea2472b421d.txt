The numero uno position is now held by Airtel, with a brand value of $43.2 billion, which grew 5% during the same period.Not all banks lost their sheen, though. India’s largest bank, SBI, gained 26% in brand value to $23.6 billion, making it the fourth most valuable brand overall. Like SBI, LIC also moved up two positions to number 8, even as it lost 1% of its brand value, which stood at $10.2 billion. Advt Kotak Mahindra Bank also shed 17% of its brand value to $7.8 billion and slipped three places. Axis Bank, however, gained 16% to $6.9 billion. Axis Bank’s strong growth in brand value was ‘powered by greater presence’, the report said, both physically and digitally. In FY26, it added approximately 400 branches, taking its network to 6,275 physical locations, with nearly half located in rural and semi-urban markets.“That means expanding physical reach while boosting participation in digital payments. Strong brand equity and Demand Power have been crucial to these efforts. Digitally, the brand remains a leader in everyday UPI transactions while also excelling at ‘higher-touch’ undertakings like wealth management and merchant acquisition,” the report added.Fintechs gain groundFintechs registered strong growth in brand value. PhonePe’s brand value went up 31% to $5.4 billion, while Paytm’s rose 23% to $2.3 billion. CRED also gained 23% to reach $1.3 billion.Four financial services companies – HDFC Bank, SBI, ICICI Bank and LIC – ranked among the top 10. Three more made it to the top 25 among India’s most valuable brands, which include companies across sectors.The financial services sector’s total brand value accounts for 29% of the combined brand value of the top 100 brands, amounting to $135,391 million. It holds the largest share of brand value among sectors, followed by business technology and services, telecom providers and retail, each accounting for 11%.
