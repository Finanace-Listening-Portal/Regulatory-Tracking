Rationale behind stock split A stock split is when a company divides its existing shares into multiple shares, reducing the price per share while keeping your total investment value unchanged. A stock split increases a company's shares, which lowers the price per share while ensuring the total market cap or investment value remains unchanged. In general, a stock split helps boost participation among retail investors, signals growth confidence and improves liquidity via higher volume and narrower bid-ask spreads. The rationale behind a stock split is to enhance the liquidity of the company's equity shares and encourage participation from small investors by making the equity shares more attractive to invest in.
