Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Star Food and Beverages Vs State of Punjab and another (Punjab and Haryana High Court) Summary: The Punjab and Haryana High Court allowed the writ petition filed by Star Food and Beverages challenging an adjudication order dated 24 April 2024 under Section 74 of the Punjab Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, relating to the tax period April 2018 to March 2019. The department issued a show cause notice dated 25 September 2023, to which the petitioner submitted a detailed reply on 25 October 2023. Supporting evidence was also produced during the personal hearing. However, the adjudicating authority failed to consider either the written response to the SCN or the evidence submitted by the petitioner. The High Court observed that the adjudication order was virtually a copy of the show cause notice and merely concluded that the petitioner’s explanation was unsatisfactory. The Court found that the order had been passed without independent application of mind. The ruling concerns the requirement that adjudicating authorities examine taxpayer submissions rather than mechanically reproduce allegations from an SCN. Comparable judicial discussions have emphasised the importance of considering the taxpayer’s reply and issuing a reasoned adjudication order. Accordingly, the High Court set aside the impugned order dated 24 April 2024 (Annexure P-7), while expressly granting the respondents liberty to proceed afresh in accordance with law. The petition was allowed. The Court did not determine the underlying GST liability on its merits.
