Articles 1 min read Policy support, exporter resilience drive strong October trade performance: CII India's goods exports rose significantly to nearly USD 50 billion in October compared to the prior year. Imports also increased, leading to a trade deficit of USD 39.88 billion. Strong shipments in petroleum, electronics, and engineering products contributed to the growth in exports. The government's policy support, including Free Trade Agreements, has fostered an enabling business environment. Advt Also Read: Union Minister Goyal meets ElSewedy Electric CEO; discusses investment opportunities in India"India's export performance for October is indeed encouraging and energising. It reflects the resilience, competitiveness and growing global footprint of Indian industry," he said.The growth in non-petroleum exports is encouraging, with exports of electronic goods, engineering products, chemicals, gems and jewellery, and textiles showing strong growth, Budhia said, adding that this reflects the growing diversity and strength of India's exports.This performance reflects the efforts of Indian exporters in dealing with challenges in the global market, he said."Equally, the government's sustained policy support, particularly through Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) and the Export Promotion Mission, is helping create a more enabling ecosystem for Indian businesses to expand their global presence," he said.He expressed confidence that, with continued collaboration between the government and industry, India is well poised to realise its ambitious vision of achieving USD 1 trillion each in merchandise and services exports.
