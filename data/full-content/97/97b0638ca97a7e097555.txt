Ltd (IEC: AADCO2827M) – Consolidated Adjudication of Multiple Show Cause Notices arising from SVB Invesigation Report No. 219/AC/SVB/AVB/2024-25 dated 31.03.2025. Summary: The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), through Notification No. 92/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 16 November 2026, has appointed the Principal Commissioner/Commissioner of Customs (Import), Air Cargo Complex, New Delhi, as the common adjudicating authority for six show cause notices issued to OnePlus Technology India Pvt. Ltd. The notification has been issued under Section 4(1), read with Section 3 and Sections 5(1) and 5(1A) of the Customs Act, 1962. The notices originate from different Customs formations in New Delhi, Noida, Bengaluru, Nhava Sheva and Chennai. The notification identifies the individual show cause notices and their respective adjudicating authorities and assigns the powers and duties relating to their adjudication to the designated common authority in New Delhi. This arrangement enables the specified proceedings involving the same noticee to be adjudicated by a single authority. The notification concerns the appointment and jurisdiction of the adjudicating authority and does not determine the merits of the allegations contained in the show cause notices.
