CompaniesNCLT Declares Former Videocon Promoter Venugopal Dhoot Bankrupt Over Rs 12,668 Crore SBI DuesEdited by: Priya RaghuvanshiUpdated: Nov 6, 2026, 15:16 ISTNCLT declares former Videocon promoter Venugopal Dhoot bankrupt over Rs 12,668 crore in SBI dues after rejecting his proposed repayment plan.National Company Law Tribunal (File Photo)follow usfollow usThe National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, has declared former Videocon Industries promoter and director Venugopal Nandlal Dhoot bankrupt in connection with outstanding loans worth Rs 12,668 crore owed to the State Bank of India (SBI). The tribunal accepted a bankruptcy petition filed by SBI after an earlier personal insolvency resolution process failed to produce an acceptable repayment arrangement. Dhoot had provided personal guarantees for credit facilities extended to Videocon Industries by SBI and several banks that subsequently merged with it.Along with declaring Dhoot bankrupt, the tribunal has appointed a Bankruptcy Trustee to oversee the proceedings and manage the distribution of his estate among creditors."Venugopal Nandlal Dhoot, the Personal Guarantor herein, is declared as Bankrupt," said an order passed by a two-member bench of NCLT on Thursday.NCLT Appoints Bankruptcy TrusteeFollowing its decision, the Mumbai bench instructed the Bankruptcy Trustee to issue a public notice inviting claims from creditors. The tribunal also clarified that Dhoot's estate would be distributed among those to whom he owes money.As part of the process, Dhoot must provide the trustee with a detailed account of his financial position within seven days of the commencement of bankruptcy proceedings."The Bankrupt (Dhoot) shall submit his statement of financial position to the Bankruptcy Trustee in the prescribed form within seven days from the date of this order, i.e. the bankruptcy commencement date," it said.The trustee has also been tasked with preparing an initial report for submission to the tribunal and the Committee of Creditors (CoC) within 90 days. Subsequent updates on the progress of the proceedings will be submitted every quarter.The bench comprised Member (Judicial) Vinay Goel and Member (Technical) Charanjeet Singh Gulati.The tribunal further stated that the bankruptcy proceedings would remain in force until Dhoot receives a discharge under Section 138 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).SBI's Claim Includes Loans Taken by Videocon Group CompaniesThe bankruptcy case stems from credit facilities obtained by Videocon Industries Ltd (VIL) from SBI and its former associate banks, including State Bank of Hyderabad, State Bank of Mysore, State Bank of Travancore, State Bank of Bikaner and Jaipur, and State Bank of Patiala.These banks were subsequently amalgamated with SBI in April 2017. Videocon Telecommunications Ltd (VTL) had also availed certain facilities as a co-obligor.According to SBI's petition, the total outstanding default, including interest and penalties, stood at Rs 12,668.06 crore as of September 30, 2026.The claimed amount comprises Rs 9,322.10 crore in rupee term loans, Rs 1,437.76 crore relating to the VTL facility and Rs 1,908.19 crore in working capital borrowings.SBI had invoked Dhoot's personal guarantees through demand notices issued in February 2018 after the loan accounts turned irregular.Dhoot's Rs 600 Crore Repayment Proposal RejectedThe latest bankruptcy order follows an unsuccessful attempt to resolve the matter through the personal insolvency resolution mechanism.The NCLT had initiated personal insolvency resolution proceedings against Dhoot on April 8, 2026. During this process, he submitted a repayment proposal on June 16, offering to pay Rs 600 crore over 36 months.The proposed amount was linked to a development project in Siliguri and represented just 0.42 per cent of the admitted liabilities.However, the resolution professional raised concerns about the proposal's viability. The repayment depended on securing future approvals and generating sufficient cash flows from the proposed project. It also did not provide for any immediate payment.The professional further noted that the proposal had not been prepared in consultation with him, as required under Section 105 of the IBC.On July 17, the tribunal rejected the repayment plan under Section 114 and brought the resolution process to an end.Subsequently, SBI approached the NCLT with a bankruptcy petition on September 24, within the prescribed three-month period under Section 121(2) of the Code.Tribunal Finds SBI's Bankruptcy Petition Compliant With IBCWhile allowing the lender's plea, the NCLT observed that the application fulfilled the statutory conditions necessary for admission."We are satisfied that the Petition complies with all the requirements prescribed in Section 123 of the Code. Therefore, we consider it proper to admit this Petition."The tribunal has directed the Bankruptcy Trustee to submit the preliminary report to both the adjudicating authority and the Committee of Creditors within 90 days of the bankruptcy commencement date.The order also records that there was no appearance on behalf of Dhoot during the proceedings.(With PTI Inputs)Latest News indiaPunjab Govt Gives Diwali Bonanza To Employees With 40% DA Hike For 6,507 Computer Teachers: CM Bhagwant Singh MannindiaWest Bengal SIR: Fourth Retired Judge Steps Down, Murshidabad Appeals Likely To Sufferentertainment newsRamayana's Hanuman Actor Sunny Deol Says 'Jai Shri Ram' In His Review; Calls It 'Even Better Than Hollywood' - WatchsportsGael Monfils Lands New Tennis Role After Retirement And Split With Wifeindia'Targeted Smear Campaign': Sonam Wangchuk Seeks President Murmu's Interventionentertainment newsDivyanka Tripathi-Vivek Dahiya Name Their Twin Sons Daivik And Vaidvik – Details Insideentertainment newsRamayana Deleted VIRAL Scene: Rama Rescues Sita, Lakshman In International Cut - Fans Ask Makers 'Why Did You Rob Us?'world‘Horrific Mass Shooting’: Several Killed In Attack At Poland’s Historic Krakow SquarePriya Raghuvanshi authorShe is working as a Chief Copy Editor at Times Now’s Business Desk, where she covers key developments in the stock market, Indian corporates across se...
