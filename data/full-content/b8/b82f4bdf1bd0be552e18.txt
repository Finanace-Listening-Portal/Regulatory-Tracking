(AI Generated Image)follow usfollow usShares of Tata Group companies rallied up to 9% on Tuesday after the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) nudged Tata Sons to comply with the listing requirements applicable to upper-layer non-banking financial companies (NBFCs). The development has renewed investor interest in Tata Group stocks, as the possibility of Tata Sons being listed could have significant implications for the group's corporate structure, valuation and governance. The rally comes amid heightened attention on Tata Sons' listing status, with the RBI's latest communication adding pressure on the holding company to meet the regulatory requirements.Tata Chemicals surged 9%, while Tata Investment Corporation advanced more than 3%. Shares of Tata Motors and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles rose around 2% each.Tata Chemicals owns a 2.53% stake in Tata Sons, valued at Rs 30,052.2 crore. On the other hand, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles and Tata Steel each own slightly more than 3% of Tata Sons. What is driving the gains?The sharp rally came amid renewed expectations of a potential stock market listing of Tata Sons. The RBI has reiterated that Tata Sons must comply with the regulatory requirements applicable to upper-layer NBFCs, explicitly including the mandatory listing provision.
