Articles 1 min read PNB settles three claims worth Rs 43 lakh over Greater Noida branch account irregularities Punjab National Bank has settled three claims worth Rs 43 lakh following complaints of alleged irregularities in customer savings and fixed deposit accounts at its Greater Noida branch. The bank is examining the matter and has said all genuine claims will be honoured. The bank also assured customers that “all genuine claims will be honoured”. Advt The development comes after complaints from customers alleging that funds were withdrawn or transferred from their savings and fixed deposit accounts without their authorisation. The total amount involved is being examined by the bank and authorities.PNB said it maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards fraud, financial irregularities and misconduct, and that stern action will be taken against those found responsible in accordance with applicable rules and procedures.The bank added that it remains committed to safeguarding customer interests and strengthening its internal controls and monitoring mechanisms to prevent recurrence of such incidents.
