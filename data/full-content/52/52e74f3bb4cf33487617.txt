Excluding the one-off gain, net profit rose 14 per cent from ₹1,929 crore. Sequentially, net profit rose 4 per cent from ₹2,115 crore in Q1FY27. Non-interest income declined 46 per cent year-on-year to ₹1,353 crore from ₹2,489 crore, which included the NSDL gain. On a sequential basis, it rose 31 per cent from ₹1,032 crore. Operating profit fell 18 per cent year-on-year to ₹2,893 crore from ₹3,523 crore in Q2FY26. Excluding the one-off gain, operating profit rose 59 per cent from ₹1,824 crore. On a sequential basis, operating profit rose 33 per cent from ₹2,168 crore in Q1FY27. Net interest income (NII) rose 29 per cent year-on-year to ₹4,228 crore in Q2FY27 from ₹3,285 crore a year ago. Sequentially, NII increased 21 per cent from ₹3,486 crore in the previous quarter. Interest income rose 20 per cent year-on-year to ₹8,512 crore from ₹7,104 crore and 13 per cent from ₹7,541 crore in Q1FY27. Net interest margin (NIM) improved to 4.11 per cent in Q2FY27 from 3.71 per cent in the year-ago period and 3.61 per cent in the previous quarter.
