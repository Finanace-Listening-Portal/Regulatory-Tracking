The Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs, communicated the decision through Office Memorandum F. No. 1/4/2019-NS dated 30 June 2026, which was subsequently circulated by the Department of Posts through SB Order No. 07/2026 dated 30 June 2026. Accordingly, the rates are 4% for Savings Deposit, 6.9% for one-year Time Deposit, 7% for two-year Time Deposit, 7.1% for three-year Time Deposit, 7.5% for five-year Time Deposit, 6.7% for five-year Recurring Deposit, 8.2% for Senior Citizen Savings Scheme, 7.4% for Monthly Income Account Scheme, 7.7% for National Savings Certificate, 7.1% for Public Provident Fund, 7.5% for Kisan Vikas Patra with maturity in 115 months, and 8.2% for Sukanya Samriddhi Account. This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority. F.
