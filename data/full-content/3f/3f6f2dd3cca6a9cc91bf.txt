Advt Foreign currency assets, the largest component of India's forex reserves, rose to USD 608.26 billion at the end of September from USD 552.28 billion at the end of March.RBI holds 880.52 tonnes of goldAs of September-end, the RBI held 880.52 metric tonnes of gold, of which 740.13 tonnes were held domestically and 140.39 tonnes were kept in safe custody with the Bank of England and the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), the report said.The decline in the value of gold reserves comes against the backdrop of a fall in international gold prices during the six-month period. Gold prices averaged around USD 4,721 per troy ounce in April 2026 before easing to around USD 4,319 in September, marking a decline of approximately 9 per cent.The RBI attributed the movement in international gold prices to rising US Treasury yields, a stronger US dollar and expectations of tighter monetary policy, despite continued geopolitical uncertainty and investor demand for gold as a safe-haven asset and for portfolio diversification.Indian central bank also said that global central bank reserve accumulation remained broadly stable during the first half of calendar year 2026, with total reserves increasing from USD 13.10 trillion in the first quarter to USD 13.22 trillion in the second quarter.
