TCS Q2 results Tata Consultancy Services (TCS), India's largest information technology (IT) services company, announced its Q2FY27 earnings on Thursday. It reported a net profit of ₹13,884 crore for Q2FY27, up 14.9 per cent year-on-year (Y-o-Y) from ₹12,075 crore in Q2FY26. Revenue grew 11.2 per cent in reported terms to ₹73,188 crore from ₹65,799 crore a year earlier. On a quarter-on-quarter (Q-o-Q) basis, TCS' net profit rose 4 per cent, while revenue increased 1.3 per cent. In constant currency terms, revenue grew 0.5 per cent. The company's Q2 performance beat Bloomberg analyst estimates, although growth remained modest amid global uncertainty and artificial intelligence (AI)-led disruptions.
