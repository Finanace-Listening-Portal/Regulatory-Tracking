Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, Indian Bank, Bank of Baroda, Uco Bank and Bank of Maharashtra were up in the range of 2 per cent to 4 per cent. Canara Bank, State Bank of India (SBI), Central Bank of India and Union Bank of India were up 1 per cent each. What’s driving PSU banks? Indian Bank reported a steady business growth in Q2FY27, with gross advances rising to ₹7.23 trillion, up 16.6 per cent year-on-year (YoY) (5.5 per cent quarter-on-quarter (QoQ)). Retail, Agriculture, and MSME (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises) or RAM advances stood at ₹4.43 trillion, up 17.8 per cent YoY (6.2% QoQ). Total deposits increased to ₹8.73 trillion, up 12.4 per cent YoY (3.4 per cent QoQ), while savings and current account deposits grew 10.8 per cent YoY (1.8 per cent QoQ) and 27.5 per cent YoY (6.3 per cent QoQ), respectively.
