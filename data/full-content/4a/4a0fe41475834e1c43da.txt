Financial Information Providers (FIPs) and Financial Information Users (FIUs). They facilitate the secure transfer of customers’ financial information, with their consent, from FIPs that hold the data to FIUs that use it to provide financial services. FIPs are institutions such as banks and NBFCs that hold customers’ financial information and share it with FIUs through requests routed via an AA. FIUs receive digitally signed financial data from FIPs through AAs and use it to provide services such as loans, insurance and wealth management. As many as 17 RBI-licensed Account Aggregators (AAs) are active in India, including CAMSFinServ, CRIF Connect, NESL Asset Data, Protean and PB Financial. As of August, 338.04 million accounts were linked through the AA framework, up 24 per cent from 272.46 million in February. In August alone, 11.72 million accounts were linked and 27.94 million new consents were fulfilled. The cumulative number of consents fulfilled stood at 566.26 million as of August. AAs also delivered 352.48 million datasets on linked accounts during the month, the highest monthly level between February and August. This compares with 265.67 million data sets delivered in February, indicating a substantial increase in the use of the AA framework for sharing financial information. Depositories hold investors’ securities such as shares, bonds and mutual fund units in electronic (dematerialised) form. There are two Sebi-regulated depositories in the country: National Securities Depository Limited and Central Depository Services (India) Limited.
