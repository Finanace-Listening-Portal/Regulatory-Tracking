Advt The central bank will also facilitate semi-regulated depositories to include information related to deposit accounts in the consolidated account statement.“Therefore, people should now going forward be getting a consolidated statement not only related to securities and debts, but also deposit accounts from the bank,” Malhotra said.The two measures will be implemented by the end of 2026, he said.Separately, the RBI will constitute a Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets in response to the rapidly evolving developments in financial markets.“The committee will serve as a forum for structured engagement with market participants and other stakeholders on policy and operational matters related to financial markets,” Malhotra said.
