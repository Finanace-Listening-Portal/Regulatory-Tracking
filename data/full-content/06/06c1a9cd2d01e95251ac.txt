ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Oct 13, 2026 at 12:00 PM IST Read by 100 Professionals var news_msid = 134910159; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai-based independent wealth-management and multi-family office platform Nexedge Capital on Tuesday appointed Apurva Sahijwani, former Managing Director and CEO of Avendus Wealth Management, as its co-founder.Sahijwani brings over 28 years of experience in private banking and wealth management. He served as Managing Director and CEO of Avendus Wealth Management from May 2024 to September 2026, where he led the rebuilding of the business and its expansion in the private wealth segment.Prior to this, Sahijwani served as Head of Private Banking at Axis Bank, where he played a role in launching and scaling Burgundy Private. He began his career at Kotak Securities and subsequently held senior positions at ICICI Bank, HSBC, Citibank, Axis Bank and Kotak Wealth Management. Advt "Apurva is one of the most accomplished builders in Indian private banking. He has launched, scaled and transformed wealth businesses at some of the country's leading institutions. Bringing him on board as Co-Founder reflects the scale of our ambition. We are building an independent wealth platformdesigned to lead the next decade of Indian wealth management, and Apurva's leadership significantly accelerates that journey," said Anirudha Taparia, Founder & CEO, Nexedge Capital.
