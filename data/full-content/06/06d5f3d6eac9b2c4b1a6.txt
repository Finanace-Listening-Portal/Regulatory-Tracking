The comparison, however, needs to account for differences in the book-value measures, financial condition of the target banks and the nature of the transactions. Advt The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved Fairfax's bid of Rs 52,878 crore for the combined 60.72% stake held by the government and Life Insurance Corporation of India. The transaction covers the government's 30.48% holding and LIC's 30.24% stake. The government will retain 15% and LIC 19% after the proposed sale. Fairfax's offer of Rs 81 per share is above the independent valuation benchmark of Rs 75.99 per share.IDBI Bank's FY26 annual results report an adjusted book value per share of Rs 51.85, excluding revaluation reserves, foreign-currency translation reserves, intangible assets and deferred tax assets. On this basis, the Rs 81 offer implies 1.56 times book value. Fairfax has separately said the offer represents a 23% premium to the bank's June 30, 2026 book value per share under Indian Accounting Standards. The two multiples use different reporting dates and book-value definitions and should not be treated as interchangeable.The price-to-book multiple is widely used in bank valuations because a lender's net worth represents the capital available to absorb losses and support business growth. A bank's ability to generate returns on that capital is central to the multiple it commands. A lender with a sustainable return on equity above its cost of equity can generally justify a higher price-to-book valuation than one whose returns are weaker or more volatile.Earlier mergersThe IDBI offer multiple is below some of the valuations reported in earlier bank acquisitions. Kotak Mahindra Bank's 2014 acquisition of ING Vysya Bank was reported at about twice its trailing book value, or around 2.2 times estimated FY15 adjusted book value. ICICI Bank's 2010 acquisition of Bank of Rajasthan was valued at about 5.2 times its current book value, while a broker assessment put it at 5.3 times estimated adjusted book value. The HDFC Bank-Centurion Bank of Punjab merger in 2008 has also been reported at widely differing multiples, including 2.4 times and 5.4 times one-year forward book value in separate accounts. These variations underline why the valuation period and denominator need to be specified when comparing bank deals. Advt The earlier transactions were also driven by different strategic considerations. Kotak's acquisition of ING Vysya expanded its branch network and customer franchise, while ICICI Bank's acquisition of Bank of Rajasthan gave it access to a network of 463 branches, particularly in northern and western India. HDFC Bank's merger with Centurion Bank of Punjab added nearly 400 branches and expanded its customer base. These were share-swap mergers, unlike IDBI's proposed strategic sale, in which the consideration for the existing shares will be paid to the government and LIC.IDBI Bank's financial position has improved considerably since it was placed under the Reserve Bank of India's Prompt Corrective Action framework in May 2017 because of high non-performing assets and losses. The restrictions were lifted in March 2021 after its financial health improved. By March 2026, its gross NPA ratio had declined to 2.32%, net NPAs stood at 0.15% and its provision coverage ratio, including technical write-offs, was 99.39%. Advt The bank reported a 27% increase in FY26 net profit to Rs 9,513 crore. Its return on assets improved to 2.27% from 1.98%, while capital adequacy rose to 26.65% from 25.05%. Net advances grew 16% to Rs 2.54 lakh crore and deposits increased 12% to Rs 3.47 lakh crore. These indicators point to a stronger balance sheet and improved capacity to absorb credit losses.However, the earnings profile presents a more mixed picture. Net interest income declined 8% to Rs 13,512 crore in FY26, while net interest margin contracted to 3.77% from 4.56%. Return on equity fell to 18.93% from 20.15%, despite the increase in net profit. The fourth quarter showed some recovery, with net interest income rising 17% year on year to Rs 3,851 crore and NIM improving to 4.15%. The sustainability of this recovery will be important in assessing the bank's earnings potential under new ownership. Advt The government's valuation exercise was based on two independent methods rather than the bank's prevailing stock-market price. The Market Multiple Method assessed IDBI Bank against public-sector bank peers using their March 31, 2026 closing accounts. The Asset Valuation Method considered tangible and intangible assets, including land and properties, as well as liabilities, using IDBI Bank's audited accounts for the same date. The average of the two independent valuations was Rs 75.99 per share, which became the benchmark for evaluating the bids. Fairfax's Rs 81 offer was above both individual fair-value estimates.The initial reserve price had been fixed at Rs 94.42 per share, but the bids received in the first round fell short of that threshold. The government said this reserve price had been influenced by the bank's market price, which had risen after the disinvestment process was announced. Before the initial bids were invited in January 2026, the stock had closed at Rs 108.12. Before the revised best-offer bids were invited in May, it stood at Rs 70.38. During the valuation period, the stock fluctuated between Rs 61.01 and Rs 118.38.The valuation also needs to be seen in the context of Fairfax's proposed plans for the bank beyond the stake purchase. The company intends to contribute its interests in Go Digit Life Insurance, Go Digit General Insurance and IIFL Capital Services to IDBI Bank at their respective market prices. The proposed contributions would expand the lender's presence into insurance, investment banking and wealth management, but remain subject to regulatory and other approvals. They are separate from the Rs 81 per share purchase offer and should not be treated as completed transactions.The comparison with previous acquisitions therefore suggests that IDBI Bank is being valued below some earlier bank deals, but does not by itself establish that the sale price is low. Its improved asset quality, high capital adequacy and profitability need to be weighed against declining full-year margins and return on equity. The bank's ability to sustain earnings growth and translate its balance-sheet strength into higher returns will ultimately determine whether the acquisition price proves attractive to Fairfax.
