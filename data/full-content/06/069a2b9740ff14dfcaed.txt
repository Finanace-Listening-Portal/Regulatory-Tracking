This project is set to supply renewable power to Tata Power Mumbai Distribution (TPC-D) and is anticipated to generate approximately 308 million units of electricity annually, contributing significantly to the provision of clean and reliable energy. The project features 28 Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine Generators (WTGs) utilising the SG 3.6-145 model, each designed to enhance the efficiency of renewable power generation. With its substantial annual generation capacity, the project is expected to reduce approximately 252,560 tonnes of CO₂ emissions each year. This development marks a significant step in TPREL’s efforts to bolster its clean energy footprint and facilitate the transition towards a lower-carbon power ecosystem. During the execution phase, the project faced a notable challenge when the grid connectivity plan required a revision, altering the EHV line configuration from underground cable to Monopole. This change introduced considerable design, procurement, and execution complexities. Nonetheless, TPREL’s Wind Project Execution Division adeptly managed the transition, successfully integrating the revised configuration without affecting the overall project schedule. With the commissioning of the Ausa Wind Project, TPREL’s total renewable utility capacity has reached 12.1 GW, of which 7.3 GW is operational, comprising 5.9 GW of solar and 1.4 GW of wind energy. The remaining 4.8 GW is in various stages of development. This expansion underscores Tata Power’s commitment to enhancing its renewable energy portfolio, aligning with its broader strategy to achieve Net Zero by 2045. Analysts view this development as a positive stride towards sustainable energy solutions, reinforcing Tata Power’s position as a leader in India’s clean energy transition.
