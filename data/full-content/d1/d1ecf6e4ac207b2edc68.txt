Having been a part of the founding team of the Company, most recently serving as its Financial Controller, Indu brings deep institutional knowledge and extensive experience of the Company's business and financial operations to the role. Over the years, he has helped strengthen the Company's financial governance, control environment and finance operations while contributing to key strategic milestones, including its successful IPO. In his new capacity, he will lead the Company's finance function and work closely with the leadership team as the organisation continues to strengthen its financial and governance framework. Advt Commenting, Anuj Mathur, MD & CEO, Canara HSBC Life Insurance, said, "Indu has been at the heart of our team since the Company's inception and has played a key role in how we plan, report and manage capital, including during our IPO. We have always preferred to build leaders from within, and his elevation is a natural step in that direction. Over the years, he has strengthened our financial governance and control environment, driven the modernisation of our finance function, and consistently helped the organisation navigate growth with discipline. As he takes on this expanded responsibility, his experience and institutional knowledge will be valuable as we continue to strengthen the business and build for sustainable long-term growth."Sharing his perspective, Indu Swaroop said, "I am grateful for the trust and confidence placed in me by the Board and the Company. Over the last 18+ years at Canara HSBC Life, I have worked alongside a strong finance team and colleagues across the business. I look forward to working with the leadership team to strengthen our financial foundations, create sustained value for our policyholders and shareholders, and support the Company in its next phase of growth."The appointment reflects Canara HSBC Life Insurance's focus on building leadership continuity and leveraging strong internal capabilities as the Company enters its next phase of growth.
