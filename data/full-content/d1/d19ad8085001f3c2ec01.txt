Articles 2 min read NBFCs shift towards secured retail credit as microfinance loans decline NBFCs are increasingly shifting towards secured retail lending, with credit to microfinance and self-help group loans declining at end-December 2025, while gold, housing and vehicle loans drove credit growth, the RBI said. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Oct 24, 2026 at 12:11 AM IST var news_msid = 135153797; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Non-banking financial companies are shifting towards secured lending options as credit to microfinance and self-help group (SHG) loans declined at end-December 2025, while gold loans, housing and vehicle loans continued to drive retail credit growth, the Reserve Bank of India said in its October Bulletin.“Credit to micro finance/ SHG loans declined in end-December 2025 reflecting regulatory guardrails and shifts by lenders toward secured lending options,” the central bank said in its review of the NBFC sector for 2025.The shift was visible even as retail credit remained a key driver of overall NBFC credit growth. The RBI said retail and industry sectors were the drivers of overall credit growth among NBFCs, with retail loans continuing to expand at a rapid pace given NBFCs’ expertise in the segment. Advt Within retail lending, gold loans gained prominence. Gold loans contributed 10.5 per cent of total incremental NBFC credit between December 2024 and December 2025, which the RBI attributed to higher eligible loan values supported by elevated gold prices.Housing and vehicle finance also remained important drivers of NBFC retail credit. The RBI’s latest data showed retail loans growing 22 per cent year-on-year in August 2026, with housing loans growing 12.1 per cent, vehicle loans 15.4 per cent and loans against gold jewellery 69.1 per cent.The broader shift towards secured retail products comes as NBFCs continue to see strong demand in segments where the underlying exposure is backed by an asset or collateral. The RBI stated that credit extended by NBFCs had exhibited robust expansion since August 2025, driven primarily by strong retail credit, with marked acceleration in segments such as housing and gold loans.Vehicle loans have also strengthened. In the banking system, vehicle-loan growth accelerated sharply during the first half of FY27, nearly doubling from the previous year, with the RBI attributing the increase to persistent demand following the GST cut.At the same time, deposit-taking NBFCs recorded 14.9 per cent growth in deposits at end-December 2025, providing them with an additional source of funding, the RBI said.The RBI’s assessment indicates a change in the composition of NBFC lending rather than a broad-based slowdown in retail credit. While microfinance and SHG lending contracted, lenders increased their focus on secured products such as gold, housing and vehicle finance. Advt The shift also comes against the backdrop of regulatory changes affecting lending to NBFCs and specific retail products. The RBI restored risk weights on bank lending to NBFCs to levels prevailing before the November 2023 increase with effect from April 1, 2025.Separately, the RBI revised its directions on consumption loans against gold collateral from April 1, 2026, moving from a flat 75 per cent loan-to-value cap to a tiered structure allowing higher borrowing limits for smaller loans.
