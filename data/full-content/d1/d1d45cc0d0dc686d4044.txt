This partnership is set to facilitate non-trade current account remittances, including person-to-person (P2P) and education-related payments, ensuring swift, reliable, and seamless transactions from India to major destinations such as the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, Canada, and Australia. The collaboration promises greater transparency and end-to-end visibility in payment processes. IndusInd Bank has long been a facilitator of international trade and payment flows, offering remittance capabilities that support education, travel, business, and other overseas payment requirements. The partnership with Mastercard marks a significant milestone for Mastercard Move in India, with plans to expand its scope to include small and medium enterprise (SME) payments. This move aligns with the growing demand for efficient cross-border money movement, as evidenced by the $12.1 billion in personal remittances sent from India in 2025, according to the World Bank. Additionally, over 1.2 million Indian students were enrolled in higher education abroad in 2025, underscoring the substantial scale of outbound payments for Indian consumers and families. Mastercard Move enables banks and fintechs to modernise the cross-border payment experience without the need to establish individual connections in every market. For IndusInd Bank, this collaboration is an opportunity to strengthen its cross-border payments proposition, deepen customer relationships, and create a scalable foundation to address evolving customer needs across the global payments ecosystem. Pratik Khowala, Global Head of Transfer Solutions at Mastercard, emphasised the dynamic nature of India’s market for cross-border money movement and the partnership’s potential to address critical use cases such as remittances and education payments. Ganesh Sankaran, Executive Director of IndusInd Bank, highlighted the increasing importance of cross-border payments as customer needs become more global. The collaboration with Mastercard is expected to simplify the international payments experience and reinforce IndusInd Bank’s position in the remittances market. Analysts view this partnership as a strategic move to capture a larger share of the growing outbound payments market, driven by India’s expanding global footprint and the increasing financial needs of its diaspora.
