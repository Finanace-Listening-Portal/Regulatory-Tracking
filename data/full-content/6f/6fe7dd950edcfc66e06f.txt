Articles 1 min read Bond market to stay cautious ahead of RBI policy: Equirus The bond market is expected to remain cautious due to persistent inflationary pressures and anticipated rate hikes. Analysts predict a 25 basis points hike in the RBI's upcoming monetary policy review. Market yields are likely to be influenced by geopolitical tensions and global bond movements. Foreign portfolio investor inflows and stable currency conditions may provide some support for bond yields. Advt While crude oil price risks currently appear relatively contained, any escalation in geopolitical tensions, particularly in West Asia, could trigger fresh volatility in energy prices and global bond markets, he said.Developments in US monetary policy, movements in global bond yields and expectations surrounding the upcoming Union Budget, particularly the government's borrowing requirements, will also remain key factors influencing the domestic bond market.On the positive side, improving foreign portfolio investor (FPI) inflows, stable currency conditions and supportive domestic liquidity could provide some cushion to bond yields, Pai said."Overall, the bond market is likely to remain range-bound with a mild upward bias in yields, as investors closely monitor inflation dynamics, crude oil prices, liquidity conditions and the RBI's policy stance," he said.
