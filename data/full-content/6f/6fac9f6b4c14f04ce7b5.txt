Articles 2 min read India's gold imports plunge 62.57% to $5.51 billion in October as prices fall below $4,000/oz India's gold imports fell 39.9% to $24.78 billion during April-October 2026, down from $41.24 billion a year ago, according to Commerce Ministry data. October imports plunged 62.57% year-on-year to $5.51 billion amid a correction in global gold prices. Meanwhile, India's gems and jewellery exports rose to $18.50 billion during the seven-month period. Exports rose to $49.47 billion from $44.40 billion, while imports increased to $89.35 billion. The trade figures reflect pressures from high import costs, particularly in energy markets amid global uncertainty. India imports nearly 85% of its crude oil, impacting the overall trade balance significantly.The WGC report highlighted that two of the three historical conditions associated with a rebound in gold prices have already been met, although elevated speculative positioning remains a concern. Advt Gold ended October at USD 3,913 per ounce, declining 6.3 per cent during the month, with prices falling across all major currencies tracked by the council. The precious metal is currently trading around 25 per cent below its record high of USD 5,405 per ounce, reached in January 2026.The WGC noted that technical indicators and a strengthening US dollar suggest the recent sell-off may be nearing exhaustion. However, speculative positions have yet to unwind sufficiently to support a sustained recovery."Today, two of the three conditions are in place. Gold is trading well below its 200-day average and the dollar has rallied into its top tier. Positioning, however, remains elevated," the report stated.
