The accolade underscores Tata Communications’ strategic transition from traditional connectivity to becoming a pivotal digital infrastructure partner, aiding large enterprises in simplifying operations, enhancing resilience, and accelerating digital transformation. The company’s long-term growth strategy focuses on addressing the intricate nature of enterprise technology environments. By integrating global connectivity, cloud, security, edge, customer experience, automation, and end-to-end orchestration, Tata Communications provides a unified operating model that modernises digital infrastructure. Its software-defined platforms and proprietary technologies enhance visibility, centralise management, and establish secure, resilient foundations essential for scaling AI and digital workloads. Tata Communications’ approach combines infrastructure, platforms, and managed services to meet enterprise needs through interconnected capabilities rather than isolated solutions. The company employs AI-enabled life cycle processes to support service feasibility, provisioning, delivery, assurance, and account management. This proactive monitoring and unified control improve operational visibility and service reliability, enabling enterprises to pre-emptively identify service risks, streamline workflows, and bolster service assurance. Ganesh Lakshminarayan, Managing Director and CEO of Tata Communications, highlighted the company’s global reach, with over 60% of revenue derived from international markets, while also being recognised as a leader in India. He emphasised the company’s strength in integrating its five operational fabrics—network, cloud, cybersecurity, interaction, and intelligence—to craft tailored solutions that meet customer needs. The resilience and accountability of these solutions, he noted, are key to earning the trust of large enterprises. Frost & Sullivan commended Tata Communications for its high standards in strategic execution, platform leadership, and market responsiveness. The company’s digital portfolio and dedication to large enterprises are shaping the future of India’s communications service provider market, enabling organisations to pursue innovation with enhanced efficiency and control. This recognition reflects Tata Communications’ role as a forward-thinking organisation reshaping the industry through innovation and growth excellence.
