The authorised manufacturers must comply with the procedures prescribed under Rules 55, 56, 58, 59 and 62 of the said Rules, along with the conditions specified in the notification. Drugs or formulations containing imported alkaloids must be exported within 180 days from the date of import. An extension not exceeding 30 days may be granted upon submission of an application to the Narcotics Commissioner specifying the reasons and grounds for seeking such extension. However, the total period, including extensions, cannot exceed 270 days under any circumstances. Any imported alkaloids or finished formulations intended for export that remain unutilised must be surrendered to the Government Opium and Alkaloids Works The manufacturers must also comply with additional conditions and limitations prescribed by the Narcotics Commissioner. The notification shall remain in force until 31 December 2027. Any import certificate issued in Form 4A up to 31 December 2027 shall remain valid for 180 days from its date of issue. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 3rd November S.O. 6351(E).— In pursuance of the proviso to rule 54 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Rules, 1985 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), the Central Government hereby notifies that the manufacturers mentioned in the Table below are permitted to import morphine, codeine, thebaine and their salts (hereinafter called alkaloids) for use exclusively in manufacture of drugs or formulations to be exported and the said manufacturers shall comply with the procedure specified in rules 55, 56, 58, 59 and 62 of the said rules, and the following conditions, namely :- a. the drug or the formulation containing the imported alkaloids shall be exported within a period of one hundred and eighty days from the date of import which may be extended by a further period not exceeding thirty days by making an application to the Narcotics Commissioner with the reasons and grounds for seeking such extension: Provided that the total period including extension thereof shall not exceed two hundred and seventy days in any case; b. any quantity of alkaloids or finished formulations for export that remain unutilised shall be surrendered to the Government Opium and Alkaloids Works; and c. such other conditions and limitations as may be specified by the Narcotics Commissioner.
