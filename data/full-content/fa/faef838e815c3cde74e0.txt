The window is available from 13 October 2026 at 11:00 AM until 19 October 2026 at 5:30 PM. During this period, students may submit requests for examination enrolment with a late fee, addition of a module with a late fee, and exemption on the basis of higher qualification. The announcement specifies late fees for examination enrolment and module addition. The online window for change services, covering changes of examination centre, medium, group and elective subject, will remain closed throughout the reopened enrolment period. Requests for these changes may instead be submitted from 20 October 2026 to 21 November 2026 up to 4:00 PM. Requests received thereafter will not be entertained under any circumstances. Students who have not completed the Pre-Examination Test may complete it between 13 October 2026 and 18 October 2026, latest by 5:30 PM. Executive students must also ensure completion of TDOP, if pending, on or before 18 October 2026. The announcement therefore provides separate timelines for enrolment, change services and completion of the pending examination-related requirements. THE INSTITUTE OF COMPANY SECRETARIES OF INDIA ATTENTION STUDENTS!! October 13, 2026 Sub.: Reopening of Online Examination Enrolment Window for December 2026 Session of CS Examinations This is to inform that the Online Examination Enrolment window for the December 2026 Session of CS Examinations will reopen from 13th October 2026 at 11:00 AM to 19th October 2026 till 5:30 PM and the students may submit request(s) for: – a) Examination enrolment (with Late Fee) b) Addition of Module (with Late Fee) c) Exemption on the basis of higher qualification During the period from 13th October 2026 at 11:00 AM to 19th October 2026 till 5:30 PM, the online window for change services, (i.e., change of Centre, Medium, Group & Elective Subject) shall remain closed. Students may submit request(s) for change of Examination Centre, Medium, Group and Elective Subject during the period from 20th October 2026 to 21st November 2026 up to 04:00 P.M. Thereafter, requests for any change shall NOT be entertained under any circumstances. Students may also complete the Pre-Examination Test (if not yet completed) during the period from 13th October 2026 to 18th October 2026 latest by 05:30 P.M. Executive Students are requested to ensure completion of the TDOP (if not already completed) on or before 18th October 2026. All students may take note of the above.
