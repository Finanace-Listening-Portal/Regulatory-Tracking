Articles 2 min read Debit card transactions remain weak in August; credit card volumes rise 26%, spends however soften Debit card transaction volumes increased around 2 per cent month-on-month in August 2026, while transaction value rose about 1 per cent, according to RBI data analysed by 1Lattice. On a year-on-year basis, however, debit card transaction volumes declined around 10 per cent and value fell about 6 per cent. Credit card transaction volumes grew around 4 per cent sequentially and 26 per cent annually, while transaction value declined about 3 per cent month-on-month but increased around 6 per cent year-on-year. Total debit card transaction value also rose about 1 per cent from July.On a year-on-year basis, however, debit card transactions remained under pressure. Total transaction volume declined approximately 10 per cent, while transaction value contracted around 6 per cent, indicating continued weakness in debit card usage as consumers increasingly shift towards other digital payment modes.ATM transactions continued to account for the bulk of debit card activity. In August, ATM transactions contributed around 80 per cent of total debit card transaction volume, while POS transactions accounted for 14.8 per cent and e-commerce transactions for 5.4 per cent. Advt In value terms, ATM transactions accounted for nearly 86 per cent of total debit card transaction value in August. POS transactions contributed 8.8 per cent, while e-commerce transactions accounted for around 5.2 per cent.Credit cards, meanwhile, recorded stronger growth in transaction volumes in August. Total credit card transaction volume increased around 4 per cent month-on-month and approximately 26 per cent year-on-year.However, the growth in transaction volumes did not translate into higher spending sequentially. Credit card transaction value declined around 3 per cent month-on-month to Rs 2.02 lakh crore in August, from Rs 2.08 lakh crore in July. On a year-on-year basis, transaction value grew approximately 6 per cent.E-commerce continued to account for the largest share of credit card transactions by value. Online transactions represented around 60.7 per cent of total credit card transaction value in August, while POS transactions accounted for 39.1 per cent. ATM transactions remained marginal at around 0.2 per cent.By volume, e-commerce transactions accounted for 50.9 per cent of total credit card transactions in August, marginally higher than the 48.3 per cent share of POS transactions. ATM transactions contributed only around 0.1 per cent.While credit card transaction volumes rose around 4 per cent from July, transaction value declined around 3 per cent, suggesting that the increase in transaction activity was accompanied by a lower average transaction value. Advt E-commerce's share of credit card transaction volume increased from 48.7 per cent in the April-June 2026 period to 50.9 per cent in August, while its share in transaction value stood at 60.7 per cent.In debit cards, the dominance of ATM transactions remained broadly unchanged. ATM transactions accounted for 79.9 per cent of volume and 85.9 per cent of value in August, compared with 80 per cent and 85.3 per cent, respectively, in July.
