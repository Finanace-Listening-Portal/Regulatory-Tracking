Articles 1 min read UBS says it does not permit the use of external AI models for client transactions Sergio Ermotti, the CEO of UBS, has declared that external AI models will not be allowed for transactions involving clients. This decision comes amid heightened concerns from leading banks regarding the effects of AI on financial stability, leading several major US banks to block third-party AI interactions with their customer accounts. Ermotti warned against relying on opaque AI models and shared UBS's dedication to advancing technology post-Credit Suisse merger.
