} } Listen to This ArticleAdani Wind, the wind manufacturing division of Adani New Industries Limited (ANIL), on Monday said it had commenced production of India's longest onshore wind turbine blades, measuring 91.2 metres, at its integrated manufacturing facility in Mundra, Gujarat. The company, which is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Adani Enterprises, has also secured certification for its MWL185-5.0 MW wind turbine platform from WindGuard Certification GmbH, an independent German wind turbine testing and certification body, clearing the way for serial production. The blades have been developed for the 5 MW platform, which features a rotor diameter of 185 metres and a hub height of 140 metres. The rotor sweeps an area of nearly 26,600 square metres, equivalent to more than three and a half standard football pitches, the company said in a press release. The platform is designed to capture wind at varying speeds, including at low- and moderate-wind locations, and improve energy generation and capacity utilisation.
