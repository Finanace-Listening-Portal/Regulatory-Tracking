The Hon’ble Allahabad High Court in the case of CIT vs., Goyal Private Family Specific Trust [1988] 171 ITR 698 held as under : “There is no finding by the Commissioner that the Income-tax Officer reached an erroneous conclusion and that, on the facts and circumstances of the case, the conclusion would have been different. Writing an order in detail may be a legal requirement, but the order not fulfilling this 142 ITA.Nos.383, 384, 385 & 386/Del./2021 Smt. Abha Bansal, Sh Pankaj Bansal, Sh Basant Bansal And Sh Roop Kumar Bansal, Gurgaon. requirement, cannot be said to be erroneous and prejudicial to the interest of the Revenue. The Commissioner having failed to point out any error, no error can be inferred from the orders of the Income-tax Officer for the simple reason that they are bereft of details.
