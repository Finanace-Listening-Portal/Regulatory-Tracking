Excluding this component, global advances grew 13.5%, indicating a more moderate pace of underlying credit expansion. FCNR(B) deposits also accounted for 72% of domestic deposit accretion during the quarter.Bank of Baroda’s international advances rose 22% sequentially in the September quarter, including Rs 60,500 crore of self-leverage against FCNR(B) deposits. Excluding this component, global advances grew 13.5% year on year, while domestic loans also expanded at the same rate, according to a report by Nomura. Advt The bank’s total deposits increased 16.4% year on year and 6.8% sequentially. FCNR(B) deposits rose Rs 73,500 crore during the quarter to Rs 1.03 lakh crore, accounting for 72% of domestic deposit accretion. Excluding FCNR(B) deposits, domestic deposits grew 10.1% year on year and 2.1% sequentially.The numbersThe reported CASA ratio declined 136 basis points sequentially to 36.4%. However, excluding FCNR(B) deposits, the ratio improved 54 basis points to 39.1%.Domestic credit growth was led by retail, agriculture and MSME lending. Retail advances grew 18.6% year on year, with auto loans rising 28%. Agricultural advances increased 19%, driven by 36% growth in agricultural gold loans. MSME advances grew 21% year on year and 7.3% sequentially.Corporate advances, in contrast, grew 6.3% year on year and declined 0.3% sequentially, highlighting the divergence between growth in the bank’s retail and smaller-business portfolios and its corporate lending book.The bank reported a strong earnings performance, with net profit rising 36% year on year to Rs 6,530 crore. Net interest income increased 9% to Rs 13,030 crore, while calculated net interest margin improved three basis points sequentially to 2.59%. Strong fee income helped drive core pre-provision operating profit up 12% to Rs 6,970 crore, although higher operating expenses partly offset the gains. Advt Treasury income was Rs 3,730 crore, compared with Nomura’s estimate of Rs 1,400 crore. The bank used the stronger treasury income to make additional provisions of Rs 1,600 crore. Adjusting for these provisions, credit cost stood at 0.22%, below Nomura’s estimate of 0.35%.Asset quality improved during the quarter. Gross non-performing assets declined 21 basis points sequentially to 1.78%, while net non-performing assets fell 13 basis points to 0.37%. The gross and net slippage ratios improved to 0.79% and 0.16%, respectively. The additional prudential provision lifted the provision coverage ratio by 440 basis points to 79.5%. Advt Management raised its FY27 credit growth guidance to 13-15% and deposit growth guidance to 11-13%. It retained its net interest margin guidance of 2.75-2.95% and expects to reach the upper end of the range in the third and fourth quarters as the repo rate increase is transmitted to its 38% repo-linked loan book.
