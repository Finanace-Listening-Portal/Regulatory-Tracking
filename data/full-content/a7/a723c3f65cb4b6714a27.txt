The working group will function as a standing expert body to examine technical, operational, legal and regulatory matters concerning the establishment and functioning of CICs within the IFSC. Its principal objective is to recommend measures for developing an efficient, secure, interoperable and globally aligned credit information ecosystem, strengthening the infrastructure for collection, reporting and dissemination of credit information. The Technical Working Group will include representatives from Credit Information Companies, banks operating in IFSC and finance companies registered with IFSCA. Representatives from banks and finance companies will serve for three years, while the four CICs will act as convenors by annual rotation in alphabetical order. An IFSCA officer of at least Manager rank will participate as an observer. The group is tasked with reviewing and recommending standardised reporting formats for credit information submitted by Credit Institutions operating in IFSC, proposing procedures for rejection, correction and resubmission of erroneous data, and identifying implementation challenges faced by Regulated Entities and CICs, including onboarding, data submission frequency, reconciliation and reporting. The IFSCA may also assign other matters relating to the collection and dissemination of credit information to the working group. International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) PRESS RELEASE Formation of IFSCA Technical Working Group on CIC The International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA), with an objective to develop the financial products and financial services in the Gujarat International Finance Tec-City International Financial Services Centre (GIFT IFSC) has constituted a Technical Working Group on Credit Information Companies. Objective: The Technical Working Group (TWG) shall function as a standing expert body to examine technical, operational, legal and regulatory issues relating to the establishment and functioning of Credit Information Companies (CICs) in the International Financial Services Centre (IFSC). The TWG shall make recommendations to IFSCA for developing an efficient, secure, interoperable and globally aligned credit information ecosystem within IFSC. Constitution: The constitution of the committee would include one representative from each CIC, if available, two representatives, one each from banks present in IFSC and two representatives, one each from Finance Company registered with IFSCA. The membership of the working group will be for a period of three years for representatives of the banks and finance companies. The four CICs shall act as convenor of the TWG by rotation (for each calendar year), in alphabetical order of the name. An officer of IFSCA not below the rank of Manager will be nominated as the observer to this working group. The current constitution of the working group is as per Annex I below. Terms of Reference: The terms of reference for the group shall be as follows: i. Review and recommend standardised data reporting formats for reporting credit information by Credit Institutions operating in IFSC and make recommendations to IFSCA in this regard. ii. Recommend procedures for rejection, correction and resubmission of erroneous/wrong data, if applicable iii. Identify implementation challenges faced by Regulated Entities and CICs and Examine issues relating to onboarding, data submission frequency, reconciliation and reporting. iv. Any other matter relating to collection and dissemination of credit information as the IFSCA may assign from time to time.
