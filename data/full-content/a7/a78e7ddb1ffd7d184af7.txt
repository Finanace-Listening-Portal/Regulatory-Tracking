Advt "The trajectory of crude oil prices, domestic inflation and Reserve Bank of India's liquidity management will also remain key drivers of the domestic bond market."US Treasury yields steadied after last week's sharp rise, suggesting that investors are beginning to absorb the likelihood of tighter financial conditions, while the benchmark Brent crude contract was also consolidating around $92 per barrel levels.The minutes from the Fed's latest meeting indicated that support for keeping interest rates unchanged was less unanimous than the headline 10-2 vote initially suggested.The prospect of firmer policy action from the Fed in December reflects confidence in the resilience of the US economy as well as the Fed's commitment to bringing inflation under control, traders said.Meanwhile, even as oil prices have steadied, the outlook remains sensitive to developments around tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz and any extended disruption could tighten global supply conditions quickly.For India, a major energy importer, this remains an important macroeconomic risk because higher oil prices can add pressure to the current account deficit, the rupee and domestic inflation.RATESOvernight index swap rates also ended little changed amid consolidation around current levels.The one-year rate ended at 6.51%, while the two-year rate ended at 6.485%. The liquid five-year rate settled at 6.5075%.
