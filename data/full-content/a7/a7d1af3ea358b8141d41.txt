The 10-year government bond yield has risen 25-40 basis points since August, while yields on three and five-year AAA-rated NBFC bonds have increased 50-60 basis points since June as markets factor in a possible repo rate hike, JM Financial said in a report. Advt The NBFCs under coverage are expected to report a return on assets (RoA) of about 3% and return on equity (RoE) of 15.1% in Q2FY27. Modest expansion in net interest margins is expected to be largely offset by a marginal increase in credit costs. Resilient retail demand, a gradual recovery in loans against property (LAP) and a rising share of gold loans are expected to support diversified financiers and MSME and gold lenders.For vehicle financiers, disbursement momentum is expected to remain healthy, although credit costs could rise for Shriram Finance and Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services. Housing finance companies (HFCs) and affordable housing finance companies (AHFCs) are expected to see AUM growth rise to about 13% year-on-year, driven by about 24% growth in disbursements.Diversified NBFCsAUM growth for diversified financiers is expected to remain robust at about 26% year-on-year, driven by resilient retail demand and disbursement growth of 24% year-on-year and 11% sequentially. Poonawalla Fincorp is expected to post about 55% AUM growth, followed by Aditya Birla Capital, Piramal Finance, Bajaj Finance and L&T Finance at 26-27%. Tata Capital is expected to grow about 24%, while HDB Financial Services is likely to lag at about 12%.Margins for diversified financiers are expected to remain stable at about 8.1%, while credit costs are estimated to decline marginally to about 1.7%. Piramal Finance, Poonawalla Fincorp and L&T Finance are better placed for margin expansion on the back of a better mix, rating upgrade and lower liquidity. NIMs at Bajaj Finance and HDB Financial Services could decline by about 10 basis points sequentially. Advt Vehicle financiersVehicle financiers are expected to report about 18% year-on-year AUM growth, with disbursements rising 22% year-on-year and 4% sequentially. At Shriram Finance, the recent equity infusion is expected to support margins and offset some of the drag on RoA from higher credit costs.Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company's margins are expected to remain stable, supported by a better loan mix, while a slight improvement in credit costs is expected to be offset by higher operating expenses. Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services' RoA is expected to decline because of lower margins, higher credit costs and operating expenses. Advt Housing financiersHousing financiers are expected to report AUM growth of about 13% year-on-year and 4% sequentially, supported by disbursement growth of about 24% year-on-year and 17% sequentially on a seasonal pickup.Margins are expected to remain broadly stable at 3.33%. Credit costs are expected to normalise to about 13 basis points, largely because LIC Housing Finance's credit cost is expected to move into positive territory at 13 basis points in Q2FY27 from negative 20 basis points in Q1FY27. This is expected to compress its RoA by about 23 basis points sequentially.Profit after tax for HFCs is expected to decline about 2.4% sequentially, while year-on-year growth is expected to slow to about 5% from 14% in Q1FY27 and 12% in Q2FY26.Gold, MSME and LAP lenders Advt Gold, MSME and LAP-focused lenders are expected to report AUM growth of about 27% year-on-year and 6% sequentially, supported by a gradual recovery in LAP and healthy growth in gold loans. Disbursements are expected to rise about 33% year-on-year.Fedbank Financial Services is expected to remain the fastest-growing lender in the segment, with AUM growth of about 37%, followed by SBFC Finance at about 27%. Five-Star Business Finance's disbursement growth is expected to rise to about 70% year-on-year on a low base.Aggregate profit after tax for the segment is expected to rise about 13% year-on-year and 5% sequentially, led by Fedbank Financial Services and SBFC Finance on operating leverage. Five-Star's profit is expected to remain subdued because of lower margins, higher credit costs and operating expenses.NBFC-MFIsNBFC-MFIs are expected to see AUM growth accelerate to about 21% year-on-year from 17% in Q1FY27, supported by sustained disbursement momentum, higher-ticket lending and a recovery in the core microfinance business.Disbursements are expected to grow about 28% year-on-year, while credit costs are projected at around 3.3%, compared with 3.2% in Q1FY27. Lenders are expected to build a buffer against the impact of a deficient monsoon, which could weigh on rural collections in the second half of FY27.NIMs are expected to remain broadly stable at about 15%, while improving operating leverage is expected to lift RoA to 4.9%.The preferred picks are Aditya Birla Capital and Piramal Finance among diversified NBFCs, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company among vehicle financiers, PNB Housing Finance and Aadhar Housing Finance among HFCs and AHFCs, Fedbank Financial Services and Five-Star Business Finance among MSME lenders, and CreditAccess Grameen among MFIs.
