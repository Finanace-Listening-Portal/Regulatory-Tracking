Articles 5 min read HDFC Bank and Kotak get new CEOs, while insurers, wealth firms and fintechs make key hires In a significant shift within India's banking and financial services landscape, several notable appointments have been announced. Sudhakar Malli has been appointed as Executive Director at RBI, effective October 1. Meanwhile, Anup Bagchi and Anup Kumar Saha have taken on roles as MD & CEO at HDFC Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank respectively. Additionally, Lavanya R Mundayur has been appointed CMD of New India Assurance, effective September 30. The biggest move came at RBI, HDFC Bank, Kotak, South Indian Bank, a public-sector insurer, two Bajaj companies and a fintech all made senior appointments around the same time. One coincidence stands out: the two largest private-bank CEO announcements involve naming Anup Bagchi at HDFC and Anup Saha at Kotak.RBI appoints Sudhakar Malli as new Executive DirectorRBI has appointed Sudhakar Malli as Executive Director, effective October 1, with responsibility for the Department of Supervision (Supervisory Assessment). A career central banker with nearly three decades of experience, Malli has extensive expertise in supervising banks, NBFCs and cooperative banks. Advt HDFC Bank appoints Anup Bagchi as MD & CEOBagchi will take over from Sashidhar Jagdishan, whose tenure ends at the close of business hours on October 26, 2026. The new appointment is for three years and runs until October 26, 2029. The handover marks the end of Jagdishan's term at the head of the country's largest private-sector lender. Bagchi brings more than three decades of experience across banking, capital markets, wealth management and insurance, with a long association with the ICICI Group. He has also served as Executive Director at ICICI Bank and CEO & MD of ICICI Securities. His appointment adds another major leadership transition to HDFC Bank’s management landscape this year.Kotak Mahindra Bank appoints Anup Kumar Saha as MD & CEOSaha is already on the bank's board as a whole-time director. Since March 2026 he has overseen retail banking, data analytics and marketing. He joined Kotak only in January this year, so his rise to the top job has come within a few months of his arrival. He has been overseeing the Retail Bank, Government Business, Data Analytics and Marketing functions bringing more than 32 years of professional experience, including 25 years across banking and NBFCs.Mahesh Muralidhar Pai takes charge as MD & CEO of South Indian BankMahesh Muralidhar Pai has taken charge as Managing Director & CEO of South Indian Bank for a three-year term, effective October 1, 2026. With nearly three decades of experience at Canara Bank, including leading digital banking and innovation, Pai is expected to focus on digital transformation, stronger execution, customer relationships and disciplined business growth at South Indian Bank. Advt Government appoints Lavanya Mundayur as CMD of New India AssuranceThe Centre has approved Lavanya R Mundayur for the top post at New India Assurance Company Ltd. She took charge on September 30, 2026, replacing the arrangement where the role was held under additional charge. Her appointment is set to run until May 31, 2029, or until further orders. The move brings a new leadership chapter to New India Assurance, as Mundayur’s experience spans the insurance sector and public-sector insurance institutions.HDFC Bank names V.N. Srivatsan as Chief Compliance OfficerSrivatsan has about 34 years of professional experience, 25 of which are in BFSI. His three-year term at HDFC Bank begins on his joining date, January 4, 2027. He previously handled retail banking compliance and regulatory inspection, with responsibilities spanning compliance frameworks, regulatory reviews and engagement with senior management, boards and regulators. He will join HDFC Bank from ICICI Bank, where he currently heads compliance for overseas banking and subsidiaries and serves as Data Privacy Officer. Advt HDFC Bank Group General Counsel Sudhir Kumar Jha retires after 22 yearsJha stepped down on September 30, 2026, after 22 years with the bank. HDFC Bank acknowledged his contribution to its legal and compliance framework. Jigar Shah takes over as General Counsel from October 1, 2026. This is the third senior HDFC Bank change in this list. Jha’s tenure included his association with both Housing Development Finance Corporation and HDFC Bank, where he contributed to the bank’s legal and compliance framework.Bajaj Capital appoints Samir Dewan as Group CEO of SBGDewan, who previously worked at IndusInd Bank, joins the wealth management firm to drive integration and scale across its businesses. He will lead the group’s wealth businesses and report directly to Joint Chairman & Managing Director Sanjiv Bajaj. Dewan brings over three decades of experience in wealth management, private banking and affluent banking across India and international markets. His mandate includes building greater scale and integration across the group’s wealth businesses, while Sanjiv Bajaj focuses on the group’s longer-term vision and strategic partnerships. Advt Bajaj Life Insurance elevates Vivek Gupta as Chief Operations and CX OfficerBajaj Life Insurance has elevated Vivek Gupta to Chief Operations & Customer Experience Officer, expanding his leadership responsibilities across the company’s operations and customer experience functions. Gupta brings more than two decades of experience in the insurance industry, having worked across business transformation, innovation and strategic growth. He previously served as Head of Strategy at the company.Groww appoints Santosh Hegde as Head of GrowthFintech platform Groww appointed Santosh Hegde as Head of Growth, with responsibilities spanning acquisition, brand, content, distribution and user engagement. Hegde joins Groww after a two-year stint at travel-tech company Atlys, where he led global marketing and managed brand, creative, content, performance marketing, revenue and growth. His earlier experience includes leadership roles at Licious and Disney+ Hotstar, along with seven years in brand management at Marico.This round of movements is notable not just for the number of appointments, but for the range of functions changing hands. A clear mix of leadership succession and functional strengthening: RBI, HDFC Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank have new CEO tenures, New India Assurance has a new CMD, and financial firms are simultaneously bringing experienced executives into areas closer to regulation, customer engagement, wealth and growth. For the BFSI sector, the leadership map is therefore changing at multiple levels from the corner office to the functions increasingly central to how financial businesses scale, manage risk and engage customers.
