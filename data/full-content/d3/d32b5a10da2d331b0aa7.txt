Advt About Moneyview Ltd.Incorporated in 2014, Moneyview Ltd. is an India-based fintech company that provides digital financial services through its mobile platform. Its offerings include personal loans, credit tracking and financial management solutions, with technology and data analytics used to assess creditworthiness and facilitate faster loan approvals.As of June 30, 2026, the platform had 140.28 million registered users and 48 financial partners, including banks, NBFCs, insurers and other financial institutions.The company has expanded beyond personal loans into insurance, credit cards, digital gold, payments and earned wage access. Its technology- and AI-led model is designed to provide a largely unassisted customer journey while supporting scalability and cost efficiency.
