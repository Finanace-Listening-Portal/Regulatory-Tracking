D’Souza is Group Head – Business Banking, Emerging Enterprises and Micro Enterprises, while Roy is Group Head – Operations, ATM and Administration. HDFC Bank's FY25 disclosure had listed both at 58 as of March 31, 2025. Advt The largest concentration is projected for 2028, when three senior executives could reach the benchmark. Ashish Parthasarthy, who was 58 in the 2026 reference year, heads functions spanning branch banking, payments, treasury, liability products, marketing, virtual channels and infrastructure. N Srinivasan, also projected at 58, is Group Head – Lending Operations, while Arun Mediratta is Group Head – Retail Branch Banking and Alternate Banking Channel & Partnerships.Parthasarthy was 57 in HDFC Bank's FY25 remuneration disclosure, while Srinivasan was 57. The same disclosure lists Parthasarthy with 35 years of experience and Srinivasan with 34 years.The next two potential transitions fall in 2029. Rakesh Singh, 57 in the 2026 reference year, oversees private banking, international banking, financial institutions and BaaS, while Arup Kumar Rakshit, also 57, is Group Head – Treasury. HDFC Bank's FY25 disclosure had listed Singh at 56 and Rakshit at 56 as of March 31, 2025.In 2030, two more senior executives could reach 60 — Vinayak Ravindra Mavinkurve and Ravi Santhanam. Mavinkurve is Group Head – Realty Business Finance, while Santhanam is Chief Marketing Officer. Both were 55 in HDFC Bank's FY25 disclosure, with Mavinkurve having 30 years of experience and Santhanam 32 years. Advt Five of the nine potential transitions fall in 2027-28, making those two years the most concentrated period in the projected timeline.The count excludes Jimmy M Tata, who was 60 in the 2026 retirement timeline. Tata has since been appointed as a Whole-time Director for a three-year term, taking him out of the age-60 senior-management superannuation pool considered here. HDFC Bank's board had approved his appointment alongside Rangan's reappointment in September.The actual superannuation dates would depend on the applicable terms and decisions concerning each executive.KMP ChangesAt the KMP and whole-time director level, HDFC Bank has undergone a series of changes as it prepares for the leadership transition. Anup Bagchi has been appointed MD & CEO for a three-year term, replacing Sashidhar Jagdishan, who opted not to seek a third term. Puneet Sharma joined as CFO-designate and is scheduled to take over as CFO from December 1, while V Srinivasa Rangan has been reappointed as Executive Director for a further term from November 23, 2026 to November 22, 2027. Jimmy Tata has also been appointed as a Whole-time Director for three years. Advt Exits in the pastChairman Atanu Chakraborty’s resignation citing concerns over “values and ethics” came in March this year. Over the past two years across its board and top management, the departures include CHRO Vinay Razdan, Group Head Rahul Shyam Shukla, Retail Assets head Arvind Kapil, Payments and Consumer Finance head Parag Rao, and Independent Director P J Jayasinghe, and Executive Director Bhavesh Zaveri stepped down in April 2026 after completing his three-year term.
