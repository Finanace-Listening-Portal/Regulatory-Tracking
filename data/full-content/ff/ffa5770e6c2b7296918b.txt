Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Oct 23, 2026 at 11:29 PM IST var news_msid = 135152837; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook April 1, 2027: RBI sets implementation date for the Basel III Standardised Approach for operational-risk capital.Detailed mapping: Central bank specifies indicative mapping of Business Indicator items to banks' financial-statement schedules.Derivatives: Interest-rate derivative assets affecting interest income to be captured at fair value, with negative fair values not deducted.The Reserve Bank of India has set April 1, 2027 as the implementation date for the Basel III Standardised Approach for operational-risk capital, while adding detailed mapping of Business Indicator components to financial-statement schedules and clarifying treatment of leases and derivatives.The central bank, in an amendment to its 2025 Directions issued on Friday, said the provisions under the chapter on minimum capital requirements for operational risk will come into effect from April 1, 2027.The 2025 Directions had already prescribed the shift from the existing Basic Indicator Approach to the Basel III Standardised Approach, but had said that the effective implementation date would be communicated separately. Advt The new framework is based on three components — the Business Indicator (BI), Business Indicator Component (BIC) and Internal Loss Multiplier (ILM). The BI is calculated as the sum of the Interest, Lease and Dividend Component (ILDC), Services Component (SC) and Financial Component (FC).Detailed mapping of Business Indicator itemsThe latest amendment provides an indicative mapping of the BI components to specific schedules in banks' balance sheets and profit and loss accounts.The 2025 framework had said that such mapping of BI items with schedules and line items of the prescribed financial-statement format would be provided separately.Under the amended framework, interest income under the ILDC is mapped to Schedules 13 and 14, while interest expenses are mapped to Schedules 15 and 16. Interest-earning assets are mapped to Schedules 6, 7, 8, 9 and 11.RBI has clarified that the mapping is indicative. Where the measurement, classification or presentation of an item under the financial-statement Directions differs from that prescribed under the operational-risk framework, banks will have to ensure that the items used for calculating operational-risk capital are consistent with the descriptions and sub-items prescribed under the framework. Advt The amendment also specifically adds rental income from operating leases to the typical sub-items under interest income. On the expense side, it specifies rental expenses, depreciation and impairment of operating leased assets.Clarification on interest-rate derivativesRBI has also clarified the treatment of interest-rate derivative assets while calculating interest-earning assets.Interest-rate derivative assets that, depending on accounting treatment, affect a bank's interest income and are included in the ILDC will have to be included in interest-earning assets.Such derivative assets will be captured at fair value and not notional value. RBI has further specified that the fair value used should be positive and that a negative fair value should not be subtracted. Advt The underlying definition of interest-earning assets remains broad and includes gross outstanding loans and advances, interest-bearing securities including government securities and leased assets, with non-performing assets also included.Operational-loss historyThe latest amendment does not fundamentally alter the operational-risk capital methodology prescribed in 2025.Under the framework, the Business Indicator is used to calculate the Business Indicator Component, while the Internal Loss Multiplier acts as a scaling factor based on a bank's historical operational losses.The 2025 framework had already prescribed the use of historical operational-loss data in calculating the Loss Component and provided for the use of up to 10 years of loss data, subject to the specified requirements. It also laid down requirements for identification, collection, validation and independent review of internal loss data for banks in the relevant buckets. Advt Banks are also required to disclose BI sub-items and, for banks in buckets 2 or 3, annual operational-loss data for the last 10 years or for each year for which such data is available.
