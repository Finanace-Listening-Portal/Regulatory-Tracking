Advt Doshi brings over 30 years of experience across banking, NBFCs and capital markets. He joined Mirae Asset Financial Services in 2022 and has held leadership responsibilities across its secured lending business.Earlier, Doshi held senior leadership positions across leading financial institutions, including Bank of Baroda, where he headed Capital Markets, Reliance Financial Services and Reliance Securities, ICICI Bank, and ICICI Capital.Ravi Doshi, Chief Executive Officer, Mirae Asset Financial Services, said, “Over the years, Mirae Asset Financial Services has built its reputation as a trusted and transparent lender, helping thousands of customers meet their financial needs through convenient and hassle-free experiences. I have great admiration for what the team has built under Krishna’s leadership, and I am excited to build upon this strong foundation."Krishna Kanhaiya, former CEO, Mirae Asset Financial Services, said, “After 19 years with the Group, moving on is not easy. It has been a deeply fulfilling journey, during which I have had the opportunity to build and scale multiple businesses and work with an exceptional team. I am happy to be making this transition at a time when the company is in a strong position, with a diversified loan book of Rs 2,800 crore, sustainable profitability and a strong, capable team. I am confident that the team will continue to build on this foundation and take the company to greater heights. I wish Ravi all the very best in his new role and am confident that his deep understanding of the business will help take the company forward.” By Online Bureau , ETHRWorld Published On Sep 11, 2026 at 06:36 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
