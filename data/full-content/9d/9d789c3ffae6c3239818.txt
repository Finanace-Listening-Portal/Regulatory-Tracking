The new MDR would further benefit the company given its strong merchant payment franchise.Pine Labs has a merchant base of more than 1 million, gross transaction value of Rs 17.2 lakh crore and 7.4 billion transactions in FY26. Its digital infrastructure and transaction platform, or DITP, is the largest revenue contributor and is estimated to deliver around 24.5 per cent revenue CAGR over FY26-28, according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. Advt The company has built a merchant commerce platform spanning payment acceptance, affordability, issuing and fintech infrastructure, with DITP and its issuing and acquiring platform, or IAP, generating a mix of recurring and transaction-linked revenue. DITP's contribution margin is expected to remain around 82-83 per cent by FY28.Affordability is expected to remain a key growth driver for DITP. Affordability revenue is estimated to grow around 27 per cent CAGR over FY26-28, while POS rental subscription revenue is expected to grow around 11 per cent. Transaction-led EMI, BNPL and other affordability solutions are expected to support growth.Expanding businessIAP has started to scale up meaningfully, with FY26 GTV growing 24 per cent year-on-year to Rs 64,000 crore. The business is estimated to post around 25 per cent GTV CAGR over FY26-28, aided by prepaid programmes and increasing traction across international markets, while revenue is estimated to grow 23 per cent over the same period.Pine Labs is also expected to expand its merchant franchise, with digital checkout point additions estimated to grow around 17 per cent CAGR over FY26-28. Incremental deployments are increasingly expected to be skewed towards small and mid-sized merchants, where digital payment penetration remains relatively low. Advt Overall, revenue is estimated to grow at a 24 per cent CAGR over FY26-28, while adjusted EBITDA and PAT are expected to grow at 46 per cent and 129 per cent, respectively. Adjusted EBITDA margin is expected to improve to 28.7 per cent by FY28 from around 9 per cent in FY24, aided by the shift towards higher-margin, transaction-led businesses and improving operating leverage.
