Articles 2 min read India bonds track Treasury weakness ahead of debt supply Indian government bonds are currently facing losses as traders brace for an influx of debt supply and mounting inflation pressures. The 10-year US Treasury yield is nearing the 5% mark, fueled by predictions of the Federal Reserve's interest rate hikes in December. With India's retail inflation climbing to 6.09%, expectations rise for further rate adjustments by the Reserve Bank of India. The yield had logged its sharpest one-day jump in a month on Wednesday. Advt India's annual retail inflation accelerated to 6.09% in October from 5.54% in September, government data showed, slightly above the 5.93% market estimate.Traders were relieved that inflation did not overshoot forecasts by much, but its breach of the Reserve Bank of India's 2% to 6% tolerance band cemented wagers that the central bank will raise rates again in December.The RBI lifted rates in October for the first time in nearly four years and adopted a "calibrated tightening" stance. While a 25-basis-point increase is widely expected next month, some traders still see scope for a larger 50-basis-point move.Attention now turns to the Middle East conflict and its impact on crude oil. Oil has retreated to about $92 per barrel from $117 ahead of the US midterm elections, offering some relief to oil-import-dependent India."There is some room for rates to ease if oil prices retrace further, although additional rate hikes on the horizon make a deeper correction unlikely," said Radhika Rao, senior economist at DBS Bank.A renewed oil-price spike or a broader AI-led equity selloff could unsettle risk appetite, she added.RATESOvernight index swap rates edged higher, though the move was capped as inflation was only slightly above forecasts. Advt The 1-year, 2-year and 5-year OIS rates were up 4 to 5 basis points at 6.44%, 6.43% and 6.53%, respectively.
