Articles 1 min read Govt appoint Dinesh Pant and Girija Subramanian as members of IRDAI In a significant restructuring of the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), Dinesh Pant, Managing Director of LIC, and Girija Subramanian, ex-CMD of New India Assurance, have been appointed as new members. Pant's retirement is effective September 24, 2026, while Subramanian stepped down in July. These changes are indicative of the dynamic shifts within India's insurance regulatory landscape.
