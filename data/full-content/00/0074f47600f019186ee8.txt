Articles 2 min read SBI ATM charges waived during proposed bank strike of September 28-30: What customers need to know As the nation prepares for a bank strike from September 28 to September 30, 2026, the State Bank of India has decided to eliminate ATM charges for transactions during this time. Although branches will continue to operate, customers should prioritize completing necessary transactions beforehand and consider using digital banking options. Advt SBI ATM charges waived from September 28 to 30In a social media post on X, SBI said on Thursday that ATM transaction charges would be waived for transactions made through ATMs of any bank in the country during the September 28-30 bank strike.This means customers can use ATMs for their cash requirements during the three-day period without having to worry about the applicable ATM transaction charges covered under the waiver scheme.Customers should, however, check their bank's communication for the exact terms and conditions of the waiver.SBI customers can use ATM and digital banking servicesState Bank of India has advised its customers to complete important branch-related transactions before the proposed strike and use digital and self-service banking channels wherever possible.— TheOfficialSBI (@TheOfficialSBI)Customers can continue to use ATM services as well as digital banking facilities during the strike period. These include mobile banking, internet banking and WhatsApp banking. Customers can also make payments and transfer funds through the Unified Payments Interface (UPI), Immediate Payment Service (IMPS), National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) and the Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS), allowing them to carry out routine banking transactions without visiting a branch.Customers should keep in mind that during the strike, the availability of individual bank services can depend on operational arrangements made by each bank. Alternatively, they sought compensatory leave along with double pay for employees who report for duty on the day.
