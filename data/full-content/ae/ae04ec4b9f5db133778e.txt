Following this operation, overnight borrowing costs surged as the RBI raised rates to tackle inflation. A notable increase in bids suggested strong bank interest. With liquidity conditions set to stabilize, immediate further action may not be necessary. Advt Monday's operation saw the central bank infuse 1 trillion rupees ($10.53 billion) through a seven-day variable rate repo operation, attracting bids worth more than 1.5 trillion rupees ahead of a large tax outflow."It (RBI's infusion) is a symptomatic response to volatility in liquidity conditions," said Sakshi Gupta, an economist at HDFC Bank."As liquidity surplus has normalised, we are back to fine-tuning operations and further durable liquidity absorption measures like open market sale of bonds or foreign exchange sell/buy swaps may not be needed further."The weighted average interbank call money rate ended above the Marginal Standing Facility rate for the first time in this financial year on Friday, and was close to that level on Monday.The central bank's repo rate was at 5.50% and the MSF rate at 5.75%.India's banking system liquidity surplus eased below 2 trillion rupees to 0.7% of deposits as of October 16, for the first time in two-and-a-half months."Since Tuesday is a holiday, we will witness a large chunk of goods and services tax outflows today itself, which must have necessitated a timely intervention," a trader with a private bank said.($1 = 94.9600 Indian rupees)(Reporting by Dharamraj Dhutia; Editing by Harikrishnan Nair)Inflation has stayed above the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) target of 4% for the past three months (Photo: Reuters).
