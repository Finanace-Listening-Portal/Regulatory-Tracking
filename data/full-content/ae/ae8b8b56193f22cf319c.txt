The initiative seeks to evaluate whether the existing framework adequately addresses the financing and listing requirements of emerging innovation-led enterprises, particularly those operating in deep-tech, biotechnology, semiconductors, space technology and other research-intensive sectors, while maintaining investor protection and market integrity. The Working Group will examine eight principal areas, including existing IGP eligibility and listing requirements, suitability for research-stage and pre-revenue enterprises, investor eligibility and participation, public issues and offers for sale, pricing and allocation, minimum subscription requirements, post-listing disclosures and governance, and migration from IGP to the Main Board. It will also benchmark the Indian framework against international regulatory models and recommend necessary amendments, transitional arrangements and implementation measures. The Working Group includes representatives from venture capital, private equity, biotechnology, technology-driven enterprises, industry associations, professional institutions, stock exchanges and SEBI. Shri Srini Srinivasan, Chairman of the Indian Venture and Alternate Capital Association, and Shri Prashanth Prakash, Partner at Accel Partners, have been appointed Co-Chairs. Other members include representatives from Biocon, Skyroot Aerospace, Catamaran Ventures, 3one4 Capital, Kedaara Capital, ICAI, ICSI and relevant government and industry bodies. Members may engage with startups, industry associations and other stakeholders to identify sector-specific challenges and measures to improve access to capital markets. The Working Group will submit its recommendations to SEBI for consideration. The press release announces a regulatory review and does not itself amend the existing IGP regulations. Securities and Exchange Board of India PR No.69/2026 SEBI Constitutes Working Group to Review the Innovators Growth Platform Framework The start-up and innovation ecosystem has evolved significantly, with the emergence of enterprises in areas such as deep-tech, biotechnology, semiconductors, space and other research-intensive sectors. Such enterprises may have distinct financing requirements and business models. In this context, a review of the existing regulatory framework governing the Innovators Growth Platform (IGP) is being undertaken to assess its relevance and effectiveness and to facilitate access to capital markets for innovation-led enterprises, while maintaining appropriate standards of investor protection. Against this backdrop, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has constituted a Working Group to undertake a comprehensive review of the regulatory framework governing the IGP under the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018. The Terms of Reference of the Working Group are as follows: 1. Existing IGP Framework: Review the existing regulatory framework governing IGP, including eligibility and listing requirements, and assess its relevance and effectiveness. 2. Research-stage and Innovation-led Enterprises: Examine the suitability of the IGP framework for research-stage, pre-revenue, IP-led, deep-tech and other innovation- and technology-intensive enterprises. 3. IGP Investor Framework: Review the eligibility, participation and investment requirements applicable to IGP Investors, while ensuring appropriate investor protection. 4. Listing and Capital Raising: Review the framework for listing and capital raising by IGP entities, including public issue, OFS, pricing, allocation and minimum subscription requirements. 5. Post-listing Framework: Review the post-listing regulatory framework applicable to IGP entities, including disclosure, governance, trading and continuing obligations. 6. Migration to Main Board: Review the framework for migration from IGP to the Main Board, including eligibility and procedural requirements. 7. International Benchmarking: Benchmark the IGP framework against relevant international frameworks for innovative, technology-led and growth-stage companies and consider suitable adaptations for the Indian securities market. 8. Recommendations and Implementation: Recommend necessary amendments to the applicable regulatory framework, including transitional and implementation measures, and examine any other matter incidental or consequential to the above.
