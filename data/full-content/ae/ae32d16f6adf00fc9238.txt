Articles 2 min read India bonds slump as oil spike adds to RBI hike bets Indian government bonds experienced a decline this week, largely influenced by rising oil prices and US Treasury yields. The benchmark bond yield reached its highest point in a week at 7.2650% on Friday. Analysts are anticipating a potential increase in interest rates from the Reserve Bank of India in their upcoming policy decision. A surge in oil prices raises inflation concerns, especially since retail inflation exceeded the RBI's tolerance threshold.
