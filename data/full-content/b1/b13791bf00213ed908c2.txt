Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Sep 7, 2026 at 03:24 PM IST var news_msid = 133874843; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Indian FinTechs raised USD 217.2 million across 11 deals in August 2026, according to data collated by 1Lattice.The month witnessed funding activity across wealth management, asset management, financial services, lending, payments, digital banking, insurance technology and artificial intelligence.The largest deal of the month came from Navi, a provider of financial products including UPI, loans, insurance and mutual funds, which raised USD 100 million from Prosus.This was followed by Centricity, a digital private wealth management platform for individuals, which secured USD 27 million from SMBC Asia Rising Fund, Lightspeed, Burman Family and RAAY Global Investments, among others. Advt Another notable transaction was Inrisk Labs, a provider of parametric insurance products and solutions for various industries, which raised USD 27 million from Bessemer Venture Partners and Northpoint Capital.Together, the three largest transactions accounted for USD 154 million, or nearly 71 per cent of the total funding raised during August.Among the other deals during the month:Growwmf, an asset management company, raised USD 20.7 million from State Street Global Advisors.Rezolv, a provider of an artificial intelligence platform for lending and debt collection, secured USD 12.5 million from Norwest, Vertex Ventures and 3one4 Capital.Mintoak, a provider of merchant engagement, payment acceptance and banking platform solutions, raised USD 9 million from BlackSoil.GetVantage, a provider of growth capital and embedded financing for digital brands, raised USD 6.6 million from SanRaj Family Ventures, Chiratae Ventures, Varanium and VCMint.Centricity also raised another USD 6 million from Lightspeed India Partners during the month.Unico Housing Finance, an online lending platform offering mortgage loans, raised USD 5.8 million from Anicut Capital and UC Impower.Consint, a provider of AI-driven solutions for healthcare claims, fraud detection and automation, raised USD 2.3 million from BIG Global Investments USC, Equanimity Investments and Seafund. Advt Savemfi, a financial services provider offering accessible microcredit solutions, raised USD 0.3 million from State Bank of India.Funding activity in August was higher than the previous month, with total funding rising to USD 217.2 million across 11 deals, compared with USD 124.4 million across 8 deals in July 2026.
