Articles 1 min read Supreme Court upholds Bombay HC ruling quashing YES Bank's Rs 8,415 crore AT1 bond write-off The Supreme Court has confirmed the Bombay High Court's verdict that invalidated YES Bank's write-off of AT1 bonds totaling Rs 8,415 crore, part of a March 2020 restructuring plan. Investors, both institutional and retail, took their grievances to the High Court, leading to YES Bank, the RBI, and the Centre appealing this outcome. This ruling now grants bondholders a pathway to recover their investments that were previously at risk.
