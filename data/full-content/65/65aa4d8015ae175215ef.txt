Reserve money grew at 10.3 per cent year-on-year to ₹53.24 trillion. “Reserve money (adjusted for CRR) expanded at a robust pace with growth in currency in circulation recording its highest print over the past five years as on September 30, 2026,” the report, authored by RBI staffers, said.
