Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Train Theme Restaurant Kompally Vs Superintendent of Central Tax (Telangana High Court) Telangana High Court Permits Manual Application for Revocation of Cancelled GST Registration Where GST Portal Does Not Accept Application Summary: The Telangana High Court considered a writ petition filed by Train Theme Restaurant Kompally seeking revocation of its GST registration cancellation. The registration had been cancelled through an order in Form GST REG-19 dated 12 September 2024 for failure to furnish returns for six consecutive months. The petitioner submitted that heavy financial losses had compelled it to discontinue business operations from January 2024 and that its part-time accountant had been instructed to file Nil returns. It contended that the delay was not intentional and that the GST portal did not permit submission of the revocation application because the prescribed time limit had expired. The Revenue maintained that the registration had been cancelled for continuous non-filing of returns. Considering the circumstances, the High Court granted the petitioner liberty to approach the competent authority within two weeks to submit an online application for revocation of cancellation of GST registration. If the portal did not accept the application for technical reasons, the petitioner was permitted to submit it in physical form. The competent authority was directed to entertain the application and decide it in accordance with law within three weeks thereafter. The Court disposed of the writ petition without costs, without directly restoring the GST registration. FULL TEXT OF THE JUDGMENT/ORDER OF TELANGANA HIGH COURT Heard Mr. Mohammed Rafi, learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Dominic Fernandes, learned Senior Standing Counsel for Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) appears for respondent Nos.1 and 3. 2. The Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration certificate of the petitioner bearing No.36AARFT5340F1ZY was cancelled vide impugned order in Form GST REG-19 dated 12.09.2024 on the ground of failure to furnish returns for a continuous period of six months. Thereafter, the petitioner has filed the instant Writ Petition for revocation of cancellation of GST registration. 3. Learned counsel for the petitioner contended that since the petitioner was a small business establishment and due to heavy financial losses in the business, the petitioner was compelled to discontinue his business operations from January, 2024 and since there was no business operations, he instructed his part-time Accountant to file Nil returns and there was no intentional delay. Though the petitioner has sought to file an application for revocation of cancellation of GST registration, the GST portal did not permit the petitioner as being beyond the time limit prescribed for submission. Therefore, the petitioner prays that respondent No.1 may be directed to entertain the petitioner’s application manually and take a decision thereupon in accordance with law. 4. Learned Senior Standing Counsel for CBIC submits that the apparent reason for cancellation of GST registration of the petitioner was on account of non-filing of returns for a continuous period of six months. 5. Having regard to the aforesaid facts and circumstances, we are inclined to allow liberty to the petitioner to approach the competent authority within a period of two weeks from today for submission of application for revocation of cancellation of GST registration online and if it is not being accepted for any technical reasons, then to submit it in physical form. The competent authority would entertain it and take a decision thereon in accordance with law within a period of three weeks thereafter. 6. The instant Writ Petition is accordingly disposed of. There shall be no order as to costs. Miscellaneous applications, if any pending, shall stand closed.
