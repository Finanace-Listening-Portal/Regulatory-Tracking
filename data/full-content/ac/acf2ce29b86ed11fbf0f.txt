Articles 1 min read Pine Labs, WEBXPAY partner to bring UPI, Indian payment options to Sri Lanka The partnership enables Sri Lankan merchants to accept UPI, cards, net banking and other Indian payment options under India’s PA-CB framework, with plans to expand the model across Southeast Asia. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Oct 22, 2026 at 12:39 PM IST var news_msid = 135110863; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Digital payments and fintech firm Pine Labs on Thursday announced a partnership with Sri Lankan digital payments platform WEBXPAY to enable Indian payment methods for Sri Lankan merchants. The partnership will allow merchants to accept UPI, UPI AutoPay, cards, net banking across more than 100 banks.EMI conversions and Credit on UPI under India’s PA-CB framework, with plans to expand the model across Southeast Asia.The partnership is the first Payment Aggregator-to-Payment Aggregator (PA-on-PA) collaboration of its kind in Sri Lanka under India’s RBI-authorised Payment Aggregator-Cross Border (PA-CB) framework, according to the companies. The arrangement also provides for T+1 settlement and is integrated into WEBXPAY’s existing merchant network across hospitality, travel, retail and online commerce. Advt The collaboration comes as India remains Sri Lanka’s largest tourism source market. More than 5.3 lakh Indian travellers visited Sri Lanka in 2025, according to the announcement.The PA-CB framework allows licensed Indian payment aggregators to partner directly with international aggregators, enabling Indian payment methods to be accepted overseas within a regulated framework.“India is Sri Lanka’s largest tourism market and one of our most important economic partners. Indian customers already know and trust payment methods such as UPI, cards and net banking. Bringing that familiarity into the online checkout experience of Sri Lankan merchants can remove a significant point of friction and help businesses convert more Indian demand into revenue,” said Thilak Piyadigama, executive chairman of WEBXPAY,Founded in 2015, WEBXPAY provides digital payment solutions for enterprises, small and medium-sized businesses, and micro, small and medium enterprises across online and physical channels.Pine Labs provides payment, issuing, credit and fintech solutions for merchants, enterprises, financial institutions and consumers across multiple international markets.
