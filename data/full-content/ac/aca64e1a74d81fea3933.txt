} } Listen to This ArticleThe Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on Monday further extended the supersession of the board of Mumbai-based Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank by another year, effective November 24, 2026, citing public interest. The RBI had superseded the bank’s board of directors on November 24, 2023, for one year, citing material concerns arising from poor governance standards. The supersession was subsequently extended by a year each in November 2024 and November 2025. “Upon a review, the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that, in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of supersession beyond the close of business of the bank as on November 23, 2026,” the banking regulator said in a statement. While superseding the board in 2023, the RBI had said no business restrictions had been placed and the bank would continue to carry on its normal banking activities under the guidance of the administrator. The administrator of the bank, Satya Prakash Pathak, will continue for another year, the RBI said.
