The agreement aims to collaborate on the recovery of scandium, a critical mineral, from red mud. The MoU was formalised during the 44th International Conference and Exhibition ICSOBA 2026 in Bengaluru on 17th November 2026. The signing ceremony was attended by key figures, including Shri Brijendra Pratap Singh, Chairman and Managing Director of NALCO, Shri Jagdish Arora, Director of Projects & Technical at NALCO, and Mr. Viktor Mann, Technical Director of RUSAL, along with other senior officials from both organisations. This strategic partnership is part of India’s broader initiative to secure essential mineral resources vital for emerging technologies and clean energy applications. The collaboration between NALCO and RUSAL is anticipated to foster knowledge exchange and explore viable methods for scandium recovery from red mud. RUSAL, a leading entity in the global aluminium industry and the largest producer of low-carbon aluminium worldwide, has pioneered technology for extracting scandium from red mud, a by-product of alumina production. This presents significant opportunities for technology sharing and cooperation in the critical minerals sector. The collaboration is expected to enhance India’s capabilities in the extraction and utilisation of strategic minerals, which are increasingly important for various high-tech and green energy applications. Scandium, in particular, is used in aerospace components, solid oxide fuel cells, and other advanced technology applications, making its recovery from industrial by-products like red mud an attractive proposition. Analysts observe that this partnership could bolster India’s position in the critical minerals market, reducing dependency on imports and fostering technological advancements within the country. The initiative aligns with global trends towards sustainable resource utilisation and low-carbon technologies, potentially offering economic and environmental benefits.
