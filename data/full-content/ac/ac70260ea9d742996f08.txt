#AD A Personal Loan is a convenient financial tool for managing planned and unplanned expenses. However, the repayment discipline you maintain on this loan has a direct and lasting impact on your credit or CIBIL score. The EMIs are supposed to be paid before the deadline of every month. Delayed or unorganised payments can negatively impact a credit profile, which can, in turn, cause difficulties with future loan applications. Payment history is the largest contributor to your CIBIL score, accounting for a significant share of the total Credit utilisation, credit history, types of credit and new enquiries also influence the score On-time repayment of a Personal Loan consistently builds a positive payment history Any default, late payment or settlement is recorded and lowers the payment history component Credit bureaus in India receive repayment data from lenders every 30 to 45 days, so records update regularly The CIBIL score is one of the most important factors lenders evaluate when reviewing a Personal Loan eligibility assessment. A score above 750 is generally considered favourable. Even a single missed or delayed payment can cause a noticeable drop, and repeated defaults can push the score to a level where loan approval becomes difficult. What Happens When You Miss a Personal Loan EMI? Your credit score is directly linked to your loan repayments. When you miss an EMI: The lender marks the payment as overdue in its records and reports it to credit bureaus A late payment fee is charged on the overdue amount as per the loan terms The missed payment is recorded in your credit report and remains visible for up to 7 years Your CIBIL score can drop by 50 to 100 points or more, depending on the severity of the delay(s) Subsequent missed payments compound the damage, making recovery of the score more difficult How Late Payments Affect Personal Loan Eligibility When you miss EMIs or delay payments, it affects your credit score. Consequently, a lower score directly impacts your eligibility for future borrowing. Lenders view a poor repayment history as a signal of financial risk, reducing approval chances A score below 650 can result in rejection of new loan applications or an offer of a lower loan amount Even if approved, a lower score may result in less favourable loan terms Multiple late payment records can lead to loan denial, regardless of current income stability Rebuilding Personal Loan eligibility after a history of late payments requires consistent repayment over a period of time The Difference Between Late Payments and Defaults Not all payment delays have the same severity of impact on your CIBIL score. A payment that is 30 days overdue is considered a minor delay, but is still reported to credit bureaus Payments overdue by 60 to 90 days are treated more seriously and carry a larger score impact Non-payment for 90 days or more is classified as a Non Performing Asset (NPA), resulting in a significant score drop A settled Personal Loan, where the lender accepts less than the full outstanding, is also noted negatively in the credit report Note: Full repayment, even if delayed, is far better than settlement from a credit score perspective Steps to Take If You Cannot Make an EMI on Time If you anticipate difficulty meeting a Personal Loan EMI, proactive communication with the lender is essential. Contact your lender as soon as you foresee a payment difficulty and do not wait until the due date has passed Request a short-term moratorium or restructuring of the EMI schedule if the lender offers this Partial payment is better than no payment, it reduces the outstanding and signals intent to repay Explore liquidating a short-term investment or savings to cover the EMI and avoid a missed payment Document all communications with your lender in case of any disputes around the repayment record Recovering Your CIBIL Score After Late Payments A damaged score can be rebuilt over time with consistent financial behaviour. Resume timely repayments immediately and maintain this without further delays Clear all outstanding dues and penalties on the Personal Loan as quickly as possible Review your credit report through authorised bureaus to confirm all payments are accurately recorded Dispute any incorrect entries, errors in credit reports can unfairly lower your score A consistent repayment record of 12 to 24 months can meaningfully improve Personal Loan eligibility over time Role of Digital Platforms Many financial institutions in India, such as ICICI Bank, offer digital platforms that make managing your Personal Loan and staying on top of EMI due dates straightforward. Set up an auto-debit mandate to ensure EMIs are cleared on time without manual intervention Maintain a sufficient account balance before the EMI due date every month Avoid taking on more credit than your monthly income can comfortably service Review your credit report at least once a year to stay aware of your score and any changes Treat timely Personal Loan repayment as a non-negotiable financial commitment Final Thoughts Late payments on a Personal Loan have consequences that extend beyond a late fee; they damage your CIBIL score and reduce your Personal Loan eligibility for future borrowing. Understanding how payment history shapes your credit profile empowers you to take repayment seriously from the very first EMI.
