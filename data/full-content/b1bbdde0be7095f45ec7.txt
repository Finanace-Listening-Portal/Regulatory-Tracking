Sivasagar, Charaideo, Jorhat and Golaghat (Declared as Natural Disaster), there is an urgent need for the insurance industry to take immediate steps to mitigate the hardships of the affected insured population for quick registration and to expedite claim settlement process. , : In this regard, all Insurance Companies are advised to mobilize all resources to ensure immediate service response as outlined below: : , Immediate Response: Insurers must mobilize resources, including surveyors and investigators, and appoint senior Nodal Officers to oversee claims in affected areas. The Nodal Officer appointment should be communicated to the Chief Secretary immediately.
