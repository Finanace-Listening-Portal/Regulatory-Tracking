Torrent Power, which has already been in discussions with bankers, may look to raise a larger amount through long-term bonds, they added. Advt The companies did not reply to Reuters emails seeking comment.State-run PFC sold 18-month bonds at a coupon of 8.21% and 5-year debt at 8.05%, reflecting the inversion.The current yield dynamics reflect a nuanced interplay of liquidity conditions, credit risk and evolving investor expectations, said Ankit Gupta, founder and managing director of Digifinn, an online bond trading platform.The Reserve Bank of India raised its benchmark repo rate by 25 basis points in October, its first increase in nearly four years. Markets have fully priced in another 25-bp hike in December after data showed October retail inflation breached the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit.Some analysts have also flagged the possibility of a larger 50-bp hike in December, with ICICI Securities Primary Dealership preferring a 50-bp December hike, a 25-bp increase in February and a potentially higher terminal rate in this tightening cycle.Also exacerbating the yield-curve inversion are tight liquidity conditions and robust demand for longer-term bonds, where supply has been limited and valuations remain attractive.India's banking system liquidity surplus averaged around 1.25 trillion rupees ($13.28 billion) in October, sharply down from over 4.5 trillion rupees in the preceding period.
