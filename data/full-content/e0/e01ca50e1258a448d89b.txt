The notification appoints the officer specified in column (3) of the Table to exercise the powers and discharge the duties conferred or imposed on the Commissioner of Customs, Nhava Sheva-V, Jawaharlal Nehru Customs House, for adjudication of the specified show cause notices concerning Meenakshi Trading Corporation and others. The two show cause notices are DRI/MZU/NS/INV-02/2011-12 dated February 10, 2014 and DRI/MZU/NS/INV-02/2011-12 dated February 17, 2014. The Principal Commissioner or Commissioner of Customs (Import-I), Mumbai Customs Zone-I, New Custom House, Ballard Estate, Mumbai-400001, is specified as the common adjudicating authority. The notification further provides that it shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. The notification bears S.O. 5237(E) and F. No. 450/98/2024-Cus-lV and is issued by Anurima Sharma, Director (Customs).
