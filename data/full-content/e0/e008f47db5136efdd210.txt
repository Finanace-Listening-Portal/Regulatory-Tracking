The stock is seen trading close to its 52-week low of ₹223.82 hit on September 29, 2026. The stock hit a 52-week high at ₹325.50 on February 26, 2026. At 10:05 AM, Bank of Baroda (BoB) stock traded with a 2.7 per cent loss at ₹232 on the NSE. The counter saw trades of around 48.80 shares on the NSE. Meanwhile, the Nifty PSU Bank index was down 0.6 per cent. Bank of Maharashtra and Indian Bank - down over 1 per cent each - were the other notable losers. On the other hand, Central Bank of India surged 4.4 per cent. Indian Overseas Bank, Uco Bank and PSB were up around 1 per cent each. Canara Bank quoted 0.8 per cent higher ahead of its Q2 results. What's hurting the sentiment at BoB counter today? B R Shetty, founder of the NMC Healthcare group, has filed a lawsuit in the Bombay High Court seeking $15 billion in damages from BoB and 25 other defendants. The group has alleged that BoB and former NMC executives enabled the diversion of funds, concealed liabilities and subsequently pursued him on guarantees bearing forged signatures. The suit also alleges that BoB ignored repeated anti-money laundering (AML) alerts, extended credit without adequate security, facilitated transactions through supplier accounts allegedly used for round-tripping and concealed its lending exposure from auditors. READ MORE BoB issues clarification BoB in an exchange filing on Wednesday, said it saw media reports of a suit said to have been filed by Dr. Bavaguthu Raghuram Shetty in the Bombay High Court on October 13, 2026. The bank has not been served with such a suit and cannot comment on the contents of pleadings which are untested in court. "If and when the Bank is served, the Bank will defend the suit firmly and take all steps necessary to protect its interests," the release stated.
