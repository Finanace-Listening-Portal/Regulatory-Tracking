Anup Bagchi and Anup Kumar Saha built substantial parts of their careers at ICICI, before moving to other financial institutions and eventually being appointed to lead two of India's largest private sector banks.Bagchi, currently managing director and chief executive of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance, will take over as MD and CEO of HDFC Bank on October 27, succeeding Sashidhar Jagdishan. He joined the ICICI Group in 1992 and has held senior positions across ICICI Bank, ICICI Securities and ICICI Prudential Life. Saha, who joined Kotak Mahindra Bank in January after more than seven years at Bajaj Finance, has been appointed MD and CEO of Kotak Mahindra Bank from January 1, succeeding Ashok Vaswani. He spent 14 years at ICICI Bank before moving to Bajaj Finance. Advt The appointments add to a long list of senior ICICI executives who have moved into leadership positions across banks, insurers, asset managers and other financial institutions. Shikha Sharma, who spent more than two decades with ICICI and helped establish ICICI Prudential Life, went on to head Axis Bank. V. Vaidyanathan, a former ICICI Bank executive director and chief executive of ICICI Prudential Life, has led IDFC FIRST Bank since 2018. Vishakha Mulye, another ICICI veteran, is the managing director and chief executive of Aditya Birla Capital.Stellar peer groupThe alumni network extends beyond bank chief executives. Sonjoy Chatterjee, who spent 16 years at ICICI Bank, is chairman and chief executive of Goldman Sachs India Securities. Kalpana Morparia spent more than two decades at ICICI before becoming chairman of JPMorgan's South and Southeast Asia operations, while Renuka Ramnath, a former ICICI executive, went on to establish private equity firm Multiples.The breadth of the alumni network also reflects the range of businesses in which ICICI executives gained experience. Bagchi's career has spanned treasury, corporate banking, retail banking, capital markets and insurance. Saha's 14 years at ICICI Bank included retail secured assets, business intelligence, retail and rural collections, credit cards and retail structured finance before he moved to Bajaj Finance.The latest appointments come as India's banking industry goes through a period of leadership transitions, increasing the importance of experienced external candidates alongside internal succession pipelines. The movement of ICICI veterans into senior roles at competing banks and other financial institutions has created a pool of executives with experience across retail banking, corporate banking, technology, analytics, insurance, capital markets and consumer finance.
