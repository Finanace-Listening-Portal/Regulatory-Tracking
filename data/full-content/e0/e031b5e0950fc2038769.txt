Advt The central bank’s dollar buying comes against a period of heightened foreign exchange market activity, with the RBI using its reserves and market operations to manage liquidity and volatility in the rupee.The RBI’s outstanding net forward sales stood at USD 200.055 billion at end-August 2026, according to the bulletin.In the currency futures segment, the RBI recorded purchases and sales of USD 5.034 billion each in August, leaving the net position unchanged.The data also showed a sharp change from August 2025, when the RBI had recorded net dollar sales of USD 7.695 billion, with no purchases and sales of USD 7.695 billion in the onshore and offshore OTC segment.
