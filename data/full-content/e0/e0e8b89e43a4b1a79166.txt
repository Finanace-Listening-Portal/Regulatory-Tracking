Articles 1 min read Exclusive: Utkarsh Small Finance Bank ropes in Manmohan Shetty as CFO Manmohan Shetty, who most recently served as CFO of IREP Credit Capital (now Viksit Capital) and previously spent nearly nine years with Barclays, is set to join Utkarsh Small Finance Bank as its Chief Financial Officer, two people aware of the discussion told ETBFSI. The bank’s board meeting is scheduled for September 19, with approval for Shetty’s appointment expected to be taken up at the meeting. Simaria relinquished his CFO responsibilities effective June 20, 2026, ahead of taking charge as Whole-time Director. Advt Shetty was most recently the CFO of IREP Credit Capital (now Viksit Capital), where he worked from February 2016 until March 2026. He is a chartered accountant with over two decades of experience in banking and financial services, with expertise spanning financial control, financial reporting, regulatory reporting, FP&A, treasury management and governance.Before joining IREP Credit Capital, Shetty spent nearly nine years with Barclays, where he served as Vice President – Finance and CFO of Barclays Investments & Loans India.The appointment follows Simaria's elevation to the board-level Whole-time Director role. Utkarsh SFB had announced in June that Simaria would take over as Whole-time Director effective June 22, following approval from the Reserve Bank of India.Simaria had joined Utkarsh SFB in September 2022 and was appointed CFO by the bank's board in October 2022.The leadership changes at Utkarsh also follow the departure of Pramod Kumar Dubey, who served as Executive Director of the bank from September 2024 until December 2025.
