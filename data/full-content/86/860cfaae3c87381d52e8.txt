Gross collections stood at ₹16,03,066.12 crore, compared with ₹13,95,278.39 crore during the corresponding period of FY 2025-26, registering growth of 14.89%. Refunds increased by 24.22% to ₹2,65,199.14 crore from ₹2,13,491.41 crore. After refunds, net direct tax collections reached ₹13,37,866.98 crore against ₹11,81,786.98 crore a year earlier, reflecting growth of 13.21%. Within gross collections, corporate tax contributed ₹7,52,961.56 crore, non-corporate tax ₹8,03,245.26 crore and Securities Transaction Tax (STT) ₹46,840.43 crore. Gross receipts under other taxes were ₹18.87 crore. Corporate tax refunds amounted to ₹1,53,899.08 crore, while non-corporate tax refunds stood at ₹1,11,279.55 crore. No STT refunds were recorded; refunds under other taxes amounted to ₹20.51 crore. Net corporate tax collections were ₹5,99,062.48 crore, compared with ₹4,96,798.62 crore in the corresponding previous-year period. Net non-corporate tax collections increased to ₹6,91,965.71 crore from ₹6,53,804.16 crore, while net STT collections rose from ₹30,901.81 crore to ₹46,840.43 crore. Net collections under other taxes were negative at ₹1.64 crore. The statement identifies TINMIS as its source and clarifies that non-corporate tax includes taxes paid by individuals, HUFs, firms, AoPs, BoIs, local authorities and artificial juridical persons.
