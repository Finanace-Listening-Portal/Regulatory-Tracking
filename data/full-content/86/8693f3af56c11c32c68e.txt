Vs Additional Commissioner (Orissa High Court) Summary: The Orissa High Court, in M/s Kshipra Consumer Marketing Pvt. Ltd. Vs Additional Commissioner, CGST & Central Excise, Rourkela & Ors., granted an interim stay of a GST adjudication order dated 3 February 2025 while examining the legality of consolidating multiple tax periods into a single show cause notice and adjudication proceeding. The petitioner challenged the show cause notice dated 8 July 2021 and the subsequent order, relying on Section 73(10) of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Andhra Pradesh High Court’s decision in Sahiti Agencies Vs Assistant Commissioner of Central Tax and Central Excise. The petitioner argued that the statutory time limits governing adjudication and the impermissibility of combining different tax periods warranted judicial interference. The petitioner submitted that the Andhra Pradesh High Court, in its judgment dated 26 September 2025 in W.P. No. 14874 of 2025, had held that neither a common show cause notice nor a common adjudication order could be issued for different tax periods. The Revenue opposed interference by referring to the definition of tax period under Section 2(106) of the CGST Act. However, Revenue’s counsel acknowledged that although the Supreme Court had issued notice in SLP (C) Diary No. 1429 of 2026 challenging the Sahiti Agencies judgment, no stay of that judgment had been granted. Considering the competing submissions and the precedent relied upon by the petitioner, the Division Bench comprising Dr. Justice Sanjeeb K. Panigrahi and Justice Murahari Sri Raman held that the writ petition required further hearing. The Court directed the Revenue to file its counter-affidavit by the next date and ordered the matter to be listed along with W.P.(C) No. 8858 of 2025. Importantly, the Court directed that the impugned adjudication order would remain stayed until the next hearing, while expressly keeping all other issues open. The order is interlocutory and does not finally determine the validity of consolidated GST notices. Cases Discussed Sahiti Agencies Vs Assistant Commissioner of Central Tax and Central Excise (Andhra Pradesh High Court); W.P. No. 14874 of 2025; 26/09/2025; AY: Not Applicable. The petitioner relied on this judgment concerning the impermissibility of common show cause notices and orders covering different tax periods. The Orissa High Court recorded that notice had been issued by the Supreme Court in SLP (C) Diary No. 1429 of 2026, without any stay of the judgment. FULL TEXT OF THE ORDER OF ORISSA HIGH COURT 1. This matter is taken up through hybrid arrangement. 2. Heard. 3. Learned Advocate appearing on behalf of the Petitioner submits that the impugned is the show cause notice dated 08.07.2021 and the order dated 03.02.2025. Referring to, inter alia, Sub-Section (10) in Section 73 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, he submit that there is prescribed period for issuance of order i.e. within five years from due date of furnishing of annual return for the particular Financial Year. 4. He relies on the view taken by a Division Bench of the High Court of Andhra Pradesh at Amaravati in Writ Petition No.14874 of 2025 on judgment dated 26th September, 2025 (Sahiti Agencies Vrs. The Assistant Commissioner of Central Tax and Central Excise and another) wherein the learned Division Bench held that neither a common show cause notice nor a common order can be issued for different tax periods and submitted that consolidation of multiple assessment years into single show cause notice contravenes the provisions. He seeks interference. 5. Learned Standing Counsel appears on behalf of the Revenue and draws the attention of this Court to the definitions Section 2 (106) to submit, meaning given to “Tax Period”, when applied to the show cause notice brooks no interference. He fairly submitted that the Hon’ble Supreme Court in SLP (C) Diary No.1429/2026 against the decision in Sahiti Agencies (supra) issued notice, but no order of stay of the judgment has been passed. 6. Petitioner is relying on the view taken by a Division Bench of the Andhra Pradesh High Court against consolidation of tax period. In the circumstances, the Writ Petition requires hearing. 7. Counter affidavit be filed by the next date. 8. List this matter along with W.P.(C) No.8858 of 2025, when the said matter is directed to be listed. Impugned order will remain stayed till the next date of hearing. 9. It is made clear that all other points involved in the instant Writ Petition are kept open.
