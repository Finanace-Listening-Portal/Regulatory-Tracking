RBI Deputy Governor Poonam Gupta (Photo@LinkedIn/Poonam Gupta)Photo : ANIfollow usfollow usReserve Bank of India (RBI) Deputy Governor Poonam Gupta on Monday said the new gross domestic product (GDP) series has narrowed the gap between estimates derived from the demand and supply sides of the economy, addressing a key limitation of the earlier series.She highlighted that under the previous series, the two estimates did not always align, with the resulting discrepancy at times being large and volatile. The revised series brings them together through a supply-use table framework, resulting in a much closer alignment of nominal GDP estimates.“In the earlier series, GDP estimates from the demand and supply sides did not always align. The resulting gap was reported separately as a discrepancy, and it could at times be large and volatile,” RBI DG said.“The new series brings the two sides together through the supply-use table framework. As a result, nominal GDP estimates from both sides align much more closely. A small gap may still persist in real terms because the two sides are deflated differently, but it is much narrower than before,” Gupta said.“The new series reflects an economy that has become larger, more diversified and more complex since the base was last revised in 2011-12,” she said.“One can now assess not just how much is being invested, but also how much is being invested and in what,” Gupta said.The new series also addresses the lumpy jumps in house rent inflation that follow pay commission revisions, as employer-provided and government accommodation are now excluded from the housing index, she said on new CPI series.The revised IIP assigns larger weights to technology and consumption-oriented sectors such as motor vehicles, pharmaceuticals and electronics, while lowering the weights of refined petroleum products, basic metals and mining, she said.Get Latest News Live on Times Now along with Breaking News and Top Headlines from Business, Economy and around the World.Latest News sportsSyed Modi India International 2026: HS Prannoy, Treesa-Gayatri Lead Indian ChallengeeducationPM Modi Urges Equal Respect for ITI and IIT Students, Highlights Role of Skilled Workforce in Viksit Bharatastrology2027 Will be the Year of the Fire Goat: What Does it Mean for Your Chinese Zodiac Sign?kolkataGerman Tourist Arrived In Kolkata On November 21, Found Dead At Hotel Two Days LaterlifestyleA Book On Basavanna Has Sparked Police Cases, Protests and a Debate Over Literary FreedomsportsShubman Gill Urges ICC To Bring Back Pre-COVID Rule, Questions If 'Quality Of Balls Have Come Down'lifestyleThe Jacket-And-Skirt Formula Is Emerging As Winter's Chicest Celebrity Style Trend And We Are Taking NotesVikas Kumar authorVikas Kumar is Deputy Editor (Business) at Times Now driving coverage across policy, economy and markets.
