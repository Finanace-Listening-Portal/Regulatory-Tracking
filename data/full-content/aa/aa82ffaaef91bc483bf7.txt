Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), through its Examination Department, issued an important announcement dated October 14, 2026, regarding the declaration of results for the Chartered Accountants Intermediate Examination held in September 2026. According to the announcement, the results are likely to be declared by the evening of October 22, 2026. Candidates will be able to access their results through the official result portal, caresults.icai.org. To view their results, candidates must enter both their Registration Number and Roll Number on the result website. Accordingly, candidates should keep these credentials available to access their results once they are declared. The announcement communicates the expected date and time of declaration and identifies the website through which candidates can check their examination results. The announcement has been issued by the Examination Department of ICAI and bears the signature of Anand Kumar Chaturvedi, Additional Secretary (Exams). As the announcement describes the declaration as likely to take place by the evening of October 22, 2026, candidates should treat the stated date as the expected schedule communicated by ICAI. Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Examination Department The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India October 14, 2026 IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT The results of the Chartered Accountants Intermediate Examination held in September 2026 is likely to be declared by the evening of 22nd October 2026 and the same can be accessed by candidates on the website caresults.icai.org It may be noted that for accessing the result at the above mentioned website, the candidate shall have to enter his/her Registration Number along with his/her Roll Number.
