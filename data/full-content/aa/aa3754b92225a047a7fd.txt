Articles 1 min read Procol expands AI-led procurement automation across India’s BFSI sector Procol is revolutionizing procurement processes in India's banking and financial services sector with its automated workflows. Major players like KFintech and CRISIL have embraced this innovative multi-agent AI platform, achieving remarkable cost reductions of up to 10 percent. Furthermore, it streamlines procurement cycles and enhances audit readiness by ensuring vendor compliance is monitored in real-time, significantly reducing manual tasks for finance teams. Advt Under the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) IT governance and outsourcing directions, banks and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) are required to maintain oversight of third-party vendors and demonstrate compliance with internal controls.Fragmented manual systems can make it difficult for institutions to maintain audit trails and track vendor risks in real time. Reliance on periodic vendor reviews can also leave gaps in monitoring, particularly when certifications expire or a vendor’s financial position changes between reviews.Procol said its platform combines source-to-pay workflows with more than 40 specialised AI agents that execute tasks within an institution’s defined policies.“We built Procol so that AI agents run the compliance checks, the vendor monitoring, and the invoice matching, which gives finance and procurement teams a continuous view of spend and the time to focus on decisions that actually require human judgement,” he added.Procol said its BFSI deployments have reduced manual administrative work and helped identify cost savings of up to 10 per cent, while improving procurement visibility and audit readiness.
