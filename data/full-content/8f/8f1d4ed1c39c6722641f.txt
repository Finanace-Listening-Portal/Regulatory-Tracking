Articles 1 min read India's forex reserves jump USD 2.49 bn to USD 719.547 bn On November 6, India's foreign exchange reserves saw a notable rise of USD 2.49 billion, bringing the total to USD 719.547 billion. This rebound comes after a previous decline of USD 2.691 billion the week before. Gold reserves also climbed to USD 104.796 billion, while Special Drawing Rights increased by USD 52 million, reaching USD 18.610 billion, indicating a positive shift in foreign currency assets overall. Advt Expressed in dollar terms, the foreign currency assets include effects of appreciation or depreciation of non-US units, such as the euro, pound, and yen, held in foreign exchange reserves.The value of gold reserves increased sharply by USD 2.205 billion to USD 104.796 billion during the week, RBI said.The Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) were up by USD 52 million to USD 18.610 billion, the central bank said. India's reserve position with the IMF was up by USD 1 million to USD 4.791 billion, RBI said.
