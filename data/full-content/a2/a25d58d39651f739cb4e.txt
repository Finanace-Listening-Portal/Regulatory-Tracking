Permitted Venue, Instruments and Purpose Rules RBI’s Master Direction permits recognised stock exchanges to offer foreign-exchange futures and European call and put options in specified pairs. The current list includes USD-INR, EUR-INR, GBP-INR, JPY-INR, EUR-USD, GBP-USD and USD-JPY. Contracts involving INR may be used only to hedge a valid contracted exposure. Users may take positions within the regulatory aggregate threshold without producing exposure documents in advance, but they must possess the valid unhedged exposure and be able to establish it if requested. The documentary threshold is not a licence for speculation and does not remove the prohibition on double hedging. For permitted non-INR pairs, RBI’s direction does not impose the same purpose restriction, but the exchange contract, broker eligibility, risk controls and Indian law still apply. A client should confirm that the exact contract is currently listed, the lot size and expiry remain current and the broker has enabled it. Published Brokerage, Margins and Total Cost Nuvama’s February 2026 account and tariff material publishes a standard-plan rate of 0.03 per cent for currency futures and ₹15 per lot for currency options premium. It also states a minimum brokerage of ₹25 per order, subject to the applicable regulatory ceiling. A client’s signed tariff, negotiated plan or later notice can differ, so these figures must be treated as the reviewed standard schedule, not an irrevocable quote. The option premium, bid-ask spread, slippage and market impact are separate from brokerage. Total cost can also include exchange and clearing charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty and GST on taxable broker services. Securities transaction tax ordinarily does not apply to exchange-traded currency derivatives, but current statutory treatment and the contract note should be checked. Margin is collateral, not a fee: the exchange and broker collect initial and other risk margins, may revise requirements intraday and can close positions when a shortfall is not cured. Published historical margin percentages should not be reused because contract volatility and exchange files change. Daily mark-to-market settlement and final settlement follow exchange rules, with current NSE material identifying T+1 daily and T+2 final settlement cycles for relevant contracts. The broker issues a contract note within the prescribed period and maintains a client ledger and margin statement. A closed profit is not automatically available for withdrawal before settlement, and a free cash balance can still be held against other open positions, obligations or risk controls. A rejected or cancelled order should not create a trade obligation, but an executed trade remains binding even if the client misunderstood the product. Exchange bust or error-trade rules are narrow. Withdrawal requests depend on cleared funds, cut-off time, bank verification and outstanding obligations. A margin surplus, premium refund or settlement correction should be reconciled against the ledger and contract note rather than inferred from an app balance. Unauthorised trades must be reported immediately using official channels. Grievance and Escalation Route Nuvama’s investor charter provides a written complaint route and identifies exchange, SEBI SCORES and online dispute resolution escalation. The broker charter says contract notes should generally be issued within 24 hours and grievances should be addressed within 21 calendar days, subject to the applicable framework. A complaint should state the client code, contract, order and trade numbers, time, amount, margin effect, contract note and requested remedy. Passwords and one-time codes should never be sent. If the broker’s response is absent or unsatisfactory, the client can use the relevant exchange complaint mechanism, SCORES and the dispute-resolution route in the prescribed sequence. Indian Tax and Accounting Considerations Profit and loss from exchange-traded currency derivatives is generally analysed as business income or loss under Indian tax law, subject to the taxpayer’s facts, records and judicial principles. Recognised-exchange derivatives are excluded from the statutory definition of speculative transaction when the conditions are met, but that does not guarantee a loss deduction or determine whether a person is carrying on business. Turnover for tax-audit analysis is not the contract notional; professional bodies use specific methods based on positive and negative differences and option premium. A chartered accountant should apply the current rule to the complete activity. GST charged by the broker is a service tax component and is distinct from income tax. An individual investor may not have input-tax-credit entitlement. LRS TCS is not part of the normal exchange-traded INR settlement described here because this service does not involve an outward remittance to an overseas margin account. The taxpayer should retain contract notes, broker ledger, bank entries, expense invoices, margin statements, exposure documents and year-end position reports.
