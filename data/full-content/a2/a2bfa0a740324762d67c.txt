Articles 2 min read Financial Services adds 28 rich listers with ₹1.02 lakh crore wealth; rupee depreciation keeps 168 Indians out of billionaire club: Report The M3M Hurun India Rich List 2026 finds the rupee’s fall from ₹61.01/$ in 2014 to ₹95.63/$ has pushed the dollar-billionaire threshold from ₹6,101 crore to ₹9,563 crore, masking 168 potential billionaires in dollar terms. Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Sep 23, 2026 at 03:19 PM IST var news_msid = 134431175; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The weaker rupee is masking a significantly larger pool of dollar-denominated wealth among India’s richest, with the country potentially having nearly 600 dollar billionaires if the rupee-dollar exchange rate had remained at 2014 levels, Anas Rahman Junaid, Founder and Chief Researcher, Hurun India, highlighted.The M3M Hurun India Rich List 2026 puts India’s current dollar-billionaire count at 391. At the 2014 exchange rate, the number would rise to 559, a difference of 168 individuals.In response to an ETBFSI question on how much the weaker rupee is masking India’s dollar wealth, Junaid said the size of India’s dollar-billionaire pool would have been about 50% higher if the rupee-dollar exchange rate had remained unchanged. Advt “I think this is a clear example, that the size of dollar billionaires would have gone up by 50% if the rupee and dollar had stayed the same,” Junaid said.He also compared India’s potential dollar-billionaire pool with China’s, saying that while China had “almost 1,000 US dollar billionaires” in the latest Hurun China Rich List, India could potentially have been at “about 600 dollar billionaires” if the rupee and dollar had remained at the same level.“That's only the top,” Junaid said, adding that the impact would become more pronounced further down the wealth pyramid, where a larger number of individuals could have been affected in dollar terms.The report shows that the rupee-equivalent threshold for a dollar billionaire has risen from ₹6,101 crore in 2014, when the exchange rate was ₹61.01/$, to ₹9,563 crore at the current rate of ₹95.63/$.The broader concentration figures in the report show that 23 individuals with wealth above ₹1 lakh crore collectively hold ₹54.21 lakh crore, equivalent to 28.9% of the total wealth represented by the list, despite accounting for only 1.3% of the 1,810 individuals on the list.The top 10 alone command ₹39.01 lakh crore, compared with ₹5.44 lakh crore in 2012.Financial Services adds 28 new rich listers Advt Financial Services added 28 new rich listers and ₹1.02 lakh crore in wealth during the year, with sector wealth rising 23% on a like-for-like basis.The sector was the second-largest source of new entrants after Industrial Products, which added 44 individuals and ₹1.13 lakh crore in wealth. Software & Services added 21 new entrants with ₹96,900 crore in wealth.Uday Kotak topped the Financial Services wealth rankings with ₹1.19 lakh crore, followed by Vinod Khosla at ₹1.16 lakh crore. Nithin Kamath and family had ₹59,000 crore, Nikhil Kamath ₹39,900 crore, and Sanjay Dangi and family ₹36,300 crore.
