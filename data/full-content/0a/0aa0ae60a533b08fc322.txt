Advt Speaking at the inauguration, Pandit emphasised the need for banks to accelerate digital transformation to improve customer convenience and financial inclusion.Chandra said the bank has been making progress across business parameters, supported by strong performance in core operating areas and the trust of its stakeholders.The new branch reflects PNB's commitment to combining its core banking strengths with digital capabilities to improve customer service, it said.The bank also aims to extend the benefits of digital banking initiatives to the last mile, including unbanked and rural areas, he added.
