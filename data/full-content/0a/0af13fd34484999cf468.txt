Roughly 2.18 lakh responses were received, and the committee's recommendations are due by December 31, 2026.The consultation paper had proposed three intermediary categories—Insurance Distribution Entities, Insurance Distribution Persons, and Market Infrastructure Institutions (including Bima Sugam)—alongside tighter expense-of-management limits (15 per cent for life and 25 per cent for general insurers within two years, tightening to 12.5 per cent and 20 per cent within five years) and product-level commission caps such as 15–20 per cent first-year commissions and 5–10 per cent renewal or porting payouts in health insurance. Investors read the panel as raising hopes that proposed caps could be softened, sparking a rally on November 25, the same day IRDAI also set up panels to standardise health insurance claim rejections and enable real-time claim tracking.Among insurers, SBI Life Insurance gained 1.62 per cent over the week to Rs 1,856.00, while ICICI Lombard General Insurance rose 0.14 per cent to Rs 1,738.50. HDFC Life Insurance edged down 0.03 per cent to Rs 588.45 and Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) declined 1.29 per cent to Rs 405.40.Benchmark overviewNifty Bank fell 2.47 per cent for the week to 63,040.90 on profit-taking following the week ended November 20, when the index hit a record intraday high of 64,680.80 and closed at 64,635.20. The Nifty 50 declined 0.75 per cent over the week to 25,607.20 after dropping 277.30 points, or 1.07 per cent, on Friday. Axis Bank dropped 4.56 per cent to Rs 1,380.10, with no specific trigger identified. Advt NBFCsNBFC stocks were mostly lower over the week, led on the downside by Shriram Finance. Muthoot Finance gained 2.31 per cent to close at Rs 2,609.50.Tata Capital fell 1.46 per cent to Rs 340.10, Bajaj Finance declined 3.74 per cent to Rs 1,080.00, and Cholamandalam Investment & Finance dropped 3.87 per cent to Rs 1,802.00. Shriram Finance fell 4.99 per cent to Rs 923.50, with no specific trigger identified.Fintech stocksFintech movers diverged between insurance distribution and digital payments. PB Fintech surged 16.01 per cent over the week to Rs 1,186.50, rallying on November 25 alongside insurance counters on hopes that IRDAI's review committee could soften proposed distribution commission caps. Advt Among payments-linked fintechs, Paytm edged up 0.11 per cent to Rs 1,741.90, One MobiKwik Systems declined 0.88 per cent to Rs 210.38, and OnEMI Technology Solutions fell 1.42 per cent to Rs 333.05. As of November 27, NPCI had still not notified a new implementation date for its proposed 0.4 per cent merchant discount rate (MDR) on eligible person-to-merchant UPI transactions above Rs 2,000 (capped at Rs 300), leaving uncertainty over the timeline after its October 13 deferral from the original October 15 date.
