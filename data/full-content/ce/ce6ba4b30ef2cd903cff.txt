This collaboration aims to bolster bp’s performance and improve its long-term cost competitiveness by leveraging Wipro’s expertise in AI, automation, and transformation. The agreement will see approximately 3,000 bp employees from India, Hungary, and Malaysia transition to Wipro, as the company seeks to streamline its finance operations and enhance process efficiency. Kate Thomson, Chief Financial Officer at bp, expressed confidence in the partnership, stating that it represents the optimal path forward for the company and its employees. She highlighted the potential for bp to achieve best-in-class finance operations while providing its workforce with opportunities to further develop their skills. The collaboration is expected to enable bp to make better use of finance operations technology, deliver business value more swiftly, and simplify work processes. Srini Pallia, Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director of Wipro Limited, emphasised the significance of this engagement with bp, which underscores Wipro’s commitment to driving large-scale technology transformation through its deep domain expertise and AI capabilities. Pallia also welcomed the bp employees joining Wipro, expressing enthusiasm for integrating their specialised skills into Wipro’s consulting-led, AI-powered strategy. The new operating model is anticipated to come into effect in 2027, subject to applicable conditions. While Wipro will manage the transformation of bp’s finance operations, bp will retain overall accountability for standards, controls, data, and technology governance. PwC will provide oversight of controls, ensuring that the transition aligns with bp’s strategic objectives. Market analysts view this partnership as a strategic move for both companies, allowing bp to focus on its core business while benefiting from Wipro’s technological expertise. The collaboration is expected to set a precedent for similar partnerships in the industry, as companies increasingly seek to enhance operational efficiency through technology-driven solutions.
