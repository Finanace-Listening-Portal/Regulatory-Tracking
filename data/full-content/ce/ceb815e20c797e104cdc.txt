The notification appoints the officer specified in column (3) of the Table to exercise the powers and discharge the duties conferred or imposed on the Commissioner of Customs and Central Excise, Hyderabad-IV Commissionerate, Hyderabad, for adjudication of the specified show cause notice concerning Tirupati Udyog Limited, Kotur. The show cause notice is numbered VIII/26/23/2008-HRU and dated November 6, 2008, and was issued by the Additional Director General (ADG), Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI), Zonal Unit, Chennai. The Principal Commissioner or Commissioner of Customs, Hyderabad, GST Bhavan, Lal Bahadur Stadium Road, Basheerbagh, Hyderabad-500004, is specified as the common adjudicating authority. The notification bears S.O. 5239(E) and F. No. 437/06/2021-Cus-lV-Pt.II. It further provides that the notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. The notification is issued by Anurima Sharma, Director (Customs).
