Sharma step down from their respective finance leadership roles. ETCFO Staff ETCFO Published On Nov 20, 2026 at 06:50 PM IST var news_msid = 136027082; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook -Jaspreet KaurOn Thursday, Vedant Fashions appointed Vineet Poddar as its chief financial officer (CFO) and key managerial personnel, effective November 23, 2026. The company's board approved the appointment, based on the recommendation of the nomination and remuneration committee.Poddar, a fellow chartered accountant and associate company secretary, has approximately three decades of leadership experience across sectors, multinational companies and diversified conglomerates in India and Dubai. Previously, he served as CFO of Safari Industries India and held senior finance roles at Nshama (Emaar Group), Bombay Dyeing/Wadia Group, Marsh India and Birla Corporation. Advt Vedant Fashions headquartered in Kolkata, is an Indian ethnic wear company operating brands such as Manyavar, Mohey, Twamev, Mebaz and Manthan.Noida-based Jubilant Ingrevia’s CFO and key managerial personnel Varun Gupta resigned, effective November 30, 2026.Gupta resigned because he had received a better opportunity, the company stated in the regulatory filing.The company provides specialty chemicals and contract development and manufacturing (CDMO) solutions to the pharmaceutical, nutrition, agrochemical, semiconductor and industrial sectors.Carysil's executive director and group CFO Anand H. Sharma ceased to hold his position and membership on the company's board committees, effective November 16, 2026.Sharma resigned because he could not relocate to the company’s Bhavnagar headquarters due to personal reasons. As Carysil begins its next growth phase under Carysil 2.0, the company requires its leadership team to be based at the headquarters around the chairman. The company said it would fill the CFO vacancy within the timeline prescribed by the SEBI Listing Regulations, according to the regulatory filing.Mumbai-based Carysil manufactures kitchen and bathroom solutions, with a portfolio spanning quartz and stainless-steel sinks, faucets and built-in kitchen appliances.
