ETBFSI Research ETBFSI Published On Nov 27, 2026 at 06:01 AM IST var news_msid = 136206104; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Borrowers who pledged gold jewellery for loans are facing demands from banks and non-banking finance companies (NBFCs) to repay part of their outstanding dues or provide additional collateral as falling gold prices erode the value of their pledged assets. Lenders have started issuing margin notices and legal auction notices in accounts where loan-to-value (LTV) ratios have breached permissible limits.The price of 999-purity gold fell to Rs 1,21,038 per 10 grams on November 25, from Rs 1,41,710 on October 26, according to India Bullion and Jewellers Association rates. The decline of about 14.6 per cent in a month has reduced the value of pledged jewellery while borrowers' outstanding loan amounts have remained unchanged. Advt For instance, a borrower who took a loan of Rs 75,000 against jewellery valued at Rs 1 lakh would initially have an LTV ratio of 75 per cent, assuming Rs 75,000 is the amount considered for the LTV calculation. If the value of the pledged gold falls by 15 per cent to Rs 85,000, the LTV rises to about 88 per cent. A 25 per cent decline would push it to 100 per cent, even though the borrower has not increased the loan amount.Under the Reserve Bank of India's revised gold and silver lending directions, the maximum LTV for consumption loans is 85 per cent for loans up to Rs 2.5 lakh, 80 per cent for loans above Rs 2.5 lakh and up to Rs 5 lakh, and 75 per cent for loans above Rs 5 lakh. These limits have to be maintained throughout the loan tenure. The directions were required to be adopted by lenders no later than April 1, 2026. Loans sanctioned before a lender adopted the revised directions continue to be governed by the earlier applicable guidelines.How lenders value pledged goldThe value used to calculate the LTV ratio is not necessarily the prevailing retail jewellery price. RBI requires lenders to use the lower of the average closing price over the preceding 30 days or the previous day's closing price for the relevant gold purity, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association or a regulated commodity exchange. Only the intrinsic value of the gold is considered. Stones, gems and other components are excluded.What happens when the LTV limit is breachedBorrowers whose collateral coverage falls below the applicable limit may be asked to make partial repayments or pledge additional gold. Lenders have been contacting customers to address the shortfall before proceeding with auction notices. An LTV breach does not mean that the jewellery is automatically auctioned. The lender's credit policy and loan agreement specify the action to be taken, the circumstances that can trigger an auction and the notice period available to the borrower. Advt The RBI requires lenders to give borrowers adequate notice to repay or settle their dues before initiating auction proceedings. The pledged jewellery can be auctioned if the borrower does not regularise the account within the applicable notice period.The reserve price at the first auction cannot be lower than 90 per cent of the current value of the pledged gold. If two auctions fail, the reserve price can be reduced to 85 per cent of its current value. The first auction must be conducted physically in the district where the lending branch is located.After the auction, lenders must provide borrowers with details of the proceeds and the amount adjusted against their outstanding dues. Any surplus must be returned within seven working days of receipt of the full auction proceeds. If the proceeds are insufficient to cover the outstanding dues, lenders can recover the shortfall in accordance with the loan agreement. Advt Early repayment stress is increasingThe pressure comes after a rapid expansion in gold-backed lending. Outstanding gold loans rose 60.6 per cent year on year to Rs 23.3 lakh crore at the end of September 2026, according to CRIF High Mark. The portfolio accounted for 12.6 per cent of total retail credit.Early-stage repayment stress has also increased. The portfolio-at-risk for loans overdue by 31-90 days rose to 1.3 per cent in September from 0.9 per cent in June. However, the portfolio-at-risk for loans overdue by 31-180 days declined to 1.7 per cent from 2 per cent a year earlier, indicating that the increase in stress is more visible in early-stage delinquencies. Advt For borrowers receiving margin notices, the immediate step is to seek a written calculation of the outstanding amount, the current valuation of the pledged gold and the additional repayment or collateral required. They should also check their loan agreements for the applicable repayment conditions, auction triggers, notice period and charges.The risk for borrowers is that a further decline in gold prices could reduce collateral coverage further. Those unable to meet margin requirements or settle their dues could lose their pledged jewellery through auction.
