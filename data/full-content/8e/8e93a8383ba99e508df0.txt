Articles 1 min read Equitas Small Finance Bank gets Rs 3.8 cr GST demand notice Equitas Small Finance Bank has received a demand notice for Rs 3.8 crore due to alleged GST issues. The notice was issued by tax authorities regarding non-payment of GST for the fiscal year 2024-25. This order includes a tax demand, interest, and penalty concerning reverse charges on rent. The bank is currently evaluating the order and plans to file an appeal.
