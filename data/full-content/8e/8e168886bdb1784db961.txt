Markets were shut on Tuesday for a holiday. Advt Traders also sold the 10-year bond ahead of Friday's 340 billion rupee ($3.62 billion) auction, which will add substantial supply.Brent crude fell $6 over three sessions to $92.4 a barrel after US officials downplayed further military action against Iran. It edged higher on Wednesday after sliding to a two-month low of $91.41 in the previous session.While cheaper oil eases India's inflation and fiscal pressures, investors fear growing odds of a December Fed rate hike could also push the Reserve Bank of India to tighten policy."With US CPI affirming possibility of a Fed rate hike in December, it raises the probability of RBI also raising rates in the upcoming policy," a private-bank trader said."The 10-year yield may trade in 7.10% to 7.25% range till the next RBI policy."Traders will now watch India's October inflation data due Thursday. A Reuters poll forecasts annual inflation at 5.93%, up from 5.54% in September.RATESOvernight index swaps also rose sharply in tandem with US yields.The one-year, two-year and five-year rates gained 7-8 bps to 6.38%, 6.3850% and 6.48%, respectively.($1 = 93.9275 Indian rupees)(Reporting by Khushi Malhotra; Editing by Nivedita Bhattacharjee) Reuters Published On Nov 11, 2026 at 11:01 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
