The five-member Committee will be chaired by Shri Sriram Taranikanti, IAS (Retd.), and includes Shri Parmod Kumar Arora, former Whole Time Member of IRDAI; Shri Atul Sahai, former Insurance Ombudsman and former CMD of New India Assurance Co. Ltd.; Ms. Pushpa Girimaji, Consumer Right Advocate; and Ms. Mandakini Balodhi, Director (Insurance), Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, as the Central Government nominee. The Head of IRDAI’s Policyholders’ Protection and Grievance Redressal Department will act as the Committee’s convener. The Committee will have a tenure of two years and will review the performance of Insurance Ombudsmen and matters concerning the efficiency of the institution. It will generally meet at least twice a year and submit an annual report to IRDAI for review and further action, as deemed necessary. Three members will constitute a quorum. The Chairperson and members will be eligible for travel, boarding, lodging and sitting fees at par with other committees constituted by IRDAI. The reconstitution aims to facilitate periodic performance assessment and strengthen the institutional efficiency of the Insurance Ombudsman system, which plays an important role in policyholder protection and grievance redressal.
