Articles 2 min read Credit card transactions rise 30% YoY in Oct ‘26, debit card usage declines 8% Credit card transaction volumes grew 30 per cent year-on-year in October 2026, while the value of spending increased 14 per cent, according to data collated by 1Lattice. Debit card transactions, meanwhile, declined 8 per cent by volume and 7 per cent by value. This implies a decline of approximately 12 per cent in the average transaction value, based on the reported growth rates.On a month-on-month basis, the value of credit card transactions increased around 20 per cent to Rs 2.45 lakh crore in October from Rs 2.04 lakh crore in September. Advt In comparison, debit card transaction volumes declined 8 per cent year-on-year, while transaction value fell 7 per cent during the month. However, on a month-on-month basis, debit card transactions increased around 6 per cent by volume to 520 million in October from 490 million in September. Transaction value rose 8 per cent to Rs 2.73 lakh crore from Rs 2.52 lakh crore.ATM withdrawals continued to account for the largest share of debit card transactions, representing 80 per cent of transaction volumes and 85.2 per cent of transaction value in October.Point-of-sale (POS) transactions accounted for 14.5 per cent of debit card transaction volumes and 9.2 per cent of transaction value, while e-commerce transactions made up 5.5 per cent and 5.6 per cent, respectively.Credit card usage, on the other hand, remained concentrated in merchant payments, particularly e-commerce. Online transactions accounted for 63 per cent of credit card spending value in October, while POS transactions contributed 37 per cent. ATM transactions accounted for a negligible 0.2 per cent.Debit card transaction volumes declined from 8.83 billion in FY24 to 7.60 billion in FY25 and 6.61 billion in FY26. Credit card transaction volumes, in contrast, increased from 3.56 billion in FY24 to 4.78 billion in FY25 and 6.02 billion in FY26.
