The 91.2-metre blades, designed for the MWL185-5.0 MW turbine platform, have also received certification from WindGuard Certification GmbH, a German wind turbine testing and certification body, paving the way for serial production. The newly produced blades are a significant advancement in India’s wind energy sector, marking the longest blades manufactured in the country. These blades are tailored for a turbine with a 185-metre rotor diameter and a 140-metre hub height, the largest in India. This design aims to optimise energy capture across varying wind speeds, thereby enhancing the capacity utilisation factor (CUF) and contributing to more consistent energy generation. The rotor’s sweep area of approximately 26,600 square metres is equivalent to more than three and a half standard football pitches, underscoring the scale of this technological achievement. The development of these blades represents a significant leap in manufacturing capabilities, extending the range of products at the Mundra facility, which already produces blades measuring 78.6 metres and 80.5 metres. This progress underscores India’s growing capacity to produce larger wind turbine components, crucial for the next generation of wind energy technology. Milind Kulkarni, CEO of Adani Wind, highlighted the importance of this milestone, noting that larger blades and higher-capacity turbines can capture more energy, even at sites with lower wind speeds, thus expanding the potential geography for wind generation. Adani Wind’s MWL185-5.0 MW platform, certified by WindGuard Certification GmbH, confirms compliance with the IEC 61400 series of international standards for wind turbine design and manufacturing. The platform’s design aims to enhance energy capture at low- and moderate-wind locations, potentially making a broader range of sites viable for wind power projects. Adani Wind’s inclusion in BloombergNEF’s Global Top 15 turbine manufacturers ranking for 2025 further cements its position as a leader in the wind energy sector. The company’s continued innovation and expansion in wind energy technology align with India’s growing clean energy needs, offering scalable solutions for domestic and international markets. As Adani Wind plans to further scale its manufacturing capacity, its efforts contribute significantly to the global clean energy transition, reinforcing India’s role in the renewable energy landscape.
