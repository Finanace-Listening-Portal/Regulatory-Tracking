Articles 4 min read BFSI stocks in a week: Bank Nifty retreats; PNB, PB Fintech, Paytm among top weekly gainers Several banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) stocks outperformed benchmark indices during the week ended November 13, with public sector banks and fintech companies leading the gains. Punjab National Bank (PNB) was the best-performing banking stock, while PB Fintech and Paytm led gains among fintech companies. Insurance stocks also ended higher, while NBFCs recorded mixed performance. The Sensex ended the week at 81,592.45, while the Nifty Bank declined 0.79 per cent on Friday to close at 61,034.10, remaining close to its 52-week high of 61,764.85.Despite Friday's decline, several banking and financial services stocks outperformed the broader market during the week, led by public sector banks and fintech companies. Advt PSBs outperform private peersPublic sector banks outperformed their private sector counterparts during the week, with second largest public sector lender PNB (Punjab National Bank) emerging as the best-performing banking stock among the lenders tracked.PNB gained 12.85 per cent over the week to close at Rs 142.81 on Friday. The stock touched a 52-week high of Rs 145.14 during the session. Bank of Baroda followed with a 9.40 per cent weekly gain, closing at Rs 273.84, while State Bank of India (SBI) rose 6.04 per cent to end at Rs 1,080.Private sector banks recorded relatively modest gains. Largest private sector lender HDFC Bank rose 2 per cent during the week to close at Rs 804, while Kotak Mahindra Bank gained 1.78 per cent to end at Rs 488.55.ICICI Bank and Axis Bank also ended higher, gaining 0.79 per cent and 0.61 per cent, respectively. ICICI Bank closed at Rs 1,583.60, while Axis Bank ended at Rs 1,331.The gains came despite a decline in banking stocks on Friday, with the Nifty Bank falling 0.79 per cent to close at 61,034.10.Insurance stocks end week higherInsurance stocks also recorded gains during the week, with HDFC Life leading the pack.HDFC Life gained 4.77 per cent to close at Rs 592.25, followed by ICICI Lombard, which rose 2.56 per cent to Rs 1,691.20.Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) gained 1.35 per cent during the week to close at Rs 399.30, while SBI Life Insurance edged up 0.33 per cent to Rs 1,789.90. Advt NBFCs record mixed performanceNon-banking financial companies (NBFCs) recorded mixed performance during the week, with Cholamandalam Investment and Finance leading the gains.Cholamandalam Investment and Finance gained 3.64 per cent to close at Rs 1,850, while Shriram Finance rose 0.92 per cent to Rs 970.On the other hand, Tata Capital declined 0.71 per cent to close at Rs 355.25, while Muthoot Finance fell 1.01 per cent to Rs 2,840.Manappuram Finance also declined 4.47 per cent during the week to close at Rs 311.10, with the stock coming under pressure after its Q2 FY27 results despite reporting a more than three-fold year-on-year increase in consolidated net profit to Rs 701 crore. A tenfold increase in Stage 2 gold loans from 0.5 per cent in Q1 FY27 to 5 per cent in Q2 FY27 weighed on investor sentiment. Advt Fintech stocks lead broader financial sector gainsFintech stocks were among the strongest performers during the week, led by PB Fintech and Paytm.PB Fintech surged 14.27 per cent to close at Rs 1,105 on Friday on media reports suggesting that IRDAI may consider reviewing draft paper on distribution, while One 97 Communications, the parent of Paytm, gained 8.11 per cent to end at Rs 1,939.90.Paytm's gains came after its wholly owned subsidiary, Paytm Payments Services Ltd (PPSL), received in-principle authorisation from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to issue and operate prepaid payment instruments, including digital wallets. The approval comes nearly three years after RBI restrictions on Paytm Payments Bank in January 2024 effectively halted fresh wallet top-ups and deposits, disrupting Paytm's wallet business. Advt OnEMI Technology Solutions rose 2.03 per cent during the week to close at Rs 352.10, while One MobiKwik Systems gained 0.89 per cent to Rs 226.82.Bajaj Finance gained 2.69 per cent during the week to close at Rs 1,117, outperforming the Nifty 50, which rose 0.46 per cent. The company's stock has also gained 13.66 per cent over the past month.The gains come as Bajaj Finance looks to strengthen its capital position through its proposed Rs 11,700 crore qualified institutional placement (QIP) and Rs 5,800 crore promoter warrant allotment.
