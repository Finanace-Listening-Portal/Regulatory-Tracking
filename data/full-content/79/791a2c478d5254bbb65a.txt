Key levels to track Gold and silver prices rebounded on the MCX on Wednesday, snapping a three-day losing streak as investors awaited US inflation data and the Federal Reserve’s policy cues. Expectations of higher interest rates continued to weigh on bullion demand. Traders are also tracking the US nonfarm payrolls report, while analysts advise caution amid market volatility and outline key support and resistance levels for gold and silver. All three metals remained on track for monthly declines.How should you trade gold?Manoj Kumar Jain of Prithvi Finmart said gold has support at $4,164-4,140 per troy ounce, while resistance is placed at $4,220-4,250. For silver, support is seen at $60.60-60.00, with resistance at $62.40-63.00 per troy ounce in Wednesday’s session.On the MCX, gold has support at Rs 1,47,700-1,47,200 and resistance at Rs 1,49,650-1,50,150, while silver has support at Rs 2,24,000-2,22,000 and resistance at Rs 2,27,000-2,28,800.
