Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); DRK Constructions Vs Assistant Commissioner (State Taxes) (Telangana High Court) Summary: The Telangana High Court, in DRK Constructions Vs Assistant Commissioner (State Taxes), disposed of a writ petition challenging the show cause notice dated 22 November 2024 and the consequential order dated 9 September 2025 for the tax period April 2022 to March 2023. The petitioner contended that the proceedings were illegal, arbitrary, without jurisdiction and legally unenforceable, alleging that the notice was vague and issued without establishing the essential ingredients for invoking Section 74 of the CGST Act, 2017, including fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression of facts. The petitioner also alleged that the proceedings were contrary to earlier decisions of the High Court and the provisions of the CGST and Telangana GST Acts, 2017. However, the High Court observed that the impugned order was appealable and accordingly disposed of the writ petition, reserving the petitioner’s right to pursue the statutory appellate remedy under Section 107 of the GST Act. The Court did not adjudicate the merits of the allegations concerning the validity of the show cause notice or the invocation of Section 74. No order as to costs was passed, and all pending miscellaneous applications were closed. FULL TEXT OF THE JUDGMENT/ORDER OF TELANGANA HIGH COURT Heard Mr. K.P.Amarnath Reddy, learned counsel appearing for the petitioner and Mr. K.Sai Akarsh, learned Assistant Government Pleader representing Mr. Swaroop Oorilla, learned Special Government Pleader for State Tax for the respondents. 2. The writ petition has been preferred with the following prayer: “For the reasons stated in the accompanying affidavit, it is prayed that the Hon’ble Court may be pleased to issue a Writ of Mandamus or any other appropriate writ or order or direction by setting aside the show cause notice dated 22.11.2024 along with the impugned order dated 9.9.2025 passed by the 1st Respondent for the tax period April, 2022 to March, 2023 as illegal, arbitrary, without jurisdiction, legally unenforceable, issued vaguely without proper satisfaction of ingredients of Section 74 and same is contrary to the decisions passed by this Hon’ble Court and also contrary the provisions under the Central Goods and Service Tax Act, 2017 and the Telangana Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, and pass such other order or orders as the Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper in the circumstances of the case.” 3. Considering the fact that the impugned order under challenge is one which is an appealable order reserving the right of the petitioner to prefer an appeal against the said impugned order, the present Writ Petition stands disposed of with the aforesaid liberty. There shall be no order as to costs. Miscellaneous applications, if any pending, shall stand closed.
