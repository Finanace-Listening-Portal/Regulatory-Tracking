While there is no confirmed evidence of disruption to core banking, payment processing or settlement systems in India, the incident has exposed the operational risks of financial institutions relying on common technology platforms for critical workplace services. The outage, which began on Thursday, affected Outlook and other Microsoft 365 applications, with some services continuing to face problems into Friday. Advt The disruption affected employees at Indian brokerages who were unable to access research reports ahead of morning meetings and faced difficulties communicating with clients. Some firms had to rely on alternative channels, including telephone calls, to maintain communication.The immediate impact on India's banking, financial services and insurance sector appears to have been concentrated on workplace applications rather than transaction-processing infrastructure. There have been no confirmed reports of widespread disruption to core banking, UPI, NEFT, RTGS, ATM networks or payment settlement systems. This distinction is important because Microsoft 365 applications such as Outlook and Teams are used for communication and collaboration, whereas banks' core systems and payment networks perform the actual processing of customer transactions.The outage began on Thursday, November 12, and Indian users started reporting problems around 11 pm. Reports of service failures increased overnight and again on Friday morning as employees attempted to access their work mailboxes. The disruption affected users in several countries and recovery efforts continued into Friday.Exchange Online, Microsoft's cloud-based email service that powers Outlook, was at the centre of the disruption. Users faced difficulties accessing mailboxes through web and desktop applications, while email delivery was delayed. The outage also affected other Microsoft 365 services that depend on Exchange Online, including some Copilot, SharePoint, Planner and security-related functions. Teams was not completely unavailable: its core messaging and online meetings largely remained functional, but features linked to Exchange Online, such as calendars and meeting scheduling, were affected.The impact Advt For brokerages, investment banks and asset managers, such disruptions can affect more than routine correspondence. Research reports, market updates, client instructions and transaction-related communication are frequently circulated through corporate email. Delayed access can slow the distribution of research, postpone client responses and complicate coordination between analysts, sales teams, dealers and back-office staff. Where email is used to confirm or document instructions, firms may have to shift to approved alternative channels and ensure that records are subsequently reconciled.Financial market participants overseas also faced communication difficulties. Some traders switched to telephone calls and other communication channels, while some banks in Singapore faced difficulties completing financing transactions. Such disruptions underline the importance of reliable communication and confirmation processes in financial markets, where delays or discrepancies can complicate trade execution and back-office reconciliation. Advt The incident highlights the concentration risk created when multiple organisations depend on the same technology provider. Microsoft 365 is widely used for email, document sharing, meetings and collaboration. A failure in a central service such as Exchange Online can therefore affect several business functions across different organisations at the same time, even when their own banking and transaction-processing systems remain operational.Microsoft's investigation found that a number of servers had become unexpectedly unavailable, shifting their workload to the remaining infrastructure. A process on affected servers responsible for establishing secure connections also became overwhelmed, causing the servers to stop processing requests. The company restarted affected servers, redirected traffic and tested configuration changes to restore capacity. On Friday, it reported improved server stability after applying a patch to part of its infrastructure and began expanding the fix. However, it had not yet published a definitive explanation of the original trigger. Microsoft also said it had no indication that malicious activity had caused the disruption. Advt For India's financial services sector, the incident reinforces the need for contingency arrangements that extend beyond core banking and payment systems. Financial institutions need alternative communication channels, procedures for verifying client instructions when email is unavailable, backup access to critical documents and clear protocols for recording and reconciling transactions handled through alternative channels. They also need to assess whether their subsidiaries, vendors and business partners rely on the same technology platforms.
