Advt Asked about stress in the sector, he said there has not been much uptick in NPA level due to recent geopolitical uncertainties triggered by the West Asia crisis.The bank also has specific business banking benefits for women entrepreneurs, including a 1 per cent interest-rate concession on eligible CGTMSE-backed overdraft and term loans, and a 0.5 per cent concession on secured products, subject to applicable terms, he said.Relationship-led advisory complements these offerings, helping businesses navigate their banking and financing requirements as they expand, he said.Along with their business requirements, he said the bank addresses women's broader financial needs through ARISE, its dedicated women's savings account.ARISE combines savings, investment and credit solutions with digital onboarding through VCIP, and benefits of up to Rs 38,000 across healthcare, lockers, Axis Demat, credit cards and gold loans.
