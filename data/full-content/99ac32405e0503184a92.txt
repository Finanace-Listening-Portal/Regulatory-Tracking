The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) has undertaken the upgradation of its Business Analytics Platform (BAP) to facilitate an integrated, streamlined and enhanced platform for regulatory reporting and related Reinsurance functionalities. 2. As part of the aforesaid initiative, the Reinsurance regulatory returns, Reinsurance Summary Formats (Advance and Final), and applications for issuance of File Reference Number (FRN) to Cross Border Reinsurers (CBRs) have been migrated to the upgraded BAP platform. 3. In addition to the above, applications for the appointment and remuneration of the Chairperson/CEO of Reinsurers and FRBs have also been integrated into the upgraded BAP platform. 4. Accordingly, the aforesaid Reinsurance regulatory returns, Reinsurance Summary Formats, FRN applications for CBRs and other Reinsurance functionalities are now available for use through the BAP portal. 5. All entities shall make submissions on the the integrated Reinsurance BAP portal as specified below: (a) Reinsurance Regulatory Returns from FY 2024–25. (b) Corporate Governance Returns from FY 2025–26. (c) Pool Administrator Returns from FY 2024–25. (d) Reinsurance Programme Summary Returns Final Reinsurance Programme Summary Returns from FY 2026–27. (e) Request for approvals-appointment & remuneration of CEOs/KMPs, FRN for CBRs etc 6. The operational guidelines and instructions for the various functions relating to reinsurance shall be available on the BAP portal for all the users. 7.
