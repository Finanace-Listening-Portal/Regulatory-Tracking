Stock Market Closingfollow usfollow usIndian stock markets ended Monday's trading session on a positive note after recovering from sharp intraday losses, with a late rally in banking stocks helping benchmark indices close with modest gains. At close, Sensex settled 107.25 points, or 0.13%, higher at 81,106.65, while the Nifty advanced 58.25 points, or 0.23%, to close at 25,044.65. PSU bank stocks led the sectoral rally, while IT and metal shares remained under pressure.The Nifty index was led by gains in the SBI, Trent and NTPC stocks, while Tata Steel, HCLTech and UltraTech Cement were among the biggest losers.Vinod Nair, Head of Research, Geojit Investments Limited said, "Indian equities traded range-bound with a positive bias, as intermittent profit booking following the recent rally capped gains. Weakness in IT and metal stocks also weighed on the benchmarks. Improving FII participation and better-than-expected corporate earnings have strengthened expectations of an upgrade to the FY27 earnings outlook, supporting market sentiment. While several key macro tailwinds appear to be increasingly priced in, valuation comfort, particularly in large caps, easing geopolitical concerns, moderation in crude oil prices from recent peaks, and prospects of a double-digit earnings recovery provide a constructive medium-term backdrop." "Investors are now awaiting India and US inflation data for fresh cues on the interest-rate trajectory, while crude oil prices and global risk sentiment remain key near-term monitorables."Ajit Mishra, SVP – research, Religare Broking said, "Among the key factors, investors appeared to consolidate positions after the sharp upmove of the previous week. Foreign investor participation continued to provide support, with overseas investors having infused over $4.1 billion into Indian equities so far in November. Brent crude hovered around $98 per barrel, while easing concerns over further escalation in West Asia offered some comfort. However, weakness in global technology stocks and uncertainty surrounding the broader geopolitical and economic outlook continued to warrant caution."Stock market will remain closed for trading for Diwali-Balipratipada.
