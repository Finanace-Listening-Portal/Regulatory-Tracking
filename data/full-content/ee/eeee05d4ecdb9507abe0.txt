It covers natural cut and polished diamonds, including semi-processed, half-cut and broken diamonds, weighing up to 25 cents each. Laboratory-grown diamonds are excluded. A DIA is valid for imports for 12 months from issuance, with one authorisation per IEC per financial year. The export obligation must be fulfilled within 18 months of issuance, without extension, through physical exports after import and with at least 10% value addition realised in free foreign exchange. All imports and exports must pass through Mumbai Airport, with applicable clearance procedures at the Precious Cargo Customs Clearance Centre. Eligibility requires Two-Star Export House status or higher, at least US$15 million in cut and polished diamond exports in each of the preceding three financial years, and compliance with GST and income-tax return filing requirements. Import value is capped at 5% of average annual export performance over those years, subject to a US$15 million ceiling. The circular also specifies lot-wise recordkeeping, shipping bill endorsement, actual-user and non-transferability conditions, and the conditions for using an endorsed jobber or supporting manufacturer. Importers must execute a bond equal to the export obligation and furnish a bank guarantee equal to the duty foregone. To regularise a bona fide default, the authorisation holder must pay customs duty on the unutilised import value and applicable section 28AA interest through ICEGATE’s Voluntary/Self-Initiated Payment facility. Circular No. 48/2026-Customs | Dated: 20th of November, 2026 No. 450/73/2024-CusIV Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) (Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs) To, All Principal Chief Commissioners/Chief Commissioners of Customs/ Customs (Preventive)/ Customs and Central Tax and All Principal Director General/ Directorate Generals under CBIC. Madam/Sir, Subject: Implementation of the Diamond Imprest Authorisation (DIA) Scheme – Notification No. 37/2026-Customs dated 20.11.2026 – reg. Reference is invited to Notification No. 53/2024-25 dated 21.01.2025 issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), introducing the Diamond Imprest Authorisation scheme and Public Notice No. 42/2024-25 dated 21.01.2025 issued in this regard. The salient features of the scheme are as under: i. The scheme provides for grant of Diamond Imprest Authorisation for the import of natural cut and polished diamonds, including semi-processed, half-cut, and broken diamonds, each weighing no more than 1/4 Carat or 25 Cents against which physical export of natural cut and polished diamonds is required to be carried out. ii. For the purpose of import, the validity of DIA is 12 months from the date of issuance of Authorisation. iii. Only one authorisation is allowed for one IEC for a particular financial year. iv. The authorisation shall carry an export obligation to achieve a minimum value addition of 10% by realizing in free foreign exchange in accordance with the procedure specified in this regard. The period for fulfilment of export obligation shall be 18 months from the date of issuance of authorisation, no extension of export obligation shall be allowed against DIA. v. The exemptions under this scheme shall not be available for Laboratory Grown Diamonds. vi. Imports made under the DIA Scheme are eligible for exemption from applicable customs duties, subject to fulfilment of the conditions prescribed under the relevant Customs notification. vii. The authorisation and material imported under the authorisation shall be subject to pre-import and actual user condition and is non-transferable even after completion of the export obligation. 2. In pursuance of the aforesaid provisions, Notification No. 37/2026-Customs, dated 20.11.2026, has been issued by the Board under sub-section (1) of section 25 of the Customs Act, 1962, providing exemption from the applicable customs duties in respect of eligible imports made under the DIA Scheme, subject to the conditions prescribed therein. 3. The following clarifications are issued for uniform implementation by the field formations: i. All imports and exports under the Scheme shall be routed only through Mumbai Airport and customs clearance-related procedures shall be undertaken, as applicable, at the Precious Cargo Customs Clearance Centre situated at Bharat Diamond Bourse, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai. ii. The importer shall have the status of a Two-Star Export House or above under the Foreign Trade Policy and shall have achieved a minimum export performance of US$ 15 million in cut and polished diamonds in each of the preceding three financial years to avail the benefits of the Scheme. The scheme permits imports of Natural Cut & Polished Diamonds up to 5% of average annual export performance over previous three years, capped at US$15 million. iii. The importer must have filed all Goods and Services Tax returns and Income Tax returns for the preceding three years. Where the Income Tax return for the preceding year has not been finalised, a certificate issued by a Chartered Accountant may be submitted, undertaking that proof of submission of the said return shall be produced by 31st December of the year of application. iv. The importer shall maintain appropriate records containing the description of goods, including the size of diamonds in carat, colour, clarity and cut, and the value of each lot of imports and exports, bill of entry-wise and shipping bill-wise, respectively, for subsequent inspection by customs officers, wherever required. v. The importer shall execute a Bond equivalent to the export obligation stipulated under the DIA along with a Bank Guarantee for an amount equivalent to the duty foregone, with the jurisdictional proper officer. Single All-India Multipurpose electronic Bond and e-Bank Guarantee prescribed vide circular No 04/2025-customs dated 17.02.2025 may be utilised for the same. vi. The DIA Scheme and the goods imported thereunder shall be subject to the actual user and pre-import condition. Accordingly, the imported goods shall be utilised by the authorisation holder for the purpose for which they have been imported and in accordance with the conditions of the Scheme. The importer shall not be permitted to transfer or sell the imported goods to any other person, even after fulfilling the prescribed export obligation. vii. The imported goods may be used by the DIA holder at any of its units, subject to the condition of paragraph 4.10 of the Handbook of Procedures, or by a jobber or supporting manufacturer, provided same is duly endorsed on the authorisation by the Regional Authority. However, the facility of a co-licensee shall not be available for the DIA Scheme. The importer shall also comply with all the applicable provisions and conditions of the DIA Scheme as specified in Chapter 4 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP), 2023 and the Handbook of Procedures, 2023. viii. Only such exports as are made subsequent to the import under the DIA shall be accepted towards discharge of the export obligation under the Scheme. The shipping bills filed in respect of such exports, bearing the relevant Scheme Code(s) shall be endorsed with the DIA Number, to establish correlation of exports with the DIA issued. ix. For regularisation of bonafide default in fulfilment of export obligation, the Authorisation holder shall pay Customs Duty leviable on the unutilised value of imported material, together with applicable interest thereon as prescribed in section 28 AA of Customs Act,1962, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Customs Act, 1962. The Customs Duty and applicable interest shall be paid through the Voluntary/Self-Initiated Payment (SIP) facility on the ICEGATE e-Payment Platform, as provided vide CBIC Circular No. 27/2024-Customs dated 23.12.2024. 4. All conditions, requirements and procedures prescribed under the relevant DGFT Notifications and Public Notices issued in relation to the Diamond Imprest Authorisation (DIA) Scheme shall be duly complied with by the concerned Authorisation Holders. 5. DG system shall issue scheme codes and suitable advisory for smooth implementation of the scheme. Suitable Public Notice publicising the scheme for guidance of exporters and trade and Standing Order for officers should be issued for implementation. 6. Any difficulty faced in implementation may be brought to the notice of the Board. Hindi version shall follow.
