Articles 2 min read India Inc earnings outlook remains upbeat for second half of FY27 on broad-based demand: Report Corporate earnings are expected to remain strong in the second half of FY27, supported by healthy demand, business expansion and robust order pipelines, Motilal Oswal Financial Services said. The brokerage cited broad-based earnings growth in the September quarter, led by banking, financial services, metals, oil and gas, and automobiles, while flagging cost pressures and geopolitical risks. Advt Earnings growth was primarily driven by banking, financial services, insurance, metals, oil and gas, and automobiles. These four broad sectors accounted for 77 per cent of the incremental year-on-year earnings growth, while other sectors also made positive contributions.The outlook for automobiles remained positive, supported by strong retail performance, lean channel inventories and export growth. Two-wheeler manufacturers expect industry growth in the mid-to-high single digits in the second half, while passenger vehicle manufacturers are guiding for high-single-digit industry growth.In capital goods, healthy order inflows across domestic and export markets, particularly from private-sector customers, are expected to support growth. Demand remains strong across power transmission and distribution, data centres, infrastructure, utilities and renewable energy.Banks also reported healthy credit demand across corporate, small and medium enterprises and secured retail segments. The brokerage expects a gradual recovery in net interest margins during the second half of FY27, supported by the deployment of surplus liquidity and easing funding costs, although pressure could persist for some lenders in the near term.Metals companies expect improved performance in the second half, aided by higher volumes, better realisations and an improved product mix. Rising coking coal costs in the third quarter could, however, weigh on margins.Consumer companies expect sequential margin improvement in the second half as pricing actions, cost-saving initiatives and operating leverage take effect. Advt The technology sector remained a notable area of divergence, with demand supported by artificial intelligence-led transformation and cost optimisation, while discretionary spending and macroeconomic uncertainty continued to constrain some segments.
