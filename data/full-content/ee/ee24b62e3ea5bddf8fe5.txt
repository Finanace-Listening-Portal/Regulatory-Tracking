Advt "We have issued instructions to all superintendents of police that if anyone harasses farmers, their complaints must be registered immediately and criminal cases filed," he said.Fadnavis said the government would also communicate with the Reserve Bank to ensure that microfinance companies did not use coercive methods or intimidate borrowers."If microfinance companies threaten farmers or use force, action will be taken against their senior officials, and criminal cases will be registered," he added.
