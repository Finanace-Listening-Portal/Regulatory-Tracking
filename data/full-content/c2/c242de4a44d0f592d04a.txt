Articles 2 min read Tiger-backed Razorpay set to file for India IPO as early as next week Tiger Global-backed Razorpay is preparing to file its IPO papers in India as early as next week, with investor roadshows expected to follow immediately. The Bengaluru-based fintech firm could launch its public offering as early as February, aiming to raise up to $600 million through a mix of fresh and secondary share sales. The offering, which will comprise both primary and secondary shares, could raise as much as $600 million, the people said.Axis Capital Ltd., Kotak Mahindra Capital Co., Citigroup Inc., Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and JPMorgan Chase & Co. are advising on the offering, they added. Advt Deliberations are ongoing and details including the size, structure and timing of the transaction could change, the people said. Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan declined to comment. Representatives for Razorpay and the other banks didn’t respond to requests for comment.The company confidentially filed draft IPO documents in June and received regulatory approval last week. India’s confidential filing route allows prospective issuers to keep their draft offer documents private, with details disclosed closer to the share sale.Razorpay could be among a number of companies looking to tap India’s buoyant IPO market next year. Companies have raised about $18.6 billion through first-time share sales in India so far in 2026, putting the market within reach of last year’s record $22.4 billion.The company, which also counts GIC Pte., Peak XV Partners, Y Combinator and Lightspeed among its investors, competes with fintech firms including PayU, Cashfree Payments, BillDesk and CCAvenue in India’s rapidly expanding digital-payments market.Razorpay is a full-stack financial-services company that provides digital payments and business-banking solutions to Indian companies, according to its website. Founded in 2014, the company provides payment and banking infrastructure to businesses including Meta Platforms Inc.’s Facebook, Bharti Airtel Ltd., Lenskart, Indian Oil Corp., Tata Consultancy Services Ltd. and Swiggy.
