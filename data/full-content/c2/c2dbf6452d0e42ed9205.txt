The notification, published as S.O. 6293(E), comes into force on 20 November 2026 and prescribes tariff values for specified imported commodities. Under Table 1, the tariff values for crude palm oil, RBD palm oil, other palm oil, crude palmolein, RBD palmolein, other palmolein, crude soybean oil and brass scrap are USD 1,141, USD 1,193, USD 1,167, USD 1,201, USD 1,204, USD 1,203, USD 1,282 and USD 8,154 per metric tonne, respectively, all expressly marked as unchanged. Table 2 specifies the tariff value of gold at USD 1,245 per 10 grams, also unchanged, and silver at USD 1,496 per kilogram, subject to the descriptions and conditions specified for the respective entries. Table 3 retains the tariff value of areca nuts at USD 10,433 per metric tonne. The revised tables govern the tariff values applicable to the specified goods for customs valuation purposes from the effective date. The notification does not itself revise customs duty rates. The principal notification was last amended by Notification No. 91/2026-Customs (N.T.), dated 13 November 2026.
