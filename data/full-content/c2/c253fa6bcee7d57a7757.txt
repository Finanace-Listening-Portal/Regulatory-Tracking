Advt The turnaround comes after overseas investors offloaded $18.91 billion of Indian equities in 2025 and $31.41 billion through October this year on high corporate valuations, a lack of AI-linked investments and elevated oil prices.November's record buying has, therefore, trimmed net foreign selling in Indian stocks to $22.47 billion in 2026.While the surge has helped Indian equities recoup some of their losses relative to regional peers, the Nifty 50 is still down 3.5% for the year, with India among the worst performing markets in Asia.Brokerages including Citi, HSBC and Goldman Sachs have become more optimistic about Indian stocks as investors rotate away from AI-heavy markets in South Korea and Taiwan."We see strong credit growth, resilient earnings and measures supporting growth and the currency," HSBC analysts led by Herald van der Linde said in note last week, adding that India offers diversification and lower volatility compared to Asian markets dominated by AI stocks.Still, sustaining the rally will require steady earnings growth to support India's valuation premium, said Angela Lan, senior strategist at State Street Investment Management.The Nifty 50 trades at 19 times its 12-month forward earnings, a premium to its Asian and emerging-market peers.FINANCIALS POWER THE RALLYHeavyweight financials have led sectoral gains in November, with financial services, banks, private banks and state-owned lenders rising between 11.5% and 13.5%. Advt HDFC Bank, the heaviest-weighted stock on the Nifty 50, has surged 17%, partially reversing its year-to-date losses, while ICICI Bank has climbed 13.5% after upbeat quarterly results reinforced expectations of improving loan growth and easing margin pressure.Financials are particularly well placed as credit growth picks up and margin pressures ease, alongside domestic consumption and EV-linked automakers, said Peeyush Mittal, portfolio manager at Matthews Asia.Other top performers include the auto index, which has risen 8.7% this month. Heavyweight Mahindra & Mahindra has advanced 17.2% in November, helped by a quarterly profit beat due to resilient SUV demand.
