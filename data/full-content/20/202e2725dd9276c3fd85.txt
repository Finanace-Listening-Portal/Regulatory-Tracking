Summary: Polygon’s transition from MATIC to POL works differently depending on where the tokens are held. MATIC on Polygon PoS converted automatically at a 1:1 ratio, and POL now pays transaction fees on that network. Legacy MATIC on Ethereum requires migration through the official Polygon Portal. The article also explains the separate steps for zkEVM and exchange-held balances. Validator staking takes place on Ethereum and requires ETH for gas. The article covers practical risks such as unsupported deposit networks, phishing, malicious token approvals, private-key loss and bridge failures. For Indian tax reporting, it explains the VDA income and TDS provisions and why the 1:1 ratio alone does not determine whether a migration constitutes a taxable transfer. Automatic conversions, transactions through the migrator and exchange-led conversions each require analysis of what actually occurred. Transaction hashes, network and contract details, acquisition costs, INR valuations, fees and withholding records help substantiate that analysis and distinguish wallet movements from disposals. Polygon’s official migration material says that MATIC held on Polygon PoS was converted automatically on a one-for-one basis, whereas MATIC held on Ethereum requires a user-initiated migration through the Polygon Portal. This distinction is operationally important: an address may show a familiar balance while the network, contract and transaction process have changed. The project stated on 3 September 2025 that the migration was 99% complete and that every Polygon PoS transaction had used POL as native gas since September 2024. A holder should therefore identify the chain, token contract and custody route before approving any transaction. This article explains the current official design without forecasting price, adoption or investment returns. Question Verified position Practical consequence What is the current token? POL is the ecosystem token used for Polygon PoS gas and staking. Wallet labels and exchange tickers should be checked against the network and contract. What happened to Polygon PoS MATIC? Official guidance says it converted automatically 1:1 to POL. A separate manual swap is generally not required for that on-chain balance. What about Ethereum MATIC? It can be migrated 1:1 through the Polygon Portal migration contract. The user pays Ethereum gas and must verify the official interface and contract. Where does staking occur? Polygon’s official staking guide describes staking on Ethereum. POL must be on Ethereum and ETH is needed for transaction gas. Migration routes depend on the network that holds the asset Polygon PoS balances and wallet display changes The automatic Polygon PoS conversion changed the native token from MATIC to POL without requiring the holder to sign a migration transaction. Some wallets may continue to display the old symbol until their token metadata is refreshed. The underlying risk is not merely cosmetic: users can misread a symbol, add an unofficial custom token or send an Ethereum token to a Polygon PoS address through an unsupported path. A reliable review starts with the network selected in the wallet and the transaction explorer, not with the ticker alone. Native gas POL on Polygon PoS is different from an ERC-20 balance held on Ethereum even if the economic migration ratio was one-for-one. Ethereum MATIC and the official migrator For MATIC held on Ethereum, Polygon directs users to the Polygon Portal. The official support material identifies the POL Ethereum contract as 0x455e53CBB86018Ac2B8092FdCd39d8444aFFC3F6 and the migrator as 0x29e7DF7b6A1B2b07b731457f499E1696c60E2C4e. Contract strings should be copied only after independently opening an official Polygon source because search advertisements, cloned interfaces and malicious approvals can imitate a migration page. A migration transaction may involve an approval followed by the migration call, and both consume Ethereum gas. A transaction submitted to an incorrect contract is generally irreversible. zkEVM and exchange-held balances are separate workflows Polygon’s support note says that legacy MATIC on Polygon zkEVM should first be bridged to Ethereum and then migrated. That multi-step route introduces bridge, gas, approval and address-selection risks. Custodial exchanges may conduct their own token conversion and temporarily suspend deposits or withdrawals; their internal ticker change does not prove that a particular on-chain deposit route is supported. The platform’s current deposit network, memo requirements and migration notice must be read before transferring. An exchange account entry is a contractual claim against the custodian, whereas self-custodied POL is controlled by private keys. POL utility, staking mechanics and token-supply governance Gas and validator staking are distinct uses POL pays transaction fees on Polygon PoS. Staking, by contrast, is performed through the official staking interface on Ethereum under Polygon’s stated process. A staker therefore needs POL on Ethereum and ETH to pay gas, must evaluate validator commission and performance, and remains exposed to smart-contract, key-management and protocol risks. Delegating tokens does not create a guaranteed deposit or fixed return. Rewards, validator rules, withdrawal timing and the user interface can change through protocol governance or technical upgrades. Emission design is not a price or yield promise Polygon described a 2% annual POL emission model, split at launch between validator rewards and a Community Treasury, while also stating that the parameters may be changed through community governance. The PIP-17 proposal described an initial supply of 10 billion POL and a one-for-one migration from MATIC. These protocol parameters explain potential dilution and validator incentives; they do not establish market value, an enforceable income stream or regulatory approval in India. Holders should separate technical issuance rules from the market price quoted by a trading venue. Indian VDA tax and TDS records for POL transactions Section 115BBH and the cost-acquisition restriction Indian income-tax law applies a 30% rate to income from transfer of a virtual digital asset under section 115BBH, with applicable surcharge and health and education cess. The official Income Tax Department explainer states that, apart from cost of acquisition, no expenditure or allowance is deductible and a VDA loss cannot be set off against other income or carried forward under that provision. The taxable computation therefore may differ sharply from an ordinary business-profit calculation. Each disposal must be reconstructed in INR using a defensible contemporaneous valuation and documented acquisition cost. Section 194S may require one-per-cent tax deduction Section 194S generally requires tax deduction at one per cent on consideration for transfer of a VDA when the statutory conditions are met. The official guidance refers to annual thresholds of ₹50,000 for a specified person and ₹10,000 for others. The person responsible, whether consideration is in cash or kind, the role of an exchange, and credit or payment timing can affect compliance. Platform withholding data should be reconciled with Form 26AS/AIS and the transaction ledger rather than treated as a final computation of income. The MATIC-to-POL migration needs event-specific tax analysis The protocol describes migration as a one-for-one technical upgrade, but that description does not by itself decide Indian tax treatment. The legal question is whether the holder’s particular action constitutes a transfer of a VDA and whether any consideration or gain arises. Automatic Polygon PoS conversion, an Ethereum migrator transaction and an exchange’s internal redenomination are factually different. Because the statute does not provide a bespoke Polygon migration rule, a taxpayer should preserve wallet statements, transaction hashes, contract addresses, timestamps, INR values and the official migration notice, then obtain advice based on the exact custody route. A custody and record trail suited to the POL architecture A useful audit file links the acquisition record to every subsequent wallet move, bridge, migration, stake, reward, sale or exchange conversion. It should record the wallet address under the taxpayer’s control, transaction hash, network, token contract, quantity, gas paid, counterparty or platform, INR valuation source and tax deducted. Transfers between wallets owned by the same person may still create reconciliation issues even when no economic sale occurred. Staking rewards require separate characterization and valuation analysis, and later disposal may create an additional VDA transfer event. Clear records also reduce the risk that an exchange deposit is mistaken for a taxable sale or that a bridge fee is silently netted from quantity. Questions readers ask about this subject Is POL simply a new ticker for MATIC? No. POL is the current Polygon ecosystem token, while legacy MATIC may remain on Ethereum or in older custody records. The network and contract must be identified, not only the ticker. Must a Polygon PoS holder manually migrate MATIC? Polygon’s official guidance says the Polygon PoS balance converted automatically 1:1 to POL. Wallet metadata may still need updating. Can Ethereum MATIC be sent directly to a Polygon PoS POL deposit address? Only if the receiving platform explicitly supports that network and route. Migration and bridging are different actions, and an unsupported deposit may be unrecoverable. Does staking POL occur on Polygon PoS? Polygon’s official staking guide describes validator staking on Ethereum. POL and ETH for gas are required on that network under the current process. Is the 2% emission rate a guaranteed staking return? No. Emission allocation is a protocol parameter subject to governance; realized rewards depend on validator and protocol conditions and are not guaranteed. Is a one-for-one MATIC-to-POL upgrade automatically tax-free in India? There is no Polygon-specific statutory exemption. Treatment depends on the exact event and whether it is a transfer; automatic, self-executed and custodial migrations need separate analysis. What records are most important for Indian tax reporting? Keep contract addresses, network, transaction hashes, timestamps, quantities, INR values, acquisition cost, gas, exchange statements and section 194S withholding data.
