Advt Under the draft, the NPV of PB Fintech’s general insurance business could fall to around 35-40% of current levels, implying a 60-65% cut. Life insurance NPV is expected to remain “in the same neighborhood”, albeit not the same.PB Fintech’s core revenue is roughly split 50:50 between general insurance and life insurance. A 60% cut in general insurance economics would therefore translate into about a 30% cut to core revenue before factoring in any offsets.PB Fintech works with its general insurance partners on a combined operating ratio model, so lower commissions should pass through to customer prices. Price elasticity has been around one, and management expects to “get back” 15-20% through volume growth.The company’s cost base is about Rs 30 billion, and management indicated potential savings of 10-15%, although concrete steps will be taken based on how the final regulations evolve.Potential new revenue streams include charging for services, reinsurance broking and possibly manufacturing. PB Fintech’s service businesses include PB Garage, PB Care+ hospital network, PB Health hospital chain and PB Wheels, which are separate companies outside the licensed distributor and do not currently charge fees.FY27 impactManagement expects no impact in FY27, with implementation most likely from FY28. It described FY28 as a year of “challenges and discovery” and aims to return to a similar position by FY29. Advt The biggest open risk is health renewal commissions. It remains unclear whether the cuts would apply to existing business retrospectively or only to new business.The top 50% of the sales force accounts for about 80% of sales. Around 6,000 people were hired in the first half of FY27 to support growth, and management indicated that it would not have hired at this scale had the draft come earlier.Staff with more than six months of tenure are significantly more productive, so a slower hiring pace combined with a maturing sales force should drive productivity. Management said there would be no mass layoffs or knee-jerk decisions. Advt At the margin, PB Fintech was spending approximately Rs 100 to generate Rs 100 of revenue through Google spending and tier-2 and tier-3 expansion across about 150 cities. At around Rs 30 of revenue per Rs 100 of spending, marginal spending is no longer economically viable, implying likely cuts to digital marketing, brand spending and expansion hiring.PB Fintech incurs costs upfront to acquire fresh, young health insurance customers and earns through renewals over time. The company accounts for about 50% of fresh, young customers entering retail health and has the lowest portability-out share in the industry. If this funnel stops, insurers’ books will age, potentially leading to higher claims ratios. Advt The draft recognises that quality of business should be given value, although it does not specify how. PB Fintech will push for approval of a managing general agent framework alongside these rules.Management now sees a higher probability of PB Fintech setting up its own insurer or reinsurer. Its reasoning is that if distributors are paid less but the savings do not reach customers, the benefit simply moves from PB Fintech to insurers.The proposed commissions make both participating and non-participating savings products unattractive, even for large agents. PB Fintech believes it is better placed to capture any organic demand that exists, although management acknowledges that such demand may be limited.
