We further suggest that: (a) where an object of the issue is investment in another entity, the listed entity may, before making the investment, obtain an undertaking from the investee to furnish information and allow access to the monitoring agency; (b) the monitoring agency may report every payment out of the issue proceeds to a related party of the listed entity, with the nature of the payment; and (c) for proceeds placed with scheduled commercial banks, the monitoring agency may confirm that no lien or charge has been created on the deposits and that they have not been given as security for any borrowing, including borrowings of group entities. This note is illustrative and appears in a format rather than in the Regulations. As the Working Group proposes to use only the term “variation” and to align it with section 27 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Regulation 59 of the ICDR Regulations (para 10.2 of the WG Report), a definition in the Regulations will be useful.
