Articles 1 min read DCB Bank Q2 profit rises 29% Rs 236 cr DCB Bank reported a net profit of Rs 236 crore for the second quarter ending September 30, 2026. This represents a significant increase from Rs 184 crore in the same period last year. Total income also rose to Rs 2,300 crore, up from Rs 2,009 crore a year earlier. Furthermore, asset quality improved, with gross non-performing assets declining to 2.29 percent. The bank's capital adequacy ratio increased to 16. Total income increased to Rs 2,300 crore from Rs 2,009 crore in the year-ago period, DCB Bank said in a regulatory filing.Its net interest income improved to Rs 2,051 crore in July-September FY27 from Rs 1,907 crore a year ago.Asset quality of the bank improved, with gross non-performing assets (NPAs) declining to 2.29 per cent of gross advances at the end of the September 2026 against 2.91 per cent a year ago. Advt Net NPAs or bad loans also eased to 0.73 per cent against 1.21 per cent in the year-ago period.Capital adequacy ratio of the bank increased to 16.63 per cent from 16.41 per cent at the end of September 2025.
