Advt Net NPAs or bad loans also moderated to 0.4 per cent, as against 0.52 per cent in the year-ago period.The Capital Adequacy Ratio of the bank increased to 14.67 per cent, from 14.11 per cent at the end of September 2025.During Q2 FY27, the bank mobilised Rs 34,390 crore (approximately USD 3.6 billion) of FCNR(B) deposits despite having no branch, subsidiary or representative office outside India.This demonstrates the bank's digital capabilities and the trust it enjoys among customers, it said.
