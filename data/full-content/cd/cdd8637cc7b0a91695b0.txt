Its origins go back to the post of the Auditor General of the British Crown in India, created under the Government of India Act, 1858. On November 16, 1860, Sir Edward Drummond assumed charge as the first Auditor General. November 16 is celebrated each year as 'Audit Diwas' to mark this event. Advt K Sanjay Murthy, CAG of India, along with senior management, principal accountants general, directors general, accountants general and principal directors, as well as officials from ministries and departments, will attend the event.Audit Diwas 2026 will showcase the work of the department in innovation, capacity building and technological interventions, an official release said.A key highlight of Audit Diwas 2026 will be the unveiling of the department's Large Language Model (LLM) 2.0, which features three distinct streams dedicated to compliance audit, financial audit, and works audit.The release further said that 'Artificial Intelligence Champions' will present a roadmap for enterprise-wide roll-out within the Indian Audit and Account Department in areas of financial audit, Income Tax audit and finalisation of pension cases of state government employees.The CAG HR Management System portal 2.0 and an Executive MBA programme for IA&AD officers with IIM Mumbai will also be launched, the release said.Audit Diwas will be followed by AG Conclave on the theme, 'Fostering Excellence -- People, Process and Partnerships', on November 17.
