The strategic investment has been made through Bajaj Finance, part of Bajaj Finserv, the Group said in a statement on Wednesday.Besides, it said, Pennant Technologies, a Bajaj Finance's investee company that provides cloud-native, AI-powered lending solutions to global banks and financial institutions, has also invested USD 2 million in Soket AI.Soket AI, which is supported by the IndiaAI Mission, is building research capabilities in large-scale foundation models, with the ambition of developing globally competitive AI technologies, it said. Advt The investment will support its work on indigenous AI solutions for applications where data security, reliability and technological sovereignty are critical.Beyond capital, the partnership is expected to open opportunities for applied AI research, enterprise applications and capability building by combining Bajaj Finance's industry expertise with Soket AI's foundational research capabilities."Our investment in Soket AI reflects our commitment to strengthening India's sovereign AI capabilities and building scalable solutions for India and the world," Bajaj Finserv Ltd Chairman and Managing Director Sanjiv Bajaj said.
