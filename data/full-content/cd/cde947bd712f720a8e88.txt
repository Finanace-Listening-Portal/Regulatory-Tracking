This will require the central bank to accumulate dollars as capital inflows improve, limiting the rupee's appreciation, IDFC First Bank Economic Research report said.Around $40 billion of the short position matures within a year, $56 billion in one to three years and the remaining $143 billion in three to five years. The longer-duration positions are largely linked to the FCNR (B) scheme, it added. "The large negative forward book implies that the RBI will need to accumulate dollars in order to meet the commitments when these buy-sell swaps mature.
