The authorisation follows approval granted by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, in consultation with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), through its letter dated 29th October, 2026. The notification has been issued under Rule 5 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020, read with Section 4(4)(b)(ii) of the Aadhaar Act, 2016. NUCFDC may undertake Aadhaar authentication for one-time registration of employees and outsourced personnel under its attendance management system, identification of visitors during registration and issuance of visitor cards, and verification of officials for administrative purposes. The authentication facilities are intended to prevent impersonation and duplication while ensuring accuracy and integrity. NUCFDC must obtain the consent of Aadhaar number holders before authentication. Consistent with the voluntary authentication safeguards introduced through the Aadhaar and Other Laws (Amendment) Act, 2019, the agency must provide alternative and viable identification mechanisms and cannot deny services to individuals who refuse or are unable to undergo Aadhaar authentication. Alternative documents include PAN cards, voter identity cards, bank or post office passbooks with photographs, ration cards, passports, driving licences and domicile certificates. The notification comes into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. MINISTRY OF COOPERATION NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 16th November, 2026 S.O. 6259(E).—Whereas the use of Aadhaar number to establish identity enables individuals to receive subsidies, benefits and services in a convenient and seamless manner, obviates the need for multiplicity of documents to establish identity, simplifies processes and promotes transparency and efficiency; And whereas, the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, after consultation with the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), had authorised vide its letter number 13(9)/2023-EG-II dated the 29th October, 2026, the Ministry of Cooperation, Government of India, to allow the National Urban Cooperative Finance and Development Corporation Limited (NUCFDC) (hereinafter referred to as the said Agency) for the purposes specified under sub-rule (1) of rule 3 of the Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance (Social Welfare, Innovation, Knowledge) Rules, 2020 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), to perform Aadhaar authentication and to use Aadhaar number during authentication for establishing the identity of Aadhaar number holder and notify the same under rule 5 of the said rules read with sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of Section 4 of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as the Act); And whereas, the Aadhaar authentication shall be performed for usage of digital platforms to ensure good governance, promoting ease of living of residents and enabling better access to services for them (hereinafter referred to as the said purpose) as specified under sub-rule (1) of rule 3 of the said rules and for performing Aadhaar authentication on a voluntary basis for the following purposes, namely:— a. to establish the identity of employees and outsourced personnel during one-time registration for creation of a personnel database under the attendance b. management system; b. to establish the identity of visitors at the time of registration and issuance of visitor cards; and c. to establish the identity of NUCFDC officials for administrative purposes, to prevent impersonation and duplication, and to ensure accuracy and integrity, using Yes/No or/and eKYC authentication facilities. Now, therefore, in pursuance of rule 5 of the said rules read with sub-clause (ii) of clause (b) of sub-section (4) of section 4 of the Act, the Ministry of Cooperation hereby notifies the following, namely: – 4. (1) The said Agency, as provided in the Act, shall obtain the consent of the Aadhaar number holder for the purpose of authentication herein. (2) As per sub-rule (2) of rule 3 of the said rules, Aadhaar authentication is on voluntary basis, the said Agency shall inform the Aadhaar number holder of alternate and viable means of identification and shall not deny any service to the Aadhaar number holder for refusing to, or being unable to, undergo Aadhaar authentication, namely:- a. Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card; b. Voter Identity Card; c. Bank or Post Office Passbook with Photo; d. Ration Card; e. Passport; f. Driving license issued by the Licensing Authority under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988; g. Domicile Certificate 2. This Notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. [F. No. 20-20011/27/2024-MoC (120821)] RAMAN KUMAR, Jt. Secy.
