The lender's legacy portfolio has fallen to less than 2% of assets under management (AUM), while retail accounts for about 85% of AUM. Improving net interest margins, operating leverage, a richer product mix and controlled credit costs are expected to drive the earnings recovery. Advt The company is expected to deliver AUM growth of around 25% annually over FY26-FY28, taking total AUM to approximately Rs 1.6 lakh crore by FY28. Its growth portfolio accounts for about 98% of AUM, while retail disbursements rose 44% year on year to Rs 12,500 crore in the first quarter of FY27 and consolidated AUM grew around 25% , according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. These trends support the visibility of its FY27 AUM growth target of about 25%.The next phase of growth is expected to be supported by a richer portfolio mix, with higher-yielding businesses such as gold loans, micro-loans against property, microfinance and unsecured lending complementing the established housing and loans-against-property franchise. Disbursement yields stood at 15.5% for used-car loans, 16.6% for salaried personal loans and 18.8% for unsecured business loans, compared with 11.7% for housing loans and 12.8% for loans against property.Gold loans could become another meaningful growth avenue. The company expanded its gold-loan branch network to 67 in June 2026 from 22 in March and is targeting around 200 branches by the end of FY27. Cross-selling already contributes about 28-29% of business volumes, providing scope to grow the franchise through its customer base of around six million while potentially lowering customer-acquisition costs and supporting credit outcomes.NIM boostNet interest margin (NIM) is estimated to improve to 5.4% in FY27 and 5.9% in FY28, compared with 4.7% in FY26. The expansion is expected to be driven by higher-yielding originations and a gradual reduction in borrowing costs, supported by the company's improved credit rating and greater access to diversified and multilateral funding. NIM stood at about 6.5% in the first quarter of FY27, up around 47 basis points year on year, while the cost of borrowings was about 8.8%. The first-quarter figure and full-year estimates are reproduced as stated in the brokerage report. Advt Operating leverage is expected to provide another significant boost to profitability. AUM grew around 25% year on year in the first quarter of FY27, while operating expenses increased about 10%. As business growth outpaced expense growth, the cost-to-income ratio declined to 52.5% from 65.6% a year earlier. The operating expense-to-AUM ratio fell to 3.5% from 4.9% over eight quarters, despite the resumption of branch expansion. The company expects a further 40-50 basis-point improvement in the opex-to-AUM ratio over the next four to six quarters and is targeting a return on assets under management of about 2.5% by the fourth quarter of FY27. Advt Credit costs in the growth businesses remain manageable at about 1.6%, with tighter underwriting and stable asset quality expected to support credit costs at current levels through FY27-FY28. A primary equity infusion of about Rs 3,850 crore is also expected to strengthen the capital base and provide flexibility to fund growth while maintaining a prudent risk profile.The brokerage estimates return on assets and return on equity to improve to 2.8% and about 11.4%, respectively, by FY28. Motilal Oswal Financial Services has reiterated its Buy rating on the stock with a target price of Rs 2,480, based on a sum-of-the-parts valuation for March 2028. The stock trades at around 1.3 times estimated FY28 price-to-book value.
