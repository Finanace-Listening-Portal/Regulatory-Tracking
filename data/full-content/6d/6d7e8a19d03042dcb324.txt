Articles 1 min read RBI recognises Unified Fintech Forum as second SRO in fintech sector after FACE: Governor Malhotra Indian central bank has finally recognised Unified Fintech Forum (UFF) as the second self-regulatory organisation for the fintech sector, nearly two years after FACE became the first fintech SRO. UFF, formerly Digital Lenders Association of India, had been pursuing the recognition since the RBI’s SRO framework was introduced. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Sep 10, 2026 at 05:01 PM IST var news_msid = 134007138; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Governor Sanjay Malhotra on Thursday announced the recognition of Unified Fintech Forum (UFF) as the second Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) in the fintech sector, nearly two years after the central bank gave the first such recognition to Fintech Association for Consumer Empowerment (FACE).“For a regulated development of the fintech industry, we have implemented self-regulatory organisation framework for this sector. It shall be conducive to promoting responsible conduct, developing industry-wide standards for everyone to follow, and facilitating constructive engagement with us, other regulators, policymakers and stakeholders,” Malhotra said. Advt “Two years ago, we had given recognition to the first SRO in fintech. Today, I’m pleased to announce the recognition of Unified Fintech Forum as the 2nd SRO in fintech. My congratulations to the team,” he added.The RBI had recognised FACE as the first SRO-FT on August 28, 2024, after receiving three applications under its framework for fintech SROs. The announcement was made by then RBI Governor Shaktikanta Das at the Global Fintech Fest. At the time, the central bank said one of the other applications had been returned with an opportunity to resubmit, while the third application was under examination.DLAI, which was among the applicants, said its application had not been returned and remained under consideration.The framework had been finalised by the RBI in May 2024, with the SRO-FT envisaged as an industry-led body that would set baseline standards and codes of conduct, monitor adherence and facilitate communication between fintech firms and the regulator.UFF was earlier known as the Digital Lenders Association of India (DLAI). The association, established in October 2016, had been working towards industry self-regulation well before the formal SRO framework was finalised. It also changed its name in a bid to achieve SRO status. By Shrishti Sharma , ETBFSI Published On Sep 10, 2026 at 05:01 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
