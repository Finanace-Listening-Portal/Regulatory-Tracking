Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Summary: The International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA), through Circular No. IFSCA/CMD/MIIT/PID/2026/001 dated November 27, 2026, has issued guidelines on critical issues to be discussed at meetings of Public Interest Directors (PIDs) of Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs), including stock exchanges, clearing corporations and the depository in the IFSC. Under the MII Regulations, 2021, PIDs must meet separately at least once every six months. The circular requires every PID to attend these meetings and specifies eight areas for deliberation: regulatory compliance; functioning of the critical operations and regulatory, compliance, risk management and investor grievances verticals; adequacy of financial and human resources; conflicts of interest and other significant issues; reliance on parent entities; investor data protection and risks arising from artificial intelligence and machine learning; adequacy of management information and follow-up on PID concerns; and corrective action on inspection, system audit and cyber security audit findings. Reports must be prepared for each half-year, April–September and October–March, and emailed directly to the Authority within the timeline prescribed in the Master Circular dated June 05, 2026. Reports must cover deliberations, observations, concerns and outcomes. Copies are to be placed before the governing board where deemed appropriate, without making board submission a prerequisite for timely reporting to IFSCA. PIDs serving on committees must brief other PIDs on committee functioning, and PIDs may consult relevant personnel. The circular takes effect immediately. ****** International Financial Services Centre Authority Circular No. IFSCA/CMD/MIIT/PID/2026/001 | Dated: November 27, 2026 To, All Stock Exchanges in the International Financial Services Centre (IFSC); All Clearing Corporations in the IFSC; The Depository in the IFSC; (Collectively the ‘Market Infrastructure Institutions’) Madam/Sir, Subject: Guidelines on critical issues to be discussed during the meetings of Public Interest Directors of the Market Infrastructure Institutions 1. Reference may be drawn to the International Financial Services Centre Authority (Market Infrastructure Institutions) Regulations, 2021 [hereinafter referred to as “MII Regulations”] particularly, sub-regulation (1) of regulation 25, which requires the governing board, directors, committee members and key management personnel of Market Infrastructure Institutions to abide by the Code of Conduct specified under Part B of Schedule I of the MII Regulations. 2. Reference may also be drawn to clause (b) of Section II.C of Part B under Schedule I of the MII Regulations, which inter-alia, requires Public Interest Directors of the Market Infrastructure Institutions to meet separately, at least once in six months to exchange views on critical issues and submit a report of such meeting to the Authority and to the governing board of the Market Infrastructure Institution. 3. To ensure that such meetings, and the reports focus on critical issues, the Authority hereby specifies guidelines for the conduct of such meetings and deliberations to be made therein. 4. The critical issues to be discussed in the meetings of the Public Interest Director shall, inter-alia, include the following: i. to review the status of compliance with all applicable Acts, regulations, circulars, guidelines, communications issued by the Authority, from time to time; ii. to review the functioning of Vertical-1 i.e. “Critical Operations” and Vertical-2 i.e. “Regulatory, Compliance, Risk Management and Investor Grievances”, as specified under Schedule-II of the MII Regulations; iii. to review the adequacy of resources (both financial and human) for effective functioning of Verticals 1 and 2, as specified under Schedule-II of the MII Regulations; iv. to identify issues which may involve conflict of interest for the Market Infrastructure Institutions or may have significant impact on the functioning of the Market Infrastructure Institutions or may not be in the interest of securities market; v. to review and rationalize the reliance of the Market Infrastructure Institutions on the parent entity, particularly in relation to technology infrastructure, critical operational functions or any other material matters; vi. to review and evaluate the adequacy of the safeguards adopted by the Market Infrastructure Institutions for the protection, confidentiality, integrity and security of investor data, including measures to address risks arising from the use of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML); vii. to assess the quality, adequacy and timeliness of the flow of information furnished by the management of the Market Infrastructure Institutions to its governing board and to the Public Interest Directors, and to identify the observations and concerns raised by the Public Interest Directors in the meetings of the governing board or its committees that have not been appropriately documented, satisfactorily addressed or followed through to their logical conclusion; and viii. to review the corrective steps taken by the Market Infrastructure Institutions on the observations made by the Authority in its inspections, and on the findings of system audit and cyber security audit of the Market Infrastructure Institutions, particularly in respect of governance standards, technology and cyber security. 5. Every Public Interest Director shall mandatorily attend the meetings of the Public Interest Directors held to deliberate on the objectives/critical issues specified in clause 4 above; 6. The report of such meetings of the Public Interest Directors shall be prepared for each half-year, namely, April to September and October to March, and shall be submitted to the Authority vide email to [email protected] , within such time as stipulated under the ‘Master Circular for recognised Stock Exchanges and recognised Clearing Corporations’ dated June 05, 2026; 7. The report referred in clause 6 above, shall cover the matters specified in clause 4, including the deliberations held, the key observations made, the concerns raised and the outcome of such meetings; 8. The Public Interest Directors shall also place a copy of the report before the Governing Board of the Market Infrastructure Institutions, where deemed appropriate; Provided that such submission of report to the Governing Board shall not be a prerequisite for, or otherwise affect the obligation of the Public Interest Directors to submit the report directly to the Authority within the stipulated timeline. 9. Every Public Interest Director who is a member of the committee of the Market Infrastructure Institutions, including a statutory committee, shall brief to the other Public Interest Directors on the working of that committee. 10. For the purpose of deliberating on the critical issues specified in clause 4, Public Interest Directors may discuss with the concerned Vertical Heads or Key Management Personnel or any other personnel of the Market Infrastructure Institution, as deemed appropriate. 11. This Circular is issued in exercise of powers conferred by Sections 12 and 13 of the International Financial Services Centres Authority Act, 2019 read with regulation 72 of the MII Regulations, and shall come into force with immediate effect. A copy of this Circular is available on the website of the International Financial Services Centres Authority at www.ifsca.gov.in .
