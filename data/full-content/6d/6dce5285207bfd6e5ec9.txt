} } Listen to This ArticleSecuritisation volumes rose 42 per cent year-on-year (YoY) to a record ₹1.47 trillion in the first half of 2026-27 (H1 FY27), driven by a sharp increase in gold loan securitisation and a recovery in vehicle loan transactions, according to ICRA estimates. A pick-up in activity during the July-September quarter pushed half-yearly volumes to an all-time high. Volumes stood at ₹1.04 trillion in H1 FY26. In Q2 FY27, securitisation volumes reached ₹86,000 crore, supported by growing traction in gold loans, a revival in vehicle loan securitisation and steady activity in the micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and business loan segments. Gold loans continued to lead growth in H1 FY27, with securitisation volumes in the segment rising nearly 1.5 times over the year-ago period. Traditional segments, including vehicle, MSME/business and mortgage loans, also recorded steady growth, said Sachin Joglekar, vice-president and co-group head, Structured Finance Ratings, Icra. Vehicle loans accounted for a third of overall securitisation volumes in H1 FY27, compared with 25 per cent in Q1FY27.
