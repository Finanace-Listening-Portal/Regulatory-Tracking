The disclosure triggered a sharp reaction in the stock market, with the bank’s shares falling 27% on March 11, 2025. The discrepancies involved accounting lapses and losses were then estimated at nearly Rs 2,000 crore. Kathpalia resigned weeks after the disclosure, taking what he described as moral responsibility for the various acts of commission and omission brought to his notice. Deputy CEO Arun Khurana had also resigned a day earlier. The episode subsequently attracted regulatory scrutiny.
