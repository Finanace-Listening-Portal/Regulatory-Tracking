The revised tariff values include crude palm oil at US$1,141 per metric tonne, RBD palm oil at US$1,193, crude soya bean oil at US$1,282 and brass scrap at US$8,154. Gold is valued at US$1,245 per 10 grams and silver at US$1,663 per kilogram for the specified categories. The tariff value of areca nuts remains unchanged at US$10,433 per metric tonne. These values are relevant for customs duty assessment of the specified goods. The notification takes effect from 14 November 2026.
