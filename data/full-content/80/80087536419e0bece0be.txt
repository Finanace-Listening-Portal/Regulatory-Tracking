Articles 2 min read Life insurers' new business premium rises 26% to ₹42,914 crore in October, LIC leads growth In October 2026, life insurance companies reported a 26.19% growth in new business premiums. The Life Insurance Corporation of India contributed significantly with a 31.35% increase. Private insurers also saw substantial growth, with ICICI Prudential Life leading at 37.46%. The growth in premiums outpaced policy additions, indicating higher average premium realization. Group single premium business was the biggest contributor to overall collections for the month. Anushka Sengupta ETBFSI Published On Nov 12, 2026 at 12:48 PM IST var news_msid = 135759615; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Life insurance companies reported a 26.19% year-on-year (Y-o-Y) growth in new business premium (NBP) to ₹42,914.34 crore in October 2026, compared with ₹34,006.95 crore in the same month last year, according to provisional data released by the Life Insurance Council.The growth was largely driven by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), which recorded a 31.35% increase in new business premium to ₹25,315.52 crore during the month. Private life insurers collectively reported a 19.45% growth to ₹17,598.82 crore.Among private insurers, ICICI Prudential Life recorded a 37.46% rise in October new business premium to ₹2,437.44 crore, while HDFC Life reported an 18.2% increase to ₹3,333.60 crore. SBI Life's premium grew 11.65% to ₹3,556 crore. Bajaj Life Insurance registered a 19.4% growth to ₹1,355.19 crore, while Aditya Birla Sun Life reported a 20.05% increase to ₹1,347.80 crore. Advt Group single premium business drives October collectionsA closer look at the product mix shows that group single premium business remained the biggest contributor to October collections. It generated ₹25,926.86 crore, registering a 32.97% growth over the corresponding month last year. Individual single premium business grew 14.79% to ₹4,548.33 crore, while individual non-single premium rose 4.82% to ₹9,298.24 crore.Cumulative premium growth outpaces policy additionsThe cumulative performance for April-October FY27 also remained strong, with the industry's new business premium rising 21.39% to ₹2.89 lakh crore, against ₹2.38 lakh crore in the year-ago period. Private insurers recorded a faster growth of 22.04% to ₹1.19 lakh crore, compared with LIC's 20.94% increase to ₹1.70 lakh crore.However, premium growth continued to outpace policy additions. The industry issued 1.88 million policies and schemes in October, taking the cumulative number to 13.56 million during April-October, a marginal growth of 1.15% year-on-year.While private insurers recorded an 8.48% increase in cumulative policy additions to 5.30 million, LIC saw a 3.05% decline to 8.27 million policies. The divergence between premium growth and policy volumes points to higher average premium realisation and the growing contribution of group and single-premium business to overall collections.
