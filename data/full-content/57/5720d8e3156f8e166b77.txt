The company successfully commissioned 419 MW of utility-scale projects, marking a remarkable growth of over 130% compared to the 182 MW executed in the same quarter of the previous year. Additionally, TPREL completed 131.2 MWhr of battery storage projects during this period. The first half of FY27 saw TPREL executing a total of 645 MW of utility-scale projects and 161.2 MWhr of storage projects. Notable projects commissioned during this period include the Jewali Wind Project with a capacity of 100.8 MW, the Kalasar FDRE Project with 227.5 MW of solar capacity and a 101.2 MWhr Battery Energy Storage System (BESS), the 100 MW Kayathar Group Captive Project, and the 72.5 MW Tata Steel Captive Project. These achievements underscore TPREL’s commitment to advancing India’s clean energy goals. TPREL’s operational portfolio now stands at 7.2 GW, comprising 5.8 GW of solar, 1.3 GW of wind, and 281.2 MWhr of battery storage capacity. The company attributes its success to a robust supply chain, experienced partners, and strong project management capabilities. This expansion aligns with India’s national energy security and sustainability objectives. Looking ahead, TPREL plans to execute more than 2 GW of projects in the second half of FY27, including 1,075 MW of solar, 969 MW of wind, and 274 MWhr of BESS. By the end of the fiscal year, the company expects to have executed nearly 9.3 GW of projects. Analysts view TPREL’s growth trajectory as a positive indicator for the renewable energy sector, reflecting the increasing demand for sustainable energy solutions in India.
