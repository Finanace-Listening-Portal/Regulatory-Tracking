The notification has been issued in exercise of powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 43 of the PMLA and in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court of Himachal Pradesh. It amends the principal Notification S.O. 372(E) dated 5 February 2016, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii). Specifically, in the table contained in the principal notification, against serial number 9 relating to the State of Himachal Pradesh, the first entries appearing in columns (3) and (4) are substituted. Under the revised entries, the “Additional Sessions Judge (CBI) Shimla” is specified in column (3), while its corresponding territorial jurisdiction in column (4) comprises the districts of Shimla, Kinnaur, Solan and Sirmaur at Nahan of Himachal Pradesh. The notification therefore modifies the relevant court and territorial jurisdiction entries applicable to these Himachal Pradesh districts for purposes of the PMLA framework. The notification bears file number F. No. C-18015/2/2026-Ad.ED and has been issued by Rajeev Lochan, Under Secretary. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 16th September, 2026 S.O. 5082(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 43 of the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 (15 of 2003) and in consultation with the Chief Justice of High Court of Himachal Pradesh, the Central Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, section 3, sub-section (ii) vide number S.O 372(E), dated the 5th February, 2016 namely:- In the said notification, in the TABLE, against serial number 9 relating to the State of Himachal Pradesh, for the first entries in columns (3) and (4), the following entries shall respectively be substituted, namely: — (3) (4) “Additional Sessions Judge (CBI) Shimla. Districts Shimla, Kinnaur, Solan and Sirmaur at Nahan of Himachal Pradesh.” [F. No. C-18015/2/2026-Ad.ED] RAJEEV LOCHAN, Under Secy. Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii), vide number S.O. 372(E), dated the 5th February, 2016.
