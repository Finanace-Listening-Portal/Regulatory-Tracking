JM Financial assumes a 20% share for Paytm, implying about 8 basis points of effective pass-throughOne 97 Communications, the parent of Paytm, could see incremental revenue of Rs 214 crore in FY27 and Rs 473 crore in FY28 from the introduction of merchant discount rate (MDR) on eligible UPI transactions. Incremental adjusted EBITDA of Rs 140 crore and Rs 443 crore, respectively, is estimated after factoring in the loss of UPI incentives, according to JM Financial Research.The estimates follow NPCI's introduction of an MDR of up to 0.4% on eligible person-to-merchant (P2M) UPI transactions above Rs 2,000 from October 15, 2026. JM Financial said the notified rate is higher than its earlier assumption of 0.25%, but broader exemptions and concessional rates led it to reduce its eligible GMV assumption to 20% from 30%. The brokerage retained its assumption that Paytm would receive 20% of the MDR pool, implying an effective pass-through of about 8 basis points. NPCI has not yet specified how the MDR pool will be distributed across participants in the UPI ecosystem. Advt The new regimeTransactions up to Rs 2,000, which account for more than 95% of P2M volume, will remain at zero MDR, while P2P transfers will also remain free. Small merchants classified as P2PM, receiving up to Rs 1 lakh a month through UPI QR, will remain exempt irrespective of individual transaction size. Such merchants will move to the P2M category only after exceeding the threshold for three consecutive months.The 0.4% MDR will be capped at Rs 300 for transactions of Rs 75,000 and above. Several categories will have concessional rates, including a flat Rs 5 for railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and utility bill collections, while capital market transactions such as mutual funds and securities will attract 0.02%, capped at Rs 300.Consumers will not be charged for UPI payments under the new framework. Paytm has said the MDR will generate additional revenue from its merchant business for transactions that were previously free, while adding that it will make further disclosures once the framework becomes effective and its impact is ascertained.The brokerage estimated that Paytm's adjusted EBITDA could be 9.8% higher in FY27 and 19% higher in FY28 than its estimates without MDR. It expects adjusted EBITDA margins to rise to 14.6% and 20.6%, respectively, under its base case.It said the key uncertainty remains the eventual distribution of the MDR pool among participants in the UPI ecosystem. It has assumed a 20% share for Paytm in its base case. Its sensitivity analysis shows the FY28 adjusted EBITDA uplift ranging from 2.5% with a 10% eligible GMV and 3 basis points of pass-through to 52.1% with a 30% eligible GMV and 14 basis points of pass-through. Advt The brokerage retained its BUY rating on One 97 Communications and raised its target price to Rs 2,150 from Rs 1,950, based on a valuation of 40 times September 2028 estimated EV/EBITDA.
