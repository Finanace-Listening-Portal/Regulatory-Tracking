ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 25, 2026 at 09:42 PM IST var news_msid = 136167210; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) has appointed Sonija M.S. as its Chief Financial Officer (CFO), effective November 26, 2026, while Meenakshi Malhotra has been named the insurer's Appointed Actuary, effective January 1, 2027, subject to regulatory approval.The appointments were approved by LIC's board at a meeting held on November 25, following recommendations from the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The appointments have been disclosed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.Sonija, who was previously Chief (In-charge) of LIC's Finance and Accounts Department at the Central Office, will take charge as CFO from November 26. She joined LIC in 1993 as a Direct Recruit Officer and is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). Advt Over her three-decade-long career at LIC, Sonija has held several responsibilities across Finance and Accounts, Debt and Project Portfolio, and stressed assets within the investment departments. She has also worked across Claims, Office Services and Personnel departments. Additionally, she served as Secretary at the Insurance Ombudsman office, handling the resolution of complaints and disputes relating to insurance claim settlements.Meanwhile, Meenakshi Malhotra, currently serving as Chief (Actuarial/Valuation Actuary), has been appointed as Appointed Actuary with effect from January 1, 2027, or the date of approval by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), whichever is later.Malhotra joined LIC in 1993 in the New Business and Actuarial Department at its Northern Zonal Office. She subsequently joined the Actuarial Core Group at the Central Office in 2005 and qualified as a Fellow Member of the Institute of Actuaries in 2008. She is also a Fellow Actuary of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries, UK.She has over three decades of experience spanning product pricing, actuarial valuation, reinsurance and underwriting. Malhotra was involved in the design and pricing of LIC products from 2005 and took over as the insurer's Designated Actuary in the role of Product Actuary in January 2017. She was also involved in actuarial work related to LIC's initial public offering in May 2022. Advt In May 2022, she took over as Designated Actuary in the role of Valuation Actuary and has been serving as Chief (Actuarial/Valuation Actuary) since April 2026.Both appointments will continue until the respective officials' superannuation or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
