Interest received during the investment period must be added separately to calculate the overall return. How is the redemption price calculated? The RBI calculates the premature redemption price using the simple average of the closing prices of 999-purity gold published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA) for the three preceding working days. For today's redemption, the calculation is based on gold prices recorded on October 14, October 15 and October 16, 2026. Since the redemption value is linked to prevailing gold prices, returns differ across SGB tranches depending on their original issue prices and redemption dates. What interest do SGB investors earn?SGBs carry a fixed interest rate of 2.5 per cent per annum on the initial investment amount. Interest is paid separately from the redemption proceeds, generally in half-yearly instalments. The interest is calculated on the original investment, while the redemption value changes with gold prices. Investors should account for both components when assessing their total returns.Can investors redeem SGBs before maturity?SGBs have an eight-year tenure, but investors can seek premature redemption through the RBI after completing five years from the issue date, on an eligible interest payment date. The bonds can also be sold on stock exchanges, subject to liquidity and the prevailing market price. An exchange sale may fetch a different amount from the official RBI redemption value. Investors should check the applicable redemption process and tax treatment before exiting.
