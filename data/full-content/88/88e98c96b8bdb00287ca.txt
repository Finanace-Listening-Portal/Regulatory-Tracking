The appointment was approved by the bank's board at a meeting held on November 18, following the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI's) approval conveyed on November 16, 2026. Hebbar has been appointed as an additional director and whole-time director in the category of MD & CEO, with his tenure commencing from the date he assumes charge.
