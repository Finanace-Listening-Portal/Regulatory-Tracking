The broader small-caps and mid-caps gained about 1% each.The 50-stock Nifty index and the Sensex are up 0.2% each so far this week, consolidating after a nearly 15% surge over the last four weeks that saw Indian benchmarks outperforming Asian and emerging market peers. Advt The rally in November was driven by record monthly foreign inflows, worth $15.5 billion, aided by a global AI trade reversal and robust earnings.Brent crude futures hovered around $96.5 per barrel, rising 4.2% over the week as ongoing attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz and Saudi energy infrastructure heightened concerns over oil exports from the conflict-hit region."The rise in Brent crude is negative news in the context of rising inflation in India, while the US tariff proposal is another threat," said VK Vijayakumar, chief investment strategist at Geojit Investments.After the recent rally, "a consolidation phase appears likely in the near term," Vijayakumar said.IT index fell 1.5% after rising 8% in the last four sessions.Among stocks, Waaree Renewable Technologies surged 10% after receiving a letter of award for a 120 MWp turnkey EPC project.Freight wagon manufacturer Texmaco Rail gained 2.6% on winning an order worth $6.02 million from Utkal Alumina International for supply of 108 railway wagons.Engineering, procurement and construction firm EMS added 2% on securing a $2.8 million order from the National Highways Authority of India.Coal India unit Mahanadi Coalfields fell 1.5% in its trading debutk.Jet Freight Logistics fell 2% after the logistics firm said India's markets regulator summoned its chairman and managing director as part of an ongoing investigation into the company.
