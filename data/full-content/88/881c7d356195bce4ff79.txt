This latest investment elevates AAHL’s total equity raise to approximately USD 1.35 billion, underscoring the company’s strategic expansion in India’s aviation sector. Previously, AAHL secured USD 1 billion in primary equity capital from a consortium of prominent investors, including Alpha Wave Global, Premji Invest, Temasek, and funds managed by BlackRock. This earlier investment marked one of the largest financial commitments in India’s airport infrastructure sector. The new agreement with 360 ONE Asset follows the same terms and valuation as the initial investors, maintaining a pre-money equity valuation of approximately USD 18 billion. Upon completion, the collective investor group will hold approximately 7.01% of AAHL, subject to customary approvals. Jeet Adani, Non-Executive Director of Adani Airport Holdings Limited, expressed confidence in the partnership, highlighting its significance in advancing the Adani Airports platform. He emphasised the commitment to transforming airports into vibrant cities that integrate aviation, commerce, leisure, and logistics, thereby fostering sustained national growth. The infusion of capital from 360 ONE Asset, alongside existing investors, reflects a strong institutional endorsement of AAHL’s operational capabilities and growth potential. The funds raised are earmarked for strategic initiatives aimed at doubling AAHL’s historical passenger handling capacity from 96 million to 200 million annually. The company has embarked on a comprehensive modernisation and upgrade programme across its airport network, focusing on developing Adani Airport Cities. This initiative aims to enhance infrastructure, improve passenger services, and expand non-aeronautical businesses, ultimately strengthening the group’s integrated airport ecosystem. Analysts view this development as a positive indicator of investor confidence in India’s aviation sector, particularly in light of the country’s growing passenger traffic and infrastructure needs. The strategic investments by both domestic and international entities signal a robust outlook for AAHL’s continued expansion and innovation within the industry.
