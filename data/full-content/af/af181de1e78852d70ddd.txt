The company, promoted by Reliance Industries, served 533.3 million customers as of June 30, 2026, including 503.6 million mobile broadband customers, while its network carried about 60% of India's wireless data traffic in Fiscal 2026.The company combines mobile, fixed broadband and digital services on a unified platform and is transitioning towards an AI native digital services model built on a technology stack developed primarily in house. Its standalone revenue, broadly representing its digital services layer, rose from Rs75,706 million in Fiscal 2024 to Rs161,667 million in Fiscal 2026, while overall revenue from operations increased at a 15.79% CAGR over the same period, according to a report by Motilal Oswal Financial Services.Massive customer base Advt Jio Platforms served 533.3 million customers as of June 30, 2026, including 503.6 million mobile broadband users. It added about 27 million net active mobility customers in Fiscal 2026, roughly three times the additions of the second placed operator, according to the report.Its wireless broadband network covers more than 99% of India's population and is supported by 360,537 network towers and spectrum holdings of 26,800.8 MHz.Data consumption continues to riseJio's network carried about 60% of India's wireless data traffic in Fiscal 2026. Total data traffic increased from 148.5 billion GB in Fiscal 2024 to 241.4 billion GB in Fiscal 2026. Monthly data consumption per customer also increased from 28.7 GB to 42.3 GB over the same period, providing a larger base for the company's connectivity and digital services.Proprietary technology is central to the strategyJio has built much of its network and services stack in house, spanning radio access, cloud native core technology, operating systems and digital applications. The company says this reduces dependence on third party vendors, creates cost advantages and shortens innovation cycles. As of June 30, 2026, it had filed 7,220 patent applications, including 2,655 in India and 4,565 overseas, covering areas such as 4G, 5G, 6G, cloud native core architectures and AI driven network automation. It employed 12,477 people in digital products and technology development, representing about 45% of its full time workforce. Advt AI is being built into the networkJio's proprietary AI engine, JioBrain, is embedded across network operations for capacity allocation, fault prediction and real time optimisation. The company is transitioning towards an AI native digital services model built on its own technology stack. Its distributed, AI optimised architecture is also identified as a source of network cost advantage.A large migration opportunity remainsAbout 261 million users remained on 2G networks as of June 30, 2026. Jio is targeting this market through its Rs799 JioBharat phone, affordable plans and digital services. The device strategy is supported by in house engineering and JioBharat OS, which runs on low cost chipsets with a minimal memory footprint. Advt Home broadband is an expanding businessJioFiber and JioAirFiber had a combined 28.6 million customers as of June 30, 2026. JioAirFiber, launched in September 2023, had reached 14 million customers, giving it India's largest fixed wireless access customer base, according to the report. Fixed broadband customers grew 55.19% between Fiscal 2025 and Fiscal 2026, during which Jio captured 61.73% of net market additions. The connected home platform also offers entertainment, cloud computing through JioPC, cloud gaming through JioGames and smart home solutions. Its JioFiber and JioAirFiber packages include more than 1,000 digital TV channels and 15 OTT services. Advt Digital services extend beyond connectivityThe MyJio app had 227.6 million average monthly active users in the three months ended June 30, 2026, and 215.9 million in Fiscal 2026.The app serves as a discovery platform for shopping, financial services, media and entertainment. It also generated around 20 million JioHome leads in Fiscal 2026.Jio has a physical distribution network of 1,059 Jio Centres and 6,322 Jio Points. On the enterprise side, it is targeting more than 79 million micro, small and medium enterprises with managed services spanning infrastructure, connectivity, cloud, CPaaS and cybersecurity.Revenue and EBITDA are growingRevenue from operations increased at a 15.79% CAGR from Rs1,095,581 million in Fiscal 2024 to Rs1,468,853 million in Fiscal 2026. EBITDA grew at a 17.79% CAGR from Rs549,587 million to Rs762,554 million over the same period. EBITDA margin widened from 50.16% to 51.91%, reaching 53.26% in the three months ended June 30, 2026. Profit after tax increased from Rs214,232 million in Fiscal 2024 to Rs300,491 million in Fiscal 2026. PAT margin stood at 20.46% in Fiscal 2026, while diluted earnings per share rose from Rs23.93 to Rs33.59.Cash generation has improved sharplyWith the major phase of the 5G build largely complete, EBITDA less cash capex increased from Rs14,491 million in Fiscal 2024 to Rs420,711 million in Fiscal 2026. Operating cash flow reached Rs775,563 million in Fiscal 2026. Net leverage declined from 0.88x in Fiscal 2024 to 0.36x in Fiscal 2026. Return on average net worth stood at 9.42% in Fiscal 2026. Return on average capital employed, however, eased from 12.83% to 10.76% as the asset base expanded.ARPU has increased and churn has declinedExit quarter average revenue per user rose from Rs181.7 in Fiscal 2024 to Rs214.0 in Fiscal 2026. Monthly churn declined to 1.67%. The reported figures show higher revenue per user alongside rising data consumption, broadband expansion and growth in digital services. The company is also generating substantially more cash after capital expenditure than it did in Fiscal 2024, following the completion of much of its 5G network build out.
