Articles 2 min read Bank credit to industry rises 18% in August, medium-industry lending jumps 32%: RBI Bank credit to industry stood at Rs 47.95 lakh crore as of August 31, 2026, with lending to medium industries at Rs 4.89 lakh crore, micro and small industries at Rs 11.15 lakh crore and large industries at Rs 31.91 lakh crore. On a year-on-year basis, credit to medium industries grew 31.8%, followed by micro and small industries at 23%, while large-industry credit rose 14.9%. Credit to micro and small industries increased 23 per cent to Rs 11.15 lakh crore from Rs 9.07 lakh crore during the same period.In comparison, bank credit to large industries rose 14.9 per cent year-on-year to Rs 31.91 lakh crore from Rs 27.78 lakh crore. Advt On a financial-year basis, credit to industry increased 4.6 per cent between March 31 and August 31. Credit to medium industries rose 10.4 per cent during the period, while lending to micro and small industries increased 5.3 per cent. Large-industry credit grew 3.5 per cent.Overall non-food credit grew 18.8 per cent year-on-year as of August 31, while total bank credit, including food credit, increased 19.1 per cent.Services remained the fastest-growing major segment, with bank credit rising 24.3 per cent year-on-year to Rs 62.98 lakh crore. Credit to agriculture and allied activities rose 17.2 per cent to Rs 27.25 lakh crore, while personal loan growth stood at 16.9 per cent, with outstanding credit at Rs 72.87 lakh crore.The data showed that credit to micro and small enterprises under the priority sector rose 25.9 per cent year-on-year to Rs 31.62 lakh crore, while credit to medium enterprises under the priority sector increased 31 per cent to Rs 7.92 lakh crore.The RBI data also showed strong growth in bank credit to some services segments. Credit to computer software rose 41.8 per cent year-on-year, while lending to aviation increased 32.1 per cent and tourism, hotels and restaurants grew 28.3 per cent.Credit to NBFCs rose 37.5 per cent year-on-year to Rs 21.64 lakh crore, while credit to commercial real estate increased 22.5 per cent to Rs 6.75 lakh crore. Advt Sectoral non-food credit data are based on the sector-wise and industry-wise bank credit return covering select banks accounting for about 95 per cent of total non-food credit extended by scheduled commercial banks.
