Articles 2 min read Foreign subsidiaries in India export Rs 23.76 lakh crore in FY26; services contribute 74%: RBI Census Exports reported by foreign subsidiary companies operating in India rose 14.3% year-on-year to Rs 23.76 lakh crore in FY26, with services accounting for nearly three-fourths of the total. Telecommunications, computing and information services alone contributed more than half of the exports. Of the 27,373 foreign subsidiaries covered in the census, 17,443 reported exports during FY26.Services accounted for Rs 17.63 lakh crore of the total exports, compared with Rs 15.30 lakh crore in the previous financial year. The segment contributed 74.2% of the total exports reported by foreign subsidiaries. Advt Within services, telecommunications, computer programming, consultancy, computing infrastructure and other information service activities accounted for the largest share. Exports from this segment increased to Rs 12.26 lakh crore in FY26 from Rs 10.62 lakh crore in FY25, accounting for 51.6% of the total exports reported by foreign subsidiaries.Other service activities contributed Rs 4.45 lakh crore, while wholesale and retail trade accounted for Rs 50,011 crore. Exports from financial and insurance activities stood at Rs 12,181 crore.Manufacturing exports rise to Rs 6.01 lakh croreManufacturing exports by foreign subsidiaries increased to Rs 6.01 lakh crore in FY26 from Rs 5.38 lakh crore in the previous year, accounting for 25.3% of their total exports.Within manufacturing, exports of motor vehicles, trailers and semi-trailers rose to Rs 1 lakh crore from Rs 86,594 crore in FY25. Exports of computer, electronic and optical products stood at Rs 77,816 crore, while machinery and equipment exports were Rs 70,854 crore.Pharmaceutical exports increased to Rs 44,839 crore from Rs 39,363 crore in the previous financial year.The RBI data also showed that exports accounted for 36.3% of the total sales reported by foreign subsidiaries in India in FY26. Their aggregate sales stood at Rs 65.49 lakh crore during the year, of which services contributed 51.1% and manufacturing 47%. Advt The census captures the sales, purchases, exports and imports of foreign subsidiary companies operating in India, alongside the foreign assets and liabilities of Indian entities with direct investment links.
