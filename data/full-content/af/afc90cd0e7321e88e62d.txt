The notification substitutes Table-1, Table-2 and Table-3 of the principal notification with revised tariff values for specified imported goods. Table-1 prescribes tariff values for crude palm oil, RBD palm oil, other palm oil, crude palmolein, RBD palmolein, other palmolein, crude soya bean oil and brass scrap. Table-2 prescribes tariff values for specified gold and silver products, including goods covered by entries at serial numbers 194 and 195 of Notification No. 45/2025-Customs dated 24 October 2025, silver in specified forms and gold bars, gold coins and gold findings. The prescribed tariff value for gold covered by the relevant entries is US $1,373 per 10 grams, while the tariff value for silver is US $2,028 per kilogram. Table-3 prescribes a tariff value of US $11,574 per metric tonne for areca nuts, with the notification stating that there is no change. The notification also contains an explanation concerning the scope of silver in the relevant entry and defines “gold findings” for the purposes of the specified gold entry. The revised tariff values take effect from 16 September 2026. The notification is issued under F. No. 467/01/2026-Cus.V and is signed by Indrajit Panda, Under Secretary. The principal notification was Notification No. 36/2001-Customs (N.T.), and the supplied text records Notification No. 72/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 31 August 2026 as the immediately preceding amendment.
