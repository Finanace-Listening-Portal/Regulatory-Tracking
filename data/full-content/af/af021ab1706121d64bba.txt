Articles 2 min read US Treasury gains lift India bonds ahead of RBI meet On Wednesday, Indian government bonds rallied, influenced by a decrease in US Treasury yields. The benchmark 10-year bond yield fell to 7.1641%, marking a two-week low. The Indian rupee strengthened to its highest level for the fiscal year against the dollar. Overseas investors returned as net buyers of Indian debt, purchasing over 93 billion rupees. The Reserve Bank of India's upcoming decision will significantly affect market movements and trends. India's debt market was shut on Tuesday for a local holiday.The US 10-year Treasury yield, a benchmark for global borrowing costs, dropped 5 basis points in Asian trade to 4.8541%. Treasuries have drawn safe-haven flows as the AI-led equity rally loses momentum. Global funds have also rotated out of AI-linked stocks into markets that lagged during the technology rally, including India. Advt The shift lifted the Indian rupee to 92.23 per dollar on Wednesday, its strongest level so far this financial year, while Indian equities have nearly erased their 2026 losses.Overseas investors also became net buyers of Indian debt in November, after three months of selling, purchasing nearly 93 billion rupees ($1.01 billion) of index-eligible bonds so far this month.The RBI decision on December 4 will now be pivotal for any further rally, traders said. The majority of economists polled in a Reuters survey expect a 25-basis-point increase in the repo rate, although some see a small chance of a larger move."Market is sitting extremely light," said Kruti Chheta, Mumbai-based fund manager at Mirae Asset Mutual Fund, adding that traders may build up their positions if oil prices fall below $90 and the rally in US Treasuries continues.Brent crude was last at $90.99 per barrel, down 0.5% in Asian trade.RATESThe overnight index swap curve inverted further as declining US Treasury yields pulled longer-dated rates down faster than short-end rates.The 1-year rate was down 2 bps at 6.45%, while the 2-year rate eased 5.5 bps to 6.38% and the liquid 5-year rate dropped 6.75 bps to 6.3875%.
