Articles 1 min read Digital payment platform for e-challans to go live on Nov 16 Faryal Rumi Published On Nov 5, 2026 at 06:40 PM IST var news_msid = 135574281; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Patna: Vehicle owners in Bihar will soon be able to pay traffic challans through Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other digital payment applications, without visiting govt offices or using a specific payment portal. The state transport department will launch the Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) platform on Nov 16 to simplify the process.Testing of the platform has been completed, along with its integration with the department’s digital payment system through the National Informatics Centre (NIC).A demonstration was held at the transport department office on Thursday, where officials reviewed preparations for the launch. State Bank of India (SBI) representatives confirmed successful testing and said the platform was expected to go live on Nov 16. Advt Once operational, the system will allow vehicle owners to clear pending traffic challans through their preferred digital payment applications, including Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm, as well as internet banking. The move is expected to make payments quicker, easier and more transparent.Transport secretary Raj Kumar said the state govt’s priority was to make transport services technology-driven, transparent and accessible. He said BBPS would spare vehicle owners the trouble of visiting offices, allowing them to pay challans from home.State transport commissioner Arif Ahsan said the platform would initially be used for traffic challan payments. Other payment-related services of the department would also be integrated in the coming days, enabling citizens to access multiple online payment facilities through a single system.
