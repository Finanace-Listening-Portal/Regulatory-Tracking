The Reserve Bank of India’s supervisory officers have reviewed early indicators of stress, asset quality and loan amounts against pledged gold, the people said, asking not to be identified as they are not authorised to speak to the media. The stress in gold credit portfolios is expected to persist over the next few quarters, the people said.
