The new branch is located in Santacruz, Mumbai, while the company has also opened another outlet in Andheri. The non-banking financial company (NBFC) had announced plans in August 2026 to open 200-300 dedicated gold loan branches by March 2027 across markets with high growth potential. The gold loan business had assets under management (AUM) of ₹200 crore as of September 30, 2026. The company is looking to tap the organised gold lending market through a branch-led distribution model supported by digital capabilities. “The opening of our 100th Gold Loan branch is a special milestone for us. It reflects the strength of our execution capabilities and the confidence that customers place in Aditya Birla Capital,” said Vishakha Mulye, managing director and chief executive officer of ABCL. The expansion comes amid continued growth in the company's NBFC business.
