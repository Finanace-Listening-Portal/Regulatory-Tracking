The notification further amends Notification No. 08/2026-Central Excise dated 26 March 2026, by substituting the entry in column (4) against serial number 1 of its Table. The amendment has been issued under Section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944, read with Section 147 of the Finance Act, 2002. The revised rate applies from the date of publication in the Official Gazette. The principal notification, published as G.S.R. 207(E) dated 26 March 2026, was last amended by Notification No. 56/2026-Central Excise dated 31 October 2026, published as G.S.R. 964(E). The latest notification changes the specified SAED rate without modifying the remaining provisions of the principal notification. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) Notification No. 58/2026-Central Excise | Dated: 16th November, 2026 G.S.R. 994(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944 (1 of 1944) read with Section 147 of Finance Act, 2002 (20 of 2002), the Central Government, being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), No. 08/2026-Central Excise, dated the 26th March, 2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 207 (E), dated the 26th March, 2026, namely:- In the said notification, in the Table, against serial number 1, in column (4), for the entry, the entry “Rs. 12.5 per litre” shall be substituted. 2. This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette. [F. No. 190349/13/2026-TRU] DHEERAJ SHARMA, Under Secy. Note.- The principal notification No. 08/2026-Central Excise, dated the 26th March, 2026 was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 207 (E), dated the 26th March, 2026 and last amended by notification No. 56/2026-Central Excise dated 31st October, 2026 published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 964(E), dated 31st October, 2026.
