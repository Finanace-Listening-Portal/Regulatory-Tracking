Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Visakhapatnam, November 12, 2026: GAIL (India) Limited and Krishak Bharati Cooperative Limited (KRIBHCO) have entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Andhra Pradesh Economic Development Board (APEDB) to explore the establishment of a gas-based urea plant in Andhra Pradesh. This development was announced at the 31st CII Partnership Summit 2026, marking a significant step towards enhancing India’s self-sufficiency in fertilizer production. The MoU was signed by Shri Sagili Shan Mohan, CEO of APEDB, Shri Sanjay Agarwal, Executive Director of Business Development, Start-up & R&D at GAIL, and Shri C. S. Azad, Executive Director (Technical) at KRIBHCO. The signing ceremony was attended by prominent figures, including Shri N. Chandra Babu Naidu, the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh, along with senior officials from GAIL and KRIBHCO. The proposed urea production facility is planned to have a capacity of 1.27 million metric tons per annum (MMTPA) and will be located in Mulapeta, Srikakulam district of Andhra Pradesh. The plant is strategically positioned along GAIL’s Srikakulam Angul Natural Gas Pipeline (SAPL), leveraging the region’s existing infrastructure to facilitate efficient production and distribution. Analysts view this collaboration as a strategic move to bolster the agricultural sector in India by ensuring a steady supply of domestically produced fertilizers. The initiative aligns with the government’s vision of reducing dependency on imports and promoting sustainable agricultural practices. The partnership between GAIL, KRIBHCO, and APEDB is expected to create significant economic opportunities in the region, contributing to the overall development of Andhra Pradesh.
