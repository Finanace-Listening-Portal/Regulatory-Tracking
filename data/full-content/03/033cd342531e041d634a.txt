Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); SICOM Investments & Finance Limited (NCLT Mumbai) Summary: The National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, approved the application filed by SICOM Investments & Finance Limited under Section 55(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, read with Rule 9 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, seeking issuance of fresh cumulative redeemable preference shares in lieu of existing unredeemed preference shares. The petitioner had issued 25 lakh 10% cumulative redeemable preference shares of ₹100 each, aggregating to ₹25 crore, to its holding company, SICOM Limited, on 26 December 2013. These preference shares were scheduled for redemption in December 2017. However, owing to inadequate financial resources and financial difficulties, the company could neither redeem the preference shares nor pay the dividend outstanding from FY 2015-16. The petitioner had incurred substantial financial losses continuously from FY 2016-17 to FY 2021-22. Although it reported a profit of approximately ₹132.58 crore during FY 2022-23, the petitioner explained that the profit arose from the writeback of provisions for bad and doubtful debts, which was a non-cash item and did not generate funds for redemption of preference shares. The Board of Directors, at its meeting held on 21 March 2023, resolved to obtain consent from the holding company for issuance of fresh preference shares under Section 55(3). SICOM Limited, being the sole preference shareholder, granted its consent at its Board meeting held on 22 June 2023. SICOM Limited also waived unpaid accrued dividends amounting to ₹21.25 crore through its letter dated 27 September 2023 and agreed to reduce the coupon rate from 10% to 0.01% in respect of the fresh preference shares. The petitioner’s Board of Directors approved the issuance of 25 lakh fresh 0.01% cumulative redeemable preference shares of ₹100 each, aggregating to ₹25 crore, at its meeting held on 25 August 2023. The equity shareholders subsequently approved the proposal through a special resolution passed at the extraordinary general meeting held on 6 October 2023. After examining the petition and supporting documents, the Tribunal observed that the petitioner had duly passed the shareholders’ resolution and filed Form MGT-14 with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai. Having found compliance with Section 55(3), NCLT allowed the petition and authorised issuance and allotment of fresh preference shares aggregating to ₹25 crore. The existing 10% cumulative redeemable preference shares would be deemed redeemed upon issuance and allotment of the fresh shares. The Tribunal further directed the petitioner to file e-Form INC-28 with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai, enclosing a certified copy of the order within 30 days. FULL TEXT OF THE NCLAT JUDGMENT/ORDER For the Petitioner(s): Mr. Ashish O. Lalpuria, a/w. CS Kamal Lahoty, Practising Company Secretary 1. The present Petition is filed by SICOM INVESTMENTS & FINANCE LIMITED, the Petitioner under Section 55(3) of the Companies Act, 2013 for seeking approval for issuance of fresh Redeemable Preference Shares of the Company in lieu of existing Unredeemed Preference Shares to the holder of the Unredeemed Preference Shares. 2. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that the Share Capital of the Petitioner as on the 31st day of March, 2023 is as under: Particulars Amount (INR) AUTHORISED SHARE CAPITAL 2,50,00,000 equity shares of INR 10/- each. 25,00,00,000 25,00,000 Preference shares of INR 100/- each. 25,00,00,000 Total 50,00,00,000 ISSUED, SUBSCRIBED AND PAID UP SHARE CAPITAL 1,48,22,128 equity shares of INR 10/- each. 14,82,21,280 25,00,000 Preference shares of INR 100/- each. 25,00,00,000 Total 39,82,21,280 3. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that Article 11 of the Articles of Association of the Company authorises it to issue preference shares. 4. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that the Petitioner had issued 25,00,000, 10% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs. 100/- each aggregating to Rs. 25,00,00,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Crores Only) on 26th December, 2013 to its Holding Company, SICOM Ltd, which were scheduled to be redeemed in December, 2017 i.e., at the end of four years. 5. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that due to inadequate resources and financial difficulties, the Petitioner could not redeem the said existing preference shares nor could pay the dividend amount due from FY 2015-16 and the same remains outstanding as on date. A brief snap shot of the profit and loss account for the FY 2016-17 to FY 2022-23 is provided below: Financial Year Total revenue (in Rs.) Profit / (Loss) before Tax (in Rs.) Profit / (Loss) after Tax (in Rs.) 2016-17 22,42,006/- (1,25,59,81,415/-) (1,25,59,81,415/-) 2017-18 1,92,78,441/- (1,12,89,66,830/-) (1,12,89,66,830/-) 2018-19 0/- (24,68,12,975/-) (24,68,12,975/-) 2019-20 1,34,60,477/- (23,92,47,103/-) (23,92,47,103/-) 2020-21 1,62,491/- (23,66,30,975/-) (23,66,30,975/-) 2021-22 6,42,56,459/- (16,84,09,950/-) (16,84,09,950/-) 2022-23 2,04,02,33,413/- 1,32,57,74,324/- 1,32,57,74,324/- As is evident from the perusal of the above table, the Petitioner Company has been incurring losses continuously and therefore it did not have sufficient funds to redeem the existing preference shares. Further, the profits in FY 2022-23 were on account of writeback of provisions on bad & doubtful debts which is a non-cash item and does not contribute to any cash or cash equivalent items. 6. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that since the existing preference shares could not be redeemed in December, 2017, the default further continued due to financial constraints and also due to various administrative issues. In view of this, the Board of Directors of the Company at their meeting held on 21st March, 2023 resolved to obtain consent from SICOM Limited, its Holding Company, to issue further new Preference Shares in lieu of unredeemed Preference shares as per the provisions of Section 55(3) of the Companies Act, 2013. 7. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that SICOM Limited, the only existing preference shareholder, at their Board Meeting held on 22nd June, 2023 issued its consent to the Petitioner Company to approach the Hon’ble Tribunal for redemption of the existing preference shares and issue of new preference shares in lieu of existing preference shares and also to vary the terms of preference shares. 8. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that SICOM Limited has waived off the unpaid accrued dividend of Rs. 21.25 crores vide its letter dated 27th September, 2023 to the existing preference shareholder. SICOM Limited has also confirmed the reduction in coupon rate from 10% to 0.01% w.r.t. the fresh issue of Preference Shares in lieu of the unredeemed preference shares. 9. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that upon receipt of the consent from the only existing preference shareholder, the Board of Directors of the Petitioner Company at their meeting held on 25th August, 2023 approved the issue of further 25,00,000, 0.01% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each (“CRPS”) aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores to SICOM Limited in lieu of the existing unredeemed 25,00,000, 10% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores held by SICOM Limited. 10. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that the Equity Shareholders of the Petitioner Company through special resolution at their Extra Ordinary General Meeting held on 06th October, 2023 also approved the issue of further 25,00,000, 0.01% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each (“CRPS”) aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores to SICOM Limited in lieu of the existing unredeemed 25,00,000, 10% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores held by SICOM Limited. 11. The Authorised Representative of the Petitioner submits that in view of the facts and circumstances as narrated, the Petitioner Company is seeking approval of this Tribunal for issue of new Redeemable Preference shares in lieu of existing unredeemed Preference Shares as laid down under Section 55(3) of the Companies Act, 2013. ORDER 12. We have perused the Petition and the documents as filed by the Petitioner Company and heard the submission of the authorized representative of the Petitioner. 13. The Petitioner has duly passed the shareholders resolution for issue of fresh preference shares in lieu of the existing Preference Shares and have also filed relevant Form MGT-14 with the Registrar of Companies, Mumbai. 14. Since the compliance of Section 55(3) of the Companies Act, 2023 has been made, the Petition is allowed with the following directions: a) The Petitioner is allowed to issue and allot fresh 25,00,000, 0.01% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each (“CRPS”) aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores to SICOM Limited in lieu of the exiting unredeemed 25,00,000, 10% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs.100/- each aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores held by SICOM Limited. b) The existing 10% Cumulative Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs. 100/- each aggregating to Rs. 25.00 crores held by SICOM Limited shall be deemed to be redeemed upon issue and allotment of the aforesaid fresh CRPS. c) The Petitioner to file e-form INC 28 with the Registrar of companies, Mumbai enclosing a certified copy of the order within 30 days from the date of this Order. 15. Ordered accordingly. File be consigned to record.
