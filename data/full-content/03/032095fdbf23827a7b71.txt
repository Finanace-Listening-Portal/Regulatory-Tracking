Assets under management grew 25% to Rs 1.58 lakh crore, with asset quality remaining largely stable. Net total income increased 15% to Rs 1,254 crore from Rs 1,090 crore a year earlier, according to the exchange filing submitted by the company.The company's operating expenses to net total income ratio improved to 19.1% in Q2FY27 from 19.7% in the year-ago quarter. Loan losses and provisions declined to Rs 35 crore from Rs 43 crore in Q2FY26. Gross non-performing assets (GNPA) stood at 0.29% as of September 30, 2026, compared with 0.26% a year earlier. Net non-performing assets (NNPA) remained unchanged at 0.12%.The provisioning coverage ratio on Stage 3 assets was reported at 60%, while the company's capital adequacy ratio, including Tier-II capital, stood at 21.24% as of September 30, 2026.The housing finance company holds an AAA/Stable rating for its long-term debt programme from CRISIL and India Ratings. Its short-term debt programme carries an A1+ rating from both agencies.Bajaj Housing Finance share priceShares of Bajaj Housing Finance ended Friday's trading session at Rs 83.36 on the NSE, down 0.50% from the previous close of Rs 83.78, according to exchange data.The stock has declined 13.22% in 2026 so far. It has traded in a 52-week range of Rs 72.65 to Rs 112.40 on the NSE.At the latest closing price, the company's market capitalisation stood at approximately Rs 69,458 crore on the NSE. Its shares have been listed on the NSE and BSE since September 2024.
