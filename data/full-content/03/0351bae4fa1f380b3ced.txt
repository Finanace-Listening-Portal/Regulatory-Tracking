Articles 1 min read UBS acquires 4.8% stake in 360 ONE WAM through warrant exercise UBS AG has exercised all warrants allotted by 360 ONE WAM, resulting in the allotment of 20.5 million equity shares representing approximately 4.8% stake in the wealth and asset management firm. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Oct 22, 2026 at 01:19 PM IST var news_msid = 135111801; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Union Bank of Switzerland Aktiengesellschaf, on Thursday announced the acquisition of an approximately 4.8% equity stake in 360 ONE WAM through the exercise of all warrants allotted to it.The exercise resulted in the allotment of 20,502,939 equity shares in 360 ONE WAM, one of India's largest independent wealth and asset management firms.UBS AG had subscribed to the warrants on a preferential basis under an agreement with 360 ONE. The warrants were allotted on July 21, 2025, with UBS paying 25% of the exercise price at the time of allotment.The bank has now exercised all the warrants and paid the remaining 75% of the exercise price. Advt Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is a global wealth manager and the country’s leading universal bank, offering diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. As of the second quarter of 2026, it managed $7.3 trillion in invested assets and operated in more than 50 markets worldwide. UBS Group shares are listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE).
