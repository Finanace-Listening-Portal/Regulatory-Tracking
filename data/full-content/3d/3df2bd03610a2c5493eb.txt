Issued under Section 43(1), in consultation with the Chief Justice of the High Court of Karnataka, the amendment inserts additional entries in columns (3) and (4) against serial number 13 of Notification S.O. 372(E), dated 5 February 2016. The designated courts comprise the XLIX Additional City Civil and Sessions Judge, Bengaluru; the Principal District and Sessions Judge and III Additional District and Sessions Judge, Dharwad; the Principal District and Sessions Judge and I Additional District and Sessions Judge, Kalaburagi; and the Principal District and Sessions Judge and VI Additional District and Sessions Judge, Mangaluru. The Bengaluru court is assigned twelve districts, including Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural, Mysuru and Davanagere. The Principal District and Sessions Judge, Dharwad, is assigned nine districts, while the III Additional District and Sessions Judge, Dharwad, covers those districts together with Davanagere. Both Kalaburagi courts cover Kalaburagi, Bidar, Vijayapura, Yadgir and Raichur. Both Mangaluru courts cover Dakshina Kannada, Udupi, Shivamogga, Chikkamagaluru and Kodagu-Madikeri. These entries are added after the existing entries. The notification’s concluding note identifies S.O. 5082(E), dated 16 September 2026, as the preceding amendment to the principal notification.
