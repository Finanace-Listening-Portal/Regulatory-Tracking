Particulars of Remittance are to be provided to SEBI by email at od-ddhs@sebi.gov.in, immediately after the remittance is made, in the following format: Name of the Remitter Date of Remittance Amount Remitted Name of the Origin Bank UTR/ Transaction Ref No. Purpose for which remittance is made e.
