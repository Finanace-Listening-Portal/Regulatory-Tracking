Articles 2 min read Books, coffee and banking: Anup Bagchi the ICICI lifer set to lead HDFC Bank Anup Bagchi, who has spent 34 years at ICICI Group, will join HDFC Bank as managing director. He has held various leadership roles, overseeing retail and wholesale banking functions. Bagchi's appointment comes after the RBI approved it, succeeding Sashidhar Jagdishan. Known for his informal manner, he led the bank through challenging times like demonetisation. He has run its securities arm, led retail and wholesale banking, and headed its life insurer. On October 27, he will leave that fold for the first time to occupy the corner office at HDFC Bank, India’s largest private lender.The RBI has approved his appointment as managing director and chief executive for three years. He will succeed Sashidhar Jagdishan, whose term ends on October 26. Bagchi has switched businesses several times, but always within ICICI. The ICICI veteran set to take charge at India's largest private lender“He would call and ask, ‘Are you busy?’” recalled a banking official who worked with him. Say no and he might find you work; say yes and he could tease you about being too busy for a director. A good idea could elicit another joke: “You’re talking sense. Now you’ll have to buy me a coffee.”That ease with colleagues stands out to people who have worked with him. “In my experience, people with very high IQ often have low EQ. He is an exception,” said one official, who asked not to be identified.Bagchi took charge of retail banking at ICICI Bank on November 1, 2016. Demonetisation was announced a week later, sending banks into an operational scramble. “He handled the situation so smoothly that we would say among ourselves: it didn’t even feel as though a new boss had arrived,” a former colleague said.A chemical engineering graduate from IIT Kanpur’s 1990 batch, Bagchi also has a management degree from IIM Bangalore. He joined ICICI in 1992 and worked across treasury, corporate banking and retail financial services. As managing director and CEO of ICICI Securities from 2011, he oversaw broking, wealth management, product distribution and corporate finance. He also helped develop ICICI Direct’s online trading platform and ICICI Bank’s internet banking platform.
