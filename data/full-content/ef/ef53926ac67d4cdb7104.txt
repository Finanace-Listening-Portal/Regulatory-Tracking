Articles 1 min read Rupee outpaces Asian peers on fresh FX curbs, RBI intervention The Indian rupee has strengthened against the dollar, thanks to recent initiatives by the Reserve Bank of India designed to curb dollar demand and stabilize the foreign exchange market. Experts suggest that these regulatory changes may bolster the rupee's short-term resilience. Moreover, oil prices are trending downwards as new supplies from Russia potentially enter the market. Central bank actions have generally fostered support for the Indian currency. Advt The regulator has also told banks to offer non-deliverable contracts to clients only for protecting underlying exposures and slashed the size of derivative transactions users can undertake without underlying exposure to $5 million from $100 million.The tough curbs and heightened scrutiny have rattled banks and triggered unintended consequences such as widening of bid-ask spreads in the forwards market and a migration of activity into the non-deliverable forward market.Analysts expect the regulatory steps to help steady the rupee even as the underlying balance of payments picture for India remains challenging."The measures should support INR in the near term by reducing USD demand while making it more difficult to take derivative positions," Union Bancaire Privee said in a note."Markets increasingly view USD/INR 97.00 as a level the RBI is prepared to defend."The dollar index was down 0.2% at 102.3 while Asian currencies were largely range bound.Oil prices were on track for their first decline in nearly a week as traders weighed the prospect of additional diesel supplies from Russia.
