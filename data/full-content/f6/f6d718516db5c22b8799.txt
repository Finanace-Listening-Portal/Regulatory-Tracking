Indian debt markets were shut on Tuesday for a local holiday."With little changes in macroeconomic fundamentals, any move in bonds should be rangebound, until there is any major change in triggers," the trader said. Advt Benchmark Brent crude oil prices held near $92 a barrel, with focus remaining on risks of escalation between the US and Iran.High oil prices pose risks for major importer India, threatening to widen its current account deficit, weaken the rupee and accelerate inflation.Investors are also focusing on the Reserve Bank of India's policy decision due on December 4. A majority of economists surveyed by Reuters expect the central bank to raise its policy repo rate by 25 basis points, though some have not ruled out a larger increase.Meanwhile, the 10-year US Treasury yield remained around 4.90%, as safe-haven demand persisted and amid growing expectations that the Federal Reserve could adopt a more measured approach at its December policy meeting.RATESOvernight index swap rates are likely to consolidate in line with bond yields.On Monday, the 1-year rate ended at 6.47%, while the 2-year rate ended at 6.435% and the liquid 5-year rate ended at 6.45%.(Reporting by Dharamraj Dhutia; Editing by Jochelle Mendonca) Reuters Published On Nov 25, 2026 at 09:31 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
