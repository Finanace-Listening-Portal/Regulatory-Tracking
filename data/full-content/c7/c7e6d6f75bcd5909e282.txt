Articles 2 min read HDFC Bank names ICICI veteran banker V.N. Srivatsan as Chief Compliance Officer HDFC Bank has appointed V.N. Srivatsan as its Chief Compliance Officer for a three-year term, effective from his joining date on January 4, 2027. Srivatsan, currently Head – Compliance: Overseas Banking and Subsidiaries & Data Privacy Officer at ICICI Bank, has around 34 years of professional experience, including 25 years in BFSI. Advt Until Srivatsan joins, Venkateswaran L, currently Compliance Head - Credit, Asset Product & Regulatory Reporting, will hold the responsibility of Chief Compliance Officer, the bank said.Who is V.N. Srivatsan?Srivatsan currently serves as Head – Compliance: Overseas Banking and Subsidiaries & Data Privacy Officer at ICICI Bank. He has approximately 34 years of overall professional experience, including around 25 years in the banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) sector and nearly 10 years of direct experience in compliance and regulatory inspection.He has previously led Retail Banking Compliance and Regulatory Inspection for more than eight years. His responsibilities have included regulatory compliance oversight, regulatory inspections, designing and implementing compliance frameworks and policies, and working with senior management, the Audit Committee, the Board and regulators.Also read: HDFC Bank CEO transition nears; Jefferies sees rerating scope, sets target at Rs 880Srivatsan holds a postgraduate degree in Business Administration from PSG College of Technology and a Bachelor of Technology degree from Sri Venkateshwara University.HDFC Bank said the appointment is in accordance with the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks- Compliance Function) Directions, 2026. Srivatsan will join as a full-time employee of the bank.Meanwhile, Rajput, who also remains a full-time employee of HDFC Bank, holds a B.Sc. (Honours) degree, is a Certified Associate of the Indian Institute of Bankers and has an Advanced Diploma in Business Management.
