Articles 2 min read OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Financial Services with built-in financial data The new offering combines GPT-6 Astra with data from LSEG News, Daloopa, PitchBook and Crunchbase to help investment bankers and equity researchers with research, financial modelling and client materials. Advt ChatGPT for Financial Services builds on the security controls of ChatGPT Enterprise, including role-based access, encryption and workspace management.The product includes financial datasets from providers such as Daloopa, PitchBook, LSEG News and Crunchbase, covering earnings transcripts, financial statements, company fundamentals and private-company information.OpenAI said the data is indexed and hosted on its infrastructure to improve retrieval and allow users to trace figures and claims to their sources.The offering also includes data from Quartr and other providers. Financial firms with existing subscriptions can connect them through integrations with providers including S&P Global, LSEG, MSCI, Moody’s and FactSet, allowing users to access data they are already entitled to through their ChatGPT sign-in.Research, modelling and pitchbooksGPT-6 Astra is designed to handle financial documents, tables, figures and supporting notes, and can use those inputs for analysis before generating documents, spreadsheets and presentations, OpenAI said.Users can conduct research across multiple sources, compare financial information across periods, build financial models and prepare materials such as valuation analyses, research notes and pitchbooks.Firms can also publish approved Excel, Word and PowerPoint templates through an administrative interface, allowing teams to generate client materials in their organisation’s formats and styles. Advt Security and governanceFor financial institutions, OpenAI is positioning data controls as a central part of the product. By default, OpenAI does not use business data to train its models, and it encrypts data in transit and at rest, according to the company.Administrators can configure data retention, control access to applications and their read/write functions, and create separate workspaces to establish information barriers.Compliance teams can also export supported workspace logs through OpenAI’s Compliance Platform for use in existing audit and investigation workflows.The product supports more than 50 connectors, including Datasite, Box, Preqin, FactSet and Intapp, while OpenAI said it has optimised commonly used financial-services connectors to reduce the need for users to troubleshoot data requests. Advt Expanding beyond investment bankingOpenAI stated that its initial focus on investment banking and equity research was guided by its work with Morgan Stanley and Evercore, particularly around data access and producing high-quality financial documents.The company plans to expand the product into other areas of financial services and add more financial data providers.LSEG, which distributes Reuters news, financial data and real-time alerts to financial professionals, is among the providers connected to the offering.ChatGPT for Financial Services is currently available to eligible financial institutions, OpenAI said.
