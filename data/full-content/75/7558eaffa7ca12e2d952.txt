ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Sep 23, 2026 at 04:41 PM IST var news_msid = 134433945; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook ICICI Prudential Life to operate as ICICI Life Insurance LimitedThe insurance arm of ICICI Group, has informed BSE and the National Stock Exchange on Tuesday that it has changed its name from ICICI Prudential Life Insurance to ICICI Life Insurance Limited, which came into effect from September 22, 2026. The transition is subject to approval from the Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre, and Ministry of Corporate Affairs.The Insurance Company informed that the change also follows approval from the company’s members, which was disclosed on September 19, 2026, following which the formal ROC approval was obtained on Tuesday. Advt ICICI Life Insurance Limited, as it will operate now onwards, is yet to file an intimation with the IRDAI for issuance of a revised Certificate of Registration. It will also undertake all the necessary compliances in relation to the name change with the stock exchanges, depositories, Registrar and Share Transfer Agent and other statutory or regulatory authorities, as applicable, shortly.Established in 2001, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited will now operate as ICICI Life Insurance Limited.
