Articles 1 min read Sebi extends digital accessibility compliance deadline for regulated entities to Jan 31 The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) has granted an extension until January 31 of the coming year for regulated entities to comply with digital accessibility standards. This decision, prompted by requests from market participants, aligns with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. The extension includes requirements for appointing an IAAP auditor and conducting necessary accessibility assessments. Advt The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Sebi) said it had received representations from regulated entities seeking an extension for submitting their compliance status and had also reviewed the progress made by them.The circular refers to five earlier communications issued by the regulator in July, August, September and December 2025, and July 31, 2026, relating to digital accessibility requirements.The regulator said all other provisions of the earlier circulars will remain unchanged and must be complied with by regulated entities.The direction was issued to protect investors' interests, promote the development and regulate the securities market.
