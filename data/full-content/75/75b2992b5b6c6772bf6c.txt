} } Listen to This ArticleState-owned Bank of Baroda (BoB) on Friday reported a 36 per cent year-on-year (Y-o-Y) rise in net profit at ₹6,532 crore for the second quarter of financial year 2026-27 (Q2FY27). The growth was largely aided by a sharp rise in non-interest income, driven by the sale of a portion of its National Stock Exchange (NSE) shares during the exchange's recent initial public offering (IPO). This is the lender’s highest ever quarterly profit. The bank’s net interest income (NII) was up 9 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹13,026 crore in Q2FY27, while non-interest income was up nearly 96 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹6,881 crore, as the lender made treasury income of ₹4,093 crore during the quarter compared to ₹1,086 crore in Q2FY26, and ₹893 crore in Q1FY27. In the quarter under review, the lender’s net interest margin (NIM) contracted 3 basis points (bps) to 2.74 per cent compared to Q1FY27. "Our NIM stood at 2.74 per cent for the quarter, against the guidance range of 2.75-2.95 per cent. However, the half-year NIM was 2.76 per cent, which is in line with our guidance. Given the interest rate outlook for the third and fourth quarters, we expect margins to improve and remain within the guided range", said Debadatta Chand, MD&CEO, Bank of Baroda.
