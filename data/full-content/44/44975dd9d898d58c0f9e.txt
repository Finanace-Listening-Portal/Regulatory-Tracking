The detailed conditions issued by the Federal Tax Authority were published on September 28, just three days before the new rules took effect on October 1, leaving limited time to revise existing arrangements. A "crucial threshold for cash transactions remained unspecified," she added. They include certain transportation costs, food and beverages at remote locations, operational accommodation, temporary accommodation for new employees, mobile phones and internet services, and work-related parking. Under the earlier rules, businesses could recover VAT on food and services provided to employees. Under the revised rules, food and beverage expenses must meet the conditions applicable to remote locations. Work phones, data packages and home internet expenses may also require a written usage policy, evidence of employer monitoring, and records of non-compliance.
