Articles 2 min read Investments in emerging sectors rise, but bank and NBFC financing remain limited Investments in emerging sectors, including AI, semiconductors, and defense, have surged past Rs 5 lakh crore. Yet, lending from banks and non-banking financial institutions has not kept pace, causing concerns among potential investors. The report suggests the formation of specialized financing desks to tackle these challenges. As technology evolves, investor focus is likely to shift toward storage solutions, hydrogen production, data centers, and defense initiatives. "AI, semiconductors and defence each carry over Rs 5 lakh crore of announced public and private investment," it said.However, banks may not be the primary financiers of all these projects, as financing patterns vary significantly across sectors, with debt financing dominating manufacturing and power projects "while AI and space stay equity-funded." Advt Noting that "Technology obsolescence is the top risk," it highlighted, "chip nodes shift every 3-5 years and GPUs refresh every 18-24 months." At the same time, the lending sector faces offtake and payment risks due to "few bankable hydrogen buyers, discom delays in renewables and long MoD (Ministry of Defence) payment cycles."At the same time, investment recovery periods range from five to eight years in semiconductors and seven to ten years in space, often exceeding typical loan tenors.As per the report, banks should establish specialised financing desks for semiconductors, hydrogen, data centres and battery storage. It also suggested milestone-linked disbursements, receivables securitisation, order-book and TReDS financing, equipment leasing and green loans.Going forward, "Over 3-5 years, demand shifts to storage, ATMP, offtake-linked hydrogen, edge data centres and the defence MSME tier," it noted.Apart from this, 12 emerging sectors, led by artificial intelligence (AI), semiconductors and defence, are expected to drive India's next phase of capital expenditure growth, supported by government schemes and rising private investments.AI leads with around Rs 5.9 lakh crore in disclosed public and private investments, followed by semiconductors at Rs 5.27 lakh crore and defence at Rs 5.07 lakh crore. Biotechnology and green hydrogen are also emerging as major investment destinations, it noted. Advt The report noted that private companies and global hyperscalers have pledged over USD 400 billion towards AI and data-centre infrastructure, while the semiconductor sector is targeting Rs 4 lakh crore in private investments between 2026 and 2032."Banks can lead with milestone-linked, receivables-backed and leasing products, backed by specialist sector desks," it said.
