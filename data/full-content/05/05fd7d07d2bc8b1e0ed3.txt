"An attacker moved approximately $6.36 billion out of Binance wallets on ten blockchains, most of it in about 20 minutes," TRM Labs said in a blog post on its website."TRM attributes the attack to TraderTraitor, North Korea's elite hacking unit, based on robust on- and off-chain intelligence. We make this judgment with high confidence,” it further said. The FBI had named TraderTraitor as responsible for the $1.5 billion Bybit hack in February 2025.TRM said several features of the latest hack on Binance match the earlier North Korea-linked heists, including the Bybit hack and the KelpDAO hack in April 2026. This includes small Tornado Cash withdrawals funding an operator wallet, staging that began well before the theft, and signatures obtained in advance, then executed in one burst.
