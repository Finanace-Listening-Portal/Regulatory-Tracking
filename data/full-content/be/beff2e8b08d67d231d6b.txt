Which borrowers will be affected?The increase will affect HDFC Bank customers whose existing loans are linked to MCLR. These may include home loan, personal loan and other borrowers, depending on their loan agreements. However, the revision will not necessarily increase everyone's EMI immediately. The actual impact depends on the interest rate reset date specified in the loan agreement. Borrowers should check their loan statements and repayment schedules to find out when the revised rate will apply. What is MCLR?MCLR is the minimum interest rate below which a bank generally cannot lend, except in certain cases permitted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It is calculated using factors such as the bank's cost of funds, operating expenses and the cost of maintaining cash reserves. For borrowers, an increase in MCLR can raise the interest cost when their loan rate is reset. Depending on the loan terms, banks may increase the EMI or extend the repayment period.
