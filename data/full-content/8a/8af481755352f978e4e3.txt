The focus now shifts to whether the upcoming MD & CEO Anup Bagchi can drive growth and earnings recovery. Motilal Oswal also said the stock had declined 25 per cent over the past year and 8 per cent over five years. The prolonged weakness has also resulted in a significant de-rating of the stock. Emkay said the valuation has become attractive following the prolonged underperformance and expects a sustained rerating as the new leadership delivers on key business priorities. It also said a moderation or reversal in FPI outflows as geopolitical uncertainty eases could support the rerating. Advt The appointment of Anup Bagchi as Managing Director & CEO has removed one of the key uncertainties surrounding the bank, according to the brokerages. Motilal Oswal said the leadership change removes the succession overhang and brings an external perspective at a time when HDFC Bank is seeking to rebuild investor confidence. It expects a new leadership team, along with an improvement in growth and earnings, to improve investor sentiment.BrokerageRatingTarget priceCMP in reportImplied upsideEmkay ResearchBUYRs 1,225Rs 72169.90%PL CapitalBUYRs 950Rs 721~32%Motilal OswalBUYRs 925Rs 721~28%JefferiesBUYRs 880Rs 719.35~22%Emkay has the most bullish target among the four at Rs 1,225 and has reiterated BUY. The brokerage said HDFC Bank has de-rated significantly over the past several years and that progress on deposit and credit growth should help restore investor confidence and drive a rerating.PL Capital has retained BUY with a target price of Rs 950, valuing the bank at 1.9 times March 2028 core adjusted book value. It sees Bagchi’s appointment positively given his track record, although it cautioned that an external CEO could lead to changes in processes and management at the mid- and senior levels, which could be disruptive in the short term.Motilal Oswal has also retained BUY with a Rs 925 target, based on 1.8 times FY28E adjusted book value plus Rs 128 for subsidiaries. It expects HDFC Bank’s earnings performance to improve from FY28 onward, with RoA sustaining at around 1.7 per cent. Advt Jefferies has the most conservative target among the four at Rs 880, but has also retained BUY. Its valuation assigns Rs 750 per share to the standalone bank at 1.6 times adjusted price-to-book and adds Rs 133 per share for group companies after the holding-company discount.Earnings recovery is central to the rerating thesisThe brokerages broadly see FY28 as the point at which HDFC Bank’s operating performance could begin to show a more meaningful improvement.Motilal Oswal expects earnings growth to recover to 13 per cent in FY28, compared with an average of around 9 per cent during FY24-27. It expects the improvement to be supported by a recovery in loan growth and margins. The brokerage estimates loan growth at a 14 per cent CAGR over FY26-28. Advt Emkay is more optimistic on the earnings trajectory, estimating loan growth of 18 per cent in FY28 and FY29, NII growth of 19.3 per cent in FY28 and 22.7 per cent in FY29, with RoA improving from 1.8 per cent in FY26 to 1.9 per cent in FY27-28 and 2 per cent in FY29.Jefferies, meanwhile, expects net profit to rise from Rs 756 billion in FY27E to Rs 882 billion in FY28E and Rs 1,017 billion in FY29E. It forecasts EPS growth of 17 per cent in FY28 and 15 per cent in FY29, against just 1 per cent in FY27.Deposit growth and LDR remain criticalPL Capital has identified the loan-to-deposit ratio, or LDR, as one of the key fundamental issues that Bagchi will need to address, alongside NIM pressure and the gap between management guidance and actual delivery. Advt Emkay similarly lists revival in deposit growth among Bagchi’s immediate priorities and sees progress in both deposits and credit as necessary for investor confidence to return.The latest quarterly trends, however, provide some comfort. Jefferies said HDFC Bank reported 16.3 per cent year-on-year loan growth and 18.8 per cent deposit growth in the September quarter, with LDR at 97 per cent. It noted that the growth was helped by FCNR(B) inflows, including USD 11.5 billion of FCNR(B) deposits.Jefferies expects loan and deposit growth of 13 per cent and 15 per cent respectively in FY27.Management stability will determine how quickly the rerating plays outWhile the brokerages are positive on Bagchi’s appointment, they do not see the leadership change as an automatic trigger for the stock.Emkay said Bagchi’s non-obtrusive leadership style and preference for delegation could help him carry the existing team along, although some senior-management churn cannot be ruled out because HDFC Bank will, for the first time in its 32-year history, be led by an external CEO. It has listed stability in upper management as an immediate priority.PL Capital similarly cautioned that an external CEO could result in operational and management-level changes that may be disruptive in the short term. It said execution under the new leadership would remain a key monitorable.HDFC Bank shares were trading 1.91 per cent lower at Rs 705.60 as of 2:50 pm on October 5, after touching an intraday low of Rs 701.20.
