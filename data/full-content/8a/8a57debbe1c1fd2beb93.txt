Paytm, PB Fintech and Kissht are expected to deliver a healthy Q2FY27, while Paytm is being preferred over PB Fintech, according to JM Financial.For Paytm, revenue is expected to grow 25.7 per cent year on year and 5.8 per cent quarter on quarter to Rs25.9 billion, with EBITDA margin expanding 123 basis points quarter on quarter to 9.5 per cent. Adjusted EBITDA margin is expected to improve about 110 basis points to 12.1 per cent, while PAT is expected to grow 26.8 per cent quarter on quarter. UPI MDR, applicable from October 15, is expected to contribute positively from Q3FY27. Advt Paytm's revenue growth is expected to be supported by a 28 per cent year-on-year increase in GMV, while merchant subscription devices are expected to increase by about 0.5 million in Q2FY27 to 16.2 million. Financial services revenue is expected to grow 42.3 per cent year on year and 7 per cent quarter on quarter, while payment services revenue is expected to increase 20 per cent year on year and 5 per cent quarter on quarter.Regulatory glareFor PB Fintech, total insurance premium excluding GST is expected to grow 33.9 per cent year on year and 9.2 per cent quarter on quarter, led by retail health and term life and supported by a compounding renewal book. Revenue is expected to rise 29.4 per cent year on year and 10.6 per cent quarter on quarter, while PAT is expected to jump 34 per cent year on year and 11 per cent quarter on quarter.However, the proposed IRDAI commission caps remain the key overhang for PB Fintech, with the changes expected to start impacting earnings from FY28. Mapping the draft caps onto PB Fintech's business mix implies a 40-45 per cent reduction in insurance revenue. The brokerage expects management may re-evaluate continuing the PB Partners vertical if the draft commission caps are incorporated into the final circular.Kissht's provisional update showed AUM growth of 68.4 per cent year on year and 16.4 per cent quarter on quarter, while disbursements rose 55.6 per cent year on year. LAP increased to 7.9 per cent of AUM from 4.1 per cent in Q2FY26, while the registered user base reached 79.5 million. Advt The brokerage expects Kissht's NII to surge 53 per cent year on year to Rs6.6 billion, supported by sustained AUM growth. Credit costs are expected to moderate to 6.3 per cent of AUM from 9.3 per cent, while reported profit is expected to soar 90 per cent year on year and 25 per cent quarter on quarter to Rs1.2 billion, taking RoAAUM to 5.5 per cent. JM Financial prefers Paytm over PB Fintech given the latter's earnings trajectory and regulatory skew while remaining positive on Kissht.
