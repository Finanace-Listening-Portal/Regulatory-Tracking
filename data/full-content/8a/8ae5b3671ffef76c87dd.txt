Articles 3 min read 49% of MSMEs report better business conditions; confidence index rises to 60.4: Report According to a report by SIDBI, MSME business confidence rose to 60.4 in Q2 FY27 from 59.3 in the previous quarter, with manufacturing, services and trading all improving. While 49% of MSMEs reported better business conditions, business expectations for the coming quarters also strengthened. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Updated On Oct 23, 2026 at 05:28 PM IST var news_msid = 135144735; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook -Jaspreet KaurBusiness confidence among India’s micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) improved in July–September 2026, reversing the moderation seen in recent quarters, as per the latest report by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).The MSME Business Confidence Index (M-BCI) rose to 60.4 in the second quarter of FY2026–27 from 59.3 in the previous quarter. The index remained above the neutral level of 50 across manufacturing, trading and services, the survey showed.The MSME Business Expectations Index (M-BEI) is projected at 61.7 for October–December 2026 and 62.2 for July–September 2027. Advt Finance access varies across sectorsAccess to overall finance improved for 47% of manufacturing MSMEs and 44% of services MSMEs during the quarter. In trading, 35% reported improved access to overall finance, with the report indicating expectations of gradual improvement over the coming year.Working-capital access remained a challenge for a section of enterprises, particularly in trading, where 31% reported a decline in access to working-capital finance in the quarter ended September 2026.In manufacturing, 46% of firms reported improved working-capital access. In services, the share reporting better access stood at 38% and is expected to rise to around 40%.Manufacturing leads sectoral confidenceThe improvement in business confidence was recorded across all three sectors. The manufacturing M-BCI rose to 61.0 in July–September 2026 from 59.5 in the previous quarter. Its expectations index is projected at 63.3 for October–December 2026 and 64.8 for July–September 2027.The services M-BCI increased to 61.3 from 60.4, while the trading index rose to 57.5 from 56.5. The expectations indices for services and trading are projected at 61.0 and 61.1, respectively, for October–December 2026.For July–September 2027, the services expectations index is projected at 61.1, while the trading index is projected at 60.9. Advt Sales sentiment improves, but margins remain under pressureSales sentiment improved across MSME sectors in the July–September quarter, with the exception of trading, where pessimistic responses edged up.In manufacturing, 43% of enterprises reported positive sales growth, while pessimistic responses eased to 27%. In services, 45% of MSMEs were optimistic about sales, and pessimistic responses declined to 23%.Trading sentiment remained relatively subdued, with 38% of MSMEs reporting optimistic sales sentiment. Pessimistic responses in the sector increased to 22% in Round 8 from 15% in the previous round. Advt The report also highlighted continued pressure on net profit margins. Across sectors, 48%–58% of MSMEs reported no significant change in margins, while 20%–30% reported a decline. Margin challenges remained higher in trading.However, around 31%–35% of MSMEs across sectors expect margins to improve over the coming year.Skilled-labour outlook differs by sectorThe survey recorded an improvement in skilled-labour availability across sectors during the quarter.In manufacturing, the share of firms expecting better skilled-labour availability is projected to rise from 25% in July–September 2026 to 40% over the one-year-ahead period. Advt In services, the share reporting better availability is projected to moderate to 37%, compared with 40% currently. In trading, it is projected to decline to 31% from 42%.Nearly half report better business conditionsOverall, an average of 49% of MSMEs reported improved business conditions in July–September 2026. Another 33% indicated that conditions remained unchanged, while 18% reported softer conditions compared with the previous quarter.The report covers approximately 1,200 MSMEs across manufacturing, trading and services. The MSME Business Confidence Index measures current-quarter business conditions, while the Business Expectations Index captures expectations for future quarters.The report also notes that improved business confidence, sales sentiment and access to finance point to continued resilience and adaptability among MSMEs.
