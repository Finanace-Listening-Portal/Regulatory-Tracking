This version check can prevent a practical claims misunderstanding. A family may save an attractive brochure and later receive a schedule with different selections or endorsements. The documents should be reconciled while there is time to correct an error, rather than first compared at hospital admission. Keep the final issued contract as well as the pre-purchase quotation. Individual cover and family floater The policy wording distinguishes individual and floater limits. Under a floater, the relevant annual benefit limits generally apply per policy, unless expressly stated otherwise; individual cover generally operates per insured person. The sum insured should therefore be read together with the unit to which it applies. For a family, a useful review records each covered person, age, declared medical history and chosen cover arrangement. Do not assume that listing several insured members creates a separate full floater limit for each. Ask the insurer to resolve any mismatch between the proposal and schedule in writing. Read the benefit menu with its conditions The official brochure and prospectus describe hospitalisation-related protection and additional benefit categories. MediCare Premier includes features such as maternity, outpatient and global planned-hospitalisation benefits subject to the relevant terms. Naming a benefit does not establish that every associated expense is payable, or that each benefit has the full base sum insured available without a sublimit. Age, insured members, address, underwriting and selected options can affect a quotation. Compare the annual amount actually payable for the same people and selections. A quoted discount should not obscure an excluded benefit or changed cost-sharing condition. Keep any premium illustration distinct from the insurer’s accepted offer. Waiting periods and exclusions The customer information sheet lists an initial waiting period of 30 days, a 24-month waiting period for specified diseases or procedures, and a 24-month pre-existing-disease waiting period. These are different categories. The detailed wording, including applicable exceptions and continuity credit, determines how they operate for a particular claim. The sheet also summarises exclusions and warns that the policy wording contains the complete terms. Review disclosures of medical history and any change between proposal and commencement. Completion of one waiting period does not make an excluded or otherwise inadmissible expense payable. A claim decision still turns on the contract and circumstances. Keep continuity evidence When moving from another policy, preserve previous schedules, renewal receipts and claim records. Ask for written confirmation of the continuity credit actually recognised. A new document bearing a fresh start date should not be interpreted in isolation, but neither should an assumed credit be treated as granted without confirmation. This document review is particularly relevant when a family member’s existing cover is being replaced. Global planned hospitalisation has restrictions The wording’s global benefit concerns planned treatment abroad following diagnosis in India. It is not unrestricted overseas travel insurance. The clause excludes eligibility where the policyholder or any insured is a foreign national, NRI or OCI, and it is unavailable when a resident Indian national has opted out. The wording states that cover cannot be taken again after that opt-out. The proposal form specifically asks about residency and the global-cover choice. Readers considering treatment abroad should therefore check every insured person’s status and the actual option recorded. Do not rely on a brochure’s global-cover heading alone. Seek written confirmation of admissibility, documents, payment arrangements and the applicable limits before making a planned financial commitment. A hospital estimate and an insurer’s approval serve different purposes. Health insurance premium deduction in Tax Year 2026–27 Section 126 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 governs the health-premium deduction for the current tax year, subject to eligibility and the applicable tax regime. For an individual, the ordinary self/family limit is ₹25,000, with a separate ₹25,000 parent limit; the relevant insurance limit becomes ₹50,000 where the insured person is a senior citizen. Family means spouse and dependent children. Premium payments must be non-cash. A lump-sum premium covering several years is apportioned across the relevant tax years under the statutory rule. Do not equate a premium receipt with an automatic refund of the premium or tax. The deduction reduces eligible taxable income where it is available. The resulting benefit depends on the claimant’s circumstances, tax regime and limits already used. Preserve payment evidence and identify which person paid the premium; avoid claiming the same payment twice within a family. A simple deduction review Prepare a small working with the amount paid, policy period, covered persons, ages, payment mode and amount claimed. If the premium covers more than one tax year, retain the allocation calculation with the receipt. This makes it easier to explain the claim without mixing a product benefit with a personal tax entitlement. Section 80D references in older literature concern the earlier statutory framework and should not replace the current-year analysis. GST exemption for eligible individual health insurance The Department of Financial Services confirms the exemption for individual health-insurance policies, effective 22 September 2025. Notification No. 16/2025-Central Tax (Rate), dated 17 September 2025, amended the service-exemption framework. Individual family-floater and senior-citizen policies fall within the individual-health category; group insurance must be examined separately. TaxGuru’s notification coverage provides the text and context. Check the actual invoice and policy classification. GST exemption does not freeze the insurer’s underlying premium or create an additional income-tax deduction. If an invoice appears inconsistent, obtain an explanation of the classification and tax treatment rather than applying an old percentage mechanically. Claims documents and practical review For a planned admission, contact the insurer and hospital early and preserve the authorisation trail. Keep prescriptions, investigation reports, discharge records, itemised bills and payment evidence. Ask for a reasoned explanation where a charge is disallowed. This helps separate a documentation gap from a benefit sublimit or exclusion. An insurer’s cashless arrangement does not make every hospital charge admissible. Maintain a record of the approved amount and any amount paid personally. Review renewals promptly so the schedule continues to reflect the insured family and selected options. The objective is accurate understanding of this contract, not a ranking against competing insurers.
