The lender’s NII — the difference between interest earned and interest expended — grew 12 per cent year-on-year to ₹15,371 crore during the quarter. Excluding the impact of foreign currency non-resident (FCNR-B) deposits, NIM stood at 3.47 per cent. Other income slipped 1 per cent year-on-year to ₹6,531 crore, as trading income fell sharply to ₹85 crore from ₹498 crore a year earlier. Fee income, which forms a substantial component of other income, rose 6 per cent to ₹6,381 crore. Advances grow 23%, deposits up 21% The bank’s advances grew 23 per cent year-on-year and 9 per cent sequentially to ₹13.74 trillion as of September 30, 2026.
