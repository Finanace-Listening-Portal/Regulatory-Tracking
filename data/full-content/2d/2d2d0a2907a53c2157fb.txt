This creates scope for greater investment, technology exchange and collaboration between businesses in both countries, it added. Advt The report -- which was released during the India-Italy Business Forum in Rome -- was presented to Antonio Tajani, Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of Italy, by Saurabh Sanyal, Secretary General, ASSOCHAM.Sanyal said that India and Italy have considerable potential to deepen their economic partnership by combining India's manufacturing scale, skilled workforce and expanding market with Italy's strengths in quality, design, precision engineering and advanced industrial technologies.Stronger business connections, technology exchange and investment partnerships can help enterprises in both countries access new markets and strengthen their global competitiveness, he added.Together, these strengths provide a strong foundation for expanding trade, investment and integrated value chains, the report further states.It also examines the evolving economic relationship between the two countries and identifies emerging opportunities across trade, investment, manufacturing, technology and connectivity, highlighting the potential to move beyond conventional trade towards deeper industrial collaboration, joint ventures, technology partnerships and investment-led cooperation.Italy's global imports stood at $669 billion in 2025, while its imports from India reached $9.7 billion. Advt India's share in Italy's total imports increased from around 1.2 per cent in 2019 to 1.5 per cent in 2025.Cooperation opportunities are particularly promising across advanced manufacturing and industrial automation, automobiles and components, pharmaceuticals and medical technologies, textiles, fashion and technical textiles, food processing and agricultural technology, according to the report.Emerging areas such as artificial intelligence, semiconductors, fintech, quantum computing, digital innovation, renewable energy, green hydrogen and energy storage also offer great scope for joint research, technology development and commercial partnerships. Advt Beyond traditional manufacturing, both countries have considerable scope to strengthen cooperation in critical minerals, maritime infrastructure and logistics.ASSOCHAM believes the complementary strengths of the two economies, supported by sustained institutional engagement and active industry participation, can transform the longstanding India-Italy relationship into a dynamic partnership driven by trade, investment, innovation, and shared economic growth.--IANSsps/ag IANS Published On Nov 20, 2026 at 05:40 PM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
