Ltd., New Delhi. The notification has been issued in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 4(1), read with Sections 3, 5(1) and 5(1A) of the Customs Act, 1962. The notices were originally assigned to the Assistant Commissioner of Customs, PAG, Airport & Air Cargo Complex, Bengaluru, and the Assistant Commissioner of Customs, ICD, Whitefield, Bengaluru City Customs Commissionerate, respectively. The appointed Common Adjudicating Authority is empowered to exercise the powers and discharge the duties conferred or imposed upon these respective officers for adjudication of the specified show cause notices. The notification facilitates common adjudication of the proceedings involving the same noticee and does not determine the merits of the underlying customs disputes.
