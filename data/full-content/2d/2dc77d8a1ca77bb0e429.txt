Articles 2 min read Exclusive: Unity SFB extends Inderjit Camotra’s tenure by 6 months; RBI approval for new CEO likely in 2-3 weeks Unity Small Finance Bank submitted names for its next MD & CEO to the RBI around the end of July, with regulatory approval expected in the next 2-3 weeks, people aware of the development told ETBFSI. The lender confirmed its first CEO Camotra’s tenure has been extended only till February 4, 2027. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Sep 16, 2026 at 09:46 PM IST var news_msid = 134292147; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The Reserve Bank of India has extended the tenure of Inderjit Camotra as managing director and chief executive officer of Unity Small Finance Bank by six months till February 4, 2027, as the lender awaits regulatory approval for his successor.“The RBI has extended Mr. Inderjit Camotra’s tenure as MD & CEO of Unity Small Finance Bank till 4th February 2027,” Unity Small Finance Bank told ETBFSI in a confirmation.The extension comes as the bank has already submitted names for its next MD & CEO to the RBI. Two people aware of the development told ETBFSI that the names were sent to the regulator around the end of July, with the RBI approval likely to come in the next 2-3 weeks. Advt “The appointment of the MD & CEO of a bank is subject to prior approval from the RBI which is in the last leg of its process,” a person aware of the process said.Camotra has been associated with Unity since the bank’s inception. Before being appointed as MD & CEO in August 2022, he was an executive director at Centrum Financial Services, the promoter entity of Unity Bank, and was appointed interim CEO when the bank commenced operations.Unity Small Finance Bank commenced banking operations on November 1, 2021, after receiving the final nod from the RBI. At the time, Camotra, then an executive director at Centrum Financial Services, was named interim CEO of the new bank. Contemporary reports had identified him as the executive who would lead the bank while the search for a permanent CEO was underway.The bank subsequently appointed Camotra as its MD & CEO on August 5, 2022, with the appointment approved by both the RBI and the bank’s board, RBI had approved his appointment for a two-year period, and the board subsequently appointed him as MD & CEO with effect from August 5, 2022.At the time of his formal appointment, Camotra had more than 25 years of banking experience across Standard Chartered Bank, ANZ and Citibank. He is an IIT Delhi graduate and holds an MBA from Clarkson University, New York and had been involved in the transition of Centrum Financial Services’ business into Unity Bank. Advt In August 2024, the RBI approved Camotra’s reappointment as MD & CEO for another two years. The current six-month extension takes Camotra’s tenure beyond the end of his second two-year term, which was due to conclude in August 2026.
