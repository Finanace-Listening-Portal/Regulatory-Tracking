The yield on the benchmark 10-year government bond rose to 7.25 per cent from the previous close of 7.19 per cent. The MPC raised the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50 per cent and changed its stance to calibrated tightening, indicating that rate cuts were off the table in the near term. RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra said future policy action could only be a rate hike or a pause, depending on evolving growth and inflation conditions. The RBI raised its CPI inflation forecast for FY27 to 5.2 per cent from its earlier projection, with inflation expected at 4.9 per cent in Q2, 6 per cent in Q3 and 5.7 per cent in Q4. It projected inflation at 5.6 per cent in Q1FY28. The central bank expects supply-side pressures to persist, with deficient monsoon conditions, El Niño and volatility in international oil prices posing risks to the inflation outlook. Malhotra said early signs of inflation becoming more generalised were also visible through higher core inflation and price pressures across a larger segment of the CPI basket. On the external sector, Malhotra said India’s current account deficit (CAD) remained modest and well below sustainable levels in Q1FY27 despite external shocks. However, the merchandise trade deficit widened in July-August, driven mainly by higher imports of electronic goods and crude oil. Going forward, moderation in global trade growth, elevated energy prices and persistent trade policy uncertainties pose upside risks to India’s CAD in FY27, the RBI said. At the same time, buoyant services exports, robust remittances and the implementation of the India-UK trade deal and other bilateral agreements are expected to provide resilience to the external sector. India’s CAD stood at 0.5 per cent of GDP, or $4.2 billion, in Q1FY27, compared with 0.4 per cent of GDP a year ago. The services trade surplus stood at $85.8 billion during April-August, while net transfers, primarily comprising worker remittances, stood at $53.9 billion during April-July. The RBI said the balance of payments was expected to record a healthy surplus in FY27, while foreign exchange reserves remained adequate, with import cover of around 11 months.
