Issued under Section 14(2) of the Customs Act, 1962, the notification further amends Notification No. 36/2001-Customs (N.T.) dated 3 August 2001 by substituting Tables 1, 2 and 3. These tables prescribe tariff values for specified edible oils, brass scrap, gold, silver and areca nuts for customs valuation purposes. The notification replaces the prescribed values without separately setting out the immediately preceding figures. Under Table 1, the revised tariff values are US$1,223 per metric tonne for crude palm oil, US$1,234 for RBD palm oil, US$1,229 for other palm oil, US$1,242 for crude palmolein, US$1,245 for RBD palmolein, US$1,244 for other palmolein, US$1,287 for crude soya bean oil and US$8,247 for brass scrap of all grades. Table 2 specifies a tariff value of US$1,316 per 10 grams for the listed categories of gold and US$1,907 per kilogram for the listed categories of silver, subject to the descriptions and import conditions stated in the Schedule. Table 3 fixes the tariff value of areca nuts under tariff item 080280 at US$10,433 per metric tonne. The revised tariff values are relevant to importers, customs brokers, valuation professionals and businesses dealing in the specified commodities. Importers must identify the correct tariff heading, product description and applicable conditions before applying the notified values. The notification also refers to the customs exemption benefits under Notification No. 45/2025-Customs dated 24 October 2025 for specified gold and silver entries. Its explanatory note records that the principal tariff-value notification was last amended by Notification No. 80/2026-Customs (N.T.) dated 30 September 2026. The revised values apply from 16 October 2026, as expressly provided in the notification.
