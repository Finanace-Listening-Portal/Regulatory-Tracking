Articles 4 min read GFF 2026: NPCI showcases next phase of UPI: Credit access, instant cards and branchless deposits NPCI on Wednesday unveiled three financial inclusion-focused innovations at Global Fintech Fest 2026, aimed at taking banking services beyond traditional branches and making formal credit and cash services more accessible. The initiatives include instant RuPay card and merchant UPI QR issuance through open-source ATMs, Credit Line on UPI for KCC and PM MUDRA schemes, and Aadhaar-enabled three-party cash deposits. Advt ATMs to become instant card, QR issuance pointsUnder the new open-source Android ATM solution, customers can instantly receive personalised RuPay debit, credit and NCMC cards through a self-service journey, significantly reducing the turnaround time associated with conventional card issuance.The same ATMs will also enable merchants to obtain UPI QR codes instantly for payment collections.The machines are envisaged to go beyond conventional cash withdrawal and deposit functions, with NPCI saying they can also offer services such as cheque deposits, account opening and loan applications.The solution was demonstrated at GFF 2026, where attendees could experience the open-source ATMs deployed at the venue.The initiative could potentially alter the role of ATMs in the banking ecosystem, turning them into intelligent customer-service and banking access points rather than merely cash machines.Banks are also exploring their deployment as Digital Banking Units (DBUs), particularly in semi-urban and rural areas where traditional branch infrastructure may be limited.The machines could additionally be deployed at metro stations, airports, transit hubs, educational campuses and corporate parks, enabling instant card issuance for travellers, students and employees.For banks, instant issuance could improve customer acquisition and onboarding while reducing the operational costs associated with branch visits, courier delivery and physical card distribution. Advt Currently, nine banks are engaging with NPCI and ATM OEMs to assess and explore the solution. These include State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Bank of India, Punjab National Bank, Central Bank of India, UCO Bank, Slice Small Finance Bank and Airtel Payments Bank, alongside ATM manufacturers including Hitachi Payment Services, Perto India and Vortex Engineering.KCC, Mudra loans move onto UPINPCI has also expanded the use of Credit Line on UPI to select government-backed credit schemes, including Kisan Credit Card and PM MUDRA Yojana.The Reserve Bank of India enabled Credit Line on UPI in September 2023, allowing pre-sanctioned bank credit lines to be linked as funding accounts on UPI. Advt The latest integration allows eligible KCC and PMMY beneficiaries to use their sanctioned credit limits for merchant payments through UPI, bringing formal credit directly into the digital payments ecosystem.The scale of the two schemes makes the integration significant. According to NPCI, there are more than 7.7 crore operative KCC accounts with sanctioned limits exceeding Rs 10 lakh crore, while PMMY has crossed 57 crore loans amounting to more than Rs 40 lakh crore.The integration effectively brings a digital payment layer to credit facilities that serve farmers, small businesses and other priority segments. Advt Eligible KCC account holders of SBI, Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank, Union Bank of India, Indian Bank, Canara Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, IDBI Bank, Bank of Maharashtra and UCO Bank can now use their credit accounts as Credit Lines on UPI.For MUDRA beneficiaries, the facility is currently available through SBI, Punjab National Bank, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India, Canara Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, IDBI Bank and UCO Bank.NPCI said more banks are expected to extend these credit facilities through UPI in the coming months.AePS takes cash deposits beyond bank branchesThe third initiative, AePS Three-Party Cash Deposit, seeks to use the existing Aadhaar Enabled Payment System ecosystem and its assisted-banking network to enable cash deposits into another person's bank account.Under the model, an Aadhaar biometric-authenticated depositor can deposit cash directly into the bank account of a family member or other beneficiary through participating AePS Touchpoint Operators.The model is designed to address a key limitation of conventional cash deposits, which often depend on the beneficiary's bank branch, cash deposit machines or proprietary infrastructure.Here, the established AePS last-mile network can be used to facilitate the transaction.Aadhaar authentication adds security layerThe service uses Aadhaar-based biometric authentication to establish the identity and presence of the depositor.Beneficiary account validation provides an additional layer of security before the transaction is completed. The customer also receives confirmation of the beneficiary's name through SMS, helping ensure that the cash is credited to the intended account.NPCI said the service could reduce travel time and make it easier for customers to provide financial support to family members or make business-related payments, particularly for customers who may not be digitally savvy.The initiative is therefore aimed particularly at rural, semi-urban and underserved customers, where assisted banking remains an important component of financial inclusion.Currently, the service is enabled across Airtel Payments Bank, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, Fino Payments Bank, IDFC FIRST Bank, Indian Bank, Indian Overseas Bank, India Post Payments Bank, Karur Vysya Bank, Punjab National Bank, SBI, Tamilnad Mercantile Bank, Union Bank of India and YES Bank.More banks are expected to join the service in the coming months.Click here to read our coverage of the Global FinTech Fest 2026.
