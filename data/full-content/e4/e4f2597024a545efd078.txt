Articles 4 min read RBI introduces one-time approval route for MFs, insurers, pension funds to acquire bank shares Under the earlier framework, investors whose aggregate shareholding in a bank fell below 5 per cent had to seek RBI approval again before making a subsequent acquisition of major shareholding. RBI has now introduced a one-time approval route for mutual funds, insurance companies and pension funds for subsequent acquisitions of major shareholding, up to 10 per cent of a bank's paid-up share capital or voting rights on an aggregate basis, these directions take effect immediately from October 1, 2026. The one-time approval can cover subsequent acquisitions of major shareholding of up to 10 per cent of the paid-up share capital or voting rights of a banking company, subject to RBI's conditions and other applicable provisions. Advt What has changed in the approval process?Under the earlier framework, any person seeking to make an initial acquisition of major shareholding in a banking company was required to obtain prior approval from RBI.Further, if the person's aggregate shareholding after the initial acquisition subsequently fell below 5 per cent, prior RBI approval was required again before making a subsequent acquisition of major shareholding.RBI has now decided to provide a one-time approval route for subsequent acquisitions by qualifying mutual funds, insurance companies and pension funds.Who qualifies for the one-time approval?A "qualifying person" is defined as a mutual fund registered with SEBI, a pension fund registered with PFRDA or an insurance company registered with IRDAI.The investor must also not belong to the promoter group or group of the investee banking company.How will the one-time approval work?RBI may, at its discretion, grant one-time approval to qualifying persons, either individually or collectively, for subsequent acquisitions of major shareholding of up to 10 per cent of the paid-up share capital or voting rights of a banking company.The application has to be made through RBI's PRAVAAH platform. The concerned banking company will also have to furnish its comments to RBI in the prescribed Form A1. Advt The approval will be subject to the conditions specified by RBI as well as all other applicable provisions of the Directions.The 10 per cent limit will be computed on an aggregate basis in accordance with the existing provisions of the Directions.Can RBI revoke the one-time approval?RBI can revoke the approval if the qualifying person does not comply with the terms and conditions attached to the approval.The approval can also be revoked if the qualifying person or any person associated with it is subsequently found not to be "fit and proper".What reporting will investors have to undertake?The amendment introduces continuous monitoring requirements for qualifying persons that have obtained one-time approval. Advt After the initial acquisition of major shareholding, such investors will have to report any decrease or increase in their aggregate holding to below or above 5%, respectively, to RBI and the concerned banking company within three working days of the event.This means the one-time approval does not remove RBI's ongoing oversight of these investors.What has RBI clarified on portfolio managers?RBI has also clarified the circumstances in which an acquisition by a client will not be treated as an indirect acquisition by its portfolio manager.This will apply where the client is the registered owner of the shares and is entitled to exercise the voting rights; the portfolio manager is acting only as an adviser providing non-binding investment or divestment advice; and any voting rights exercised by the portfolio manager on behalf of the client are based on a specific mandate from the client. Advt Can banks apply on behalf of investors?The amendment allows an application for one-time approval to also be made by a bank on behalf of a qualifying person belonging to the promoter group or group of the bank.This provision appears separately in the revised approval framework and guidelines.When does the amended framework take effect?The amended Directions have come into force with immediate effect from October 1, 2026.
