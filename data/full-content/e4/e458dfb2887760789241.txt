Articles 2 min read Bank of Baroda LBO Recruitment 2026 deadline extended for 2,482 posts, apply till September 17 Bank of Baroda LBO Recruitment 2026: Bank of Baroda has extended the application deadline for its Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026 to September 17, 2026, until 11:59 PM. The recruitment drive covers 2,482 regular vacancies across multiple states. Candidates must meet the prescribed graduation, banking experience, age and local language requirements. The bank has also stated that the online test is tentatively scheduled for the first week of October 2026. Other conditions of the original advertisement remain unchanged. The bank has extended the last date for submitting online applications and paying the prescribed fee to September 17, 2026, until 11:59 PM. The extension was announced through an addendum dated September 7, 2026.The recruitment drive is being conducted for 2,482 Local Bank Officer posts on a regular basis under Advertisement No. BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/16. The original recruitment notification was issued on August 18, 2026. The bank has also stated that the online test is tentatively scheduled for the first week of October 2026. All other terms and conditions of the original advertisement remain unchanged. Advt Bank of Baroda LBO application deadline extendedCandidates who have not yet completed their applications can now register and pay the applicable fee until September 17. The official Bank of Baroda recruitment page lists 2,482 vacancies and the revised deadline of September 17, 2026.The post is Local Bank Officer in Junior Management Grade Scale-I. Applicants must hold a graduation degree and have at least one year of officer-level experience in a Scheduled Commercial Bank. Candidates must also meet the local language proficiency requirement for the state selected in the application.Check vacancies and eligibilityThe recruitment covers vacancies across multiple states, with Gujarat accounting for the highest number of posts at 1,185, followed by Karnataka with 418 and Maharashtra with 218.ParticularDetailsPostLocal Bank OfficerTotal vacancies2,482Age limit21 to 30 yearsQualificationGraduationExperienceMinimum one year as an officer in a Scheduled Commercial BankRevised last dateSeptember 17, 2026Online testTentatively first week of October 2026• Direct link to apply onlineApplication fee and selection processThe application fee is Rs. 850 for General, OBC and EWS candidates. SC, ST, PwD, ESM and female candidates are required to pay Rs. 175.The selection process comprises an online written examination, Local Language Proficiency Test, Group Discussion or Personal Interview, document verification and medical examination.Candidates selected for the post must execute a service bond requiring a minimum of three years of active service with Bank of Baroda. Leaving before completion of the stipulated period attracts a penalty equivalent to three months' gross salary, subject to a maximum of Rs. 2 lakh. Advt Applications can be submitted through the Bank of Baroda careers section. Candidates should complete registration and fee payment before the revised deadline.
