The visit coincided with the celebration of Bharat Forge’s 60th anniversary. Shri Amit Shah was welcomed by Padma Bhushan Baba Kalyani, Chairman and Managing Director of Bharat Forge Ltd. During the visit, the Home Minister inaugurated a milestone plaque commemorating the company’s six decades of engineering excellence, innovation, and contributions to nation-building. The visit provided an opportunity for the Home Minister to tour the world’s largest forging facility and engage with the leadership of Bharat Forge. Discussions focused on the company’s growth trajectory and its pivotal role in enhancing India’s manufacturing and defence capabilities, aligning with the national vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat (self-reliant India) and Sashakt Bharat, Surakshit Bharat (empowered and secure India). A highlight of the visit was the display of Bharat Forge’s indigenously designed and manufactured products, including small arms, artillery guns, and unmanned systems, presented by Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited (KSSL), the wholly-owned defence subsidiary of Bharat Forge. These products underscore the company’s commitment to self-reliance in defence manufacturing. Shri Amit Shah also flagged off the first consignment of 5.56 x 45 mm rifles destined for the Uttar Pradesh Special Task Force, reinforcing Bharat Forge’s contribution to national security. Bharat Forge Limited, headquartered in Pune, is a global leader in providing innovative and high-performance components across various sectors, including automotive, aerospace, and defence. With a strong global manufacturing presence, the company is instrumental in supporting India’s strategic industrial and defence objectives. Kalyani Strategic Systems Limited, a subsidiary of Bharat Forge, is a key player in the defence sector, offering a wide range of products and fostering technology partnerships to build resilient defence capabilities. The visit of the Union Home Minister highlights Bharat Forge’s significant role in India’s industrial and defence landscape, reinforcing its position as a trusted partner in the global defence sector.
