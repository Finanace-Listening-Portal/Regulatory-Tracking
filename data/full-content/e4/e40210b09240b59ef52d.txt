Articles 1 min read Bajaj General, Swiss Re to provide global cover for companies Swiss Re and Bajaj General Insurance have teamed up to provide insurance solutions on a global scale. This collaboration focuses on assisting Indian businesses that are expanding internationally and multinational firms looking to establish themselves in India. With the growing trend of cross-border investments, there's an increasing need for comprehensive risk management strategies. Mayur Shetty TNN Published On Sep 30, 2026 at 11:58 AM IST var news_msid = 134583943; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook MUMBAI: Swiss Re and Bajaj General Insurance have joined hands to offer global insurance solutions to Indian companies expanding overseas and multinational corporations setting up or growing their businesses in India, as rising cross-border investments increase demand for coordinated risk cover.The exclusive partnership between Swiss Re Corporate Solutions and Bajaj General Insurance will allow Indian companies to access multinational insurance programmes covering operations across more than 150 countries, while foreign companies expanding in India can access insurance solutions through Bajaj General. Advt Swiss Re Corporate Solutions CEO Ivan Gonzalez said the partnership was driven by the growing internationalisation of Indian companies and the increasing sophistication of their risk-management requirements.“As we develop our reinsurance business, we also recognise that there is a very attractive opportunity for us to focus on the middle and large corporations in India,” Gonzalez said. Swiss Re has operated in India for more than 163 years and has participated in the country’s property and casualty, life and health reinsurance markets. Gonzalez said the company had seen a growing opportunity to work directly with large Indian corporations as they expand globally.Bajaj General Insurance MD & CEO Tapan Singhel said the number of Indian companies expanding overseas had risen 40% over the past two years, making international risk management an increasingly important requirement.
