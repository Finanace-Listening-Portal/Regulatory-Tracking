The company is targeting a return on equity (RoE) of 10% by the fourth quarter of FY27, compared with 7.7% in the second quarter.The company's in-house artificial intelligence programme is beginning to deliver measurable gains in employee productivity, credit risk management and digital lending. New business lines, including digital lending and cross-selling, now account for quarterly disbursements of about Rs 4,000 crore. These developments were highlighted at the company's AI Day, according to an assessment by ICICI Securities. Advt Monthly disbursements per employee rose 33% year-on-year to Rs 27.5 lakh in September 2026, while AUM per employee increased 23% to Rs 5.1 crore. Management attributed about half of these productivity improvements to AI. The company's operations headcount has remained flat for two years despite business growth, while 634 employees without prior coding experience have built 277 applications in-house.Management attributes a 50% reduction in credit risk to its AI programme. The 90-day-plus overdue ratio in retail unsecured loans declined to 0.86% in Q2FY27 from 1.65% in Q2FY25, remaining around 0.9% over the past three quarters. Loans rejected by the company's AI models carry about five times the risk of loans taken by an average customer.RoA targetThe lender is targeting a return on assets under management (RoAUM) of about 2.5% by the end of FY27. Over the medium term, it aims to achieve RoAUM of around 3%, supported by leverage of about five times, to deliver RoE above 15%, alongside annual AUM growth of 20-25%.The company has withheld formal FY28 guidance pending clarity on the final insurance commission rules. The draft rules could affect RoA by 18-24 basis points, although management expects the net impact to be lower. It expects to provide greater clarity on FY28 performance after the final guidelines are issued.Piramal Finance plans to accelerate branch expansion from the fourth quarter of FY27, adding 100-125 retail branches, around 200 gold loan branches and about 300 rural branches. Its total branch network is expected to reach approximately 1,000 by the end of the year. Despite the expansion, management expects operating expense ratios to decline. The retail operating expense-to-AUM ratio stood at 3.48%. Advt A recognised tax shield on around Rs 16,000 crore of future profits is expected to keep the company's tax rate low over the next three to four years. Capital released from its investment book is also expected to flow into the lending business over the next 18-20 months.
