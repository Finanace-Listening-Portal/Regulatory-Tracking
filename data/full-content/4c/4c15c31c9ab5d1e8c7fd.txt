The initial offering under this platform is the H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund, which aims to provide investors with a novel investment strategy that combines equity and debt securities, including limited short exposure through derivatives. This New Fund Offer (NFO) opens on 13 November 2026 and will close on 27 November 2026. The H-SIF platform is designed to leverage HDFC Mutual Fund’s extensive experience of over 25 years in the financial market, serving a significant investor base of 1.75 crore unique investors. The fund house’s entry into the SIF space comes amidst a backdrop of market volatility, with the Indian equity markets experiencing notable fluctuations throughout 2026. This volatility has prompted investors to seek alternatives that offer risk management alongside market participation, a demand reflected in the rapid growth of the SIF category, which saw assets rise from ₹2,010 crore in October 2025 to ₹38,287 crore by September 2026. Navneet Munot, Managing Director and CEO of HDFC Asset Management Company, highlighted the trust investors have placed in HDFC Mutual Fund over the years. He emphasised that the launch of H-SIF is aligned with the evolving needs of investors, offering sophisticated strategies within a transparent and regulated framework. The H-SIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund is characterised by its Arbitrage+ strategy, which combines a stable core of high-safety debt papers, arbitrage, and real estate investment trusts (REITs)/infrastructure investment trusts (InvITs), supplemented by derivative strategies to manage risk and capture opportunities. Paul S Parampreet, Fund Manager for Equities at HDFC Asset Management Company, explained that the fund’s strategy is designed to navigate volatile markets by not relying on a single source of return. The approach focuses on generating returns from interest income, arbitrage spreads, option premiums, and special situations, making it suitable for flat, mildly rising, and volatile markets. The strategy is managed by a team of experienced professionals, including Mr. Parampreet, Mr. Ihab Dalwai (Equity), Mr. Anil Bamboli (Debt), and Mr. Gopal Agrawal (Overseas Investments). The minimum investment for the strategy is ₹10 lakh across SIF strategies at the PAN level, with accredited investors having the option to invest a minimum of ₹10,000. The fund will allow daily subscriptions and bi-weekly redemptions, with an exit load of 0.5% applicable for units redeemed or switched out within 90 days of allotment. As the market continues to evolve, analysts suggest that HDFC Mutual Fund’s strategic entry into the SIF space could cater to the growing demand for diversified and risk-managed investment options.
