Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Cosmic Ferro Alloys Limited Vs Commissioner of CGST & Excise (CESTAT Kolkata) Summary: CESTAT Kolkata dismissed the service tax appeal filed by Cosmic Ferro Alloys Limited against the Commissioner of CGST & Excise as infructuous following the initiation of insolvency proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC). Upon examination of the records and after hearing both parties, the Tribunal noted that the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench, had passed an order dated 11.10.2018 in CP (IB) No. 596/KB/2017, along with connected applications, initiating insolvency proceedings against the appellant and granting a moratorium. The Tribunal further observed that the NCLT had appointed an Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) for the corporate debtor to discharge the functions prescribed under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. A copy of the aforesaid NCLT order was also placed on record before CESTAT. Taking into consideration the moratorium granted to the appellant pursuant to the insolvency proceedings, the Tribunal concluded that the present proceedings against the appellant did not survive in the circumstances of the case. Consequently, CESTAT Kolkata held that the service tax appeal filed by Cosmic Ferro Alloys Limited had become infructuous and accordingly dismissed the same. The Tribunal disposed of the appeal solely on account of the insolvency proceedings and the moratorium granted by the NCLT, without examining or adjudicating the merits of the underlying service tax dispute. The order was dictated and pronounced in the open court. FULL TEXT OF THE ORIGINAL JUDGMENT/ORDER On perusal of records and after hearing both sides, we find that the insolvency proceedings had been initiated against the appellant herein in terms of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (hereinafter referred to as “the Code”) and thereafter, the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench has passed an Order dated 11.10.2018 in CP (IB) No.596/KB/2017 along with CA(IB) Nos.808,811,472,562/KB/2018 and CA(IB) No.898/KB/2018 providing a moratorium under Section 7 of the Code, thereby also appointing an Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) of the Corporate Debtor to carry out the functions as per the Code. A copy of the aforesaid order passed by the NCLT has also been placed on record. 2. We find that in terms of the above Order dated 11.10.2018 passed by the NCLT, Kolkata Bench, a moratorium has been provided to the appellant in terms of Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. In these circumstances, the present proceedings against the appellant do not survive. 3. In view of the above, the appeal filed by the appellant has become infructuous and hence, the same is dismissed.
