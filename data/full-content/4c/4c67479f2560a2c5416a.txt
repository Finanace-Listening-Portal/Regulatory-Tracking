(Calcutta High Court) Summary : Ramkrishna Datta challenged the adjudication order dated 28 February 2024 under Section 73 of the West Bengal Goods and Services Tax Act and the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. The petitioner submitted that the show cause notice dated 9 November 2023 in Form GST DRC-01 demanded tax, interest and penalty and appeared under the GST portal’s ‘Additional Notice and Orders’ tab. He attributed his late reply to the manner of upload and submitted that the notice fixed the personal hearing before the date for replying. He argued that respondent No. 3 gave him no opportunity of personal hearing after receiving his reply. The State opposed the writ petition, maintaining that the petitioner had ample opportunities to defend his case and that the adjudication order was rightly passed. The Calcutta High Court found that the show cause notice had been uploaded only under the additional notices tab without separate intimation. The petitioner replied after the due date, and respondent No. 3 failed to afford a personal hearing after receiving that reply, as required under Section 75(4). The Court treated this omission as a breach of natural justice and also found a breach in the upload of the ex parte adjudication order under the ‘Additional Notices and orders’ tab. The Court quashed the adjudication order and the recovery notice dated 19 August 2026. It directed the petitioner to file an additional reply to the existing show cause notice within two weeks. The authority was to consider the reply, afford a hearing and pass a fresh reasoned and speaking order within four weeks of receiving the reply, then communicate its decision within one week. The Court disposed of the writ petition. On 14 October 2026, it directed deletion of the statement in paragraph 3 that the petitioner’s appeal had been dismissed on limitation and replacement of the corresponding statement in paragraph 5 with the State’s submission that the adjudication order had been rightly passed. Appellant Was Represented by: Adv. Himangshu Kumar Ray, Saptak Sanyal Adv. Subhasis Podder, Adv. Shiwani Shaw, Adv. Subhasis Podder FULL TEXT OF THE JUDGMENT/ORDER OF CALCUTTA HIGH COURT 1. Affidavit of service filed in Court today be kept with the record. 2. Mr. Berlia, learned Additional Government Pleader with Ms. Dudhoria, learned Government Advocate are engaged in this matter on behalf of the State. Their appearances be regularized accordingly from the office of the Legal Remembrancer. 3. The present petition has been filed challenging inter alia, the legality and validity of the adjudication order dated 28.02.2024 passed by the respondent No.3 under Section 73 of the West Bengal Goods and Services Tax Act(hereinafter referred to as the said ‘WBGST Act’) and the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017(hereinafter referred to as the said ‘CGST Act’), whereby the appeal of the petitioner has been dismissed on ground of limitation. 4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits as follows; i. That a show cause notice in Form DRC 01 dated 09.11.2023 has been issued to the petitioner demanding tax, interest and penalty. ii. That said notice has been uploaded on the GST portal under the tab ‘Additional Notice and Orders’. Due to this, the petitioner submitted his reply after the due date. iii. That in the show cause notice, the date of personal hearing was mentioned before the date of reply. Further, even after submission of reply, the respondent no. 3 did not provide any opportunity of personal hearing before passing the adverse order, as mandated under Section 75(4) of the GST Act. This constitutes a violation of the principles of natural justice. iv. That the entire proceedings and the impugned order are contrary to law and have been passed in violation of principles of natural justice and without due compliance with the procedure prescribed under the said Act. 5. Learned counsel appearing for the State respondents opposed the writ petition and submitted since the petitioner has been granted ample opportunities to defend his case. The appeal has been rightly dismissed on limitation. 6. Having heard the parties and upon perusing the records made available this Court observes as follows; i. The petitioner has been able to make a prima facie case. ii. The fact that show cause notice has been only uploaded under the ‘tab Additional Notice and Orders’ and no separate intimation has been given. The petitioner submitted reply after the due date. iii. After receipt of the reply, the respondent no. 3 did not provide an opportunity of personal hearing as per Section 75(4) of the GST Act. This constitutes a violation of the principles of natural justice. iv. The ex-parte adjudication order was uploaded in “Additional Notices and orders,” tab. This constitutes a violation of natural justice. 7. In view of the above this Court directs as follows: a. The adjudication order dated 28.02.2024, recovery notice dated 19.08.2026 are hereby quashed and set aside. b. The petitioner is directed to file an additional reply to the show cause notice dated 09.11.2023 before the concerned authority within two weeks from date. Such reply shall be considered within four weeks from the date of receipt of the reply by passing a fresh reasoned and speaking order in accordance with law upon affording an opportunity of hearing to the petitioner. Such decision shall be communicated to the petitioner within a week thereafter. 8. With the above observations and directions this writ petition stands disposed of. 9. Since no affidavit in opposition has been called for, the allegations contained in the writ petition are deemed to have been denied and not admitted. 10. Urgent Photostat certified copy of this order be supplied to the parties, if applied for, as early as possible. 1. The matter has appeared in the list under the heading “To Be Mentioned‘ for correction of the order dated 6.10.2026 2. In the 9th and 10th line of the para no.3, the words “ whereby the appeal of the petitioner has been dismissed on ground of limitation” should be deleted. 3. In the 4th and fifth line of the para no. 5, the words “ the appeal has been rightly dismissed on limitation” should be deleted. The words “ the order of adjudication has been rightly passed” should be incorporated. 4. Other portions of the order dated 6.10.2026 shall remain same. 5. Department is directed to take steps accordingly.
