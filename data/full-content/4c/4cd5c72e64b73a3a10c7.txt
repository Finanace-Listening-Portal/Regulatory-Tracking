National Stock Exchange of India Circular Department: LISTING Download Ref No: NSE/CML/2023/03 Date: January 06, 2023 To All Members, Subject: Filing of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 disclosures to Exchange This is in continuation to Exchange Circular no NSE/CML/2022/03 dated January 06, 2022, and Circular Ref. No: NSE/CML/2022/09 dated February 17, 2022, informing about the launch of New Digital Portal which was made live for filings with the Exchange. With reference to above filing of disclosure under SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 will now required to be filed on Takeover Email ID (takeover@nse.co.in) with effect from January 07, 2023. Contact details of the Exchange Officers are available on the NEAPS portal and the companies can get in touch with the respective officers for issues and queries pertaining to filings of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Yours faithfully For National Stock Exchange of India Limited Yogesh Deshmukh Senior Manager
