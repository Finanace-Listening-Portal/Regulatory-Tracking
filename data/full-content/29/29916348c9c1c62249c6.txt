Advt "Softer oil prices and a more benign US rate outlook could allow the RBI to pause after another 25 bps, with a further 25 bps only if necessary, potentially triggering a bond rally."India could be at a rare inflection point, with both equities and bonds poised to benefit over the next months, he added.The Indian rupee strengthened past the 93-per-dollar mark on Friday for the first time since April, and gained 1.7% for the week, as equities attracted investors fleeing AI-driven markets.Foreigners have poured $12 billion into Indian shares so far in November, the largest-ever monthly inflow.US Treasury yields also gave up some of their declines from the previous week as a selloff in US equities prompted investors to move into safe-haven government bonds.The decline in yields also comes as traders are increasingly pricing in the possibility that the Federal Reserve will take a more measured approach at its December meeting.Benchmark Brent crude also eased to $92 per barrel. Moves in oil prices are critical for import-dependent India as they impact the nation's current account deficit, local currency and inflation trajectory.The RBI's next policy decision is due on December 4, and a majority of economists in a Reuters poll expect a 25 bps hike.RATESOvernight index swap rates also slumped, with the key one-year and five-year points inverting for the first time since May 2025. Advt The one-year rate ended at 6.47%, while the two-year rate closed at 6.43%.
