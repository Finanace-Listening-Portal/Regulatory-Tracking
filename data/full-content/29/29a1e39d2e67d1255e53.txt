The notification has been issued in exercise of powers conferred under Sections 3 and 5 of the Foreign Trade (Development & Regulation) Act, 1992, read with paragraphs 1.02 and 2.01 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, and in continuation of Notification No. 17/2015-2020 dated 9th July, 2020. Under the existing policy condition, imports of cut flowers were permitted exclusively through Chennai Airport. The revised policy condition permits imports through Chennai Airport, Amritsar Airport and Kandla Seaport only, subject to compliance with applicable Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) requirements, including mandatory plant quarantine inspection, testing and certification under the Plant Quarantine (Regulation of Import into India) Order, 2003, as amended. The amendment covers roses, carnations, orchids, chrysanthemums, lilies and other cut flowers and flower buds falling under HS Code 0603. These include flowers suitable for bouquets or ornamental purposes, whether fresh, dried, dyed, bleached, impregnated or otherwise prepared. The import policy classification continues to remain ‘Free’, while the permitted points of entry have been expanded from one to three. Imports remain subject to the specified entry-point restrictions and applicable plant quarantine requirements. The notification has been issued with the approval of the Minister of Commerce and Industry.
