Articles 2 min read Sammaan Capital Q2 net inches up 1.25 pc to Rs 312.34 cr PTI Published On Nov 14, 2026 at 04:08 PM IST var news_msid = 135830229; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai, Non-bank lender Sammaan Capital on Saturday reported a 1.25 per cent increase in its September quarter net profit to Rs 312.34 crore compared to Rs 308.47 crore in the same quarter of last year.The company had reported a net profit of Rs 243.30 crore in the preceding June quarter.Its total income declined to Rs 1,850.85 crore in the reporting quarter from Rs 2,260.52 crore in the year-ago period, which included a decline in the core interest income to Rs 1,161.26 crore from Rs 1,449.02 crore.Total expenses also declined to Rs 1,426.28 crore from Rs 1,835.49 crore in the year-ago period, as per a regulatory filing. Advt Assets under management grew by 5 per cent to Rs 59,188 crore as of end-June 2026, driven by a 62 per cent jump in disbursements to Rs 6,278 crore. It aims to increase AUM to Rs 70,000 crore by March 2027.The company added more branches during the quarter to take its overall network to 245 as of September 30, and is aiming to take the same to 270 by end of the fiscal, as per an official statement.From a product perspective, it is transitioning from being a portfolio buyer in the personal loan category to originating personal loans, and is also launching business loans.The company is also aiming for a soft launch of the gold loan business by December and a commercial launch by end-March 2027, the statement said.It has hired 1,123 people in April-September 2026 to take its manpower to 4,552 as of the quarter-end, and another 251 are pending joinees who have accepted offers but are yet to come over.On the asset quality front, the gross non-performing assets ratio declined by 0.06 per cent on-quarter to 0.15 per cent as of September 30."Our focus remains on building a diversified and resilient lending franchise, supported by disciplined underwriting, strong collections and a robust capital position," its managing director Gagan Banga said.The capital adequacy for the lender, which recently received a strong infusion from UAE's IHC, stood at 20 per cent as of September 30.
