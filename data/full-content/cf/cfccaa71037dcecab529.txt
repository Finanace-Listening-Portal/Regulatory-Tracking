The Board of Directors approved the reviewed standalone and consolidated financial results, highlighting the company’s strategic positioning in the life insurance sector. The company reported a 12% increase in the value of new business (VNB), amounting to ₹2,037 crore for the first half of the fiscal year 2027. This growth outpaced the annualised premium equivalent (APE), which saw an 11% rise. The new business margin stood at 24.7%, reflecting the company’s focus on profitability and efficient operations. Assets under management (AUM) also experienced a significant increase, reaching ₹4.1 trillion, marking a 13.8% year-on-year growth. Vibha Padalkar, Managing Director and CEO of HDFC Life, noted the favourable macroeconomic conditions, with the Reserve Bank of India revising its growth projection for FY2027 to 7.1%. Despite challenges such as elevated energy prices and a below-normal monsoon, the company remains optimistic about sustaining its growth trajectory. The individual weighted received premium (WRP) grew by 11% in Q2, surpassing the private industry growth of 10%, and the company’s market share improved to 16.2% from 16.1% in the previous quarter. Niraj Shah, Executive Director and CFO, highlighted the company’s strong financial performance, with a profit after tax of ₹1,119 crore, reflecting a 12.7% increase. The embedded value stood at ₹66,461 crore, with a rolling 12-month operating return on embedded value of 14.9%. The solvency ratio remained healthy at 180%, underscoring the company’s financial stability and resilience. The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) has released consultation papers proposing significant changes in the insurance distribution landscape. HDFC Life is actively engaging with the regulator, focusing on policyholder interests. Analysts view HDFC Life’s performance as indicative of its strong market position and ability to navigate regulatory changes, positioning it well for future growth in the evolving insurance sector.
