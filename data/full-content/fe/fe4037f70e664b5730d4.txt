Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); PCIT Vs Vaibhav Global Limited (Rajasthan High Court) Summary:The Rajasthan High Court considered the Revenue’s appeal under Section 260A of the Income-tax Act, 1961, challenging the Income Tax Appellate Tribunal’s decision on two transfer pricing adjustments relating to Vaibhav Global Limited. The first concerned the Transfer Pricing Officer’s adjustment exceeding ₹82 crore towards interest payable but not recovered, which the Tribunal had modified by applying a different interest rate. The second concerned the Tribunal’s reduction of the arm’s length corporate guarantee fee to 0.53%. The Revenue contended that both modifications were erroneous and warranted interference by the High Court. The High Court observed that the arm’s length price determinations underlying both adjustments had already been considered in earlier proceedings, in which the ITAT had decided in favour of the assessee and the Revenue’s subsequent appeal before the High Court had been unsuccessful. Regarding interest on amounts utilised by associated enterprises abroad, the Court held that applying LIBOR-based rates was reasonable, since the funds were used outside India and the appropriate benchmark was therefore linked to LIBOR. Concerning the corporate guarantee fee, the Court held that the mere reduction of the arm’s length rate to 0.53% did not, by itself, raise a substantial question of law. Accordingly, the Rajasthan High Court found no merit in the Revenue’s objections to the Tribunal’s transfer pricing determinations. It declined to interfere with the application of LIBOR-based interest rates or the reduced corporate guarantee fee, holding that the Revenue had failed to establish grounds warranting appellate interference under Section 260A. The Revenue’s appeal was dismissed, leaving the Tribunal’s decision undisturbed. Cases Discussed Vaibhav Global Limited v. ACIT — ITA Nos. 730 & 731/JP/2018, ITAT Jaipur, order dated 19 December 2018. Related proceedings concerning transfer pricing adjustments on interest-free loans to overseas associated enterprises and application of LIBOR-based rates. The order discusses earlier decisions in the assessee’s own case. Vaibhav Global Limited v. DCIT — IT(TP)A No. 01/JP/2019, ITAT Jaipur, order dated 14 November 2019. Subsequent related proceedings applying the corporate guarantee commission rate of 0.53% in the assessee’s own case. This decision postdates the principal High Court judgment and is not a precedent cited in that judgment. FULL TEXT OF THE JUDGMENT/ORDER OF RAJASTHAN HIGH COURT The revenue its appeal under Section 260-A of the Income Tax Act urges two questions of law and contends that the ITAT fell into error. Firstly it is urged that the Transfer Pricing Officer’s (TPO’s) order directing adjustment of over 82 crore towards interest payable but not recovered, was erroneously modified so far as rate is concerned. The second question urged is that the reduction of the corporate guarantee fee to 0.53% was not justified. This court notices that ALP determination which led to adjustments on both counts, were the subject matter of previous appeal decided in its favour by the ITAT. Further appeal to this court by the revenue was unsuccessful. Moreover the reduction of ALP determination on interest, in this court’s opinion, by applying LIBOR rates, is reasonable because the amounts were used by the AE Abroad and the yardstick, therefore, was naturally as per LIBOR rates. Likewise, as far as the ALP determination of the cooperate guarantee fee, mere reduction per se does not result in a question of law, as is urged in this appeal. For the above reason, this court is of the opinion that there is no merit in the appeal; it is accordingly dismissed.
