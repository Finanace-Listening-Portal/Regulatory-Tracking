The insurer also expects to be among the least-impacted insurers, with the attachment rate for credit-life insurance on SBI loans currently below 50% and limited credit-life partnerships outside SBI.SBI Life will recalibrate its product offerings, operating expenses and commission structure once the guidelines are finalised, while viewing the IRDAI consultation paper as a constructive step towards strengthening the insurance ecosystem, improving transparency and enhancing policyholder outcomes, according to Axis Securities. Advt The insurer retained its FY27 individual rated premium growth guidance of 14-15%, despite relatively subdued growth in the second quarter following stronger performance in the first quarter. IRP grew 12% year-on-year in H1, with management expecting the seasonally stronger third quarter to support growth.SBI Life's VNB margin improved sharply on a favourable shift in product mix towards non-PAR savings and annuity products, along with a relatively lower contribution from group and ULIP business. VNB margin stood at 29.2% in Q2FY27, compared with 27.9% in the previous quarter and 26.2% in the comparable period, according to the report. Management retained its FY27 VNB margin guidance of 26-28%.Yield-curve movements also contributed to the margin expansion, with a positive impact of around 70 basis points reflected in H1. While higher yields have supported margins, SBI Life plans to pass on part of the benefit to customers through periodic product repricing.Q2 performanceGross premium stood at Rs 271.2 billion in Q2FY27, up 8% year-on-year and 27% quarter-on-quarter. GWP growth was driven by 9% growth in first-year premium and 13% growth in renewal premium, while single premium declined 3% year-on-year. NBP grew 27% year-on-year and 2% quarter-on-quarter.APE stood at Rs 63.7 billion in Q2FY27, rising 7% year-on-year and 18% quarter-on-quarter, while VNB stood at Rs 18.6 billion. Embedded value increased 15% year-on-year and 3% quarter-on-quarter to Rs 873.5 billion. Advt The distribution mix continued to shift in H1, with bancassurance accounting for 53% of APE, down from 57% a year earlier. Agency accounted for 28% against 29% a year earlier, while other channels increased their share to 19% from 14%. Agency APE grew 14% year-on-year, compared with 9% growth in the bancassurance channel.Protection business also recorded strong growth, with protection NBP rising to Rs 20.3 billion in H1 from Rs 10.6 billion a year earlier, taking its mix to 17% of total APE. Persistency improved across the 13th, 25th and 49th months by 44 basis points, 110 basis points and 40 basis points, respectively. Advt SBI Life's improving product mix, expanding agency and non-SBI distribution network and strong bancassurance channel are expected to support its VNB growth trajectory. The company has retained its FY27 guidance of 26-28% for VNB margin and 14-15% for IRP growth, while the expected limited impact of the IRDAI consultation paper reduces the risk of material disruption to its business model.
