The currency gained 1%, its biggest jump since April, to 95.7575 per dollar on Monday. One-month dollar-rupee non-deliverable forwards slid as much as 0.8% earlier. Bond fell, with benchmark 10-year yield rising five basis points to 7.35%, the highest level since November 2023. The Reserve Bank of India on Saturday unveiled measures to prop up the currency after it neared a record low of 97 per dollar. The steps include a special window to meet state-run oil companies’ dollar needs and a reserve requirement on certain foreign-exchange derivatives. The RBI had raised interest rates Wednesday and signaled it was open to further tightening.
