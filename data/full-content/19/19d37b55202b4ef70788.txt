The extension relates to the regulatory amendment introduced through Notification S.O. 2204(E) dated 5 May 2026, which substituted the entry “USB Type External Hard Disk Drives” with “Standalone Hard Disk Drives” at Serial No. 50 of the Schedule to the Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirement of Compulsory Registration) Order, 2021. The applicable safety standard remains IS 13252: Part 1: 2010. The notification has been issued under Sections 16(1), 16(2) and 25(3) of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016. The extension provides additional time for manufacturers, importers and other affected stakeholders to prepare for the expanded compulsory registration requirements. The original amendment distinguished USB Type External Hard Disk Drives, which continue to be governed by existing notified provisions, from other standalone HDDs covered by the revised implementation timeline. The related CBIC Instruction No. 09/2026-Customs dated 12 June 2026 had earlier informed Customs field formations about the amendment. The present notification changes the implementation date to 1 May 2027 without specifying any alteration to the prescribed Indian Standard or product description. MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY NOTIFICATION New Delhi, the 16th October, 2026 Subject: Extension in timelines for the implementation of the order for Standalone Hard Disk Drives included in the schedule of CRO vide S.O. 2204(E) dated 05.05.2026 published in Gazette of India S.O. 5689(E).— Whereas in exercise of the powers conferred sub-section (1) and (2) of section 16 read with sub section (3) of section 25 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, (11 of 2016), the Central Government, vide S.O. 2204(E) dated 5th May 2026, published in Gazette of India, had substituted “USB Type External Hard Disk Drives” with “Standalone Hard Disk Drives” at Sl. No 50 of the Schedule of the “Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirement of Compulsory Registration) Order, 2021” for compliance to IS 13252 : Part 1 : 2010. The date of coming into effect of the Order was 05th November, 2026. It is hereby notified that the date of implementation of the Order notified vide S.O. 2204(E) published in Gazette of India on 5th May, 2026 stands extended to 1st May, 2027. [F.
