The due diligence for new customers in GIFT City typically takes 10 to 12 days, though that period is expected to shorten as know-your-customer requirements are being streamlined. Assisted video KYC is already available to NRIs, while unassisted face authentication via a biometric system is expected to be enabled by the end of 2026, he said.
