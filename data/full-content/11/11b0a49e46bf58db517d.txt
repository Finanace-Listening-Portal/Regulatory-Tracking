What we call attack surface area is increasing. And the security and digital adoption need not be mutually exclusive," said Setty. He said govt, public sector institutions and private entities were collectively working to ensure that cyber incidents did not undermine trust in digital systems. Advt IBA had also developed a comprehensive framework to address AI-related cybersecurity concerns. However, Setty said, a key priority was to strengthen information sharing across banks and financial institutions.While incidents were already being reported to regulators and security agencies, a structured mechanism to alert other banks as soon as an incident occurred would enable them to take preventive measures immediately. "This is something that we are seriously working under the IBA," he said.The other initiative involves creating an industry-level sandbox where frontier AI models can be tested in a regulated and controlled environment.
