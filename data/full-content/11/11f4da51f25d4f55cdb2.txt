Articles 3 min read Small finance banks offer up to 8.25% on FDs, 8.60% for senior citizens Suryoday, Ujjivan, Utkarsh, Unity, and ESAF emerge as the top five small finance banks for fixed deposits, giving general investors up to 8.25% and senior citizens up to 8.60% on select tenures. Utkarsh Small Finance Bank and Jana Small Finance Bank are among the institutions that recently revised their interest rates starting in November. The revised rates came into effect on October 24. Advt Utkarsh Small Finance Bank, which revised its rates from November 1, is offering 8.10% to general depositors and 8.50% to senior citizens on a special 666-day deposit.Among other small finance banks, Unity Small Finance Bank and ESAF Small Finance Bank are offering 8% and 8.50%, respectively, to general depositors and senior citizens. The applicable tenures are 501 days at Unity SFB and 800 days at ESAF SFB.Jana Small Finance Bank is offering 8% to general depositors and 8.30% to senior citizens on three-year deposits, with its revised rates effective November 6. Shivalik Small Finance Bank offers 8.05% to general depositors and 8.30% to senior citizens on deposits ranging from 23 months and one day to 27 months.Equitas Small Finance Bank offers 8% to general depositors and 8.24% to senior citizens on a three-year-and-one-day deposit. Meanwhile, slice Small Finance Bank offers 7.75% across its 18-month-and-one-day to 24-month deposit tenure.AU Small Finance Bank offers 7.40% to general depositors and 7.90% to senior citizens on deposits ranging from 30 months and one day to 36 months.Bank rate disparityAmong private sector banks, Kotak Mahindra Bank, following its November 4 revision, offers a maximum of 6.80% to general depositors and 7.55% to senior citizens on deposits with tenures of two years to less than three years. Advt Axis Bank's highest fixed deposit rate stands at 6.60% for general depositors and 7.35% for senior citizens. Canara Bank offers up to 6.75% and 7.25%, respectively, on its special 555-day deposit.HDFC Bank, which revised select fixed deposit rates by up to 50 basis points from November 4, offers a maximum of 6.60% to general depositors. Its highest rate for senior citizens stands at 7.20%. ICICI Bank also offers up to 6.50% to general depositors and 7.20% to senior citizens on select tenures.Punjab National Bank offers a maximum of 7.10% to senior citizens.The rate differential is particularly pronounced for general depositors. Suryoday SFB's peak rate of 8.25% is 145 basis points higher than Kotak Mahindra Bank's maximum of 6.80%, and 165 basis points higher than HDFC Bank's 6.60%. Advt For senior citizens, the difference between the highest SFB rate of 8.60% and Kotak Mahindra Bank's 7.55% stands at 105 basis points.
