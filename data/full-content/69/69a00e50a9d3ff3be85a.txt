This approval, granted via the 505(b)(2) pathway, allows the use of the injection as a single-dose regimen for the treatment of acute bacterial skin and skin structure infections (ABSSSI) in both adult and pediatric patients. The Dalbavancin Injection developed by Lupin is a liquid formulation of the active ingredient found in Dalvance® by AbbVie. It is designed to simplify preparation by providing a ready-to-dilute solution. The single-dose regimen is indicated for infections caused by designated susceptible strains of Gram-positive microorganisms, offering a streamlined treatment option for healthcare providers. However, the use of Dalbavancin Injection is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to the drug. The company has issued warnings regarding potential hypersensitivity reactions, infusion-related reactions, hepatic effects, and the risk of Clostridioides difficile infection. Patients are advised to monitor for adverse reactions, including nausea, headache, and diarrhea, which have been observed in more than 4% of adult patients treated with the injection. Lupin Limited, headquartered in Mumbai, India, is renowned for its diversified portfolio that includes generics, complex generics, specialty medicines, and biosimilars. The company maintains a strong presence in the U.S. and India across core therapeutic areas such as cardiovascular, respiratory, diabetes, gastrointestinal, and women’s health. With 15 manufacturing facilities and six research centres, Lupin continues to expand its market position and sustain operations globally. The approval of Dalbavancin Injection is expected to strengthen Lupin’s market presence in the U.S., a key market for the company. Analysts suggest that this development could enhance Lupin’s competitive edge in the pharmaceutical industry, particularly in the area of infectious disease treatment. As the company continues to innovate and expand its product offerings, it remains committed to addressing the evolving needs of the healthcare ecosystem.
