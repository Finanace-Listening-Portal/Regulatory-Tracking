Articles 3 min read RBI’s 4th data centre in Bhubaneswar to improve uptime, reduce downtime of fin services The Reserve Bank of India’s new data centre in Bhubaneswar will strengthen the resilience of India’s financial system, improve uptime and reduce downtime of critical services, Governor Sanjay Malhotra said. The facility will house mission-critical payment and settlement systems, while a cybersecurity institute will build capabilities in emerging technologies. The accompanying Cybersecurity Training Institute will build expertise in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies. Advt The Governor said the new facility has the highest Tier IV accreditation from the Uptime Institute and will have four independent data centres, which will provide greater strength and resilience.The infrastructure will support RBI’s payment systems, including NEFT and RTGS, which operate round the clock. While UPI is operated by NPCI, its final settlement takes place through RBI’s data centres and applications, Malhotra said.The facility will also support RBI’s e-Kuber platform, through which the central bank conducts banking operations with the central and state governments and banks. Other applications, including those used by regulated entities for licences and approvals, will also be hosted at the new centre.Malhotra said the new facility would help provide better-quality and more efficient services, while “improving our uptime”. It would also reduce downtime and enhance the resilience of the overall financial system, he said.The choice of Bhubaneswar also has a resilience component. Malhotra said RBI already has three data centres in Maharashtra, while Bhubaneswar is geographically distant from them and is located in a different seismic zone.The availability of talent in the city was another factor behind the choice, he said.The Governor also said RBI does not depend on third-party cloud service providers for the infrastructure, instead operating its own data centre. The sovereign nature of the infrastructure, he said, would add to the RBI’s strength and resilience. Advt The facility is spread across 18.55 acres at Info Valley-II in Jatni near Bhubaneswar and was inaugurated along with the Cybersecurity Training Institute.Cybersecurity institute to train banking, financial sector personnelTwo-thirds of the premises has been allocated to the Cybersecurity Training Institute, while one-third is allocated to the data centre, Malhotra said.The institute will not be restricted to RBI personnel. It will also train employees of banks and other financial-sector organisations on emerging technologies and cybersecurity.The Governor said the institute will focus on areas including AI, big data analytics, quantum technology and blockchain, with the aim of keeping personnel abreast of the latest technological developments. Advt “Cyber security is increasingly becoming important especially in the wake of AI,” Malhotra said, adding that the institute would also undertake research to help RBI stay ahead in making financial systems more robust, secure and efficient.
