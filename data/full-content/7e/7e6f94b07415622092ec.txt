} } Listen to This ArticleIIFL Finance seeks to strengthen itself in the secured lending market by sharpening focus on gold loans and small business credit, says Nirmal Jain, founder and managing director of the non-banking financial company. The lender expects the ₹6,414-crore capital raise announced on Tuesday— led by Blackstone and backed by promoters — will bolster its balance sheet, lower borrowing costs and assist growth, Jain tells Subrata Panda in an email interview. Edited excerpts: How will the capital infusion be deployed? Are you considering entering new business segments or markets? About 70 per cent of the ₹6,414 crore will go into new lending, mainly gold loans and secured business loans. The remaining 30 per cent will repay debt, which lowers our gearing and strengthens our hand in negotiating terms with lenders. We are not looking at new segments. Gold, housing and secured small-business credit, primarily loans against property, are among the most under-penetrated markets in India. We already have more than 5,000 branches, the technology and the teams to serve them. This capital lets us grow faster in businesses we know well and already lead in. How will the capital infusion help growth? What are your assets under management (AUM) targets for the next three to five years? According to September 2026 numbers, standalone capital adequacy rose from 17 per cent to about 30 per cent and net gearing [a measure of financial leverage] fell from 4.6x to 2.8x. That gives us several years of growth capital. A substantial jump in Tier-I capital, lower gross and net non-performing assets, a book secured mainly by gold and mortgages, a strong management team and a marquee investor together make a strong case for a rating upgrade. Every 25 basis points saved on borrowings is worth about ₹200 crore a year. At the start of FY27, we guided for about 25 per cent AUM growth, a 3.1–3.3 per cent return on assets and a 16–18 per cent return on equity. Given the first-half trend and the capital raise, we should do much better than that guidance. Consolidated AUM is ₹1.24 trillion today. At 25 per cent a year it roughly doubles in three years, and we see that pace as sustainable. Our gold loan book alone is on track to reach ₹1 trillion in less than two years from now. How will Blackstone’s entry change IIFL Finance’s board composition and governance structure? Blackstone will nominate two directors to our board. They bring global best practices in risk, governance and operations, along with a deep understanding of India and substantial experience of investing in the financial sector. Management and the promoters continue, and so does our entrepreneurial, execution-focused culture. The partnership raises the bar on governance and performance. It does not change who we are. Blackstone's board representation will strengthen oversight further. If and when the RBI classifies us in the upper layer, the transition will be seamless. Fairfax will need to exit its stake in IIFL Finance. How will this happen? Is Blackstone considering increasing its stake to acquire Fairfax’s holding? Fairfax has been a valued, supportive long-term shareholder, and we are grateful for that partnership. Any decision on its holding is for Fairfax to take. It will not be appropriate for us to speculate. I do not think Blackstone will acquire Fairfax’s stake. They see themselves as a non-controlling partner with a 15 to 16 per cent holding. Are you exploring a stake sale in IIFL Samasta, the microfinance arm, to bring in a strategic investor? IIFL Samasta has 26 per cent capital adequacy and does not need capital. Our focus there is on asset quality and returns. Quality franchises always attract investor interest, and we will always evaluate what creates value for shareholders. But there is no decision and nothing to announce. What is your growth strategy for 5paisa, the digital financial services platform? Are you looking to bring in a strategic investor? Trading is 5paisa’s DNA. We are investing seriously in a full-featured trading platform, AI-driven research and stronger marketing to win active traders. Alongside that, we are building a low-cost, direct-plan investment platform to bring long-term SIP investors from Tier-II, -III and -IV cities into the market. Over time, this extends into digital, AI-led wealth management for India's emerging investor class. After this year’s rights issue, capital is not a constraint for 5paisa. Our priorities are execution and strengthening the CXO team, and the Trendlyne acquisition adds complementary capabilities. We are open to strategic partnerships at the right time that multiply what we can do. Are you considering expanding into other financial services businesses, including insurance? Our focus is secured retail lending. Insurance is a cross-sell product for us, and we do not envisage allocating any capital to it. We do not need new businesses to compound. Gold, housing and MSME credit can keep us busy in the foreseeable future. What is your outlook for the gold loan business amid concerns about rising delinquencies and asset quality? Our gold loan AUM crossed ₹65,000 crore in September 2026. Only 6 to 8 per cent of household gold is pledged with organised lenders, so the opportunity is enormous, and customers are moving from informal to regulated lenders. The risk is well covered. A typical 75 per cent loan-to-value (LTV) ratio already means a 33 per cent cushion on the loan value, and the real cushion is larger. Lenders value only the pure gold content, while the borrower has also paid for design and making charges, and the jewellery carries sentimental value. Ultimate default is therefore unlikely. Short-term moves in gold prices affect share prices of gold loan companies more than the business itself. A sustained fall can slow growth. Some marginal borrowers may then be unable to renew or transfer their loans within LTV limits, which can lift reported delinquency temporarily, but the loss given default stays negligible. Gold is the most liquid collateral there is; our loans are short-tenor and our LTV discipline is strict. We have overhauled our gold loan processes completely and now use AI-led branch supervision and audit.
