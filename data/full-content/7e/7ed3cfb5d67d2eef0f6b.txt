This recognition comes as part of the November 2026 update, marking the second edition of the Scorecard this year following its initial release in May. Waaree is among seven global manufacturers to have at least one model type recognised as a Top Performer across every testing category. The Kiwa PVEL Scorecard, an independent evaluation of solar module reliability and performance, named 48 manufacturers and 311 model types as Top Performers this year. Waaree’s achievement is particularly notable given the challenging testing environment, with 87% of manufacturers and 61% of bills of materials (BOMs) failing at least one test. Waaree’s recognition spans 18 module series within its BiN and WMN product families, which underwent rigorous testing to simulate decades of field exposure. This accolade follows Waaree’s earlier success in the May 2026 edition, where its M10R and G12R glass-to-glass N-type TOPCon modules were also recognised as Top Performers. This marks the fifth consecutive year that Waaree has been acknowledged in the Scorecard, underscoring the company’s consistent engineering excellence and product reliability. Jignesh Rathod, Whole-Time Director & CEO of Waaree Energies Limited, expressed pride in the company’s achievement, highlighting the significance of independent validation in reinforcing customer confidence. Rathod noted that the recognition is a testament to the quality and reliability inherent in Waaree’s solar modules, which are designed to deliver dependable performance over a 30-year lifecycle. Waaree Energies, established in 1990, continues to expand its influence in the renewable energy sector, supplying solar modules to clients across India and more than 25 countries. The company operates advanced manufacturing facilities in India and the United States, supporting its mission to drive the global energy transition with innovative and sustainable solar solutions. As the demand for reliable renewable energy sources grows, Waaree’s consistent performance in independent evaluations positions it favourably in the competitive solar industry landscape.
