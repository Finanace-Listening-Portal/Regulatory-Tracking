Advances grew 20.4% year on year in the September quarter, while the gross non-performing asset ratio fell to 1.33%, already below the bank’s full-year target of 1.50%.The bank’s net interest income rose 20% year on year and 8% sequentially, helping net profit increase 8% to Rs 5,175 crore despite an 87% decline in profit on sale of investments. The reported net interest margin improved three basis points sequentially to 2.55%, while lower provisions also supported earnings, according to JM Financial. Advt Domestic advances grew 17% year on year, led by retail, micro, small and medium enterprises, and agriculture lending. Retail advances rose about 32%, while MSME advances increased around 15%. Overseas advances surged 58% year on year and 28% sequentially, taking overall credit growth to 20.4%. Deposits grew 13% year on year, supported by the mobilisation of about $5.8 billion through FCNR(B) deposits.The faster growth in advances pushed the global credit-deposit ratio to 80.2%, up 485 basis points from a year earlier. The bank expects to maintain its net interest margin at around 2.55%, supported by loan repricing and strong retail lending margins. About 53% of its loan book has already been repriced, while replacing higher-cost bulk deposits with FCNR(B) deposits has helped reduce funding costs.Asset qualityAsset quality remained strong, with the gross NPA ratio declining 24 basis points sequentially to 1.33% and the net NPA ratio falling seven basis points to 0.30%. The calculated credit cost eased to 0.54%, while the gross slippage ratio remained broadly stable at 0.58%. The share of special mention accounts among exposures of Rs 5 crore and above declined to 0.51% from 0.58% in the June quarter, with the SMA-2 category falling to 0.21% from 0.27%.Management expects to phase the estimated one-time provision requirement under the expected credit loss framework over about two years to limit its effect on profitability and capital ratios. The bank estimates the requirement at Rs 12,000-15,000 crore, including mark-to-market adjustments on held-to-maturity investments, depending on yields at the time of transition. Management expects the impact on return on assets and credit costs to be offset over time by improving asset quality and lower slippages. Advt The bank also has a corporate lending pipeline of Rs 47,000-50,000 crore, concentrated in green energy, battery storage, power evacuation, data centres and infrastructure projects. Management expects demand for retail lending to remain strong during the festive quarter, supporting its outlook for broad-based growth.The brokerage upgraded Canara Bank to Buy and raised its target price to Rs 150 from Rs 142, citing attractive valuations and improving fundamentals. It raised its FY27 and FY28 earnings estimates by 6% and 5%, respectively, and expects the bank to deliver a return on assets of around 1% and return on equity of 17.6% over FY27-FY29. The planned ECL transition remains a key variable for the earnings outlook.
