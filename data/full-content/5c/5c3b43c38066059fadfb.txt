Articles 1 min read Olyv raises USD 6 mn from Nippon Life India AIF, others; Series B funding reaches USD 30 mn Olyv, a Bengaluru-based fintech platform, announced a new funding round raising USD 6 million from Nippon Life India AIF Management Limited. With this latest investment, the firm's total Series B funding reaches approximately USD 30 million. The company aims to expand its platform and reach underserved consumers while enhancing its technological investments and products. Advt "As we enter our next phase, we are continuing to expand the breadth of our platform, invest in technology and products, and reach the next billion hardworking and underserved consumers with greater financial access and utility," Olyv Co-Founder and CEO Rohit Garg said.Founded in 2016, Olyv offers personal loans, gold savings and credit health management products.The company has nearly 20 lakh monthly active users and serves customers across over 19,000 pin codes in India.By FY29, Olyv is targeting a user base of 10 crore and assets under management (AUM) of over USD 1 billion.Its investors also include Lightrock, Unicorn India Ventures and India SME, as well as family offices, including Gauri Khan's Family Office.
