The amendment introduces Rule 281A, requiring assessees claiming exemption under Schedule IV, Table Serial Numbers 13D and 13E, of the Income-tax Act, 2025, to furnish information in newly prescribed Form No. 173A. The form relates to exemptions on interest income and capital gains arising from government securities. Issued under Sections 11 and 533 read with Schedule IV of the Income-tax Act, 2025, the amendment establishes a reporting mechanism for taxpayers seeking the specified exemptions. The amended rules come into force from 19 October 2026, the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. Form No. 173A requires details of the assessee, including name, country of residence, address, Permanent Account Number (PAN), Tax Identification Number (TIN), SEBI registration number as an FPI/FII, tax year, email address and contact information. It also requires security-wise information, including International Securities Identification Number (ISIN), type of government securities, tenor, opening and closing investment values, purchases, sales, gross interest received or accrued, and capital gains earned during the tax year. Covered securities include Treasury Bills, fixed-rate bonds, floating-rate bonds and State Development Loans. The form contains a declaration confirming the accuracy of the information furnished. Amounts must ordinarily be reported in Indian rupees, while TIN and SEBI registration details are optional for the Bank for International Settlements (BIS). The amendment introduces a prescribed information-furnishing requirement for claiming the specified exemptions. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) (CENTRAL BOARD OF DIRECT TAXES) Notification No. 137/2026-Income Tax | Dated: 19th October, 2026 G.S.R. 901(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 533 read with section 11 and Schedule IV of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025), the Central Board of Direct Taxes hereby makes the following rules further to amend the Income-tax Rules, 2026, namely:— 1. (1) These rules may be called the Income-tax (Seventh Amendment) Rules, 2026. (2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 2. In Income-tax Rules, 2026,— (a) after rule 281, the following rule shall be inserted, namely:— “281A. Information to be furnished under Schedule IV (Table: Sl. Nos. 13D and 13E).- For the purposes of claiming exemption under Schedule IV (Table: Sl. Nos. 13D and 13E), the assessee shall be required to furnish information in Form No. 173A.”; (b) after Form 173, the following Form shall be inserted, namely:— “FORM NO. 173A [See Schedule IV (Table: Sl. Nos. 13D and 13E) of the Income-tax Act, 2025] Information to be furnished for claiming exemption on interest and capital gains from Government securities PART A: (Basic Information) 1. (i) Name (Refer Note 1) (ii) Country of Residence (iii) Address in the Country of Residence/ Headquarters (Refer Note 2) (iv) Permanent Account Number (PAN) (v) Tax Identification Number (TIN) in country of residence (Refer Note 4) (vi) SEBI Registration Number as FPI/FII (Refer Note 4) (vii) Tax Year for which the exemption is claimed (viii) Email Id: (ix) Contact Number Country code: ______ Number: ______ PART B: (Details of exempt income claimed on government securities) 2 A (i) International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) (ii) Type of Government securities (Select one) a. Treasury Bills b. Fixed Rate bonds c. Floating Rate bonds d. State Development Loans (SDLs) e. Others (please specify) (iii) Tenor (iv) Total value of securities as on 1st April (Refer Note 3) (v) Value of securities purchased during the year (Refer Note 3) (vi) Sale consideration of securities sold during the year (Refer Note 3) (vii) Total value of securities as on 31st March (Refer Note 3) (viii) Gross amount of interest received/accrued during the tax year (Refer Note 3) (ix) Capital gains earned during the tax year (Refer Note 3) B Repeat if required Declaration I certify that all the above information is true to the best of my knowledge and belief and is in accordance with the provisions of Schedule IV (Table: Sl. Nos. 13D and 13E) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (30 of 2025). Date: Place: Signature Name of the Principal officer: Notes: 1. The name shall be provided in full. 2. The address shall include i. Country/Specified territory, ii. Flat/Door/Building, iii. Road/Street/ Block/Sector, iv. PIN/ZIP Code, v. Town/Area/locality, vi. District, vii. State 3. Amounts to be filled in ₹ unless otherwise provided. 4. The details in 1(v) and (vi) is optional for Bank for International Settlements (BIS).”. [No. 137/2026/F.
