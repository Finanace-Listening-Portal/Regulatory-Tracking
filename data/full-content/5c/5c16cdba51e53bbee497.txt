This strategic move underscores the company’s commitment to expanding its premium motorcycle portfolio, offering enhanced performance, advanced technology, and distinctive styling. The Xtreme 160R 4V, a significant redesign of the model first introduced in 2023, features a sporty naked design with a “Cybersharp” aesthetic. It boasts a 163cc, 4-valve, oil-cooled engine, delivering 17.1 PS of peak power and 14.6 Nm of torque. The motorcycle is equipped with segment-first features such as cruise control and Panic Brake Alert, alongside ride-by-wire technology offering three distinct ride modes. This model is aimed at young riders seeking performance and technology, with a top speed acceleration of 0 to 60 km/h in just 4.4 seconds. Meanwhile, the Xtreme 250R has been refreshed to enhance its appeal in the 250cc segment. It is powered by a 250cc liquid-cooled, 4-valve DOHC engine, producing 30 PS of power and 25.3 Nm of torque, making it the fastest in its class with a 0 to 60 km/h sprint in 3.2 seconds. Notable features include segment-first cruise control, a bi-directional quickshifter, and a Tyre Pressure Monitoring System. The motorcycle’s design has been refined with gold anodised front forks and carbon-fibre detailing, enhancing its premium look and feel. Anuj Dua, Chief Business Officer – Premium Segment at Hero MotoCorp, highlighted the importance of premiumisation as a growth opportunity. He noted that the Xtreme family is central to this strategy, offering differentiated products that enhance the riding experience. Both models are available at special introductory prices, with the Xtreme 160R 4V starting at Rs. 1,27,000 and the Xtreme 250R at Rs. 1,98,000 (ex-showroom, Delhi). Bookings for both motorcycles begin on 17th October 2026, with deliveries set to commence on 1st November 2026. Analysts view Hero MotoCorp’s aggressive push into the premium segment as a strategic effort to capture a larger market share, leveraging its extensive dealership network and innovative product offerings.
