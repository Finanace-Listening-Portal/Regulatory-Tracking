The company’s Indian operations recorded a capacity utilisation rate of 92% for the month, reflecting robust operational efficiency and demand in the domestic market. Breaking down the production figures, JSW Steel’s Indian operations contributed 24.86 lakh tonnes, a 19% increase from the previous year’s 20.85 lakh tonnes. Meanwhile, its US subsidiary, JSW Steel USA – Ohio, produced 0.87 lakh tonnes, up by 5% compared to October 2025. This growth in production comes amidst a strategic restructuring earlier in the year, where the steel business undertaking of Bhushan Power and Steel Limited was transferred to JSW-JFE Steel Limited, a joint venture, which has been reflected in the adjusted production figures for comparative purposes. JSW Steel, a flagship entity of the JSW Group, continues to expand its influence as India’s leading integrated steel company. With a current crude steel capacity of 37.9 million tonnes per annum (MTPA), including contributions from its joint venture with JFE Steel, the company is on track to increase this capacity to 54.8 MTPA over the next four years. The expansion of its Vijayanagar plant in Karnataka, already the largest single-location steel-producing facility in India, is a significant part of this growth strategy, aiming to reach a capacity of approximately 25 MTPA by the end of the decade. Analysts view JSW Steel’s production growth as a positive indicator of the company’s strategic positioning and operational resilience in a competitive global steel market. The company’s commitment to sustainability, innovation, and strategic partnerships, particularly with JFE Steel, positions it well to meet the evolving demands of industries reliant on high-quality steel products. As the global steel industry faces challenges such as fluctuating demand and environmental regulations, JSW Steel’s proactive approach to capacity expansion and sustainability may provide it with a competitive edge.
