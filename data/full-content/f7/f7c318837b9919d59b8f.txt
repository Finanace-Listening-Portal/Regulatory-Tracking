Summary: The Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue) has issued Notification No. 51/2026-Central Excise dated 16 September 2026, further amending Notification No. 11/2026-Central Excise dated 26 March 2026. Exercising powers under Section 5A of the Central Excise Act, 1944 read with Section 112 of the Finance Act, 2018, the Central Government has substituted the rate specified against serial number 2 of the Table in the principal notification with “Nil”. Accordingly, the Road and Infrastructure Cess on exports of high-speed diesel oil has been reduced from Rs. 1 per litre to Nil with effect from 16 September 2026, being the date of publication in the Official Gazette. The rate had earlier been increased to Rs. 1 per litre through Notification No. 48/2026-Central Excise dated 1 September 2026. During 2026, the rate was initially fixed at Rs. 9.50 per litre from 26 March, increased to Rs. 31.50 per litre from 11 April, reduced to Nil from 1 May, increased to Rs. 1.50 per litre from 3 August, restored to Nil from 15 August and increased to Rs. 1 per litre from 1 September. The latest notification once again restores the rate to Nil from 16 September 2026.
