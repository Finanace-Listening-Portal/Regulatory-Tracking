The company's net interest margin (NIM) slipped one basis point sequentially to 10.8% in Q2 FY27, while total spending fell 7% quarter on quarter and receivables declined 3% to Rs 58,400 crore.The shift in portfolio mix towards lower yielding EMI products could limit valuation rerating, according to InCred Equities, which downgraded SBI Cards to Reduce from Hold and cut its target price to Rs 520 from Rs 640. The brokerage said the improvement in return ratios from lower credit costs would remain capped until profitable growth picks up. Advt EMI balances accounted for 33% of receivables in Q2 FY27, while revolving credit contributed 22% and transactor balances made up 45%. The revolver mix declined 3% sequentially to 22%, while the EMI mix fell 3% sequentially to 33%. Transactor balances also declined 3% quarter on quarter. Management indicated strong demand from the EMI segment and expects retail spending growth to pick up to the mid-to-high teens in the second half of FY27.Total spending fell 7% sequentially, primarily due to a 37% decline in corporate spending, while retail spending grew just 1%. Spending increased 3% year on year. Total receivables fell 2% year on year to Rs 58,400 crore.NIMs range-boundNIM stood at 10.8%, with management expecting it to remain range-bound as anticipated increases in funding costs are passed on incrementally. SBI Cards reported a yield of 16.1% and a cost of funds of 6.8%. The loan book repricing period is 60-75 days, which will influence how quickly changes in funding costs are reflected in lending rates.Net profit rose 75% year on year and 17% sequentially to Rs 780 crore, beating the brokerage's estimate by 19%, primarily because credit costs were lower than expected. Credit costs were Rs 180 crore, against an estimated credit-cost ratio of 6.6%, while the actual ratio was reported at 5.6% in the asset-quality section. Pre-provision operating profit stood at Rs 1,870 crore, down 1% year on year and up 1% sequentially, indicating sluggish underlying operating performance. Advt Credit costs eased 98 basis points sequentially to 5.6%, supported by lower write-offs, which fell 16% quarter on quarter, and improving portfolio quality. Stage 3 assets declined 10 basis points to 1.9%, while Stage 2 assets increased 10 basis points to 3.7%. The provision coverage ratio remained stable at 59.8%. Credit costs are expected to remain around 5.5-6% in FY27.The cost-to-income ratio remained elevated at 59%, despite operating expenses rising just 1%. Management expects the ratio to remain between 57% and 59% in FY27. Corporate spending is expected to account for around 20% of total spending. Advt InCred values SBI Cards at 2.4 times estimated FY28 book value or 16 times estimated FY28 earnings per share. It sees limited scope for a valuation rerating while growth remains weak and the portfolio shifts towards lower-yielding EMI products. A sharp pickup in receivables is identified as an upside risk.
