Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); In re Sanmina-SCI India Private Limited (CAAR Mumbai) The Customs Authority for Advance Rulings (CAAR), Mumbai, in In re Sanmina-SCI India Private Limited, ruled that RSA Backplane and Chassis Assemblies imported for manufacturing photonic service switches are classifiable under Customs Tariff Item 8517 79 90 as parts of telecommunication apparatus and eligible for Nil Basic Customs Duty (BCD) under Serial No. 5(a) of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs dated 30 June 2017, as amended. The applicant, an electronic contract manufacturer, proposed importing three models of customised chassis assemblies manufactured from aluminium and pre-plated steel with integrated backplane printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs). These assemblies were designed exclusively for photonic service switches used in optical communication networks and performed essential functions such as mounting interface cards, distributing electrical power, enabling high-speed data interconnection, providing electromagnetic shielding and managing heat dissipation. The applicant contended that the imported goods had no independent functional use and could not be classified merely as articles of iron, steel or aluminium under tariff items 7326 90 99 or 7616 99 90. It sought confirmation of classification under tariff item 8517 79 90 and the corresponding customs duty exemption. The Authority examined the technical characteristics of the imported assemblies, the General Rules for Interpretation and the relevant Section Notes to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. It observed that photonic service switches were machines falling under Heading 8517 because they received, converted, switched and transmitted data through wired optical networks. However, the imported chassis assemblies could not independently perform these functions because they lacked interface cards, processors, power supply units and other functional modules. Consequently, they did not possess the essential character of complete photonic service switches and were appropriately regarded as parts of such equipment. Applying Note 2(b) to Section XVI, the Authority held that the assemblies were suitable for use solely with the specified switches and were therefore classifiable under Heading 8517. It further distinguished the imported assemblies from populated printed circuit boards classifiable under tariff item 8517 79 10, noting that the goods comprised integrated mechanical chassis and backplane structures performing multiple structural, protective and electrical functions. The Authority accordingly determined their classification under tariff item 8517 79 90, consistent with its earlier rulings concerning chassis and related components imported by the same applicant. Regarding exemption eligibility, the CAAR held that Serial No. 5(a) of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs prescribed a Nil rate of Basic Customs Duty for goods falling under tariff item 8517 79 90, excluding parts of cellular mobile phones and wrist-wearable devices. Since the imported assemblies were specifically intended for photonic service switches and did not fall within these exclusions, they qualified for the exemption without any additional condition. However, the Authority clarified that the exemption was restricted to Basic Customs Duty and did not extend to integrated tax under Section 3(7) of the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, or any other applicable duty, tax or cess outside the First Schedule. The ruling was confined to the specified complete chassis assemblies with integrated backplane PCBAs imported without interface cards, processors, power supply units or fan modules. Separately imported backplane PCBAs or differently configured assemblies would require independent classification. Accordingly, the Authority answered the classification and exemption questions in favour of the applicant and held that the alternative classification question did not arise. This dual approach ensures that heat is both moved away from critical components and dissipated into the surrounding environment. 4.14.15By effectively channeling heat away from the internal electronic components to the environment, the aluminium plays a crucial role in maintaining the thermal equilibrium of the photonic service switches. Had the chassis and the covers not been made of aluminium or pre-plated steel, they would have lacked the adequate heat dissipation capabilities. The continuous 24/7 operation of the photonic service switches could lead to excessive thermal accumulation on the interface cards. This thermal buildup poses a significant risk of thermal overstress to critical internal components, on the interface cards, processors, and power management circuits, potentially leading to malfunction or failure of the device. Vibration Reduction, Electrical Insulation, Structural integrity and Physical protection 4.14.16 The use of aluminium and pre-plated steel in the construction of chassis and covers for photonic service switches provides a range of technical benefits, focusing particularly on strength, corrosion resistance, and protective capabilities. These attributes are crucial for ensuring the durability and longevity of switches in demanding data center environments. Steel, by its nature, offers high tensile strength and rigidity, making it an ideal material for supporting and protecting the sensitive internal components of data center switches. Its robustness ensures that the chassis can withstand the mechanical stresses typical in such environments, including vibrations and impacts. The strong framework provided by steel helps maintain the alignment and connectivity of internal components, which is critical for optimal electrical performance. This structural integrity minimizes the risk of misalignment that could lead to connectivity issues or signal degradation. 4.14.17 The toughness of steel, combined with the protective plating and aluminium, enhances the chassis’s resistance to physical damage. This resilience ensures that the switch can endure accidental knocks or impacts without compromising its protective role, safeguarding the internal electronics from damage. 4.14.18 The mass and rigidity of steel aid in damping vibrations. This damping capability is vital in reducing mechanical stress on components and minimizing the risk of damage or displacement due to persistent vibrations arising out of the continuous operational fan assembly. 4.14.19 The robust construction of pre-plated steel chassis and covers provides an effective barrier against environmental contaminants such as dust, moisture, and chemical vapors, which could otherwise compromise the performance and reliabi 1 ity of sensitive electronic components. The chassis acts as a protective shell, shielding the internal parts from physical damage due to accidental impacts or mechanical stresses. This protection is crucial in maintaining the operational reliability of switches in dynamic data center environments. The plating process adds a layer of sound insulation to the steel chassis, which helps reduce the acoustic footprint of the photonic service switches. This is particularly beneficial in data centers where maintaining a quieter environment is desired. The design of pre-plated steel chassis can incorporate additional sound-dampening features, leveraging the material’s mass and rigidity to minimize noise from fans and other moving parts. 4.15 Applicant’s Interpretation of the Questions Raised Applicant’s Interpretation on Question 1: The Applicant submits as follows: a. Discussion and Findings 7.1. 1 have carefully considered the application, the written submissions of the applicant, the submissions made at the personal hearing, and the technical literature and other documents placed on record. 7.2. The issues raised in Question 1 and 2 in the Form CAA R- l are covered under Section 28H (2) of the Customs Act, 1962, being matter of classification and of applicability of import duty benefit notification. The questions posed for advance ruling are: Question 1: Whether the following goods, imported by the applicant for the manufacture of Photonic Service Switches,, are classifiable under tariff item 8517 79 90 of the Customs Tariff of India as “Parts of other apparatus for transmission or reception of voice, images or other data, including apparatus for communication in a wired or wireless network (such as a local or wide area network)”: RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-8x-LFIALC3TD00071AAAC RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-12x-LFIALC3TD00286AAAC RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-24x-LFIALC3KC50378ABAC Question 2: If the answer to Question 1 is in the affirmative, whether the applicant is eligible to avail the benefit of concessional rate of duty under Serial No. 5 of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs dated 30.06.2017? Question 3: If the answers to the above questions are in the negative, what would be the correct classification of the above items under the Customs Tariff of India. 7.3. Classification of the finished product – Photonic service switches: As per the datasheet (Exhibit-2), the 1830 PSS-8x, PSS-12x and PSS-24x are Optical Transport Network (OTN) switching and transport platforms. These deliver multi-terabit switching scale along with integrated high-capacity wavelength division multiplexing (WDM) transport optics, and expanded range of service options using high-density, multi—rate client interfaces. Such equipment receives, converts, switches and transmits data in a wired network. It is apparatus of heading 8517, falling under subheading 8517 62 as “machines for the reception, conversion and transmission or regeneration of voice, images or other data, including switching and routing apparatus”. This is consistent with Serial No. 20 of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs read with Circular No. 08/2023-Customs dated 13.03.2023, which describe optical transport equipment and Optical Transport Network products, including DWDM equipment, among the goods falling under tariff items 8517 62 90 and 8517 69 90. For the present case, it is sufficient to hold that the photonic service switch is a machine of heading 8517. ” 7.4. Classification of the goods under consideration 7.4.1. Each of the goods is a single assembled unit described as RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-8x- LFIALC3TD00071AAAC, RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-12x-LFIALC3TD00286AAAC and RSA Backplane and Chassis Assembly PSS-24x-LFIALC3KC50378ABAC consisting of: a chassis of aluminium and pre-plated steel, vents, meshes, ventilation slots, for fans and other cooling mechanisms integrated within the chassis, pre-specified demarcated protrusions, compartments, circumscribed & delimited projections, boundaried protuberances, insulators, exclusive pre-measured chambers with identified partitions for mounting, bolting and installation of modular interface cards, power supply modules and other components; backplane printed circuit board assemblies (PCBA) integrated with the chassis, which acts as the high-speed interconnection backbone between cards inserted into the shelf. 7.4.2. Past consignments of the goods were declared under tariff items 7326 90 99 and 7616 99 90, as articles of iron or steel and of aluminium. However, Note 1(f) to Section XV excludes from that Section the articles of Section XVI. Note 1(g) to Section XVI, in turn, excludes from Section XVI “parts of general use, as defined in Note 2 to Section XV, of base metal”. Note 2 to Section XV confines the “parts of general use” to articles of headings 7307, 7312, 7315, 7317 or 7318 and similar articles of other base metal, springs and leaves for springs of base metal, and articles of headings 8301, 8302, 8308 and 8310 and frames and mirrors of heading 8306. The subject goods are not articles of that kind. 7.4.3. As described by the applicant, the interface cards, the processor, control and management cards. the power supply units and the cooling system of the switch are separate modules. „which are inserted into the imported assembly in the course of manufacture of the switch. This ruling proceeds on the basis that the goods are imported without any of those modules and are used as such, without further processing, in the manufacture of photonic service switches. Under Rule 2(a) of the General Rules for Interpretation, a reference to an article includes that article incomplete or unfinished, provided that, as presented, it has the essential character of the complete article. The imported goods, in which no such module is present, cannot by themselves receive, convert, switch or transmit any signal. As presented, they do not have the essential character of a photonic service switch and cannot be classified as such under subheading 8517 62. As per applicant’s submission, the subject goods have no function apart from the switch and cannot operate independently of it. At the same time, the switch cannot be assembled or function without them, since its cards and power modules are mounted, powered or interconnected by the said assembly. The goods are, accordingly, Parts of the Photonic service switch. 7.4.4. Under Note 2(b), “other parts, if suitable for use solely or principally with a particular kind of machine, or with a number of machines of the same heading (including a machine of heading 8479 or 8543) are to be classified with the machines of that kind or in the heading 8409, 8431, 8448, 8466, 8473, 8503, 8522, 8529 or 8538 as appropriate. However, parts which are equally suitable for the use of principally with the goods of headings 8517 and 8525 to 8528 are to be classified in the heading 8517, and parts which are suitable for use solely or principally with the goods of heading 8524 are to be classified in the heading 8529”. In other words, the parts of goods are to be classified with the machines of that kind. Each of the subject assemblies bears a part number specific to one model of the Photonic Service Switches,,, is built to that model’s design. It has no use other than as the shelf of that switch. The goods are therefore suitable for use solely withPhotonic Service Switches,,, which are machines of heading 8517, and by virtue of Note 2(b) to Section XVI they are classifiable in heading 8517. 7.4.5. Heading 8517 groups parts under the third single-dash entry “- Parts”, divided into subheading 8517 71 (aerials and aerial reflectors of all kinds; parts suitable for use therewith) and subheading 8517 79 (other). The subject goods are not aerials or parts of aerials, and fall under subheading 8517 79. The relevant entries are extracted below: Sl. No. Chapter, heading, sub-heading or tariff item Description of goods 5 8517 71 00 or 8517 79 90 (a) All goods other than the parts of cellular mobile phones or wrist wearable devices (commonly known as smart watches) (b) Inputs or sub-parts for use in manufacture of parts mentioned at (a) above 7.4.6. Tariff item 8517 79 10 covers “populated, loaded or stuffed printed circuit boards”. The expression describes a specific article: a printed circuit board on which components have been mounted. The goods presented are not such a board. The supplier’s documentation identifies the goods as an “RSA (Rack/Subrack assembly) or chassis” and an “RSA7,Shelf7Backplane Assembly” i.e. chassis assemblies with integrated backplanes. Most of the functions attributed to the goods are housing, guiding and immobilising the cards, protecting them, managing airflow and heat, shielding against EMI/RFI and damping vibration, and the backplane can perform its interconnection function only because the chassis aligns and holds each card against its connectors. The goods are therefore, “parts” covered under subheading 8517 79, and more particularly undertariff item 8517 79 90 . It may also be noted that a backplane PCBA imported on its own would be a populated printed circuit board of tariff item 8517 79 10, and is not covered by this ruling. 7.4.7. The judgements and rulings in the compilation filed by the applicant in the application and at the personal hearing have been considered. These judgements/ rulings lay down the tests for treating an article as a part: that it is an essential component without which the whole cannot function (Insulation Electricals, Prasoon Enterprises and Welkin Foods), and that it has no separate identifiable function and cannot operate independently of the main machine (Vodafone Idea Ltd.). Those tests are satisfied, as found at paragraph 7.4.3. Further, it is also ruled that the goods are not “parts of general use”. 7.4.8. In the applicant’s own case, this Authority has classified chassis, covers and backing plates of pre-plated steel imported for the manufacture of data center switches undertariff item 8517 79 90 as parts of apparatus of heading 8517, and has held them eligible for exemption under Serial No. 5 of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs (Ruling No. CAAR/Mum/ARC/102/2025-26 dated 31.10.2025; Ruling No. CAAR/Mum/ARC/56/2025-26 dated 25.07.2025). The classification of the subject goods as parts under tariff item 8517 79 90 is therefore consistent with those rulings. 7.5. Eligibility under Serial No. 5 of Notification No. 57/2017-Customs 7.5.1 The Notification No. 57/2017-Customs dated 30.06.201 7, exempts goods of the description specified in column (3) of its Table, and falling under the entries in column (2), from so much of the duty of customs leviable under the First Schedule as is in excess of the standard rate specified in column (4), subject to the conditions in its Annexure where a condition number is specified in column (5). The relevant entries read: Tariff Item Description 8517 Telephone sets, including smartphones and other telephones for cellular networks or for other wireless networks; other apparatus for the transmission or reception of voice, images or other data, including apparatus for communication in a wired or wireless network (such as a local or wide area network), other than transmission or reception apparatus of heading 8443, 8525, 8527 or 8528 – Parts: 8517 71 00 – – Aerials and aerial reflectors of all kinds; parts suitable for use therewith 8517 79 – – Other: 8517 79 10 – – – Populated, loaded or stuffed printed circuit boards 8517 79 90 – – – Other 7.5.2 Serial No. 5 covers all goods falling under tariff item 8517 71 00 or 8517 79 90, other than parts of cellular mobile phones or wrist wearable devices. It has been held above that the subject goods fall under tariff item 8517 79 90 They are parts of photonic service switches, and are neither parts of cellular mobile phones nor of wrist wearable devices. No condition is specified against Serial No. 5 in column (5) of the Table. The subject goods are therefore eligible for the “Nil” rate of Basic Customs Duty under Serial No. 5(a). 7.5.3 Scope of the exemption: Serial No. 5 exempts only duty leviable under the First Schedule, that is, Basic Customs Duty. It does not exempt integrated tax under Section 3(7) of the Customs Tariff Act, 1975, or any other duty, tax or cess not leviable under the First Schedule. 7.6. In view of the answers to Questions 1 and 2, Question 3 does not arise. Ruling 8.1.
