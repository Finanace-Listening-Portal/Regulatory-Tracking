The bank said it had approached the apex court under Article 137 of the Constitution of India, seeking a review of its judgment and order dated October 15, 2026, relating to the write-down of the bonds issued by the lender. The development comes after the Supreme Court upheld the Bombay High Court’s January 2023 ruling that quashed YES Bank’s decision to write down the entire outstanding value of the AT-1 bonds in March 2020 as part of the bank’s reconstruction exercise. The dispute concerns two issuances of AT-1 bonds — ₹3,000 crore carrying a coupon rate of 9.5 per cent, issued in December 2016, and ₹5,415 crore carrying a 9 per cent coupon, issued in October 2017.
