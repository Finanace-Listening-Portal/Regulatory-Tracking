Articles 1 min read Exclusive: SBI MF appoints former CIBIL exec Amol Joshi as CFO Amol Joshi, who recently resigned as CFO of TransUnion CIBIL, has been appointed as CFO of SBI Mutual Fund and will formally take charge from October 1, two people aware of the development told ETBFSI. He had succeeded Rajeev Mantri in the role.Prior to joining TransUnion CIBIL, Joshi was CFO of IndoStar Capital Finance. He was earlier Group Head-Finance at L&T Financial Services and spent over a decade at Standard Chartered Bank in various finance leadership roles. Advt His appointment at SBI MF comes shortly after the asset manager's parent, SBI Funds Management, made its stock market debut in July following a Rs 9,813-crore initial public offering. The IPO, which was entirely an offer for sale by State Bank of India and Amundi India Holding, was subscribed over 41 times.
