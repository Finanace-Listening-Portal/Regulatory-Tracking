RBI/2026-27/296 dated 19 October 2026, has communicated amendments to three entries in the United Nations Security Council’s ISIL (Da’esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List, notified through press release SC/16480 dated 15 October 2026. The amendments concern Mounir El Motassadeq (QDi.082), Noor Wali Mehsud (QDi.427) and Tehrik-e Taliban Pakistan (QDe.132). The circulated particulars include an address in Marrakesh and information concerning Mounir El Motassadeq’s deportation from Germany to Morocco in October 2018, photograph availability for the INTERPOL-UN Special Notices of both individuals, and identification of Noor Wali Mehsud as TTP’s leader. The listed individuals and entity remain subject to the asset freeze, travel ban and arms embargo specified in Security Council resolution 2734 (2024). The circular reiterates obligations under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 and Chapter IX of the applicable RBI KYC Directions, 2025, including those governing commercial banks, small finance banks and payments banks. Regulated entities must ensure that they do not maintain accounts in the names of listed individuals or entities, take appropriate action and strictly follow the UAPA Order dated 2 February 2021, as amended on 22 April 2024. RBI also provides access details for updated sanctions lists, listing narratives, sanctions measures and exemptions. Banks receiving de-listing requests must forward them electronically to the Joint Secretary (CTCR), Ministry of Home Affairs. Persons and entities seeking removal from the ISIL and Al-Qaida list may approach the independent UN Ombudsperson. RBI requires meticulous compliance with the communication.
