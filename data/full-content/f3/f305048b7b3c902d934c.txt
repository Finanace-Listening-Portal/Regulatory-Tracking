This strategic move marks an expansion of the company’s commercial mobility offerings within the African market. The Armado tricycle, designed specifically for last-mile commercial cargo applications, is equipped with a robust 200 cc engine and a spacious 2-metre cargo bed capable of carrying loads up to 1 ton. The Armado tricycle is engineered to meet the demands of various cargo transportation needs, providing a comfortable and efficient solution for commercial operators. It features ergonomic seats, a suspension system designed to enhance ride comfort under load, and a digital cluster with a USB charging port, underscoring its practicality and reliability. The vehicle is available in two colours, blue and red, and is being offered at a special introductory price of 2,255,000 Angolan Kwanza (AOA). Peyman Kargar, President of International Business at TVS Motor Company, highlighted the significance of the Angolan market for the company. He emphasised that the introduction of the Armado tricycle aligns with TVS Motor’s commitment to developing products that cater to the practical needs of customers and businesses across various markets. The vehicle’s high load-carrying capacity and robust performance are expected to support the everyday operations of businesses in Angola. The Armado tricycle will be distributed in Angola through SANZI MOTO, TVS Motor Company’s authorised distributor, ensuring comprehensive after-sales support and service initiatives. The vehicle also comes with a six-month warranty, providing customers with added confidence in its long-term reliability. Flexible financing options are available to make the vehicle more accessible to businesses and commercial operators. Market analysts view TVS Motor Company’s expansion into Angola as a strategic move to strengthen its presence in the African market, where demand for reliable and efficient commercial vehicles is on the rise. The introduction of the Armado tricycle is expected to enhance TVS Motor’s competitive position and contribute to its growth in the region.
