The net profit, which was bolstered by a sharp drop in provisions and a jump in total other income, was up 20 per cent sequentially. Net interest Income (NII) of the lender at ₹4,569 crore was up 4 per cent year-on-year (Y-o-Y) but down 2 per cent sequentially in Q2FY27, while total other income was up 36 per cent Y-o-Y to ₹2,248 crore. Meanwhile, the lender's net interest margin (NIM) stood at 3.23 per cent in Q2FY27, down 34 basis points (bps) from the previous quarter, and 9 bps from the corresponding period a year ago. Its provisions and contingencies were down 54 per cent Y-o-Y and 12 per cent sequentially to ₹1,223 crore. The bank reported gross slippages of ₹1,460 crore in Q2FY27, compared to ₹1,660 crore in Q1FY27, and ₹2,537 crore in the corresponding period a year ago.
