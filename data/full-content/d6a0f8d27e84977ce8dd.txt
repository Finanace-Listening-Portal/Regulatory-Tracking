Brent crude oil prices were last up more than 1% at $97.4 per barrel. [O/R]Most Asian currencies slipped while the dollar index was perched at 99.1, with market participants keeping their eyes peeled for U.S. consumer and wholesale inflation data this week to gauge ‌the future path of Federal Reserve policy rates."These data are not likely to be strong enough to ​see the ​Fed hike next ⁠week. This is a challenge to the USD's near-term outlook with OIS (overnight index swaps) pricing about 15bps of tightening for the meeting," analysts ​at HSBC said in a note.Meanwhile, the European Central Bank is widely expected to deliver a rate hike on Thursday, with markets pricing in another hike for December that would take the benchmark borrowing cost to 3%.(Reporting by Jaspreet Kalra; Editing by Jochelle Mendonca and Harikrishnan Nair) Reuters Published On Sep 7, 2026 at 11:47 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
