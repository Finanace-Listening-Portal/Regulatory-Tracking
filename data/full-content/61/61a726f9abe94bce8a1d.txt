Readers' discretion is advised. Bank shares witnessed brisk trading activity after the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced a 25 basis points (bps) rate hike in October policy meeting on Wednesday. The RBI-appointed Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) not only decided to increase repo rates by 25 bps to 5.5 per cent, but also ruled out rate cuts in the near-term. RBI changed its policy stance from 'Neutral' to 'Calibrated Tightening'. Following the announcement, the Nifty Bank index recovered sharply from an intraday low of 54,636, and rallied to a high of 55,341 - up 1.3 per cent or 705 points from the day's low. At 11:40 AM, the Bank Nifty quoted 0.3 per cent higher at 55,290. Meanwhile, the NSE Nifty 50 index was down 0.4 per cent at 22,684. READ MORE Sameer Sawant, Research Analyst, Mirae Asset Sharekhan reckons that rate hikes are positive for banks in the near-term as a large share of loans are floating which would reprice upwards, while deposit costs adjust with a lag, supporting Net Interest Margins (NIMs). Against this background, technical analysts Ajit Mishra, SVP-Research at Religare Broking and Hitesh Rathi, Technical Analyst -Equity & Derivatives at Angel One have identified 5 bank stocks with strong technical chart set-up in the current environment. Axis Bank Current Market Price: ₹1,257 Despite the recent consolidation, the medium-term bullish view on Axis Bank remains intact, as buyers have successfully defended the lows of the Anchor Column around the ₹1,180 mark, highlights Hitesh Rathi of Angel One. "Alongside this, a Weak Breakdown on the candlestick charts, followed by the formation of an Exhaustion Gap, further points towards the possibility of a resumption of the prior uptrend," he adds.
