The company is targeting annual growth of 23-25% in assets under management (AUM) and profit after tax (PAT), while delivering return on equity (RoE) of 19-21% over FY25-30.Bajaj Finance currently commands a 54% market share in consumer loans and 45% in professional loans, but its share in other key lending products is only 1-9%, compared with industry market shares of 18-62% on its existing customer franchise. The company plans to expand its total customer base to 200-220 million by FY30, including 120-130 million customers in its cross-selling franchise. Its customer-centric approach and wider product range could help it gain market share, ICICI Securities said. Advt The opportunityThe opportunity extends to lending categories where Bajaj Finance has a relatively small share despite its large customer base. The overall disbursement market stood at Rs 363 lakh crore as of March 2025, of which the company accounted for just 1%. However, industry disbursements to customers within Bajaj Finance's existing customer base represented 35.9% of the market, indicating considerable scope to increase lending to these customers.New car financing is one area where the company could gain market share by leveraging its existing customer franchise. Its strategy is built around expanding product offerings, deepening customer relationships and using a revamped AI-enabled technology platform to support business processes and growth.Bajaj Finance has delivered compound annual growth rates (CAGRs) of 34% in AUM and 46% in PAT, with an average RoE of 18% over FY08-26. It aims to sustain AUM and PAT growth of more than 20% in the medium term, despite being among the largest NBFCs by AUM as of September 2026.The company recently raised Rs 17,500 crore through a qualified institutional placement and promoter warrants. The capital raise is projected to drive 8-10% accretion in estimated book value per share in FY27-28. Unlike earlier capital raises, which were executed at leverage of more than 5 times, the latest fund-raising has taken place at less than 5 times leverage while sustaining RoE of around 20%. Advt The brokerage maintained its BUY rating and raised its target price to Rs 1,350 from Rs 1,250, valuing Bajaj Finance at 4.5 times its estimated consolidated September 2027 book value per share.
