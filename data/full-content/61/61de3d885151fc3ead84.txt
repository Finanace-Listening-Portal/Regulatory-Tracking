The proposal follows the substantial volume of E-Way Bills generated, which is expected to exhaust the capacity available under the existing numbering formats. The E-Way Bill Number is proposed to increase from 12 digits to 14 digits, while the Consolidated E-Way Bill Number is proposed to increase from 10 digits to 11 digits. GSTN has advised all stakeholders, including taxpayers, GST Suvidha Providers (GSPs), ERP and software providers, and API integrators, to modify their applications, APIs, database schemas and validation rules to accommodate the proposed formats. These modifications are necessary to ensure that systems can process the revised identification numbers without validation failures or operational disruptions.The proposed changes will be made available in the Sandbox environment shortly to enable stakeholders to conduct testing. Further technical details will be published on the E-Way Bill Portal. GSTN has requested stakeholders to complete the necessary system modifications and ensure readiness well before 1 January 2027. The advisory therefore highlights the need for timely technical preparation and testing to facilitate a seamless transition to the revised numbering formats. Goods and Services Tax Government of India, States and Union Territories Revision in E-Way Bill and Consolidated E-Way Bill Numbering Formats w.e.f. 1 January 2027 Nov 4th, 2026 It is hereby informed that, in view of the substantial volume of E-Way Bills generated, the existing numbering capacity is expected to be exhausted. Accordingly, the following revisions in the numbering formats are proposed with effect from 1 January 2027 Document Type Existing Format Proposed Format E-Way Bill Number 12 digits 14 digits Consolidated E-Way Bill Number 10 digits 11 digits All stakeholders, including taxpayers, GSPs, ERP/software providers and API integrators, are advised to make necessary modifications to their applications, APIs, database schemas and validation rules to accommodate the revised numbering formats. The changes will be released in the Sandbox environment shortly for testing, and the relevant details will be published on the E-Way Bill Portal. Stakeholders are requested to ensure system readiness well before 1 January 2027 to facilitate a seamless transition and avoid any disruption to E-Way Bill-related operations.
