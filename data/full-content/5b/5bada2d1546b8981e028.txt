Exercising powers under Paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of FTP 2023, the DGFT has inserted the India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement as a new entry, numbered xvii, in the list of Free Trade Agreements under Paragraph 2.88(a). The amendment also incorporates the agreement into Appendix 2B, identifying the agencies authorised to issue preferential Certificates of Origin for exports covered by the agreement. The authorised agencies include the Directorate General of Foreign Trade and its regional offices, Export Inspection Council and Export Inspection Agencies, APEDA, Marine Products Export Development Authority, Central Silk Board, Coir Board, Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Spices Board, Textile Committee, Tobacco Board and specified Special Economic Zones. Their authorisation varies according to product category and territorial jurisdiction. DGFT and the Export Inspection Council agencies are listed for all products, while other agencies have product-specific or SEZ-jurisdiction-based responsibilities. The amendment establishes the administrative framework for issuing preferential Certificates of Origin under the India–New Zealand FTA. The Public Notice does not independently specify the date on which preferential tariff benefits commence or the applicable tariff concessions. Government of India Ministry of Commerce and Industry Department of Commerce Directorate General of Foreign Trade Public Notice No. 40/2026-27-DGFT | Dated: 13th November, 2026 Subject: Amendments in Para 2.88 of Handbook of Procedures, 2023 and Appendix 2B of Foreign Trade Policy, 2023-reg. In exercise of powers conferred under paragraphs 1.03 and 2.04 of the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023, as amended from time to time, the Director General of Foreign Trade hereby makes the following amendments: (A) Under Para 2.88 (a) of the Handbook of Procedures 2023, a new entry with serial no. xvii is inserted after the existing list of Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) as under: “xvii. India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (India-New Zealand FTA)” (B) Under Appendix-2B [List of Authorised to issue Certificate of Origin (CoO) (Preferential)] of the FTP, the list of Authorised Agencies allowed to issue CoO for India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement is included, as under: S.No. Name of the Agreement Authorized Agencies Product Category 22.
