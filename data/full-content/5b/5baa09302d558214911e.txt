NBFCs’ overall gross non-performing assets as a proportion of total loans (GNPA ratio) stood at 2.4 per cent in financial year 2025-26 (FY26), an improvement from 6.1 per cent during the NBFC crisis that began in September 2018. Even so, it remains higher than that of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs), a reversal from the pre-crisis period, when NBFCs had fared better than SCBs. Sector-wise, commercial real estate shows the highest asset-quality stress, followed by medium industry, agriculture, and micro and small enterprises. In terms of credit distribution, industry and retail loans together accounted for over 80 per cent of NBFC lending in FY25. On the other hand, the GNPA ratio for industry was 2.5 per cent and for retail loans, it was 3 per cent.
