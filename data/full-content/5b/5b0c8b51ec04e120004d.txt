New tools aim to enhance product discoverability for small merchants. AI solutions to streamline payments and customer engagement in India. Pine Labs said the initiative is intended to help businesses make their product catalogues accessible to AI systems, manage customer acquisition and complete transactions through emerging AI-driven commerce channels.Pine Labs has entered into a collaboration with Google Cloud to develop agentic commerce infrastructure for Indian merchants, including small and medium businesses, the fintech company said in a regulatory filing on Thursday.The partnership will use Google Cloud’s Gemini-powered AI capabilities to support merchant operations, customer engagement and payments.Pine Labs said the initiative is intended to help businesses make their product catalogues accessible to AI systems, manage customer acquisition and complete transactions through emerging AI-driven commerce channels. Advt Under the collaboration, Pine Labs plans to develop an AI advertising agent for small and medium businesses. The tool will create and place advertisements across platforms such as Google Search, YouTube and Pine Labs’ point-of-sale devices.At the same time, Pine Labs Issuing, its issuing solutions business, is also developing Jarvis, a multi-agent platform built on Gemini Enterprise.The platform is intended to assist with programme creation, system diagnostics and customer support across the company’s Woohoo platform.AI-powered commerce infrastructureThe companies said their collaboration will address three challenges for smaller businesses seeking to participate in agentic commerce: product discoverability, payment processing and governance.Pine Labs said that by using Google Cloud’s AI and data infrastructure, they aim to help merchants make their products discoverable to AI agents that can recommend products and facilitate purchases.For payments, Pine Labs will use its P3P payment layer, which the company said will support real-time and compliant settlement of transactions initiated through agentic commerce. The solution will initially focus on UPI and expand to other payment rails.The company also said its Grantex platform will support transaction auditability and compliance with India’s financial regulatory requirements.Pine Labs will use the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform for catalogue enrichment, search and the development of commerce agents. Advt Its platform will also offer native integration support for the universal commerce protocol (UCP) and agent-to-agent (A2A) frameworks, enabling transactions across different platforms.“Without the right platform, agentic commerce repeats a pattern India has seen before: new technology reaches the top of the market first, and small businesses wait,” said B Amrish Rau, CEO, Pine Labs.Rau said the partnership is intended to make agentic commerce accessible to Indian merchants, including technology-focused businesses operating across different digital platforms.Amit Kumar, managing director, Digital Natives Business, Google Cloud India, said the collaboration would combine Pine Labs’ commerce infrastructure with Google Cloud’s AI and analytics capabilities to help merchants become more discoverable and participate in agentic commerce.
