Advt UPI Tap & Pay allows users to make payments by unlocking an NFC-enabled smartphone and tapping it on an NFC-enabled POS terminal. Unlike conventional UPI payments, the feature does not require the user’s phone to have mobile internet connectivity, as the transaction can use the POS terminal’s internet connection.The feature supports multiple UPI-linked account types, including RuPay Credit Card on UPI. Users can make PIN-less transactions of up to Rs 5,000, while transactions above that threshold require the UPI PIN to be entered on the POS terminal.The move builds on NPCI’s introduction of UPI Biometric Authentication at GFF 2025. According to NPCI, biometric-authenticated UPI transactions crossed 6.29 billion as of August 31, 2026, signalling growing adoption of faster and device-native authentication methods.MyUPI brings AI into UPI customer supportAlongside Tap & Pay, the RBI Governor announced MyUPI, an AI-powered platform built on NPCI’s Small Language Model, FiMI, as part of an expanded UPI Help framework.MyUPI provides users with a single view of their UPI transactions and AutoPay mandates across banks and UPI applications. It also introduces a set of controls designed to give users greater visibility and control over their payments.These include a Safety Switch that allows users to request the decline of UPI debit transactions in cases of suspected compromise, as well as UPI Number Delink, which enables users to delink their mobile number for receiving UPI payments. Advt The platform also introduces payee context-based information, giving users additional information about a beneficiary before a transaction is completed. For banks and UPI apps, automated chargeback processing is designed to enable real-time chargeback creation for eligible transactions, potentially reducing complaint resolution time and improving operational efficiency.Another feature, Transaction Replay, will allow users to recall and repeat past transactions through any UPI application.From payment support to proactive control layerNPCI said MyUPI is intended to move beyond conventional customer support towards a proactive trust and control layer for the UPI ecosystem. Advt The platform will provide 24x7 multilingual support and self-service capabilities through a user-friendly interface, with the stated objective of improving transparency, safety and convenience for users while enabling banks and UPI apps to provide faster assistance.The launches were announced in the presence of Ajay Kumar Choudhary, Non-Executive Chairman and Independent Director, NPCI, along with senior bankers, fintech founders and other participants from the banking and fintech ecosystem.Click here to read our coverage of the Global FinTech Fest 2026.
