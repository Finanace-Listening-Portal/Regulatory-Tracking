Nomura has also cut the fintech firm's FY28 and FY29 net profit estimates by 72 per cent and 51 per cent, respectively, assuming the company exits its point of sales person (POSP) business and adjusts to the proposed commission caps.The revised target price implies an upside of 5.8 per cent from PB Fintech’s closing price of Rs 1,040 on October 7. Insurance premium estimates for FY28 and FY29 have been cut by 13 per cent and 19 per cent, respectively, while EBITDA margin is expected to fall to 7.9 per cent in FY28 before recovering to 18.2 per cent in FY29, according to Nomura. Advt The changed scenarioThe revised estimates assume that PB Fintech exits the POSP business while keeping its PolicyBazaar, Dubai and corporate segments unchanged. The model also incorporates the proposed commission caps and cuts total expenses by 40 per cent in FY28 and 48 per cent in FY29.The brokerage has lowered its estimated revenue compound annual growth rate for FY26 to FY50 to 13.2 per cent from 15.1 per cent earlier. The revised target price implies price to earnings multiples of 70 times for September 2028 and 40 times for September 2029.In an alternative scenario, the target price rises to Rs 1,335 if health insurance commission caps are increased by two percentage points from the proposed levels. Revenue estimates would rise by 3 percentage points in FY28 and 4 percentage points in FY29, while net profit estimates would increase by 37 per cent and 19 per cent, respectively, compared with the base case.A second scenario assumes PolicyBazaar begins selling traditional savings insurance products, accounting for 0.5 per cent to 1.1 per cent of total premiums in FY28 and FY29. This would raise the estimated FY26 to FY50 revenue growth rate by 80 basis points and lift net profit estimates by 5 to 6 percentage points over the base case, implying a fair price of Rs 1,366.
