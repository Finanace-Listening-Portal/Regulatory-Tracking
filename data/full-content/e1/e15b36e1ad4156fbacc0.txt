Articles 1 min read Tata AIA Life leases nearly one lakh sq ft office in Thane’s Hiranandani Estate Tata AIA Life Insurance has secured nearly 99,000 sq ft of office space in Thane under a lease agreement involving a rental commitment of around Rs 98 crore over nearly a decade. The agreement, which took effect in March 2026, includes an eight-month fit-out period, with rent payments scheduled to begin in November 2026. The deal highlights the growing demand for large office spaces in Thane’s expanding commercial market. The lease has a tenure of nine years and 11 months.The lease commenced on March 1, 2026, while the rent commencement date is November 1, 2026, following an eight-month fit-out period from March 1 to October 31, 2026, showed registration documents accessed through CRE Matrix, a data analytics platform. Advt The lease agreement provides for a 5% annual escalation in rent. Tata AIA will pay common area maintenance (CAM) charges of Rs 10 per sq ft per month. The transaction was registered on July 31.Based on the starting monthly rent of Rs 67.09 lakh, the total base rental payout over the nine-year-and-11-month tenure would be around Rs 79 crore before factoring in the annual escalation. Including the contracted 5% annual escalation, the cumulative rental payout is estimated at around Rs 98 crore, excluding CAM charges.ET’s email query to Tata AIA Life Insurance Company remained unanswered.With nearly 1 lakh sq ft of space leased in a single transaction, the deal is among the sizeable office leasing transactions recorded in the Thane market this year.The lease adds to the growing activity in Thane’s commercial office market, where established business districts and large integrated developments are attracting occupiers seeking sizeable office spaces outside Mumbai’s traditional commercial locations.The transaction also reflects continued demand for large-format office spaces from financial services and insurance companies, as businesses expand their operational footprint and consolidate teams into larger workplaces.
