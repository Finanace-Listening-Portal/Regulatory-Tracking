Bajaj Finance fundraise In another related development, the NBFC's board has approved plans to raise up to ₹175 billion (₹17,500 crore) through a QIP and a preferential issue of warrants to its promoter and holding company, Bajaj Finserv. As per the details available, funds to the tune of ₹117 billion will be raised through a QIP by issuing equity shares to qualified institutional buyers. Besides, ₹58 billion will be raised through a preferential issue of warrants convertible into equity shares to Bajaj Finserv. The issue price for the preferential warrants will be determined at a later stage. Bajaj Finance stock: Technical outlook Harish Jujarey, AVP head - technical equity research, Prithvi Finmart, said that Bajaj Finance has remained under pressure over the past few weeks amid weakness in the broader market. The stock has corrected nearly 20 per cent from its recent high and found support near the 61.8 per cent Fibonacci retracement level of ₹936 last week. In today’s session, the stock showed a decent recovery from these levels. Going ahead, ₹936 will remain an important support, while on the upside, the stock is likely to face resistance around ₹1,020, which coincides with the downward-sloping trendline. "As long as the stock remains below this trendline, the broader trend is likely to remain weak. Hence, investors should remain cautious and watch the ₹936-1,020 range closely," he said.
