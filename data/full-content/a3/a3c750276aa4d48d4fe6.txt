The results, approved by the company’s Board of Directors during a meeting held on 12 November 2026, highlight a significant increase in revenue and operational performance compared to the previous year. For the quarter ended 30 September 2026, Hitachi Energy India reported a total revenue from operations of ₹2,996.51 crore, marking a substantial rise from ₹1,832.55 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The company’s revenue growth was driven by increased sales and income from operations, which amounted to ₹2,928.47 crore, compared to ₹1,760.23 crore in the same period last year. Additionally, other operating revenue contributed ₹68.04 crore to the total revenue. The company’s total income for the quarter, including other income, stood at ₹3,053.34 crore, a significant improvement from ₹1,915.15 crore in the previous year. Other income for the quarter was ₹56.83 crore, slightly down from ₹82.60 crore in the corresponding period last year. The increase in total income reflects the company’s strong operational performance and effective cost management strategies. On the expenditure front, the cost of raw materials and components consumed increased to ₹1,808.73 crore, up from ₹985.50 crore in the previous year. Employee benefits expenses were reported at ₹186.74 crore, indicating the company’s continued investment in its workforce. Despite the rise in costs, Hitachi Energy India managed to maintain a robust financial position, showcasing its resilience in the current market environment. Market analysts view Hitachi Energy India’s financial performance as a positive indicator of its strategic growth initiatives and operational efficiency. The company’s ability to significantly enhance its revenue and manage costs effectively positions it well for future growth in the energy sector. The results are expected to bolster investor confidence and reinforce Hitachi Energy India’s standing in the Indian market.
