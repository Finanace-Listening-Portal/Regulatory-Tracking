Advt India and the US had finalised the framework for the first phase of the bilateral trade agreement in February, with both sides aiming to deepen economic engagement and expand market access.However, the signing of the initial tranche has been delayed as the two countries continue negotiations amid changes in the US tariff regime.The minister's remarks come days after a senior government official said Washington had assured New Delhi of a comparative tariff advantage over competing nations under the proposed agreement.The two sides are currently working towards concluding the first tranche of the pact by the end of the current financial year.According to the official, discussions are continuing both on the first tranche and the broader bilateral trade agreement, while the final extent of tariff concessions and competitive advantages for Indian exports will depend on the framework eventually agreed upon.Trade negotiations have gained added significance after the US Supreme Court struck down the Trump administration's reciprocal tariffs that had formed the basis of earlier discussions.Following the ruling, the US has been exploring alternative mechanisms under provisions of the Trade Act to develop a new tariff architecture.Goyal is currently in Egypt to hold discussions aimed at enhancing bilateral trade and investment ties, even as India pursues trade negotiations with several key partners, including the United States.
