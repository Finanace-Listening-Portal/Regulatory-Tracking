} } Listen to This ArticleKotak Mahindra Bank on Saturday reported a 26 per cent year-on-year (YoY) rise in consolidated net profit to ₹5,645 crore for the July-September quarter of FY27 (Q2FY27), from ₹4,468 crore in Q2FY26. Sequentially, profit was up 3 per cent from ₹5,480 crore in the June quarter (Q1FY27). On a standalone basis, the bank reported a 29 per cent YoY rise in net profit to ₹4,212 crore for the September quarter (Q2FY27). The rise came on lower provisions and 16 per cent growth in net interest income (NII), even as the bank's margin contracted on inflows of foreign currency non-resident (bank) [FCNR(B)] deposits. The bank had reported a net profit of ₹3,253 crore in Q2FY26. Sequentially, profit was up 2 per cent from ₹4,123 crore in Q1FY27. Net interest income (NII) rose 16 per cent YoY to ₹8,459 crore from ₹7,311 crore in Q2FY26. Sequentially, it was up 7 per cent from ₹7,928 crore in Q1FY27. Net interest margin (NIM) stood at 4.31 per cent, against 4.54 per cent a year earlier and 4.53 per cent in Q1. The bank said the moderation was largely on account of FCNR(B) mobilisation during the quarter. It raised FCNR(B) deposits of about ₹55,344 crore and extended loans of about ₹16,110 crore through its international branches.
