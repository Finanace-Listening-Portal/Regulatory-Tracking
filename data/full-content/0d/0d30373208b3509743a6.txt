The exercise seeks to facilitate easier access to capital markets for eligible issuers, simplify compliance requirements, eliminate repetitive disclosures and procedures, and improve regulatory clarity and consistency without compromising investor protection or market integrity. The Sub-Group will examine eligibility conditions, promoter and pre-issue capital lock-in requirements, application and allotment mechanisms, disclosure obligations, pricing and price discovery frameworks, due diligence certificates, and related regulatory directions. Its review covers initial public offerings (IPOs), further public offerings (FPOs), preferential issues and qualified institutions placements (QIPs), wherever applicable. It will also examine inconsistencies, ambiguities and implementation difficulties and recommend appropriate regulatory changes. The ten-member Sub-Group is chaired by Shri Ashish Chauhan, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of the National Stock Exchange of India Limited. Other members represent BSE Limited, the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, capital market intermediaries, professional firms and industry organisations. The Sub-Group may invite additional experts and stakeholders, with SEBI providing secretarial and technical assistance. Its recommendations will be submitted to PMAC for consideration. The announcement initiates a regulatory review and does not itself amend the existing ICDR Regulations. SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA PR No.73/2026 SEBI Constitutes a Sub-Group of PMAC to Review Specified Provisions of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018 The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has constituted a Sub-Group of the Primary Market Advisory Committee (PMAC) to review Chapters II, IIA, IV, V and VI of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 (“ICDR Regulations”), along with the relevant Schedules and related regulatory directions. The review aims to identify opportunities to facilitate ease of access to the capital market for eligible issuers, simplify and rationalise regulatory requirements, eliminate duplicative disclosures and procedures, and improve regulatory clarity and consistency, while maintaining adequate disclosures, investor protection and market integrity. The Terms of Reference of the Sub-Group are as follows: 1. Review of Eligibility Conditions: Review of eligibility conditions applicable to issuers under the relevant modes of capital raising, including conditions governing IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues and QIPs, as applicable. 2. Review of Lock-in Provisions: Review of lock-in requirements applicable to promoters, pre-issue capital, securities allotted under preferential issues and other relevant categories, as applicable to the respective modes of capital raising. 3. Review of Application, Allocation and Allotment Mechanism: Review of the application process, allocation methodology and allotment mechanism applicable to the respective modes of capital raising, including related procedural requirements. 4. Review of Disclosure Requirements: Review and rationalisation of disclosure requirements applicable to IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues and QIPs, including disclosures in offer documents and other relevant documents prescribed under the ICDR Regulations and the relevant Schedules. 5. Review of Pricing and Price Discovery Framework: Review of pricing requirements and the applicable price discovery framework, including determination and disclosure of the price or price band, floor price and other related requirements, as applicable to the respective modes of capital raising. 6. Review of Due Diligence Requirements: Review of the format, content and requirements relating to due diligence certificates and other due diligence submissions by lead manager(s) and other relevant intermediaries, as applicable. 7. Review of Related Regulatory Directions: Review of relevant SEBI circulars, advisories, general orders and other directions or instructions issued in relation to the aforesaid provisions, with a view to identifying the need for their consolidation, modification or alignment with the ICDR Regulations. 8. Review of Other Related Requirements: Examination of other regulatory and procedural requirements under the aforesaid Chapters and Schedules, including inconsistencies, ambiguities, duplicative requirements and implementation-related issues, and recommendation of appropriate changes, wherever necessary. 9. Other Matters: Examination of any other matter incidental or relevant to the above, as considered necessary by the Sub-Group. The composition of the Sub-Group is as follows: S.
