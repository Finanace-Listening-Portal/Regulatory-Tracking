ETBFSI Research ETBFSI Published On Nov 27, 2026 at 06:11 AM IST var news_msid = 136206136; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Union Bank of India plans to scale up its robotic process automation (RPA) initiatives to around 500 processes by FY28 from 120 implemented so far, as it seeks to improve operational efficiency and strengthen monitoring of business and risk functions. The bank expects each robotic process to potentially deliver work equivalent to four to five employees. It plans to increase the number of RPAs to around 200 by FY27 before taking it to 500 by FY28.The bank has recently inaugurated Sudarshan, a fully automated centralised intelligence hub that continuously monitors its business, operations, risk, compliance, employee support, physical security, cybersecurity and technology functions. The initiatives were discussed during an investor meeting in Singapore hosted by the bank, according to a report by ICICI Securities. Advt Sudarshan is designed to function as a unified nerve centre for monitoring the bank's business and operational activities. Its coverage extends to risk management, compliance, employee support and physical and cyber security. The initiative is part of the bank's efforts to strengthen operational risk management, business transformation and automation.The bank has also been pursuing ecosystem banking and Muskaan as part of its broader initiatives. Ecosystem banking, headed by a chief general manager, has a workforce of around 900 employees and focuses on capturing customer flows and fee income. The bank indicated that early traction from the initiative had been strong. The report did not provide further details on Muskaan.RoA targetThe automation push comes as the lender seeks to sustain core return on assets (RoA) above 1%. Union Bank reported core RoA, excluding treasury gains and technical write-offs, of 1.03% in Q2FY27, compared with 0.62% a year earlier. It intends to maintain core RoA above 1% going forward.The bank's net interest margin (NIM) improved by 22 basis points over the past two quarters, partly supported by better churn of lower-yielding loans, repricing and resource management. It expects policy rates and improving efficiencies to provide additional support to margins. The brokerage estimates NIM at around 3% by the end of Q4FY27. Advt Union Bank's loan growth stood at around 19% year on year in Q2FY27, while balance sheet growth was about 9%. The difference partly reflects the accelerated churn of its lower-yielding corporate loan book. The bank indicated that the bulk of this process was over, although it would continue. It expects loan growth to remain in line with or marginally ahead of the banking system.The bank has also been focusing on average business figures to improve portfolio granularity. Its CET-1 capital ratio stood at 16%, while the brokerage noted its operational risk management and capital position as strengths. Advt The planned expansion of RPA is expected to support operating efficiencies over the next two financial years. The bank's estimate that each automated process could potentially be equivalent to four to five employees indicates the possible productivity gains from the programme, though the report does not say that this would translate into an equivalent reduction in headcount.
