Articles 2 min read India's Angel One weighs active funds launch, expansion of securities-backed loans, executive says Angel One is planning to diversify its services beyond broking by entering active mutual fund management. The company's asset management arm currently manages three passive index funds, with a total AUM of 5.7 billion rupees. Additionally, Angel One is testing a loans against securities program to support its clients. The company aims to enhance its offerings by including bonds and focusing on wealth management. Advt Indian equity mutual funds logged 68 consecutive months of net inflows through October, holding steady despite broader market underperformance over much of the past two years."We have been working on making mutual funds a much larger and more important component of our core business... So far, we have chosen to focus on the passive side. We are (now) evaluating opportunities in active fund management," Angel One Group Chief Strategy Officer Amit Majumdar told Reuters in an interview.The $883 billion asset management industry is seeing an increase in competition, with the number of fund houses rising to about 60 from 42 in March 2020.While Angel One currently manages three passive index funds, including the Angel One Nifty 50 index fund, weak index-level returns in recent years have underscored the appeal of stock-picking strategies, Majumdar said.Adding active funds will allow the firm to adapt to shifting investor appetite.As of September 30, Angel One's asset management arm held 5.7 billion rupees ($60.61 million) in assets under management (AUM) across 11 schemes, according to data from the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI).LOANS AGAINST SECURITIES AND MARGIN TRADING FACILITYBeyond asset management, Angel One is testing loans against securities, allowing clients to borrow against their existing shares and mutual fund holdings. Advt Majumdar said the business was in beta mode and had synergies with its broking and margin trading facility (MTF), which allows investors to buy shares using funds provided by the broker.Nationwide, the MTF book has more than tripled over the past three years as regulatory curbs on equity derivatives pushed retail traders towards leveraged cash-market bets.Angel One's own average client funding book has surged nearly fivefold since fiscal year 2024.The company also plans to include bonds to its offerings and continue focus on wealth management, US stocks, and other products in a bid to retain users on a single platform, Majumdar said.
