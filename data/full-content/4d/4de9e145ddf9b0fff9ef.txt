Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); Summary: The Board of Studies (Academic) of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India has announced two Mock Test Paper series for students appearing in the CA Foundation January 2027 Examination. Series I will commence on 1 December 2026, while Series II will begin on 14 December 2026. Both series will be conducted in physical and virtual modes; students interested in physical tests may approach their respective local branches. Series I covers Accounting on 1 December, Business Laws on 3 December, Quantitative Aptitude on 7 December and Business Economics on 9 December. Series II covers these subjects on 14, 16, 18 and 21 December, respectively. Accounting and Business Laws papers are scheduled from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, while Quantitative Aptitude and Business Economics papers will run from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM. Question papers and answers can be accessed through the BoS Knowledge Portal, ICAI BoS Mobile App and BoS MTP Registration Portal. First-time users of the registration portal must register using their registration number and date of birth, then log in using their registered email ID, as recorded in SSP, and an email OTP. Question papers will be uploaded by 1:30 PM on the scheduled dates. Students should attempt them within the prescribed time. Answer keys will be uploaded within 48 hours of commencement of each paper, enabling students to compare their answers and self-assess their performance. Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Board of Studies (Academic) The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India Date: 21.10.2026 ANNOUNCEMENT Mock Test Papers Series – I & Series – II for students appearing in CA Foundation for January 2027 Examination The Board of Studies (Academic) is commencing Mock Test Papers Series – I from 1st December, 2026 and Mock Test Papers Series – II from 14th December, 2026 for students appearing in the Foundation Course for January 2027 Examination. The Mock Test Papers Series – I & Series – II will be conducted in physical/virtual mode(s). Students interested in physical mode may approach the respective branches in their area. The schedule for the same is as follows: Series – I Date Day Foundation Time 01.12.2026 Tuesday Paper 1: Accounting 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM 03.12.2026 Thursday Paper 2: Business Laws 07.12.2026 Monday Paper 3: Quantitative Aptitude 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM 09.12.2026 Wednesday Paper 4: Business Economics Series – II Date Day Foundation Time 14.12.2026 Monday Paper 1: Accounting 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM 16.12.2026 Wednesday Paper 2: Business Laws 18.12.2026 Friday Paper 3: Quantitative Aptitude 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM 21.12.2026 Monday Paper 4: Business Economics Board of Studies (Academic), ICAI — Page 1 of 2 Access to Question Papers and Registration Purpose Platform / Procedure Download Question Papers & Answers at BoS Knowledge Portal and ICAI BoS Mobile App Login BoS Knowledge Portal at https://boslive.icai.org/ Login ICAI BoS App (Android / iOS) Download Question Papers & Answers Register at ICAI Branches / Regional Offices to appear for Physical Mock Tests across India Register & Login BoS MTP Registration Portal: https://bosmtp.icai.org/ Steps to Register / Login: Step 1: Click on Student Login Step 2: If you are a first-time user, click on Register Now Step 3: Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth Step 4: After successful registration, enter your registered Email ID (as per SSP) Step 5: Enter the OTP received on your email to log in The Question Papers for each subject will be uploaded at BoS Knowledge Portal / BoS MTP Registration Portal, as per the schedule, by 1:30 PM during this period. Students are advised to download and attempt these papers in the stipulated time limit designated for the papers. The Answer Key to these papers will be uploaded within 48 hours from the date and time of commencement of the respective paper, as per the schedule. Students can examine their answers with respect to the Answer Keys and self-assess their performance.
