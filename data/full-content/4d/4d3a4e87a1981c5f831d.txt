RBI/2026-27/282 FMOD.MAOG.No.154/01.01.001/2026-27 October 07, 2026 All Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) participants Madam/Sir, Liquidity Adjustment Facility - Change in rates As announced in the Monetary Policy Statement dated October 07, 2026, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to increase the policy repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 5.25 per cent to 5.50 per cent with immediate effect. 2. Consequently, the standing deposit facility (SDF) rate and marginal standing facility (MSF) rate stand adjusted to 5.25 per cent and 5.75 per cent respectively, with immediate effect. 3. All other terms and conditions of the extant LAF Scheme will remain unchanged. Yours sincerely, (G. Seshsayee) Chief General Manager
