The meeting will now be held on Thursday, 8 October 2026, from 11:00 AM onwards in the Summit Room, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. The memorandum refers to the earlier Office Memorandum bearing F. No. 1392/57/GSTCM/GSTC/2026 dated 6 September 2026. The Secretariat has requested that the change in schedule be brought to the attention of the Hon’ble Member of the GST Council and has regretted the inconvenience caused by the rescheduling. The Office Memorandum further records that it has been issued with the approval of the Revenue Secretary, who is the ex-officio Secretary to the GST Council. The communication is signed digitally by Shaifali G. Singh, Commissioner, GST Council Secretariat. This latest memorandum changes the previously communicated schedule for the 57th GST Council Meeting and confirms both the revised date and venue. Accordingly, the 57th GST Council Meeting is now scheduled for 8 October 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, beginning at 11:00 AM. Earlier TaxGuru coverage relating to the 57th GST Council Meeting reflected the schedule prevailing before this latest rescheduling.
