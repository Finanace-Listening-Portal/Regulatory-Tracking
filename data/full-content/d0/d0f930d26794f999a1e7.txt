Articles 1 min read Fairfax to sell 13% via 3.4k cr block deal, exit IIFL Finance Fairfax Group is divesting its full 13.3% stake in IIFL Finance through a block deal appraised at Rs 3,450 crore. To facilitate this transaction, the Canadian firm has engaged IIFL Capital to handle the sale of over 5.6 crore shares. This divestment follows the company's recent acquisition of close to 61% of IDBI Bank. Partha Sinha & partha.sinha TNN Published On Nov 18, 2026 at 08:14 AM IST var news_msid = 135946350; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Mumbai: Canada's Fairfax Group, which is in the process of acquiring IDBI Bank, is selling its entire 13.3% stake in IIFL Finance through a block deal worth about Rs 3,450 crore (around $370 million). FIH Mauritius Investments, an arm of Fairfax, has mandated IIFL Capital, a group company, to sell over 5.6 crore shares of IIFL Finance on Wednesday, Nov 18, a term sheet for the deal showed.While Fairfax is exiting IIFL Finance, it continues to hold 38% in IIFL Capital, currently worth about Rs 4,200 crore, shareholding data on BSE showed.The block of IIFL Finance shares is being sold at a floor price of Rs 611 per share, a 3.9% discount to the stock's closing price of Rs 636 on BSE on Tuesday. Advt A little over a month back, Canada-headquartered Fairfax Financial Holdings said that it had entered a deal to acquire a nearly 61% stake in IDBI Bank for Rs 53,000 crore. Since the announcement of the deal, it was expected that the Canadian financial services group would exit some of its holdings in India, mainly to part-finance the IDBI Bank stake acquisition.On Oct 15, the cabinet committee on economic affairs approved an arm of Fairfax to buy a 30.5% stake held by govt for Rs 26,620 crore and another 30.2% from LIC for Rs 26,440 crore.
