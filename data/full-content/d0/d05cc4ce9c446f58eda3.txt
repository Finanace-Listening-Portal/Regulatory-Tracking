Articles 2 min read IDBI Bank unions urge RBI to withhold approval for proposed Fairfax stake sale IDBI Bank's officers' and employees' unions have urged the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to withhold approval for the proposed transfer of management control to Fairfax Financial Holdings, seeking an independent review of the transaction and raising concerns over valuation, employee safeguards and depositor interests. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 16, 2026 at 01:17 AM IST var news_msid = 135865102; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook The United Forum of IDBI Officers & Employees (UFIOE) and the All India IDBI Bank Employees Association (AIIBEA) have urged the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to withhold all approvals for the proposed strategic disinvestment of IDBI Bank and transfer of management control to Fairfax Financial Holdings.In a representation dated November 15, the two organisations have sought an independent examination of the proposed Rs 52,878 crore transaction, including the source of acquisition funds, ultimate beneficial ownership and the prospective acquirer's group structure.The unions have asked the RBI to examine whether IDBI Bank's deposits, assets, capital, guarantees or future cash flows could be used, directly or indirectly, to finance the acquisition. They have also sought scrutiny of the prospective acquirer's existing control over CSB Bank and the possibility of conflicts of interest and connected transactions. Advt The representation has raised questions over the valuation of IDBI Bank, with the unions comparing its financial performance and net worth with those of RBL Bank, acquired by Emirates NBD in June 2026. They have sought an independent review of the valuation methodology and disclosure of the reports used by the Department of Investment and Public Asset Management (DIPAM).The employee organisations have also flagged concerns over the implications of the proposed ownership change for IDBI Bank's depositors, citing its deposits of Rs 3,55,684 crore as of September 30, 2026. They have raised questions about the potential impact on lending priorities, banking services and the bank's role in serving different customer segments.On employee interests, the unions have sought enforceable safeguards covering job security, service conditions, pension and retirement benefits, transfers, promotions and reservation commitments following the proposed acquisition.The representation has also referred to Life Insurance Corporation of India's (LIC) 51 per cent investment in IDBI Bank in January 2019 for around Rs 21,624 crore. The unions have questioned the proposed sale price in relation to LIC's investment and raised concerns over the potential impact on its policyholders.The organisations have further referred to parliamentary assurances regarding the government's minimum 51 per cent shareholding in IDBI Bank and sought examination of the implications of the proposed transfer of management control. Advt The unions have requested the RBI to place the process in abeyance until their concerns are independently examined. They have also sought an opportunity to present supporting material to the regulator before it takes a decision and requested a reasoned response to their representation.
