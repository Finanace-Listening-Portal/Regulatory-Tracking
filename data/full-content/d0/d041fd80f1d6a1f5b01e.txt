Exercising powers under Sections 5, 6 and 20 of the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, read with Sections 15, 16 and 148 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, the Central Government revises input tax credit conditions and introduces specified service-rate entries with effect from 1 November 2026. The amendments revise ITC restrictions for hotel accommodation, restaurant services, specified railway catering and outdoor catering. They permit specified credits relating to inputs or input services procured in the same line of business while restricting other credits. For hotel accommodation and outdoor catering, where the supplier of the eligible input service charges integrated tax above 5%, the available same-line-business credit is restricted to the amount calculated at 5%. The notification illustrates these restrictions through hotel accommodation and wedding catering transactions. MINISTRY OF FINANCE (Department of Revenue) Notification No. 06/2026-Integrated Tax (Rate) | Dated: 27th October, 2026 G.S.R. 937(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-sections (1), (3) and (4) of section 5, sub-section (1) and sub-section (3) of section 6 and section 20 of the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (13 of 2017), read with sub-section (5) of section 15, sub-section (1) of section 16 and section 148 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (12 of 2017), the Central Government, on being satisfied that it is necessary in the public interest so to do, on the recommendations of the Council, hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India, in the Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), number 8/2017- Integrated Tax (Rate), dated the 28th June, 2017, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide number G.S.R. 683(E), dated the 28th June, 2017, namely:- In the said notification, – (a) in the Table, – (i) against serial number 7, in column (3), – (A) against item (i), in column (5), the following entry shall be substituted, namely: – “Provided that credit of input tax charged on goods and services used in supplying the service, other than the input tax credit of input service in the same line of business (i.e. hotel accommodation service procured from another supplier of hotel accommodation service), has not been taken [Please refer to Explanation No. (iv)] Provided further that where the supplier of input service in the same line of business charges integrated tax at a rate higher than 5%, credit of input tax charged on the input service in the same line of business in excess of the tax paid or payable at the rate of 5%, shall not be taken.
