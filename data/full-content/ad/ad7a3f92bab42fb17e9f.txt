Articles 2 min read Apple Pay set for India launch next month with Axis Bank credit cards: Report Apple is set to launch Apple Pay in India next month, debuting with Axis Bank's credit card integration. Talks with prominent banks like HDFC and ICICI are underway to expand the service. This innovative payment solution enables iPhone users to effortlessly make transactions, positioning Apple within India's thriving digital payment landscape. ICICI and Axis said no comment when contacted by Reuters.Also Read: Apple iPhone 18 Pro, AirPods 5, Apple Watch Series 12 sales start in India: Check prices, features and availabilityApple Pay allows iPhone users to make tap-and-pay purchases using tokenised card details stored in their devices, without opening a separate payments app.Apple plans to add lenders over time through direct, bank-by-bank deals, mirroring phased rollouts in other markets, said one of the three sources.Apple's share of India's smartphone market has doubled to 8% since 2022, according to Counterpoint Research, establishing it as one of its largest markets.Apple Pay will join a crowded digital payments market.Payments via the home-grown Unified Payments Interface, which allows transfers via QR codes, phone numbers and tap and pay, dominate the market, making up 84% of India's digital payments volume.Last year, the Reserve Bank of India allowed biometric authentication for card payments, enabling Apple's payment service to work with Indian bank cards using Face ID and Touch ID.Axis Bank, with 16.26 million active credit cards in July, trails HDFC Bank, SBI Cards and ICICI Bank in terms of issuance, according to RBI data.
