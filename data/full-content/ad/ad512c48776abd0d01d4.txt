RBI/2026-27/310 DoR.RET.REC.262/12.01.001/2026-27 November 27, 2026 All Scheduled Banks, Madam / Sir, Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Change in Daily Minimum Cash Reserve Maintenance Requirement Please refer to our circular DoR.RET.REC.247/12.01.001/2026-27 dated October 09, 2026 on the captioned subject. 2.
