The notice has been issued in exercise of powers conferred under paragraph 1.03 of the Foreign Trade Policy, 2023, as amended from time to time. Three entries cover doxycycline tablets, while the fourth covers clobetasol propionate. Under SION A-3715, the export product is Doxycycline Tablets, USP 50 MG 100 S, with an export quantity of one number and an import quantity of 54.84 mg of Doxycycline USP (Monohydrate). SION A-3716 covers Doxycycline Tablets, USP 100 MG 50 S, while SION A-3717 covers Doxycycline Tablets, USP 100 MG 500 S. Both prescribe an export quantity of one number and an import quantity of 109.68 mg of Doxycycline USP (Monohydrate). Under SION A-3718, the prescribed import quantity is 1.08 kg of Betamethasone 17-Propionate for an export quantity of one kg of Clobetasol Propionate BP/EP/USP. The stated effect of the notice is to facilitate expeditious issuance of Advance Authorisations by Regional Authorities and ensure uniformity in the application of norms.
