Advt The ADB Country Director also expressed a commitment to support projects worth around $2 billion in Rajasthan over the next five years as part of its partnership with the state.The meeting also discussed identifying projects based on the state's emerging development requirements and their implementation.ADB suggested developing projects in areas including irrigation, renewable energy and asset monetisation. The state government said projects would be identified in these areas in line with its priorities and taken forward with ADB support.Srinivas discussed the ongoing ADB-supported projects and the need to advance upcoming projects within timelines. He also discussed expanding cooperation in new areas aligned with the state's development priorities.
