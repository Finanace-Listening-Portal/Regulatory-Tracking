Articles 1 min read Fino Payments Bank gets RBI nod to appoint Pratap Singh as MD & CEO for three years Fino Payments Bank has received Reserve Bank of India's approval to appoint Pratap Singh as its Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer for three years, effective November 27. Singh brings over 29 years of banking experience across India and the UK, including stints at ICICI Bank and SBI UK. ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Nov 13, 2026 at 08:36 AM IST Read by 100 Professionals var news_msid = 135787410; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Fino Payments Bank has received approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) to appoint Pratap Singh as its Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer for a period of three years, the bank said in a regulatory filing on Friday.The approval was communicated by the central bank through a letter dated November 12. Singh's tenure will begin from November 27, 2026, or the date he takes charge, whichever is earlier.Singh brings over 29 years of banking experience across India and the UK, with expertise spanning retail banking, SME banking, liabilities, digital transformation, branch-led profitability and operating model redesign. Advt He has been associated with financial institutions including ICICI Bank India, ICICI Bank UK and SBI UK. At ICICI Bank UK, he served as Head of Retail & SME Banking for the UK and Europe, where he led the transformation of the franchise from a corporate-led bank into a more diversified retail and SME banking platform.The transformation focused on current accounts, remittances, commercial real estate lending, corridor banking and digital channels. Earlier, in India, Singh led large retail and SME banking zones for ICICI Bank.His experience also includes building liability franchises, improving branch productivity and executing business turnarounds.
