The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) has undertaken the upgradation of its Business Analytics Platform (BAP) to facilitate an integrated, streamlined and enhanced platform for regulatory reporting and Health product related functionalities. 2. As part of this initiative, functionalities related to the submission of Health Regulatory Returns, generation of Unique Identification Numbers (UINs) for new Health Insurance Products and modification to existing Health Insurance Products have been incorporated into the Health module of BAP. 3. Accordingly, the functionalities relating the submission of Health Regulatory Returns and Health Product UIN generation are now available through the BAP portal. Health Regulatory Returns module 4. All concerned insurers are required to submit the following Health Regulatory Returns through the Health module of BAP: (a) Health Regulatory Returns for FY 2024–25. (b) Health Regulatory Returns for FY 2025–26. . 115/1, , , 500 032 / Page | 3 Survey No. 115/1, Financial District, Nanakramguda, Hyderabad 500 032 Phone: 040-20204000; www.irdai.gov.in (c) Health Regulatory Return – RET 197 for Q1 and Q2 of FY 2026-27 (except life insurers) (d) Health Regulatory Returns for subsequent reporting periods, in accordance with the prescribed frequency. Health Product module 5. All concerned insurers shall, with effect from 02 nd November 2026, generate UINs for new Health Insurance Products and modifications to existing Health Insurance Products, and submit updates relating to the withdrawal of Health Insurance Products, exclusively through the Health Product Module of the BAP portal. 6. In view of the above, the Health Department's email communication dated 10th June 2024 regarding the generation of UINs for Health Insurance Products shall cease to be applicable. 7. The operational guidelines and detailed instructions relating to the various functionalities of the Health Regulatory Returns Module and Health Product Module are available on the BAP portal for reference and compliance by all concerned users.
