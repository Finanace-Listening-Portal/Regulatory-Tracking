The availability of well-developed reinsurance market ensures that insurance products are available at reasonable prices to the policyholders. The insurer has to ensure that CBRs meet, interalia, rating requirements as per IRDAI (Reinsurance) Regulations, 2018 and shall have File Reference Number (FRN), before placing any business with them.
