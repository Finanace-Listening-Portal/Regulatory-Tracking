ETBFSI Staff ETBFSI Published On Oct 24, 2026 at 07:13 PM IST var news_msid = 135171806; console.log(news_msid); Select ETBFSI as Preferred Choice on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Kotak Mahindra Bank on Saturday appointed Anu Aggarwal, President in its Wholesale Banking Group, as a Senior Management Personnel (SMP) with immediate effect, as the lender also formalised key leadership changes including Anup Kumar Saha’s appointment as Managing Director & CEO and Ashu Suyash’s reappointment as an independent director.The appointment of Aggarwal as SMP is effective October 24, according to an exchange filing by the bank.Aggarwal is currently the Head-Corporate and Transaction Banking in Kotak’s Wholesale Banking Group, a role she has held since August 2025. She is also a member of the bank’s Key Leadership Team and has been associated with Kotak for more than 19 years. Advt She has more than 25 years of experience handling large corporate clients and joined Kotak in April 2007. During her tenure, Aggarwal has headed the Conglomerates, Multinationals, New Age companies and Corporate Client Groups within the Wholesale Banking Division.Prior to her current responsibilities, she also led Lending, Transaction Banking, Foreign Exchange, Debt Capital Markets, Equity Capital Markets and advisory functions for corporate clients across India. The bank said she has been instrumental in building its corporate banking franchise.Before joining Kotak, Aggarwal spent more than a decade with Citibank’s Global Corporate and Investment Banking team. She holds a B.Com. degree in Commerce and Economics from Punjab University and an MBA from Jamnalal Bajaj Institute of Management Studies, Mumbai.Kotak’s board also approved the reappointment of Ashu Suyash as an independent director for a second consecutive three-year term from January 24, 2027 to January 23, 2030, subject to shareholder approval.Suyash is the Founder and CEO of Colossa Ventures and has more than three decades of experience across banking, investment management and global information services.
