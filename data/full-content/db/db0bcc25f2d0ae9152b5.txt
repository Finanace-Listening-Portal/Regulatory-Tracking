OFCBs and ECBs mobilised under the RBI’s concessional swap window stood at $5.32 billion and $5.3 billion, respectively, as of September 18. The FCNR(B) mobilisation figure is as of August 31, when the window for mobilising fresh deposits closed. Earlier, the RBI had said on September 2 that banks had mobilised a little over $127 billion through FCNR(B) deposits under the scheme by August 31. The FCNR(B) deposit mobilisation figure rose as the RBI had allowed banks to avail of the swap facility with the central bank until September 11, 2026, for deposits mobilised up to August 31.
