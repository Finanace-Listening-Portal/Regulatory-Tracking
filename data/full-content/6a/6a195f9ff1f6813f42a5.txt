} } Listen to This ArticleOutstanding loans against gold jewellery by non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) grew 66.1 per cent year-on-year (YoY) to ₹3.74 trillion in September 2026, making it one of the fastest-growing segments in their retail loan portfolios. The pace of growth was significantly higher than the 50.7 per cent recorded in September 2025, according to the latest sectoral deployment of credit data released by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). In absolute terms, outstanding gold loans increased by nearly ₹1.49 trillion over the one-year period. The expansion in the segment comes amid elevated gold prices, which have increased the value of collateral and enabled borrowers to raise larger amounts against their jewellery. The segment's growth has outpaced that of other major retail loan categories. Overall retail loans by NBFCs grew 21.9 per cent YoY to ₹26.84 trillion in September, compared with 16.8 per cent growth a year ago.
