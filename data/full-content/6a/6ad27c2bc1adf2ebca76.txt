Excess FCNR(B) liquidity also weighed on NIM," observed Elara analysts. While management expects an 18-20 bps NIM recovery to 7 per cent in Q4 FY27, Elara has a more conservative view as it expects the rebound to be gradual. Nirmal Bang, on the other hand, noted that it expects healthy loan growth to remain of ~25 per cent in FY27 to drive NIM by ~20bps over the next two quarters as surplus liquidity is deployed. Sustained fee income growth and operating leverage shouldfurther support profitability, partly offset by a normalisation in credit costs in 2HFY27, it added.
