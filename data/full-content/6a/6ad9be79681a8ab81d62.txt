Summary : ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited’s published resident fixed-deposit rate card, effective 17 August 2026 and checked on 16 November 2026, lists 8.00% per annum for the exact 800-day slab on deposits below ₹3 crore and 8.50% for senior citizens. The regular fixed-deposit product starts at ₹1,000 in multiples of ₹1,000 and offers tenures from seven days to ten years. This article explains the narrow 800-day rate, the bank’s callable premature-closure rule, maturity and renewal controls, DICGC aggregation and the resident interest-TDS framework. It is an information guide, not a recommendation or ranking. Product eligibility and booking controls The bank describes the regular FD as a resident fixed-deposit account with a minimum deposit of ₹1,000 and further multiples of ₹1,000, and an available tenure range from seven days to ten years. The rate table discussed here is for resident fixed deposits below ₹300 lakh. Deposits of ₹3 crore and above are bulk deposits under the RBI framework and require the bank’s applicable bulk-rate process; they should not be priced from this retail table. Identity, address, PAN or Form 60, senior-citizen status, account mandate and beneficial ownership must be established under the bank’s onboarding and KYC requirements. Joint depositors should specify the operating instruction and survivor clause. Nomination is distinct from ownership and succession; the bank’s form and applicable law govern how the nominee may receive funds. A depositor should compare the FD receipt with the application immediately and correct any discrepancy through an authorised channel. Callable terms change the result on early closure No interest before completion of seven days The official rate notes state that no interest is payable if a resident fixed deposit is prematurely withdrawn before completing seven days. This is particularly important for emergency liquidity planning: a deposit is not equivalent to an on-demand savings balance, even when the bank permits premature closure. After seven days, the completed-period rate and penalty matter For premature closure after the minimum period, ESAF states that the interest rate is the lower of: the contracted rate for the original tenor, and the rate that applied on the opening date for the period the deposit actually remained with the bank. A one-percentage-point penalty is then applied to the applicable completed-period rate. Consequently, an 800-day deposit closed early does not simply earn 8.00% or 8.50% for the elapsed days less one point; the historical rate for the completed tenure may be lower and controls the calculation. DICGC cover is aggregated by bank, right and capacity ESAF Small Finance Bank is a banking company, and eligible deposits with insured banks—including fixed deposits—fall within the DICGC framework. The maximum insurance amount is ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank in the same right and same capacity, including principal and accrued interest at the relevant cut-off. Accounts at different branches of the same bank are aggregated; opening several FDs at ESAF does not multiply cover for the same ownership capacity. Different capacities may be separately recognised under DICGC rules, but labels should not be engineered without genuine legal capacity. Joint-account order, guardianship, partnership, trusteeship and proprietary holdings have specific aggregation consequences. DICGC protection is a statutory backstop in defined bank-failure or restriction circumstances; it is not a guarantee that every rupee above ₹5 lakh will be repaid and does not eliminate liquidity, documentation or set-off risk. Interest is taxable and TDS is only a collection mechanism Resident FD interest is generally taxable under the applicable income-tax provisions according to the depositor’s facts and accounting method. For tax year 2026-27, section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 sets the bank-interest TDS threshold at ₹50,000 for a person other than a senior citizen and ₹1,00,000 for a senior citizen. The threshold is applied to the aggregate interest credited or paid by the banking company, not separately to each branch or FD when the bank operates through core banking. Crossing the threshold does not make only the excess taxable, and remaining below it does not make interest exempt. TDS is creditable tax subject to correct PAN, reporting and return conditions. Eligible residents may consider the prescribed self-declaration process for non-deduction only when every legal condition is satisfied; an incorrect declaration can create consequences. The depositor should reconcile the bank’s interest certificate, AIS, Form 26AS and books or return before claiming credit. The official card effective 17 August 2026 lists 8.00% per annum for normal resident deposits below ₹3 crore and 8.50% for senior citizens, subject to the bank’s eligibility and booking terms. 2. Does 799 or 801 days earn the same rate? No. The published adjacent slabs—24 months to 799 days and 801 days to less than 36 months—show 7.75% for normal depositors and 8.25% for senior citizens. 3. What happens if the FD is closed within seven days? The bank’s rate notes state that no interest is payable on a resident FD prematurely withdrawn before completing seven days. 4. Is the premature-closure rate simply 8.00% minus 1%? Not necessarily. ESAF first uses the lower of the contracted rate and the opening-date rate for the completed tenure, then applies the stated one-percentage-point penalty. 5. Is the whole FD insured by DICGC? Eligible principal and accrued interest are covered only up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank in the same right and capacity. Other deposits at ESAF in that capacity and across branches are aggregated. 6. Is interest tax-free if the bank deducts no TDS? No. The TDS threshold controls deduction, not taxability. Interest must be reported and taxed according to the depositor’s applicable law and facts. 7. Will an auto-renewal retain the 800-day rate? Not automatically. Renewal is ordinarily at the rate and product terms available on the renewal date. Review the maturity instruction and fresh advice.
