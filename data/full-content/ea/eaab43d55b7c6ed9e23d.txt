Articles 1 min read Pakistan to chair IMF, World Bank boards in 2027; first time in over 70 years Pakistan will assume the chairmanship of the IMF and World Bank Group boards in 2027, marking a significant return. This follows a long absence from the position that last occurred in 1953. The role will allow Pakistan to engage in global economic discussions and foster collaboration among member nations. Pakistan is currently utilizing financial assistance from the IMF through various programs aimed at economic stabilization. Advt The annual meetings, scheduled from October 12 to 18, have brought together more than 15,000 participants representing 191 countries.The appointment comes as Pakistan continues to rely on financial assistance from the IMF through two programmes: a USD 7 billion Extended Fund Facility (EFF) and a USD 1.4 billion Resilience and Sustainability Facility (RSF).While the EFF is aimed at meeting the country’s balance-of-payments needs and supporting economic reforms, the RSF provides longer-term funding to help Pakistan tackle climate-related risks and improve its resilience.Pakistan could receive a further USD 1.2 billion from the IMF, subject to approval by the fund’s Executive Board. The anticipated disbursement follows an agreement reached between IMF staff and Pakistani authorities on the latest reviews of both programmes.
