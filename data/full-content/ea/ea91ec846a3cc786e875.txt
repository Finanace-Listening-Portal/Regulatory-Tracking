policy repo rate at its October 5-7 meeting, according to a Business Standard poll. Eight of the 10 respondents surveyed expect a rate hike. The RBI last raised the repo rate in February 2023, when it increased the rate by 25 bps to 6.5 per cent. The MPC has kept the repo rate unchanged at 5.25 per cent in its last four policy reviews. This followed cumulative rate cuts of 125 bps in 2025.What happened in the August MPC meeting?The RBI kept the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25 per cent at its August meeting. The MPC, which met from August 3 to August 5, also retained its monetary policy stance at ‘neutral’. The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate was kept unchanged at 5 per cent, while the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and the bank rate remained at 5.5 per cent. The RBI governor said underlying inflation had remained under control for some time and was expected to converge with overall core inflation by the end of the current financial year. On economic growth, Malhotra said activity continued to be supported by strong domestic demand, steady growth in the manufacturing and services sectors, and healthy export performance.
