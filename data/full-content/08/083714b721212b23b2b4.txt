Articles 1 min read Arohan Financial Services secures Sebi approval to launch IPO Arohan Financial Services has received regulatory approval for its initial public offering from Sebi. This IPO will include a fresh share issue worth Rs 600 crore and an offer for sale. Proceeds from the fresh issue will strengthen the company's capital base and support corporate objectives. The company currently operates 1,073 branches across 17 states, catering to rural and semi-urban customers. Advt According to an update, the company filed preliminary IPO papers with Sebi in May. After examining the draft offer documents, the regulator issued its final observation on November 13, paving the way for the company to proceed with the next stage of the IPO process.Going by the draft papers, proceeds from the fresh issue will be used to boost the company's capital base and for general corporate purposes.Arohan Financial Services is a technology-enabled non-banking financial company-microfinance institution (NBFC-MFI) offering income-generating loans and a suite of financial and non-financial products to customers primarily across rural and semi-urban states in India.It had assets under management (AUM) of Rs 6,308 crore as of December 2025.Arohan Financial Services, which commenced its business in 2006 with operations in a single location in Kolkata, has gradually expanded its footprint. As of December 2025, it operates 1,073 branches across 17 states in the country.The filing comes after successful listings of Aye Finance and Kissht in 2026.DAM Capital Advisors, Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors and SBI Capital Markets are managing the issue. PTIArohan Financial’s IPO plan reflects improving microfinance sector health, with better asset quality and growth prospects encouraging renewed investor interest after prolonged stress.
