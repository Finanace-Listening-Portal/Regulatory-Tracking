Articles 2 min read IIFL Finance shares surge over 6 pc as company announces over Rs 6,000 cr fundraise PTI Published On Nov 11, 2026 at 07:20 PM IST var news_msid = 135739068; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook New Delhi, Shares of non-banking financial company IIFL Finance on Wednesday jumped over 6 per cent after the firm announced a Rs 6,414-crore capital raise through a preferential issue, with global investment firm Blackstone set to invest Rs 5,000 crore to drive the next phase of growth.The stock surged 6.30 per cent to Rs 666.10 on the BSE. During the day,it jumped 10.85 per cent to Rs 694.65.On the NSE, the stock climbed 6.25 per cent to end at Rs 666.25.The fundraise also includes an investment of Rs 1,264 crore by the company's promoters, Nirmal Jain and R Venkataraman, through preferential allotment of warrants, while Anchorage Capital will invest Rs 150 crore through a primary equity investment. Advt The proposed transaction is subject to customary regulatory and shareholder approvals, IIFL Finance said in a regulatory filing on Tuesday.This will raise the capital adequacy ratio from 17 per cent to about 30 per cent and support the next phase of growth across its secured lending businesses, and could improve its credit ratings and lower its borrowing costs over time, it said.The fundraising plan was approved by the Board at its meeting held on November 10, 2026, the NBFC said.Blackstone Private Equity will invest Rs 5,000 crore through compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS), which will convert into equity shares at Rs 602 apiece, in line with the regulatory price, within 18 months of allotment.As per the statement, it is one of the largest primary private equity infusions in a listed NBFC.The statement further said the capital infusion will strengthen its capital adequacy, improve cost of funds and accelerate growth in its gold finance business, besides supporting its ambition to emerge as one of India's largest diversified retail-focused NBFCs.The company and its promoters have entered into definitive agreements with Blackstone and Anchorage Capital in this regard, it said.
