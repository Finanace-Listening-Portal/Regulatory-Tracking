The EBITDA margin, excluding financial companies, stood at 17.5 per cent, ahead of the estimated 17 per cent. Advt Among the major sectors, Metals reported the strongest PAT growth of 78 per cent YoY, followed by Oil & Gas at 55 per cent and Automobiles at 34 per cent.Other notable performers included Utilities (40 per cent), Cement (38 per cent), Consumer Durables (30 per cent), Private Banks (25 per cent), NBFCs (25 per cent), Defence (22 per cent) and Capital Goods (21 per cent).The performance indicates that earnings expansion extended beyond a handful of industries, said the report.The market-cap analysis also reflected healthy earnings growth across segments. Large-cap companies recorded 31 per cent YoY earnings growth, outperforming the estimated 24 per cent.Mid-caps registered a 22 per cent increase against expectations of 20 per cent, while small-caps delivered 32 per cent growth, compared with the estimated 29 per cent. These figures exclude OMCs.According to the report, the Nifty-50 recorded a 27 per cent YoY increase in PAT, its highest growth in 17 quarters.For the second half, the report estimates earnings growth of 11 per cent for the coverage universe and 18 per cent for the Nifty.
