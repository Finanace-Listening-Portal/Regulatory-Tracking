The two top players processed 77.4 per cent of all UPI transactions in September. Apps such as Cred, HDFC Bank, and Paytm lead on Rupay-linked credit card payments on UPI, across features such as biometric authentication, equated monthly installment (EMI) conversions, and autopay, respectively, in October. The data comes at a time when sources in the industry believe larger players are not competing on RuPay credit cards on UPI, even as NPCI seeks to expand their adoption. RuPay credit cards are the only credit cards allowed to be linked on UPI. In autopay, which covers recurring payments, PhonePe and Google Pay together held 22.8 per cent share of RuPay credit card transactions on UPI in October. Paytm alone had 40 per cent, though its share of all UPI transactions in September was 8.1 per cent. More than half (54.7 per cent) of Cred’s RuPay credit card transactions on UPI of up to ₹10,000 were approved through biometric authentication, where users confirm a payment with a fingerprint or face scan, instead of entering a UPI PIN, leading the segment.
