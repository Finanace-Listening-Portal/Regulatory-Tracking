The company’s consolidated total income for the quarter stood at ₹8,791.58 crore, marking a significant rise from ₹7,489.00 crore in the same period last year. However, the consolidated profit before tax decreased to ₹521.81 crore from ₹700.45 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year. The decline in profit can be attributed to the steep increase in raw material prices, driven by global uncertainties. This rise in costs adversely impacted the company’s profitability despite the strong double-digit sales growth. MRF capitalised on sustained demand from both original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and the replacement segment, which contributed to the increase in sales revenue. To counteract the cost pressures, MRF implemented price increases and cost-saving measures, which helped mitigate some of the adverse effects on its financial performance. After accounting for a tax provision of ₹134.82 crore, MRF’s consolidated net profit for the quarter was ₹386.99 crore, down from ₹527.43 crore in the same period last year. The company anticipates continued disruptions and volatility in the commodity markets, with raw material and logistics costs expected to remain elevated. MRF has expressed its commitment to taking measured steps to address these challenges as it navigates the complex market environment. In addition to its financial performance, MRF’s Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹3 per share (30%) for the financial year ending 31st March 2027. This decision reflects the company’s ongoing commitment to delivering value to its shareholders despite the challenging market conditions. Analysts remain cautious about the outlook for MRF, given the persistent volatility in commodity prices and the potential for continued cost pressures. However, the company’s proactive measures to address these challenges and its ability to capitalise on market demand are seen as positive indicators of its resilience and adaptability in a dynamic industry landscape.
