Advt * The bankers requested anonymity as they are not authorised to speak to the media, while Bank of Maharashtra did not reply to a Reuters email seeking comment.* Last week, Reuters reported that Bank of Maharashtra and Canara Bank were looking to raise $500 million each through dollar debt in September.* The bonds will be rated BBB by S&P and BBB- by Fitch Ratings, in line with Bank of Maharashtra's credit ratings.* The proceeds will be used to meet funding requirements at the bank's India head office and foreign branches, and for general corporate purposes.* Indian banks have raised $12.5 billion through dollar bonds since June, when the central bank announced the swap window.* State Bank of India, Bank of Baroda and the Union Bank of India are the other state-run lenders that have tapped the dollar debt market in 2026.(Reporting by Dharamraj Dhutia; Editing by Eileen Soreng) Reuters Published On Sep 17, 2026 at 11:52 AM IST Telegram Facebook Copy Link Comments What are your thoughts?
