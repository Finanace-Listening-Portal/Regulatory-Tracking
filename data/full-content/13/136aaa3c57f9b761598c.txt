Outward remittance for permitted purposes Resident individuals may use the Liberalised Remittance Scheme for permitted current or capital account transactions within the prevailing aggregate annual limit, subject to exclusions and documentation. Prithvi’s terms refer to the USD 250,000 per financial year LRS ceiling. The customer must aggregate remittances across authorised dealers and disclose prior utilisation. Education, medical treatment, travel, maintenance of relatives, emigration and employment-related remittances can require purpose-specific evidence in addition to PAN and Form A2. LRS does not permit remittances for trading in foreign exchange abroad, including margin or margin-call payments to overseas exchanges or counterparties. An overseas platform’s FCA, ASIC, CySEC or other foreign licence does not create Indian resident eligibility. The absence of a name from RBI’s Alert List is likewise not affirmative approval. A customer should test the purpose against RBI rules, not merely against a recipient’s marketing description. Rates Card Charges GST and TCS Foreign-exchange cost is not only the visible service fee. It can include the spread between the provider’s buy or sell rate and a reference market rate, fixed processing charges, correspondent-bank deductions, card network charges and taxes. Prithvi’s exchange rate is quote-dependent. The customer should compare the all-in rupee amount for the specified currency and delivery result rather than assuming that an online headline rate is executable. For the company’s Iconic travel card, the published schedule lists a card-issuance charge of ₹100, nil reload and wallet-transfer charges, a USD 2 equivalent ATM-withdrawal charge, a USD 0.50 equivalent balance-enquiry charge, a 3 percent cross-currency charge, a 2 percent markup and specified inactivity, replacement and courier charges, with taxes extra. Those figures relate to that published product schedule and may be amended; they should not be generalized to every service or card programme. GST may apply to money-changing value and service charges under the statutory valuation rules. For LRS remittances, tax collection at source depends on the amount, purpose, source of education funding and current threshold and rate. The authorised dealer may collect TCS at execution, but the amount is generally a tax credit rather than a separate remittance-purpose entitlement. Customers should verify the current rule and ensure PAN details permit credit matching.
