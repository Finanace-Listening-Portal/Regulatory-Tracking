Government bonds also came under heavy selling pressure, with the yield on the benchmark 10-year government bond jumping 9 basis points to 7.39 per cent, the highest since March 10, 2023. The RBI’s measures to drain liquidity from the banking system directly, alongside foreign exchange operations that could indirectly reduce the surplus, added to pressure on bond prices. The resulting rise in yields signalled higher borrowing costs, weighing in turn on equity valuations, particularly in interest-rate-sensitive sectors, such as banking. The 30-share Sensex erased its opening gains to close down 547.65 points, or 0.76 per cent, at 71,924.68. It fell as much as 796.23 points, or 1 per cent, to 71,676.10 during the session. The Nifty 50 declined 157.10 points, or 0.70 per cent, to settle at 22,363.35.
