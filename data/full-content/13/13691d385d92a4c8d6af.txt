Summary: This article explains the procedure for creating and managing stock groups and stock items using TallyPrime, along with recording Goods and Services Tax (GST) transactions and preparing accounting reports. It describes how stock groups help classify similar inventory items and how individual stock items are created for effective inventory management. Step-by-step instructions are provided for creating, displaying, altering and deleting stock groups and stock items through the Gateway of Tally, supported by screenshots and tables. The article also covers the basic configuration required for GST accounting, including enabling GST, creating tax ledgers for Input CGST, SGST and IGST and Output CGST, SGST and IGST, and recording purchase and sales transactions through appropriate vouchers. Illustrative journal entries explain the accounting treatment of intra-state and inter-state purchases and sales. Additionally, the article introduces budgeting and forecasting through accounting software, highlighting how TallyPrime assists businesses in preparing financial plans, allocating resources and comparing actual performance against budgets. Overall, the article serves as a practical introductory guide for students, accounting professionals and businesses seeking to understand inventory management, GST accounting and budgeting functions in TallyPrime.
