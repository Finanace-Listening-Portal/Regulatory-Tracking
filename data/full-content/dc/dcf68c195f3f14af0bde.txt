EconomyLending To Small Businesses Can Deliver Greater Economic Benefits Than Big Corporate Loans: RBI GovernorAuthored by: Vikas KumarUpdated: Nov 22, 2026, 16:13 ISTRBI Governor further added that the benefits could be a thousand times greater if the amount were distributed among micro-enterprise borrowers.Reserve Bank of India Governor Sanjay Malhotra (PTI Photo/Kunal Patil)Photo : PTIfollow usfollow usThe Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Governor Sanjay Malhotra urged banks to increase lending to small and micro enterprises. He said the economic benefits of distributing credit among smaller businesses could be significantly greater than extending large loans to individual corporate borrowers.Governor Malhotra said a loan of Rs 1,000 crore to a large business enterprise would benefit only one individual or business."However, if we provide the same amount to small enterprises, the overall impact and benefits could be at least a hundred times greater," he said while speaking at a financial inclusion camp organised by Bank of Maharashtra at Vaksai village in Mawal taluka, Pune district. The RBI Governor further added that the benefits could be a thousand times greater if the amount were distributed among micro-enterprise borrowers.The RBI Governor said his appeal was based on the gains achieved through banks' efforts to extend services to rural communities and economically weaker sections."We must remember that development should be inclusive. When we talk about development, it must be development for everyone.
