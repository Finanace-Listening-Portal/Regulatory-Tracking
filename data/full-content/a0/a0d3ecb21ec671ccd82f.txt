These tablets, available in dosages of 50 mg/500 mg and 50 mg/1,000 mg, are bioequivalent to the reference listed drug Janumet® Tablets, produced by Merck Sharp and Dohme LLC. The approval marks a significant step for Lupin in expanding its portfolio in the U.S. market, particularly in the diabetes treatment segment. The Sitagliptin and Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets are designed to serve as an adjunct to diet and exercise, aiming to improve glycemic control in adults suffering from type 2 diabetes mellitus. With the increasing prevalence of diabetes, the approval of these tablets is poised to offer an essential alternative for patients seeking effective management of their condition. The reference drug, Janumet®, had estimated annual sales of USD 439.2 million in the United States as of September 2026, according to IQVIA data, indicating a substantial market opportunity for Lupin. Headquartered in Mumbai, India, Lupin Limited is a global pharmaceutical leader with a robust presence across various markets, including the U.S., India, and other developed and emerging markets. The company boasts a diversified portfolio that spans generics, complex generics, specialty medicines, and biosimilars. Lupin’s strategic focus on strengthening its market position through a differentiated portfolio is evident in its leadership across core therapeutic areas such as cardiovascular, respiratory, diabetes, gastrointestinal, and women’s health. The approval from the U.S. FDA is expected to bolster Lupin’s standing in the competitive pharmaceutical landscape, particularly in the diabetes segment. Analysts view this development as a positive stride for Lupin, enhancing its product offerings and potentially increasing its market share in the U.S. The company’s commitment to sustainability and innovation continues to underpin its growth strategy, as it expands its footprint in the global healthcare ecosystem.
