Articles 3 min read Equitas SFB says CEO transition will not alter universal banking license strategy Equitas Small Finance Bank appointed an interim MD and CEO despite PN Vasudevan being available after his resignation, citing governance practices and stakeholder interests. The bank expects to appoint a full-time successor within four months and is evaluating both internal and external candidates, while maintaining that its five year business plan remains unchanged. Its current strategy will continue, and the change in leadership is not expected to affect its long-term plans.The bank is in the process of finding a successor to former managing director and chief executive officer PN Vasudevan, who resigned on October 29, 2026. Advt The board appointed executive director Balaji Nuthalapadi as interim MD and CEO, with the RBI approving his appointment on November 6. Vasudevan was also relieved of his responsibilities on the same day, at his request.The board said the appointment of an interim chief executive was considered necessary in the interest of governance and stakeholders after Vasudevan's resignation came unexpectedly."The board decided what it thought appropriate at that moment. However, when the board met again to deliberate on the matter, it was felt that appointing an interim CEO would be in line with the best governance practices and will protect the interest of all the stakeholders," chairman Anil Kumar Sharma said during the bank's analyst call.Search for new CEOThe bank has initiated the process to appoint a full-time MD and CEO and is considering both internal and external candidates. A four-member committee comprising independent directors has been constituted to oversee the selection process.The committee has already met search agencies, and the board hopes to shortlist candidates within a month. The appointment, along with the necessary regulatory approvals, is expected to be completed within four months."We will be evaluating both internal as well as external candidates, and we will be going by what we have decided we are looking for in the new MD and CEO. Whosoever meets the criteria, internal or external, is not important," Sharma said. Advt The board is looking for a candidate with experience in financial services and banking, a track record of achievements, leadership and team-building capabilities, and the ability to take the bank's growth forward and build a pan-India franchise.Vasudevan submitted his resignation on October 29, citing a "perceived conflict of interest", according to the explanation in his resignation letter, the chairman said.The issue relates to his interest in Unico Housing Finance. Responding to questions on whether this had been a concern for the board or the regulator, Sharma said both were aware of the interest and had no concerns. Advt Five-year strategy remains intactThe board said the change in leadership would not affect the bank's five-year business plan or its long-term vision. While the incoming full-time CEO may make adjustments, the broad strategic direction is expected to remain unchanged.Nuthalapadi, who has been with Equitas since March 2025 as executive director overseeing technology, operations, digital services, regulatory compliance and governance, said his immediate priority was to ensure stability and continuity.He also said the bank's growth momentum and execution of its existing plans remained on track, with no fundamental change in its internal business environment following the leadership transition. Advt The bank said its experienced leadership team and established institutional processes would help ensure continuity in day-to-day operations during the interim period.
