This timing mismatch creates an immediate earnings drag even though the economics play out over a longer period.Net payment margin, excluding UPI merchant discount rate (MDR), improved to 9 basis points from 8.5 basis points, supported by pricing discipline, equated monthly instalment (EMI) offerings, dynamic currency conversion (DCC) and enterprise acquiring. Management expects absolute EBITDA to increase quarter-on-quarter despite DLG costs, according to JM Financial. Advt One large lender has resumed DLG arrangements with Paytm after a gap of six quarters. Reserve Bank of India guidelines on offsetting DLG against credit losses may be a factor in the resumption, the report said.Paytm's consumer personal loan business is scaling faster than expected and follows a pure-distribution model, with no incremental first-loss default guarantee (FLDG) exposure for the company. Merchant lending remains strong across smaller-ticket and larger-ticket products. Merchant-loan expected credit loss metrics remain broadly within the previously indicated range.The company's postpaid business is scaling well and remains contribution-margin positive. Paytm aims to rebuild the business towards historical quarterly disbursements of around Rs 90 billion and approximately 10 million customers over time. It is also using daily gross merchandise value (GMV) monitoring and early-warning signals to improve collections.Higher device capital expenditure is expected to increase depreciation, although management expects the investment to improve merchant engagement, retention and financial services revenue. Spending remains focused on high-quality merchants with stronger engagement and monetisation potential.Cloud costs have increased mainly because of the consolidation of online and offline platforms, with some impact potentially continuing into the next quarter. Paytm expects its artificial intelligence investments to deliver immediate benefits through higher revenue, improved collections and operating efficiency.AI push Advt The company is also developing an AI-led operating platform for small businesses, with subscription fees and transaction-linked charges as its monetisation model. Revenue from the platform will be classified under marketing services. Deployment timelines have improved from three to four weeks to two to three days. Paytm already charges a monthly platform fee and takes a percentage of advertising spend through its tie-up with Meta.Paytm may invest up to Rs 5 billion over the next 12 months in PCTL and/or overseas subsidiaries, although actual deployment could be lower. PCTL primarily funds overseas merchant-payment and lending ventures, while One97 Communications Singapore houses the legacy marketing-cloud business. Advt Softer market volumes are affecting equity broking, while travel and wealth/gold businesses are also facing headwinds.
