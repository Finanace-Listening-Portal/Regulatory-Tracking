Advertisement (adsbygoogle = window.adsbygoogle || []).push({}); In re Siemens Limited (CAAR Mumbai) Summary: The Customs Authority for Advance Rulings (CAAR), Mumbai, examained an application filed by Siemens Limited concerning the classification of three components proposed to be imported for manufacturing and assembling its SIMATIC Industrial Computing Platform (IPC), model RW-543B, in India. The components comprised a motherboard, chassis and power supply unit (PSU), which Siemens proposed to import from China. The applicant explained that the complete industrial computer was an Automatic Data Processing (ADP) machine classifiable under Heading 8471, but sought separate classification of the three imported components under the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. The principal questions concerned their classification under the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act and the eligibility of the power supply for exemption under Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus., dated 01.03.2005. The Authority examined the technical characteristics of the proposed imports, the General Rules for the Interpretation of the Customs Tariff, the relevant Section and Chapter Notes, and the HSN Explanatory Notes. It observed that the motherboard constituted the principal printed circuit board of the IPC, providing electronic connectivity between the processor, memory, storage and other components. The chassis was a specifically designed mechanical structure intended to house and support the components of the industrial computer. Both were intended for incorporation into the complete SIMATIC IPC and, in their imported condition, did not independently constitute complete ADP machines. Applying the provisions governing parts and accessories of machines falling under Heading 8471, the Authority held that the motherboard was classifiable under Tariff Item 8473 30 20 and the chassis under Tariff Item 8473 30 99. Regarding the power supply unit, the Authority found that its principal function was to receive alternating current (AC), convert it into regulated direct current (DC) and supply the resulting power to electronic components. Since the PSU performed the identifiable function of a static converter, it was covered independently under Heading 8504 rather than being classified merely as a part of an ADP machine under Heading 8473. Applying Note 2(a) to Section XVI and the relevant HSN Explanatory Notes, the Authority classified the PSU under Tariff Item 8504 40 29. It further held that the PSU, being a static converter specifically designed and intended for use with an ADP machine falling under Heading 8471, was eligible for exemption under Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus., dated 01.03.2005, subject to fulfilment of the notification conditions and applicable BIS certification requirements. The Authority accordingly ruled in favour of the stated classifications of all three components and the conditional exemption eligibility of the power supply unit. It expressly confined the ruling to the goods, technical specifications, documents and submissions examined, leaving the appropriate customs authorities to verify the imported goods, their intended use, compliance with technical requirements and fulfilment of statutory and notification conditions at the time of importation. Since there is no specific tariff item under Sub-I leading 847330 for such Chassis, the same falls under the residual tariff item “Other”, namely tariff item 8473 30 99. 10.27 Accordingly, I find that the Chassis, being a specially designed and model-specific part suitable for use solely or principally with the Industrial PC classifiable under [leading 8471, and not being specifically covered by any other tariff item under Sub-heading 8473.30, is appropriately classifiable under Tariff Item 8473 30 99 of the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. Classification of “PSU” 10.28 I now proceed to examine the classification of the Power Supply Unit (“PSU”). From the technical description furnished by the Applicant and the material available on record, I find that the PSU is an AC-to-DC switching power supply. Its primary function is to receive incoming AC electrical power and convert the same into regulated DC power required for operation of the CPU, RAM, storage devices and other electronic components of the IPC. 10.29 The IISN Explanatory Notes to Heading 8504, under “Electrical Static Converters”, state that such apparatus is used to convert electrical energy in order to adapt it for further use. The Notes further include rectifiers by which alternating current is converted to direct current, generally accompanied by a voltage change. The Notes also clarify that such apparatus may incorporate auxiliary devices for regulation of voltage or current without affecting their classification as static converters. The relevant portion the HSN Notes is reproduced below: “(II) ELECTRICAL STATIC CONVERTERS The apparatus of this group are used to convert electrical energy in order to adapt it for further use. They incorporate converting elements (e.g., valves) of different types. They may also incorporate various auxiliary devices (e.g, transformers, induction coils, resistors, command regulators, etc.). Their operation is based on the principle that the converting elements act alternately as conductors and non-conductors. The fact that these apparatuses often incorporate auxiliary circuits to regulate the voltage of the emerging current does not affect their classification in this group, nor does the fact that they are sometimes referred to as voltage or current regulators. This group includes: A. Rectifiers by which alternating current (single or polrphase) is converted to direct current, generally accompanied by a voltage change. B. Inverters by which direct current is converted to alternating current. C. Alternating current converters and cycle converters by which alternating current (single or polyphase) is converted to a different frequency or voltage. D. Direct current converters by which direct current is converted to a different direct current voltages. *** This heading also includes stabilised suppliers (rectifiers combined with a regulator), e.g., uninterruptible power supply units for a range of electronic equipment. 10.30 In the present case, the PSU receives incoming AC electrical power and converts it into regulated I)C power required for the operation of the electronic components of the IPC. I find that its essential function is, therefore, the conversion of electrical energy from AC to I)C and that it possesses the essential characteristics of an electrical static converter (rectifier) covered under Ileading 8504. 10.31 Since the PSU is itself a good specifically covered under a heading of Chapter 85, Note 2(a) to Section XVI (supra para 10.17) requires it to be classified in its respective heading, notwithstanding that it is designed for installation in the IPC. Accordingly, its classification is to be determined under 1-16ading 8504 rather than as a part of the ADP machine under Heading 8473 10.32 Heading 8504 of the Customs Tariff covers “Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rectifiers) and inductors”. The relevant tariff structure is reproduced below: 8504 – Electrical transformers, static converters (for example, rectifiers) and Inductors 850410 – Ballasts for discharge lamps or tubes: * * * 850440 – Static converters: 85044010 – UPS (Static Converters) 85044010 – Electric inverter Rectifier: 85044021 – Dip bridge rectifier 85044029 – Other 10.33 Applying Rule 1 of the General Rules for the Interpretation of the Customs Tariff, read with Note 2(a) to Section XVI, I find that the subject Power Supply Unit, being an AC-to-DC switching power supply having the essential characteristics of a static converter (rectifier) and not being covered by the specific tariff items for UPS, electric inverter or dip bridge rectifier, is appropriately classifiable under Tariff Item 85044029 of the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. 10.34 The jurisdictional Commissionerate has also examined the technical description of the PSU and has opined that the same performs the function of conversion of AC power into regulated DC power and is therefore covered under Heading 8504 as a static converter. TheCommissionerate has further agreed that, in terms of Note 2(a) to Section XVI, the PSU is required to be classified in its own specific heading and has specifically indicated Tariff Item 8504 40 29, subject to verification of the technical specifications and actual nature of the goods at the time of importation. 10.35 I find the above view of the jurisdictional Commissionerate to be consistent with the tariff provisions and the technical characteristics of the PSU placed on record. Accordingly, the Power Supply Unit proposed to be imported is appropriately classifiable under Tariff Item 85044029 of the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. BIS Requirement 10.36 I further find that, having been classified under Tariff Item 8504 40 29, the subject Power Supply Unit (PSU) falls under CTH 8504 40, which is specifically covered at Sr. No. 18 of the First Schedule to the Machinery and Electrical Equipment Safety (Omnibus Technical Regulation) Order, 2024, notified vide S.O. 3649(E) dated 28.08.2024. The relevant of the First Schedule is reproduced below: The First Schedule fSee rules 1(3), 3(1), 3(2), 4 and8i List of Machinery and Electrical Equipment covered under this Order: S No Description of Machinery and Electrical Equipment HS Code 2 18 All types of Power Semiconductor Converter and (or) their assemblies/sub-assemblies/components 850440 The aforesaid entry covers “All types of Power Semiconductor Converter and (or) their assemblies/sub-assemblies/components” falling under CITI 850440. Since the subject PSU is classifiable under Tariff Item 85044029 and consequently falls under CTH 850440, I find that it is covered within the scope of the said regulatory requirement. Accordingly, the subject PSU shall be required to comply with the applicable BIS certification requirements at the time of importation, in accordance with the provisions of the said Order. Eligibility of PSU for exemption under Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005 10.37 Having determined the classification of the PSU under Tariff Item 85044029, the next issue for consideration is whether the same is eligible for exemption from Basic Customs Duty under Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005. 10.38 Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005 provides exemption in respect of the following goods: Sr. No. Heading/Subheading/Tariff Item Description of goods 4 850440 Static converters for automatic data processing machines and units thereof, and telecommunication apparatus, other than static converters for cellular mobile phones 10.39 From the above entry, it is evident that the exemption is not linked merely to the tariff classification under Sub-Heading 850440, but also to the nature and intended use of the static converter, namely, for automatic data processing machines and units thereof or telecommunication apparatus, subject to the exclusion specified in the notification. 10.40 In the present case, I have already found that the-complete SIMATIC IPC is an Automatic Data Processing machine falling under I leading 8471. The technical documents and submissions establish that the subject PSU is specifically designed and intended to supply regulated DC power to the various components of the said IPC. The PSU is therefore a static converter intended for use with an Automatic Data Processing machine. 10.41 I further find that there is no material indicate that the subject PSU is a static converter specifically designed for use with cellular mobile phones. On the contrary, the material placed on record establishes that the PSU is specifically designed and intended for use in the manufacture and assembly of the Industrial Computing Platform (IPC). Therefore, its use with the IPC is clearly established on record. 10.42 Accordingly, I find that the subject PSU satisfies the description of goods specified at Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005. The Power Supply Unit, being a static converter falling under Sub-I leading 850440 and specifically designed and intended for use with an Automatic Data Processing machine, is therefore eligible for the benefit of the exemption under Serial No. 4 of the said Notification, subject to fulfilment of the other applicable conditions, if any, prescribed therein and compliance at the time of importation. 10.43 In view of the foregoing discussion and findings, I hold that the classification of the subject goods proposed to be imported by the Applicant is required to be determined independently on the basis of the nature, characteristics and specific tariff description of each item in its imported condition. The Motherboard and Chassis, being parts of an Industrial Computing Platform classifiable under Heading 84.71, fall under Heading 84.73, whereas the PSU, being itself a static converter specifically covered under Heading 85.04, is required to be classified in its own heading in terms of Note 2(a) to Section XVI. 10.44 Accordingly, I find that: i. the Motherboard is classifiable under Tariff Item 84733020; ii. the Chassis is classifiable under Tariff Item 84733099; and iii. the Power Supply Unit (PSU) is classifiable under Tariff item 85044029 and is subject to the applicable BIS Certification requirements 10.45 Further, the Power Supply Unit, being a static converter falling under Sub-Heading 850440 and specifically designed and intended for use with an Automatic Data Processing machine, is eligible for the benefit of exemption under Serial No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005, subject to fulfilment of the conditions prescribed under the said notification. 11. In view of the foregoing, it is clarified that the present ruling is confined to determination of the classification of the subject Motherboard, Chassis and Power Supply Unit (PSU) on the basis of the facts, technical specifications, documents and submissions placed on record. The ruling determines the classification of the subject goods under the respective tariff items as set out hereinabove and, in the case of the PSU, also examines the applicability of the relevant regulatory requirement under the Machinery and Electrical Equipment Safely (Omnibus Technical Regulation) Order, 2024. The ruling is based on the technical literature, product descriptions, model details and other documents furnished by the Applicant. The jurisdictional Customs authorities shall, however, be at liberty to undertake such examination, sampling and verification of the imported goods as may be deemed necessary at the time of importation, including verification with reference to the relevant technical literature, catalogues, model numbers, specifications and other accompanying documents, to ascertain that the goods imported conform to the goods and technical particulars considered in the present ruling and comply with the applicable Acts, Rules, Regulations and other statutory requirements. 12. In view of the foregoing discussion, findings, and the legal provisions discussed hereinabove, I rule as under: I. The subject goods, namely (i) Motherboard, (ii) Chassis, and (iii) Power Supply Unit (PSU), being parts of an Automatic Data Processing (ADP) machine falling under I leading 8471, are appropriately classifiable under Customs Tariff Items (CTIs) 8473 30 20, 8473 30 99 and 8504 40 29, respectively, of the First Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1975. II. The subject goods, namely, “Power Supply Unit (PSU)”, being a static converter for an Automatic Data Processing (ADP) machine falling under Heading 8471, is covered under SI. No. 4 of Notification No. 25/2005-Cus. dated 01.03.2005, as amended and is accordingly eligible for exemption from the applicable Basic Customs Duty (13CD) under the said notification, subject to fulfilment of the prescribed conditions, including the production of the requisite BIS Certificate, and verification thereof by the jurisdictional Customs authorities at the time of importation. 13. I rule accordingly.
