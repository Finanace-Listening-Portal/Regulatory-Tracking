Articles 2 min read Global FinTech Funding August 2026: Global FinTechs raise USD 5.77 billion across 106 deals Global FinTech funding stood at USD 5.77 billion across 106 deals in August 2026, excluding India, with funding led by large transactions in payments, digital banking, commercial financing, digital assets, wealth management, lending and financial infrastructure. The top 20 deals alone accounted for USD 5.26 billion. Funding activity spanned digital payments, digital banking, commercial financing, cryptocurrency, blockchain infrastructure, wealth management, mobility financing, insurance and core banking technology.The largest deal of the month came from Imprint, which raised USD 2 billion for its white-label solutions for payment cards. The round was backed by Scotiabank, RBC Royal Bank, TD Bank, Citi, Mizuho Financial Group, Truist and HSBC.This was followed by Kish Bank, a provider of digital banking solutions for payroll and employees, which secured USD 900 million from Grupo Leste. Advt Another major transaction was Bridgemarketplace, which raised USD 500 million for its commercial financing platform connecting businesses. The round was backed by LuminArx.The fourth-largest deal came from inKind, which raised USD 414 million for its digital dining rewards platform that allows users to earn cashback and rewards. Investors included Citi, Cross River Bank, Sagard, Varadero Capital and Trinity Capital.Among other notable large deals:Ripple raised USD 275 million for its blockchain and financial infrastructure solutions from Piper Sandler.Aajab secured USD 270 million for its online investing platform for stocks and mutual funds from SBI Group.Moove raised USD 250 million for its revenue-based vehicle financing business from Mubadala, Woven Capital, Ion Pacific, Sona Asset Management, BlueCrest Capital Management, Raptor Group, BlackRock, MUFG, Franklin Templeton Investments, Uber and Left Lane.Mundi raised USD 150 million for its online marketplace for invoice factoring from Column.USD AI secured USD 100 million for its financial technology platform from Bullish.Natural raised USD 100 million for its financial technology infrastructure for agentic payments from Upper90.Digital assets, accounting technology and financial infrastructure also attracted significant investor interest: Advt Rillet raised USD 100 million for its AI-native ERP platform designed to automate accounting. The round saw participation from Iconiq, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Bain Capital Ventures, Oak HC/FT, Battery Ventures, FirstMark, Scale Venture Partners and Creandum.Clearco secured USD 100 million for its revenue-based financing solution from Macquarie.Ume raised USD 96.7 million for its online point-of-sale financing platform from Itaú BBA, Banco Bradesco, XP, Milenio Capital, Saison International and Verde Asset Management.RQD raised USD 74 million for its clearing and settlement platform for trades from Bain Capital, ABN Amro and Nyca Partners. Advt Fasset secured USD 68 million for its blockchain-based platform to buy and sell crypto assets and tokens from SBI Group, Speedinvest and others.Travel insurance, payment infrastructure, cloud banking and cryptocurrency platforms also featured among the month's larger fundraises. Faye raised USD 50 million for its app-based travel insurance platform, while Yuno secured USD 45 million for payment routing APIs for businesses. 10x raised USD 40 million for its cloud-native SaaS platform for core banking, and Yellow Card Financial raised USD 40 million for its online cryptocurrency trading and selling platform. Advt Meanwhile, JPYC, which operates a prepaid Japanese Yen stablecoin on a public blockchain, raised USD 38.1 million from Az-Com-Maruwa-Hd.
