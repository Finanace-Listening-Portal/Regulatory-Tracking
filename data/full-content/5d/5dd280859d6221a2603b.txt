Exercising its powers under Section 14(2) of the Customs Act, 1962, the Board has substituted all three tables prescribing tariff values for specified imported goods. The revised Table-1 prescribes tariff values for crude palm oil at US$ 1,174, RBD palm oil at US$ 1,200, other palm oil at US$ 1,187, crude palmolein at US$ 1,204, RBD palmolein at US$ 1,207 and other palmolein at US$ 1,206 per metric tonne. Crude soya bean oil is valued at US$ 1,291 per metric tonne, while brass scrap of all grades is valued at US$ 8,242 per metric tonne. Under Table-2, the tariff value for specified gold imports is fixed at US$ 1,295 per 10 grams, while specified silver imports attract a tariff value of US$ 1,711 per kilogram. These values also apply to the other specified forms of gold and silver, subject to the descriptions, conditions and exclusions prescribed in the notification. Table-3 retains the tariff value of areca nuts at US$ 10,433 per metric tonne, expressly indicating no change. The revised tariff values become effective from 31 October 2026. Importers and customs professionals should apply the substituted values while determining the assessable value of the covered imported goods from the effective date.
