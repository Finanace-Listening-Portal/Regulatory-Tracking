In order to facilitate the data requirements of Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MOSPI) in preparation of Index of Service Production (ISP), information on Premium Income, Investment Income and Claims of insurers is required. 2. General and Health Insurers (excluding Reinsurers) are required from July 2026 onwards to submit Gross Direct Premium, Claims Paid (Direct) and Investment Income of Policyholders and Shareholders. Life insurers are required to submit Premium (Gross), Benefits paid (Gross) and Investment Income for Policyholders and Shareholders. 3. The details shall be submitted through the National Informatics Centre (NIC) online data templates created for the purpose. Annexure A- Data Template for Life Insurers Annexure B- Data Template for General and Health Insurers The link for the online data templates will be shared separately. 4. The details through the NIC form shall be submitted on or before the 15 th day of the month succeeding the reporting month. Insurers are advised to ensure that the information furnished is complete and accurate at the time of submission. 5. This circular is issued under Section 14(2)(h) of IRDA Act, 1999, with the limited purpose of enabling life insurers, General and Health Insurers to meet the data requirements of Index of Service Production (ISP). These requirements do not in any manner, modify the disclosure requirements or the manner of preparation of financial statements as required under the Insurance Act, 1938, IRDA Act,1999 and the Regulations framed thereunder. ./Sd./- ( ) / Member (F&I)
