The appointment was approved by the corporation's board at its meeting held on November 25.She joined the corporation in 1993 as a Direct Recruit Officer and is a Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). With more than three decades of experience, she has handled Finance and Accounts, Debt & Project Portfolio, and stressed assets in the Investment departments, in addition to working across claims, office services and personnel departments. Advt Garware Hi-Tech Films appointed Anand H. Sharma as CFO and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), effective November 26, 2026. The appointment was approved based on the recommendations of the nomination and remuneration committee and audit committee. Consequent to the appointment, Prashant L. Pai, who was serving as interim CFO, ceased to hold the position with immediate effect.Sharma is a qualified chartered accountant, company secretary and cost & management accountant with over 25 years of experience across finance, accounts, audit, taxation, risk management, cross-border acquisitions and investor relations.The Mumbai-based company manufactures specialty performance polyester films including sun-control and paint-protection films.Meanwhile, Confidence Petroleum India appointed Krishna Kumar as joint chief financial officer and KMP with effect from November 25, 2026. According to the company's disclosure, Kumar is a qualified finance professional with over 18 years of experience across corporate finance, investor relations, treasury and capital markets.The company operates in the LPG and CNG business and is headquartered at Nagpur.Fino Payments Bank regularised the appointment of Anup Agarwal as CFO, effective November 25, 2026 following the recommendation of its audit committee. He will continue as KMP and senior management personnel of the bank. Agarwal is a qualified chartered accountant with 20 years of experience in finance and has been associated with the bank since June 2022. Advt Canara HSBC Life Insurance CFO Tarun Rastogi ceased to hold the position effective from the close of business hours on November 24, 2026. He had tendered his resignation on August 25, 2026, to pursue growth opportunities outside the organisation.
