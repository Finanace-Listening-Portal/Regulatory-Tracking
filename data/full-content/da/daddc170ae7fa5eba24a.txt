Articles 1 min read FinTech Funding October 2026: Indian FinTechs raise USD 76 million across 6 deals The largest deal of the month came from Even, which raised USD 50 million from Khosla Ventures and Alpha Wave Global, followed by StockGro and Fission Labs, which raised USD 10 million each. Shrishti Sharma ETBFSI Published On Nov 11, 2026 at 06:07 PM IST var news_msid = 135737055; console.log(news_msid); Set ETBFSI as your preferred source on Google Copy Link Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on X Share on Telegram Share on Facebook Indian FinTechs raised USD 76 million across six equity funding rounds in October 2026, according to data collated by Tracxn.The month witnessed funding activity across healthcare and insurance technology, investment and trading platforms, blockchain-based financial infrastructure, lending and debt resolution, and cross-border payments.The largest deal of the month came from Even, a healthcare platform offering managed care services, doctor consultations and health insurance solutions, which raised USD 50 million in a Series B round from Khosla Ventures and Alpha Wave Global.This was followed by StockGro, a stock market advisory and research platform, which raised USD 10 million in a Series B round from Aart Corporate Advisors. Advt Another notable transaction was Fission Labs, a financial technology company building blockchain-based infrastructure for tokenised investment funds, which raised USD 10 million in a Series A round. The round saw participation from Pantera Capital, Circle, Borderless Capital, New Form Capital, gCC, Manhattan Venture Partners, Reflexive Capital and Lattice, along with angel investors Ari Rubenstein and Anthony Scaramucci.Together, the three largest transactions accounted for USD 70 million, or nearly 92 per cent of the total funding raised during October.Among the other deals during the month:Tradl, an artificial intelligence-powered trading platform, raised USD 4 million in a seed funding round from Stellaris Venture Partners.CredFix, a financial technology platform connecting lenders and borrowers, particularly those facing financial distress, raised USD 2 million in a seed round from Stargazer Growth and Tanglin Venture Partners.Saber, a provider of stablecoin-powered cross-border payment infrastructure, also raised an undisclosed amount in a seed funding round from Tether and Titan.Funding activity in October was lower than the previous month, with total funding declining to USD 76 million across six deals, compared with USD 111 million across 10 deals in September 2026.
