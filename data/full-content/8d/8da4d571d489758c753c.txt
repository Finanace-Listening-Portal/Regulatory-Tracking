RBL and Kotak are also among the banks with the lowest leverage against these deposits, giving them greater headroom to deploy the funds into higher-yielding assets.RBL Bank accounted for 2.6% of the total FCNR(B) deposits mobilised under the scheme, against a 0.5% share of the overall banking deposit market. ICICI Bank had the largest absolute share at 13.4%, followed by HSBC at 10.9%, HDFC Bank at 8.6%, State Bank of India at 8.2% and Axis Bank at 8%, according to Motilal Oswal Financial Services. However, the extent to which banks can benefit from the mobilisation depends on how much of these funds they have already deployed or leveraged.RBL Bank and Kotak Mahindra Bank have among the lowest leverage against FCNR(B) deposits, leaving them with greater scope to deploy the funds into higher-yielding assets. Aggregate leverage against these deposits across the banking sector stood at around 58%.IDFC First Bank, despite its relatively high share of FCNR(B) mobilisation, was among the banks that had self-leveraged 70-86% of these deposits, along with YES Bank. IDFC First Bank also reported a 16 basis points contraction in net interest margin in the September quarter, reflecting the cost of carrying surplus liquidity.The FCNR(B) windowThe RBI's special FCNR(B) window was open from June 8 to August 31, 2026, and mobilised $133 billion in deposits with tenures of three to five years. The central bank bore the entire hedging cost, while banks were exempted from maintaining cash reserve ratio and statutory liquidity ratio requirements against these deposits.Public sector banks mobilised a relatively smaller proportion, equivalent to 2-5% of their deposit base. Their lower mobilisation and use of a substantial part of the funds to repay high-cost borrowings helped support margins in the September quarter, while private banks faced greater near-term margin pressure. Advt The deposits are expected to remain a drag on net interest margins through the December quarter. Motilal Oswal expects surplus liquidity to be progressively deployed into higher-yielding assets, supporting earnings accretion from the March quarter onwards.
