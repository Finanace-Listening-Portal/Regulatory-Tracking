JUSTICE GITA GOPI Date : 14/06/2023 COMMON ORAL JUDGMENT 1.The Central Bureau of Investigation has challenged the orders passed by learned Special C.B.I. Judge, Ahmedabad, discharging the accused of the matters, where the C.B.I. had registered a case as FIR No.RC-12(A)/2000, at Gandhinagar on 23.05.2000 under sections 120B, 420, 467, 468 and 471 of Indian Penal Code and section 13(2) read with section 13(1)(d) of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 (for short ‘P.C. Act’). 2.The allegation against the officials of four public sector oil companies viz. IOCL, HPCL, BPCL and IBP, are that they sold the High Speed Diesel (for short ‘HSD’) to various private industries of three States viz.
