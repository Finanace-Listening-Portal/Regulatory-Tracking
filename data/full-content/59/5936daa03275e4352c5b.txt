UPI already accounts for 75-77% of transactions on its point-of-sale (POS) terminals, with volumes growing at an annual rate of around 68% over the past two years.More than 65% of its UPI GTV comes from transactions exceeding Rs 2,000, potentially providing a sizeable base for monetisation if MDR is introduced on eligible transactions. The company also plans to deploy at least one million additional soundboxes to capture transactions currently processed through competing devices at existing merchant locations, according to an ICICI Securities report. Advt However, the potential revenue from UPI monetisation will depend on the final MDR framework and the company's ability to pass on the charges to merchants. Management acknowledged that commercial negotiations may be necessary with large merchants. The net take rate remains uncertain because of merchant-category-based pricing, exemptions and individual commercial arrangements.The brokerage has factored in Rs 160 crore of MDR revenue for FY28. It estimates EBITDA at Rs 950 crore for the year with MDR, compared with Rs 800 crore without it. The company is seeking to increase its share of UPI transactions through its existing merchant network, supported by additional soundbox deployments.Pine Labs' digital infrastructure and transaction platform (DITP) processed Rs 4.3 lakh crore of GTV in Q2FY27, up 5% year on year. The segment comprises in-store payments, flow, affordability and transaction processing, online payments and fintech infrastructure. In-store payments contributed Rs 1.25 lakh crore, while flow, affordability and transaction processing accounted for Rs 96,000 crore. Online payments contributed Rs 27,000 crore and fintech infrastructure Rs 1.79 lakh crore.The flow, affordability and transaction-processing business recorded 51.7% growth in GTV to Rs 96,000 crore, supported by broad-based expansion across affordability, UPI and transaction processing. Flow-based income grew 25% year on year, while transaction volumes increased around 50%, aided by market-share gains in UPI and growth across payment services. Advt The company is also diversifying this revenue stream beyond consumer financing and equated monthly instalment (EMI) transactions. It includes dynamic currency conversion, aggregator-based transaction fees, services that help online platforms complete transactions at physical stores, and data-driven marketing and advertising services. These newer offerings are becoming a larger part of the business and helping diversify its revenue sources.Expansion plansPine Labs is expanding its affordability business beyond consumer durables and information technology products. Growth in non-electronics categories and newer use cases is helping offset weakness in individual product segments. The company also provides instalment payment services to online platforms, including quick-commerce businesses selling electronics, mobile phones and consumer durables. Management expects affordability services to expand further in Q3, with a wider range of product categories beyond electronics. Advt Its merchant infrastructure business is also expanding. Subscription revenues account for around 27% of total revenue, while its digital commerce platform (DCP) base increased 17% year on year to 2.23 million in Q2FY27. The mid-market DCP base grew 49%. Online payment growth also remained strong, with revenues increasing more than 45% year on year.Fintech infrastructure continued to scale, with transactions growing more than 35% year on year to 370 million. Signal IQ, Pine Labs' lending analytics platform, processes more than 125,000 loans monthly.Pine Labs is increasing investments in sales and field operations across offline, online, issuing and international businesses to support expansion. It added around 675-680 employees over the past year, taking its workforce to approximately 5,200. Management said new sales employees take around nine months to become productive and cover their costs, reflecting the upfront investment required to expand merchant acquisition. Advt The company is also expanding its international presence, with operations across 22 countries. Its footprint includes around 100,000 POS terminals in Malaysia, 50,000 POS machines in the Philippines and approximately 100,000 transactions a day in the UAE. Its issuing and acquiring platform recorded 31% revenue growth, with India GTV increasing 40% year on year.The international revenue mix declined partly because of a reduction in low-contribution-margin gift card distribution in Singapore following the exit of a large international retailer. Management said the impact on EBITDA was immaterial, while the core international payments businesses continued to scale.Pine Labs also plans to launch its employee tax-benefit card, Prism, by the end of November or early December. The solution is aimed at corporates already working with the company and will allow them to provide employee benefits through a card-based platform.The brokerage expects Pine Labs to sustain strong growth, estimating a revenue compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 23.7% between FY26 and FY30. It projects revenue of Rs 3,380 crore in FY27 and Rs 4,370 crore in FY28, compared with Rs 2,710 crore in FY26. EBITDA is estimated at Rs 580 crore and Rs 800 crore in FY27 and FY28, respectively, without MDR, rising to Rs 950 crore in FY28 with MDR. It has maintained a buy rating on the stock with an unchanged target price of Rs 210.
