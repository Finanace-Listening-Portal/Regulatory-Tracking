Articles 2 min read NBFC credit grows 15.4% in September, led by 66% surge in gold loans; industrial lending lags: RBI Outstanding credit of non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), including housing finance companies (HFCs), grew 15.4 per cent year-on-year to Rs 61.7 lakh crore in September 2026, with retail lending expanding nearly 22 per cent, led by a sharp surge in gold loans and consumer durable financing. Retail credit stood at Rs 26.84 lakh crore at the end of September 2026, compared with Rs 22.01 lakh crore in the corresponding period last year.Within the retail segment, loans against gold jewellery witnessed the sharpest expansion, with outstanding credit rising to Rs 3.74 lakh crore from Rs 2.25 lakh crore a year ago. In absolute terms, the portfolio expanded by nearly Rs 1.49 lakh crore during the period. Advt Consumer durable financing also recorded strong growth, with outstanding loans increasing to Rs 77,896 crore in September 2026 from Rs 50,441 crore a year ago. Vehicle loans grew 16.1 per cent to Rs 6.46 lakh crore, while housing loans expanded 12.6 per cent to Rs 8.70 lakh crore.Industrial credit growth moderatesIn contrast, credit to the industrial sector grew 7.4 per cent year-on-year to Rs 22.69 lakh crore in September 2026, compared with 10.2 per cent growth in the corresponding period last year.Infrastructure lending, which accounts for the bulk of NBFC exposure to industry, grew 5.9 per cent to Rs 20.60 lakh crore, moderating from 9.4 per cent growth a year ago.Within infrastructure, power sector lending grew 7.9 per cent to Rs 14.80 lakh crore, compared with 11.5 per cent growth in September 2025.Services credit expands 17.3%Credit to the services sector grew 17.3 per cent year-on-year to Rs 8.02 lakh crore in September 2026, compared with 23 per cent growth in the year-ago period.Within services, lending to commercial real estate grew 23.4 per cent to Rs 1.06 lakh crore, while trade credit increased 19.8 per cent to Rs 1.23 lakh crore. Credit to transport operators rose 12.3 per cent to Rs 1.82 lakh crore.Agriculture and allied activities recorded 17.8 per cent growth, with outstanding credit increasing to Rs 81,732 crore. Advt Overall, NBFC credit outstanding increased to Rs 61.70 lakh crore in September 2026 from Rs 53.46 lakh crore a year ago. The growth rate accelerated to 15.4 per cent from 13.3 per cent in September 2025.
