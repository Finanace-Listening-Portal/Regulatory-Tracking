The amendments modify rules 215, 218 and 219 and Appendix III in relation to tax deduction at source under section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, where a resident individual or Hindu undivided family is required to deduct tax on consideration for transfer of immovable property. Rule 215 is amended to include such deductions, while corresponding changes are made in rules 218 and 219. Form No. 132 is amended to cover transfer of immovable property by a non-resident to a resident individual or Hindu undivided family and to revise certain references and notes. Form No. 141 is also amended to incorporate section 393(2) transactions and a new Schedule E for reporting TDS on consideration for transfer of immovable property. Schedule E seeks property particulars, details of buyers and non-resident sellers, stamp duty value, sale consideration, instalment information, capital gains particulars, applicable TDS rate, certificates under section 395, and tax deducted. Additional requirements address contact and overseas address details, tax residency certificates and tax identification numbers of non-resident deductees. The notification is numbered 121/2026 and was issued on 22 September 2026.
