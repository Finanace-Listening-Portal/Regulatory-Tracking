According to the result analysis released by the Institute’s Public Relations Committee, 1,04,240 candidates appeared in the examination, of whom 19,736 passed. The male candidates recorded a pass percentage of 20.47%, with 10,931 successful candidates out of 53,398 who appeared. Among female candidates, 8,805 passed out of 50,842 who appeared, resulting in a pass percentage of 17.32%. The Institute also announced the top three All India ranks. Kansika from Hisar and Mahek Gupta from Hyderabad jointly secured the first rank with 361 marks each, representing 90.25%. Sparsh Khandelwal from Ajmer and J Jayanth from Chennai jointly obtained the second rank with 359 marks each and 89.75%. Vaibhavi Singhal from Gwalior secured the third rank with 358 marks and 89.50%. The release separately states that 1,17,458 candidates appeared in the September 2026 Foundation Examination conducted across 580 centres, a figure different from the 1,04,240 candidates shown in the result analysis table. ICAI President CA. Prasanna Kumar D congratulated the successful candidates and emphasised professional responsibility, transparency, public trust and contribution to India’s economic development. The release states that examination results and marks are available at caresults.icai.org and that candidates must provide their registration and roll numbers to access them. However, its concluding paragraph refers to CA Intermediate results, although the announcement concerns the CA Foundation Examination.
