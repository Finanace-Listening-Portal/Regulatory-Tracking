Articles 1 min read Outward remittances rise to USD 2.7 bn in August; overseas investments ease Outward remittances rose to USD 2.7 billion in August 2026 from USD 2.6 billion in July, with travel accounting for 63 per cent of total outflows. Education remittances rose 29 per cent month-on-month, while overseas property and debt/equity investments declined. Advt Investment in overseas property declined around 22 per cent month-on-month in August, with its share falling to around 12 per cent from 13 per cent in July.Investments in overseas debt and equity also declined in August to USD 322 million from USD 345 million in July, a fall of nearly 7 per cent.On a broader basis, investments in overseas debt and equity stood at USD 2.65 billion in FY26, up from USD 1.70 billion in FY25. In the April-June quarter of FY27, such investments stood at USD 1.06 billion, compared with USD 885 million in January-March.
